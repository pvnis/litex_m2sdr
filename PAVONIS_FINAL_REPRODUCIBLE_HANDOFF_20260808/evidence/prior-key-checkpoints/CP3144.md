# CP3144 - Primer-free transaction-recovery result

## Verdict

Phase 1 closes as an honest partial.

The cold run used modern OCUDU and the LiteX-M2SDR without ever starting the
legacy primer. It reached repeated RAR, Msg3-sized uplink, RRC setup, and qcore
registration. The isolated qcore build executed the corrected unknown-session
and unsupported-IMS rejection paths, but the handset did not fall back to a
fresh request for the supported private DNN. It repeated retained-state
reactivation and IMS requests until the bounded activation watcher expired.

The private PDU session, echo, and two HTTP directions therefore did not run.
This checkpoint does not claim that the primer-free data milestone works.

## Evidence

All cold-entry gates passed: fresh handset epoch, SIM power cycle, policy
audit, reboot under target, and both host preflights.

Compact metadata-only analysis of the gNB trace produced:

- `37` RAR grants;
- `256` PUSCH results: `93` CRC OK and `163` CRC KO (`36.33%`);
- `7` CRC-OK 11-byte uplinks;
- `6` complete RRC setup-request paths;
- `6` completed DU UE-create procedures and `6` initial UL RRC notifications
  to the CU;
- `75` new and `8` retransmitted fallback PDSCH allocations;
- zero Soapy TX timeout events and zero downlink-late events.

qcore established one RAN association and handled seven UE connections. It
performed one initial registration, reused the resulting security context
twice, rejected four unknown-session reactivation requests, and rejected four
unsupported IMS requests. It recorded zero target user-plane activations. The
handset never exposed the private data address.

This proves the run was not clean zero-RACH pre-access scatter. The standing
single-void retry rule therefore does not permit an unchanged retry. The first
remaining semantic blocker is the handset's failure to transition from the
correct reject responses to a fresh supported-DNN request. The connected-mode
response-delivery boundary remains the leading place to investigate in future
work, but Phase 1 explicitly forbids adding another live crutch.

## Phase 1 Close

The two identified implementation gaps now have no-RF fixes and focused tests:
qcore emits standards-correct stale-session and IMS rejects with NAS request
timer recovery, and the legacy connected-mode RRC transaction no longer
requires the false-NACK workaround to retransmit its response. Scope analysis
also established that the latter implementation belongs to the legacy primer,
not modern OCUDU.

The cold primer-free success criterion remains unmet. The honest partial is:
OCUDU/LiteX-M2SDR reaches the real handset through RF access, RRC, and qcore
registration without the primer, but retained handset session negotiation does
not converge on the supported private PDU session after the correct rejects.
Per the goal, Phase 2 may now begin without further RF [interface-redacted].
