## Verdict

Pass. The first valid sustained conducted/high-SNR benchmark is established.

## Measurement

The isolated conducted shape preserves the 10 MHz Band 3 FDD, 52 PRB,
single-layer, MCS-ceiling-9 baseline. The benchmark uses sequential TCP
transfers over the private srsUE data session, with receiver-side authority and
a 45-second deadline in each direction.

| Direction | Receiver bytes | Receiver time | Sustained rate |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Downlink | 17,506,320 | 45.000261 s | 3.112217 Mbit/s |
| Uplink | 1,049,800 | 45.000125 s | 0.186631 Mbit/s |

Both endpoint records are complete and the duration gate passes. The downlink
sender wrote 20,119,424 bytes and the uplink sender wrote 1,103,376 bytes; the
receiver-side figures above remain authoritative.

- Median PUSCH TBS: `544 bytes`.

The high-SNR path is therefore valid. The baseline is not a speed improvement
over the real-phone OTA result (`8.281312/0.212563 Mbit/s` DL/UL); it is the
controlled reference from which Phase B levers must be measured one at a time.
