PASS. A new append-only report incorporates the current all-wireless phone and
srsUE baselines and the completed bandwidth sweep. It does not edit CP3121 or
the CP3134, CP3138, and CP3171 reports.

The report is installed in the supervisor package, the package entry page now
points to it, and selected CP3175-CP3181 checkpoint evidence is included
separately from the main documentation. Privacy scan, all four source-bundle
checks, reference-controller verification, and full package verification pass.

- Phone OTA, 10 MHz: `8.331264/0.124854 Mbit/s` DL/UL over 45 seconds.
- srsUE OTA, 10 MHz: `2.983260/0.167066 Mbit/s` DL/UL over 45 seconds.

- Ten megahertz is the highest width with proven sustained end-to-end data.
