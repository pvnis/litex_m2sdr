## Bandwidth sensitivity

Against CP3176's valid 10 MHz OTA baseline
(`2.983260/0.167066 Mbit/s` DL/UL), 15 MHz changes throughput by:

- downlink: `+0.186623 Mbit/s`, or `+6.26%`;
- uplink: `+0.033980 Mbit/s`, or `+20.34%`.

The 15 MHz compatibility gap is fixed and the wider shape is modestly faster,
but 50% more nominal channel bandwidth does not produce a proportional
throughput gain. Bandwidth is therefore not the dominant remaining ceiling in
this stack. The unchanged median PUSCH TBS of `528 bytes` is a concrete sign
that scheduling/grant behavior remains constrained independently of channel
width.
