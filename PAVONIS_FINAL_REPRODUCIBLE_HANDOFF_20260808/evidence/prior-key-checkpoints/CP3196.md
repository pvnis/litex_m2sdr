Date: 2026-07-28

## Verdict

Pass. CP3191 already provides the valid 15 MHz reference without the legacy
phone-primer follow-up grant branch. No RF rerun is justified.

CP3191 is a modern OCUDU+srsUE run, not an srsENB phone-primer run. Its
complete 45-second-per-direction result is `3.169883 Mbit/s` downlink and
`0.201046 Mbit/s` uplink at 15 MHz / 79 PRB / 15.36 Msps. Radio health is
clean at zero Soapy TX timeouts and zero downlink-late rows.

The audit streamed all 69 text/config/log members in both archived role
artifacts, including four members larger than 5 MiB. It scanned
`112,449,080` bytes without printing or retaining raw rows. Exact counts are
zero for:

- `PAVONIS_NR_SETUP_FOLLOWUP_UL_GRANTS`;
- the corresponding active/injected runtime marker prefix;
- the CP3194 zero-follow-up primer entrypoint;
- an srsENB process invocation.

This does not claim that CP3191 tests the phone primer. It proves the opposite:
the wider-bandwidth benchmark is an independent OCUDU+srsUE reference, so the
512-grant retirement established by CP3195 does not invalidate or require a
repeat of the 15 MHz result.

## Decision

Close the 15 MHz confirmation step no-RF. Preserve CP3191 byte-identically.
The current POC facts are:

1. the 10 MHz phone attach/data chain passes with the legacy follow-up budget
   set to zero (CP3195);
2. the independent 15 MHz OCUDU+srsUE benchmark passes and never invokes that
   legacy branch (CP3191/CP3196);
3. bandwidth raises the measured result only modestly, so the remaining
   throughput ceiling is elsewhere.

The next work is append-only report and handoff refresh. It must remove the
512 synthetic branch from the required successful-roll inventory while
retaining the other known fixtures honestly.
