# CHECKPOINT 19 - srsRAN PRACH offset probe

Date: [date-redacted]

Scope: Stage 6 local synthetic diagnostic. No RF hardware was used.

## Purpose

Checkpoint 18 showed that the observed `msg1-FrequencyStart=8` on the gNB side and `freq_offset=7` on the UE side are an expected compatibility translation for a 52 PRB NR carrier carrying a 50 PRB LTE/NR UE cell view. This checkpoint tests whether the srsRAN PRACH implementation can generate and detect the CP17-equivalent long-format PRACH waveform at that translated UE offset.

## Inputs

CP17-equivalent srsRAN PRACH parameters:

- `is_nr=1`
- `config_idx=1`
- `root_seq_idx=1`
- `zero_corr_zone=0`
- `freq_offset=7`
- `nof_prb=50`
- `carrier_nof_prb=52`
- `preamble_index=0`

The stock srsRAN PRACH self-test completed with `Done`.

The custom offset probe completed with:

```text

N_zc=839 N_cs=0 N_roots=64 N_ifft_ul=768 N_ifft_prach=9216 N_cp=1188 N_seq=9216 len=10404 avg_power=9.102021158e-02
```

## Interpretation

PASS: srsRAN can generate a CP17-equivalent long-format NR PRACH waveform at the UE-side translated offset `freq_offset=7`, then detect that same waveform with exactly one preamble, index `0`.

This narrows the Stage 6 failure. The UE-side srsRAN PRACH generator is not internally broken at the translated offset used in CP17.

Remaining suspects:

- OCUDU demodulator/detector compatibility with this generated waveform.
- RF path effects between UE TX and gNB RX.
- Runtime timing/windowing in the live prearmed run.

## Limitation

This checkpoint does not prove OCUDU can demodulate or detect the generated waveform. It is an srsRAN-only synthetic test.
