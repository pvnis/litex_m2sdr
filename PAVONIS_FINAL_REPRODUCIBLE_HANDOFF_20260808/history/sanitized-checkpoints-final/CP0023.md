## Interpretation

INCONCLUSIVE / weak evidence.

The M2SDR RX capture path works, and the bladeRF can be opened and configured after CP22's ACL fix. However, the expected +100 kHz transmitted tone was not strongly visible at the M2SDR receiver during the overlapped retry. It rose from `-4.00 dB` over median in the no-overlap control to `+4.38 dB` over median in the overlapped attempt, but this is not a strong proof of a healthy UL RF path.

This result is consistent with one of:

- wrong bladeRF TX physical port/channel for the connected RF path,
- weak or disconnected UL RF path,
- tone-generation/CLI TX issue despite apparent bladeRF configuration,
- capture being dominated by an unrelated persistent spur near `+2.493896 MHz`.

It does not prove the UE PRACH reaches M2SDR.
