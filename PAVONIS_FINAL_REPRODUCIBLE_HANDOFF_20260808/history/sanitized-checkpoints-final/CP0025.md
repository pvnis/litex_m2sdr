## Interpretation

PASS for controlled UL RF-path feasibility.

TX1 is the strong path. The received tone moved with the requested command:

- requested +100 kHz -> observed about +97.9 kHz
- requested +700 kHz -> observed about +698.0 kHz

That behavior rules out the persistent +2.494 MHz spur as the controlled TX signal. It also proves that [host-redacted] bladeRF TX1 can place an intentional UL-band signal into [host-redacted]'s M2SDR receiver path.

TX2 is much weaker than TX1 under the same settings. TX2 still shows peaks near the requested offsets, but roughly 29-31 dB lower in peak-over-median than TX1. This points to TX1 as the likely intended physical path.

The missing CP17 live PRACH is therefore unlikely to be caused by a gross disconnected RF path between [host-redacted] and [host-redacted]. The next failure is higher in the ladder: live srsUE PRACH emission details, timing, frequency placement, gain, or the gNB receive/detection path during the actual attach run.
