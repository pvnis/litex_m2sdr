## Conclusion

CP29 passed as a diagnostic checkpoint:

- PASS: the known CP19/CP20-compatible PRACH waveform was radiated over [host-redacted] bladeRF TX1.
- PASS: [host-redacted]/M2SDR captured that waveform at the uplink frequency.
- PASS: local correlation found the waveform in the capture with `BEST_NORM_CORR=0.757`.
- PASS: OCUDU replay detected the expected `rb8/p0` preamble in 6 of 7 aligned RF windows.

This strengthens the CP28 interpretation. The gross uplink RF path and the OCUDU demod/detector can see a PRACH-like waveform over RF. CP28's failure is therefore more likely specific to the live srsUE PRACH emission: burst power/scaling, timing alignment, frequency behavior, bladeRF TX behavior for short PRACH bursts, or a UE-side baseband/RF mismatch.

The Stage 6 goal is still not complete because no live gNB Msg1 detection occurred.

## Caveats

CP29 intentionally used a repeated, high-duty synthetic PRACH waveform with bladeRF TX gain 35 and SC16 scale 1024. It was captured by a standalone M2SDR receive script, not by the live gNB PRACH detector in a full attach run. It proves that the RF path and detector can work under controlled conditions; it does not prove that srsUE's live PRACH bursts are strong, aligned, or correctly radiated.
