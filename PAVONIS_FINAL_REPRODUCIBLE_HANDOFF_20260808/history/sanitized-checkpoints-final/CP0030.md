## Conclusion

CP30 is an invalid diagnostic checkpoint:

- FAIL: the gNB live TX path entered a large Soapy timeout flood.
- FAIL: the UE did not transmit PRACH.
- INCONCLUSIVE: `SRSUE_PRACH_TX_DBG` produced no `PRACH-TX max_abs` lines because there was no PRACH handoff to measure.

The Stage 6 goal remains incomplete. CP30 did reveal a tooling gap: the CP28/CP30 gate checked the OTA-stage `gnb.log`, but the authoritative live gNB log for the timeout flood was `/tmp/gnb_m2sdr_band3_ota.log`. Future gates must check both logs, and preferably wait long enough after startup to catch the early flood before starting the UE.
