## Conclusion

CP31 is a valid diagnostic checkpoint:

- PASS: strict gNB gate passed and the full run had zero Soapy TX timeouts.
- PASS: UE generated and scheduled six Msg1 PRACH attempts.
- PASS: UE PRACH chunks handed to the TX worker were near full-scale amplitude (`max_abs` about `0.99` to `1.02`).
- FAIL: gNB live detector did not detect Msg1.
- FAIL: offline replay of the PRACH-aligned raw windows did not recover nominal `rb8/p0`.

This narrows the Stage 6 failure further. The UE baseband PRACH buffer is not obviously too small at the worker handoff. CP29 proved that a controlled bladeRF TX1 PRACH waveform can be received and detected over RF. CP31 therefore points downstream of the UE worker handoff and upstream of gNB detection: the srsUE/bladeRF radio write path for short timed PRACH bursts, the actual RF port/channel used by srsUE, the timestamped transmit scheduling, or a frequency/timing mismatch specific to srsUE live PRACH.

The Stage 6 Msg1 goal is still not complete.

## Next rung
