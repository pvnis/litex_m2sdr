## Interpretation

PASS as a diagnostic, FAIL as a Stage 6 Msg1 OTA result.

CP33 moves the failure boundary. CP31 proved the UE PRACH worker produced full-scale baseband; CP33 now proves that the full-scale PRACH samples also reached `rf_blade_send_timed()`, were converted to SC16, were written through `bladerf_sync_tx()`, and were accepted by libbladeRF with no late/underflow metadata. The dumped TX buffers are independently detectable by OCUDU.

Despite that, the [host-redacted]/M2SDR PRACH windows remain noise-floor-scale and gNB detects no Msg1. Combined with CP29, where a known PRACH waveform radiated through `bladeRF-cli` TX1 was visible at [host-redacted]/M2SDR and detectable offline, the likely fault is now specific to live srsUE/bladeRF-plugin transmission behavior after `bladerf_sync_tx()` acceptance: actual RF emission, timed continuous-stream behavior, bladeRF RF state/gain/port state under srsUE, or an emission/capture timing mismatch that does not occur in the controlled `bladeRF-cli` CP29 test.
