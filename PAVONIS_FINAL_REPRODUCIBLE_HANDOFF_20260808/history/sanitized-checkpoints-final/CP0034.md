## Interpretation

PASS as a diagnostic.

CP34 proves that the srsRAN bladeRF plugin path itself can radiate a PRACH waveform that [host-redacted]/M2SDR receives strongly and OCUDU detects at the expected nominal configuration. This rules out a generic `libsrsran_rf_blade.so` / libbladeRF / bladeRF RF-path failure and narrows CP31/CP33 to conditions specific to the live `srsUE` PRACH transmission.

The important remaining difference is not "plugin versus no plugin." CP34 used the same plugin, but transmitted a high-duty repeated waveform with immediate TX semantics. CP33 used live `srsUE` continuous/timed stream behavior: an initial stream start, then sparse PRACH subframes later in the stream where high-peak PRACH writes had `has_time=1`, `sob=0`, `flags=0x0`, and `timestamp=0`.
