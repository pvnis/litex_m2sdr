## Interpretation

PASS as a diagnostic.

CP35 rules out sparse/timed continuous-stream emission as the generic explanation for CP31/CP33. A plugin-controlled stream with:

- timed start,
- a nonzero initial TX timestamp,
- later PRACH writes with `has_time=1`, `sob=0`, `timestamp=0`, `flags=0x0`,
- live `srsUE`-style 160 ms PRACH cadence,
- and the CP33 frequency condition (`1747505998`)

is clearly visible at [host-redacted]/M2SDR and is detected by OCUDU.

Combined with CP33, the remaining failure is now specific to live `srsUE` behavior outside the generic bladeRF plugin send path and outside sparse/timed streaming mechanics. The likely remaining categories are live `srsUE` RF-state/configuration interaction, exact live waveform/slot timing relationship, or a live `srsUE` path condition that differs from the controlled plugin harness despite producing detector-valid TX dumps.
