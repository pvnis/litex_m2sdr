## Interpretation

CP51 directly tested the CP49 hypothesis. The live high-peak Msg1 buffers were present and the RF plugin attempted to turn each one into a timed `TX_BURST_START`, but libbladeRF rejected every high-peak write with `BLADERF_ERR_INVAL`.

This rules out the simple "mark PRACH as a new SOB inside the existing continuous stream" workaround. To test whether the continuous zero TX stream is the root of the missing RF, the next experiment should avoid opening a continuous TX burst before Msg1: skip low-peak zero TX writes, and send only high-peak PRACH buffers as explicit timed single-buffer bursts (`SOB|EOB`) under a new env flag.
