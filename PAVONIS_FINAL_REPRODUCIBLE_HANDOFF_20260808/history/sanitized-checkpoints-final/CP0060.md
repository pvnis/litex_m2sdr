## Interpretation

CP60 separates two issues:

1. CP57 source is implicated in the no-PRACH regression. With the CP56 source restored, the UE again reaches Msg1 and produces high-peak PRACH dumps. This happened with the same CP56 launch environment used in CP59.
2. The original Stage 6 failure remains: even when high-peak PRACH attempts are generated, the gNB still reports empty detections and detector ratios far below threshold.

This means the guard experiment should not continue from CP57 as-is. The next checkpoint should audit the CP56-to-CP57 source delta and isolate why the guard patch perturbs acquisition/Msg1 reachability even when `PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_TX_DBG_TX_GUARD_SAMPLES` is unset.
