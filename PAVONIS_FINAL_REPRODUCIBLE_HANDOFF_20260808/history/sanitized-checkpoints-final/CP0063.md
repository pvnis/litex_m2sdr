## Interpretation

CP63 gives a real guard-path result:

1. Smaller guard=24000 does not prevent usable PRACH reachability.
2. Guard=24000 does not eliminate `-14`.
3. Some `-14` statuses occur even after the guard adjusts the timestamp to exactly `pre_tx_timestamp + 24000`.
4. The gNB still sees no valid PRACH detection.

The next timing experiment should either test a larger guard that exceeds the observed failure leads, or change the strategy from "minimum lead relative to sampled TX timestamp" to a more structural burst/stream scheduling change. Since guard=30000 previously coincided with no-PRACH but source-level A/B now showed acquisition variability, a single CP64 rerun with guard=30000 after CP62/CP63 success may be worth doing before abandoning the guard ladder.
