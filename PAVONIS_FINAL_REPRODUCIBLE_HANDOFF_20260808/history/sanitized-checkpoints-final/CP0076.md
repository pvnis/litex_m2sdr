## Interpretation

CP76 separates two parts of the Msg1 failure:

- PRACH generation is valid enough to pass the local detector sanity check. The four CP75 UE TX dumps are high-amplitude and produce strong nominal `rb8/p0` hits.
- The CP75 gNB pre-demod raw corpus does not contain a strong copy of those UE TX buffers under the searched frequency offsets. The best normalized template score is only about 0.074, and no score reaches 0.10.

This supports the CP75 live observation that the gNB is not merely failing to decode a present, strong PRACH. The stronger explanation is that the PRACH is not arriving in the gNB captured detection window with usable strength/timing, or that the RF path is transmitting it at a time/frequency/window the gNB raw capture did not include.
