## Interpretation

CP78 exercised the new timing-offset hook and showed that reducing the guarded PRACH lead from 40000 to 24000 samples does not make the UE PRACH visible to the gNB. The gNB did not decode a preamble, and offline template search found no strong copy of any CP78 UE PRACH dump in the 512 raw gNB windows.

The failure is therefore not explained solely by CP75's 40000-sample guard pushing Msg1 too late. The next rung should change burst framing rather than only timestamp lead: force each high-peak PRACH buffer into its own SOB/EOB burst (`PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_PRACH_BURST_ONLY=1`) with the CP75 sync path retained, then repeat the same gNB raw-template search.
