## Interpretation

CP79 proves that the failure is not only caused by high-peak PRACH burst framing or by bladeRF write status. The UE generated strong local PRACH dumps, each high-peak RF write completed successfully as an isolated timed SOB/EOB burst with an effective 24000-sample TX lead, and the gNB still produced no live preamble decode.

The offline global search also found no strong copy of any CP79 UE PRACH dump in the 512 gNB raw windows. That means the next rung should stop changing only UE RF burst framing and instead inspect receive-window alignment or RF-path presence at the gNB: either capture wider gNB-side windows around the expected absolute TX time, or run a controlled PRACH replay using the exact CP79 dumps and the same timed/burst parameters to separate srsUE scheduling from RF observability.
