## Interpretation

CP80 is a meaningful negative control. It shows that the CP79 burst-only plus guard plus `-16000` timestamp-offset treatment can produce clean bladeRF write statuses while still degrading RF observability in a controlled replay.

That makes CP79's live gNB no-detect less surprising: CP79 did not just fail because the live UE scheduler was hard to observe. The same CP79 dump, replayed by the standalone paced harness with CP79's RF environment, was only intermittently visible on the receiver and was not nominally decoded by OCUDU.

The next rung should isolate whether the regression comes from the CP79 waveform itself or from the CP79 RF environment. The direct CP81 control is to replay the same exact CP79 dump through the same CP47 paced harness with gain `60`, RX load, first burst `500 ms`, and pace lead `2 ms`, but with burst-only, guard, and PRACH timestamp-offset variables unset.

Expected discriminator:

- If CP81 is clean, CP80 isolates the regression to the burst-only/guard/offset RF hook.
- If CP81 is still intermittent or OCUDU-negative, the CP79 PRACH dump content differs from earlier passing dumps in a way that template replay can partially see but nominal detection cannot decode.
