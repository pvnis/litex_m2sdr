## Interpretation

CP82 means CP80 and CP81 cannot be interpreted as a clean indictment of the CP79 waveform yet. A waveform that historically passed now only partially replays through the current RF/capture path.

The most likely immediate issue is current RF/capture-path reliability, not srsUE PRACH generation. TX-side process status, bladeRF status, RX load, and pacing counters all look clean, so the next rung should focus on restoring or characterizing the controlled replay path before drawing more conclusions from CP79:

- repeat a known-good replay after a device/process state reset, or
- run a short RF-path health check/control capture that verifies the receiver sees every burst in sequence, or
- inspect whether [host-redacted] capture timing, RX stream state, or external RF conditions changed since CP48.

Until a known-good replay returns to CP48 quality, CP79/CP80/CP81 results remain useful but not decisive for live UE scheduling.
