## Interpretation

CP83 shows the current [host-redacted] receive environment is already elevated without the controlled transmitter. CP82's degraded known-good replay is therefore not just a [host-redacted] TX or CP45 waveform issue. The receiver is seeing current background/interference at roughly the same RMS scale as the degraded replay capture.

Because the strongest no-TX spectral components are mostly in the outer part of the 10 MHz passband, the next low-risk control is to rerun the known-good CP45 replay with a narrower [host-redacted] capture bandwidth while keeping the same TX waveform, TX gain, cadence, and RX load. If that restores CP48-style recovery, controlled replay analysis can continue with the narrowed capture bandwidth. If it does not, the path needs a stronger RF reset or environmental fix before CP79 conclusions are reliable.
