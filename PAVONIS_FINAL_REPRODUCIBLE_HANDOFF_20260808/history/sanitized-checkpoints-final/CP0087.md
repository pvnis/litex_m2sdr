## Interpretation

CP87 confirms that the 10 MHz no-TX receive floor remains elevated without our transmitter. It is high across all tested RX gains:

- Gain 20, BW 10 MHz: `8.683e-03`
- Gain 30, BW 10 MHz: `8.912e-03`
- Gain 40, BW 10 MHz: `9.211e-03`

That weak gain dependence does not look like a simple "RX gain too high" or receiver saturation-only failure. It is more consistent with external/passband RF content, time-varying bursts, or lower-level device/path state.

The 3 MHz captures reduce the all-capture RMS, but they do not remove burst energy:

- Best 3 MHz result was gain 30: all RMS `4.014e-03`, max 10 ms RMS `1.103e-02`.
- Gain 40 at 3 MHz still had all RMS `6.859e-03` and max 10 ms RMS `1.516e-02`.

The quietest setting, RX gain `30` and BW `3 MHz`, is the closest current no-TX condition to the historical CP48 receive floor. It is not as clean as CP48, but it is the best low-risk software-side setting found by the matrix.
