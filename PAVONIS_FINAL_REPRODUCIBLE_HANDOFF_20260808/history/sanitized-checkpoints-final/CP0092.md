## Interpretation

CP92 does not prove guard/offset-alone behavior, because the plugin did not apply those transforms without the burst-only/high-peak force path. The relevant debug fields are decisive: `tx_guard_samples=40000` and `prach_time_offset_samples=-16000` were visible, but `tx_guard_applied=0` and `prach_time_offset_applied=0`.

The RF waveform itself remained strong:

- CP89 baseline: `7/7` sparse, `7/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`
- CP90 full CP79 env: `7/7` sparse, `4/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`
- CP91 burst-only/SOB/EOB only: `7/7` sparse, `1/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`
- CP92 guard/offset env present but inactive: `7/7` sparse, `2/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`

The stable invariant is that the recovered [host-redacted] RX path keeps finding the replayed CP79 waveform by sparse correlation. The unstable part is OCUDU's nominal `rb8` interpretation, which has varied across otherwise healthy captures and hook modes. CP91 still most directly implicates forced burst-only/SOB/EOB behavior because it produced the worst nominal-RB result while keeping sparse recovery strong. CP92 adds that merely setting the guard/offset environment is not enough to explain the behavior, because those settings were not applied by the plugin path.
