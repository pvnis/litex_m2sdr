## Interpretation

CP93 reproduces the strong RF/sparse result but does not reproduce CP89's nominal OCUDU `rb8` result:

- CP89 baseline: `7/7` sparse, `7/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`
- CP90 full CP79 env: `7/7` sparse, `4/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`
- CP91 burst-only/SOB/EOB only: `7/7` sparse, `1/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`
- CP92 guard/offset env present but inactive: `7/7` sparse, `2/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`
- CP93 baseline repeat: `7/7` sparse, `0/7` nominal OCUDU `rb8`

The CP89 and CP93 TX debug fields match the baseline path: no forced SOB/EOB, no burst-only skip, no guard, no PRACH timestamp offset, and write status OK. Therefore the nominal `s0/rb8/p0` compact OCUDU metric is not stable enough to isolate the RF hooks by itself.

The reliable result from CP88 through CP93 is that the recovered [host-redacted] receive path sees strong CP45 and CP79 replay waveforms at RX gain `30` and bandwidth `3 MHz`. The nominal OCUDU RB result is sensitive to run/capture alignment, selected-window choice, or detector sweep behavior, and should not be treated as a hard RF-path pass/fail without more context.
