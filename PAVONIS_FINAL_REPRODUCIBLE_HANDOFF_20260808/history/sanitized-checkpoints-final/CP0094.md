# Checkpoint 94: OCUDU Sweep Variability Across CP89-CP93

Date: [date-redacted]

Scope: Stage 6 local detector/alignment analysis. CP94 did not run the radios. It parsed the existing CP89-CP93 OCUDU sweep artifacts to determine whether the nominal `s0/rb8/p0` result was a reliable RF-path gate or a run/capture-sensitive detector artifact.

## Inputs

Parser:

- `cp94_analyze_ocudu_sweep_variability.py`

Generated outputs:

- `cp94_ocudu_sweep_variability_summary.tsv`
- `cp94_ocudu_sweep_variability_windows.tsv`

Input runs:

- CP89: baseline CP79 dump replay, recovered RX settings
- CP90: full CP79 RF environment
- CP91: burst-only/SOB/EOB only
- CP92: guard/offset env present but inactive
- CP93: baseline repeat

For each selected OCUDU window, the parser extracted:

- nominal `s0/rb8/p0` hit presence
- leading compact RB sweep hit
- count of wider time-sweep `rb8` hits
- best wider time-sweep `rb8` hit
- best wider time-sweep `rb6`-`rb10` hit
- sparse selected capture start and score
- top unselected sparse candidate

## Summary Result

Compact nominal `s0/rb8` is unstable across runs:

| Checkpoint | Mode | Windows | Nominal `rb8` | Compact lead RBs | Best wider `rb8` metric range | Best wider `rb8` offsets |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |
| CP89 | baseline | 7 | 7 | `rb8:7` | `3.85..4.59` | `696,696,688,696,696,0,0` |
| CP90 | full CP79 env | 7 | 4 | `rb12:3,rb8:4` | `3.88..4.55` | `696` for all |
| CP91 | burst-only only | 7 | 1 | `rb12:6,rb8:1` | `3.54..4.48` | mostly `696`, two at `776` |
| CP92 | guard/offset env inactive | 7 | 2 | `rb12:5,rb8:2` | `3.62..4.29` | mostly `696`, one at `704` |
| CP93 | baseline repeat | 7 | 0 | `rb12:7` | `3.77..4.29` | `696` for all |

Key observations:

- Every CP89-CP93 selected window still had wider time-sweep `rb8` hits.
- The best wider `rb8` result usually landed near sample offset `696`, not at nominal `s0`.
- CP89 was the only run where compact `s0/rb8` was stable across all seven selected windows.
- CP93 was a clean baseline repeat but had `0/7` compact nominal `rb8`, proving the compact nominal gate is not reproducible under otherwise baseline RF hooks.
- The wider `rb6`-`rb10` sweep always found an even stronger `rb7` hit, usually around sample offset `928` or `936`, with metrics around `22..25`. That means the wide sweep is useful for sensitivity analysis, but it is too permissive to become a pass/fail gate by itself.
