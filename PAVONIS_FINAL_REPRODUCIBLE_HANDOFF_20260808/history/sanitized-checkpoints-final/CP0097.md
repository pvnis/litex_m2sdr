## Interpretation

CP97 shows that `rx_gain: 30` is not itself a reproducible gNB TX-timeout trigger. After CP96 cleaned stale state and proved the control path, the same `rx_gain: 30` change passed with zero TX timeouts.

This narrows CP95:

- CP95 was likely transient or contaminated by run-state/process hygiene.
- CP95 should not be treated as evidence that `rx_gain: 30` is impossible for live PRACH.
- Before any UE-bearing live run, the [host-redacted] wrapper cleanup issue should be fixed or explicitly cleaned, otherwise leftover wrappers can pollute the next checkpoint.
