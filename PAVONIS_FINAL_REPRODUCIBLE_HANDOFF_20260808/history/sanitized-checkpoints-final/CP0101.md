## Interpretation

CP101 strengthens the CP99 conclusion:

- The CP99 UE TX dumps are locally valid PRACH-like waveforms.
- The CP99 gNB raw predemod corpus contains energy, but not a recognizable
  strong copy of those UE dumps.
- The best global sliding matches are weak and cross raw-window boundaries.
- The stricter same-window search is weaker still.

This points away from a simple detector-threshold-only explanation. The failure
is earlier in the live receive chain or alignment: live PRACH energy is not
arriving in the gNB predemod windows as the waveform that the UE says it
transmitted.
