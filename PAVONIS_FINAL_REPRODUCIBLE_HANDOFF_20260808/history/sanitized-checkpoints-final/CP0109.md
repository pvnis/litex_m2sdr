## Interpretation

after force cleanup, so the CP105 timing instrumentation remains feasible for a
live diagnostic rerun.

However, the normal stop helper still leaks the outer OTA wrapper. A force
cleanup checkpoint is required before starting the next live UE run to avoid
mixing stale-wrapper state into the timing diagnostic.
