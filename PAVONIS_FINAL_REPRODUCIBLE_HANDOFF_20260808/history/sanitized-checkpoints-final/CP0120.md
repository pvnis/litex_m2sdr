No RX [interface-redacted], gain, stream start/stop, frequency, or cell-selection-adjacent code
changed in this deploy delta.

## Conclusion

CP120 rules out an accidental broad source overwrite as the reason CP116/CP118
failed cell selection. The repeated cell-selection failures should be treated as
a downlink/UE RX health issue or transient RF/environment condition, not as a
confirmed result of the zero-EOB patch.
