## Interpretation

VALID live Msg1 attempt, but Stage 6 still fails.

CP123 proves the CP115 patch removed the zero-sample EOB invalid-operation symptom: those calls are now logged as `SKIPPED_ZERO_EOB` with no bad libbladeRF status. The UE also recovered from the CP116/CP118 cell-selection blocker and emitted four full-scale PRACH buffers.
