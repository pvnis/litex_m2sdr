## Interpretation

CP125 confirms CP123's accepted live UE PRACH writes do not appear as a recognizable waveform in the saved gNB raw PRACH corpus:

- Direct same-window score max: about `0.051`.
- Global sliding score max: about `0.073`.
- No score reached the weak `0.10` threshold.
- The approximate timing join lands near low-energy or non-PRACH-like raw windows.
- gNB detector trace in CP123 had `accepted=0` and best ratio max `0.226` against threshold `0.610`.

Combined with CP102/CP103 replay success, this preserves the core split:

- The dumped UE PRACH waveform is valid and can work through controlled RF replay.
- During live srsUE attach, the gNB raw receive windows do not contain that waveform strongly enough to correlate.

CP115/CP123 also ruled out zero-sample EOB invalid operations as the sufficient explanation: after the patch, zero-EOB calls are skipped cleanly, yet live PRACH remains absent at gNB.
