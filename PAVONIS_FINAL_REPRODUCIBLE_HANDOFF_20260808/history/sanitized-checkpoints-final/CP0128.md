## Interpretation

CP128 argues against a broad current RF-path failure. Under the same current lab state, same TX/RX gain, same capture bandwidth, same RX-load setting, and same baseline plugin environment, the CP99 waveform again replayed cleanly by sparse correlation.

This makes passive antenna overheating an unlikely primary explanation for the current failure pattern. If the antenna/path were broadly failing, CP128 should also have degraded. The more likely suspects are now:

- CP123 dump-specific waveform or replay behavior,
- live srsUE/bladeRF stream-state timing around CP123 Msg1,
- live gNB PRACH receive-window timing/context,
- an intermittent issue that is triggered by the CP123/live path but not by the CP99 controlled replay.

## Next step

Replay a different CP123 dump, especially `000004`, in baseline mode. CP123 dump `000004` was the later live PRACH attempt with a slightly different RMS/peak and CFO context. If dump `000004` replays cleanly, then dump `000001` or its surrounding replay timing is suspicious. If dump `000004` is also degraded while CP99 stays clean, the problem follows the CP123 dump family or live CP123 generation path.
