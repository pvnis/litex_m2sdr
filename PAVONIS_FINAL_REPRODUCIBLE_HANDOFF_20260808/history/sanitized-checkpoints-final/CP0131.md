## Interpretation

CP131 does not show an obvious host-level overheating or device-enumeration failure:

- [host-redacted] bladeRF is visible, loaded, SuperSpeed, and idle after tests.
- [host-redacted] M2SDR is visible to PCIe/Soapy and idle after tests.
- host CPU/PCH/NVMe temperatures are not high enough to explain immediate RF collapse.
- filtered recent dmesg did not show USB, PCIe, DMA, thermal, or timeout evidence.

However, CP131 does not directly measure bladeRF RFIC temperature, M2SDR RFIC temperature, antenna/contact temperature, cable contact quality, or analog front-end gain drift. Therefore it weakens a host-overheating theory, but it does not eliminate active RF-chain thermal/intermittency.

## Next step

Run an explicit idle cool-down followed by a CP99/CP123 replay pair. If CP99 returns to `7/7` and CP123 remains degraded, the CP123-specific theory strengthens. If CP99 stays degraded after cool-down, the active RF-path variability theory strengthens.
