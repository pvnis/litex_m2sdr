## Interpretation

CP132 strongly supports active RF/path variability with recovery after idle time:

- CP130 degraded to `4/7` after activity.
- A five-minute idle interval restored CP99 to `7/7` sparse and `7/7` OCUDU nominal hits.
- This is compatible with thermal drift, contact/interconnect intermittency, or device/stream-state recovery after idle.

This still does not prove passive antenna overheating. The better framing is active RF-chain/path variability because the bladeRF/M2SDR system recovered after idle and the host health snapshot did not show host-level overheating.
