## Interpretation

CP133 shows that the controlled RF/path state is a major contributor:

- CP123 dump `000001` improved from `2/7` in CP127 to `5/7` after the same idle recovery that restored CP99 to `7/7`.
- The replay path still does not carry CP123 as consistently as CP99 in the recovered state.

This points to overlapping causes:

- active RF/path state or thermal/intermittent behavior affects controlled replay quality,
- CP123-specific waveform/live-generation conditions still matter because CP123 `000001` remains `5/7`, not `7/7`, under conditions where CP99 was `7/7`.

The passive antenna-only theory remains too simple. A more plausible version is active RF-chain/path state plus CP123-specific timing/waveform sensitivity.
