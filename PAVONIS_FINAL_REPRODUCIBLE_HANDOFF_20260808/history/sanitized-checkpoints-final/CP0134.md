## Interpretation

CP134 is a decisive recovered-path pass for CP123 dump `000004`.

The same dump that failed at `0/7` in CP129 passed at `7/7` sparse correlation and `7/7` OCUDU nominal hits after the path had idled and recovered. Therefore CP129 was not caused by intrinsic invalidity of dump `000004`.

The strongest current explanation is active RF/path state:

- CP130 showed CP99 degraded after replay activity.
- CP132 showed a five-minute idle interval restored CP99 to `7/7`.
- CP133 showed CP123 dump `000001` improved from `2/7` to `5/7` after that recovery.
- CP134 showed CP123 dump `000004` improved from `0/7` to `7/7`.

This does not prove passive antenna overheating. It is more consistent with active RF-chain/path variability, thermal drift, contact/interconnect intermittency, or SDR analog/digital stream state that recovers after idle.
