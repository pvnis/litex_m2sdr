## Interpretation

CP141 is a raw-IQ negative result.

The CP139 live UE produced four high-peak PRACH TX dumps, but none of those waveform templates appears in the 512-window gNB raw corpus at a meaningful correlation level:

- best same-window score: about `0.0536`
- best global sliding score: about `0.0749`
- hits >= `0.10`: `0`
- hits >= `0.25`: `0`
- hits >= `0.50`: `0`

This supports CP139's detector-negative result with raw-IQ evidence. The live waveform was not merely missed by the PRACH detector; it was not visibly present in the saved receiver raw windows.

The current working split is:

- controlled replay after idle can be clean (`7/7` in CP132 and CP134),
- live srsUE/bladeRF writes can report OK,
- but the live waveform is absent from the gNB raw corpus.

That points away from pure gNB detector threshold [interface-redacted] and toward live emission/timestamping, active RF path state, or a physical/interconnect state that differs between controlled replay and live srsUE.
