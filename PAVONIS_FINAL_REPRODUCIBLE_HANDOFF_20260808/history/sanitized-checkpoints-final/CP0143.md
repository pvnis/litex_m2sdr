## Interpretation

CP143 is the strongest controlled-path evidence since CP134.

The exact CP139 dump `000004` that was absent from the CP139 live gNB raw corpus passes cleanly when replayed through the controlled bladeRF plugin path. That means:

- the waveform itself is valid enough for the receiver/detector path,
- the bladeRF to M2SDR RF path can carry it cleanly in the current session,
- the gNB-side detector/offline analysis can recognize it when RF energy is actually present.

This shifts the primary live-failure fork toward live srsUE/bladeRF behavior:

- live timed TX semantics,
- live timestamp alignment against gNB capture windows,
- live RF state changes during srsUE operation,
- or live-only interaction between srsUE scheduling and bladeRF metadata/SOB/EOB handling.

The active RF path is still not fully exonerated because CP142 was only `5/7`, but CP143 makes a passive antenna-overheating explanation substantially weaker. If the antenna/path were simply unable to carry the waveform, CP143 should not have produced `7/7` clean controlled hits minutes after CP139.
