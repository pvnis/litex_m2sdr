## Interpretation

CP148 reproduces the CP139 live failure under a cleaner lead-in:

- CP143 proved the controlled path can carry the exact live-style PRACH waveform cleanly.
- CP146 proved the gNB strict gate can pass with zero Soapy TX timeouts.
- CP147 cleaned the leftover process state.
- CP148 then launched the UE and produced five high-peak bladeRF PRACH writes with `status=0`, but the gNB still saw no preamble.

This strongly shifts the failure away from waveform validity, gNB detector threshold, and passive antenna inability. The main suspects remain live-only behavior:

- live srsUE/bladeRF timed TX semantics,
- live timestamp alignment against gNB receive windows,
- live SOB/EOB/metadata interaction,
- or live harness SDR state that differs from controlled replay.
