## Interpretation

CP151 proves the CP148 dump `000005` is not intrinsically invalid. The same waveform that CP150 could not find in the live CP148 gNB raw corpus is visible when transmitted through the controlled bladeRF plugin path, and it produces nominal RB8 OCUDU hits in five of seven expected windows.

This strengthens the live-vs-controlled split:

- live CP148 srsUE reported high-peak timed PRACH writes with `status=0`,
- CP150 found that waveform absent from saved gNB raw windows, including near expected wall-clock windows,
- CP151 can carry the exact dump through the controlled RF path.

However, CP151 is only a partial pass, like CP142 and unlike CP143. The controlled path is usable but currently variable: five windows are strong, two expected windows are dropouts. That keeps an active RF-chain/path state, timing/capture intermittency, or SDR state issue on the table. Passive antenna overheating alone remains weak as a sole explanation because the same physical path can still deliver strong controlled hits minutes after live failure.
