## Interpretation

CP155 does not support a currently elevated [host-redacted] receive floor as the explanation for CP151-CP154 degradation.

The exact settings used by CP151-CP154, RX gain `30` and BW `3 MHz`, are now quiet:

- CP155 gain 30 / 3 MHz all-capture RMS: `1.337905414e-03`
- CP155 gain 30 / 3 MHz max 10 ms RMS: `1.652834704e-03`

The cleanest current no-TX setting is RX gain `40`, BW `3 MHz`:

- all-capture RMS: `1.322126249e-03`
- peak: `9.178453125e-03`
- max 10 ms RMS: `1.389369369e-03`

That means the controlled replay dropouts are more likely on the TX side, the physical RF path, active SDR/RF-chain state, or replay/capture timing/state interaction than on a persistently noisy [host-redacted] receiver floor. It also weakens an external broad-band interference theory for the current moment, though not for earlier checkpoints.
