## Interpretation

CP156 is a strong recovery result. It shows that the CP148 dump `000005` and the controlled bladeRF-to-M2SDR RF path can still pass cleanly when [host-redacted] RX gain is raised to `40` while keeping BW at `3 MHz`.

This changes the active RF-path reading:

- The CP151-CP154 decline at RX gain `30` was not a dead path and not a bad waveform.
- CP155 showed the no-TX floor at RX gain `40` / BW `3 MHz` is quiet.
- CP156 then restored all seven controlled hits at that receive setting.

The best current explanation is a received-signal-margin problem at RX gain `30` in the controlled path, possibly caused by path loss, antenna/cable/contact state, or analog/RF gain drift. It does not require passive antenna overheating as the primary cause, and it does not contradict the earlier live-failure split: CP148 live still had OK TX writes that were absent from the gNB raw corpus at RX gain `30`.
