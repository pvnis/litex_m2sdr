## Interpretation

CP163 is a strong raw-IQ negative result for the RX gain `40` live retry.

The CP161 live UE produced five high-peak PRACH TX dumps, but none of those waveform templates appears in the 512-window gNB raw corpus at a meaningful correlation level:

- best same-window score: about `0.0450`
- best global sliding score: about `0.0638`
- hits >= `0.10`: `0`
- hits >= `0.25`: `0`
- hits >= `0.50`: `0`

The wall-clock join makes the result especially strong: every estimated CP161 UE TX time has a saved gNB raw window within `0.296 ms`, yet those windows remain near receiver floor and contain no matching PRACH waveform.

This reproduces the CP150 conclusion at RX gain `40`: live srsUE/bladeRF reports OK high-peak timed writes, but the gNB raw IQ does not contain the transmitted waveform.
