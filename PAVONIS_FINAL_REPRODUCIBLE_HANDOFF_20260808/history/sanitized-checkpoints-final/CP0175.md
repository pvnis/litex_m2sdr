## Interpretation

CP175 is another strong raw-IQ negative result.

CP173 produced five high-peak PRACH TX dumps, but none appears in the 512-window gNB raw corpus at meaningful correlation:

- best same-window score: about `0.0443`
- best global sliding score: about `0.0729`
- hits >= `0.10`: `0`
- hits >= `0.25`: `0`
- hits >= `0.50`: `0`

The nearest manifest windows around the estimated TX times are all low-energy. This reproduces the CP163/CP150 pattern at RX gain 40: live srsUE/bladeRF reports valid high-peak timed PRACH writes, but gNB raw IQ does not contain the waveform.
