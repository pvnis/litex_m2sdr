## Interpretation

CP176 shows that CP173 dump `000005` is not an invalid waveform. The same CP173 live PRACH waveform that was absent from the CP173 gNB raw corpus in CP175 is strongly received when replayed through the controlled paced plugin path.

This reinforces the live-vs-controlled split:

- CP173 live srsUE/bladeRF reported valid high-peak timed PRACH writes.
- CP175 could not find those live writes in the gNB pre-demod raw corpus.
- CP176 can transmit the CP173 dump over the controlled RF path and recover it at RX gain `40` with six strong sparse/OCUDU hits.

The likely failure area remains live srsUE stream state, live RF-emission behavior, or live-path timing/state around the UE PRACH write, rather than waveform validity or gNB detector logic.
