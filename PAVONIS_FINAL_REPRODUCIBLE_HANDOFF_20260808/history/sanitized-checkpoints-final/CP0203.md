## Interpretation

CP203 is a strong controlled-path pass. The exact CP201 PRACH dump that failed
live is valid, crosses the RF path immediately after CP201/CP202, and remains
detectable when the controlled plugin applies the same high-peak `TX_NOW`
metadata path:

- CP201 live: 5 high-peak PRACH writes with `TX_NOW` applied, but gNB saw 0
  Msg1 detections and only noise-scale PRACH raw windows.
- CP203 controlled replay: same CP201 dump `000005`, CP201 hook env including
  `TX_NOW`, and RX gain 40 produces `7/7` strong sparse hits and `7/7` nominal
  OCUDU RB8/preamble-0 hits.

This makes the antenna-overheating or dead RF-path theory unlikely as the
primary explanation for CP201. If the passive antenna/path were simply no
longer working after CP201, CP203 would not have produced clean controlled
replay immediately afterward. The remaining suspect is still live-only
srsUE/bladeRF stream behavior: stream state, timed-stream boundary handling,
SOB/EOB context, interaction with the long-running low-peak stream, or live
sync/timing state.

The first-failed-stage rule still applies: Stage 6 PRACH Msg1 is generated by
the UE path, but the live gNB still does not detect it.
