## Important defect found

The generated CP207 sequence did include:

```text
PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_TX_DBG_RESTART_TX_STREAM_ON_FIRST_HIGH_PEAK=1
```

But the packaged `launch.env` and `run_srsue_bounded.sh` did not include or forward that variable into the final `sudo -n env ... srsue` invocation.

Root cause: `cp193_sens_start_stage6_high_peak_tx_now_ue_remote.sh` uses an explicit `sudo env` allow-list and does not include `PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_TX_DBG_RESTART_TX_STREAM_ON_FIRST_HIGH_PEAK`.
