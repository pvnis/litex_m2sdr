## Interpretation

CP213 is a strong controlled-path pass. The exact CP211 PRACH dump that failed
live is valid, crosses the RF path immediately after CP211/CP212, and remains
detectable with timed metadata and `TX_NOW` disabled.

This further weakens the antenna-overheating/dead-path theory for the current
failure mode. CP191, CP203, and now CP213 all show clean controlled replay over
the same radios/cabling conditions after live failures. The remaining failure is
more likely that the live UE PRACH energy is not landing in the gNB PRACH receive
windows, or that the live path differs in scheduling/timestamp alignment from
the controlled replay path.

CP211 dump `000001` template to check for off-window energy before changing more
RF code.
