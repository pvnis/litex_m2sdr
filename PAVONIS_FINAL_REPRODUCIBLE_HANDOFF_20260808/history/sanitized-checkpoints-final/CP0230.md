## Interpretation

CP230 rules out CP228's 11.52 Msps / 10 MHz capture geometry as the only reason
for the downlink failure. With the detector-recommended 5.76 Msps / 5.6 MHz
capture, the signal is still present and unclipped, PSS is detected, and the
best PSS NID2 is compatible with PCI 1 on the 0.10 s trim, but SSS/PCI still
does not prove expected PCI 1.

This strengthens the current ladder diagnosis:

- the current first-failed stage is downlink identity/cell acquisition, before
  SIB/RACH-config reception and before Msg1;
- PRACH timing tests are not meaningful until expected PCI 1 is proven again;
- the next safe diagnostic should adjust receive-side observation first, e.g.
  repeat the same smoke with the previously used bladeRF gain 40, after cleanup.
