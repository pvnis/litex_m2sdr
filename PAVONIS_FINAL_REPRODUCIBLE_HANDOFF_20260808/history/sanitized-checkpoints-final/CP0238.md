## Interpretation

CP238 is not a PRACH timing result. The UE did not reach real RACH config,
Random Access, PRACH-TX, or RF TX debug. Under the standing first-failed-stage
rule, the first failed stage is still downlink acquisition/RACH configuration,
not Msg1 timing at the gNB.

Lowering UE config `rx_gain` to 30 did not recover acquisition in this live
srsUE run. In fact, CP238 is earlier than CP225: CP225 at least reached a
placeholder PRACH config line, while CP238 emitted no PRACH config line at all.

The result weakens the direct hypothesis that "gain30 fixes UE acquisition,"
even though CP236 showed an independent downlink capture could see PCI 1 at
gain30 with no clipping. The live UE path may still be limited by low/unstable
downlink margin, the bladeRF gain mapping, or another UE acquisition issue.
