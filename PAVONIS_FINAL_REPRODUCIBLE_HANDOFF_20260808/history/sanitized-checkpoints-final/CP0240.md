## Interpretation

holding gNB `tx_gain_backoff: 20` still produced no real RACH config, Random
Access, PRACH-TX, or RF TX debug rows.

CP240 confirms that CP238's no-acquisition result was not explained solely by
changing the UE config file from `rx_gain = 40` to `rx_gain = 30`. With
backoff20, both UE config variants failed before Msg1.

Under the standing first-failed-stage rule, the active blocker remains
downlink acquisition/RACH configuration. Further PRACH timing changes are not
meaningful until the UE again reaches Random Access and emits a high-peak PRACH
write.
