# Checkpoint 251 - +160 ms original-phase PRACH shift, no gNB detection

## Summary

CP251 reran the CP247-style backoff10/rx_gain40 condition with one controlled timing change:

```text
PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_TX_DBG_TX_GUARD_SAMPLES=0
PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_TX_DBG_PRACH_TIME_OFFSET_SAMPLES=1843200
```

At 11.52 Msps, `1843200` samples is exactly `160 ms`, matching the observed PRACH detector occasion spacing. The intent was to preserve the UE's original timed PRACH phase and transmit the same waveform one PRACH opportunity later. This should allow the gNB detector to see Msg1 even if the UE's RA-RNTI/RAR expectation later fails.

Result: the offset applied correctly and bladeRF accepted four high-peak PRACH writes, but the gNB still detected no preamble.

## Interpretation

CP251 rules out a narrow version of the timing theory: even when the original UE PRACH timestamp was shifted by exactly one PRACH-period in SDR samples, the gNB detector still saw no acceptable preamble and no non-empty detection.

That does not fully rule out timing, because we still lack a shared RF clock between hosts and the detector/log wall-clock alignment is coarse. But it makes "small RF timestamp offset" an unlikely sole fix. The next useful ladder step should move toward one of:

- receiver-side verification of whether any shifted PRACH energy appears in the gNB predemod captures,
- a controlled RF path/power check,
- or a frequency/waveform compatibility check of the UE PRACH dump against the gNB detector configuration.
