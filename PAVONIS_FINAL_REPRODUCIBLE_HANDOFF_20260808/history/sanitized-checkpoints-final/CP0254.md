# CHECKPOINT 254 - Offline CP251 UE Waveform Search Report - [date-redacted]

## Question

After CP251 proved that the UE sent four high-peak PRACH dumps with the intended no-guard/+160 ms timing change, check whether the exact UE TX waveform appears anywhere in the gNB PRACH predemod raw capture corpus.

This is the next rung after CP253: if timing is merely wrong, a sliding search across the captured gNB raw windows might still find the transmitted waveform somewhere. If the waveform is absent everywhere, the evidence shifts toward RF path, power, frequency placement, or capture-point mismatch.

## Conclusion

CP254 strengthens the CP253 conclusion. The issue is probably not just a small PRACH timestamp placement error inside the gNB detector window. The exact UE TX waveform is not recognizable anywhere in the CP251 gNB predemod capture corpus, even with a sliding start offset and +/-6 kHz frequency sweep.

The next rung should be read-only RF/hardware/path checks, then a controlled path/power test if safe. Priorities:

1. Confirm current bladeRF/device health and temperatures where available.
2. Confirm TX/RX antenna and cabling assumptions.
3. Confirm gNB RX path is seeing energy when expected.
4. If passive antenna overheating is suspected, treat it as less likely than active RF device gain/thermal/path issues, but collect thermal and power evidence rather than guessing.
