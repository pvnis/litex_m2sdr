## Interpretation

The gross [host-redacted] bladeRF TX1 to [host-redacted] M2SDR RF path is still alive:

- requested `+100 kHz` on TX1 appeared at about `+97.5 kHz`
- requested `+700 kHz` on TX1 appeared at about `+697.5 kHz`
- both TX1 cases were far above the no-TX baseline and moved with the commanded
  tone

TX2 did not produce a reliable requested-offset tone in this run. The strongest
peaks during TX2 captures were unrelated high-offset signals, and the requested
tone bins stayed near baseline. This reinforces TX1 as the usable physical path.

CP262 narrows CP260's failed CP45 replay: the path is not grossly disconnected,
but PRACH-like replay at current settings is still failing. The likely problem is
not a passive antenna simply being open or overheated. It remains consistent with
active path/device/stream state, PRACH replay waveform visibility, gain/margin,
interference during PRACH-like bursts, or capture/detector-specific sensitivity.
