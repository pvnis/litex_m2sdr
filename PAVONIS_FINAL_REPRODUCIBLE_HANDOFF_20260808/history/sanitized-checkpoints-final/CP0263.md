## Interpretation

Raising [host-redacted] RX gain from `30` to `40` partially recovers the CP45 known-good
replay. That points toward received signal margin or intermittent RF path/stream
state rather than a fully disconnected path.

However, CP263 is still not clean. A live UE/gNB run remains unjustified because
only three of seven expected replay windows were strongly recovered.

The most useful next rung is a controlled-replay repeat or margin sweep, not
live UE:

- keep CP45 waveform and TX gain `60`
- stay at BW `3 MHz`
- test whether the partial recovery is repeatable at RX gain `40`
- if still partial, try a bounded RX/TX margin variant before returning to live
