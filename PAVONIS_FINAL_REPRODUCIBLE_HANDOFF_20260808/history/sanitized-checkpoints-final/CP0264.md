## Interpretation

The controlled PRACH-shaped RF path is recoverable at RX gain `40`, BW `3 MHz`,
using the historical CP45 known-good waveform. The CP260 failure was not a
permanent disconnection and not a simple passive antenna overheat. The behavior
is transient and margin/state-sensitive.

This restores the lower controlled-replay rung, but it does not yet justify a UE
Msg1 attempt. The previous accidental live leg failed earlier at the gNB strict
gate with `7449` Soapy TX timeouts. The next rung should therefore be gNB-only
strict gate at the same live settings, with no UE, and only proceed upward if the
strict gate is clean.
