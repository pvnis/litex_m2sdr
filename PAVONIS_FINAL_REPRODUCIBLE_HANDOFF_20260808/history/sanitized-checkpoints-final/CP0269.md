## Interpretation

CP269 moved cleanly through the live gate and reproduced the core failure:

- gNB strict gate is clean at `rx_gain=40`.
- UE cell search and PRACH config are real.
- UE emits five high-peak bladeRF PRACH buffers with the `+160 ms` offset applied.
- gNB PRACH detector logs every occasion but accepts zero preambles.
- Raw gNB PRACH windows at the estimated UE TX wall times remain at noise-scale
  levels, not at controlled-replay levels.

This keeps the failure above the recovered lower rungs but below successful
Msg1 detection. The next useful rung should focus on why live UE TX energy is
not present at the gNB PRACH window despite controlled replay and tone checks
passing: timing-domain mismatch, UE TX scheduling/stream timestamp semantics,
or a bladeRF live-TX RF path/state difference from the controlled replay path.
