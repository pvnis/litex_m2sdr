## Interpretation

CP271 is a strong negative result for CP269:

- The five CP269 UE TX dumps are valid high-amplitude templates.
- They are not recognizable in their nearest gNB PRACH windows.
- They are not recognizable anywhere in the first 512 saved CP269 gNB PRACH raw
  windows, even with a `+/-6 kHz` frequency sweep and sliding start offset.

This repeats and strengthens the CP254 conclusion using the fresh CP269 run:
the live srsUE/bladeRF PRACH waveform is not present in the captured gNB PRACH
predemod corpus. The problem is unlikely to be a small detector-window timing
offset alone. The remaining likely fork is live bladeRF emission/RF state versus
the controlled replay path, or a capture-chain/path difference specific to live
srsUE operation.
