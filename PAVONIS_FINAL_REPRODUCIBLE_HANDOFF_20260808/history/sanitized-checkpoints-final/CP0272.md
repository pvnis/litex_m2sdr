## Interpretation

The exact CP269 UE waveform is not intrinsically bad: it can be transmitted by
the bladeRF-controlled replay path and recovered by [host-redacted] at RX gain `40`, BW
`3 MHz`.

Combined with CP269 and CP271, this strongly points away from "bad PRACH
samples" and away from a passive antenna-only explanation. The remaining
failure is more likely specific to the live srsUE Msg1 transmit path or its
runtime RF/device state: timed TX scheduling, stream state around the live
receive/transmit transition, channel/path selection, or another live-only
bladeRF state difference. The controlled path is still margin-sensitive
(`5/7`, not CP264's `7/7`), but it is clearly not dead.
