## Interpretation

The live-style bladeRF metadata path is cleared in controlled replay:

- one-chunk high-peak PRACH bursts with `force_sob=1` and `force_eob=1`
- `flags=0x3`
- `+1843200` sample PRACH timestamp offset
- restart on first high peak
- no TX_NOW

Those conditions still produce clean RF capture and nominal OCUDU detections when
driven by the controlled replay program. Therefore CP269's live failure is not
explained by the waveform, the passive RF path, TX/RX frequency/gain/channel
selection, or the burst-only/restart/offset metadata mechanism alone.

The remaining likely failure is live timing alignment between the srsUE Msg1
scheduler and the gNB PRACH receive windows. CP269 can schedule and submit valid
high-peak PRACH writes, but their energy is not present in the saved gNB PRACH
raw windows. The next rung should target live timing/window alignment rather
than another RF-path replay.

## Next rung

Run a live timing probe that changes the PRACH timestamp offset relative to
CP269 and reports the wall-clock delta to gNB PRACH raw windows before another
full interpretation. The useful test is not more gain or burst metadata; it is
to move the live PRACH energy into the gNB's actual raw PRACH window.
