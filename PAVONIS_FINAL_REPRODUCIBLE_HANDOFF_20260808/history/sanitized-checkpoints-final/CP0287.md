## Interpretation

CP287 resolves the gain-readback ambiguity from CP283:

- the immediate `set_rx_gain` readback of `60.000` is pre-enable/stale
- after RX stream enable, requested `rx_gain=30` is visible as `rx_actual=30.000`

The backoff20 live UE path still failed before cell acquisition. Therefore
backoff20 plus real UE `rx_gain=30` does not recover the srsUE live cell-search
path, despite CP281's independent SSB detector seeing expected PCI 1 at low
margin under a related backoff20/gain30 capture condition.
