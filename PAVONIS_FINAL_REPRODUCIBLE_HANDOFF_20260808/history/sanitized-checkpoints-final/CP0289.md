## Interpretation

CP289 re-establishes the live RACH-producing condition and confirms actual
post-enable UE RX gain:

- backoff10 / UE `rx_gain=40` still reaches cell acquisition, SFN sync, and
  five PRACH writes
- the bladeRF RX gain is not stale after stream enable: requested `40` reads
  back as actual `40`
- all five high-peak PRACH writes returned OK and produced `.sc16` dumps
- the gNB still did not accept or even list a candidate preamble

The active fault is therefore beyond initial UE acquisition and beyond simple
UE gain-readback ambiguity. The next rung should be offline correlation of the
five CP289 UE PRACH dumps against the CP289 gNB predemod/raw PRACH windows and
their timing metadata.
