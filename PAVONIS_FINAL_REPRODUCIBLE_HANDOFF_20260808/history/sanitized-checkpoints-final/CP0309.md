## Interpretation

CP309 found no copy of the UE PRACH dumps in the saved gNB PRACH-window raw corpus. This matches CP308:

- UE generated four valid native-timed PRACH bursts.
- gNB PRACH detector never accepted a preamble.
- gNB raw PRACH windows stayed near floor at the nearest estimated times.

This keeps the failure above "UE never reached PRACH" and below "gNB successfully receives Msg1": the UE writes are accepted locally, but the expected waveform is not visible in the saved gNB PRACH raw windows.
