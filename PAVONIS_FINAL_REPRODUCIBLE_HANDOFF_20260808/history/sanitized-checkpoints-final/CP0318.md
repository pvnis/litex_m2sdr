# Checkpoint 318: CP316 offline timing and raw analysis

## Purpose

Search the CP316 gNB PRACH raw corpus for copies of the five UE PRACH TX debug
waveforms. CP316 had already shown the positive offset was applied and moved the
scheduled TX estimate near the target gNB PRACH rows, but the online detector
still accepted no preambles. CP318 checks whether the waveform is present in the
saved raw corpus below detector threshold or absent from the saved corpus.

## UL symbol dump check

CP316 also produced a large UL symbol manifest:

- manifest rows: `160000`
- saved UL symbol `.cf32` rows/files: `1129`
- manifest span: `131657142` samples, about `11.428 s`
- saved symbol sample total: `929052`

The saved UL symbol files did not include the CP316 target PRACH rows:

- target rows from the timing audit: `sfn 721/737/753/769/785`, slot `4`
- no saved UL symbol raw rows were available around those target rows
- saved UL symbol subsets covered other SFN/slot regions, such as `673`, `705`,
  and `817`

Therefore the UL symbol dump set cannot currently confirm whether the PRACH
waveform landed in the target windows.

## Interpretation

CP318 did not find a UE waveform copy in the saved gNB PRACH raw corpus. The
best same-window scores were about `0.043`, and the best global sliding scores
were about `0.077`, both below the `0.10` working threshold.

Combined with CP316, the evidence is:

- UE transmitted five positive-offset PRACH bursts.
- Timing moved close to the intended gNB PRACH rows.
- The online detector accepted no preambles.
- The saved PRACH raw corpus still does not contain a convincing copy of the UE
  PRACH waveform.
- The auxiliary UL symbol dumps do not cover the target SFN/slot rows.

The next rung should improve receive-side observability around the CP316 target
windows. A useful live RF retry should first add targeted contiguous RX capture
or deterministic UL-symbol saving for the target PRACH SFN/slot rows, otherwise
another offset-only attempt is unlikely to distinguish RF-path absence from a
capture/detector visibility problem.
