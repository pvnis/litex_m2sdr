# Checkpoint 321: self-contained positive offset with targeted UL-symbol capture

## Purpose

Run a CP316-style self-contained RF attempt with the proven positive PRACH
timestamp offset while forcing UL-symbol raw saves for the expected PRACH target
slot pattern. CP320 had already installed the targeted save trigger on [host-redacted].

The point of CP321 was receive-side observability, not another blind timing
[interface-redacted]: if the UE PRACH reached the gNB receive chain, the target UL-symbol files
should contain a stronger waveform copy even when the online detector stays
below threshold.

## Interpretation

CP321 proves that the all-or-nothing two-NUC RF orchestration still works under
the unstable laptop link, that the UE can still emit five valid positive-offset
PRACH bursts, and that the CP319/CP320 targeted UL-symbol save path is active.

The online gNB detector still accepted no preambles. CP321 by itself does not
settle whether the UE waveform is absent from the gNB receive chain; that is the
job of the CP322 timing audit and CP323 offline waveform search.
