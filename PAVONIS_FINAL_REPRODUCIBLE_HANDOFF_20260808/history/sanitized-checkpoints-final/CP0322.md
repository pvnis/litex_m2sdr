## Interpretation

CP321 timing was close enough to target the right slot family, but worse than
CP316. CP316's best scheduled-TX estimate was within about `0.35-0.76 ms` of the
target PRACH rows; CP321 was about `3.65-4.29 ms` after the nearest target rows.

That timing error could still matter for detector acceptance, but it is not the
best next bet by itself. CP316 already had sub-millisecond timing and still
showed no convincing UE waveform copy in the saved gNB PRACH raw corpus. CP323
therefore searched both the PRACH raw corpus and the newly forced target
UL-symbol corpus before deciding whether another offset-only retry is justified.
