Search CP321's saved gNB receive artifacts for copies of the five UE PRACH TX
debug waveforms. CP321 added deterministic target UL-symbol saves, so CP323
searched both:

- the saved PRACH predemod raw corpus

## Interpretation

CP323 is the strongest receive-observability check so far. It searched the
normal PRACH raw save corpus and the newly forced target UL-symbol corpus. Both
searches stayed below a `0.10` match score.

Combined with CP316 and CP321:

- the UE can produce valid PRACH TX waveforms
- the positive offset is being applied
- CP316 got sub-millisecond timing to the target rows
- CP321 forced target UL-symbol raw saves and those saves covered the expected
  CP321 target slots
- the gNB still accepted no preambles
- no convincing UE waveform copy appears in either saved gNB corpus

The next useful rung should validate the gNB receive/RF path itself with a
known signal or loopback/cabled path. Another PRACH timestamp-only retry is low
value unless paired with an independent RX-chain sanity check.
