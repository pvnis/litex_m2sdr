## Interpretation

Increasing TX gain made the detected tone peak very strong. The peak was within
`2.881 kHz` of the requested +100 kHz offset and was `67.34 dB` over median.
That is a strong gross-path pass.

The strict exact-bin gate still failed because the FFT bin at `100019.53 Hz`
was only `3.32 dB` over median. Given the observed tone peak is consistently
near `+97.1 kHz`, the exact-bin metric is too strict for this path unless it is
offset-corrected.

CP331 narrows the current problem:

- the [host-redacted] bladeRF TX1 to [host-redacted] M2SDR RX gross path can be strong
- a simple tone recovers with TX gain margin
- CP327's CP45 replay failure is not explained by a dead antenna/path alone
- the next replay-level check should account for the observed few-kHz RF offset
  and/or use a margin sweep rather than returning to live UE PRACH
