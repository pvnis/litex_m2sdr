## Interpretation

CP339 is a valid live attempt, not a control-plane failure. The NUC-owned
orchestration worked, the gNB strict gate passed with zero M2SDR TX timeouts, the
UE found PCI `1`, SFN sync completed, and five positive-offset PRACH bursts were
transmitted with good UE-side waveform peaks.

Raising [host-redacted] RX gain to `50` did not make the online gNB detector accept Msg1.
The detector trace stayed below threshold, with max `best_peak` only about
`0.150` against the `0.610` threshold. The targeted UL-symbol capture also
completed and saved all configured target rows, so the next useful work is an
offline timing/waveform search over CP339 artifacts after cleanup, not another
blind live retry.
