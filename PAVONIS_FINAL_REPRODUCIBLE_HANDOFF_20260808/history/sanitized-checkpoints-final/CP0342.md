## Interpretation

CP342 did not pass the strict sparse replay gate, but it changes the failure
shape: the CP339 dump waveform is not intrinsically invisible. Four controlled
replay bursts correlated strongly, all at about score `0.92`, and those windows
also produced nominal rb8 OCUDU hits.

The three weak selected windows are still a problem. The TX runner reported
`47` bursts, `OK_CHUNKS=8000`, and no RX-load errors, so the partial result is
not explained by the obvious runner counters. Compared with CP333, which replayed
the CP45 waveform at the same RX gain and produced `7/7` strong windows, this
points to a CP339-dump replay repeatability or waveform/harness interaction
rather than a fully dead gross RF path.

The next RF step should not be a blind live UE retry. First clean the stale
CP342 wrappers, then repeat or broaden the controlled CP339-dump replay rung
under the self-contained launcher to separate one-off repeatability from a
stable CP339 waveform behavior.
