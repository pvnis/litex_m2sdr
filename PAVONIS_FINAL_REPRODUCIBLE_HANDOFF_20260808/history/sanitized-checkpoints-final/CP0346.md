## Interpretation

CP346 proves the known-good controlled CP45 replay path is still clean at [host-redacted]
RX gain `50` after the CP339 dump partials. The sparse gate passed with `7/7`
selected windows above score `0.50`, and the minimum selected score was about
`0.905`.

This sharply separates the CP339 dump000001 behavior from a general RF/replay
path collapse. CP342 and CP344 showed CP339 dump000001 is RF-visible, but it
remained partial (`4/7` then `5/7` strong windows). CP346 shows the same
self-contained harness can still produce a clean replay with the CP45 known-good
waveform. The next useful controlled step is to replay other CP339 dumps, or to
analyze CP339 waveform/frequency/RB alignment directly, before spending another
live UE attempt.
