## Interpretation

CP348 shows dump000002 is substantially healthier than dump000001. It does not
pass the strict CP45-style margin gate because one selected window scored
`0.684`, but all seven selected windows were above `0.50` and all seven had both
nominal rb8 and rb12 sweep hits.

This means CP339 live UE dumps are not uniformly bad. Dump000001 was partial
across two controlled replays, while dump000002 is consistently visible with one
weaker margin window. The next useful step is to replay the remaining CP339
dumps, or inspect the per-dump waveform differences, before returning to live UE
PRACH.
