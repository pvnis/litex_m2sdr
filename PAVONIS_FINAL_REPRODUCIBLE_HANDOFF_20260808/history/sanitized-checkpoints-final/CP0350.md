## Interpretation

CP350 is the first CP339-dump controlled replay that passes the same strict
sparse gate used for CP45. This proves the CP339 UE waveform family can replay
cleanly through the controlled RF path at RX gain `50`.

The per-dump pattern is now:

- dump000001: partial across two runs (`4/7`, then `5/7` strong)
- dump000002: all visible but one-window margin fail (`7/7 >= 0.50`, min
  `0.684`)
- dump000003: strict pass (`7/7 >= 0.50`, min `0.917`)

That points away from a generic RF-path or CP339-format failure and toward
per-dump timing/content/replay stability differences. Finish replaying dumps
000004 and 000005, then compare the per-dump waveforms and timing metadata
before returning to live UE PRACH.
