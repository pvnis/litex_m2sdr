## Interpretation

CP352 is a cleanly executed controlled replay but a strict gate fail. Six of
seven selected windows were strong (`>= 0.899`), while selected window `154`
collapsed to score `0.1056913453` and had no OCUDU hit. This is closer to the
partial behavior seen on dump000001 than to the margin-only miss on dump000002
or the strict pass on dump000003.

The per-dump CP339 pattern is now:

- dump000001: partial across two runs (`4/7`, then `5/7` strong)
- dump000002: all visible but one-window margin fail (`7/7 >= 0.50`, min
  `0.684`)
- dump000003: strict pass (`7/7 >= 0.50`, min `0.917`)
- dump000004: partial (`6/7 >= 0.50`, one weak/no-hit window at idx `154`)

Because CP346's CP45 known-good replay passed cleanly shortly before these
CP339 dump checks, CP352 continues to point at per-dump CP339 waveform,
alignment, detector, or replay interaction differences rather than a general
controlled RF-path outage.
