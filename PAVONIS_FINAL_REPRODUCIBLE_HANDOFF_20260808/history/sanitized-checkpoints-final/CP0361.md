## Interpretation

CP361 is a valid live attempt, not a launch or strict-gate failure. The NUC-side
orchestration completed, strict gate passed with zero TX timeouts, the UE found
PCI `1`, completed SFN sync, and transmitted five positive-offset PRACH bursts
with good UE-side waveform peaks.

The gNB still did not accept Msg1. The detector trace stayed below threshold:
max `best_peak` was only about `0.178` against a `0.610` threshold, and
`accepted=1` never occurred. The nearest gNB PRACH rows for the five UE dumps are
present but low-energy. The forced target-slot capture also saved the configured
target rows, but those target rows have lower max energy than CP339 and do not
show an obvious received copy online.

This confirms the CP339 live failure was not a one-off strict-gate or stale-state
artifact. The path still needs cleanup, then an offline CP361 waveform search.
If CP361 also lacks a convincing copy in the gNB raw artifacts, the next useful
controlled rung is replaying a fresh CP361 UE dump through the controlled
RX-gain-50 path to separate live-runtime RF/timing behavior from waveform
content.
