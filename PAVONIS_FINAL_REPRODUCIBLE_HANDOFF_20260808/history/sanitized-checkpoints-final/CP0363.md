## Interpretation

CP363 confirms CP361's online no-detect result offline. The UE produced five
valid positive-offset PRACH waveforms, the gNB saved the relevant PRACH
predemod windows, and the first three corresponding target UL slots are present
in the reconstructed target-slot corpus. Neither corpus contains a convincing
copy of the UE waveform.

Compared with CP341/CP339, CP361 has lower receive-corpus energy near the live
target and a target raw cap that stops before the fourth and fifth nearest
target slots. That weakens the target-slot corpus, but the PRACH predemod search
still covers all five attempts and also finds no copy.

Since CP350 and CP354 proved CP339-family dumps can pass controlled replay at
RX gain `50`, the next useful RF rung is a controlled replay of a fresh CP361
dump, starting with `dump000001`. If the fresh CP361 dump is visible in
controlled replay, the fault remains specific to live UE runtime timing/RF
coupling into the gNB receiver. If it is not visible, then the CP361 dump
content or replay assumptions need direct investigation.
