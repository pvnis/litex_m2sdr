## Interpretation

CP364 is a strong controlled replay pass. The fresh CP361 dump000001 waveform
is not intrinsically invisible to the RF path: the controlled replay harness
found seven periodic receive windows, all with sparse-correlation scores above
`0.924`, and OCUDU saw nominal rb8 structure in every selected window.

This separates the CP361 waveform content from the live no-detect failure. The
live CP361 run still showed valid UE PRACH TX dumps and no gNB Msg1 detection,
while CP363 could not find those same waveforms in the saved live gNB receive
artifacts. CP364 shows the dump content can be transmitted and received
strongly when replayed by the controlled harness. The remaining fault is
therefore specific to live UE runtime timing, coupling into the gNB receive
artifacts, gNB detector/raw-save timing, or transient device/RF state during the
live path.

Because the final process snapshots still showed CP325-tagged helpers, the next
checkpoint should be a no-RF process probe/cleanup before any further RF rung.
