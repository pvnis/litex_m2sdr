fixed for all 47 replay bursts.

## Interpretation

CP368 is a cleanly executed active-gNB replay, but it is not a valid
in-PRACH-window acceptance test. The waveform was transmitted correctly, the gNB
was running, and the strict gate passed, but the replay bursts were phase-locked
about `44 ms` after the saved PRACH windows. Empty gNB detections are therefore
expected and do not contradict CP364's strong passive replay pass.

The useful next RF rung is a self-contained phase-corrected active-gNB replay.
Based on CP368, shifting the first replay burst later by roughly `116 ms` should
place the CP361 dump near the next gNB PRACH receive window, while keeping the
same `160 ms` replay period.
