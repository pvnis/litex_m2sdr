## Interpretation

CP370 does not prove that the active gNB rejects an in-window copy of the CP361
waveform. It proves that a `+116 ms` correction was not stable enough to land in
the PRACH receive window on this run. The remaining offset is about `21 ms`, so
the immediate single-phase follow-up would use `FIRST_BURST_MS` around `595 ms`
with the same `160 ms` period.

A more robust follow-up would be a bounded self-contained phase sweep in one
NUC-owned run, because run-to-run startup jitter can move the replay phase by
tens of milliseconds.
