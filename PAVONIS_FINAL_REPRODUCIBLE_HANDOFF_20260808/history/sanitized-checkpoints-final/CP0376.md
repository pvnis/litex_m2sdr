# Checkpoint 376: CP374 near-window direct correlation

## Purpose

Perform a no-RF local check after CP374. CP374 swept replay bursts through the
active gNB PRACH row phase but still produced zero detections and low raw
energy. CP376 asks whether the CP361 replay waveform is present in the CP374
near-window raw files at any meaningful normalized-correlation score.

## Interpretation

CP376 makes the CP374 result sharper: the active-gNB PRACH raw rows do not
contain a convincing copy of the CP361 replay waveform, even when the phase
sweep put high replay bursts near the saved PRACH rows. The same direct
correlator sees the CP364 controlled-pass windows at `0.924..0.930`, while the
best CP374 row is only `0.0559` and the near-window rows top out at `0.0278`.

The next work should stop treating replay phase as the primary blocker. The
stronger hypotheses are now active-gNB receive path/capture-point behavior,
frequency or RB alignment under active gNB runtime, detector-input differences
between controlled capture and gNB predemod dumps, or runtime RF/device state.
