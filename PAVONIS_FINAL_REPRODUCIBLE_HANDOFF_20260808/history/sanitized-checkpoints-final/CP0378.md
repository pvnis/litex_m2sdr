## Interpretation

CP378 directly tested the CP377 UL-center mismatch candidate and did not improve
the active-gNB result. The run was clean, replay TX was valid, strict gate
passed, and the phase sweep crossed within `0.095 ms` of a saved PRACH raw row.
Despite that, the active gNB accepted zero candidates and the direct correlation
remained noise-like compared with the CP364 controlled-pass calibration.

This makes simple replay phase and the `+5998 Hz` center-frequency mismatch
unlikely as the primary explanation. After cleanup, the next useful rung should
move below replay timing/frequency and capture or instrument closer to the active
gNB RF input or detector input.
