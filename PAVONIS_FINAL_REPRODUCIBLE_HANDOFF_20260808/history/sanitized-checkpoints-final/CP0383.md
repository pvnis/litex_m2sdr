## Result

CP383 launched the active-gNB CP361 replay path with the new [host-redacted] Soapy RX tap
enabled, but it never reached strict gate or replay `GO`.

- Stamp: `[date-redacted]`
- gNB start entered `start_gnb` and did not return before the watchdog.
- Watchdog wrote `CANCEL` at `[date-redacted]`.

- No `/tmp/pavonis_soapy_rx_manifest.tsv`, PRACH predemod manifest, or
  UL-symbol manifest was created.

```text

```

Then gNB failed N2:


## Interpretation

creation, and before replay TX. The Soapy RX tap was not exercised.
