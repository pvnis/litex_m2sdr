## Interpretation

CP394 confirms that the Soapy threshold plumbing works and that the active-gNB
replay still executes cleanly. It also shows that the stronger Soapy peaks are
not in PRACH windows. The PRACH windows themselves are quiet at the Soapy
boundary, with max overlap peak below `0.001`.

The next useful rung is a controlled receive-path or known-good replay baseline,
not another active-gNB phase/frequency retry. If a controlled baseline is still
strong, focus on why the CP361 replay is not producing in-window PRACH energy at
the active gNB boundary. If the controlled baseline is weak, pivot to the
physical/RF receive path, device state, or thermal/state behavior.
