## Interpretation

The no-TX all-capture RMS at gain `50`, BW `3 MHz` is not catastrophically high
and is lower than CP396's controlled replay capture RMS. However, the no-TX
best-10ms RMS (`1.2707e-02`) is close to CP396's controlled replay capture
best-10ms RMS (`1.3390e-02`). That means CP396's controlled baseline degradation
is compatible with bursty receive-floor/interference/path state, not just a TX
waveform problem.

This does not prove antenna overheating. It keeps the physical/RF path,
receiver/device state, or intermittent interference branch alive. A read-only
RF health/thermal snapshot is the next low-risk checkpoint before more TX.
