## Interpretation

CP401 supports time/idle-sensitive RF-chain/path behavior. The five-minute idle
did not prove passive antenna overheating, but it did reduce the no-TX receive
floor enough to make another controlled baseline meaningful.

The RFIC temperature data from CP399 still argue against a simple measured SDR
overtemperature explanation. A better current framing is intermittent RF path,
receiver state, local/passband interference, or active chain behavior that
improves after idle.
