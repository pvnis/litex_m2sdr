## Interpretation

CP402 restores the known-good controlled CP45 baseline after CP401's idle/floor
improvement:

- CP396 at RX gain `50`, BW `3 MHz`: `4/7` strong selected windows, degraded.
- CP401 no-TX floor improved after idle.
- CP402 at RX gain `30`, BW `3 MHz`: `7/7` selected windows, minimum score
  `0.896`, sparse gate pass.

This confirms the controlled RF replay path is not dead. It is state-dependent:
when the no-TX floor is degraded, CP45 can drop to partial; after idle and a
recovered receive setting, CP45 is clean again.

The active-gNB CP361 issue should not be debugged while the controlled baseline
is degraded. Any future live/active-gNB replay attempt should be preceded by a
quick no-TX or CP45 control check, or at least run soon after a recovered-state
checkpoint.
