## Interpretation

The current no-TX floor is clean again and is close to the historical clean
floor from CP155. It is much lower than the degraded CP400/CP401 floor and much
lower than CP398's post-CP396 gain-50 floor.

This supports moving one rung up to a current controlled CP45 replay at
RX gain `30`/BW `3 MHz` before spending another active-gNB replay attempt.
