## Interpretation

CP417 confirms the active-gNB startup timeout state is intermittent or
sequence/state-sensitive: after CP411 failed strict gate with `7254` M2SDR TX
timeouts, CP417 passed strict gate with zero timeouts and sens replay ran.

It also confirms that this active replay did not produce a detector-accepted
PRACH at the gNB. The gNB-side PRACH detector stayed far below threshold
(`0.191` best ratio versus `0.6100000143` threshold), preamble 0 did not become
dominant, and the raw levels were only modestly above the CP415 no-replay
isolate. The next useful rung should be analysis of waveform visibility,
timing/frequency alignment, detector assumptions, or active-gNB receive
conditions, not another blind replay with identical settings.
