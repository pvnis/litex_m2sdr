## Interpretation

The direct correlator is calibrated: it reproduces the CP364 controlled pass
with all seven controlled windows scoring above `0.92`.

The same correlator finds no convincing CP361 waveform copy in CP417's active
gNB PRACH predemod rows. The best CP417 score is only `0.0433`, with zero rows
above `0.25` or `0.50`.

CP417 is also not a strong in-window timing proof: the wall-clock phase estimate
puts the closest replay high-peak record `36.5 ms` from a PRACH row. That does
not change the all-row correlation result, but it means CP417 should be treated
as a clean active replay plus low-level Soapy capture, not as a near-window
acceptance test.

Compared with CP394, CP417 captured more weak Soapy snapshots, including eight
snapshots overlapping PRACH windows. Those overlapping samples are still tiny
relative to controlled replay and do not contain a detectable CP361 copy at the
PRACH predemod point.
