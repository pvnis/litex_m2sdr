## Interpretation

CP420 proves that the active UL center is not equivalent to the earlier CP364
controlled pass at `1747505998 Hz`. The exact-center controlled capture has
strong raw RF energy, and four selected windows correlate strongly with the
CP361 template, but the strict sparse gate fails because three selected windows
are essentially non-matches.

This partially reopens the frequency/channelization hypothesis. It does not by
itself explain CP417's much weaker active-gNB Soapy/PRACH rows, but it means the
`+5998 Hz` controlled-pass condition should not be treated as identical to the
active-gNB nominal-UL-center condition.

The next useful controlled rung is to keep exact UL center and match the active
gNB receive bandwidth/channelization more closely before spending another active
gNB replay attempt.
