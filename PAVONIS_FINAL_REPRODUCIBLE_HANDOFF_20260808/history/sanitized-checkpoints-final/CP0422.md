## Interpretation

CP422 resolves the controlled exact-center branch. CP420 was partial at
`3 MHz`, but exact active UL center plus `10 MHz` passive receiver bandwidth is
a clean controlled pass with `7/7` selected windows above `0.93`.

The active nominal UL center is therefore not inherently bad. The failure in
CP417 is not explained by the `1747500000 Hz` center alone, and CP420's partial
result was a receiver bandwidth/channelization artifact.

The remaining gap is active-gNB runtime reception: CP417's active Soapy/PRACH
rows were much weaker and contained no direct CP361 copy, while the passive
controlled BW10 path sees the same waveform strongly at the same center.
