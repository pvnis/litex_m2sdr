## Interpretation

The missing CP431 Soapy manifest was not caused by CP426 removing the CP382 tap
source patch, and it was not caused by the rebuilt [host-redacted] `gnb` binary lacking the
tap strings.

The remaining possibilities are runtime-specific:

- Soapy tap env did not reach the actual gNB process, despite being printed by
  the start helper.
- The arm-gated Soapy tap did not observe the arm file in this run.
- The active run did not execute the instrumented Soapy receive path after arm,
  even though PRACH/UL-symbol hooks produced artifacts.

The next diagnostic should be no-sens: start gNB with RX-only BW10 and Soapy tap
always-on or with explicit tap env visibility, then verify whether the manifest
appears before spending another replay.
