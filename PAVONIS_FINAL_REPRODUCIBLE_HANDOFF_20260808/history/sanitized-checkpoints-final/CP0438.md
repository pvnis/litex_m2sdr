## Interpretation

CP438 finds no strong CP361 copy at the active gNB PRACH predemod point. The
control condition remains strong at about `0.94` correlation, while CP437 PRACH
predemod reaches only about `0.041`, and the nearest TX-to-PRACH rows reach only
about `0.027`. That continues to rule out a detector-threshold-only explanation.

The Soapy result is an instrumentation coverage finding, not yet an active-window
absence finding. The Soapy raw fragments are weak, but all Soapy rows are far
from the PRACH predemod windows: overlap rows are `0`, and the nearest saved
Soapy raw chunk is more than `262M` samples away from a PRACH window. The
always-on Soapy summary budget was exhausted during gNB startup before the
replay/PRACH interval.

The next useful ladder step is a targeted active replay with
`PAVONIS_SOAPY_RX_DUMP_ARM_FILE` set to the same CP366 PRACH arm file. That will
make the Soapy manifest start at GO and determine whether the replay is visible
at the Soapy RX stream during the actual PRACH window.
