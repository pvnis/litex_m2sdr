## Interpretation

CP440 successfully fixed the Soapy coverage gap. The Soapy manifest is
arm-gated on the CP366 PRACH arm file, spans the replay interval, and contains
raw Soapy chunks overlapping PRACH windows.

The replay still produced zero accepted PRACH rows. CP442 performs the direct
correlation pass; it shows CP440's TX-to-PRACH phase landed about `18.47 ms`
from the nearest PRACH row, so the PRACH non-accept result is expected for this
run. The important CP440 evidence is the Soapy near-TX capture, not PRACH
acceptance.
