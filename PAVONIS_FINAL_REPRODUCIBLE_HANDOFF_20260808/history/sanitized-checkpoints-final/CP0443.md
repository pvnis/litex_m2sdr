## Interpretation

CP443 passes the controlled passive BW10 bracket immediately after CP440/CP442.
The physical/passive path still sees CP361 strongly at the exact active UL
center with BW10 and RX gain 50.

That substantially weakens the antenna-overheating or gross physical-path
failure theory for the CP440 active-gNB result. The remaining gap is specific to
active gNB/M2SDR runtime receive behavior: active Soapy near-TX does not show a
strong copy, while passive BW10 controlled replay still scores about `0.94`.
