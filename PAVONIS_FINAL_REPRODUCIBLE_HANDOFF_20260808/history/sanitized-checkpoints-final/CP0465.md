## Interpretation

CP465 proves that lower-impact bounded M2SDR TX probes are feasible under the
same RX/TX BW10 and `tx_gain_backoff: 20` startup condition. The CP463 timeout
state was caused by the high-volume OCUDU wrapper/RX/TX trace set, not by the
bounded M2SDR TX probes alone.

The next safe active replay rung is CP361 replay with the CP465 probe set and
the strict GO/CANCEL gate preserved.
