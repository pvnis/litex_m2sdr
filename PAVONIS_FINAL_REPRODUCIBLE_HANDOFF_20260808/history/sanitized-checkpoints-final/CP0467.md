## Interpretation

CP467 confirms that the lower-impact TX probe set can run through a full active
replay without causing TX writeStream timeouts. The replay path was clean enough
to spend TX and collect bounded TX input/DMA evidence.

It still does not solve the active-gNB visibility gap. Compared with CP458, the
detector ratios improved slightly and the nearest timing improved from about
`23.8 ms` to `13.35 ms`, but the gNB still accepted no PRACH and the relevant
active Soapy/PRACH windows stayed far below the controlled BW10 reference.

The next local checkpoint should inspect the TX probe metadata and timing
relationship before spending more RF. The immediate questions are whether the
M2SDR TX timing offset/release behavior and the [host-redacted] burst schedule explain
the persistent `10-30 ms` active timing miss, or whether the remaining issue is
still active full-duplex RX suppression rather than replay timing.
