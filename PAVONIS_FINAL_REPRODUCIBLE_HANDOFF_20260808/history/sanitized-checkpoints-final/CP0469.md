## Interpretation

CP469 gives a concrete next RF checkpoint: rerun the lower-impact active replay
with strict GO/CANCEL, RX/TX BW10, RX gain `50`, TX backoff `20`,
`FIRST_BURST_MS=618` or `619`, and `PERIOD_MS=160`.

The safer first choice is `FIRST_BURST_MS=618`, because the CP467 wall-clock
estimate says it would move the first burst from `-13.35 ms` to about
`-0.35 ms`; `619` would move it to about `+0.65 ms`. Since the callback timing
is approximate, this should be treated as a bracket, not an exact phase lock.

The TX-probe data also says not to overinterpret the CP467 TX dump as replay
time evidence. If the next timing-aligned attempt still fails, the likely branch
remains active full-duplex RX suppression or replay visibility in the live gNB
runtime, not startup TX formatting.
