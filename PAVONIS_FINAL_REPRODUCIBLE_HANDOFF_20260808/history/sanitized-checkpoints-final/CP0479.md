## Interpretation

CP479 is the strongest negative result so far for the "we simply missed the
replay-time gNB TX evidence" branch. The replay-time TX probe armed correctly,
captured nonzero gNB TX samples after the strict gate, and showed normal
backoff20-scale TX amplitude. The replay also landed almost exactly on a PRACH
candidate (`+0.107558 ms` nearest signed delta).

Despite that, the gNB accepted zero PRACH rows, active PRACH correlation stayed
near zero (`0.0365` max), and all relevant active Soapy RX subsets near PRACH or
near TX stayed below `0.25`. Meanwhile the CP422 passive controlled BW10
reference remains `7/7 >=0.50`.

This weakens both the timing-only branch and the gross gNB TX-missing branch.
The remaining failure is more likely active RX path/state, runtime full-duplex
interaction, receiver scheduling, or the path from active Soapy RX samples into
PRACH predemod/detector under a running gNB.

Next rung should be local/no-RF CP479 artifact analysis of active RX-side timing
and window selection, or bounded active RX instrumentation, before spending more
RF on timing/backoff changes.
