## Interpretation

CP481 weakens a pure detector-threshold explanation. The detector is not seeing
a near-threshold CP361-like candidate that could be rescued by lowering
threshold; the best near-TX detector ratio is only `0.140`, and the strongest
power window has direct correlation below `0.006`.

The best-aligned replay event at idx `46` does not show CP361 energy in tight
Soapy, UL-symbol, PRACH predemod, or detector views. A later power transient is
visible in PRACH/UL-symbol views, but it does not resemble CP361.

This keeps the ladder pointed at active RX/runtime interaction, RX timing/window
mapping, or replay/RX coexistence under active gNB. The next useful rung should
be source-level inspection or bounded instrumentation of the active RX window
generation and timestamp mapping, not another blind timing/backoff RF run.
