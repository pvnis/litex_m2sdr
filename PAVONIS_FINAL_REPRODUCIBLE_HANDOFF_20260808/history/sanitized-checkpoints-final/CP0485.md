## Interpretation

CP485 proves the lower-PHY timestamp bridge works and strongly weakens a
lower-PHY timestamp-offset explanation for the active RX miss: the bridge's raw
and adjusted timestamps are identical and monotonic.

However, CP485 is not the final near-aligned bridge proof because the replay
phase missed the PRACH window by at least `43.942092 ms`, and the bridge row cap
ended before the nearest PRACH windows. The next useful RF rung is a phase-
adjusted repeat with a larger bridge cap, for example:

- `FIRST_BURST_MS=574`
- `PAVONIS_LOWER_PHY_TS_BRIDGE_SUMMARY_MAX=600000`

That should put the replay near PRACH idx `76` for this observed phase while
keeping the bridge active across the replay/PRACH neighborhood.
