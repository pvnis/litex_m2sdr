## Interpretation

CP487 removes two major explanations at once:

- It achieved near-zero replay/PRACH timing in the active gNB run.
- It proved the lower-PHY timestamp bridge has zero raw-to-adjusted offset in
  the same near-aligned PRACH windows.

Despite that, the active PRACH windows stayed weak and the detector accepted
nothing. The remaining branch is therefore not a gross TX absence, blind replay
phase miss, or lower-PHY timestamp-domain mismatch. It is more likely in the
active RF receive path/runtime state, duplex/coexistence/desense/path-loss
behavior, or a waveform visibility issue between Soapy RX and the PRACH
detector path.
