## Interpretation

CP489 confirms CP487's active failure is not a timestamp bridge coverage issue:
all `+/-5 ms` near-TX PRACH windows are covered by the bridge, and none are
accepted.

The best-aligned replay event is still noise-floor amplitude in the tight
PRACH and UL-symbol views. The strongest local power bump at idx `61` is real
in PRACH and UL-symbol amplitude, but it correlates extremely poorly with the
CP361 template and is not near detector threshold. Soapy RX can find moderate
CP361-like fragments in broader `near_tx_10ms` or top-power selections, but
those do not translate into a CP361-shaped PRACH detector input.

This reinforces the CP481/CP487 conclusion: the active receiver sees energy,
but the energy arriving at the PRACH detector windows is not shaped like the
known-good controlled CP361 replay. The next useful rung should discriminate
RF/runtime receive-path interaction from waveform/frequency visibility, not
spend another blind timing-phase attempt.
