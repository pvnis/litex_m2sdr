## Interpretation

CP492 is a valid all-or-nothing RF checkpoint, but the contiguous raw dump was
not aimed at the TX interval. It proves the first 1.0625 seconds after arm do
not contain a full CP361-shaped copy, but it does not answer whether the active
receiver sees the waveform during the actual replay bursts.

The next useful checkpoint is a bounded delayed contiguous Soapy RX dump around
manifest index `~100608`, requiring a small source patch or start-helper change
so raw saving does not begin immediately at arm.
