## Interpretation

CP495 directly tested the active gNB Soapy RX samples during real replay bursts.
The known-good CP361 template was not present in the pre-lower-PHY active RX
stream, even with nine replay bursts covered by contiguous raw capture.

This moves the remaining gap below the gNB PHY and detector:

- not a PRACH detector threshold issue;
- not a lower-PHY timestamp-domain issue;
- not a blind replay phase issue;
- not a sparse Soapy raw selection artifact.

The next useful rung is to isolate active M2SDR/radio full-duplex state from the
gNB stack, for example with a standalone Soapy full-duplex recorder on [host-redacted]:
open/configure M2SDR RX+TX similarly to gNB, optionally keep TX active with a
bounded known/zero waveform, then have [host-redacted] replay CP361 and record raw RX.
