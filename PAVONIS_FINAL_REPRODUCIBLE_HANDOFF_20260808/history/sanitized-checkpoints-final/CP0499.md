## Interpretation

CP499 rules out generic LiteXM2SDR full-duplex stream activation as the cause of
CP495. The remaining active-gNB gap is more specific than "TX and RX streams are
open together": likely actual gNB downlink TX samples/RF self-interference,
gNB-specific radio scheduling/timestamp behavior, or another gNB runtime state.
