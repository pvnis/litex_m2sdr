## Interpretation

CP504 is a valid active-gNB negative. The replay overlapped the delayed Soapy
raw capture, the strict gate passed, TX timeouts were absent, and the CP503
zero-after-arm knob activated before the capture window. Yet the full-template
search found no CP361 candidates above `0.25`, with a top score of only
`0.04536`.

Combined with CP499/CP500/CP501, this rules out the following as sufficient
causes of the CP495 active-gNB raw-RX disappearance:

- generic M2SDR full-duplex stream activation
- a modest continuous downlink-frequency tone
- the isolated actual CP495 gNB TX waveform
- active-gNB TX payload content after the replay arm

The remaining branch is active-gNB runtime RX behavior: RX stream drain,
timestamp/time-base handling, lower-PHY interaction, scheduling side effects, or
another active runtime state that the standalone full-duplex recorder does not
create.
