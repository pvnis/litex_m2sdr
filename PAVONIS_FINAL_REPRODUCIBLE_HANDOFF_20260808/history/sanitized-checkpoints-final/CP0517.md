The next RF rung should keep the CP515 active-readStream setup and delay the
scheduled TX stream by about `0.701 ms`:

- keep `FIRST_BURST_MS=585`
- set `TIMED_LEAD_MS=0.701`
- keep `PERIOD_MS=159`
- keep `OCUDU_SOAPY_RX_USE_READSTREAM=1`
- keep `M2SDR_SOAPY_RX_IGNORE_TIMED_ACTIVATION=0`
- keep `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_ZERO_AFTER_ARM=0`
- preserve strict [host-redacted] GO/CANCEL gate before [host-redacted] TX

Reason: `FIRST_BURST_MS` is rounded to whole 1 ms chunks in the replay program,
but `TIMED_LEAD_MS` shifts the scheduled stream start as a floating-point value.
That is the available sub-millisecond control without rebuilding the replay
program.
