## Interpretation

CP537 is a valid negative result. The `-0.5 ms` timed lead did place strong
active raw energy into a PRACH processing neighborhood, and one detector row
showed a large RSSI jump, but normalized PRACH correlation moved away from the
CP533 near-hit:

- CP533: best detector ratio `0.517`, RSSI `-54.40 dB`
- CP537: best detector ratio `0.400`, RSSI `-46.92 dB`

So the next rung should not push farther negative. The better follow-up is to
bracket between CP533 (`0 ms`) and CP537 (`-0.5 ms`), starting with
`TIMED_LEAD_MS=-0.25` while keeping the same strict gate and sparse full-run raw
capture. If that also regresses, switch from timing advance to a TX amplitude or
detector-margin rung.
