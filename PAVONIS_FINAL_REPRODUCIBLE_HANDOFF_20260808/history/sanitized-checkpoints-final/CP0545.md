## Interpretation

CP545 is a valid backoff10 amplitude result, and it regressed. It improved
neither detector acceptance nor normalized detector margin:

- CP533, backoff20: ratio `0.517`
- CP543, misconfigured backoff20 repeat: ratio `0.353`
- CP545, corrected backoff10: ratio `0.312`

The active raw copy is strong, including in the 2 ms PRACH neighborhood, so this
does not look like a simple "more TX amplitude fixes the detector" case. The
next rung should stop amplitude changes and move to a no-RF detector-threshold
diagnostic build, as planned in CP542, to prove whether downstream RA behavior
works once a CP533-like near-hit is allowed through the detector.
