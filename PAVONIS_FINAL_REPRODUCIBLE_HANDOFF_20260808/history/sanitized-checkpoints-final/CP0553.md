## Interpretation

CP553 did not reproduce the CP551 strong accepted event under stock threshold.
The best detector peak was `0.2954544127`, well below the stock threshold
`0.6100000143`, even though the sparse Soapy raw still saw a strong CP361-like
match in the active run.

This makes CP551 look like an intermittent strong-alignment/visibility event,
not a stable stock-threshold fix. Blindly lowering the detector threshold
further is still the wrong next move. The next useful branch is to make the
strong PRACH event reproducible or move to a live UE path with a bounded
diagnostic threshold, because fixed replay cannot validate Msg3.
