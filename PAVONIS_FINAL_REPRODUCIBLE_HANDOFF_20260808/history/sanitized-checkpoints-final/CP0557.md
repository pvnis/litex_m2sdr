## Interpretation

CP557 is a valid live UE PRACH attempt. Shortening the PRACH sync guard from
`200` to `80` recovered live Msg1 and produced six healthy UE-side PRACH
transmissions with the positive offset applied.

The gNB still did not detect any PRACH, even with detector threshold `0.24`.
The best preamble-0 detector peak was only `0.1241550595`, and the best overall
candidate was only `0.1356730461`, both well below `0.24`.

This moves the failure boundary back to live UE RF visibility/timing at the gNB
receive path. Further detector-threshold lowering would be blind at this point:
the live PRACH energy/correlation reaching the gNB detector is still much lower
than the CP551 fixed-replay accepted event.

Next useful branch: analyze CP557 UE dumps against the saved [host-redacted] PRACH/UL
captures, or run a controlled replay of one CP557 UE dump through the known
replay path to separate live `srsue` runtime RF behavior from the waveform
itself.
