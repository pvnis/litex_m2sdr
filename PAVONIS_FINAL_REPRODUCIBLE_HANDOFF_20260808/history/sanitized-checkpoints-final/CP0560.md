## Interpretation

CP560 is a clean controlled replay pass. The first CP557 live UE PRACH dump is
not malformed or intrinsically invisible. When replayed through the known
controlled path at [host-redacted] RX gain `50`, it produces seven strong sparse
correlation windows, all above `0.90`, and nominal `rb8/p0` OCUDU hits.

This sharply separates CP557's live no-detect from the waveform itself. The
remaining failure boundary is live UE runtime RF visibility/timing into the
active gNB receive path, or a live active-runtime capture/arming issue. CP559's
PRACH predemod raw cap missed the exact UE-timed rows, so the next live rung
should repeat the CP557-style live UE attempt with `PAVONIS_PRACH_PREDEMOD_RAW_MAX`
raised high enough to save rows around `828..833`.
