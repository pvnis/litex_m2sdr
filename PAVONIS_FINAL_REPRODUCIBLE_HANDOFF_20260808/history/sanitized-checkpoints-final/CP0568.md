## Interpretation

CP568 is valid orchestration and confirms that the delayed-arm mechanism works,
but it still did not reach Msg1. Delaying PRACH/UL capture arming until
`45` seconds after `GO` did not restore UE SFN sync, so the CP564/CP566
pre-Msg1 failures are not explained by immediate raw-capture load alone.

The failure boundary remains UE DL/SFN synchronization: the UE can detect PCI 1
quickly, but repeatedly fails SFN sync before SIB/RACH. Do not change PRACH
detector threshold or positive-offset timing until a run reaches Msg1 again.
