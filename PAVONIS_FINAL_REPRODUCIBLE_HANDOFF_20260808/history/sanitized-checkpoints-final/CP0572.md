## Controlled-Replay Contrast

CP560 replayed CP557 dump `000001` through the controlled path at the same [host-redacted]
RX gain `50` and produced selected scores `0.9076608360..0.9196393677`, with
capture-window RMS around `2.3e-02`.

CP572's exact live PRACH windows are around `0.02..0.03` score and raw RMS
around `1.5e-04`. That is not a detector-threshold edge case; it is a large
RF-visibility or active-runtime timing gap relative to a known-good controlled
replay of the same kind of waveform.

## Interpretation

CP572 rules out the earlier CP559 failure mode where the raw cap missed the
exact UE-timed windows. In CP570, all five expected PRACH windows were saved,
but those windows remained low-energy and did not correlate with the UE-side TX
dumps.

The remaining failure boundary is therefore live UE runtime PRACH emission into
the active gNB PRACH window, or a live active-gNB timing/reference mismatch that
places the real RF energy outside the expected PRACH predemod windows. It is
not explained by missing raw512 evidence, and it is not explained by a malformed
UE waveform, because CP560 showed the waveform class is strongly replay-visible.

The next rung should avoid another blind live UE retry. Prefer a controlled RF
replay of a CP570 dump from the current state, then, if that passes, instrument
or test the live UE transmit path/timestamp alignment rather than raising the
PRACH raw cap again.
