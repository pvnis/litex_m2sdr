## Interpretation

CP583 is valid evidence above CP577. The active gNB started cleanly, the replay
ran, and the live-style burst-only timed SOB/EOB transmit path was exercised.
Despite that, the gNB accepted no PRACH and the saved PRACH predemod windows do
not contain a CP570-like copy.

The important new boundary is that sparse lower Soapy RX can see a strong CP570
fragment, but the strong fragment is about `11.85 ms` before the corresponding
PRACH predemod window. That points away from detector threshold and away from a
gross RF outage. The likely boundary is active-gNB receive timing/window
extraction, lower-PHY timestamp bridging, or the active replay/gNB timebase
relationship.

Next useful rung: do not blind-retry the same phase. Run a targeted active
replay with contiguous/raw-period-1 Soapy capture around the observed offset, or
shift the replay timing by about `+11.85 ms` relative to the active PRACH window
and verify whether the strong Soapy copy moves into PRACH predemod windows.
