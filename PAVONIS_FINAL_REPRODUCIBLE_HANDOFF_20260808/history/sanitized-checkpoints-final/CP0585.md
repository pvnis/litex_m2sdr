## Interpretation

CP585 is a valid no-accept, but it is not the targeted negative implied by the
first sparse-Soapy pass. Adding the CP583-derived `+136531` sample offset did
not produce a valid Msg1 detection and did not move a coherent CP570 copy into
the active gNB PRACH predemod dump for the detector max row, but lower Soapy RX
did see a strong CP570-like copy within `+/-2 ms` of that row.

The same run raised the detector max from `0.1420` to `0.2236`, with preamble
`0`, but at delay `129`, beyond the `104.8` delay limit and still below
threshold `0.24`. The strongest lower-Soapy alignment implies the CP570 template
spans roughly `-0.550 ms` to `+0.450 ms` around the PRACH window start, while
the exact PRACH predemod window idx `183` scores only `0.00474` against the same
template. This sharpens the boundary: the RF path and lower Soapy visibility are
not grossly missing at CP585 timing; the mismatch is now between lower Soapy
visibility and what the active gNB PRACH predemod/detector path extracts or
accepts. The next targeted step should either bracket CP585 by small sample
offsets to pull detector delay inside the legal window, or capture a denser
lower-RX/PRACH view around the CP585 near-threshold row.
