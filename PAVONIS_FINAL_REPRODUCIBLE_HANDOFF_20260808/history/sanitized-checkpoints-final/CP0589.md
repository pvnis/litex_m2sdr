## Interpretation

CP589 is a valid threshold-validation negative. The threshold override worked,
but the CP587-quality detector/predemod alignment did not reproduce in this run:
the best detector peak fell to `0.1565`, the best delay failed, and direct PRACH
same-window correlation dropped from CP587's `0.24346` to `0.05033`.

This means CP587 was not yet a stable operating point. Lowering the detector
threshold alone is not sufficient unless the CP587 alignment recurs. The current
boundary is reproducibility of the active PRACH predemod alignment: lower Soapy
continues to see strong CP570-like copies, but the gNB PRACH predemod path only
occasionally aligns them coherently in the saved PRACH window.

The next useful step should focus on repeatability at CP587 offset rather than a
new threshold experiment: either repeat CP587 stock threshold once to measure
stability, or run a small bracket around `1773655` with the same `0.24`
threshold and compare PRACH same-window correlation plus detector delay.
