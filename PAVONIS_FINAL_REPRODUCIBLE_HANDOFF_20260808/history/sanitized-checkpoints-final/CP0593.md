## Interpretation

CP593 is a valid negative for the surgical `-2` sample hypothesis. The delay
condition did pass (`91 <= 104.80`), but the detector amplitude fell from
CP591's threshold-passing `0.2623` to `0.1654`, and the direct PRACH-window
correlation weakened from CP591's `0.05045` top score to `0.04036`.

The small offset bracket is therefore not monotonic or stable at this scale.
The next useful RF step should not keep marching earlier blindly. Better
choices are either a repeat at `1773655` to measure recurrence of CP591's
threshold-pass behavior, or a one-sample midpoint `1773654` if we want to keep
probing the delay/threshold boundary with minimal change.
