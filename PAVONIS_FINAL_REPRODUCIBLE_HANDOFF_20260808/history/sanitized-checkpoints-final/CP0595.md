## Interpretation

CP595 is a valid negative for the one-sample midpoint. It did not combine the
useful parts of CP591 and CP593: delay passed, but the peak was even lower than
CP593 (`0.1278` versus `0.1654`) and there were no threshold-pass rows.

The offset micro-bracket around `1773653..1773655` is not giving a stable,
monotonic route to acceptance. The best evidence remains CP591's threshold-pass
event at `1773655`, but CP593/CP595 show that moving earlier by one or two
samples loses amplitude. The next useful step is a repeat at `1773655` to test
whether CP591's threshold-pass behavior recurs, rather than continuing to move
earlier.
