## Interpretation

CP597 validates the CP587/CP591 timing offset as a real active acceptance point,
not just an offline or detector-only artifact. Returning to `1773655` produced
two accepted preamble-0 rows at stock threshold, and the gNB log emitted two
non-empty `detected_preambles` entries.

The key difference from CP591 is not the configured offset; it is run-to-run
alignment/repeatability. CP591 had a threshold pass at delay `106` just outside
the delay limit. CP597 at the same offset produced stronger peaks and legal
delays. The tiny earlier offsets (`1773654`, `1773653`) were not better. The
next useful step is to preserve `1773655` and measure repeatability, or move
from exploratory RF tests into a controlled reproduction/acceptance recipe.
