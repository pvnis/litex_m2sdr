## Interpretation

CP605 is a partial positive result for the CP604 timed-lead hypothesis:

- CP599 detector-max lower-RX offset: `+8508` samples
- CP601 detector-max lower-RX offset: `+8609` samples
- CP605 detector-max lower-RX offset: `+2276` samples

The `TIMED_LEAD_MS=-0.67` correction moved the strong lower-RX copy much closer
to the PRACH window and recovered a stock-threshold detector pass. The run still
failed because the detector delay was `126`, outside the `104.80` limit.

This suggests the next live step, if taken, should not revert to blind repeats.
It should keep `TIMED_LEAD_MS=-0.67` and make a small targeted delay-gate
correction, for example by reducing `RF_ENV_TX_DBG_PRACH_TIME_OFFSET_SAMPLES`
by roughly `22` samples from `1773655` to about `1773633`.
