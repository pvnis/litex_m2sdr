## Interpretation

CP614 is a valid negative live Stage 6 rung. The UE reached SFN sync, received
the real PRACH config, transmitted six healthy preamble-0 Msg1 attempts, and
the live bladeRF plugin applied the intended net CP611 offset `1765915` with
successful timed writes.

The gNB still did not detect Msg1. There were no threshold-pass rows, no
accepted rows, and no non-empty `detected_preambles`. The best preamble-0 peak
was only `0.1080539674`, lower than CP570's best p0 peak (`0.1467100233`) and
far below threshold `0.24`.

The CP613 one-knob translation did not close Stage 6. Because the live harness
does not have `TIMED_LEAD_MS`, CP614 only tested the closest single-offset
translation; it did not reproduce the full CP611 replay timing semantics or the
active replay 10 MHz Soapy bandwidth recipe.

Next useful rung should not be another blind live offset retry. The next
one-variable experiment should first decide whether to bring the live harness
closer to the CP611 accepted environment by changing the gNB Soapy RX/TX
bandwidth to `10 MHz`, or to add a live-only timing/instrumentation knob that
can emulate the missing `TIMED_LEAD_MS` explicitly.
