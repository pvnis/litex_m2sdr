## Interpretation

CP634 is not a gross RF-loss failure. It is a detector-normalization/shape
failure:

- the best detector event is preamble 0 and delay-valid
- the raw pre-demod power is comparable to or above CP611
- the lower Soapy RX correlation at the focus index is strong
- the detector numerator is high, but the denominator/reference are also high,
  suppressing the normalized metric below `0.24`

At this threshold, CP634 is a near miss rather than an absence. A threshold of
`0.20` would have accepted the best row, but CP630 already showed threshold-only
diagnostics are not stable across repeats. The more useful next step is to
target why the reference/denominator inflates on the high-energy rows, or why
the accepted CP611 signature at delay 104 is not being reproduced.
