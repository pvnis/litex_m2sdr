## Interpretation

CP634 did not fail because the peak was absent. It failed because the peak did
not concentrate enough of the reference-window energy:

- CP634's best row has strong raw pre-demod power and strong lower Soapy RX.
- The selected peak is real and delay-valid.
- The denominator is high because the reference-window residual energy remains
  high after subtracting the selected peak.
- The local metric window around the selected delay is not obviously split
  within `delay-3..delay+3`, so the missing energy may be distributed farther
  across the detection window, across sidelobes, or through waveform/timing
  shape rather than adjacent-bin splitting.

This turns the next question from "is RF present?" into "why is the correlation
energy not concentrated enough in the accepted peak bin?"
