## Instrumentation added

The existing `PAVONIS_PRACHDET_TRACE` line shape was left unchanged. CP637 adds
a second line, emitted only when the existing trace gate is active:

```text
PAVONIS_PRACHDET_TOP3
```

Fields include:

- `best_conc` and `p0_conc`
- global-best top 3 metric delays/values
- global-best top 3 numerator delays/values
- global-best top-3 numerator sum and sum/reference ratio
- preamble-0 top 3 metric delays/values
- preamble-0 top 3 numerator delays/values
- preamble-0 top-3 numerator sum and sum/reference ratio
- `accepted`

This is trace-only instrumentation. The detector threshold, delay gate, and
acceptance behavior were not changed.
