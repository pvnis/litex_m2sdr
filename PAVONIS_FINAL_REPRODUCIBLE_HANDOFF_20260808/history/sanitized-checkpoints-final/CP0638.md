## Interpretation

CP638 is not a gross RF, TX, RX, or detector-input amplitude failure:

- replay TX was healthy
- the strict gNB/M2SDR gate passed
- lower raw RX correlation was strong at the detector focus
- the pre-demod raw row was stronger than CP634 and CP611 accepted-row raw
- no detector row accepted because no single delay bin reached the normalized
  concentration threshold

The important new finding is multi-bin delay energy split. In the strongest
row, the selected bin alone is below threshold, but the two valid top numerator
bins together would clear the concentration requirement. The split bins are
separated by 16 samples (`76`, `92`, with a third at `108` just outside the
delay gate).

This shifts the next investigation from amplitude/threshold-only work to why
the active replay waveform's detector energy is divided across delay bins. The
next no-RF source/code audit should focus on detector delay indexing,
windowing/CP alignment, and any timing/waveform-shape path that could produce a
16-sample-spaced split in active gNB reception.
