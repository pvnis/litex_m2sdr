## Instrumentation added

The existing `PAVONIS_PRACHDET_TRACE` and `PAVONIS_PRACHDET_TOP3` line shapes
were left unchanged. CP640 adds a third trace line, emitted only when the
existing trace gate is active:

```text
PAVONIS_PRACHDET_SPLIT
```

For both global best and preamble 0, it samples the metric and numerator at:

```text
selected_delay - 32
selected_delay - 16
selected_delay
selected_delay + 16
selected_delay + 32
```

It also logs:

- `pm16` numerator sum and sum/reference ratio
- delay-gate-filtered `pm16` sum and ratio
- `pm32` numerator sum and sum/reference ratio
- delay-gate-filtered `pm32` sum and ratio

This is trace-only instrumentation. The detector threshold, delay gate, and
acceptance behavior were not changed.
