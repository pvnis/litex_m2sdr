## Interpretation

CP641 establishes that the active replay path can still produce an accepted
PRACH under the CP611 settings after the CP640 trace patch. It also validates
the split trace on an accepted row: single-bin concentration, top-3
concentration, and `pm16`/`pm32` valid ratios all move consistently.

Compared with CP638, the same settings can land in two modes:

- CP638: no accept; selected bin below threshold, but valid split energy would
  pass if combined.
- CP641: accept; selected bin and split sums both exceed threshold.

The next useful work is not another blind CP611 repeat. Use CP641 as the
positive accepted reference for the split instrumentation, and compare it
against CP638's no-accept split to isolate why the selected delay energy
sometimes lands below the threshold despite strong RF visibility.
