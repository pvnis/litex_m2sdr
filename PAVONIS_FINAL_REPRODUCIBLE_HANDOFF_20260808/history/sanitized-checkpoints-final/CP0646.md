## Interpretation

CP646 is a valid positive reference for the CP645 TOP8 trace. It shows that
the active path can produce a strong p0 accept under CP611 settings, and that
the detector's delay response is not just a clean three-lobe 16-bin comb. The
accepted row has:

- a dominant selected bin at delay `39`;
- visible 16-bin side energy at `23`, `55`, and `7`;
- local adjacent energy at `38`, `37`, and `41`.

Compared with CP638 and CP641, CP646 reinforces that the RF path is visible and
the main variability is where the delay energy lands and how concentrated the
selected bin is. The next useful checkpoint should be no-RF analysis of the
TOP8 trace shape against CP638/CP641, then a targeted detector/windowing source
audit or instrumentation patch if the comparison points to a stable mechanism.
