## Interpretation

CP638, CP641, and CP646 use the same RF recipe and all show good lower-RX
visibility. The main difference is how the PRACH detector response lands inside
the delay search:

- CP638 lands at delay `92` with strong 16-bin split energy but selected-bin
  concentration below threshold.
- CP641 lands at delay `1`, where the selected bin and 16-bin split sum both
  clear threshold.
- CP646 lands at delay `39`, where the selected bin dominates and TOP8 reveals
  both 16-bin side energy and adjacent-bin energy.

The raw RMS spread between these decisive rows is small compared with the
detector metric spread:

```text
raw RMS:       1.3867e-03 -> 1.4163e-03 -> 1.4791e-03
detector peak: 1.6862e-01 -> 3.8173e-01 -> 2.4177e+00
```

That is not the shape of a simple overheating/fade or gross amplitude problem.
It is the shape of PRACH demod/window alignment, frequency-domain extraction,
or detector-domain delay response moving energy between bins.
