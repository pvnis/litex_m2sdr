## Interpretation

CP654 rules out a simple demod-start/timing sweep as a way to get a stronger
valid corrected-config accept from the CP653 capture. The only actual accept is
the original nominal timing.

There is still one plausible corrected timing candidate: `sample_offset=752`.
If positive standalone `sample_offset` maps to sending the RF PRACH earlier,
then the corresponding TX-side hypothesis is:

```text
1773633 - 752 = 1772881 PRACH time-offset samples
```

That mapping is an inference from the standalone replay, not a proven RF result.
The no-RF row is just below threshold, so it should be tested with a no-RF
amplitude-scale sweep before spending RF.
