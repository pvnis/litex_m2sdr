## Interpretation

CP655 strengthens the CP654 timing conclusion:

1. The nominal corrected accept has real amplitude margin, but still sits late
   at delay 99.
2. Retiming to the inferred `offset=752` moves the delay to 79 and can accept,
   but only in a narrow scale band around `+0.26` to `+0.42 dB`.
3. Bigger scale was not monotonically better in the standalone detector, so an
   RF retimed/gain test should be treated as a narrow hypothesis, not a broad
   power-margin fix.

If mapping standalone positive `sample_offset=752` to the TX side is correct,
the corresponding RF timing hypothesis remains:

```text
1773633 - 752 = 1772881 PRACH time-offset samples
```
