## Interpretation

The CP655 retimed/gain RF hypothesis did not hold in live RF.

The retuned CP656 run did move strong RF energy into corrected detector windows, but not into an accepted detector state:

- strongest p0 peak was `0.[date-redacted]`, below the configured peak threshold `0.24`;
- strongest p0 concentration was `0.1718`, below the required `0.1935`;
- no gNB PRACH line contained detected preambles.

This rejects the specific `1772881` / `60.3 dB` RF setting. It does not by itself prove the RF path is degraded, because CP656 changed timing and gain relative to the CP653 accepting baseline.
