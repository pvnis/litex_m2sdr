## Interpretation

CP657 is a negative baseline repeat. Since CP657 reused CP653 nominal RF settings but did not accept, the live path is not in the same state as it was during CP653.

This does not prove antenna overheating by itself, but it makes RF/path/channel stability a plausible next branch. The evidence is consistent with a changed RF receive condition: the strongest rows still show PRACH-like energy, but peak and concentration dropped below the corrected detector threshold and the best delay moved outside the acceptance gate.
