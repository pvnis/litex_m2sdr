## Interpretation

CP660 rules out the simplest "just add a little TX gain" hypothesis for the current path state.

The live RF path still produces occasional strong-energy windows, but the corrected PRACH detector concentration is worse at `61.5 dB` than it was at `60.0 dB` in CP657. This points toward one of:

- the OTA path/channel is varying between runs;
- higher TX gain is worsening signal quality rather than helping;
- the system is sensitive to a timing/state variable that the gain step did not address;
- the CP653 accept was near the edge and not robust.

The next RF rung should not raise gain further. A better next step is a controlled lower/cleaner-gain or timing micro-step around the CP657/CP660 observed p0 delays, while continuing to avoid full local tar extraction.
