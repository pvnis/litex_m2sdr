## Interpretation

CP661 recovers a corrected PRACH accept by reducing TX gain to `59.0 dB` while preserving the baseline timing.

This makes the current best live RF setting:

- `RF_ENV_TX_DBG_PRACH_TIME_OFFSET_SAMPLES=1773633`
- `TX_GAIN_DB=59.0`
- `PAVONIS_PRACHDET_THRESHOLD_OVERRIDE=0.24`
- `TIMED_LEAD_MS=-0.67`
- `PERIOD_MS=159`
- `FIRST_BURST_MS=585`

The result argues against antenna/host overheating as the main cause and against raising TX power further. The path is gain-sensitive and non-monotonic in the range tested: `61.5 dB` was worse, while `59.0 dB` produced a stronger accept than the earlier `60.0 dB` CP653 accept.

The next checkpoint should repeat CP661 settings once to test reproducibility before making finer timing or gain changes.
