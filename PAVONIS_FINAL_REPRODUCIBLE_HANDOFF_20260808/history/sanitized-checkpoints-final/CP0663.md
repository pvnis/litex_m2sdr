First failed stage remains live gNB PRACH detection after valid live UE Msg1 TX.
This does not reach Stage 7.

## Interpretation

The fixed-replay 59 dB gain recovery does not by itself recover the live UE
path. CP663 proves that the live srsUE bladeRF path can set and read back 59 dB
and can emit strong local PRACH debug bursts, but the gNB still sees no accepted
preamble even with direct raw coverage.

Compared with CP661/CP662, the failure is specific to live UE Msg1 delivery or
timing/visibility, not to the general [host-redacted] bladeRF antenna path being unable
to radiate a PRACH-like waveform.

## Next

Do not move to Stage 7 yet. The next checkpoint should isolate the live srsUE
waveform/scheduling path from the RF path by replaying or scoring the CP663
captured live PRACH dumps against the same gNB raw windows, then decide whether
the issue is live waveform content, live timestamping, or live RA scheduling.
