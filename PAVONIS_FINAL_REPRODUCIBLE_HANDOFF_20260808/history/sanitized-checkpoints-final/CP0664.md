best all-window PRACH predemod score was only `0.04502136632800102`, with zero
rows at or above `0.25`.

score above `0.25` was in a broad `tx2_50ms` / nearest-PRACH bucket and was
still far below the CP624 accepted fixed-replay lower-RX score of about `0.94`.

The first failed ladder stage remains Stage 6 live gNB PRACH detection. Do not
move to Stage 7 yet.

## Interpretation

CP664 supports the CP663 conclusion: the live srsUE PRACH waveform is not
arriving at the gNB receiver as a strong, accept-like copy, even though CP663
proved the live UE believed it transmitted and CP661/CP662 proved the fixed
controlled replay path can still be accepted at `TX_GAIN_DB=59.0`.

The most useful next RF split is to replay the exact CP663 live PRACH bytes
through the known-good controlled replay path. If that replay is accepted, the
captured live waveform content is usable and the problem is likely live UE
timestamping, stream state, or real-time scheduling. If that replay is not
accepted, the live-generated waveform bytes differ materially from the accepted
controlled PRACH template or require additional conversion/normalization before
they can be compared.
