## Verdict

The CP663 live template is not a wrong-root, conjugated, reversed, or IQ-swapped
waveform. It is very close to the accepted CP570 template after a small
`+500 Hz` phase-rate correction:

- unrotated identity zero-lag score: `0.8348184362328502`
- identity with `+500 Hz`, zero lag: `0.[subscriber-redacted]`
- best combined circular score: identity, `+500 Hz`, lag `0`
- conjugate/IQ-swap/time-reverse hypotheses were effectively rejected

This makes a single transformed-template RF replay worthwhile before broader
searching. Stage 6 is still the active failed stage; do not move to Stage 7.
