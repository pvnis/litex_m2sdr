## Interpretation

The CP668 hook worked: the actual transmitted high-peak PRACH dumps were
rotated and are structurally very close to the previously accepted templates.
The remaining live failure is therefore not explained by an unrotated or
obviously malformed UE TX waveform.

The first failed ladder stage remains Stage 6 live PRACH detection. Do not move
to Stage 7.

The next conservative RF test is to keep the now-proven `+500 Hz` live TX hook
and change only the PRACH timing offset from CP624/CP663's `1765915` to the
accepted controlled-replay timing `1773633` from CP661/CP667.
