## Interpretation

CP672 re-establishes the CP661 controlled replay baseline. That changes the
CP671 interpretation from "general controlled replay path currently broken" to
"the CP667-style `+500 Hz` live-dump replay failed while the older CP661
baseline replay still accepts."

Current conclusions:

- Stage 6 live UE remains blocked. No live UE Msg1 has been accepted.
- The RF/gNB controlled baseline is not globally dead, because CP672 accepted.
- CP671 is still a real regression relative to CP667, but it appears specific
  to the `+500 Hz` CP663 live-dump replay family or to its run alignment/path
  state, not a complete loss of replay feasibility.
- The +500 Hz live TX hook still cannot be called sufficient, because CP668 and
  CP670 live UE both failed.
- Do not move to Stage 7 until a live UE Msg1 accept is observed.

Recommended next branch:

1. Compare CP667, CP671, and CP672 receiver-side metrics without extraction.
2. Separate waveform-family effects from run-start/frame-phase effects.
3. If the CP667-style `+500 Hz` replay can be made to accept again, return to
   live UE with one variable changed at a time.
