## Interpretation

CP674 failed Stage 6 controlled replay for the CP667-style `+500 Hz` live-dump
waveform, but it failed at the threshold edge rather than as a hard RF miss.

Current state after CP674:

- Live UE Msg1 is still not accepted.
- Stage 7 is still not proven.
- The CP661 baseline controlled replay remains healthier than the CP663
  `+500 Hz` live-dump replay family.
- The `+500 Hz` replay family is not hopeless, but it is marginal and
  alignment-sensitive: CP667 accepted, CP671 failed low, and CP674 nearly
  crossed threshold.

Recommended next checkpoint:

- Do not move to live Stage 7 yet.
- Use one small controlled-replay variable to characterize the threshold edge:
  either lower detector threshold slightly for the CP663 `+500 Hz` replay, or
  run a small timing-offset branch around `1773633` to see whether the late
  delay can be pulled toward the CP667 accepted delay.
