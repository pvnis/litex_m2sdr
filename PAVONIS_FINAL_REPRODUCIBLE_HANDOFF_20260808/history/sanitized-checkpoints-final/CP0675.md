# CHECKPOINT 675 - CP667 +500 Hz replay threshold 0.235: delay-gate no accept

## Scope

`0.24` detector threshold by less than `0.001`.

It repeated the CP674/CP667-style `+500 Hz` controlled replay with the same
waveform, offset, gain, timing, and bandwidth. The only intended change was:

- `PAVONIS_PRACHDET_THRESHOLD_OVERRIDE=0.235`

This was not a live UE test and cannot prove Stage 7. It tested whether the
CP674 miss was only a threshold-margin problem.

## Interpretation

CP675 rejects the idea that CP674 was only blocked by the numeric peak
threshold. Lowering the threshold to `0.235` did not produce an accept, and the
top candidate exceeded even the original `0.24` threshold.

The stronger explanation is now timing/window validity:

- CP667 accepted with delay `2`.
- CP674 nearly accepted at delay `17`.
- CP675 exceeded threshold but landed at delay `117` and still failed.

Current state after CP675:

- Live UE Msg1 remains unaccepted.
- Stage 7 remains unproven.
- CP661 baseline replay remains healthy.
- CP663 `+500 Hz` replay is not just weak; it is landing inconsistently in the
  detector timing/window space.

Recommended next checkpoint:

- Stop lowering threshold for this branch.
- Run a controlled timing-offset branch around the CP663 `+500 Hz` replay, with
  the goal of pulling the top candidate delay back toward the CP667 accepted
  delay region.
