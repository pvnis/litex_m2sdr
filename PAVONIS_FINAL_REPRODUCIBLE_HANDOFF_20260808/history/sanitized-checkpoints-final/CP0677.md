## Interpretation

CP677 confirms that the CP663 live-dump `+500 Hz` waveform family can still
produce a strong controlled Stage 6 accept when the timing offset is adjusted.

This directly supports the CP676 timing/window interpretation:

- CP675 failed despite enough peak because the top candidate landed at delay
  `117`, beyond the `104.80` delay limit.
- CP677 moved the replay earlier and landed at delay `6`, with
  `p0_peak=0.3071938455`.
- The detector accepted and the gNB scheduled RAR/PUSCH.

Current state after CP677:

- Controlled replay Stage 6 is healthy for the CP663 `+500 Hz` waveform at
  offset `1773617`.
- Live UE Msg1 is still not accepted.
- Stage 7 is still not proven.

Recommended next checkpoint:

- Return to live UE with the live `+500 Hz` TX hook enabled and set
  `RF_ENV_TX_DBG_PRACH_TIME_OFFSET_SAMPLES=1773617`, keeping UE TX gain `59.0`
  and other variables unchanged.
- Treat that as a Stage 6 live test, not Stage 7, until the gNB logs a real
  live Msg1 accept.
