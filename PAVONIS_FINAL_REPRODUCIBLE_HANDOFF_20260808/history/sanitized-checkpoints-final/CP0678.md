## Interpretation

CP678 was a valid live Msg1 attempt:

- the UE reached RACH and transmitted five PRACH attempts;
- TX gain `59` was active;
- the `+500 Hz` live PRACH hook was active;
- the reduced offset `1773617` was active;
- [host-redacted] strict gate passed with zero Soapy TX timeouts;
- capture coverage included the first live PRACH transmissions.

But the gNB still did not accept live Msg1:

- detector accepted rows remained `0`;
- no gNB PRACH line had non-empty `detected_preambles`;
- no RAR/Msg3/PUSCH/RRC/NAS evidence appeared.

This means the CP677 controlled timing recovery does not directly transfer to
the live UE path. The first failed ladder stage remains live Stage 6 PRACH
Msg1 detection. Stage 7 is still not proven.

- Do a no-RF comparison of CP670, CP677, and CP678, focusing on live TX dump

  replay after CP678 as a health check before spending more live TX attempts.
