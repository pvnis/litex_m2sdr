## Interpretation

CP679 weakens these theories:

- live `+500 Hz` hook not applied,
- live PRACH TX dump malformed,
- live TX samples too small,
- wrong live PRACH root/config,
- CP678 failed only because offset `1773617` was not set.

The stronger current interpretation is:

1. CP678's live transmitted sample buffer is close to the accepted CP677
   controlled replay buffer.
2. The gNB receiver/detector does not see comparable coherent energy during
   live operation.
3. Therefore the remaining gap is not mainly sample-domain waveform generation.
   It is likely in live-vs-controlled RF/stream behavior, live timing relative
   to the gNB receive window, or path/state differences between the srsUE RF
   plugin path and the controlled sparse replay path.

This does not prove the antenna or RF path is overheating. It does keep
transient RF/path state on the suspect list, but the high live-TX sample
correlation points more directly at live transmission semantics or receiver
window alignment than at a baseband waveform bug.
