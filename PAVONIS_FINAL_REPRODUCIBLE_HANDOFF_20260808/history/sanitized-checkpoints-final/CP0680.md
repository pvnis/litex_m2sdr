# CHECKPOINT 680 - CP678 live dump 000001 controlled replay: accepted

## Scope

CP680 was the RF follow-up to CP679.

CP679 showed that CP678 live transmitted samples closely matched the accepted
CP667/CP677 `+500 Hz` template, but live CP678 still failed gNB Msg1
detection. CP680 therefore replayed the exact CP678 live PRACH dump `000001`
through the controlled sparse replay path.

Purpose:

- If CP680 accepts, the CP678 live waveform itself is good and the remaining
  failure is live srsUE/bladeRF stream/timing/RF behavior.
- If CP680 fails, the small residual waveform difference in CP678 matters.

This is controlled replay, not live UE. It can prove controlled Stage 6 PRACH
acceptance, but it cannot prove live Stage 7 Msg3.

## Interpretation

CP680 is a decisive controlled result:

- The exact CP678 live PRACH dump is a valid waveform for the gNB detector when
  sent through the controlled replay path.
- CP678 live failed even though its TX dump was waveform-valid, frequency
  corrected, high-amplitude, and transmitted with the reduced offset.
- Therefore the remaining blocker is not PRACH baseband waveform generation.

The live Stage 6 failure now points at one of:

1. live srsUE/bladeRF stream timing relative to the gNB receive window,
2. live RF/plugin stream semantics that differ from the controlled replay path,
3. live path coupling/power/state differences despite similar TX debug samples,
4. or a receiver-window/frame-phase interaction specific to live UE scheduling.

Stage status:

- Controlled Stage 6: passes with CP677 and CP680.
- Live Stage 6: still blocked.
- Stage 7: still not proven live.

Recommended next checkpoint:

- Do a no-RF source/log audit comparing the live srsUE bladeRF TX path against
  the controlled sparse replay TX path, focusing on stream activation, timed
  metadata, SOB/EOB behavior, RX-load/pre-TX timing, and how `tx_delta_samples`
  maps to gNB PRACH occasions.
- The next live RF experiment should be based on a concrete live-vs-controlled
  stream difference, not another waveform or threshold tweak.
