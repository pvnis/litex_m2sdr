## Interpretation

CP684 was a valid live Msg1 attempt and a useful negative result.

It rejects the narrow theory that CP678 failed only because the live UE had too
few Msg1 attempts. Increasing live attempts by `3x` did not make one land in
the accepted receiver region.

The leading branch now shifts from "increase live attempt count" to "explain
why all live attempts remain weak/off-window despite waveform validity under
controlled replay." The next no-RF step should compare CP684's fifteen live
attempts against CP678/CP680 at the raw-window level:

- per-attempt detector row timing;
- PRACH predemod raw windows near the fifteen live TX events;
- Soapy RX raw visibility near those events;
- whether CP684 attempts cluster at a consistent offset from the valid delay
  region;
- whether the first CP684 saved dump still accepts under controlled replay, as
  CP678 dump `000001` did in CP680.

Do not claim Stage 7 from this run. There was no live Msg1 accept, no RAR path,
and no live Msg3 evidence.
