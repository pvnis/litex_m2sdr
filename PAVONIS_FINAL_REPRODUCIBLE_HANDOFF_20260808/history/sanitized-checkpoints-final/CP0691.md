## Interpretation

CP691 is a decisive controlled negative for CP684 dump `000001`.

It changes the branch from:

```text
CP684 live failed despite a waveform-valid dump, like CP678.
```

to:

```text
CP684 dump000001 itself does not reproduce CP680-style controlled acceptance
under the same offset/threshold/TX-gain replay path.
```

That means CP684's live no-accept was not only a live-stream/opportunity-count
problem. The first CP684 saved PRACH buffer differs enough from the CP678 dump
or accepted family that the detector metric collapses under controlled replay,
even though CP690 measured high correlation to the accepted `+500 Hz` template.

The next no-RF rung should compare CP678 dump `000001`, CP684 dump `000001`,
and the accepted CP667/CP680 templates in the detector domain, not just
time-domain waveform correlation. Likely checks:

- root/DFT-start/frequency-offset sweep on CP684 dump `000001`;
- amplitude scaling sweep for CP684 dump `000001`;
- per-delay metric/concentration comparison against CP680;
- whether another CP684 dump among `000002`-`000008` is closer to CP680 and
  should be replayed.
