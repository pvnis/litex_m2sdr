## Short phase history

### CP1-CP75: instrumentation and first RF path checks

The early checkpoints established predemod dumps, manifests, static audits,
RAW512 captures, controlled PRACH replay, SOB/EOB experiments, TX guards, and
sync/camping alignment.

What worked:

- The artifact/report discipline was established.
- Synthetic and controlled PRACH paths could be made to work.
- Live UE reached cell selection and emitted PRACH.

What did not work:

- Live UE Msg1 was transmitted but not accepted by gNB.
- Several early environment propagation mistakes had to be found and fixed.

### CP76-CP160: replay health, cooldown, gain/bandwidth

This phase showed that RF path health was variable. Known-good controlled
replay sometimes degraded, then recovered after idle periods or different
gain/bandwidth choices.

What worked:

- Controlled replay could be recovered, notably with lower RX gain and 3 MHz
  bandwidth in the early branch.
- No-TX floor and RF-health probes gave useful baselines.

What did not work:

- Live UE Msg1 still did not accept.
- Simple cooldown was useful evidence but not a reliable fix.

### CP161-CP257: live timing, UL symbol dumps, thermal theory

This phase added UL symbol dump instrumentation and tested TX-now, high-peak
first, restart-stream, wallclock debug, delay variants, and DL reacquisition.
CP257 summarized the antenna-overheating theory.

Current read of that theory:

- A passive antenna overheating explanation is unlikely.
- There is no direct antenna temperature sensor in the evidence.
- Active RF-chain variability remains plausible: SDR state, USB resets, cable
  or connector state, powered RF components, and stream timing all remain in
  scope.

### CP258-CP324: operational hardening

Network instability and SSH issues forced more self-contained remote scripts.
CP305 saw [host-redacted] SSH timeout; CP307 recovered direct OpenSSH askpass access. The
watchdog-backed launch pattern became the safe default.

What worked:

- Remote control recovered.
- CP308 and later watchdog runs made failed RF attempts safer to collect.

What did not work:

- Live UE still transmitted PRACH with no Msg1 accept.

### CP325-CP404: active replay and strict gates

This phase used self-contained gates, RX gain 50, active replay, targeted UL
captures, and qcore/gate audits.

What worked:

- The RF path was not dead.
- Strong lower-RX copies could appear in active gNB conditions.

What did not work:

- Active gNB behavior differed from standalone controlled capture.
- Same nominal settings did not reliably produce acceptance.

### CP405-CP514: Soapy RX zero-copy bug

The Soapy RX tap and bandwidth work found a concrete active-RX visibility bug.
CP512 showed `OCUDU_SOAPY_RX_USE_READSTREAM=1` recovered strong active raw
copies. CP514 identified the likely cause: LiteXM2SDR direct buffers are
two-channel interleaved while the zero-copy consumer assumed contiguous
one-channel samples.

What worked:

- The raw-RX disappearance had a concrete root cause and operational fix.

What did not work:

- Fixing raw visibility did not by itself recover live PRACH acceptance.

### CP515-CP557: timestamp bridge, threshold diagnostics, first RA path proof

Detector traces, timestamp comparisons, negative timed-lead helpers, and
threshold overrides were added. CP551 with threshold `0.24` produced a fixed
replay PRACH accept, RAR, and Msg3/PUSCH grant.

What worked:

- The gNB RA path after accepted Msg1 is alive.
- Fixed replay can reach RAR/Msg3 scheduling.

What did not work:

- Fixed replay RAR/Msg3 does not prove live Stage 7.
- Blind threshold lowering is diagnostic only; it is not the live fix.

### CP558-CP603: raw512 branch and phase stochasticity

### CP697-CP698: source audit and guarded demod input-offset knob

CP697 audited the PRACH demod input path. CP698 implemented a narrow diagnostic

```text
/home/[host-redacted]/CLionProjects/ocudu/lib/phy/lower/processors/uplink/prach/prach_processor_worker.cpp
```

New environment knob:

```text
PAVONIS_PRACH_DEMOD_INPUT_OFFSET_SAMPLES
```

Behavior:

- unset or non-positive: no behavior change;
- only active for the guarded diagnostic case:
  - format zero;
  - start symbol 0;
  - one TD occasion;
  - one FD occasion;
- positive value smaller than the collected buffer:
  - passes `cf_buf.subspan(offset, cf_buf.size() - offset)` into the PRACH
    OFDM demodulator;
  - leaves RF capture, predemod dumping, scheduling, and detector code
    unchanged.

## Where we are now

The project is past the broad "can the gNB ever accept this waveform family?"
question. Controlled replay proves yes.

The narrower blocker is:

```text
Why does a waveform family that can accept under controlled conditions fail
when its energy lands in the nominal live/CP691 PRACH demod input window?
```

CP694-CP696 showed that shifting the detector input origin by about 847 samples
can turn the CP691 failed predemod window into an accepted detector result.
CP697 found the source point where the PRACH worker passes the rounded 1 ms
capture buffer into the demodulator. CP698 added a guarded diagnostic knob to
test that hypothesis in the actual gNB path.
