## Interpretation

CP704 strongly confirms the demod input-origin hypothesis from CP694-CP698.

The same CP684 dump family that failed in CP690/CP691 can be accepted by the
patched live gNB path when the PRACH demodulator is fed from sample offset
`847` of the collected PRACH window. This moves the leading branch from
"candidate hypothesis" to "validated controlled-replay fix."

The remaining unknown is live UE behavior. CP704 proves the patched gNB can
recover a controlled replay accept. It does not prove a real live UE Msg1
accepts, and it does not prove Stage 7.
