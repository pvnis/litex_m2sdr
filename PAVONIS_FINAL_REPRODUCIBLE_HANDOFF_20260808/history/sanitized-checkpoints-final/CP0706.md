## Interpretation

CP706 is a valid negative live result.

The CP704 demod input-origin fix is real for controlled replay, but live UE
still arrives at the receiver as weak/off-threshold PRACH evidence. This means
the remaining live blocker is not simply "the gNB demodulator starts 847 samples
too early" in all cases.

The leading branch shifts back to live-vs-controlled RF/stream semantics:

- controlled replay with offset 847 gives RSSI around `-53 dB` and accepts;
- live UE with the same gNB offset remains around `-75 dB` in the best rows;
- no detector row reaches the `0.24` threshold;
- no live Stage 7 evidence exists.
