## Regressions and caveats

No RF regression was introduced by CP716 because it was no-RF.

Caveats:

- CP716 uses the same correlation method as CP707, so the CP705-vs-CP714
  comparison is internally consistent.
- Correlation loss does not by itself prove malformed over-the-air RF. It shows
  the saved bladeRF PRACH debug dumps in CP714 are less similar to the accepted
  templates than CP705's saved dumps were.
- The saved dump count remains only eight TX debug dumps, as in CP705.
- The gNB Soapy RX summary still does not cover the entire late PRACH sequence.

## Theory update

CP715 weakened "gNB RX gain 50 was simply too low."

CP716 adds a new split:

- receiver windows at RX gain `55` are still weak and unrecovered;
- the CP714 UE-side saved TX dumps are also no longer in the near-identical
  accepted waveform family seen in CP705/CP707.

This means a blind live RX-gain ladder is not the right next move. The next
branch should investigate why the CP714 UE PRACH waveform family shifted.

The most likely immediate no-RF checks are:

- frequency/phase correction sweep of CP714 saved dumps against CP667/CP680/
  CP704 accepted references;
- compare CP714's positive CFO range (`+0.16 to +0.18 kHz`) with CP705's
  negative CFO range (`-0.11 to -0.07 kHz`);
- determine whether a different PRACH frequency rotation would bring CP714
  dumps back toward the accepted family.
