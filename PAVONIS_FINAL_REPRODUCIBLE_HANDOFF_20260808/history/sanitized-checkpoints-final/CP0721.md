## Interpretation

CP721 separates the CP718 result cleanly:

- TX-side: improved. The CP718 saved TX dumps are back near the accepted CP704
  waveform family, with no extra aggregate correction needed against CP704.
- RX-side: not improved. The gNB nearest receiver windows remain weak and
  sub-threshold, with zero non-empty PRACH lines and zero accepted detector
  rows.

Therefore CP719's failure is not explained by an uncorrected CP714-style TX
waveform drift. The leading branch remains live receiver-window visibility,
timing, or active RF-chain state upstream of the detector.
