## Interpretation

CP724 and CP725 together show that RX gain `55` is not a good controlled
baseline for this known waveform:

- CP724 missed because the metric was just below threshold.
- CP725 crossed threshold but failed the delay gate.
- Both had strong raw receiver evidence and clean strict gates.

This means the live-UE failure is not only "no RF reaches the gNB"; however, the
current RX-gain-55 controlled lower rung is also not healthy enough to justify
another live UE attempt.

state:
