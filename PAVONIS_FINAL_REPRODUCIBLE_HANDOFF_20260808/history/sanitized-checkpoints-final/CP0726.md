## Interpretation

CP726 rules out the simplest RX-gain-55-only explanation. Returning the
controlled replay to RX gain `50` in the current state still missed.

The current lower-rung problem is:

```text
Known-good CP684 controlled replay still reaches the gNB as a strong raw copy,
but the PRACH detector no longer produces a clean accepted row under the CP704
accepting recipe.
```

Therefore another live UE run is not justified yet. The lower controlled
baseline should be repaired or explained first.

## Recommended next rung

Run a no-RF controlled-regression audit comparing CP704, CP724, CP725, and
CP726 without extracting tarballs:

- compare best-row raw windows, detector numerators/references, delay peaks,
  and timestamp bridge alignment;
- compare TX input/DMA dumps and controlled replay timing;
- inspect whether the CP704 accept used a different receive-window phase/state
  than the current controlled failures.

If that audit does not reveal a software/window explanation, the next RF rung
should be a conservative controlled replay reset/cooldown or RF-chain-state
experiment, not live UE.
