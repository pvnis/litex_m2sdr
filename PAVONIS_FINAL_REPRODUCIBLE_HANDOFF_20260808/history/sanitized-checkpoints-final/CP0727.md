## Phase roll-up

### CP1-CP75: first instrumentation and RF checks

Added predemod dumps, manifests, RAW512 capture, controlled PRACH replay,
SOB/EOB diagnostics, TX guards, and early camping/sync checks. The UE could
camp or reach cell selection and emit PRACH, but live Msg1 did not accept.

### CP76-CP160: replay health, cooldown, gain, and bandwidth

Tested RF health, no-TX floor, cooldown, RX gain changes, and bandwidth
variants. Some controlled replay health was recovered, but the recovery did
not transfer to live UE Msg1.

### CP161-CP257: timing, UL dumps, and thermal theory

Added UL symbol dumps, TX-now variants, high-peak-first runs, restart-stream
tests, wallclock debug, delay variants, and DL reacquisition checks. This
separated "UE transmitted" from "gNB accepted". Passive antenna overheating
became less likely than active RF-chain/state/window effects.

### CP258-CP324: operational hardening

Built self-contained remote scripts, watchdogs, SSH recovery patterns, and
safer artifact collection. This made the work survivable under laptop network
instability but did not itself recover live PRACH acceptance.

### CP325-CP404: active replay and strict gates

Added strict gNB gates, RX-gain experiments, active replay, qcore/gate audits,
and targeted UL capture under active gNB conditions. The RF path was not simply
dead, but active gNB behavior stayed state-sensitive.

### CP405-CP514: Soapy RX visibility bug

Found a real LiteXM2SDR/Soapy visibility bug: direct buffers are two-channel
interleaved, while the zero-copy consumer assumed contiguous one-channel
samples. `OCUDU_SOAPY_RX_USE_READSTREAM=1` recovered strong active raw copies,
but live Msg1 still did not accept.

### CP515-CP557: timestamp bridge and RA-path proof

Added detector traces, timestamp bridge, negative timed-lead helpers, and
threshold diagnostics. CP551 showed fixed replay can accept Msg1 and trigger
RAR/Msg3 scheduling evidence. That proves part of the RA path, but not live
UE Stage 7.

### CP558-CP603: RAW512 branch and phase/window stochasticity

Compared RAW512 live/control captures, live-derived dump replay, active replay
repetitions, and phase/window behavior. CP597 accepted active replay. Similar
settings could still pass or fail, so phase/window/state variability became a
major suspect.

### CP604-CP652: detector tracing and `ra_scs` bug

Added metric/top-N/split detector traces and source-level PRACH config audits.
Found a real live upper PRACH config bug: long-format format0 was paired with
invalid `15 kHz` `ra_scs`. CP652 patched the live correction path. Necessary,
but not sufficient.

### CP653-CP684: `+500 Hz`, offset `1773617`, and more live attempts

Tested UE TX gain `59`, live PRACH `+500 Hz`, PRACH offset `1773617`, and
`preamble_trans_max: 20`. CP667 accepted controlled replay of a corrected live
dump, and CP680 accepted controlled replay of the exact CP678 live dump. Live
runs such as CP678, CP684, and CP687 still had zero accepted Msg1 rows.

### CP686-CP704: demod input-origin hypothesis

## What has worked

- Controlled replay has accepted Msg1 in multiple historical checkpoints,
  especially CP704.
- Fixed/control replay has exercised enough of the RA path to show RAR/Msg3
  scheduling evidence after an accepted Msg1.
- Live UE can cell-select and transmit PRACH.
- Strict gates now verify startup health, RX/TX bandwidth readback, RX gain,
  and TX-timeout absence before RF runs.
- The Soapy direct-buffer visibility bug was found and worked around.
- The live `ra_scs` source bug was found and patched.
- The demod input offset knob was built, deployed, and proven in CP704.
- Saved live TX waveforms can match accepted waveform families.
- The self-contained launch/collect/report flow works, including under
  intermittent laptop connectivity.

## What has not worked

- No live UE Msg1 accept has been achieved in the current branch.
- More live attempts did not solve it.
- Threshold lowering is only diagnostic, not a correctness fix.
- `+500 Hz`, offset `1773617`, TX gain `59`, demod offset `847`, RX gain `55`,
  and freqrot `275 Hz` did not transfer to live accept.
- CP705/CP714/CP718 receiver windows are too weak or unrecoverable near saved
  live TX events.
- The current known-good controlled replay baseline is now regressed:
  CP724-CP726 all failed against the CP704 accepting family.

## Regressions and operational risks

- Controlled replay remains state-sensitive. CP704 accepted; CP724-CP726 did
  not, even though the raw receiver copy was strong.
- Similar nominal RF settings can behave differently after idle time, gain/BW
  changes, USB or stream-state changes, stale processes, or other active RF
  state changes.
- Some runs leave stale `run_ota_stage_template.sh` wrappers; CP724, CP725,
  and CP726 all required CP98 cleanup, which passed.
- CP712 exposed a real `/tmp` operational risk: stale
  `/tmp/pavonis_soapy_rx_*.cf32` files can grow until startup cleanup fails
  with `ARG_MAX`.
- CP678 had an early partial-gzip collection issue. Current practice is to
  poll remote gzip validity before collection.
- CP658/CP659 saw bladeRF USB resets aligned with failing runs.
- The local shell often prints `Failed to create stream fd: Operation not
  permitted`; this remains local tool noise unless command return codes fail.
- The OCUDU worktree is dirty because of intentional diagnostic source work,
  including the CP698 demod input offset knob.

## Current state

The broad question is no longer:

```text
Can this waveform family ever be accepted?
```

The answer is yes: CP704 accepted it.

The current narrow question is:

```text
Why does the CP684 known-good controlled replay now arrive as a strong raw
receiver copy but fail the PRACH detector under the CP704-style recipe?
```

Until that lower rung is healthy again, another live UE attempt would spend RF
without a clean controlled baseline.
