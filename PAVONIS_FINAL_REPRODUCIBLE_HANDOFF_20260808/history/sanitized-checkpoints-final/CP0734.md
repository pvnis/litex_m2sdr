## Historical phase roll-up

### CP1-CP75: first instrumentation and RF checks

We added predemod dumps, manifests, RAW512 capture, controlled PRACH replay,
SOB/EOB diagnostics, TX guards, and early camping/sync checks.

What worked:

- The artifact/checkpoint/report discipline was established.
- Synthetic and controlled PRACH paths could be observed and manipulated.
- Live UE could camp or reach cell selection and emit PRACH.

What did not work:

- Live Msg1 did not accept.
- Several early launcher and environment-propagation mistakes had to be found
  and fixed.

### CP76-CP160: replay health, cooldown, gain, and bandwidth

We tested RF health, no-TX floor, cooldown, RX gain changes, and bandwidth
variants.

What worked:

- Some known-good controlled replay paths recovered under changed gain,
  bandwidth, or idle conditions.
- No-TX floor and RF-health probes became useful gates.

What did not work:

- These recoveries did not transfer to live UE Msg1 acceptance.
- Controlled replay health was variable enough that lower-rung gates became
  mandatory before trusting RF results.

### CP161-CP257: timing, UL dumps, and the overheating theory

We added UL symbol dump instrumentation, TX-now variants, high-peak-first runs,
restart-stream tests, wallclock debug, delay variants, and DL reacquisition
checks.

What worked:

- The evidence separated "UE transmitted" from "gNB accepted".
- Timing/window visibility improved.

Theory update:

- Passive antenna overheating became unlikely as the primary explanation. There
  is no direct antenna temperature evidence, and later failures point more to
  active RF-chain state, USB/stream state, receiver-window timing, or powered
  components.

### CP258-CP324: operational hardening

Network and SSH instability forced safer remote orchestration. We built
self-contained scripts, watchdogs, direct SSH recovery patterns, and safer
artifact collection.

What worked:

- `[host-redacted]` access recovered after timeout episodes.
- All-or-nothing remote scripts made RF work safer while the laptop connection
  was unreliable.

What did not work:

- Operational stability did not itself recover live PRACH acceptance.

### CP325-CP404: active replay and strict gates

We added strict gNB gates, RX-gain experiments, active replay, qcore/gate
audits, and targeted UL capture under active gNB conditions.

What worked:

- The RF path was not simply dead.
- Strong lower-RX copies could appear in active gNB conditions.

What did not work:

- Active gNB behavior did not match standalone capture reliably.
- Similar nominal settings could produce different detector outcomes.

### CP405-CP514: Soapy RX visibility bug

We investigated Soapy RX taps, bandwidth readback, active raw copies, and
zero-copy versus `readStream()`.

What worked:

- A real LiteXM2SDR/Soapy bug was found: direct buffers are two-channel
  interleaved while the zero-copy consumer assumed contiguous one-channel
  samples.
- `OCUDU_SOAPY_RX_USE_READSTREAM=1` recovered strong active raw copies.

What did not work:

- Fixing raw visibility did not by itself recover live Msg1.

### CP515-CP557: timestamp bridge and first RA-path proof

We added detector traces, timestamp bridge, negative timed-lead helpers, and
threshold diagnostics.

What worked:

- CP551 showed fixed replay can accept Msg1 and trigger RAR/Msg3 scheduling
  evidence.
- That proved the gNB RA path after accepted Msg1 is alive.

What did not work:

- Threshold lowering stayed diagnostic, not a correctness fix.
- Fixed/control replay RAR/Msg3 does not prove live UE Stage 7.

### CP558-CP603: RAW512 branch and phase/window stochasticity

We compared RAW512 live/control captures, live-derived dump replay, active
replay repetitions, and phase/window behavior.

What worked:

- CP597 accepted active replay.
- Strong lower-RX copies could exist even in no-accept runs.

Theory update:

- Same nominal offsets/settings could pass or fail depending on phase/window
  state, so receive-window/stream-state variability became a major suspect.

### CP604-CP652: detector tracing and `ra_scs` bug

We added metric/top-N/split detector traces and audited source-level PRACH
configuration.

What worked:

  with invalid `15 kHz` `ra_scs`.
- CP652 patched the live correction path.

What did not work:

- The `ra_scs` fix was necessary correctness work but not sufficient for live
  Msg1 acceptance.

## Regressions and operational risks

- Controlled replay is state-sensitive. CP704 accepted at offset `847`; CP724,
  CP725, and CP726 later failed at offset `847`; CP733 recovered at `562`.
- Similar nominal RF settings can behave differently after idle time, bandwidth
  or gain changes, USB or stream-state changes, stale processes, or active RF
  state changes.
- Stale `run_ota_stage_template.sh` wrappers still appear after some runs and
  require CP98 cleanup.
- CP712 exposed a `/tmp` risk: stale `/tmp/pavonis_soapy_rx_*.cf32` files can
  grow until startup cleanup fails with `ARG_MAX`.
- CP678 had an early partial-gzip collection issue. CP733 exposed a related
  wrapper wait/collection race, now patched with a wait-before-collect delay.
- CP658/CP659 saw bladeRF USB resets aligned with failing runs.
- The local shell often prints `Failed to create stream fd: Operation not
  permitted`; treat this as local tool noise unless command return codes fail.
- The `ocudu` worktree is intentionally dirty with diagnostic source work,
  including `PAVONIS_PRACH_DEMOD_INPUT_OFFSET_SAMPLES`.
- Local storage is better but still 93 percent used, so broad raw artifact
  extraction remains a bad default.
