  CP724-CP726 later failed at offset `847`; CP733 recovered at offset `562`.

  stream-state changes, stale processes, gain/bandwidth changes, or active RF
  state changes.

  wrapper so that becomes evidence instead of aborting the whole paired run.

  they must be aligned to actual UE PRACH SFN/slot windows.

  require CP98 cleanup.
- CP712 exposed a `/tmp` risk from stale `pavonis_soapy_rx_*.cf32` files.
