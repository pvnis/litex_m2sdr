## Interpretation

CP752 changes the leading isolation result. Before CP752, the saved CP745 live
TX waveform looked good by correlation, and CP749 showed it was strongly
visible at offset `847`. After CP752, the corrected true-offset-562 replay says
the CP745 live dump is not acceptance-equivalent to the CP684 controlled
reference under the current gNB detector path.

This does not mean the CP745 live dump is "garbage": CP747 showed high
corrected waveform correlation to accepted references, and CP752 raw/RSSI are
not weak. It means a detector-relevant mismatch remains. The plausible buckets
are now narrower:

- residual frequency/phase treatment of the CP745 live dump;
- replay preparation/scaling detail despite normal raw power;
- effective timing/delay placement relative to the true offset-562 detector
  crop;
- CP745 dump content differs from CP684 in a way high correlation did not
  expose.

This also means we should not immediately jump back to a live UE RF run. The
controlled path itself now distinguishes CP684 from CP745 at the same true
offset.
