## Interpretation

CP754 proves that the CP752 controlled replay failure was largely caused by
residual frequency/phase treatment of the CP745 live dump relative to the CP684
controlled replay input.

The saved CP745 live dump is acceptance-equivalent over the controlled path once
the CP747-derived `-375 Hz` correction is applied. That substantially narrows
the live UE problem: the live UE path likely still needs a frequency-rotation
sign/value adjustment, rather than a broad RF, gain, or antenna reset theory.

Do not directly hard-code the live UE setting from this result without a sign
audit. CP745 live UE used `PRACH_FREQ_ROT_HZ=725`. The controlled replay result
suggests an additional `-375 Hz` correction at the replayed-buffer level, which
is directionally consistent with a live candidate near:

```text
725 - 375 = 350 Hz
```

However, that translation depends on how the live `PRACH_FREQ_ROT_HZ` code path
maps sign and reference frame relative to the offline replay correction.
