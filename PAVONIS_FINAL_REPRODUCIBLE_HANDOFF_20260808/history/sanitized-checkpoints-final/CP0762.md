## Interpretation

CP762 does not find a no-RF smoking gun in the wrapper/config surface. The
recent failure is visible as a runtime DL/cell-search/SFN-sync stability issue,
not as an obvious changed ARFCN, SSB frequency, bandwidth, gain, SCS, demod
offset, threshold, gate, or UE config.

The current first failed stage remains:

```text
UE downlink cell search / cell-select / SFN-sync stability
```

The `350 Hz` PRACH candidate remains untested at Msg1:

```text
CP758 and CP760 propagated 350 Hz correctly, but neither emitted PRACH.
```

The antenna-overheating theory is still not supported as the primary theory by
this audit. The audit does not include temperature telemetry, and the visible
regression is pre-PRACH with matching startup/config/gate evidence. A transient
active RF-chain, bladeRF receive/DL path, timing/state, or physical RF condition
remains plausible.
