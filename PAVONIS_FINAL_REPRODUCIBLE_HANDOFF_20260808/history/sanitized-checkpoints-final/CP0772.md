## Result

`CP772_BACKOFF10_DID_NOT_RECOVER_EXPECTED_PCI`

The reboot did not fix the DL/SSB rung, and a 10 dB downlink power increase did
not recover expected PCI proof. CP772 produced a stronger but still ambiguous
capture. That points away from a simple software-startup issue or a small TX
power setting problem.

The first failed rung remains below PRACH: independent SSB/PCI visibility is
not stable enough to justify another live UE PRACH attempt yet. The next useful
debug direction is the physical RF path or a direct known-good cabled/antenna
comparison, because current OTA SSB is far below the old CP199 proof behavior.
