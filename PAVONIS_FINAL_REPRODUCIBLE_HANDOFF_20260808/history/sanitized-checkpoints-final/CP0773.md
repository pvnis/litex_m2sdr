## Interpretation

`CP773_SSB_OFFSET_SWEEP_NO_EXPECTED_PCI_RECOVERY`

The simple "detector is looking at the wrong SSB center" hypothesis did not
explain CP772. Across a +/-300 kHz sweep, the capture continues to look like
real RF energy with PSS present, but not like a clean expected PCI 1 cell.

This keeps the first failed rung below PRACH:

- CP770: post-reboot CP199 geometry saw weak ambiguous SSB.
- CP772: +10 dB TX power increased received level but did not recover PCI 1.
- CP773: offline SSB frequency offsets did not recover PCI 1.

Given the user's hardware constraint, the next useful work should remain
remote and avoid requiring a new known-good physical path. The most useful next
directions are to inspect existing receive-chain controls and/or run no-TX
offline decoders against the same raw data before attempting another live UE
ladder step.
