## Interpretation

`CP774_CP772_RAW_NO_DECODABLE_PBCH_IN_LOCAL_SCAN`

CP774 strengthens the CP770/CP772/CP773 result. The CP772 raw contains RF energy
and PSS-like structure, but it does not contain a PBCH/MIB decode under the
tested pattern and CFO-shift window.

This keeps the first failed rung below PRACH:

- CP770: post-reboot CP199 geometry saw weak ambiguous SSB.
- CP772: +10 dB TX power improved level but did not recover PCI 1.
- CP773: SSB-center offset sweep did not recover PCI 1.
- CP774: independent PBCH/MIB scan found no CRC-valid decode.

With no known-good physical path available beyond the installed setup, the next
remote-only direction should inspect receive-chain and capture assumptions:
bladeRF RX channel/port selection, gain readback, AGC state, sample format, and
whether RX1/RX2 or antenna mapping can be varied by software without a physical
swap.
