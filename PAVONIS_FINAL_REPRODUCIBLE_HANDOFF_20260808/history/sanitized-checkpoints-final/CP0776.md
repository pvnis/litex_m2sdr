## Interpretation

`CP776_RX1_ACTIVE_BUT_STILL_NO_EXPECTED_PCI_RX2_NOT_HIDDEN_GOOD_PATH`

CP776 closes the software RX-port branch:

- generic bladeRF capture from CP770/CP772 is RX1-equivalent, as CP775 implied
- explicit RX1 sees the installed downlink path at the same level as CP772
- explicit RX1 still does not prove expected PCI 1
- explicit RX2 is much weaker and does not reveal a decodable cell

The current failure is therefore not explained by the capture helper using the
wrong software RX channel. The first failed rung remains independent downlink
decode visibility, below PRACH.

The evidence now points back to either the installed RF path quality, the
transmitted waveform quality at the receiver, or a non-port receiver behavior.
The next remote-only useful direction is not another live UE run; it is a
time/frequency/quality characterization of the RX1 captures versus the old CP199
passing capture to understand why the receiver now sees energy and PSS-like
structure but cannot recover a stable PCI/PBCH.
