## Interpretation

`CP778_LOCAL_PBCH_SCAN_NOT_VALIDATED_FOR_CRC_BUT_DISTINGUISHES_CP199_PCI1`

CP778 changes the interpretation of CP774:

- The local `ssb_file_test` scan did not decode PBCH CRC on CP199 either, even
  though CP199 is the old detector-proven capture.
- Therefore CP774's `PBCH_OK=0` should not be treated as independent proof that
  CP772 uniquely lacks PBCH. The PBCH CRC part of that local scanner is not
  validated for these captures.
- The scanner still distinguishes CP199 from CP772 at the PCI measurement level:
  CP199 frequently measures PCI 1, while CP772 never measured PCI 1 in the same
  scan setup.

The current strongest evidence remains the detector path:

- CP199 reaches `EXPECTED_CELL_PROVEN` at 0.50 s.
- CP772/CP776 RX1 remain `TX_SEEN_BUT_PCI_NOT_PROVEN`.
- CP777 shows current RX1 captures are about 6 dB lower and spectrally flatter,
  but do not have a gross DC/IQ/time-instability failure.

So the failed rung is still independent DL SSB/cell visibility, below PRACH.
CP778 prevents over-weighting the local PBCH CRC failure, but it does not
restore confidence in the current RX1 path.
