## Interpretation

`CP782_CP781_RAW_REMAINS_CURRENT_FAILURE_FAMILY`

CP781 backoff0 did not move the raw capture toward CP199:

- CP781 is slightly lower than CP772/CP776 by RMS.
- CP781 has no clipping and no CP199-like near-rail excursions.
- CP781 PSD shape is close to CP772/CP776, not CP199.
- CP781 center/outer PSD ratio is `0.18 dB`, while CP199 is `2.30 dB`.
- CP781 edge/center ratio is `-3.57 dB`, while CP199 is `-6.18 dB`.

This supports the CP781 interpretation: the configured `tx_gain_backoff: 0`
knob did not produce a received-power or spectrum-shape recovery in the
independent RX capture. The current captures remain a consistent failure family
across CP772, CP776 RX1, and CP781.

The first failed rung remains independent DL SSB/cell visibility below PRACH.
The next useful work should focus on why current bounded downlink captures do
not resemble the old CP199 passing capture even when the gNB config requests
more downlink power.
