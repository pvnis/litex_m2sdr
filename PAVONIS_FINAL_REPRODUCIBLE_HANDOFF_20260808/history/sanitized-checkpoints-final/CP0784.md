## Interpretation

`CP784_NATIVE_DMA_TONE_NOT_VISIBLE_AT_INDEPENDENT_RX`

CP784 did not establish the requested good repeatable tone baseline. Instead it
found:

- native LiteX DMA generation ran cleanly on [host-redacted]
- the bladeRF capture path ran cleanly on [host-redacted]
- the independent RX captures during tone TX were indistinguishable from the
  no-TX captures
- there was no strong peak at the intended +500 kHz offset or elsewhere in the
  captured 5.76 MHz band

This does not yet rule out the software path. It narrows the next question:
either the native DMA tone is not reaching the RF output, the RF output is gated
or muted for this tool path, the tone is routed to a non-installed TX path, or
the installed RF path remains unavailable to [host-redacted] at this frequency.

The next useful step is to try the lower-level AD9361/LiteX BIST TX tone path,
because it bypasses host DMA waveform streaming and directly exercises the RFIC
test-tone machinery.
