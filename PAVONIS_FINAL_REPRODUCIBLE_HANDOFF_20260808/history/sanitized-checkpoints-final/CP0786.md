## Purpose

CP786 attempted to retest the native LiteX DMA tone path after finding a concrete CP784 candidate issue: `m2sdr_play` explicitly writes `CSR_CROSSBAR_MUX_SEL_ADDR = 0` before PCIe TX, while `m2sdr_gen` does not.

The intended test was:

1. Configure RF with `m2sdr_rf`.
2. Force AD9361 BIST off.
3. Force PCIe TX crossbar mux to `0`.
4. Start `m2sdr_gen` tone in the background.
5. Capture the tone at [host-redacted] with bladeRF.

Evidence from `cp786_[date-redacted]_nuc4_stop_tone_rep1.log`:

- `m2sdr_gen.rc` was `124`, matching timeout expiry.
- `m2sdr_gen.log` shows sustained streaming around `1.48 Gbps` with zero reported underflows until timeout.

## Conclusion

CP786 is a useful control-flow checkpoint, not a valid RF verdict. It proves:

- The explicit crossbar write succeeded and read back as `0`.
- `m2sdr_gen` streamed for the intended duration with zero reported underflows.
- The current remote launch pattern does not detach the generator from SSH sufficiently for an overlapping external capture.

It does not prove that the DMA tone is absent at RF.

## Next Rung

Fix the launch pattern by starting `m2sdr_gen` under a fully detached remote process, for example with stdin redirected from `/dev/null` and the child launched through `setsid`/`nohup` so SSH can return while the generator continues to run.

Then run two baselines:

1. Corrected DMA tone to bladeRF, to re-evaluate the software TX path.
2. LiteX TX to LiteX RX on [host-redacted], to isolate bladeRF/[host-redacted] from the measurement path.
