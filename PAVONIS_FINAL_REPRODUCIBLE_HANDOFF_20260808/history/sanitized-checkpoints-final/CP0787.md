## Failure Details

The first `m2sdr_gen` launch reported as running and `record_tone_rep1` completed, but the captured file was still all zeros.

The stop routine then killed the `timeout` wrapper but did not kill the child `m2sdr_gen` process group. That left the first `m2sdr_gen` child running. Subsequent `m2sdr_gen` starts failed with:

```text
DMA not available
```

The leftover process was cleaned up after the run.

## Interpretation

CP787 did not establish a LiteX TX/RX baseline.

It found two separate issues:

1. Native `m2sdr_record` captured all zeros in this loopback configuration, including during the tone case.
2. The script's stop routine was not process-group safe, so one `m2sdr_gen` child survived until explicit cleanup.

The all-zero captures mean this cannot be used to isolate bladeRF yet. Before trusting LiteX self-RX, the native RX DMA path must be proven with a simpler source.
