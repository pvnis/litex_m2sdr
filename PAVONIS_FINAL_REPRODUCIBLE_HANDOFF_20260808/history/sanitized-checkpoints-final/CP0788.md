CP788 proves that native LiteX RX capture through `m2sdr_record` is not currently trustworthy as a bladeRF replacement in this configuration.

The AD9361 BIST RX tone was enabled by register readback, but the recorded files remained all zeros. Therefore, before using LiteX RX to isolate bladeRF, the native RX path needs its own debugging. The failure is below the requested cross-check and does not invalidate CP785's result, where bladeRF clearly observed the LiteX AD9361 BIST TX tone.
