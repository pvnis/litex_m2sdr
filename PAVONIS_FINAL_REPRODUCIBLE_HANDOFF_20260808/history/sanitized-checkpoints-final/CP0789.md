## Interpretation

CP789 establishes that the native LiteX DMA TX path is visible at the bladeRF receiver when the generator is actually running during capture.

This corrects the CP784/CP786 ambiguity:

- CP784/CP786 did not prove native DMA TX absence because their capture windows did not overlap live TX.
- CP789 proves live native DMA TX does reach the installed RF/bladeRF observation path.
- CP785 proved AD9361 BIST TX also reaches the bladeRF path.
- CP787/CP788 show native LiteX RX via `m2sdr_record` is currently all-zero and cannot yet replace bladeRF as the receiver.

The strongest DMA-tone line appears near `+1.0046 MHz` even though the requested generator tone was `+500 kHz`. The requested window also rises, but the global line is the clear repeatable signal. That frequency offset/format behavior should be understood before using `m2sdr_gen` as a calibrated RF tone source, but it is sufficient to prove that host-DMA-generated samples can reach RF.
