## Purpose

Establish a LiteX-only RX/TX baseline so the bladeRF receiver is not the only proof that the LiteX SDR is transmitting a receivable signal.

This checkpoint removes bladeRF from the measurement and uses the LiteX M2SDR itself as both transmitter and receiver through the SoapySDR path.

## Interpretation

This establishes a positive LiteX-only baseline:

- The LiteX Soapy RX path can receive samples reliably.
- The LiteX Soapy TX path can emit a known baseband tone reliably.
- The LiteX device can see its own TX tone without bladeRF in the measurement path.
- `loopback=phy` proves the FPGA-side loopback/data path very strongly.
- `loopback=ad9361` proves the RFIC internal loopback path.
- `loopback=none` also passed, which is the most useful bladeRF isolation result: with no explicit loopback enabled and no bladeRF involved, the LiteX receiver still saw the LiteX TX tone repeatably.

Nuance: the `none_zero` and `ad9361_zero` captures were not spectrally empty near 500 kHz. They had weaker nearby peaks, but the tone-on captures were stronger by `22.71 dB` for `none` and `15.22 dB` for `ad9361`. Treat this as a differential baseline, not a calibrated quiet-environment measurement.
