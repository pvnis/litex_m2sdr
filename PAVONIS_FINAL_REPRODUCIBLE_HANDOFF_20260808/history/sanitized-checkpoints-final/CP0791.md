## Interpretation

This is a clean pass:

- The LiteX Soapy TX path emits a repeatable external RF tone.
- The bladeRF path sees the tone with about `38.4 dB` SNR.
- The tone-on/off delta is about `36.3 dB`.
- The bladeRF captures did not clip (`Clip2040=0` for all captures).
- The Soapy TX process had zero write errors and zero write timeouts.

The observed external tone peak is near `502.06 kHz`, about `+2.06 kHz` from the requested `+500 kHz` offset. This offset is stable across tone reps and is small enough for this checkpoint; it likely reflects the combined oscillator/sample-rate frequency error between the LiteX transmitter and bladeRF receiver.
