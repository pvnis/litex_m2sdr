## Interpretation

CP794 is a recovery checkpoint for the lower ladder. CP793's immediate failure was UE DL/SFN/cell-select, but CP794 shows that this lower rung is intermittent rather than permanently broken: PCI/SFN/cell-select recovered and live PRACH was emitted in the same hardware session.

The current blocker after this run is therefore back at live PRACH visibility/acceptance:

- The UE produced real PRACH transmissions.
- The bladeRF TX debug path reported valid timed TX metadata.
- The gNB/LiteX side stayed healthy and reported no Soapy TX timeouts.
- The detector did see weak/background peaks, but no peak reached the `0.24` acceptance threshold.

Do not overinterpret the `725 Hz` versus `350 Hz` difference for SFN acquisition. The PRACH frequency rotation is a UE PRACH TX-path setting, while CP793 failed before PRACH. The more defensible conclusion is that DL acquisition is marginal/intermittent, and CP794 happened to recover it.
