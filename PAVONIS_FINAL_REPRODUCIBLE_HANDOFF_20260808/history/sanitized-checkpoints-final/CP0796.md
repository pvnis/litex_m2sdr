## Interpretation

CP796 shows that the no-PRACH state is not specific to `350 Hz`. The immediate `725 Hz` repeat also failed below real PRACH, but one rung lower than CP795:

- CP794 `725 Hz`: cell select succeeded, real PRACH config, 19 PRACH transmissions, no Msg1 accept.
- CP795 `350 Hz`: cell select succeeded, zero-preamble PRACH config, no PRACH.
- CP796 `725 Hz`: PCI found, SFN/cell select failed, no PRACH.

The current blocker is therefore lower-rung UE DL/SFN/control stability. Continuing to [interface-redacted] PRACH rotation is not yet meaningful because recent runs do not reliably reach PRACH emission.

## Regression Status

Recovered:

- LiteX/gNB startup and bandwidth readbacks remain good.
- The UE can still find PCI.

Regressed relative to CP794:

- SFN/cell-select is not reproducibly successful.
- Real PRACH config is not reproducibly present.
- No live PRACH burst was emitted in CP795 or CP796.

## Next Step

Stop moving upward until the physical/RF directions are independently baselined. The missing baseline is the reverse tone path: bladeRF TX into LiteX/M2SDR RX. CP790 covered LiteX-only RX/TX, and CP791 covered LiteX TX into bladeRF RX. A bladeRF TX to LiteX RX tone test will directly exercise the PRACH physical direction without involving UE cell select or gNB PRACH detection.

If that reverse tone path passes, the next debugging target is UE DL/SFN/control stability. If it fails, then the current live failures have a physical/RF-path explanation.
