## Interpretation

The reverse physical direction is alive:

- bladeRF TX1 can emit a controlled uplink-frequency tone.
- LiteX/M2SDR RX at gain 50 sees it at the expected offset with large margin.
- The observed tone offset near `+97.7 kHz` matches the prior CP329/CP331 behavior, where the nominal `+100 kHz` tone landed near `+97.1 kHz`.

This strongly argues against a currently dead bladeRF TX to LiteX RX physical/uplink path. The live PRACH failures should not be explained by "nothing can be received in the uplink direction" at the gross RF level.

The remaining caveat is process control: the helper used `REPEAT=300`, which represents about 30 seconds of tone data, while the outer CLI timeout was 18 seconds. That explains `BLADE_EXIT=124` and should be cleaned up in a shorter repeat or future helper, but it does not invalidate the RF observation because the capture saw the controlled tone with high margin.
