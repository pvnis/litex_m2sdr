## Interpretation

CP803 is a useful lower-rung result:

- The downlink is present in UE RX geometry: TX energy and PSS are detected.
- Expected PCI 1 is not cleanly identified in that geometry.
- This contrasts with CP801, where the same downlink was proven in SSB-centered geometry at `0.25 s`.

That makes the CP802 live behavior coherent:

- CP802 UE found PCI only late, at trial 61.
- CP802 SFN sync then failed 100/100.
- CP803 shows that expected PCI is weak/ambiguous when captured at the UE center/rate/filter geometry.

This still does not implicate PRACH. The live stack is failing below PRACH because DL/SFN acquisition is marginal in the UE receive geometry.

## Next Step

Run an offset sweep on the CP803 raw capture without transmitting again. Sweep the detector's assumed SSB absolute frequency around `1839650000 Hz` while keeping `FC=1842506000` and `FS=11520000`. If expected PCI recovers at an offset, that points to a frequency/LO/SSB-placement mismatch. If no offset recovers expected PCI, the next check should compare alternate bladeRF bandwidths or capture center choices against the UE's configured RX path.
