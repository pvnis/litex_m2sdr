## Interpretation

CP804 argues against a simple SSB absolute-frequency offset fix. Sweeping `-300 kHz` to `+300 kHz` around nominal did not recover expected PCI 1 in the CP803 UE-geometry capture.

Combined ladder state:

- CP801: SSB-centered capture proves expected PCI 1.
- CP803: UE-centered capture sees TX/PSS but does not prove expected PCI 1.
- CP804: changing assumed SSB absolute frequency over a broad sweep does not recover expected PCI 1.

This keeps the live blocker on the UE downlink acquisition/SFN rung. The problem is not gross RF, not gNB TX startup, and not obviously a simple SSB-frequency assumption error in the offline detector.

## Next Step

The next physical/config variable to isolate is the bladeRF receive filter/center geometry. Run one more downlink capture in UE-center geometry but with a wider/alternate capture bandwidth, especially `10 MHz`, and compare against CP803's `9.216 MHz` bandwidth. If expected PCI recovers with the wider bandwidth, the UE's effective RX filter may be suppressing or distorting the offset SSB. If it does not recover, compare center choices (`1842500000` vs `1842506000`) or inspect the srsUE SFN synchronization implementation/assumptions directly.
