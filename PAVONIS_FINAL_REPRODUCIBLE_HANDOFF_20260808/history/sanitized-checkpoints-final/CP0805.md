## Interpretation

The `10 MHz` receive bandwidth did not recover expected PCI 1 in UE-centered geometry.

Compared with CP803:

- PSS got stronger:
  - CP803 `0.25 s` best PSS: `0.5843`
  - CP805 `0.25 s` best PSS: `0.8141`
- Expected PCI still remained weak and wrong-PCIs dominated:
  - CP803 `0.25 s` expected/wrong ratio: `0.596`
  - CP805 `0.25 s` expected/wrong ratio: `0.637`

So the bandwidth change improves PSS visibility but does not fix SSS/PCI ambiguity. This is consistent with the live UE behavior: the UE can find PCI eventually, but SFN/cell-select is not reliable enough to reach PRACH.
