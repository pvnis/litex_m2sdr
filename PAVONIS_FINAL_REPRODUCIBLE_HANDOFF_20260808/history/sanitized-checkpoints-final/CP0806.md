## Interpretation

CP806 confirms that the LiteX Soapy RX/TX baseline is still healthy after
CP805. This isolates bladeRF from the measurement and rules out a general
LiteX receive/transmit path collapse.

Current ladder state:

- LiteX-only Soapy RX/TX: CP806 pass.
- LiteX Soapy TX to bladeRF: CP791 pass.
- bladeRF TX1 to LiteX RX: CP799 pass.
- Independent SSB-centered gNB downlink: CP801 pass.
- UE-centered downlink captures: CP803/CP805 see TX/PSS but do not prove
  expected PCI.

The active failure remains above the basic RF/tone layer: UE-geometry downlink
acquisition/SFN, especially expected PCI ambiguity when observing the gNB
waveform away from exact SSB-centered geometry.
