simple `+6000 Hz` capture-center theory unlikely to be the whole explanation.

- TX energy is present.
- PSS is present.
- Expected PCI 1 does not dominate SSS/PCI scoring in carrier-centered or
  UE-like receive geometry.

It also preserves the lower RF conclusions:

- CP806 LiteX-only Soapy RX/TX still passes.
- CP791 LiteX Soapy TX to bladeRF still previously passed.
- CP799 bladeRF TX1 to LiteX RX still previously passed.
- CP801 SSB-centered downlink still remains the most recent strong expected-PCI
  proof.

The active boundary remains Stage 5/UE downlink acquisition, not PRACH.
