## Interpretation

CP808 is the split we needed after CP807:

- The gNB/LiteX downlink is still emitting a decodable expected PCI 1 SSB when
  observed in SSB-centered geometry.
- The CP807 failure is therefore not a general "no receive" or "gNB stopped
  transmitting useful DL" event.
- The active problem is specific to carrier/UE-centered handling of an SSB that
  is about `-2.85 MHz` from the receive center, or to the detector/srsUE
  assumptions in that geometry.

Current boundary:

- Basic RF paths are proven by CP806, CP791, and CP799.
- SSB-centered independent downlink is proven by CP808.
- Carrier/UE-centered downlink still sees TX/PSS but does not prove expected
  PCI in CP803, CP805, and CP807.
- PRACH debugging remains premature until UE downlink acquisition/SFN is stable.
