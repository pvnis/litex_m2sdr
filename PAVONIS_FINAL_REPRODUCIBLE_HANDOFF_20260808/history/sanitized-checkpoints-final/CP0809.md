## Purpose

CP807 failed to prove PCI 1 from a carrier-centered capture even though TX and
PSS were present. CP808 then proved the same gNB downlink in SSB-centered
geometry.

CP809 performs a no-TX offline transform on the CP807 raw to test whether the
carrier-centered raw actually contains a valid PCI 1 SSB. The transform is:

1. Mix CP807 raw by `+2850000 Hz` to bring the SSB to DC.
2. Run detector at 11.52 Msps without decimation.
3. Run detector again after FFT low-pass and decimation by 2 to 5.76 Msps.

## Interpretation

CP809 proves that the CP807 carrier-centered raw did contain a valid PCI 1 SSB.
The failure is not that the gNB waveform disappeared or that the bladeRF
capture missed the cell. The cell is recoverable from the same raw once the
SSB is explicitly shifted, low-pass filtered, and decimated to the same 5.76
Msps geometry that CP808 used.

This narrows the active problem to the carrier/UE-centered interpretation path:

- The current detector's direct 11.52 Msps off-center path is insufficient.
- The raw RF content is good enough after a normal channelization step.
- If srsUE behaves like the failing off-center detector path, then the live SFN
  failure may be in UE-side SSB channelization/filtering/[interface-redacted] assumptions rather
  than in basic RF or PRACH.
