## Interpretation

CP811 clarifies the current first failed downlink stage:

- The cell identity is visible to srsRAN at CSI/PSS/SSS level.
- The same captures do not provide a valid PBCH decode or SFN.
- This remains true even for CP808, the SSB-centered capture that passed the
  independent PSS/SSS detector.

This explains why CP802 could find PCI late but fail SFN/cell selection before
PRACH. In `srsue/src/phy/nr/cell_search.cc`, a cell is only declared found when
`srsran_ssb_search` produces PBCH CRC success and sufficient SNR. CP811 shows the
current captures are below that bar.

## Next step

Do not proceed to PRACH yet. The next RF checkpoint should be a bounded
SSB-centered downlink level sweep scored by both:

- the existing PSS/SSS detector, and
- `ssb_file_test` PBCH CRC.

The immediate variables to sweep are gNB TX backoff and bladeRF RX gain while
watching for clipping. The goal is PBCH CRC `OK`, not merely `EXPECTED_CELL_PROVEN`.
