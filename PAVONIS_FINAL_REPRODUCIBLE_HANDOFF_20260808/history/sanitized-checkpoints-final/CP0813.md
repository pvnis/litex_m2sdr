## Interpretation

CP813 proves that receive-side gain `45` is better than CP812 and returns us to
SSB-centered PCI proof at the PSS/SSS detector layer. However, PBCH/SFN is still
not proven.

The raw level is close to the old-good level from prior reports:

- CP199 old pass reference: about `-16.95 dBFS`, peak `[date-redacted]`.
- CP813: about `-17.0 dBFS`, peak `1827`, no clip count.

This makes the remaining blocker more specific: PSS/SSS is strong enough, but
PBCH decode is still failing.
