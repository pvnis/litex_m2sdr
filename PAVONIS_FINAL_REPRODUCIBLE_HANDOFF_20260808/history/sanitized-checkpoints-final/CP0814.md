## Interpretation

CP814 confirms that the CP813 PBCH failure is not just a one-second replay-window
artifact. Even when `ssb_file_test` is fed short windows comparable to its
upstream file tests, no window produces PBCH CRC OK or PBCH PCI 1.

The aligned windows do show PCI 1 at CSI level, so the sample timing sweep is
finding SSB-bearing windows. The blocker remains PBCH/SFN decode, not gross SSB
timing alignment and not absence of PSS/SSS.

## Current boundary

The current first failed stage is:

```text
Downlink PBCH/SFN decode over RF
```

Do not proceed to PRACH. The next ladder step should target PBCH robustness:
filter bandwidth, residual CFO/frequency correction, or an RF/baseband
comparison against a known PBCH-decodable capture.
