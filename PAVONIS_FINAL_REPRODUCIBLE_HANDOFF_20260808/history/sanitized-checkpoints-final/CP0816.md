## Interpretation

CP816 did not improve PBCH and made the PSS/SSS detector less decisive than
CP813. This argues against `4.5 MHz` bladeRF bandwidth as the fix.

Current best SSB-centered capture setting remains CP813:

```text
TX_BACKOFF=20
bladeRF gain=45
bladeRF BW=5.6 MHz
```

The next no-RF sanity check is to sweep SSB pattern and duplex assumptions on
CP813's aligned windows. If that does not recover PBCH, the blocker remains RF
PBCH/SFN quality or PBCH waveform content, not simple bandwidth/filter choice.
