for that rerun.

## Interpretation

CP819 proves that the current gNB transmit-side samples before DMA contain a
valid PCI `1` PBCH/SFN waveform. This removes "current gNB waveform generation
lost PBCH" as the primary explanation for the CP813/CP814/CP815/CP817 RF PBCH
failures.

What remains open:

```text
Pre-DMA digital PBCH: PASS
Post-DMA ring/user-kernel payload: NOT COLLECTED
LiteX/M2SDR Soapy RX/TX tone baseline: PASS (CP818)
bladeRF RF capture PSS/SSS/PCI: present at CP813 settings
bladeRF RF capture PBCH/SFN: still KO
```

Current narrowed suspect boundary:

```text
after pre-DMA gNB samples
before or during bladeRF RF receive PBCH decode
```

The immediate next checkpoint should either make the post-DMA dump fire again or
use a comparable LiteX/M2SDR self-RX/cabled capture of the gNB waveform. Until
one of those is done, the exact failure boundary is "downstream of pre-DMA,"
not yet specifically "bladeRF only."
