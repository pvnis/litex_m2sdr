# Checkpoint 824 - Paired gain 43 RF PBCH KO, post-DMA slice anomaly

## Purpose

CP823 paired a passing zero-copy TX ring dump with a same-window bladeRF capture,
but the RF raw was slightly hot. CP824 reruns the same paired harness with only
the bladeRF receive gain changed from `45` to `43`.

The goal was to test whether mild clipping was the reason PBCH stayed KO over
RF.

## Interpretation

Lowering bladeRF gain to `43` removed clipping and improved RF CSI quality, but
PBCH still failed in both one-second and aligned-window scoring. That makes
"PBCH KO was only bladeRF clipping at gain 45" unlikely.

The new post-DMA result is a separate anomaly: the short zero-copy dump slice
saw CSI PCI `1` but not PBCH CRC OK, while pre-DMA was OK. Before treating that
as a real intermittent TX-ring fault, run a larger zero-copy TX-only control
with more DMA buffers, because the 256-buffer slice is only about 22.7 ms of
channel-0 payload after conversion.
