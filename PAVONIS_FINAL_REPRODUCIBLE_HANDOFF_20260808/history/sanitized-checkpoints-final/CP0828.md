## Interpretation

The CP824/CP825 zero-copy PBCH KO condition is intermittent or state-dependent,
not permanent. The latest boundary is:

```text
Pre-DMA PBCH: consistently OK
Copy-mode post-DMA PBCH: OK at CP821 and CP827
Zero-copy post-DMA PBCH: OK at CP822/CP823/CP828, KO at CP824/CP825
bladeRF RF PBCH: KO even when same-window zero-copy TX ring was OK at CP823
```

Next RF-side work should use paired TX/RF capture and require the same-window
zero-copy TX ring to decode before interpreting the RF result.
