## Interpretation

CP829 confirms the clean failure boundary after the LiteX RX/TX baseline and
after zero-copy recovered:

```text
Same-window zero-copy TX ring PBCH/MIB: PASS
Same-window bladeRF RF expected PCI evidence: present in aligned windows
Same-window bladeRF RF PBCH/SFN: KO in one-second score and 847 aligned windows
```

This makes CP824's post-DMA anomaly a separate intermittent diagnostic issue,
not the blocker for the current RF result. The active downlink blocker remains
downstream of the normal zero-copy TX ring: TX consumption/RF egress, the
physical RF path, bladeRF receive behavior, or receive-side PBCH tolerance.

The gain `43` repeat removed clipping but did not restore PBCH. The next
single-variable RF rung should keep the paired TX dump and move one receive-side
parameter at a time, starting with a slightly lower bladeRF gain or bandwidth.
