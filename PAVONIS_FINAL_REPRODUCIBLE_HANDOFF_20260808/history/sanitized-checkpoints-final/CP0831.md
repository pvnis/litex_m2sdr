## Interpretation

CP831 cannot be used as a clean receive-gain result because its same-window
post-DMA TX control failed:

```text
Pre-DMA waveform PBCH: PASS
Zero-copy post-DMA diagnostic slice: KO
RF expected PCI evidence: present
RF PBCH/SFN: KO
```

The RF side still shows the same pattern as CP829/CP830, but the correct next
step is to re-establish the TX post-DMA baseline before changing more RF
parameters. An immediate zero-copy TX-only control should show whether the
post-DMA anomaly persists or clears again.
