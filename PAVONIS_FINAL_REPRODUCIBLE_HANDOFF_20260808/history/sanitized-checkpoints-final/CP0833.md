## Interpretation

CP833 isolates the current post-DMA issue to zero-copy or the zero-copy
diagnostic extraction:

```text
CP832 zero-copy TX-only: pre-DMA PBCH OK, post-DMA PBCH KO
CP833 copy-mode TX-only: pre-DMA PBCH OK, post-DMA PBCH OK
```

The gNB waveform, PBCH decoder, and basic DMA-dump analysis are therefore not
generally broken. This reproduces the same split previously seen around CP827
and CP828. The next checkpoint should repeat zero-copy immediately after the
copy-mode pass to see whether zero-copy recovers again or remains in the KO
state.
