## Interpretation

CP835 gives a clean gain `41` RF datapoint:

```text
Same-window zero-copy TX ring PBCH/MIB: PASS
Remote detector at 0.25 s: EXPECTED_CELL_PROVEN
Aligned RF windows with CSI PCI1: 57
RF PBCH CRC OK windows: 0
```

Gain `41` is not the missing piece. Across clean paired runs at gains `43`,
`42`, and `41`, the RF side can see expected PCI but cannot decode PBCH/MIB.
The next single-variable branch should stop reducing gain and instead test
receive bandwidth or frequency/offset behavior while keeping the paired TX dump
requirement.
