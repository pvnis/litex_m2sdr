## Interpretation

Widening bladeRF analog bandwidth to `8 MHz` did not recover PBCH and looks
worse than CP835 at the same gain:

```text
CP835 BW5.6M: EXPECTED_CELL_PROVEN at 0.25 s, CSI PCI1 windows 57
CP836 BW8.0M: only EXPECTED_CELL_SEEN_LOW_MARGIN at 0.50 s, CSI PCI1 windows 35
RF PBCH CRC OK windows: 0 in both
```

The next branch should not keep widening bandwidth. A better low-cost step is
to analyze existing captures with explicit SSB frequency offsets, because the
detector often reports strongest expected PCI around a `-12.5 kHz` CFO.
