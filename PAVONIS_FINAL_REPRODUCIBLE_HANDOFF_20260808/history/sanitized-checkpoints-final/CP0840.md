I/Q transforms. CP840 runs the existing Pavonis parallel PBCH scanner on the
clean CP835 RF capture. This scanner sweeps frequency shifts and both SSB

## Interpretation

The RF PBCH failure is not recovered by:

```text
SSB pattern A vs C
Frequency shifts from -45 kHz to +45 kHz
One-second full-rate decode
```

This reinforces the current boundary: the TX ring carries decodable PBCH, the
RF capture preserves some PCI/SSB structure, but the PBCH payload over RF is
not decodable by the available local PBCH decoder under the tested corrections.
