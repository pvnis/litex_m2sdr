## Interpretation

This is a clean negative result for the 11.52 Msps bladeRF SSB-centered
receive geometry. It did not recover PBCH. It also degraded expected PCI
dominance relative to the stronger 5.76 Msps / 5.6 MHz gain41/gain42 runs,
even though the raw was not clipped and the TX ring was confirmed decodable.

Next work should return to the 5.76 Msps / 5.6 MHz SSB-centered receive
geometry unless a specific hypothesis requires changing it.
