## Interpretation

This does not reproduce the old known-good PBCH baseline. The TX side is good,
but bladeRF gain 20 is currently far under the useful receive level for this
path. It is not a clean proof that `tx_gain_backoff=10` is bad; it proves that
`tx_gain_backoff=10` with bladeRF gain 20 is under-level in the current setup.

Next step should keep `tx_gain_backoff=10` and raise bladeRF gain toward the
same ADC range as the better gain41/gain42 backoff20 captures, while rejecting
any run that clips or has a bad TX dump.
