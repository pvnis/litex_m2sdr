## Interpretation

This run reached a reasonable ADC level without clipping and still did not
recover expected PCI or PBCH. The `tx_gain_backoff=10` branch is therefore not
a simple recovery path in the current hardware state.

The best current receive evidence remains the `tx_backoff=20`, 5.76 Msps,
5.6 MHz BW, gain41/gain42 region: those runs at least showed expected PCI and
many CSI PCI1 windows, although PBCH stayed KO.
