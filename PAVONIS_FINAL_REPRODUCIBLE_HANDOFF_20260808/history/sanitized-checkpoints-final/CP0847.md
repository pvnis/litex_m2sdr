## Note On Initial Nonzero Harness RC

The first local analysis used a 2 kHz tone-error threshold and returned
nonzero because the measured tone was offset by about +2.185 kHz. That is too
strict for this smoke test. The offline analyzer was adjusted to a 5 kHz
threshold and rerun on the same raw capture only; no RF was rerun for the
reanalysis.

## Interpretation

The LiteX-to-bladeRF RF path can carry a simple narrowband tone at high SNR,
with no clipping and a plausible small frequency offset. Therefore the current
PBCH failure is not explained by a completely dead LiteX RF egress path or a
completely dead bladeRF receive path.

This does not prove the wideband OFDM/PBCH path is clean. It narrows the
remaining failure toward modulation-quality/channel distortion/timing/CFO
effects or another impairment that affects PBCH decoding while still allowing
PSS/SSS-like structure and a simple tone to pass.
