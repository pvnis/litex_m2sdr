## Interpretation

This is a clean negative result. A known decoder-positive PBCH vector fails after LiteX RF replay and bladeRF RF capture, even though CP847 showed the same LiteX-to-bladeRF RF path can carry a narrowband tone at high SNR.

That makes a pure live-gNB/streaming-timing explanation less likely. The current failure class is more consistent with a wideband/modulation-quality/RF-chain impairment, replay-format impairment, frequency-plan mismatch, or capture/decode assumption mismatch that preserves simple tone visibility but destroys or hides SSB/PBCH structure.

The next useful ladder step is to prove whether `test_play.py` replay itself preserves the known CF32 vector at the LiteX TX output, using a LiteX RX loopback/capture or a bladeRF capture at alternative centers/bandwidths/gains before returning to live gNB tests.
