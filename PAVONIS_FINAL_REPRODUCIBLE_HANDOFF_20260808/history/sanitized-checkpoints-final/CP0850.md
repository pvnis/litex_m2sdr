## Interpretation

Capturing at the DL center with the original sample rate did not recover PBCH. That weakens the theory that CP848 failed only because the bladeRF capture was SSB-centered or because the SSB offset was mishandled by capture geometry.

Together with CP849, the current state is:

- `test_play.py` can replay CF32 tones through LiteX into bladeRF.
- A known decoder-positive PBCH CF32 vector does not survive LiteX RF replay into bladeRF as decodable PBCH.
- The failure persists for both SSB-centered `5.76 Msps` capture and DL-centered `11.52 Msps` capture.

The next useful variable is not basic file replay or capture geometry. It is either PBCH waveform scaling/filtering through the LiteX TX chain, RF modulation quality over occupied bandwidth, or a decode assumption that differs between local CF32 and RF-captured SC16.
