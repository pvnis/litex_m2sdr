## Interpretation

bladeRF still does not cleanly correlate with a centered SSB-width probe. This rules out the idea that CP852 failed only because the waveform was placed at `-2.85 MHz` inside an `11.52 Msps` capture.

Combined state:

- CP849: bladeRF receives a narrowband CF32 tone at high SNR.
- CP852: bladeRF does not cleanly correlate with an SSB-offset wideband probe.
- CP853: LiteX RX does cleanly correlate with the same SSB-offset wideband probe in `none`, `phy`, and `ad9361` loopback modes.
- CP854: bladeRF still does not cleanly correlate with a centered wideband probe.

The remaining boundary is bladeRF/physical receive behavior for wideband waveforms. The path can pass narrowband energy, but wideband modulation is not preserved well enough for correlation or PBCH decode.
