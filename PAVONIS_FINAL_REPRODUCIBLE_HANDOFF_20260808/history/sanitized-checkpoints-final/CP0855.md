## Interpretation

Increasing replay amplitude by `2x` raised bladeRF capture RMS from about `-24.85 dBFS` in CP854 to about `-23.26 dBFS`, with no clipping, but it did not materially improve reference correlation. The best long-template score remained near random-search levels (`0.028`).
