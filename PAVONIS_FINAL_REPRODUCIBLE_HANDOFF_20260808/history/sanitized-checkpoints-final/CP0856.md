## Interpretation

At bladeRF gain42, the no-TX noise floor is already around `-25.66 dBFS`. CP854's centered wideband OFDM capture at `AMPL=1.0` was only about `0.8 dB` above this, and CP855 at `AMPL=2.0` was only about `2.4 dB` above this. That explains why wideband reference correlation remains weak: the bladeRF capture is dominated by its wideband noise floor.

The tone baseline can still pass because a narrowband tone concentrates energy into one FFT bin, so it can have high spectral SNR even when total capture RMS is similar to the no-TX noise floor.

Current boundary: LiteX can generate and internally recover the SSB-width waveform, but the bladeRF physical/capture path receives that waveform at very low wideband SNR. Improving physical coupling/cabling/antenna placement, reducing path loss, or changing bladeRF receive configuration is now more important than further gNB/PBCH software debugging.
