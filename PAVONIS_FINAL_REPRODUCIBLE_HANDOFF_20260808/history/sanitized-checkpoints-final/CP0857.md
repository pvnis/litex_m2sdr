## Interpretation

Changing bladeRF gain does not recover the wideband waveform. TX-on RMS rises relative to no-TX RMS at every gain, but the estimated signal SNR remains negative and reference correlation stays near random-search levels.

The best observed correlation score was only `0.02798` at gain42, while LiteX-only CP853 saw `0.62-0.67` for the same family of wideband probe. Gain50 increases total RMS substantially, but it increases noise with it and does not improve correlation.

This reinforces the CP856 boundary: bladeRF is seeing only a weak wideband signal above its own noise floor. The RF path is good enough for narrowband tone detection, but not good enough for a 240-subcarrier wideband waveform or PBCH decode.
