## Interpretation

Narrowing the OFDM-like waveform from 240 subcarriers to 24 subcarriers improves the best correlation score compared with the 240-subcarrier long-template cases, but only to `0.0566`. That is still far from a usable copy of the transmitted reference, and far below the LiteX-only CP853 correlations (`0.62-0.67`).

This means the bladeRF physical/capture path is not merely failing at full SSB bandwidth. It still fails to preserve a 360 kHz modulated waveform at the current coupling and gain settings, even though a single narrowband tone is detectable.

The remaining useful bandwidth-threshold question is now below 24 subcarriers: either test a very small OFDM probe, such as 4 subcarriers, or move directly to improving physical coupling/receive path before spending more RF time.
