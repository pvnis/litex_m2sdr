## Interpretation

The 4-subcarrier probe improves correlation compared with the 24-subcarrier CP858 result:

- CP854, 240 subcarriers: about `0.028` long-template best
- CP858, 24 subcarriers: `0.0566` best
- CP859, 4 subcarriers: `0.1192` best
- CP847, single continuous tone: high-SNR PASS

This is a useful monotonic trend: narrower occupied bandwidth helps, but even `60 kHz` of modulated bandwidth is still not recovered as a clean waveform through the bladeRF path. The threshold is somewhere below a few-subcarrier modulated waveform or depends on the tone being phase-continuous and spectrally concentrated.

This reinforces the physical/coupling/SNR boundary. Further software-side PBCH debugging is not productive until the bladeRF path can recover at least a narrow modulated probe with a much stronger reference-correlation score.
