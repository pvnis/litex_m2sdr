## Interpretation

The one-subcarrier probe is the best bladeRF modulated result so far, but it is still weak:

- CP854, 240 subcarriers: about `0.028` long-template best
- CP858, 24 subcarriers: `0.0566` best
- CP859, 4 subcarriers: `0.1192` best
- CP860, 1 subcarrier: `0.1741` best
- CP847, single continuous tone: high-SNR PASS

The monotonic improvement as occupied bandwidth narrows is real, but even `15 kHz` of modulated waveform does not recover cleanly through the bladeRF receive path. This keeps the current failure boundary at physical/coupling/SNR/bladeRF receive behavior rather than at LiteX CF32 replay or the local reference-correlation scanner.

The next useful isolation step is a fresh LiteX-only RX/TX baseline using the same CP860 probe. If LiteX-only recovers this waveform strongly while bladeRF remains weak, the bladeRF/physical receive path is isolated as the unexplained variable.
