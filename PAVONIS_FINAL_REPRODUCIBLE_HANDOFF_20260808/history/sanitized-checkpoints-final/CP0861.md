## Interpretation

CP861 establishes a fresh baseline: the LiteX SDR can still replay and receive the CP860 one-subcarrier waveform cleanly, repeatably, and without stream errors. The CP860 bladeRF weakness is therefore not caused by the CP860 waveform, the local reference-correlation scanner, LiteX CF32 replay, or the basic LiteX Soapy RX/TX path.

The current unexplained variable is now isolated to the path that differs from CP861: physical RF coupling/cabling/antenna geometry into bladeRF, bladeRF RX frontend/settings/calibration, or an interaction between bladeRF receive behavior and the current RF level. Further PBCH/gNB software debugging remains lower value until the bladeRF receive path can recover at least this one-subcarrier modulated probe with a much stronger score.
