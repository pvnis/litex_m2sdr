## Interpretation

Increasing LiteX replay amplitude raises bladeRF capture level dramatically and improves the weak low-level cases, but correlation plateaus at only about `0.28`:

- `AMPL=0.5`: `0.195`
- `AMPL=1`: `0.249`
- `AMPL=2`: `0.263`
- `AMPL=4`: `0.281`
- `AMPL=8`: `0.282`
- `AMPL=16`: `0.283`

This rejects a simple "not enough RF level" explanation. Estimated SNR rises from about `-2.5 dB` to `27.6 dB`, but the waveform still does not approach the CP861 LiteX-only baseline of `~0.77-0.79`.

The remaining failure looks like a bladeRF receive-path impairment or mismatch rather than only noise bandwidth or RF level: possible areas are I/Q orientation/calibration, frontend filtering/group delay, DC/LO behavior around the centered probe, physical coupling geometry, or some bladeRF-specific receive configuration. The next lower-rung test should move the probe off exact DC while staying narrow and high-SNR, to check whether bladeRF DC/LO behavior is corrupting the centered one-subcarrier waveform.
