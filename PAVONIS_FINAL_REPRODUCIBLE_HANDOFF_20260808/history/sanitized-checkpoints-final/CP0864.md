## Interpretation

Moving the narrow probe away from exact DC helps, but it does not solve the bladeRF receive-path failure. DC/LO behavior is likely a contributing factor, not the main explanation.

The failure boundary remains in the bladeRF/physical receive path. At this point, the data says:

- LiteX-only RX/TX can recover the waveform cleanly.
- bladeRF wide bandwidth is too noisy, but narrowing bandwidth only improves to `~0.27`.
- large RF level increases plateau near `~0.28`.
- moving the one-subcarrier probe to `+60 kHz` improves only to `~0.31`.

The next productive direction is bladeRF receive configuration/calibration rather than more gNB/PBCH work: I/Q correction/orientation checks, bladeRF DC calibration behavior, alternate bladeRF gain stages/settings, or a known-good cabled/coupled bladeRF RX path if hardware access becomes possible.
