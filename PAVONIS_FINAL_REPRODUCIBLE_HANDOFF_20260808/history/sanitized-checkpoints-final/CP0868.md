## Interpretation

CP868 does not show an obvious stale bladeRF2 setting:

- FIR filter is default/normal.
- RX1 bias tee is off.
- Temperature is not high.
- LMS calibration readback is not applicable to this bladeRF2 device.

The idle `OPEN` RF routing needed one more check because it could simply mean the channel is disabled. CP869 therefore audits RF routing during an active RX stream.
