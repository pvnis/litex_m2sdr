## Interpretation

Moving the one-subcarrier probe from `+60 kHz` to `+495 kHz` did not recover the transmitted symbol sequence.

This weakens the pure near-LO/DC hypothesis. The bladeRF capture again has enough RF energy to be visible and level-valid, but neither waveform correlation nor direct symbol-sequence search finds the transmitted QPSK phase sequence.

The remaining suspect after CP871 is no longer only bladeRF DC behavior. It is either:

- the external RF path is preserving a coherent narrowband component while suppressing phase modulation sidebands,
- the LiteX external file replay path is not transmitting the same phase-modulated samples that internal LiteX RX/TX sees, or
- the bladeRF receive chain is collapsing this phase-modulated single-subcarrier signal into an unrelated coherent component.
