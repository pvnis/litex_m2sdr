## Interpretation

CP878 changes the boundary from a hard "`5 ms` pass / `2 ms` absent" statement to a more nuanced edge region:

- `5 ms`, `4 ms`, `3 ms`, and `2.5 ms` are recoverable in this run with strong scores.
- `2 ms` is still degraded: score `0.659`, phase std `50 deg`.
- The degradation is not a signal-power collapse; `2 ms` has comparable mean magnitude to the other dwells.

Combined with CP876 and CP877:

- CP877 proves the file, LiteX TX, and LiteX RX path can preserve the sequence through `1 ms`.
- CP876 showed bladeRF had a strong break between `5 ms` and `2 ms` on the broader ladder.
- CP878 shows the bladeRF path is near the edge around `2 ms`, not that all sub-5 ms dwells are categorically impossible.

The practical conclusion is still that the bladeRF/physical receive path is the unexplained variable, but the threshold is run/alignment sensitive near `2 ms`. A useful next rung would repeat a narrow bladeRF ladder around `2.5, 2.25, 2.0, 1.75, 1.5 ms` with long-template anchoring from the start.
