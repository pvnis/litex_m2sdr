## Interpretation

CP882 confirms the short-period fast dwell condition is repeatable. The same `0.15 s` probe passed twice, before and after the `0.3 s` CP881 failure.

This strengthens the duration/coherence interpretation:

- bladeRF can recover deterministic phase flips down to `1 ms` when the coherent full-period reference is `0.15 s`.
- doubling the same dwell ladder to a `0.3 s` full-period reference causes poor coherent recovery.
- the failure is not simple clipping, TX underrun, missing signal, or fixed transition bandwidth.

The next useful non-destructive rung is local analysis of the CP881 capture with shorter subperiod windows, to see whether CP881 has local `0.15 s`-scale matches inside the failed `0.3 s` period.
