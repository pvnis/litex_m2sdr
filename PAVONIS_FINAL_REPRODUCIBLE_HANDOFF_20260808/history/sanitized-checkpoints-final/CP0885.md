## Interpretation

Piecewise CFO does not recover one fixed physical CP881 period.

At the quarter-implied start `902850`, windows `q0` and `q2` are strong, but `q1` and `q3` are weak. At the full-period scan start `989250`, all windows are mixed or weak. This means the strong CP884 quarter matches are real local matches, but they are not all from the same physical `0.3 s` period in the capture.

Updated model:

- CP881's `0.3 s` full-period failure is not caused by gross timing modulo error.
- CP884 local quarter matches are distributed across repeated occurrences of the waveform.
- CP885 rules out the simple fix "use per-quarter CFO at one start."

The remaining behavior looks like intermittent/local coherence within the repeated capture: some quarters of some repetitions are excellent, but no complete `0.3 s` repetition is coherent enough for a strong full-period score.

Next useful rung: search over repeated period starts and score every repetition/window explicitly, producing a heatmap of which quarters are coherent in which transmitted period.
