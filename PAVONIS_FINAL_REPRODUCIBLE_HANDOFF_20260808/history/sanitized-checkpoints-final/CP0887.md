Every in-bounds `37.5 ms` fixed-grid window scores above `0.92`. This is a stronger result than CP886: the CP881 data is not generally missing, clipped, or unrecoverable at the physical grid positions.

## Interpretation

The best CFO moves smoothly over capture time, from about `3490 Hz` early to about `3430 Hz` later. A single-CFO full-period analysis therefore smears the long period, while short windows remain highly coherent when each window gets its own local CFO.

This localizes the CP881 failure to time-varying CFO or phase drift across the bladeRF capture. It does not look like a missing-transmit problem, a gross LiteX file-generation issue, or a fixed-grid timing loss. The next local rung is to fit a time-varying CFO correction from these windows and test whether it can recover a full CP881 period at fixed physical starts.
