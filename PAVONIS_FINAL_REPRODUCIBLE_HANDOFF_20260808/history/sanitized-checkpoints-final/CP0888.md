## Interpretation

The fresh LiteX-only baseline passes on the same CP881 waveform that degraded over the LiteX-to-bladeRF path. The waveform, local file transfer, LiteX Soapy TX, LiteX Soapy RX, metadata, and dwell analyzer all recover `3 ms` through `1 ms` phase flips with high scores.

This further isolates the unexplained CP881/CP886 behavior to the bladeRF receive side or the physical LiteX-to-bladeRF RF path. Combined with CP887, the current failure model is not "LiteX can no longer transmit or receive this waveform"; it is "bladeRF captures show time-varying CFO/phase behavior over longer windows even though short fixed windows are locally coherent."
