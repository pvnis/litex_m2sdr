## Interpretation

The old CP847 capture already had weak continuous-phase behavior despite a strong spectral tone. That means CP892's "spectral tone present but coherent model poor" result is not entirely new.

The fresh CP892 capture is worse than the old CP847 capture in several ways:

- frequency offset increased from about `+2.21 kHz` to about `+4.51 kHz`
- window FFT peak drifted across `200 Hz` over `5 s` instead of staying in one `10 ms` FFT bin
- FFT-peak coherent mean fell from `0.553` to `0.109`
- autocorrelation coherence fell from `0.374` to `0.169`

This makes CP892 look like a degradation of a pre-existing limitation rather than a brand-new failure mode. The simple tone harness has always been sufficient for "RF energy and SNR exist" but weak as proof of long coherent phase stability.
