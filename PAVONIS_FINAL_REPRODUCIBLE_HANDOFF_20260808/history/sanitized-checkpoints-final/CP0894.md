## Interpretation

The deterministic file tone behaves essentially like the CP892 live-generated tone:

- strong spectral tone is present
- no clipping or capture truncation
- frequency offset is around `+4.5..+4.9 kHz`
- 10 ms FFT peak tracking drifts slowly by about `-51 Hz/s`
- continuous-phase/autocorrelation behavior remains poor

This removes the CP847 live tone generator as the main explanation. The impairment is present when the transmitted samples come from a loop-continuous CF32 file replayed by the CP872 path.

The current working model is now tighter: the LiteX-to-bladeRF RF path carries energy and a stable spectral tone, but the bladeRF capture is not preserving a clean coherent complex sinusoid over the windows needed by the phase-flip and file replay analyses. The next useful rung is to determine whether this is specific to the bladeRF RX configuration/bandwidth/gain or present across bladeRF receive settings.
