## Interpretation

The `2 MHz` bladeRF bandwidth improves the deterministic tone capture:

- higher tone SNR
- lower amplitude and no clipping
- smaller window FFT drift
- better FFT-peak coherent mean
- better autocorrelation coherence

It does not fix the continuous-phase problem. The FFT-peak coherent mean is still only `0.202`, and the autocorrelation coherence is still only `0.338`. The path remains good enough for spectral tone detection but weak as a coherent complex sinusoid over the windows relevant to CP881/CP891.

This narrows the bladeRF receive-setting theory: bandwidth matters and `2 MHz` is better than `5.6 MHz` for this tone, but bandwidth alone does not explain the full CP881/CP891 phase/coherence failures.
