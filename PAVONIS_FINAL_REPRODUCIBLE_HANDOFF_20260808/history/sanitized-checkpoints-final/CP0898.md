## Interpretation

The current gain-42, `2 MHz` bladeRF state is locally repeatable in broad
spectral terms:

- raw amplitude is nearly unchanged,
- no clipping appears,
- global tone peak remains near `502.5 kHz`,
- window FFT spread remains tens of Hz,
- FFT-peak coherence remains around `0.53-0.55`,
- autocorr coherence remains around `0.404`.

It is still not a coherent receive path in the way CP896 LiteX-only is. The
expected `500 kHz` coherent mean remains near zero, the phase-fit RMS remains far
above `1000 deg`, and the autocorrelation estimate still disagrees by several
kHz.

This makes the immediate state less "random no receive" and more "repeatably
degraded bladeRF/physical-path coherence" at the current gain/BW setting. The
next useful step is not more gain alone; it should target bladeRF receive
coherence directly, such as [interface-redacted]/calibration/reset behavior, RX stream format,
or physical RF path stability.
