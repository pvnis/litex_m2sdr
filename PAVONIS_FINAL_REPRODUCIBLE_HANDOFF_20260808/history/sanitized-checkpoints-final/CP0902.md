## Interpretation

The explicit receive reset/[interface-redacted]/[interface-redacted] sequence did not recover the
bladeRF RF tone path.

It kept the same broad signal class as CP898 and CP899:

- amplitude remained around `0.072` RMS rel2048,
- no clipping appeared,
- tone SNR stayed around `61 dB`,
- the global FFT peak stayed near `502.5 kHz`,
- expected `500 kHz` coherence remained near zero,
- autocorr coherence remained around `0.405`.

The only apparent improvement was FFT-peak phase RMS, down to about `1107 deg`,
but this is still far from the CP896 LiteX-only coherent baseline and did not
move the expected-tone or autocorrelation metrics out of the degraded class.

CP902 therefore makes stale bladeRF RF [interface-redacted] state a weaker explanation. After
CP896 through CP902, the strongest remaining boundary is the bladeRF analog RF
receive path, its correction/calibration state, or the physical path into the
bladeRF.
