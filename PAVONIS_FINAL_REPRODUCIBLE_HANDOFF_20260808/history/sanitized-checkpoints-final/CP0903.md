## Interpretation

CP903 confirms RX1 is the active installed receive path for the deterministic
LiteX tone. RX2 is software-selectable and captures a weak coupled copy, but it
is not a hidden good path.

This refreshes CP775/CP776 on the current deterministic tone:

- generic/RX1 behavior remains the relevant receive path,
- RX2 remains much lower level,
- RX2 does not improve coherence or autocorrelation behavior,
- the bladeRF problem is not explained by using the wrong software RX channel.

After CP896 through CP903, the likely unresolved boundary remains the bladeRF
RX1 analog RF receive path, its correction/calibration behavior, or the physical
path into RX1/RF3(B).
