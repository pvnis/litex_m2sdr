PBCH-decodable through LiteX-only RX/TX. CP918 transforms that CP823 vector

## Interpretation

The CP915 bladeRF capture has high RF energy (`~0.102` RMS rel2048 in the
correlation read), but it does not correlate with a known-good,
same-format SSB-centered PBCH reference.

The best correlation score stays near `0.022`, while the positive control is
`~1.0`. The best frequency bins and normal/conjugate choice also move around
between scans, which is consistent with noise-like accidental maxima rather
than a coherent copy of the transmitted PBCH waveform.

This closes one more ambiguity:

```text
The CP915 failure is not just "ssb_file_test dislikes the bladeRF file."
The CP915 bladeRF file also lacks a coherent correlation match to the
same-format PBCH-positive reference.
```

Together with CP916/CP917:

```text
Known PBCH-positive CP823 vector
  -> transformed SSB-centered reference: PBCH decode PASS
  -> LiteX-only full RX/TX captures: PBCH decode PASS
  -> CP915 bladeRF external RF capture: PBCH decode FAIL and reference correlation FAIL
```

The first failed rung is still Stage 5 after the bladeRF/external RF receive
boundary. The remaining suspects are the bladeRF normal RF receive chain, the
physical RF path into bladeRF, or bladeRF normal RF correction/calibration
state. Do not move to PRACH yet.
