## Interpretation

CP927 adds a fresh, current-state external-path result after CP925:

```text
CP925 LiteX-only PBCH/vector RX/TX: PASS
CP927 LiteX tone TX -> bladeRF RX: tone present, unclipped, but phase-unstable
```

This rules out a broad current LiteX source/TX issue. The failure remains at or
after the bladeRF external receive boundary:

- not the deterministic tone file;
- not the LiteX-only TX/RX path;
- not the local tone analysis path;
- not simple receive level or clipping.

No calibration or correction mutation was performed. The first failed ladder
rung remains Stage 5 RF receive coherence/PBCH at the bladeRF/external path.
Do not move to PRACH/Stage 6.
