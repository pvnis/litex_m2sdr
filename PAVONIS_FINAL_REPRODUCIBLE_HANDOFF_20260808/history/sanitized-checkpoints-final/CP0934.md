# Checkpoint 934 - Low RX gain external tone still phase-bursts

## Purpose

CP930 and CP933 showed a repeatable time-dependent phase-burst failure in the
external LiteX TX to bladeRF RX tone path at bladeRF RX gain `42 dB`.

CP934 repeats the same four-second external tone test with bladeRF RX gain
lowered to `30 dB`:

```text
LiteX/M2SDR TX deterministic 500 kHz tone -> installed RF path -> bladeRF RX1/RF3(B)
```

This isolates whether the failure is simply caused by the previous receive
gain/level. No bladeRF calibration or correction mutation was performed.

## Interpretation

CP934 rules out the simplest high-gain/high-level explanation:

```text
Gain 42 external tone: strong, unclipped, phase-burst failure.
Gain 30 external tone: lower level, still strong enough, unclipped, worse phase-burst failure.
```

The external bladeRF receive path still enters a sustained large phase-step
state, and at gain `30 dB` the transition starts earlier than in CP930/CP933.
This does not look like straightforward ADC clipping or too much receive gain.

Current boundary:

```text
LiteX-only PBCH/vector CP931: PASS
LiteX-only tone control: PASS
bladeRF RFIC BIST: PASS
LiteX TX -> bladeRF normal external RX: FAIL at gain 42 and gain 30
```

The first failed ladder rung remains Stage 5 RF receive coherence/PBCH at the
bladeRF/external path. Do not move to PRACH/Stage 6.
