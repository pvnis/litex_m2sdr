
tone tests. No bladeRF calibration or correction mutation was performed.

## Interpretation

CP936 rules out a simple "2 MHz RX bandwidth is too narrow for the tone path"
explanation:

```text
2.0 MHz normal RX bandwidth: phase-burst failure
5.6 MHz normal RX bandwidth: phase-burst failure
```

Widening the RX bandwidth increased captured level and changed the segment
shape, but it did not recover coherence. The 5.6 MHz run still reached a
`100%` `>=60 deg` event fraction from `2.0 s` onward, and mean coherence
declined below the 2 MHz normal run.

Current boundary:

```text
LiteX-only PBCH/vector CP931: PASS
LiteX-only tone control: PASS
bladeRF RFIC BIST: PASS
LiteX TX -> bladeRF external RX: FAIL at 2 MHz and 5.6 MHz RX bandwidth
```

The first failed ladder rung remains Stage 5 RF receive coherence/PBCH at the
bladeRF/external path. Do not move to PRACH/Stage 6.
