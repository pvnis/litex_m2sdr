
It also had one implausible transient hardware print:

```text

```

This did not persist. The same dec4 capture log later reported `36.0 degrees C`,
the final process check reported `33.3 degrees C`, and an additional read-only
post-check at `[date-redacted]` reported `32.5 degrees C` with idle routes
open. Treat the `199.1 C` value as a bogus/transient readback associated with
the dec4 mode, not as evidence of sustained overheating.

## Interpretation

external receive setting:

```text
normal external tone: failed, 262/399 adjacent steps >=60 deg
dec2 external tone:   improved, 14/399 adjacent steps >=60 deg
dec4 external tone:   not clean, CP892 WARN and unstable tone estimate
```

`dec2` is still not a full pass. It remains far worse than the LiteX-only tone
control and RFIC BIST controls:

```text

RFIC BIST:       0 events, numerical-zero steps
dec2 external:  14 events, max step 76.520 deg
```

But compared with every previous normal external-path run, `dec2` is a real
improvement and should be the next RF receive setting to test against the
actual Stage 5 PBCH replay.

Current boundary:

```text

bladeRF RFIC BIST: PASS
LiteX TX -> bladeRF normal external RX: FAIL
```

The first failed ladder rung remains Stage 5 RF receive coherence/PBCH at the
bladeRF/external path. Do not move to PRACH/Stage 6. The next ladder step should
be a Stage 5 PBCH replay/capture using bladeRF RX filter `dec2`.
