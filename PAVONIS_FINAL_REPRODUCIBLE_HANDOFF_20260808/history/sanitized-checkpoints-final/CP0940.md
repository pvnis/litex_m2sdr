## Interpretation

CP940 removes the main CP939 caveat. The `dec2` RX FIR mode that materially
improved the simple external tone path did not recover Stage 5 PBCH reception
when given enough bandwidth for the SSB/PBCH signal.

Current boundary:

```text
LiteX-only PBCH/vector path: PASS
bladeRF internal RFIC BIST: PASS
LiteX TX -> bladeRF external tone: improved under dec2 but not clean
LiteX TX -> bladeRF external PBCH: FAIL under normal and dec2
```

The first failed ladder rung remains Stage 5 RF receive coherence/PBCH at the
bladeRF/external path. The next useful step is not PRACH/Stage 6; it should
stay on Stage 5 and determine why a simple tone can be partly recovered by
`dec2` while the structured SSB/PBCH waveform has no strong reference
correlation.
