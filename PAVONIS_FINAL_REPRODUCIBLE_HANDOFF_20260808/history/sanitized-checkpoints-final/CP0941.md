or the default first-2M SSB-reference correlation. CP941 is a local-only
follow-up on the same CP940 bladeRF capture to test two narrower explanations:


## Interpretation

CP941 rules out the simplest analysis-window explanations for CP940. Scanning
three times more capture data and a gross `5 MHz` CFO span did not improve the
match. The best score is still around `0.025`, which is lower than the CP940
default scan and nowhere near the LiteX-only control scores around `0.88` to
`0.96`.

Current conclusion:

```text
CP940 dec2 PBCH failure is not explained by:
  - first-2M scan being too short
  - default +/-100 kHz CFO span being too narrow
  - expected normal/conjugate transform being missed by CP851
```

The next isolation step should remove another variable from the PBCH replay:
instead of transmitting the 11.52 Msps CP823 vector with the SSB off-center in
the transmit passband, replay the already SSB-centered 5.76 Msps reference at
the bladeRF capture center.
