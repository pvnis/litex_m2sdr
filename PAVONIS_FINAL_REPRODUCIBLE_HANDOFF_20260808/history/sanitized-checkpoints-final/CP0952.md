# Checkpoint 952 - External spectrum comparison confirms PBCH at no-TX floor

## Purpose

CP951 showed that x2 centered PBCH still fails through the external bladeRF
route and only reaches a weak `0.034` reference-correlation score. CP952 is a
local-only spectrum comparison to quantify whether the PBCH captures create any
measurable broadband lift above CP946 no-TX.

Inputs compared with identical Welch settings:

```text
CP946 no-TX centered normal capture
CP943 x1 centered PBCH normal capture
CP944 x1 centered PBCH TX_ATT15 normal capture
CP951 x2 centered PBCH normal capture
CP947 500 kHz tone normal capture
```

No RF activity and no device state changes were performed.

python3 cp952_compare_external_spectra.py \

## Interpretation

The PBCH captures are spectrally indistinguishable from no-TX at this level:

```text
x1 normal:       +0.12 dB SSB-band power vs no-TX
x1 TX_ATT15:     +0.10 dB SSB-band power vs no-TX
x2 normal:       +0.13 dB SSB-band power vs no-TX
```

Those are tiny differences and are not enough to support a measurable broadband
PBCH signal above the bladeRF receive floor. The CP947 tone control behaves very
differently: it has a clear 502.4 kHz spectral peak with `39.87 dB` peak SNR
and `+1.54 dB` SSB-band power versus no-TX.

CP952 strengthens the current boundary:

```text
The external route can pass a narrow tone.
The same route does not show meaningful broadband PBCH/SSB energy, even at x2.
```

That points away from decoder-only failure and toward insufficient broadband
PBCH energy at the bladeRF input, a waveform-dependent coupling/path issue, or
a receive-chain behavior that preserves narrow tones but buries wide OFDM-like
signals at the tested settings.
