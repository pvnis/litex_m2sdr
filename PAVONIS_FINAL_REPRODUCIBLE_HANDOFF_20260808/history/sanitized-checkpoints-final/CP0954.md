## Interpretation

The same CP953 waveform that passes the LiteX-only path is not recoverable through the external bladeRF path at `AMPL=1`. The correlation result is at the noise floor, and the best frequency is at the edge of the scan instead of the expected 0 Hz.

The spectrum comparison shows CP954 is not completely identical to no-TX: it raises SSB-band power and median by about `+0.35 dB`, more than the PBCH x1/x2 captures, but still far below a coherent waveform recovery. The CP947 centered 500 kHz tone remains the positive control: it is strongly visible with a `39.87 dB` peak SNR.

Current conclusion: the external route can pass a narrow tone, and the LiteX SDR can internally replay/receive this centered wideband OFDM probe, but the present bladeRF external capture does not preserve enough coherent wideband structure to recover the CP953 waveform.
