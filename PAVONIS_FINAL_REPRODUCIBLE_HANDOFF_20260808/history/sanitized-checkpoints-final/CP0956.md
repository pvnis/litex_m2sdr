## Interpretation

CP956 shows that high-amplitude wideband energy is reaching the bladeRF capture: relative to CP946 no-TX, CP956 raises RMS by `+2.16 dB`, SSB-band power by `+3.77 dB`, and SSB median by `+3.61 dB`, with no clipping estimate.

However, the CP953 waveform is still not coherently recoverable. The best CP851 score is only `0.02733`, which is still floor-like and much closer to CP954's failed `0.02277` than to CP955's internal `0.89..0.93`.

Current conclusion: the CP954 failure was not simply low TX amplitude. The external route can receive added wideband power, but the received content does not preserve the CP953 sequence well enough for waveform correlation. This points away from pure path loss and toward a bandwidth/coherence/receive-chain issue, or a strong unrelated component/noise process that overwhelms structured wideband recovery while still allowing narrow tones to appear.
