## Interpretation

CP958 confirms the external bladeRF path is receiving energy from the 60-subcarrier probe. It raises RMS by `+1.77 dB` and SSB power by `+3.20 dB` versus no-TX, and its strongest spectral peak has `12.31 dB` SNR. This is more structured than the 240SC x4 case, but still far below the CP947 tone's `39.87 dB` peak SNR.

The waveform correlation remains a no-recovery result. The score is above the CP957/no-TX floor (`0.0424` vs `0.0187`), but it is not a clean CP957 match and is nowhere near the LiteX-only `0.936..0.948` internal result.

Current conclusion: narrowing from 240 subcarriers to 60 subcarriers improves spectral visibility and weak correlation above no-TX, but does not restore coherent external recovery. The remaining boundary is between a narrow deterministic tone/one-subcarrier style signal that the bladeRF sees strongly and a multi-subcarrier OFDM sequence that is not preserved coherently enough.
