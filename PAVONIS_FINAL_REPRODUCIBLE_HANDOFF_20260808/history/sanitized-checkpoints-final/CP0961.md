## Interpretation

CP961 passes the LiteX-only x4 gate, but with a caveat: the one-subcarrier sequence has much less diversity than the 12SC/60SC/240SC probes, so side scores are naturally higher. The decisive internal sign is that all three loopbacks choose the expected `normal` transform at `0 Hz`.

This clears the external bladeRF test, but CP962 must include a CP961-reference no-TX floor. The external result should not be judged by absolute score alone.
