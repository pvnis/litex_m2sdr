## Interpretation

The 1SC rung is the first external OFDM-derived sequence in this ladder with a clear normal-transform recovery above no-TX. That isolates several variables:

- LiteX-only RX/TX path still works for the exact CP961 waveform.
- LiteX external TX is not silent; bladeRF RX sees +`4.10 dB` SSB power over no-TX.
- CP851 finds a signal-related normal-transform match about `6x` over the same-reference no-TX floor.

The remaining gap is large: CP962's best score is `0.1960` versus CP961 LiteX-only `0.775..0.784`. The external bladeRF path therefore has enough signal to preserve a one-subcarrier sequence partially, but not enough coherence/SNR for clean recovery at x4.
