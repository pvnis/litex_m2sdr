## Interpretation

CP971 passes the execution and energy checks:

- LiteX TX replay started, completed, and archived.
- bladeRF RX captured the expected number of samples.
- There was no clipping.
- The standard and focused correlation tools both select signal-related matches above noTX.
- Spectrum shows clear energy above noTX and a strong peak near center.

CP971 fails the clean external recovery gate:

- Best external score is only `0.434` even with a short [date-redacted]-sample template.
- Scores degrade steadily as template length increases.
- The best CFO changes with template length.
- The long-template score remains near `0.135`, much weaker than a stable waveform path.

The result is consistent with multi-subcarrier coherence loss in the external path, not with a missing TX waveform. Since CP970 already passed the LiteX-only 4SC x4 path, the next isolation step should refresh a LiteX-only RX/TX baseline in the same session, then descend the subcarrier ladder to a centered 2SC x4 waveform.
