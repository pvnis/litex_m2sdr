## Interpretation

CP976 passes execution and signal-level checks:

- LiteX TX replay started, completed, and archived.
- bladeRF RX captured the expected number of samples.
- The raw capture is stronger than CP974 but still unclipped.
- bladeRF temperature remains normal.
- Focused correlation is far above noTX and close to CP962 1SC x4 over [date-redacted] samples.

CP976 still fails clean external recovery:

- Long-template score is only `0.206`.
- Best CFO varies across template length.
- Transform is not stable across all template lengths.
- Internal x8 LiteX control remains clean, so the weakness is not introduced by the CP973 waveform or LiteX-only path.

The current model is: external received level affects short-window recoverability, while a separate coherence/phase/CFO effect limits long-window recovery for 2SC and wider multi-subcarrier probes.

## Next Step

Before raising amplitude again, the next best checkpoint is a segmented 2SC x8 analysis comparable to CP968:

- Segment CP976 into short windows.
- Track best short-template score, CFO, start, and phase per segment.
- Compare against CP974 x4 and CP966 1SC x16.

That should distinguish a mostly stable signal with slow phase/CFO drift from intermittent local recovery bursts.
