## Interpretation

CP977 shows that CP976 x8 is not a single lucky recovery burst:

- All 29 CP976 x8 chunks are strong: min `0.579932`, median `0.626350`, max `0.664100`.
- CP976 x8 is consistently above CP974 x4: median `0.626350` versus `0.450491`.
- Both external 2SC captures are far above noTX: x8 median `0.626350`, x4 median `0.450491`, noTX median `0.055987`.
- The external x8 score range is relatively tight (`0.58..0.66`), so the short-window signal is present throughout the scanned interval.

The remaining problem is not absence of signal. It is long-window coherence:

- CP976 standard long-template score is only `0.206`.
- CP976 short-template recovery is stable but the best local CFO/start/phase changes by segment.
- The segmented analysis therefore supports a model where short windows are recoverable, while longer windows accumulate enough phase/CFO/timing variation to collapse correlation.

## Next Step

The next best checkpoint is not another blind amplitude increase. The more diagnostic step is to estimate and compensate per-segment CFO/phase on CP976, then test whether stitched or locally corrected windows improve the 32768/65536-template scores.

If correction does not improve long templates, the failure is likely not a simple slow CFO/phase drift and the next rung should move toward receiver-chain I/Q/conjugation or timing-window distortion analysis.
