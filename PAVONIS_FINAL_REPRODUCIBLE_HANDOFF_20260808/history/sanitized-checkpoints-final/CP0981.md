## Interpretation

CP981 makes the CP976/CP980 conclusion repeatable:

- The external path is not dead: short-window recovery is strong and received
  energy is well above noTX.
- The path is not clipping and does not show thermal symptoms.
- The exact x8 2SC external test repeats weak long-template correlation.
- The fresh CP978 LiteX-only baseline remains the control showing that the
  waveform and LiteX RX/TX path can preserve long 2SC coherence.
- The external path repeatedly shows unstable common and differential two-bin
  structure over long windows.

## Verdict

CP981 external repeat execution: PASS.

CP981 signal presence and short-window recovery: PASS.

CP981 long-window external coherence: FAIL, repeatably.

Current first-failed boundary remains the installed external LiteX TX to bladeRF
RX path, now characterized as a repeatable long-window two-subcarrier coherence
failure rather than an intermittent no-receive condition.

## Next Step

The next rung should isolate whether this repeatable instability is in the
bladeRF RX chain or in the installed RF path before bladeRF. The least invasive
options are:

1. Run a temporary/read-back-restored bladeRF receive-chain loopback test if the
   command surface can guarantee restoration of `loopback`, `rx_mux`, and
   filters.
2. Repeat the external test with one bladeRF receive variable at a time
   (`filter`, `bandwidth`, or gain) while keeping CP978-style LiteX guards.

Avoid persistent bladeRF calibration/correction writes unless explicitly
approved.
