## Interpretation

CP982 weakens the overload/high-gain theory:

- Gain reduction produced the expected level reduction.
- Capture remained clean and unclipped.
- Short-window signal remained present.
- Long-window coherence did not recover.
- Two-subcarrier instability remained visible.

This does not prove no receive gain setting can ever matter, but it rules out
the simple explanation that `42 dB` gain was causing the CP976/CP981 failure.

## Verdict

CP982 lower-gain execution: PASS.

CP982 signal presence and short-window recovery: PASS.

CP982 long-window coherence recovery: FAIL.

Current boundary remains the external LiteX TX -> bladeRF RX path, but the
failure is not fixed by lowering bladeRF RX gain from `42 dB` to `36 dB`.

## Next Step

The next one-variable external receive rung should test bladeRF RX FIR mode or
bandwidth. Since gain reduction did not recover coherence and RFIC BIST tone was
previously clean, a reversible `CAP_FILTER=bypass` or bandwidth sweep is the
next low-risk way to separate bladeRF digital filtering from the installed RF
path.
