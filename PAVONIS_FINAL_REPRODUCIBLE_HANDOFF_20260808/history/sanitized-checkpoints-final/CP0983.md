## Interpretation

CP983 separates level from coherence:

- RX FIR bypass increases signal level and short-template score.
- It does not restore 32768/65536 coherence.
- The two-subcarrier instability seen in CP980/CP981/CP982 remains.

This weakens the theory that the bladeRF normal RX FIR alone causes the failure.
The normal FIR may cost some margin, but the main external-path coherence
problem remains after bypass.

## Verdict

CP983 RX FIR bypass execution: PASS.

Signal level and short-window recovery: IMPROVED.

Long-window coherence recovery: FAIL.

Current boundary remains the external LiteX TX -> bladeRF RX path. The failure
is not fixed by lower bladeRF RX gain and not fixed by bypassing the bladeRF RX
FIR.

## Next Step

The next low-risk external receive variable is bandwidth. Since `5.6 MHz`
bandwidth with both normal and bypass FIR still fails, test a narrower bandwidth
that comfortably contains the two occupied subcarriers, then re-check long
correlation and two-subcarrier phase stability. Keep the CP978 LiteX baseline as
the software/RXTX control and avoid persistent bladeRF calibration writes.
