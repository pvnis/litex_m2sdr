## Interpretation

CP986 narrows the result:

- 200 kHz bandwidth gives the best external short and long scores in this
  receive-settings ladder.
- The improvement is still incremental, not a recovery.
- The clean CP985 LiteX-only baseline rules out a simultaneous LiteX replay or
  source-path regression.
- The external path remains coherent enough for strong [date-redacted]-sample matches but
  not coherent enough for stable 32768/65536-sample recovery.

This weakens a simple "bandwidth was too wide" theory. Receive bandwidth is a
margin variable, but not the root fix by itself.

## Verdict
