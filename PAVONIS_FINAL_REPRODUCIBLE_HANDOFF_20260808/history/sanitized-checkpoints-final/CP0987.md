## Interpretation

CP987 gives a useful but narrow answer:

- Combining `bypass` with `200 kHz` raises signal level and gives the best
  external focused scores so far.
- The improvement is very small relative to CP986 and is not a recovery.
- PBCH remains KO across all aligned windows.
- Long-window scores remain far below the CP985 LiteX-only control.
- The two-subcarrier phase/amplitude instability remains.

The failure is therefore not fixed by receive bandwidth, RX FIR bypass, or their
combination. These settings improve receive margin, but the installed external
LiteX TX -> bladeRF RX path still loses long-window coherence.

## Verdict

CP987 combined BW200K + RX FIR bypass execution: PASS.

Signal presence and short-window recovery: PASS, best external margin so far.

Long-window coherence recovery: FAIL.

Current first-failed boundary remains the installed external path as observed by
bladeRF RX, not the LiteX-only source/RXTX path.
