## Interpretation

CP988 weakens the high-level/overload explanation for CP987:

- Lowering gain reduced capture level as intended.
- Digital clipping remained absent.
- Focused long-window scores got worse, not better.
- The two-subcarrier instability persisted.

The current best external receive setting remains CP987 (`BW200K`, `bypass`,
G42), but its advantage over CP986 is small enough that repeatability should be
checked before building new assumptions on it.

## Verdict

CP988 lower-gain combined-setting execution: PASS.

Lower-level capture: PASS.

Long-window coherence recovery: FAIL.

Current first-failed boundary remains the installed external LiteX TX ->
bladeRF RX path as observed by bladeRF RX.

or just run-to-run variation. If repeatable, then move to transmit-side
