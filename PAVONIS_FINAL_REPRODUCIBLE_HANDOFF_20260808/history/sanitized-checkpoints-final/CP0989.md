## Interpretation

CP989 is a repeatability checkpoint, not a recovery:

- The CP920 external run completed cleanly and cleanup succeeded.
- Signal is present and unclipped.
- PBCH remains KO across all aligned windows.
- Short-window correlation stays high enough to prove the waveform is visible.
- Long-window correlation remains far below CP985 LiteX-only RX/TX.
- CP987's small margin over CP986 is not robust enough to build a separate
  conclusion on; the reliable conclusion is the repeated external-path
  short-window/long-window split.

This keeps the first-failed boundary at the installed external LiteX TX ->
bladeRF RX path as observed by bladeRF RX. CP985 already showed the same LiteX
waveform and LiteX RX/TX path can recover long coherence, so CP989 does not
move suspicion back to the generated waveform or LiteX replay source.

## Verdict

CP989 repeat execution: PASS.

External signal presence and short-window recovery: PASS.

Long-window coherence recovery: FAIL.

CP987 receive-setting advantage repeatability: PARTIAL/WEAK. The failure mode
repeats, but the exact small score ranking among CP986/CP987/CP989 is run-to-run
level.

## Next Step

Move to a transmit-side linearity rung while keeping the best practical receive
settings fixed:

- keep `CAP_BW_HZ=200000`
- keep `CAP_FILTER=bypass`
- keep `CAP_GAIN_DB=42`
- reduce TX scale from `AMPL=8` to `AMPL=4`

This tests whether external-path long-coherence loss is being driven by
LiteX-side output level, PA/mixer compression, or another amplitude-dependent
effect before moving to larger hardware-path theories.
