## Interpretation

CP994 separates two facts:

- CP993 proved no-stream Soapy `ATT` updates AD9361 attenuation registers.
- CP994 shows the streaming RF replay level does not follow the large `ATT40`
  request.

The most likely next problem is not "what ATT value should we try"; it is that
the current CP848/CP920 replay path needs streaming-time instrumentation. The
register state should be captured immediately before and while `test_play.py`
is streaming to prove whether the registers still hold `ATT40` during the RF
burst.

For the external coherence goal, CP994 is not a recovery. PBCH remains KO and
long-window correlation remains far below CP985 LiteX-only.
