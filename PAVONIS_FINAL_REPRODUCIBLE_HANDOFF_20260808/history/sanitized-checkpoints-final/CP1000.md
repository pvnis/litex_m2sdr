## Verdict

CP1000 active `ATT22`: control path PASS, receive recovery FAIL.

PBCH decode or long-window coherence over `ATT20/26`.

## Next Step

The corrected attenuation sweep now covers active `ATT20`, `ATT22`, `ATT26`, and
`ATT40`. The best next ladder rung should pivot away from level and back to the
external path behavior itself: bladeRF capture phase/CFO stability, image or
sideband asymmetry, or a repeated no-configuration-change capture pair to measure
run-to-run phase drift under active `ATT22` or active `ATT20`.
