# Checkpoint 1003 - Active ATT22 Piecewise CFO/Phase No Long-Coherence Recovery

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1003 continues after the fresh CP1002 LiteX-only RX/TX baseline pass, without
transmitting more RF.

The goal is to test whether the corrected active-`ATT22` external bladeRF
captures from CP1000 and CP1001 can be rescued by a simple piecewise CFO/phase
model. The same diagnostic is also run on the current CP1002 LiteX-only `phy`
capture as a bladeRF-independent control.

## Method

Script: `cp979_piecewise_cfo_phase_long_coherence.py`

For each capture, the script:

1. Finds per-chunk [date-redacted]-sample short-template hits over a CFO grid.
2. Builds `cfo_only`, `phase_only`, and `cfo_phase` corrected capture variants.
3. Re-runs global 32768/65536 long-template correlation on raw and corrected
   captures.
4. Checks whether strong [date-redacted] hits extend to 32768/65536 at the same local start
   using a fixed-start CFO sweep.

No new RF was transmitted for this checkpoint.

## Interpretation

CP1003 narrows the active-`ATT22` failure:

- CP1000 and CP1001 have stable gross level and strong short-window matches.
- Their long windows remain weak after piecewise CFO-only, phase-only, and
  CFO-plus-phase corrections.
- The current CP1002 LiteX-only `phy` control has strong raw long coherence and
  high same-start extension maxima.
- Therefore the corrected active-`ATT22` external failure is not explained by a
  simple per-chunk CFO/phase offset, and not explained by a broad LiteX source
  regression.

The likely class remains an external receive-path impairment that preserves
local short-template energy while destroying longer coherent structure:
time-varying distortion, fractional-delay/timing instability, I/Q or image
asymmetry, frequency-selective distortion, or bladeRF-specific receive behavior.

## Verdict

CP1000/CP1001 active `ATT22` piecewise CFO/phase recovery: FAIL.

CP1002 current LiteX-only control in the same diagnostic: PASS.

First-failed boundary remains the external LiteX TX -> bladeRF RX observation
path.
