## Purpose

CP1005 is a single RF rung after CP1004's image/mirror finding.

CP1000/CP1001 used active LiteX `ATT22`, bladeRF RX bandwidth `200000`, RX gain
`42`, and bladeRF FIR `bypass`. CP1005 keeps the corrected post-setup/
post-activate LiteX attenuation ordering and all other RF settings fixed, but
switches bladeRF RX FIR back to `normal`.

The goal is to test whether the elevated mirror-bin leakage and long-coherence
failure are specific to the bladeRF `bypass` FIR state.

## Verdict

Active `ATT22`, bladeRF `BW200K`, FIR `normal`: decode recovery FAIL.

Long-coherence recovery: FAIL.

Mirror-leakage recovery: FAIL.

Changing bladeRF FIR from `bypass` to `normal` changes the received level but
does not change the underlying failure class. The issue is therefore not simply
the bypass FIR state.

## Next Step

The next RF rung should keep active `ATT22` and the corrected LiteX gain order,
but change a different bladeRF receive variable. The most direct remaining
control is RX bandwidth away from the 200 kHz minimum, because CP1005 showed that
FIR state alone does not remove either the long-coherence collapse or the
non-DC mirror leakage.
