## Verdict

Active `ATT22`, bladeRF `BW2M`, FIR `bypass`: decode recovery FAIL.

Long-coherence recovery: FAIL.

Mirror-leakage recovery: FAIL.

The 200 kHz minimum bandwidth setting is not the root cause. The external path
failure persists at `2 MHz` bandwidth with healthy capture level, no clipping,
correct LiteX active attenuation, and clean cleanup.

## Next Step

The bandwidth rung now has `200 kHz` and `2 MHz` bypass points under corrected
active `ATT22`. A full-rate/near-full RX bandwidth point (`5.6 MHz`) would finish
the bandwidth sweep, but the trend already argues that bandwidth alone is not
the fix. If prioritizing root cause over completeness, the next stronger rung is
a corrected active-`ATT22` RX gain check, because mirror leakage and phase
instability could still be gain-linearity dependent without visible clipping.
