## Verdict

Active `ATT22`, bladeRF `BW200K`, FIR `bypass`, RX gain `36`: decode recovery
FAIL.

Long-coherence recovery: FAIL.

Mirror-leakage recovery: FAIL.

Lower RX gain improves one long metric modestly, but does not change the failure
class. The issue is not simple visible clipping or a single high-gain overload
point.

## Next Step

The gain rung suggests there may be some gain-dependent margin, but no recovery
at `36 dB`. A lower gain point such as `30 dB` could test whether the improvement
continues, but the more efficient path may be to change the receive-side device
condition rather than sweep gain indefinitely: for example full bandwidth
(`5.6 MHz`) as the last bandwidth endpoint, or a no-TX/known-noise image-floor
capture to quantify how much of the mirror metric is signal-induced versus
bladeRF receive-chain baseline.
