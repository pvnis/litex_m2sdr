## Verdict

Active `ATT22`, bladeRF `BW200K`, FIR `bypass`, RX gain `30`: decode recovery
FAIL.

Long-coherence recovery: FAIL.

Mirror-leakage recovery: FAIL.

The gain trend is not monotonic. CP1007 at `36 dB` was only a modest local
improvement; CP1009 at `30 dB` falls back toward the CP1000/CP1001 band while
remaining cleanly below clipping. This rules out simple bladeRF RX gain
overload as the primary cause.
