## Verdict

No-TX bladeRF receive floor at BW `200000`, FIR `bypass`, gain `42`: PASS as a
floor baseline.

This rules out the active external captures being pure no-TX noise. Active TX
raises capture level, occupied/guard structure, and long-template correlation
above the no-TX floor. The remaining failure is coherence preservation through
the external LiteX TX to bladeRF RX path, not silence.

## Next Step

Stop gain and bandwidth stepping for now. The evidence points to an external
receive observation problem with image/mirror leakage and phase instability. A
good next checkpoint is a receive-only/device-state comparison, such as no-TX
at full `5.6 MHz` and/or a bladeRF RX channel/routing audit, before sending
more TX.
