## Verdict

No-TX bladeRF receive floor at BW `5600000`, FIR `bypass`, gain `42`: PASS as a
floor baseline.

CP1012 rules out CP1010 being pure full-BW no-TX noise. The active full-BW
capture is above the matching receive floor in level and long-template
correlation. The unresolved failure remains coherence preservation through the
external LiteX TX to bladeRF RX observation path.
