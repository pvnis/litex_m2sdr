## Interpretation

CP1015 confirms:

- LiteX TX can still put a deterministic known tone into the installed external
  path.
- bladeRF RX1 still sees that tone at the expected strong level.
- bladeRF RX2 remains much lower and is not the missing good path.
- The active tone path is repeatable after the no-TX floor and routing audits.

This isolates the current failure more sharply. The system can pass a simple
known-tone external-path test, but the centered two-subcarrier/PBCH-style
captures still lose long-window coherence and do not decode. That points away
from total silence, wrong RX channel, or a broad LiteX TX failure, and toward a
modulated-signal coherence/phase/image issue in the external observation path.

## Verdict

Deterministic LiteX tone to bladeRF explicit RX1/RX2 repeatability: PASS.

This is not a PBCH/OFDM recovery, but it proves the active external path is
still capable of carrying a strong deterministic known tone.
