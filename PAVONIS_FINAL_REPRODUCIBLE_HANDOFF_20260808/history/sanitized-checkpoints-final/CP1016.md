## Interpretation

CP1016 confirms that the centered two-subcarrier external failure survives the
new controls:

- CP1013 showed the LiteX source/RX harness can still carry this exact
  centered 2SC x8 waveform cleanly.
- CP1014 showed generic bladeRF `rx` behaves like explicit RX1 and routes to
  `RF3(B)` during active RX.
- CP1015 showed the installed external path can still carry a deterministic
  known tone into bladeRF RX1 repeatably.
- CP1016 shows that the modulated/coherent centered 2SC x8 waveform still does
  not preserve enough long-window coherence or image/phase cleanliness to
  decode through the external bladeRF observation path.

This rules against the simple explanations now bracketed by the controls:
total LiteX TX failure, wrong bladeRF RX channel, pure no-TX floor, basic route
fault, and total installed-path silence. The unresolved boundary remains the
external observation path's behavior on this waveform class.
