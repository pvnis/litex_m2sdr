## Interpretation

CP1019 separates the failure:

- Two close-in stationary carriers are not enough to reproduce the severe
  CP1016 long-window collapse.
- Removing symbol-to-symbol random modulation improves 32768/65536 recovery by
  about `2x` to `3x` versus CP1016.
- The external stationary two-tone still remains far below the CP1018 LiteX-only
  `phy` baseline, despite strong unclipped capture level.
- The selected frequency offsets move to the `2 kHz` / `17 kHz` family rather
  than staying cleanly at `0 Hz`, so the external observation still has a
  residual coherence/phase issue.

This points to a combination: modulation-dependent coherence loss is real, but
there is also a more general external-path coherence or close-in phase problem.

## Verdict

Stationary two-tone bins `-1,0` external bladeRF capture: PARTIAL PASS as a
diagnostic.

It does not decode, and decode is not expected. It does partially recover
long-template coherence compared with CP1016, proving the CP1016 failure is not
explained by "two carriers exist" alone.

## Next Step

Run a near-DC single-tone external check at `-15000 Hz` using the CP903 explicit
RX1/RX2 tone path. CP1015 proved a `500 kHz` deterministic tone is repeatable;
CP1019 shows close-in stationary two-tone is only partially coherent. A
single `-15 kHz` tone isolates whether the remaining issue starts with
near-DC tone observation or only appears when the DC and `-15 kHz` components
are combined.
