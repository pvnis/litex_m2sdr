- CP1022 LiteX-only no-DC two-tone: `phy` score `0.986167558` at 32768, pass.
- CP1023 external no-DC two-tone: score `0.300295371` at 32768 and `0.144011364` at 65536, fail.

This is a regression relative to CP1019 and much worse than CP1022. Removing DC did not restore the external path. The failure now looks broader than "DC tone interaction"; it looks like the bladeRF/external chain is seeing energy but not preserving a stable complex waveform for close-in multi-tone content.

## Verdict

FAIL for the external bladeRF capture path.

The CP1022 LiteX-only RX/TX source baseline passed immediately before this. CP1023 used the same vector and clean harness execution, but the external bladeRF capture lost long-window coherence. This isolates the current unexplained receive behavior to the external receive/RF path or bladeRF capture chain, not to the LiteX source vector generation.
