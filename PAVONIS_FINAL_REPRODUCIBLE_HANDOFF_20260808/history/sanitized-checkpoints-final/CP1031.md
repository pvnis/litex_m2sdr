## Interpretation

CP1031 confirms the external-path issue is repeatable:

- The run was clean end to end with rc `0`.
- TX replay was clean and used the same LiteX-proven vector.
- Received power stayed strong and unclipped.
- Focused short-window correlation again reached about `0.79`, then collapsed over longer windows.
- Non-symmetric image/mirror analysis again rejects a simple image/conjugate-swap explanation.
- The CP1030 `200.0 degrees C` hardware-status anomaly did not repeat, so it is likely a transient status-read anomaly rather than the primary cause.

## Verdict

The current best diagnosis is a repeatable external bladeRF/generic-path coherence failure, not a LiteX-only SDR/software failure and not a simple weak-signal, clipping, image-swap, or antenna-overheating explanation. The next useful rung is a tone-stability analysis over time: measure per-block power and phase of the `-1,+2` tones in CP1030/CP1031 to determine whether the tones drift, hop, beat, or intermittently collapse inside the bladeRF capture.
