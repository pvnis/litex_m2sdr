## Interpretation

CP1032 narrows the failure:

- Not weak signal: occupied tones remain above guard.
- Not clipping: CP1030/CP1031 raw captures have no clip evidence.
- Not image/conjugate swap: mirror scores remain low.
- Not simple tone imbalance: `+2/-1` median tone-ratio is near 0 dB.
- Not proven overheating: CP1031 repeated the failure with normal bladeRF temperature reads.
- The external captures contain locally valid tone structure, but coherence degrades rapidly as windows grow.

This points toward a time-evolution problem in the external receive chain: discontinuities, clock/sample-rate instability, phase jumps, capture-stream behavior, or state/routing issues in the bladeRF/generic RX path.

## Verdict

The most useful next rung is a discontinuity/localization pass over CP1030/CP1031: identify where the high-scoring 4- to 16-symbol islands occur, inspect phase jumps between adjacent symbols, and check whether the collapse follows a periodic cadence, burst boundary, or capture-buffer pattern. That should distinguish smooth drift from sample discontinuities or intermittent receiver-state changes.
