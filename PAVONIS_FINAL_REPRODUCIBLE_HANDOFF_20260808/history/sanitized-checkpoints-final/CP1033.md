## Periodic Island Clue

Several top coherent islands recur at structured symbol/sample intervals:

- `511 * 384 = 196224` samples.
- `192 * 1022 = 196224` samples.
- CP998 replay logs repeatedly show TX `writeStream` returns of `1022` samples.

Examples from CP1033:

- CP1030 long-start top 32-symbol islands include symbol starts `1004`, `1515`, `[date-redacted]`, `2537`, separated by `511` symbols.
- CP1031 long-start top 16-symbol islands include starts `73`, `584`, `1095`, `1606`, also separated by `511` symbols.
- CP1031 short-start top 8-symbol islands include starts `0`, `511`, `1022`, with repeated sample residue modulo `1022`.

The pattern is not perfectly clean across every case, but the recurrence at the least-common-multiple of the waveform period (`384` samples) and observed TX write size (`1022` samples) is too specific to ignore.

## Interpretation

CP1033 strengthens the chunk/buffer-boundary hypothesis:

- The external path is not simply missing the signal.
- It repeatedly contains locally coherent 4- to 16-symbol islands.
- Those islands are separated or clustered in ways consistent with the TX replay chunk cadence.
- Long coherent windows fail because the capture is effectively stitched from local pieces with phase/time discontinuity.

This is a better explanation than antenna overheating or bladeRF image/conjugate confusion. It also explains why received power remains normal while long-window reference correlation fails.
