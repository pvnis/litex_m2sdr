## Interpretation

CP1036 found a harness-control bug: `CHUNK_LEN_SAMPLES` was not propagated into the remote replay helper. Therefore the intended `3072` cadence experiment did not happen.

As an accidental same-1536 repeat, CP1036 still provides useful evidence: the partial-write fix remained active and full TX sample count was restored, but long-window coherence was worse than CP1034. That means a residual non-deterministic or state-dependent impairment remains after the dropped-partial-write bug is removed.

## Verdict

Do not use CP1036 as evidence about `3072`-sample chunk cadence. Patch or wrap the CP920 harness so `CHUNK_LEN_SAMPLES` is forwarded to the remote helper, then rerun the true `3072` split as the next checkpoint.
