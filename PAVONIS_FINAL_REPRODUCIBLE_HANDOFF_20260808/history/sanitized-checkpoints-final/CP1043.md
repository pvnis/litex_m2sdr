## Interpretation

CP1043 isolates the CP1042 PBCH failure away from:

- the PBCH source vector,
- the PBCH waveform itself,
- the LiteX Soapy file TX/RX harness,
- the current LiteX RF/sample-rate/bandwidth programming,
- the ATT22 setting,
- the local LiteX `none`, `phy`, and `ad9361` loopback paths.

The remaining failure domain is the external bladeRF/RF/cabled/capture path, or an interaction that only appears when the external path is inserted.
