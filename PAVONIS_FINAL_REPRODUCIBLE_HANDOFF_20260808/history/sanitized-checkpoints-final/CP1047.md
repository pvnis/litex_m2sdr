## Interpretation

The CP1045 external bladeRF capture is not just lower amplitude. It has high raw RMS (`0.111497410`) relative to the LiteX-only none/ad9361 captures (`0.0046` and `0.0043`), but the waveform does not remain coherent against the known reference:

- CP945 LiteX-only full-period scores are strong where enough capture is available: `0.735` for `phy`, `0.613` for `ad9361`.
- CP1045 external full-period score after the best known `+[date-redacted] Hz` correction is `0.002476`.
- At `32768` samples, CP1045 external reaches the previously observed weak island (`0.091444097`) but CP945 loopbacks are `0.719-0.867` at the same template alignment.
- At `65536` samples, CP1045 external collapses further to `0.043492544` at the template start and maxes at only `0.047852948`, while CP945 remains `0.60-0.93`.

## Conclusion

CP1047 strengthens the boundary:

CP918 centered source local decode: PASS

The failure is best described as external receive-path coherence loss or corruption, not a source waveform problem and not simply insufficient received power. This is consistent with CP1045/CP1046: weak structure exists, but it does not hold together long enough for PBCH.
