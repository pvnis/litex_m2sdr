## Conclusion

The CP1049 G30 first6M correlation island is real weak structure, but it is not a missed PBCH-good window. Lowering bladeRF RX gain to `30 dB` does not recover PBCH; it only moves weak non-decodable structure later in the capture.

Together with CP1048, this closes the simple low-gain branch:

- G42: weak structure, no PBCH CRC OK
- G36: weak structure, no PBCH CRC OK
- G30: late weak structure, targeted decode still no PBCH CRC OK

The current evidence does not support simple bladeRF RX overgain/compression as the primary cause. The external path still loses or corrupts enough waveform coherence that PBCH cannot decode despite known-good source and known-good LiteX-only RX/TX baselines.
