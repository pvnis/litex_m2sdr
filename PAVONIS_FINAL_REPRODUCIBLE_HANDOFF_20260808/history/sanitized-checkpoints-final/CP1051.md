## Conclusion

CP1051 establishes the current G30/bypass no-TX false-positive floor at about `0.026` for the CP918 32,768-sample reference-correlation scan. CP1049's late `0.099973988` island is therefore above the no-TX floor and is likely TX-related weak structure, not pure no-TX noise.

However, CP1050 showed that this TX-related weak structure is not PBCH-decodable. The G30 branch therefore does not recover the external path; it only confirms that the external path still leaks some weak waveform structure while losing enough coherence/SNR for PBCH to fail.
