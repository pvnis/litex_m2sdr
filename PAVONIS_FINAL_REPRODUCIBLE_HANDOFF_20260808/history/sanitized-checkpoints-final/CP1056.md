## Conclusion

CP1056 establishes the matched G36/bypass no-TX false-positive floor:

- raw RMS is almost the same as CP1054, so RMS alone is not discriminative;
- CSI PCI1 windows stay at `0`;
- first-6M correlation stays near `0.0268`, matching the prior no-TX floor;
- CP1054's first-6M score `0.1551` and `104` CSI PCI1 windows are therefore TX-related.

Combined with CP1055, this means the external path is carrying TX-related fragments at G36, but the fragments are still not PBCH-decodable. The current failure remains a coherence/decode integrity issue rather than a simple no-TX false-positive or raw-power issue.
