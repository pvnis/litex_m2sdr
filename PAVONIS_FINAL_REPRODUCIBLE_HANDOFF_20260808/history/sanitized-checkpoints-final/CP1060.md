## Interpretation

The external G42 capture failure is not recovered by simply changing the
`ssb_file_test -F` SSB frequency over the plausible offset neighborhoods. The
`+[date-redacted] Hz` correlation refinement is not equivalent to a successful PBCH
frequency hypothesis, and the older `+11000 Hz` external offset neighborhood
does not restore CSI or PBCH here.

This reinforces the CP1058 conclusion: the strongest late island has weak
SSB-like structure, but not PBCH-decodable integrity.

## Verdict

SSB-frequency offset sweep: FAIL to recover PBCH. Continue debugging the
external LiteX-to-bladeRF waveform/coherence path rather than treating decoder
SSB-frequency selection as the missing step.
