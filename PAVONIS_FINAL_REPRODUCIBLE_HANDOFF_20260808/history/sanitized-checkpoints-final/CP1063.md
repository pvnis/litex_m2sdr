## Interpretation

CP1063 confirms CP1062 was not a one-off. With CP1034 replay and `TX_ATT10`,
the external LiteX-to-bladeRF path repeatedly carries PBCH-decodable fragments.
Full-buffer acquisition remains unstable: CP1063 improved to CSI PCI 1 in the
one-second decode, but PBCH still selected PCI 0 and CRC KO.

The immediate problem has shifted. The external path is no longer "no PBCH at
all"; it is "PBCH appears in short windows, but full-buffer acquisition and
stable timing are not yet robust."

## Verdict

ATT10/G42/CP1034 repeatability: PASS for windowed PBCH recovery, FAIL for
full-buffer PBCH acquisition. Continue by [interface-redacted] decoder/acquisition alignment
around the repeated CRC OK window clusters, not by increasing raw power again.
