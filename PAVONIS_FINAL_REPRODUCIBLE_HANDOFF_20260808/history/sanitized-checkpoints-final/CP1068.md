## Interpretation

CP1068 prevents overfitting CP1063's sample-zero `225-600 ms` success band. A
fixed shorter prefix length is not robust across repeat captures. CP1062 still
requires sliding or offset trimmed windows around its PBCH-positive regions.

The shared conclusion from CP1064 through CP1068 is now:

- external captures contain PBCH-decodable content,
- full one-second acquisition is unreliable,
- fixed sample-zero prefix shortening is not enough,
- a deterministic strategy must scan starts/windows rather than relying on one
  global start and one global length.

## Verdict

CP1068: FAIL for fixed sample-zero prefix recovery on CP1062, PASS as a guard
against choosing a brittle `500 ms from sample zero` workaround. Continue with a
sliding-window acquisition strategy.
