## Interpretation

CP1070 confirms the CP1069 acquisition rule on a fresh RF run. The external
LiteX-to-bladeRF path continues to carry PBCH-decodable content, while the
full-buffer one-second decode remains unreliable.

This is not consistent with a current no-RX, no-TX, or overheating explanation.
The active bladeRF temperature stayed normal, replay TX health was clean, raw
levels were stable and unclipped, and sliding-window PBCH recovery repeated.

## Verdict

CP1070: PASS for the external RF path under the sliding-window PBCH policy;
FAIL only for the known-fragile full one-second decoder path. Continue by
making the sliding-window acquisition path the standard smoke test and by
investigating why the full-buffer candidate selector prefers PCI 0 despite
nearby PBCH CRC OK windows.
