# Checkpoint 1071 - Reusable Sliding-Window Smoke Evaluator

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

Turn the CP1069 sliding-window PBCH policy into a reusable local evaluator and
apply it to the three corrected ATT10/G42 CP1034 external RF captures:
CP1062, CP1063, and CP1070.

No RF hardware was used in this checkpoint.

## Policy

The evaluator reports two policy levels:

| Policy | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Primary pass | At least one aligned-window row has `pbch_crc_ok=True` and `search_pci=1`. |
| Strict pass | At least one primary-pass row also has `measure_pci=1`. |

Full one-second decode remains a diagnostic field only, because CP1064-CP1068
showed it is candidate-selection sensitive.

## Tool

New helper:

```sh
cp1071_sliding_window_smoke_evaluator.py
```

It reads aligned-window CSV files and optional one-second decode JSON files, then
writes:

- `SUMMARY.md`
- `results.csv`
- `results.json`

## Interpretation

CP1071 turns the previous interpretation into a repeatable local smoke gate:
the corrected external RF path passes when judged by sliding-window PBCH CRC,
even though every full one-second decode fails.

This is now a better standard smoke test than a full-buffer decode:

- it passes repeat captures CP1062, CP1063, and CP1070,
- it uses explicit PBCH CRC OK and PCI 1 criteria,
- it has a stricter mode requiring CSI PCI 1 in the same window,
- it preserves full one-second decode as a diagnostic rather than discarding it.

## Verdict

CP1071: PASS. Use `cp1071_sliding_window_smoke_evaluator.py` as the standard
post-run evaluator for the ATT10/G42/CP1034 external-path ladder. The next
debugging step should investigate the full-buffer candidate-selection failure
directly, while preserving the sliding-window evaluator as the RF health gate.
