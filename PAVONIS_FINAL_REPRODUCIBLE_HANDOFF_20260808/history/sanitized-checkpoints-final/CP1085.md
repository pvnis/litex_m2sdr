## Interpretation

CP1085 falsifies the simple fix of just increasing the live UE cell-search
window. The full-raster UE search path is not keeping up with real time when it
spends long windows per candidate. The extra trial budget causes RF overflows
instead of making full-raster acquisition robust.

The current ladder state is therefore:

- CP1081: lower-level live scanner sees PCI 1.
- CP1083: UE-like target-only cell search decodes PBCH/MIB and reaches SFN sync.
- CP1084: full-raster 100-trial search misses the target after an off-target window.
- CP1085: full-raster 250-trial search overruns and still misses.

## Conclusion

The reliable live UE-side baseline is target-only acquisition at the known SSB
frequency, not full-raster live scanning. Full-raster behavior should be debugged
separately with offline captures, per-candidate RF restarts/flushes, or a faster
search implementation. It is no longer a blocker for continuing up the ladder
from a known SSB frequency, because CP1083 already proves PBCH/MIB and SFN sync
through the LiteX-to-bladeRF path.
