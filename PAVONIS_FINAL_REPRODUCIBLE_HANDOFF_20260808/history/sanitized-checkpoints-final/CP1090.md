## Conclusion

Raising the trial budget from 100 to 200 is not a safe fix for the UE-like
cell-select rung. It allowed PBCH/MIB acquisition but then the test failed to
make a bounded transition out of SFN sync before timeout.

The current evidence separates the layers:

- Lower PBCH/MIB acquisition still works intermittently on the target path.
- SFN/camping is not reliably repeatable in `nr_sa_cell_search_test`.
- The next useful isolation step is to rerun the lower-level
  `nr_cell_search_rf` target scanner. If that passes, bladeRF RX and LiteX TX
  are still healthy and the instability is above the raw SSB-detection layer.
