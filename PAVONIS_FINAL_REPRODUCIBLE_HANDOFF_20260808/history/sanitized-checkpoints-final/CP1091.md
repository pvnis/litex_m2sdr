## Conclusion

The lower-level LiteX TX to bladeRF RX path is still alive. CP1091 rules out a
total bladeRF receive outage or a total LiteX replay outage at this moment.

However, the detections are weak and noisy compared with the ideal target-only
case. The scanner needed the low `-30 dB` SNR threshold and reported extra PCIs
beside PCI 1. That aligns with the UE-like symptoms: PBCH/MIB can sometimes be
decoded, but SFN/camping is not stable.

Next action: isolate TX level with a modest LiteX attenuation change
(`TX_ATT_DB=5`) in the same lower-level scanner before trying UE cell-select
again.
