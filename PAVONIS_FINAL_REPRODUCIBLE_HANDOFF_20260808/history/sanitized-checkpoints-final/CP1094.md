## Conclusion

CP1094 does not invalidate the `TX_ATT_DB=5` RF improvement: PBCH/MIB still had
good SNR and decoded cleanly. The failure is at the bounded cell-select/SFN rung.

The likely immediate problem is that `post_select_ttis=200` is not a safe
bounded wait unless SFN sync happens quickly. CP1093 happened to synchronize
within a few milliseconds; CP1094 did not, and stopping while cell selection was
still active appears to leave `nr_sa_cell_search_test` hanging until the wrapper
timeout.

Next action: keep `TX_ATT_DB=5` but run a longer cell-select window with
`post_select_ttis=1000` and `post_stop_ttis=0`. That gives the PHY time to
either synchronize SFN or hit the 100-trial failure path, while still avoiding
the long post-stop soak.
