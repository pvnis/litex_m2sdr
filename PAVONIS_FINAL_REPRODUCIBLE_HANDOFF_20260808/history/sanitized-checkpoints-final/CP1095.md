## Conclusion

`TX_ATT_DB=5` remains the right RF setting for this replay path: PBCH/MIB SNR was
good and no overflow/out-of-sync noise appeared. The remaining instability is
specifically in SFN synchronization against the short replayed SSB source.

The difference between CP1093 and CP1095 is not raw RF signal level. CP1093
synchronized SFN quickly; CP1095 saw similar or better PBCH SNR and still missed
SFN after 100 trials. That points to replay phase/window sensitivity in this
test source, not a persistent bladeRF or LiteX outage.

Next action should move back toward the live gNB/UE ladder with the improved
TX_ATT/RF-margin finding in mind, rather than overfitting the short replay file.
If live behavior becomes ambiguous again, use the LiteX-only OTA RX/TX baseline
as the next lower-rung isolation check.
