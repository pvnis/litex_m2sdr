## Interpretation

The full-config `freq_offset=6000` value does not explain the live
`nr_sa_cell_search_test` blind spot. The remaining gap is now likely a
live-vs-replay acquisition path difference, a lower-level srsUE RF buffering or
timing issue, or a sample-rate/filtering mismatch not captured by the existing
cell-select trace.

The next step is to compare the CP1093 replay pass against the live CP1104/1105
settings and identify which live-only parameter or data path remains different.
