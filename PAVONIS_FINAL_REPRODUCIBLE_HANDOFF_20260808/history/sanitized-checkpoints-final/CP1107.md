# Checkpoint 1107: Live `nr_cell_search_rf` RX gain 50 improves count but remains weak

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1106 showed that live gNB backoff-20 is visible to srsRAN's lower-level
`nr_cell_search_rf`, but weak and ambiguous at RX gain 42. CP1107 repeated the
same lower-level scan at RX gain 50 before deciding whether to rerun NR-SA at
that gain.

## Setup

- Harness: `cp1106_run_live_gnb_nr_cell_search_rf.sh`
- Stamp: `[date-redacted]TCP1107LIVE_NRCELLSEARCHRF_SSBCENTER_RXG50`
- gNB backoff: `20`
- gNB config line: `tx_gain_backoff: 20`
- Scanner: `nr_cell_search_rf`
- Scanner target SSB: `1839650000 Hz`
- Scanner sample rate: `5760000 sps`
- Scanner RX gain: `50`
- Threshold: `-30 dB`
- Measurement length/period: `20 ms / 20 ms`

## Result

Diagnostic partial pass. PCI 1 appears more consistently than in CP1106, but
the SNR remains low.

Observed cells:

- PCI 1: three hits, `rsrp=-27.9|-26.0|-23.8 dBfs`, SNR values `-27.5`, `-23.4`, `-23.5 dB`
- PCI 52: one hit, `rsrp=-26.4 dBfs`, `snr=-23.4 dB`

gNB health:

- `SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_COUNT=0`
- gNB cell line: `Cell pci=1, bw=10 MHz, 1T1R, dl_arfcn=368500 (n3), dl_freq=1842.5 MHz, dl_ssb_arfcn=367930, ul_freq=1747.5 MHz`

## Interpretation

RX gain 50 is slightly better than RX gain 42 for the lower-level live scanner:
expected PCI 1 appears three times instead of once, and there is only one wrong
PCI rather than three. However, the SNR is still far below the CP1093 replay
condition that reached PBCH/MIB and camping.

This is enough to justify one NR-SA acquisition attempt at RX gain 50, but not
enough to treat gain 50 as a solved RF-margin setting.
