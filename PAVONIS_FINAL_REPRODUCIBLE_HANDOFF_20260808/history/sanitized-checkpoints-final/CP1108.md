# Checkpoint 1108: Live NR-SA SSB-centered RX gain 50 still no cell

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1107 showed that live gNB backoff-20 is visible to srsRAN's lower-level
`nr_cell_search_rf` at RX gain 50, but only weakly and with low SNR. CP1108
tested whether that slightly better lower-level visibility is enough for the
higher-level `nr_sa_cell_search_test` path to acquire PBCH/MIB and camp.

## Result

Failure at the NR-SA acquisition layer. The RF setup completed and the gNB
window was healthy, but `nr_sa_cell_search_test` did not detect a cell.

NR-SA trace:

- RF [interface-redacted] to `1839649998 Hz`
- `cell_search_start center_freq_hz=1839650000 ssb_freq_hz=1839650000 srate_hz=5760000`
- `cell_search_done result=0 trials=100 pci=0`
- Final log line: `Cell search finished without detecting any cell`

gNB health:

- `SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_COUNT=0`
- gNB cell line: `Cell pci=1, bw=10 MHz, 1T1R, dl_arfcn=368500 (n3), dl_freq=1842.5 MHz, dl_ssb_arfcn=367930, ul_freq=1747.5 MHz`

## Interpretation

RX gain 50 does not bridge the gap between lower-level live visibility and
NR-SA acquisition. Together with CP1106 and CP1107, this points to live RF
margin/ambiguity rather than a simple receiver gain setting:

- the live SSB is visible to srsRAN's lower-level scanner;
- the lower-level scanner reports PCI 1 with very poor SNR and occasional wrong
  PCI ambiguity;
- the NR-SA path still never reaches PBCH/MIB under this live condition.

The next useful ladder step is to look for a better live transmit/RX operating
point before spending more runs on NR-SA. CP1102 already showed that backoff 10
collapses SSB detection, while CP1096 and CP1103 showed backoff 20 is
repeatably visible to the custom raw detector. A middle transmit setting should
be tested with the raw detector first.
