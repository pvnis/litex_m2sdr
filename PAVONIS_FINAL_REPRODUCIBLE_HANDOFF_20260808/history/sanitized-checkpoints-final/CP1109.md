# Checkpoint 1109: Live SSB-centered backoff 15 does not prove PCI

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1096 and CP1103 showed that live gNB backoff 20 is repeatably visible to the
custom SSB-centered raw detector, but CP1106 through CP1108 showed that this
condition is still too weak or ambiguous for srsRAN NR-SA acquisition. CP1102
showed that backoff 10 collapses the SSB detector result. CP1109 tested the
midpoint, backoff 15, with the raw detector before trying any higher-layer
scanner.

## Result

detected, but expected PCI 1 did not dominate at any trim.

## Interpretation

Backoff 15 behaves like the bad backoff 10 condition, not like the passing
backoff 20 condition. There is no ADC clipping in the bladeRF capture, so this
is not explained by simple receive saturation at the bladeRF ADC. The failure
looks more like a transmit-side operating-point problem or RF-chain nonlinearity:
increasing transmit power from backoff 20 to 15 makes PSS/SSS identification
worse even though total received RMS stays in a similar range.

This means the next ladder step should not be NR-SA at backoff 15. The better
direction is to isolate whether the bad operating point is specific to the
LiteX-to-bladeRF interaction or is also visible when using LiteX for both TX and
RX.
