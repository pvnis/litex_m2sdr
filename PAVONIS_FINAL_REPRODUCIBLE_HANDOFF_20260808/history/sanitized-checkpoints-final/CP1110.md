# Checkpoint 1110: Fresh LiteX-only centered RX/TX baseline passes, with ad9361 margin caveat

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1108 showed that live NR-SA still cannot acquire at SSB center even with RX
gain 50. CP1109 showed that increasing live gNB power from backoff 20 to
backoff 15 makes the raw SSB detector worse, not better. CP1110 reruns the
LiteX-only centered source-side baseline requested as an isolation check, so
the current failures cannot be explained by a broad "LiteX cannot transmit or
receive the centered SSB/PBCH waveform anymore" regression.

## Interpretation

CP1110 rules out a broad source-side outage:

- LiteX TX/RX programming still works at the centered SSB frequency.
- `none`, `phy`, and `ad9361` loopbacks all capture cleanly with no stream
  errors or timeouts.
- All three loopbacks correlate strongly to the centered source at 0 Hz.
- `none` and `phy` decode PBCH CRC OK directly.
- `ad9361` still decodes PBCH CRC OK in bounded windows.

The caveat is that `ad9361` PBCH robustness is worse than CP1059. That should
be treated as source-side margin variability, not as a dead LiteX source path.
The dominant current failure boundary remains the external LiteX/gNB TX to
bladeRF RX observation path, especially because CP1109 showed a non-monotonic
transmit-power effect without bladeRF ADC clipping.
