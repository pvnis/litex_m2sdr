# Checkpoint 1111: Live SSB-centered backoff 18 still does not prove PCI

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1109 showed that live gNB backoff 15 is not a better operating point than
backoff 20. CP1111 tested the midpoint between backoff 15 and the known
backoff-20 raw-detector passes, using the same SSB-centered bladeRF capture and
raw detector before trying any higher-layer srsRAN scanner.

## Result

was detected at every trim, but expected PCI 1 did not dominate.

## Interpretation

Backoff 18 is not a viable improvement over backoff 20. It is better than
backoff 15 only in the sense that the best PSS NID2 is now the expected `1`, but
SSS/PCI selection is still dominated by wrong candidates. The bladeRF capture
does not show ADC clipping, so the failure remains consistent with a
transmit-side operating-point or external-path distortion/interaction rather
than simple receive saturation.

The power bracket is now narrow: backoff 20 passes the raw detector, while
backoff 18, 15, and 10 do not. The next useful raw-detector bracket point is
backoff 19.
