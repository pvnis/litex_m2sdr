## Purpose

CP1096 and CP1103 showed that backoff 20 can pass. CP1112 tested the final
power-bracket point between 18 and 20 before deciding whether any stronger
transmit setting is usable for higher-layer srsRAN scans.

## Interpretation

Backoff 19 is not usable for the live bladeRF observation path. It produces a
stronger PSS score than backoff 18, but SSS/PCI selection is still dominated by
wrong candidates. As with the other failed stronger-power settings, there is no
bladeRF ADC clipping.

The bracket result is now:

- backoff 10: raw detector fails;
- backoff 15: raw detector fails;
- backoff 18: raw detector fails;
- backoff 19: raw detector fails;
- backoff 20: raw detector passes in CP1096 and CP1103.

This points to a narrow live RF operating region where backoff 20 is clean
enough for the custom detector, but still weak/ambiguous for srsRAN NR-SA. The
next work should use backoff 20 as the only viable live setting unless a
different mitigation is introduced.
