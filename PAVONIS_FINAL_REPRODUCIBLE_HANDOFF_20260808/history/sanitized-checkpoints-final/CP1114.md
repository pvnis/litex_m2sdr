# Checkpoint 1114: Live `nr_cell_search_rf` with +6000 Hz offset loses PCI 1

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1113 attempted a lower-level `nr_cell_search_rf` run with `rf.freq_offset=+6000`
Hz but did not reach the scanner because qcore panicked during gNB startup.
CP1114 retried the same RF scanner after cleanup to get the actual frequency
offset result.

## Setup

- Harness: `cp1106_run_live_gnb_nr_cell_search_rf.sh`
- Stamp: `[date-redacted]TCP1114LIVE_NRCELLSEARCHRF_SSBCENTER_RXG50_FOFFP6000_RETRY`
- Tag: `cp1114_live_nrcellsearchrf_ssbcenter_rxg50_foffp6000_retry_after_cp1113`
- gNB backoff: `20`
- Scanner: `nr_cell_search_rf`
- Scanner target SSB: `1839650000 Hz`
- Scanner sample rate: `5760000 sps`
- Scanner RX gain: `50`
- Scanner RF frequency offset: `+6000 Hz`
- Scanner threshold: `-30 dB`
- Wrapper SHA256: `10ce44ae8b1072fe2d99f8812518a70021589e64211ec5d3affc538c582d69cf`

## Result

The run completed through gNB start, scan, stop, final process checks, and
cleanup. The gNB was healthy, but the scanner result was worse than the zero
offset baseline.

Scanner details:

- RF [interface-redacted] to `1839656000 Hz`
- Expected PCI 1 was not reported.
- Reported cells:
  - PCI 0: three bogus zero-valued hits, `rsrp=+0.0`, `snr=+0.0`
  - PCI 730: one wrong hit, `rsrp=-40.7 dBfs`, `snr=-25.3 dB`, `cfo_hz=+10947.7`
- `NR_CELL_SEARCH_RF_RC=0`

gNB health:

- `SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_COUNT=0`
- gNB log had `==== gNB started ===`
- gNB cell line: `Cell pci=1, bw=10 MHz, 1T1R, dl_arfcn=368500 (n3), dl_freq=1842.5 MHz, dl_ssb_arfcn=367930, ul_freq=1747.5 MHz`

## Interpretation

`rf.freq_offset=+6000` is not a mitigation for the live lower-level scanner. It
pushes the observed cell away from the CP1107 zero-offset result, where PCI 1
was at least visible three times at RX gain 50. The wrong PCI 730 CFO estimate
also exceeds the printed `cfo_hz_max=+6981.8`, which is a useful sign that this
offset direction is outside the scanner's usable acquisition region.

The next offset test should use the opposite sign, `-6000 Hz`.
