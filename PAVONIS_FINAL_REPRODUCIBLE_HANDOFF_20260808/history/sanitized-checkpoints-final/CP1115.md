# Checkpoint 1115: Live `nr_cell_search_rf` with -6000 Hz offset remains wrong/ambiguous

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1114 showed that `rf.freq_offset=+6000` loses expected PCI 1 and produces
wrong/bogus scanner hits. CP1115 tested the opposite sign, `rf.freq_offset=-6000`,
while keeping the live backoff-20 gNB and RX gain 50 fixed.

## Setup

- Harness: `cp1106_run_live_gnb_nr_cell_search_rf.sh`
- Stamp: `[date-redacted]TCP1115LIVE_NRCELLSEARCHRF_SSBCENTER_RXG50_FOFFM6000`
- Tag: `cp1115_live_nrcellsearchrf_ssbcenter_rxg50_foffm6000_after_cp1114`
- gNB backoff: `20`
- Scanner: `nr_cell_search_rf`
- Scanner target SSB: `1839650000 Hz`
- Scanner sample rate: `5760000 sps`
- Scanner RX gain: `50`
- Scanner RF frequency offset: `-6000 Hz`
- Scanner threshold: `-30 dB`

## Result

The run completed cleanly, but the scanner remained ambiguous and worse than
the zero-offset CP1107 baseline.

Scanner details:

- RF [interface-redacted] to `1839644000 Hz`
- Reported cells:
  - PCI 1: one hit, `rsrp=-25.6 dBfs`, `snr=-22.6 dB`, `cfo_hz=-9688.0`
  - PCI 172: one wrong hit, `rsrp=-23.7 dBfs`, `snr=-17.7 dB`, `cfo_hz=-12197.9`
  - PCI 355: one wrong hit, `rsrp=-24.4 dBfs`, `snr=-18.2 dB`, `cfo_hz=-11516.6`
  - PCI 478: one wrong hit, `rsrp=-31.4 dBfs`, `snr=-25.3 dB`, `cfo_hz=-8221.6`
- `NR_CELL_SEARCH_RF_RC=0`

gNB health:

- `SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_COUNT=0`
- gNB log had `==== gNB started ===`
- gNB cell line: `Cell pci=1, bw=10 MHz, 1T1R, dl_arfcn=368500 (n3), dl_freq=1842.5 MHz, dl_ssb_arfcn=367930, ul_freq=1747.5 MHz`

## Interpretation

`rf.freq_offset=-6000` does not improve the lower-level scanner. It does still
show expected PCI 1 once, but the wrong PCIs are stronger by SNR, and all CFO
estimates are outside the scanner's printed `cfo_hz_max=+6981.8`.

Compared with CP1107 zero offset, this is worse:

- CP1107 zero offset: PCI 1 appeared three times at RX gain 50, with one wrong
  PCI 52 hit.
- CP1115 -6000 offset: PCI 1 appeared once, with three wrong PCI hits.
- CP1114 +6000 offset: PCI 1 disappeared.

The useful offset direction, if any, is likely a smaller positive offset because
CP1107's PCI 1 CFO estimates were around `-2.4` to `-3.6 kHz`, while `+6000 Hz`
overshot badly.
