# Checkpoint 1116: Live `nr_cell_search_rf` with +3000 Hz offset reports only PCI 1

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1114 showed that `rf.freq_offset=+6000` overshoots and loses expected PCI 1.
CP1115 showed that `rf.freq_offset=-6000` remains wrong/ambiguous. CP1116
tested a smaller positive offset, `+3000 Hz`, based on CP1107 zero-offset PCI 1
CFO estimates around `-2.4` to `-3.6 kHz`.

## Setup

- Harness: `cp1106_run_live_gnb_nr_cell_search_rf.sh`
- Stamp: `[date-redacted]TCP1116LIVE_NRCELLSEARCHRF_SSBCENTER_RXG50_FOFFP3000`
- Tag: `cp1116_live_nrcellsearchrf_ssbcenter_rxg50_foffp3000_after_cp1115`
- gNB backoff: `20`
- Scanner: `nr_cell_search_rf`
- Scanner target SSB: `1839650000 Hz`
- Scanner sample rate: `5760000 sps`
- Scanner RX gain: `50`
- Scanner RF frequency offset: `+3000 Hz`
- Scanner threshold: `-30 dB`

## Result

Best lower-level live scanner result so far. The scanner reported only expected
PCI 1 and no wrong PCI entries.

Scanner details:

- RF [interface-redacted] to `1839653000 Hz`
- PCI 1 hits:
  - `rsrp=-20.1 dBfs`, `snr=-18.7 dB`, `cfo_hz=+159.4`
  - `rsrp=-25.9 dBfs`, `snr=-24.6 dB`, `cfo_hz=+4524.8`
  - `rsrp=-27.8 dBfs`, `snr=-25.3 dB`, `cfo_hz=+3877.3`
  - `rsrp=-27.5 dBfs`, `snr=-26.2 dB`, `cfo_hz=+393.3`
- Statistics: `pci=001; count=4; rsrp=-27.8|-25.3|-20.1dBfs`
- `NR_CELL_SEARCH_RF_RC=0`

gNB health:

- `SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_COUNT=0`
- gNB log had `==== gNB started ===`
- gNB cell line: `Cell pci=1, bw=10 MHz, 1T1R, dl_arfcn=368500 (n3), dl_freq=1842.5 MHz, dl_ssb_arfcn=367930, ul_freq=1747.5 MHz`

## Comparison

| Checkpoint | RF offset | RX gain | Result |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- |
| CP1107 | 0 Hz | 50 | PCI 1 three hits plus wrong PCI 52 one hit |
| CP1114 | +6000 Hz | 50 | Expected PCI 1 disappeared; bogus/wrong PCI hits |
| CP1115 | -6000 Hz | 50 | PCI 1 one hit plus three wrong PCI hits |
| CP1116 | +3000 Hz | 50 | PCI 1 four hits, no wrong PCI entries |

## Interpretation

`rf.freq_offset=+3000` is the best lower-level srsRAN live setting found so far.
It does not make the SNR high, but it removes wrong-PCI ambiguity in
`nr_cell_search_rf` and yields one near-zero CFO PCI 1 hit. This is strong
enough to justify moving the same setting up one layer to
`nr_sa_cell_search_test` before doing more low-level sweeps.
