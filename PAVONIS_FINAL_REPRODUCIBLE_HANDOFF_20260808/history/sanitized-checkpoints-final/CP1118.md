# Checkpoint 1118 - Live SSB-centered raw capture at RX gain 50: clipped, PCI not proven

## Purpose

After CP1117 failed in `nr_sa_cell_search_test`, CP1118 captured the live gNB
downlink with bladeRF directly at the SSB center, using the same TX operating
point and RX gain as the CP1116/CP1117 srsRAN checks. The goal was to decide
whether this RF point produced a raw capture suitable for PBCH window decoding.

radio result failed: PSS was present, but SSS/PCI was ambiguous, and the capture
was clipped at this gain.

## Interpretation

CP1118 explains why the gain-50 srsRAN results should be treated carefully.
At gain 50, the live bladeRF raw capture clips and does not produce a clean
PCI 1 detector pass. That makes it a poor PBCH-decoder input.

The next step is to keep the known stable TX point (`backoff=20`) and keep the
frequency-offset clue (`+3000 Hz`), but reduce RX gain back toward the raw-clean
region. A lower-level `nr_cell_search_rf` run at gain 40 and `+3000 Hz` should
tell us whether the offset helps without clipping before another full NRSA
attempt.
