# Checkpoint 1122 - Live raw gain 40: PCI proven, PBCH not decodable

## Purpose

CP1119-1121 established that live gNB to bladeRF at RX gain 40 and
`rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz` gives clean lower-level PCI visibility, but
`nr_sa_cell_search_test` still cannot find a cell. CP1122 captured the same
live gNB downlink as raw bladeRF samples at RX gain 40 and then tested PBCH
locally on the exact captured file.

## Interpretation

CP1122 is the clearest current boundary:

- Live gNB downlink reaches bladeRF.
- The bladeRF capture is not clipped at RX gain 40.
- PSS/SSS/PCI 1 are proven in the raw capture.
- Local PBCH decoding fails across one-second, aligned-window, and focused
  correction/pattern sweeps.

This matches the `nr_sa_cell_search_test` behavior: NRSA fails because the
available live capture is not PBCH/MIB-decodable, not because PCI 1 is absent.

The next useful experiment is a narrow RX-gain sweet-spot check below the gain
50 clipping point. RX gain 42 is the first candidate because gain 40 is
non-clipped but PBCH-weak, while gain 50 is clipped and SSS-ambiguous.
