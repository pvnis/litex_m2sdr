# Checkpoints 1123-1124 - RX gain sweet spot: no PBCH recovery

## Purpose

CP1122 proved that RX gain 40 gives a clean, non-clipped raw capture with PCI 1
visible, but PBCH is not decodable. CP1118 showed RX gain 50 clips and makes
SSS/PCI ambiguous. CP1123 and CP1124 bracketed the narrow bladeRF RX-gain region
between those points.

## Interpretation

RX gain is now bracketed:

- Gain 40: clean PCI 1, no clipping, PBCH not decodable.
- Gain 41: best compromise; clean PCI 1, no clipping, stronger CSI, PBCH not
  decodable.
- Gain 42: begins to clip and becomes SSS-ambiguous, PBCH not decodable.
- Gain 50: clipped and SSS-ambiguous.

This rules out a simple bladeRF RX gain/saturation explanation for the PBCH
failure. The problem is narrower: live gNB transmissions contain enough
PSS/SSS/CSI structure to prove PCI 1, but not a PBCH payload that the available
decoder can recover. Earlier file-replay baselines did pass PBCH/NRSA through
the LiteX TX to bladeRF path, so the next isolation step should focus on the
live gNB stream or a LiteX-only live capture path rather than more bladeRF RX
gain changes.
