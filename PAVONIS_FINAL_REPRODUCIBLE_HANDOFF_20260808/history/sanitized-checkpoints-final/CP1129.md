## Interpretation

CP1129 proves the current external path can still decode the known centered PBCH
reference through LiteX TX into bladeRF RX when the replay uses the high-margin
`TX_ATT_DB=5` setting. Combined with CP1128, this rules out:

- a broad LiteX source outage,
- a dead bladeRF receive path,
- a general inability of `nr_sa_cell_search_test` to decode PBCH/MIB today.

The remaining failure boundary is narrower: the live gNB stream/RF delivery
path does not produce a bladeRF capture that yields PBCH, even though lower-level
detectors can see PCI 1 and same-window digital TX dumps can decode PBCH. The
next live-gNB work should focus on what differs between the known replay that
passes here and the live gNB transmission that fails, rather than more generic
bladeRF gain, SSB offset, or LiteX-only checks.
