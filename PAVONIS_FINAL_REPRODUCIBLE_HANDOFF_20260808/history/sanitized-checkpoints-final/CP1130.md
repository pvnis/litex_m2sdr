# Checkpoint 1130-1133 - Full 11.52/off-center geometry failure branch

## Purpose

CP1129 proved the current external path can still decode and camp on the
centered `5.76 Msps` known PBCH replay through LiteX TX into bladeRF RX.
CP1130-1133 then test the next rung: the full `11.52 Msps` carrier-centered
geometry with RF center `1842500000 Hz` and SSB `1839650000 Hz`, matching the
live gNB layout.

This branch asks whether the full/off-center geometry works for known replay
and live gNB before spending more time on UE-side or Android testing.

## Interpretation

The active failed rung is the full `11.52 Msps` off-center geometry, not a
general LiteX outage and not a general bladeRF outage:

- Centered known-vector replay at `5.76 Msps` passes NRSA and camps.
- Full/off-center known-vector replay at `11.52 Msps` gives no NRSA cell.
- Live gNB under the same full/off-center geometry gives no NRSA cell.
- SSB-centered bladeRF raw captures of the full/off-center known vector are
  weak/noise-like and do not correlate meaningfully with the reference.
- Increasing TX power did not improve the SSB-centered raw capture result.

This closes the "try more TX power at the same SSB-centered capture geometry"
branch. The next diagnostic should capture the full/off-center replay at the
carrier center and full bandwidth, then analyze with `center=1842500000` and
`ssb=1839650000`. That distinguishes a true missing RF transmission from a
failure introduced by SSB-centered bladeRF downconversion of an off-center
signal.

The Android phone on `[host-redacted]` remains a final-test asset, but this rung is still
below UE attach/application testing.
