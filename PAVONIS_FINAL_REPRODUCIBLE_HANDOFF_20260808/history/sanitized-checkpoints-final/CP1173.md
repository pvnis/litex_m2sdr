## Interpretation

The empty-EOB patch did not recover live full-geometry PBCH or correlation.

What improved relative to CP1167 is limited: the one-second off-center
`ssb_file_test` now reports CSI PCI 1, but with weak SNR and PBCH still KO. The
20 ms sweep and reference correlation remain failed. Because CP1172's
SSB-centered side-run also showed expected PCI but PBCH KO, the patch may have
changed enough presentation to make PCI easier to see in some geometry, but it
does not fix the live PBCH-bearing waveform.

The next branch should follow the memory plan: explicit live chunk-size/cadence
control or trace/count the live Soapy write segmentation, rather than another
empty-EOB-only retry.
