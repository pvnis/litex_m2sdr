## Interpretation

CP1181 shows that simply setting `OCUDU_SOAPY_TX_SUMMARY=1` in the current
start wrapper is not enough to obtain summary lines in `gnb.log`.

Two plausible explanations remain:

```text
1. The env did not reach the final gNB process.
2. The env reached gNB, but summary lines are logged at RF info level and are filtered by the default gNB log config.
```

The next checkpoint should not repeat CP1181 unchanged. It should either:

```text
1. collect /proc/<gnb_pid>/environ while the gNB is alive to prove whether the summary env reaches the process, or
2. change the opt-in summary log lines from info to warning so they are visible under the default RF log level,
3. preferably do both before another summary-enabled live run.
```
