Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

Scope: [host-redacted]-only live gNB TX runtime diagnostic after CP1183 warning-level
summary deployment. No bladeRF capture, no UE, no Android, no PBCH/NRSA
analysis.

## Result

FAIL for the intended summary output, but narrowing.

After changing the opt-in summary log lines from RF info to RF warning in
CP1182/CP1183, the same CP1181-style live diagnostic still produced zero
`Soapy TX summary enabled` lines and zero `Soapy TX summary:` lines.

This makes RF info-level filtering unlikely as the primary reason for CP1181.
The remaining leading candidate is that `OCUDU_SOAPY_TX_SUMMARY=1` is not
reaching the final gNB process launched by the detached OTA harness.

## Interpretation

CP1184 rules against "summary lines were hidden only because they were RF info
logs." The warning-level binary also produced no summary lines.

Do not repeat CP1181/CP1184 unchanged. The next checkpoint should collect the
environment of the actual live gNB process, for example:

```text
GNB_PID from $OTA/pids.txt
tr '\0' '\n' < /proc/$GNB_PID/environ | grep -E 'OCUDU_SOAPY_TX_SUMMARY|SOAPY_SDR|M2SDR'
```

If the env is absent, fix the detached harness env propagation. If the env is
present, inspect whether the Soapy TX stream constructor is reached on the live
path or whether logger behavior differs from expectation.
