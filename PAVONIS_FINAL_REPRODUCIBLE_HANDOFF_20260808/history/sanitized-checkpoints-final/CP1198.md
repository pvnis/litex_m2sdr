# Checkpoint 1198 - TX Time Summary Proves Positive `timeNs`, Still No LiteX Slip Lines

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

Scope: add and run a low-volume OCUDU direct-stderr TX timestamp summary so
CP1197's missing LiteX slip-trace lines can be interpreted. This checkpoint
includes local patch/build, [host-redacted] deploy/build/help-smoke, and a short [host-redacted]-only
live gNB no-RF probe. No bladeRF capture, UE, PRACH, NRSA, or Android test was
run.

## Result

PASS for timestamp visibility.

CP1198 proves live OCUDU `HAS_TIME` writes carry positive `timeNs` values. In
the final no-RF live gNB stop summary:

```text
has_time_writes=2541
has_time_pos_ns=2541
has_time_zero_ns=0
has_time_neg_ns=0
has_time_min_ns=[subscriber-redacted]
has_time_max_ns=[subscriber-redacted]
```

Caller-side Soapy returns remained clean and empty-gap EOB suppression remained
active. The full post-cleanup gNB log still has zero
`TX timed commit slip` lines even with `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_SLIP_TRACE=1`.

Interpretation: CP1197's missing LiteX slip lines were not because OCUDU was
sending non-positive timestamps. The next test should move into direct LiteX
driver visibility, such as direct-stderr slip counters or a sensor/counter
readback, rather than relying on `SoapySDR::logf(INFO)` lines reaching the gNB
log.

## Verdict And Next Step

Do not repeat CP1198 as-is. It closes the "maybe OCUDU is passing
`HAS_TIME` with zero/non-positive `timeNs`" theory: live `HAS_TIME` writes are
positive timestamped.

The remaining gap is below or inside the LiteX Soapy driver visibility path.
Since `SoapySDR::logf(INFO)` slip lines still do not appear in the gNB log, the
next useful checkpoint should add direct-stderr or summary counters in the
LiteX Soapy TX worker for:

```text
has_time packets observed by the driver
positive/non-positive timeNs at driver ingress
tx_slip_count
late_count
min/max slip_ns
```

That will distinguish "Soapy INFO logs are hidden" from "the worker is not
seeing the same timed packets" and will directly test late/on-time TX commits.
