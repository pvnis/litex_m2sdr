## Verdict And Next Step

Do not repeat CP1199 just to prove driver-side stderr visibility; it works.

CP1199 proves the live LiteX TX worker sees positive timed packets and emits
visible direct-stderr summary lines. It also exposes a mismatch: the worker's
pre-release `HAS_TIME` count is about twice OCUDU's `has_time_writes`, implying
duplicate timed metadata reaches the worker before `_dmaReleaseWrite` applies
deduplication.

The next useful checkpoint should move the counter boundary into
`_dmaReleaseWrite` after duplicate-`HAS_TIME` suppression and after the actual
DMA header bits are decided. That should count:

```text
pre-dedup HAS_TIME packets
post-dedup actual HAS_TIME headers
dedup_suppressed_has_time
positive/zero/negative post-dedup timeNs
post-dedup slip_min_ns/slip_max_ns
post-dedup late_count
```

Only after that can the single CP1199 `late_count=1` be interpreted as either a
real late timed header or a pre-dedup accounting artifact.
