## Interpretation

CP1216 is a clean single-run pass at 75 ms lookahead:

- the patched module and CS16-to-SC12 conversion path were proven in the remote
  helper,
- TX counters were clean with no short writes, errors, or late post-dedup
  headers,
- correct off-center one-shot PBCH was PCI1 CRC OK,
- 250 ms off-center sweep found `34/847` PBCH OK windows,
- reference correlation was in the known-pass class at `0.231`.

This narrows the current paced-CS16 ladder:

- CP1208 50 ms: decodable over 250 ms but weaker, `21/847` PBCH OK.
- CP1216 75 ms: pass, `34/847` PBCH OK, one-shot OK.
- CP1211/CP1212 100 ms: decodable, `64/847` and `37/847`.
- CP1210 150 ms: decodable, `41/847`.
- CP1209 250 ms: decodable, `25/847`.

The branch is still not a monotonic lookahead threshold. The new result says
75 ms is feasible in at least one run, but CP1215 showed phase placement and
run-to-run coherence matter. Do not jump back to live gNB from one 75 ms pass.
