## Interpretation

CP1217 is a repeat pass at 75 ms lookahead:

- the patched module and CS16-to-SC12 conversion path were proven in the remote
  helper,
- TX counters were clean with no short writes, errors, or late post-dedup
  headers,
- correct off-center one-shot PBCH was PCI1 CRC OK,
- 250 ms off-center sweep found `28/847` PBCH OK windows,
- reference correlation was in the known-pass class at `0.215`.

It is weaker than CP1216 in PBCH count (`28/847` versus `34/847`) and one-shot
CSI (`-5.4 dB` versus `3.0 dB`), but it still decodes PBCH and stays well
outside the failed-live/no-PBCH class. CP1216 and CP1217 together make 75 ms a
repeated feasible standalone rung under the patched-module CS16 paced replay
path.

The branch is still not a monotonic lookahead threshold. CP1213-CP1215 showed
that capture-start phase and run-to-run coherence can move the PBCH windows
inside the first 250 ms. The stable rule remains: use 250 ms or phase-aware
analysis for paced captures, and do not reclassify from a short early window.

## Next Step

Do not repeat 75 ms again just to prove it can pass. The next useful branch is
to compare 50 ms versus 75 ms with capture-start or hardware timestamp logging,
or to map live gNB scheduling depth/cadence onto this standalone paced replay
behavior. Keep Android and PRACH out of scope until independent downlink
PBCH/NRSA is stable.
