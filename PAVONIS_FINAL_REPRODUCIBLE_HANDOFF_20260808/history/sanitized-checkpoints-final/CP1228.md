## Interpretation

CP1228 closes the simple "the missing actual-DMA tail was hiding the answer"
branch. With `[date-redacted]` dump buffers, the reconstructed actual-DMA span covers the
full CP1223 metadata period plus margin, has no overlaps, and decodes PBCH
digitally. The live paired RF capture still has no PBCH CRC OK windows and weak
reference correlation.

This means the current live failure is not explained by:

- invalid same-window pre-DMA content,
- invalid same-window post-DMA ch0 content,
- CP1223's first `512` actual-DMA rows being intrinsically bad,
- the CP1223 actual-DMA dump being too short to inspect the missing tail, or
- bladeRF capture discontinuity during the paired RF observation.

The remaining boundary is live egress or hardware-time/stream behavior after
or at the actual DMA submission path: the material that can be reconstructed
from the live DMA dump is digitally decodable, but the over-the-air result
captured at bladeRF is not.

## Status

CP1228 is a digital pass and RF fail. It extends the current CP1222-CP1228
isolation branch and moves the next useful step to one of:

- build a CP1228 full-span actual-DMA CF32 metadata replay and verify whether
  the `[date-redacted]`-row DMA material passes RF under the controlled helper, or
- instrument/compare the LiteX TX driver path below the current dump boundary
  to determine why live DMA material that reconstructs as valid PBCH does not
  produce a matching RF capture.

Do not repeat the `512` vs `[date-redacted]` live DMA dump-depth test without a new
variable; the full-tail digital coverage question is answered.
