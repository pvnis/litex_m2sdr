# CHECKPOINT 1232 - Precompensated bit-exact full-span RF replay

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Scope

CP1232 ran the CP1229-style timestamped bladeRF RF replay using the CP1231
precompensated CF32 payload. The purpose was to test the exact CP1228 live
actual-DMA payload and normalized schedule under helper control, not the
one-LSB-lower CP1229 near-exact payload.

No UE, Android, PRACH, or NRSA work was run. The RF classifier used the
established off-center PBCH one-shot, 250 ms aligned-window sweep, and
reference correlation.

Target proof:

```text
CP1228-exact actual-DMA payload + CP1228 normalized schedule is RF-capable
through LiteX TX -> bladeRF RX under helper control.
```

## Conclusion

CP1232 closes the CP1231 RF caveat. The CP1228-exact full-span actual-DMA
payload bytes and normalized DMA schedule are RF-capable when emitted by the
controlled helper through LiteX TX to bladeRF RX.

That means the CP1228 live RF failure is not explained by:

- invalid full-span actual-DMA sample content,
- invalid observed actual-DMA gap pattern,
- the CP1229 one-LSB CF32 conversion difference,
- short missing-tail DMA coverage,
- capture discontinuity,
- gross replay TX errors,
- or simple bladeRF overheating during these runs.

The active boundary is now live-only behavior below or around the same dump
boundary: device/stream lifetime, hardware-time relationship at the live
transmit window, AD9361/LiteX state, serialized DMA/RF state, or another live
runtime interaction that is absent in the helper replay.

## Next Rung

Do not repeat CP1229 or CP1232 just to prove full-span actual-DMA replay can
produce PBCH. The next useful work should compare live and helper state lower
than the already-matching DMA dump boundary, for example:

- capture/compare LiteX/AD9361 device state immediately before and during live
  versus helper transmit,
- instrument serialized output or lower-driver state after the DMA dump point,
- or build a minimal live-cadence helper that preserves the helper RF pass while
  adding one live-only runtime variable at a time.

Keep Android, UE, PRACH, and NRSA out of scope until independent downlink PBCH
stays explainable and repeatable.
