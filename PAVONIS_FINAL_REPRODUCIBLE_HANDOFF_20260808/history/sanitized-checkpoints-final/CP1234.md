# Checkpoint 1234 - Bit-exact full-span helper replay at live-like TX attenuation

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

Test the CP1232 bit-exact full-span helper replay with the one deliberate
variable identified by CP1233: live-like AD9361 TX attenuation.

CP1232 proved that the CP1228-exact actual-DMA payload bytes and normalized
schedule are RF-capable under helper control at helper attenuation
`TX_ATT_DB=5`. CP1233 showed live gNB state uses about `20 dB` TX attenuation
while the helper used about `5 dB`. CP1234 reran the CP1232 RF proof with
`TX_ATT_DB=20`.

No UE, Android, PRACH, or NRSA work was run. The classifier remained the
timestamped bladeRF capture, one-shot off-center PBCH, 250 ms aligned-window
PBCH sweep, reference correlation, and helper-vs-live DMA dump compare.

## Conclusion

Status: RF fail, diagnostic pass.

CP1234 is the clean live-like attenuation counterpart to CP1232:

- CP1232 exact helper at `TX_ATT_DB=5` passes RF PBCH over 250 ms.
- CP1234 exact helper at `TX_ATT_DB=20` fails RF PBCH over 250 ms.
- Both use the same CP1231 precompensated payload and same normalized DMA
  schedule.
- CP1234's RF-run DMA dump still matches CP1228 live exactly on schedule and
  payload bytes.
- CP1234's capture is continuous, unclipped, and has no TX-side short writes,
  errors, or post-dedup late headers.

This strongly supports CP1233's TX attenuation mismatch as a leading live
failure explanation. The result does not prove every remaining AD9361 diff is
irrelevant, but it does prove that changing the helper from the CP1232
`5 dB` attenuation class to the live `20 dB` attenuation class is sufficient to
move the exact same bit-exact waveform from RF pass-class to failed-live class.

Next useful rung: bracket the helper attenuation threshold on the exact CP1232
payload, likely `TX_ATT_DB=10` and `TX_ATT_DB=15`, then apply the lowest
reliably passing attenuation/backoff to a live gNB full-geometry RF proof. Do
not repeat CP1232 at `TX_ATT_DB=5` or CP1234 at `TX_ATT_DB=20` without a new
variable.
