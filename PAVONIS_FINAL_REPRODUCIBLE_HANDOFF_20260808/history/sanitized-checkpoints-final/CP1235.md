# Checkpoint 1235 - Bit-exact helper attenuation threshold bracket

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

Bracket the RF attenuation threshold for the CP1232 bit-exact helper payload.
CP1232 passed at `TX_ATT_DB=5`; CP1234 failed in the live-like `TX_ATT_DB=20`
class. CP1235 ran the midpoint checks at `TX_ATT_DB=10` and `TX_ATT_DB=15`.

Both runs used the same CP1231 precompensated full-span payload, natural period,
75 ms pacing, ten loops, timestamped bladeRF capture, 250 ms PBCH sweep, and
CP1230 helper-vs-live DMA analyzer.

Status: diagnostic pass.

The CP1232 exact helper attenuation threshold is between `10 dB` and `15 dB`
on this lab path. This sharply explains why the live-like `20 dB` attenuation
state found in CP1233 and tested in CP1234 fails even though the payload and
DMA schedule are exact.

Next useful rung: run the live gNB full-geometry RF proof at a lower live TX
backoff/attenuation, starting with the `10 dB` class because it is the highest
attenuation that still produced PBCH in this exact-helper bracket. Keep the
same timestamped bladeRF capture, 250 ms PBCH sweep, refcorr, and same-window
digital/DMA dump guards. Do not repeat helper `5`, `10`, `15`, or `20 dB`
attenuation points without a new variable.
