# Checkpoint 1237 - Live TX_BACKOFF10 `nr_cell_search_rf` at +[date-redacted] Hz is wrong-PCI/weak

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP1236 recovered live full-geometry RF PBCH at `TX_BACKOFF=10`, with best
reference-correlation offsets around `+[date-redacted] Hz`. CP1237 moved one rung up from
PBCH window decoding to the lower-level srsRAN `nr_cell_search_rf` scanner at
the recovered live gNB point.

## Setup

- Wrapper: `cp1237_run_live_nr_cell_search_rf_txbackoff10_foffp2000.sh`
- Stamp: `[date-redacted]TCP1237_LIVE_NRCELLSEARCHRF_TXBACKOFF10_RXG40_FOFFP2000`
- gNB TX backoff: `10`
- gNB env: patched LiteX Soapy module, `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_CS16_TO_SC12=1`,
  `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS=1000000`,
  `OCUDU_SOAPY_TX_SUPPRESS_EMPTY_EOB=1`
- Scanner: `nr_cell_search_rf`
- Scanner target SSB: `1839650000 Hz`
- Scanner sample rate: `5760000 sps`
- bladeRF RX gain: `40 dB`
- `rf.freq_offset`: `+[date-redacted] Hz`
- Scanner threshold: `-30 dB`

## Result

Harness status was clean:

- `overall_rc=0`
- `scan_rc=0`
- cleanup-after rc `0`
- `SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_COUNT=0`
- `OCUDU_SUMMARY_LINE_COUNT=33`
- `RELEASE_LATE_TRACE_LINE_COUNT=0`
- gNB advertised expected PCI 1, `dl_arfcn=368500`, `dl_ssb_arfcn=367930`

The scanner did not produce a clean expected-PCI result. It found one weak PCI 1
hit and three wrong PCI hits:

```text
PCI=829 rsrp=-41.2 snr=-18.7 cfo_hz=-3313.6
PCI=709 rsrp=-43.3 snr=-20.9 cfo_hz=+6513.3
PCI=001 rsrp=-45.4 snr=-24.5 cfo_hz=+4511.3
PCI=850 rsrp=-43.8 snr=-21.3 cfo_hz=-2533.9
```

Statistics:

```text
pci=001; count=1; rsrp=-45.4|-45.4|-45.4dBfs
pci=709; count=1
pci=829; count=1
pci=850; count=1
NR_CELL_SEARCH_RF_RC=0
```

bladeRF RFIC temperature was normal: `32.5 C` before scanner and `34.2 C`
after scanner.

## Interpretation

CP1237 is a diagnostic harness pass but not a cell-search pass. The recovered
live `TX_BACKOFF=10` point is detectable by `nr_cell_search_rf`, but `+[date-redacted] Hz`
is not a stable lower-level scanner setting: the expected PCI appears only once,
with lower confidence than three wrong-PCIs. This closes `+[date-redacted] Hz` as the next
NRSA target despite its CP1236 reference-correlation score.

The next rung should isolate frequency offset by repeating the same gNB/RX
geometry at `+3000 Hz`, the earlier CP1119 best scanner offset.
