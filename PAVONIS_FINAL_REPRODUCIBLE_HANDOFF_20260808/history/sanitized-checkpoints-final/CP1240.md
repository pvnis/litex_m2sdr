## Goal

Prepare the next NRSA live diagnostic rung without repeating CP1239. CP1238
showed the lower-level scanner can see expected PCI 1 at `TX_BACKOFF=10`,
`RX_GAIN=40`, `rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`, while CP1239 showed full
`nr_sa_cell_search_test` still no-cells after 100 trials. CP1240 adds bounded,
env-gated visibility at the NRSA SSB search/state-machine boundary.

```text

```

New env-gated diagnostics:

```text
PAVONIS_SRSUE_NRSA_SSB_TRACE
PAVONIS_SRSUE_NRSA_SSB_TRACE_LIMIT
PAVONIS_SRSUE_NRSA_SSB_TRACE_PERIOD
PAVONIS_SRSUE_CELL_SEARCH_TRACE_ALL_TRIALS
PAVONIS_SRSUE_CELL_SELECT_TRACE_LIMIT
PAVONIS_SRSUE_CELL_SEARCH_EMPTY_TRACE
```

The diagnostics print:

- raw `srsran_ssb_search()` return status per call;
- `CELL_FOUND` / `CELL_NOT_FOUND` / `ERROR` result;
- whether the SSB result was non-empty;
- PCI, SSB index, SNR, CFO, RSRP, EPRE, timing offset, PBCH CRC;
- running counts of found, non-empty rejected, empty, and error results;
- richer `PAVONIS_CELL_SELECT_TRACE` trial and done lines;
- explicit test-harness empty-metrics markers after stack completion.
