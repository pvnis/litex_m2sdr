## Interpretation

The decisive gate is in:

```text
srsRAN_4G/srsue/src/phy/nr/cell_search.cc
```

The state machine only accepts an SSB when:

```text
ret.ssb_res.measurements.snr_dB >= -10.0f && ret.ssb_res.pbch_msg.crc
```

CP1241 candidates satisfy `pbch_crc=1`, but their NRSA SNR values are about
`-21.6 dB` and `-20.4 dB`, well below the hard `-10 dB` acceptance gate.
Therefore the CP1239/CP1241 no-cell result is not a no-candidate handoff bug
and not wrong-PCI acquisition. It is a low-SNR rejection at the NRSA
state-machine boundary.

This aligns with the preceding evidence:

- CP1236: live gNB RF PBCH becomes recoverable at `TX_BACKOFF=10`.
- CP1238: lower-level live scanner at `+3000 Hz` sees expected PCI1 only.
- CP1239: full NRSA no-cells with clean harness.
- CP1241: full NRSA sees expected PCI1 candidates, but rejects them because
  the NRSA SNR metric is below the acceptance threshold.

The next rung should compare NRSA's SNR metric at known-pass replay/camping
points against this live `-20 dB` class, or otherwise explain why the live NRSA
metric is much weaker than the lower-level scanner/offline PBCH evidence. Do
not blindly lower the acceptance gate as a fix without that comparison.
