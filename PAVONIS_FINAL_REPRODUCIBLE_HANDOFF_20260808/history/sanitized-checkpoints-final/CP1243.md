## Conclusion

Raising live level from `TX_BACKOFF=10` to `TX_BACKOFF=5` closed the initial
NRSA SNR gate for at least one live trial. CP1241 saw expected PCI1 candidates
with PBCH CRC true but SNR only `-21.6/-20.4 dB`; CP1243 found expected PCI1
with PBCH CRC true at `+5.632 dB`, close to the CP1242 known-pass replay class
(`+4.748 dB`).

The active frontier has moved from "why does full NRSA never find a cell?" to
"why does live NRSA fail SFN synchronization after finding PCI1 at healthy SNR?"
The next useful rung should keep `TX_BACKOFF=5`, preserve the same trace, and
isolate SFN/tracking stability: increase SFN/cell-select trial budget, keep
post-select runtime long enough to observe recovery, and watch RF overflow and
possible overdrive behavior.
