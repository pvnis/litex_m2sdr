## Conclusion

CP1244 proves live gNB downlink can reach NRSA `CAMPING` through LiteX TX to
bladeRF RX at `TX_BACKOFF=5`, RX gain `40`, and `rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`.
The key change from CP1243 was allowing more cell-search trials: the accepted
hit arrived at trial 255.

The active frontier is no longer initial NRSA acquisition. It is post-camping
stability: repeated out-of-sync, RF overflow, and two RF gap/TX-error events
start shortly after camping. The next rung should isolate whether this is
overdrive/overflow at the receiver, a post-select/runtime artifact in
`nr_sa_cell_search_test`, or a live gNB continuity issue visible only after
camping.
