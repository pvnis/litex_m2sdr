## Comparison To CP1247 And CP1244

CP1244 at RX gain `40` reached CAMPING, then failed with `201` out-of-sync
lines, `360` RF overflows, and two RF gap/TX-error events.

CP1247 at RX gain `36` reached CAMPING, then failed with `447` out-of-sync
lines, `124` RF overflows, and one RF gap/TX-error event.

CP1248 at RX gain `32` reached CAMPING, then failed with `574` out-of-sync
lines, only `1` RF overflow, and no RF gap/TX-failed event.

## Interpretation

CP1248 largely removes the overflow/RF-gap symptoms while preserving
acquisition and CAMPING. The post-camping out-of-sync failure remains and even
gets worse by line count. This strongly weakens simple bladeRF RX overdrive or
overflow as the primary cause of the post-camping failure.

The active frontier should now move from coarse RX-gain bracketing to
post-camping tracking/timing instrumentation. The next checkpoint should trace
why `ue_sync_nr` declares repeated out-of-sync about 200 ms after CAMPING:
for example tracking PBCH miss counts, SSB search return codes during
tracking, CFO/timing estimates, and whether the same failure happens with a
shorter `post_select_ttis` observation window.
