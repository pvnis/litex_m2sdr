## Interpretation

CP1254 closes the CP1252 concern that rejected FIND PBCH hits were still
poisoning timing/CFO state: with the new guard, six bad-delay hits were rejected
with no `q_cfo`, `q_delay`, `next_rf_sample_offset`, `ssb_idx`, `sf_idx`, or
`sfn` mutation.

That did **not** recover SFN sync or camping. The blocker is now stricter:
under this live point, the SFN-sync PBCH hits that decode successfully are still
far outside the `2.4 us` absolute-delay gate. Rejecting their feedback is
cleaner, but no small-absolute-delay PBCH hit appeared within 100 SFN trials.

Because the gNB side also recorded one TX partial/timeout counter, the next
best rung is either:

1. repeat this same CP1254 point once to see whether the gNB TX anomaly
   reproduces; or
2. if the repeat is clean or produces the same SFN behavior, widen the
   absolute-delay gate in controlled steps, starting around `10 us`, because
   CP1254 had one rejected PBCH hit at `8.076634 us`.
