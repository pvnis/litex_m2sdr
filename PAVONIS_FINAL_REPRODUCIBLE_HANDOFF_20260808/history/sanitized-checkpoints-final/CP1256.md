## Interpretation

CP1256 is a controlled CELL FOUND / SFN FAIL result with clean gNB counters.
The `10 us` widened absolute gate did not recover SFN sync because every
PBCH-CRC-true SFN hit still had absolute delay greater than `10 us`.

The useful new boundary is the nearest rejected PBCH-true hit:

```text
attempt 368: delay=-22.219061 us
```

That suggests the next conservative gate rung should be about `30 us`. A
`30 us` threshold would admit the CP1256 `-22.2 us` PBCH-true hit while still
excluding the CP1250 known-bad `-32.5 us` TRACK entry and the recurring
70-500 us large-delay class. A very wide gate remains unjustified.
