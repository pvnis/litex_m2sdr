## Conclusion

CP1269 closes the "feedback-only fallback untested" gap. The path was exercised
on a rolling boundary-crossing blind hit, preserved `q->sfn`, `q->sf_idx`, and
`ssb_idx`, applied CFO/delay feedback, advanced `next_rf_offset` to `7`, and
reset the miss counter from `16` to `0`.

Feedback-only fallback is therefore operational but not sufficient. Scheduled
TRACK stayed `0/68`, same-buffer blind search stayed `0/68`, and the only
blind PBCH hit was far from scheduled TRACK (`search_minus_selected_offset=-2184`)
and before the current-buffer boundary (`search_current_offset=-1359`). The
next rung should stop adding reset-only fallback variants and isolate why
scheduled TRACK remains pinned to `selected_sf_offset=825` when the only valid
blind evidence is rolling/boundary-shifted.
