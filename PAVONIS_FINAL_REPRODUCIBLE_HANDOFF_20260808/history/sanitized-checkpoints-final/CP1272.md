## Conclusion

CP1272 is the third consecutive CP1270-binary live attempt that SFN-failed
before TRACK. CP1270 had three outside-gate `update_ssb` hits, CP1271 had none,
and CP1272 had five outside-gate hits. None reached CAMPING/TRACK, and none
produced a fallback or `track_fallback_offset_correction` event.

This closes the "just rerun until offset correction is exercised" branch for
now. Offset-correction behavior remains untested, but the first failed stage
has moved earlier again: live SFN acquisition under the `60 us` absolute gate
plus feedback rejection is now the active blocker. The next checkpoint should
isolate SFN acquisition variability or gate width before making more
offset-correction changes.
