## Interpretation

CP1274 is a software-control regression, not an RF feasibility failure:

- LiteX TX replay was clean and identical-class to CP1242.
- Initial NRSA SSB/PBCH detection remained healthy.
- The failure moved specifically to SFN `ue_sync_nr_update_ssb()` acceptance
  under the current abs90/reject-feedback wrapper.
- The known-pass replay now produces legitimate PBCH-CRC-true SFN hits around
  `-171` to `-172 us`; CP1273's `90 us` gate rejects all of them.

This changes the active frontier. The current CP1270/CP1273 software knobs are
not safe against the known-pass CP1129 replay. The recent live SFN failures
cannot be interpreted as purely live RF/acquisition variability until the
known-pass path is restored under the current binary.
