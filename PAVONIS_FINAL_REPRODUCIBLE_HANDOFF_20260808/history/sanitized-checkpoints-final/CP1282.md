## Interpretation

CP1282 passes the isolation test. Same-opportunity direct offset override still
creates normal TRACK PBCH hits with all fallback actions disabled and stored
offset follow disabled. This closes the CP1281 rolling-fallback confound: the
direct override mechanism itself is sufficient to produce TRACK `update_ssb`
and `recv_adjust` updates.

The remaining failure is still the static scheduled TRACK anchor at
`selected_sf_offset=825`. Scheduled TRACK misses most opportunities, while
same-current blind/direct candidates can decode and can be promoted when they
are inside the current 1 ms buffer. The next rung should stop treating direct
evidence as a side diagnostic and make it the primary per-opportunity TRACK
anchor or timing-model input. Do not return to a simple last-good stored-offset
follow as the main mechanism; CP1280 and CP1281 already made that insufficient.
