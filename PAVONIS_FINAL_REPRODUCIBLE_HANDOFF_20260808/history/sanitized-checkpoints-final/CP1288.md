## Conclusion

CP1288 is a valid behavioral test of the CP1287 stateful candidate-anchor
implementation. It shows the mechanism is active: a fresh same-current blind
hit can be promoted, update TRACK, seed a stateful anchor, and then be retried
for the configured TTL.

The result is negative for the current single-offset stateful strategy. The
only successful TRACK PBCH came from the immediate same-current candidate at
opportunity 47. Reusing that same offset for the next 20 opportunities produced
`0/20` stateful CRC successes and expired. Static scheduled TRACK also remained
`0/74`, and direct-primary/follow/fallback actions stayed disabled and unused.

The next rung should not simply increase the TTL of one stale offset. The more
defensible branch is a candidate-set or fresh-evidence scheduler: carry a small
set of current/rolling candidate offsets, prefer fresh candidates each
opportunity, and only age out candidates that stop matching. CP1288 also shows
rolling sidecheck found one additional CRC-valid boundary-crossing hit at
opportunity 53, so a single same-current offset is too narrow for the live
tracking behavior.
