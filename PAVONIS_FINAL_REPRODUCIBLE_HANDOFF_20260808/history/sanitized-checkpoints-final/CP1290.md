# Checkpoint 1290 - Raw TRACK SNR TX_BACKOFF4 No-Anchors Probe

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

After CP1289, further sync/anchor scheduler work is gated on the raw live
downlink metric: scheduled `event=track_opportunity` mean `meas_snr` and the
percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

CP1290 makes one RF/link-budget-only change from the CP1283/CP1288/CP1289
baseline: lower the gNB backoff from `TX_BACKOFF=5` to `TX_BACKOFF=4` while
keeping RX gain `32` and `rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`. The run disables
sidecheck, direct-offset, anchor, follow, and fallback actions so the result is
not another `ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler experiment.


The run still found PCI1 strongly during initial acquisition, so the failure is

## Conclusion

CP1290 is a negative raw-DL link-budget result.

Lowering gNB backoff from `5` to `4` at RX gain `32` and `+3000 Hz` did not
raise scheduled TRACK SNR. It produced `80` TRACK opportunities, `0` PBCH hits,
mean `meas_snr=-22.085 dB`, and `0/80` opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. The next useful rung should change only RF/link-budget variables or
capture/compare the raw downlink path, such as a single-variable frequency
offset probe, a bounded RX-gain probe, or an independent raw capture comparison.
