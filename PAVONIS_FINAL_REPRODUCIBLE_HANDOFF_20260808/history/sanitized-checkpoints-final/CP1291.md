# Checkpoint 1291 - Raw TRACK SNR +3500 Hz No-Anchors Probe

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1290 showed that lowering backoff from `5` to `4` did not raise the raw
scheduled TRACK metric. CP1291 returns to the CP1283/CP1288/CP1289 baseline
power point (`TX_BACKOFF=5`, RX gain `32`) and changes one RF variable:
`rf.freq_offset=+3500 Hz` instead of `+3000 Hz`.

The gate remains the raw live downlink metric: scheduled
`event=track_opportunity` mean `meas_snr` and the percentage of opportunities
at or above `-10 dB`. Sidecheck, direct-offset, anchor, follow, and fallback
actions stay disabled.

## Conclusion

CP1291 is another negative raw-DL link-budget result.

Changing `rf.freq_offset` from `+3000 Hz` to `+3500 Hz` at `TX_BACKOFF=5` and
RX gain `32` did not raise scheduled TRACK SNR. It produced `69` TRACK
opportunities, `0` PBCH hits, mean `meas_snr=-22.099 dB`, and `0/69`
opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. At this point, lowering backoff to `4` and nudging RX frequency offset
to `+3500 Hz` are both closed as raw-metric fixes at RX gain `32`.
