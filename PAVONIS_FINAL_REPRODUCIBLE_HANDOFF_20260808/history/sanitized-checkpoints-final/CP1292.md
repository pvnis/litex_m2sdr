Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1291 tested `rf.freq_offset=+3500 Hz` and did not raise the raw scheduled
TRACK metric. Because `+3500 Hz` worsened acquisition residual CFO relative to
the recent `+3000 Hz` runs, CP1292 tests the symmetric lower nudge:
`rf.freq_offset=+2500 Hz`.

The raw gate remains scheduled `event=track_opportunity` mean `meas_snr` and
the percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`. Sidecheck,
direct-offset, anchor, follow, and fallback actions stay disabled.

## Conclusion

CP1292 is another negative raw-DL link-budget result.

Changing `rf.freq_offset` from `+3000 Hz` to `+2500 Hz` at `TX_BACKOFF=5` and
RX gain `32` did not raise scheduled TRACK SNR enough to cross the gate. It
produced `69` TRACK opportunities, `0` PBCH hits, mean
`meas_snr=-21.903 dB`, and `0/69` opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. The immediate frequency-offset bracket around `+3000 Hz` is now closed
for `+2500 Hz` and `+3500 Hz` at RX gain `32`; neither raises the raw metric.
