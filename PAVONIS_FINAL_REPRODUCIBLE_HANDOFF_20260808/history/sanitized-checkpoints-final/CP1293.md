## Conclusion

CP1293 is another negative raw-DL link-budget result.

Changing bladeRF RX gain from `32` to `36` at `TX_BACKOFF=5` and
`rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz` did not raise scheduled TRACK SNR enough to cross the
gate. It produced `79` TRACK opportunities, `0` PBCH hits, mean
`meas_snr=-22.008 dB`, and `0/79` opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. RX gain `36` is now closed as a simple raw-metric improvement at this
baseline RF point.
