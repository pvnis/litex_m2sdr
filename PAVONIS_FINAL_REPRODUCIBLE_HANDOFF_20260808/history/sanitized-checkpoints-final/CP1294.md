## Goal

CP1290-CP1293 closed lowering gNB backoff to `4`, the immediate
`+2500/+3500 Hz` frequency-offset bracket, and RX gain `36` as simple raw
scheduled TRACK fixes. CP1294 tests the next RX-gain point: bladeRF RX gain
`40` instead of `32`/`36`, while keeping `TX_BACKOFF=5` and
`rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`.

The gate remains scheduled `event=track_opportunity` mean `meas_snr` and the
percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`. Sidecheck, direct-offset,
anchor, follow, and fallback actions stay disabled.

## Conclusion

CP1294 is another negative raw-DL link-budget result.

Changing bladeRF RX gain from `32`/`36` to `40` at `TX_BACKOFF=5` and
`rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz` did not raise scheduled TRACK SNR enough to cross the
gate. It produced `72` TRACK opportunities, `0` PBCH hits, mean
`meas_snr=-21.529 dB`, and `0/72` opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

RX gain `40` also increased UE overflow count to `74`, so it is not a clean
raw-budget fix. The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR
and usable fraction are raised.
