# Checkpoint 1295 - Raw TRACK SNR RX Gain 28 No-Anchors Probe

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1293/CP1294 showed that increasing bladeRF RX gain from `32` to `36` and
`40` did not raise the raw scheduled TRACK metric enough to cross the
`-10 dB` gate. CP1295 tests the opposite direction as a lower-gain/overdrive
sanity probe: bladeRF RX gain `28` at the same `TX_BACKOFF=5`,
`rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz` point.

The intended gate remains scheduled `event=track_opportunity` mean `meas_snr`
and percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`. Sidecheck, direct-offset,
anchor, follow, and fallback actions stay disabled.

## Conclusion

CP1295 is a negative lower-RX-gain result.

At RX gain `28`, live NRSA still found expected PCI1 during initial cell search
but failed SFN synchronization before CAMPING/TRACK. It produced zero scheduled
TRACK opportunities, so it did not raise the raw live TRACK-opportunity metric
and cannot advance the ladder.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. RX gain `28` is closed as a simple overdrive/saturation escape at this
RF point.
