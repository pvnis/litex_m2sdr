## Goal

point, but the exact no-anchor baseline at `TX_BACKOFF=5`, bladeRF RX gain

## Conclusion

CP1296 establishes the exact no-anchor raw-DL baseline at the current live RF
point. It reaches PCI1/SFN/CAMPING/TRACK cleanly enough to measure the gate,
but scheduled TRACK still produces `75` opportunities, `0` PBCH hits, mean

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. The baseline itself is below threshold.
