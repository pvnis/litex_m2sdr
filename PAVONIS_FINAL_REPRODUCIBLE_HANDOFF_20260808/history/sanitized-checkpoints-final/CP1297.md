## Conclusion

CP1297 is a negative TX-level quality-bracket result.

At `TX_BACKOFF=6`, RX gain `32`, and `rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`, live NRSA
reaches CAMPING/TRACK but scheduled TRACK produces `68` opportunities,
`0` PBCH hits, mean `meas_snr=-22.402 dB`, and `0/68` opportunities at or
above `-10 dB`.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. `TX_BACKOFF=6` is closed as an adjacent simple TX-level improvement at
this RF point.
