# Checkpoint 1298 - Live TX Backoff 5 Independent bladeRF Raw-DL Capture

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1290-CP1297 kept the `nr_sa_cell_search_test` scheduled TRACK raw gate below
threshold at the CP1296 baseline: `TX_BACKOFF=5`, srsUE RX gain `32`, and
`rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz` gave mean scheduled TRACK `meas_snr=-21.419 dB` with
`0/75` opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

CP1298 does not edit `ue_sync_nr.c` and does not run UE attach, Android, or
PRACH. It runs an independent live bladeRF metadata capture against the same
live gNB TX level to separate "the RF signal has no usable downlink windows"
from "NRSA/srsUE scheduled TRACK does not land on usable windows or receive
level".

## Interpretation

CP1298 is an independent raw-DL capture pass with a secondary analysis gap.

At the same live gNB TX level as the CP1296 NRSA raw gate (`TX_BACKOFF=5`),
the timestamped bladeRF metadata capture is continuous, unclipped, cool, and
able to decode PBCH over many offline SSB windows: one-shot PBCH is OK and the
250 ms sweep finds `82/847` PBCH CRC OK PCI1 windows, with best expected-PCI
CSI around `-0.6 dB`.

This does not make the scheduled TRACK gate pass. It shows that the live
downlink is not globally absent in an independent bladeRF capture, while the
NRSA/srsUE scheduled TRACK path at RX gains `28/32/36/40` remains below gate.
The likely next direct raw-gate step is therefore a higher srsUE RX-gain
scheduled-TRACK bracket, informed by the independent helper's effective high
gain/readback behavior, before returning to any scheduler or anchor logic.
