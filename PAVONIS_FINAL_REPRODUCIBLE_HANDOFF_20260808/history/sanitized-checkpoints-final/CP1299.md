# Checkpoint 1299 - Raw TRACK SNR RX Gain 50 No-Anchors Probe

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1298 proved that an independent bladeRF metadata capture at the CP1296 live
TX point can see usable PBCH windows, but the helper's requested gain `32`
read back as bladeRF overall/full `60/71`. CP1299 tests the direct scheduled
TRACK raw gate at a higher srsUE bladeRF RX gain, without changing source code
or enabling any sidecheck/direct/anchor/follow/fallback scheduler actions.

The gate remains scheduled `event=track_opportunity` mean `meas_snr` and the
percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

## Conclusion

CP1299 is a negative higher-RX-gain raw-DL bracket.

At `TX_BACKOFF=5`, srsUE RX gain `50`, and `rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`, live
NRSA reaches CAMPING/TRACK but scheduled TRACK produces `75` opportunities,
`0` PBCH hits, mean `meas_snr=-22.434 dB`, and `0/75` opportunities at or
above `-10 dB`.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not continue `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic work until the raw live TRACK-opportunity SNR and usable fraction are
raised. RX gain `50` is closed as a simple raw-budget improvement at this RF
point.
