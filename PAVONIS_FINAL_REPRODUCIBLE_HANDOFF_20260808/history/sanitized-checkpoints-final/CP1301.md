percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

## Conclusion

CP1301 is a negative maximum-RX-gain raw-DL bracket.

At `TX_BACKOFF=5`, srsUE RX gain `60`, and `rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`, live
NRSA reaches CAMPING/TRACK but scheduled TRACK produces `71` opportunities,
`0` PBCH hits, mean `meas_snr=-21.473 dB`, and `0/71` opportunities at or
above `-10 dB`.

RX gain `60` is closed as a simple raw-budget improvement at this RF point. It
also introduces many startup overflow lines, so further attempts to raise the
raw live TRACK SNR should move away from srsUE RX gain bracketing and toward a
different RF/link-budget variable.
