## Goal

RX gains are applied, but gains `44` and `60` did not raise the scheduled
TRACK-opportunity raw gate. CP1302 tests a different one-variable RF/input

from the prior `4.608 MHz` class to `9.216 MHz`. The gate remains scheduled

## Conclusion

CP1302 is a negative NRSA sample-rate/RX-bandwidth raw-DL bracket.

At `TX_BACKOFF=5`, srsUE RX gain `32`, `rf.freq_offset=+3000 Hz`, and
`11.52 Msps`, live NRSA reaches CAMPING/TRACK but scheduled TRACK produces
`73` opportunities, `0` PBCH hits, mean `meas_snr=-21.986 dB`, and `0/73`
opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

Increasing the srsUE path from the prior `5.76 Msps`/`4.608 MHz` receive
bandwidth class to `11.52 Msps`/`9.216 MHz` does not explain the gap between
CP1298's independent offline PBCH windows and the sub-threshold scheduled
TRACK path. The active gate remains raw downlink/link budget; no further
`ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler or anchor logic should be edited until a probe raises
mean TRACK-opportunity SNR and creates a meaningful fraction of opportunities
at or above `-10 dB`.
