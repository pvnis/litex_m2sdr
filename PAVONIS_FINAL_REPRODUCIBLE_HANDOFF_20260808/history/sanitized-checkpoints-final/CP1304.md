## Conclusion

CP1304 is a negative raw downlink budget probe. Combining exact `10 MHz`
srsUE RX bandwidth with maximum srsUE RX gain did not pass the scheduled TRACK
gate:

```text
CP1303: RXG32,  11.52 Msps / 10.000 MHz -> mean -22.382 dB, best -16.065 dB, 0/79 >= -10 dB
CP1304: RXG60,  11.52 Msps / 10.000 MHz -> mean -22.091 dB, best -10.529 dB, 0/70 >= -10 dB
```

The near-threshold best sample means there may be a useful RF gain/BW operating
region near the high-gain setting, but the run still fails the gate and adds
substantial startup overflow. Do not move to PRACH/Android or resume
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor scheduler work from this result.
