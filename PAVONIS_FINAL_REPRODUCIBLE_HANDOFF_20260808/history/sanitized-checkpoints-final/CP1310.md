## Conclusion

CP1310 is negative. Increasing gNB TX power to `TX_BACKOFF=3` at the CP1309
exact carrier-center/no-anchor receive point does not raise the raw scheduled
TRACK gate. The mean remains about `-22.02 dB`, the best scheduled opportunity
is only `-14.274 dB`, and `0%` of scheduled opportunities are at or above
`-10 dB`.

Do not move up the ladder from this checkpoint. The raw scheduled TRACK gate
remains the blocker, and `ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler/anchor work remains paused.
