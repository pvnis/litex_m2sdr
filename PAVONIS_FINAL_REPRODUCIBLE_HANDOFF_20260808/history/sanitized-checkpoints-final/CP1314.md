## Interpretation

CP1314 is negative for the active raw scheduled-TRACK gate.

It closes the direct helper-gain-class explanation from CP1313: even when srsUE
uses actual RX gain `60` at the same `TX_BACKOFF=10`, carrier-centered, 11.52
Msps, forced-10 MHz point, scheduled TRACK remains around `-22 dB` mean and
has `0%` usable opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

CP1313 still proves the external path can contain offline PBCH windows at this
point, so the current blocker is not broad external downlink absence. CP1314
shows the srsUE scheduled-TRACK metric is not raised by matching the helper's
high-gain readback class. Do not move up the ladder and do not resume
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits until a raw scheduled-TRACK probe actually
raises the mean SNR and usable fraction.
