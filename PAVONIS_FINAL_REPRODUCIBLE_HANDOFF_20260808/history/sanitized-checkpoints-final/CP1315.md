## Interpretation

CP1315 is still a raw scheduled-TRACK gate failure. Combining
`TX_BACKOFF=3` with srsUE RX gain `60` at the exact carrier-centered receive
path did not raise the mean TRACK SNR; it remains in the same approximately
`-22 dB` class as CP1290-CP1314.

The one isolated `-7.802 dB` opportunity is worth preserving because it is the
first recent no-action scheduled TRACK row above `-10 dB`, but it is not a
ladder unlock: the usable fraction is only `1/73` (`1.37%`), PBCH remains
`0/73`, and the mean is still sub-threshold. Continue to gate all higher-rung
work on the raw scheduled TRACK metric.

Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits until a raw probe raises both the mean and the usable opportunity
fraction.
