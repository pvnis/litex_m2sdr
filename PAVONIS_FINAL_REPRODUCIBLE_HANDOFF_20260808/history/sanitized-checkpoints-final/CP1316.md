## Interpretation

CP1316 reproduces the main CP1315 observation: the high-TX/high-RX
carrier-centered point can produce one scheduled TRACK row above `-10 dB`, and
it appears at the same opportunity number (`50`) in both runs. However, it
also reproduces the failure: PBCH remains `0`, the usable fraction stays near
`1.4%`, and the mean remains around `-21.7 dB`.

This is useful evidence, but not enough to unlock the ladder. The raw scheduled
TRACK budget is still sub-threshold overall. The next raw-budget-only step can
now reasonably push TX power one notch harder, because CP1315/CP1316 show the
best scheduled window is reproducible but not yet sufficient.
