## Goal

row above `-10 dB` at `TX_BACKOFF=3`, RX gain `60`, carrier-centered
11.52 Msps / forced 10 MHz / `rf.freq_offset=0`. CP1317 pushes only TX power
one notch harder to `TX_BACKOFF=2` and keeps the receive path and no-action
policy unchanged.

This is still a raw downlink gate probe only. It does not edit `ue_sync_nr.c`,
does not enable sidecheck/direct/anchor/follow/fallback actions, and does not
move to PRACH/Android/NAS.

## Interpretation

CP1317 is a negative raw-budget probe. Lowering backoff from `3` to `2` does
not raise the scheduled TRACK gate; it removes the reproducible `>= -10 dB`
row seen in CP1315/CP1316 and worsens the mean to `-22.470 dB`.

This suggests the useful high-TX/high-RX edge is not monotonically improved by
more TX drive at this exact receive path. It may be overdrive/nonlinear,
acquisition-timeline dependent, or simply a narrow scheduled-window effect, but
it is not enough to move up the ladder.

Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits until a raw scheduled-TRACK probe raises the mean and usable fraction.
