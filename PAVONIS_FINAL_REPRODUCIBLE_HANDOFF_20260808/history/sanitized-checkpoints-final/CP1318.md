# Checkpoint 1318 - TX_BACKOFF3 RX Gain 58 Carrier-Centered Raw TRACK Gate

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1315 and CP1316 showed a reproducible but undecodable single scheduled TRACK
row above `-10 dB` at `TX_BACKOFF=3`, RX gain `60`. CP1317 showed that pushing
TX harder to `TX_BACKOFF=2` worsened the raw metric. CP1318 returns to
`TX_BACKOFF=3` and lowers only srsUE RX gain to `58` to test whether the useful
high-TX edge is being clipped or overdriven at maximum RX gain.

This remains a raw downlink gate probe only. It does not edit `ue_sync_nr.c`,
does not enable sidecheck/direct/anchor/follow/fallback actions, and does not
move to PRACH/Android/NAS.

## Interpretation

CP1318 is a negative raw-budget probe. It improves the single best scheduled
TRACK row relative to CP1315/CP1316 (`-4.362 dB` instead of roughly `-8` to
`-10 dB`), but the run still has `0/71` PBCH hits and only `1/71`
opportunities at or above `-10 dB`. The mean remains in the same failed class
at `-21.541 dB`.

The result says that lowering RX gain from `60` to `58` may reduce occasional
overdrive or scatter on the strongest row, but it does not raise the sustained
scheduled-window link budget enough to unlock the ladder.

Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits until a raw scheduled-TRACK probe raises the mean and usable fraction.
