## Interpretation

CP1319 is a negative raw-budget probe. It improves stream cleanliness
substantially compared with RX gain `58` and `60` in terms of overflow lines,
but the scheduled TRACK signal itself gets worse: the mean falls to
`-22.387 dB`, the best row is only `-8.335 dB`, and PBCH remains `0/95`.

This closes the simple receive-gain-down bracket below RX gain `58`. The next
raw-only bracket should not lower RX gain further at `TX_BACKOFF=3`; it should
test whether CP1318's best-row improvement survives with one step less gNB TX
drive: `TX_BACKOFF=4`, RX gain `58`, exact carrier-centered 11.52 Msps /
forced 10 MHz / `rf.freq_offset=0`, no sidecheck/direct/anchor/follow/fallback
actions.

Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits until a raw scheduled-TRACK probe raises the mean and usable fraction.
