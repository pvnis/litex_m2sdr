## Goal

CP1318 at `TX_BACKOFF=3`, RX gain `58` produced the strongest single scheduled
TRACK row seen in this bracket, but did not improve mean SNR or PBCH hit count
enough to pass the raw gate. CP1319 showed that lowering RX gain further to
`56` worsened the metric. CP1320 keeps RX gain `58` and changes only the gNB
TX backoff from `3` to `4` to test whether one step less TX drive keeps the
best-row improvement while reducing overdrive or nonlinear behavior.

This remains a raw downlink gate probe only. It does not edit `ue_sync_nr.c`,
does not enable sidecheck/direct/anchor/follow/fallback actions, and does not
move to PRACH/Android/NAS.

## Interpretation

CP1320 is a negative raw-budget probe. Compared with CP1318, one step less TX
drive worsens the best row from `-4.362 dB` to `-15.442 dB`, removes all
opportunities at or above `-15 dB`, and leaves PBCH at `0/74`. The mean stays
in the failed class at `-21.925 dB`.

This closes `TX_BACKOFF=4`, RX gain `58`, carrier-centered foff0 as a simple
raw-budget improvement. The remaining local high-gain interpolation worth
testing is `TX_BACKOFF=3`, RX gain `59`, exact carrier-centered 11.52 Msps /
forced 10 MHz / `rf.freq_offset=0`, still with all sidecheck/direct/anchor/
follow/fallback actions disabled.

Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits until a raw scheduled-TRACK probe raises the mean and usable fraction.
