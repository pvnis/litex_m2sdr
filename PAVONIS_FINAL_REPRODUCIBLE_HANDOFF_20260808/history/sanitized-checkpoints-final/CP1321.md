## Interpretation

CP1321 is a negative raw-budget probe. RX gain `59` gives a relatively good
mean (`-21.506 dB`) but no scheduled opportunity reaches `-15 dB`, let alone
`-10 dB`, and PBCH remains `0/70`. It does not reproduce CP1318's strong
single row.

At this point CP1318 is the only current high-gain/raw point with a strong
isolated row. Before inventing another RF knob, the next raw-only checkpoint
should repeat the exact CP1318 point (`TX_BACKOFF=3`, RX gain `58`,
carrier-centered 11.52 Msps / forced 10 MHz / `rf.freq_offset=0`) once to
separate a real narrow optimum from run-to-run scatter.

Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits until a raw scheduled-TRACK probe raises the mean and usable fraction.
