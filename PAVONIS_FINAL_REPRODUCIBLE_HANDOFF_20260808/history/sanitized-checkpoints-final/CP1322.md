## Interpretation

CP1322 is a negative reproducibility check. The exact CP1318 point did not
reproduce CP1318's `-4.362 dB` row; it produced no opportunity at or above
`-15 dB`, no PBCH hits, and mean `-21.808 dB`. CP1318's strong row should be
treated as run-to-run scatter unless another controlled repeat proves
otherwise.

The raw gate remains closed. Simple local bracketing of gNB backoff and srsUE
RX gain around the carrier-centered forced-10-MHz path has not raised the mean
scheduled-TRACK SNR or usable-opportunity fraction. The next work should be a
targeted RF-path investigation comparing the independent bladeRF capture helper
path against the srsUE bladeRF receive setup, not more random scheduler edits
or PRACH/Android/NAS rungs.

Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits until a raw scheduled-TRACK probe raises the mean and usable fraction.
