## Purpose

CP1325 follows the active raw-downlink gate: no `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic edits, no ladder movement, and judge the run by scheduled TRACK
opportunity SNR and the fraction of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

after CP1324's contiguous-metadata diagnostic, keeps normal TX pre-enable, and
adds an observational bounded raw sample/metadata dump from the srsUE bladeRF
RF plugin. The goal is to compare the default srsUE receive path against the
independent helper path without changing the scheduler or receive timing policy.

## Conclusion

CP1325 is a negative raw-gate checkpoint. It confirms that returning to
default repeated `RX_NOW` avoids CP1324's time-past fault mode but does not
raise scheduled TRACK SNR: mean remains about `-21.55 dB`, `0/78`
opportunities are at or above `-10 dB`, and TRACK PBCH remains `0`.

The bounded dump is now available for offline comparison, but the active goal
remains unchanged: do not edit `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic and do not move
up to PRACH/Android/NAS until a raw scheduled-TRACK run raises mean SNR and
the `>= -10 dB` opportunity fraction.
