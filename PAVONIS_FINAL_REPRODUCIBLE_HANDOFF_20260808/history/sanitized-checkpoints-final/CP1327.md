## Purpose

CP1327 is a live RF raw-downlink gate probe. It follows CP1326's offline
finding that the CP1325 srsUE raw dump contains PBCH but is heavily
near-clipped. The hypothesis was RX overdrive/clipping: lower only the srsUE
bladeRF RX gain, keep the CP1325 live RF point, and judge only the scheduled
TRACK metric.

No `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic was edited. Sidechecks, direct checks,
anchor/follow/fallback actions, and ladder work remained disabled.

## Interpretation

CP1327 closes simple "lower srsUE RX gain to remove CP1325 clipping/overdrive"
as a direct raw-gate fix at the CP1325 RF point. Lower gain improved transport
health but not downlink usability: scheduled TRACK remained sub-threshold with
mean `-22.370 dB`, `0/68 >= -10 dB`, and zero PBCH hits.

The active gate remains unchanged: do not edit `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic
or move to PRACH/Android/NAS until a live scheduled-TRACK raw metric improves
substantially. The next work should stay in RF/link-budget isolation, not
offset-scheduler logic.
