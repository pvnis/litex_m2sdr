## Interpretation

CP1328 is not a raw scheduled-TRACK gate pass and does not unlock ladder
movement. It found PBCH during initial cell search but failed before TRACK, so
it produced no mean TRACK SNR or `>= -10 dB` fraction to compare against
CP1325-CP1327.

The useful conclusion is narrower: current no-anchor SSB-centered
`TX_BACKOFF=5`, RX gain `32`, `5.76 Msps`, `+3000 Hz` does not by itself
restore CAMPING/TRACK, even though transport health is clean. Because the
nominally similar older CP1248 did camp under an older wrapper, this result
does not close SSB-centered receive geometry completely. The next raw-only
probe should either repeat the SSB-centered point once for stochastic SFN
behavior or raise only gNB TX drive at the same SSB-centered geometry. It must
still be judged by scheduled TRACK mean SNR and `>= -10 dB` opportunity rate,
and it still must avoid scheduler/anchor edits.
