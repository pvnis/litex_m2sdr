## Interpretation

CP1329 is a valid raw scheduled-TRACK measurement, but it is not a gate pass.
SSB-centered geometry plus higher `TX_BACKOFF=3` improves acquisition and keeps
transport health clean, but the scheduled TRACK metric remains in the same
sub-threshold family as the carrier-centered runs: mean `-21.590 dB`,
`1/84 >= -10 dB`, and zero PBCH hits.

This does not unlock ladder movement. It weakens SSB-centered receive geometry
as a simple raw-budget fix, though it produced a clean TRACK-reaching baseline
that can be bracketed once more if needed. Any next work should remain
RF/link-budget only and must still be judged by scheduled TRACK mean SNR and
the `>= -10 dB` opportunity fraction.
