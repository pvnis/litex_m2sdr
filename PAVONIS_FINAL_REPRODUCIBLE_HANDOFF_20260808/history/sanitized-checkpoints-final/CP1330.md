## Interpretation

CP1330 is a valid raw scheduled-TRACK measurement, but it is not a gate pass.
Pushing the SSB-centered point harder from `TX_BACKOFF=3` to `TX_BACKOFF=2`
removes CP1329's single above-threshold row and worsens mean SNR to
`-22.219 dB`, with `0/75 >= -10 dB` and zero PBCH hits.

Together, CP1328-CP1330 show that SSB-centered receive geometry and local
TX-drive bracketing do not raise the raw scheduled-TRACK link budget enough.
Further work should pivot away from scheduler edits and away from simple local
gain/backoff retesting; the active gate remains the raw scheduled TRACK metric.
