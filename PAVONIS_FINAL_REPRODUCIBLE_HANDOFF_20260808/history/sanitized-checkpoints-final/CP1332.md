## Receive Timeline Findings

The long RX debug timeline is the main new CP1332 evidence.

- RF debug lines cover calls `1..1159`.
- The short-read/timestamp-gap region occurs before TRACK:
  - short/gap calls from `272` through `431`
  - last short/gap: call `431`, raw offset `0.408272 s`
- TRACK opportunities are bracketed by normal full-size RX calls:
  - opportunity 1: previous RX call `441`, raw offset `0.417459 s`
  - opportunity 72: previous RX call `1151`, raw offset `1.127459 s`
  - all TRACK-neighbor calls have `actual_count=5760`, `requested_gap=0`
- No TRACK opportunity is within two RX calls of a short read or requested gap.
- TRACK opportunities advance every 10 RX calls, as expected for the 10 ms
  SSB periodicity at 1 ms RF read calls.
- Correlation between scheduled TRACK SNR and neighboring RF amplitude is weak:
  - preceding-call `sc16_abs_max` correlation `0.0832`
  - preceding-call RMS correlation `-0.0727`

The tail offline sweep overlaps the live TRACK era in raw-sample time:

- TRACK raw sample span: `0.417459 s` through `1.127459 s`
- tail PCI1 PBCH-OK sweep windows: `0.913959 s` through `1.131459 s`
- best tail offline window: raw absolute `1.100459 s`, CSI SNR `+2.4 dB`

So CP1332 shows decodable PBCH windows in the same late receive region where
live scheduled TRACK opportunities still report sub-threshold SNR and no PBCH.

## Verdict

CP1332 fails the raw scheduled-TRACK downlink gate:

- mean TRACK-opportunity SNR is `-21.481653 dB`
- `0/72` opportunities are at or above `-10 dB`
- `0/72` TRACK opportunities decode PBCH

However, CP1332 strengthens the CP1331 diagnostic conclusion. The RF receive
dump contains decodable PCI1 PBCH windows, including in a tail region that
overlaps live TRACK raw-sample time. The live scheduled TRACK failure does not
correlate with bladeRF short reads, timestamp gaps, overflows, clipping, or gNB
TX health. The next work must remain raw-gated and should focus on explaining
the receive-buffer/timing semantics or raising the live scheduled downlink
budget without resuming `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits or moving up to
PRACH/Android/NAS.
