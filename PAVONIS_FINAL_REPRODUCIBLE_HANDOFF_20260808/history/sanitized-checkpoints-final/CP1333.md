# Checkpoint CP1333 - SSB-Centered +2500 Hz Raw TRACK Frequency-Offset Probe

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Purpose

Test whether the CP1331/CP1332 SSB-centered raw scheduled-TRACK metric is
sensitive to a small RF frequency-offset correction outside `ue_sync_nr.c`
sync/anchor logic. This keeps the current raw downlink gate unchanged:

- mean `event=track_opportunity meas_snr`
- fraction of TRACK opportunities with `meas_snr >= -10 dB`

No PRACH/Android/NAS ladder movement is allowed unless that gate improves.

SSB-centered `+3000 Hz` point to `+2500 Hz`.

## Verdict

CP1333 is below the raw scheduled-TRACK downlink gate. The `+2500 Hz` offset
does not raise the mean TRACK-opportunity SNR or produce any usable
`>= -10 dB` opportunities. It improves the initial acquisition CFO but does
not translate into a scheduled TRACK metric improvement. Continue only
raw-gated RF/link-budget or receive-path diagnostics; do not resume
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits or move up the PRACH/Android/NAS ladder.
