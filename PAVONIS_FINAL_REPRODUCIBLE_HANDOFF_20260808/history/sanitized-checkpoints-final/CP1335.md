## Verdict

CP1335 is below the raw scheduled-TRACK downlink gate. Raising RX gain from
`32` to `40` at the best local SSB-centered frequency point improves initial
acquisition but does not raise scheduled TRACK SNR; it worsens the mean to
`-22.270 dB` and leaves `0/89 >= -10 dB`. Do not use RX gain `40` at this
point as a link-budget fix. Continue only raw-gated RF/link-budget or
receive-path diagnostics; do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits or
move up to PRACH/Android/NAS.
