## Verdict

CP1337 is a valid forced-`5.6 MHz` RX bandwidth test and is below the raw
scheduled-TRACK downlink gate. The override was confirmed by active readback
(`requested_bw=5600000 bw=5600000 rx_bw_override=5600000`), but scheduled TRACK
still produced mean `-22.231 dB`, `0/94 >= -10 dB`, and zero PBCH hits. Do not
repeat the SSB-centered `5.6 MHz` RX bandwidth point as a link-budget fix.
Further work remains raw-gated and should focus on a different RF/link-budget
or receive-path variable before any PRACH/Android/NAS ladder movement.
