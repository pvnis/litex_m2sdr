## Verdict

CP1338 is below the raw scheduled-TRACK downlink gate. Lowering RX gain from
`32` to `28` at the best local SSB-centered frequency point does not raise
scheduled TRACK SNR; it worsens the mean to `-22.817 dB` and leaves
`0/75 >= -10 dB` with zero PBCH hits. Do not use RX gain `28` at this point as
a link-budget fix. Further work remains raw-gated and should focus on a
different RF/link-budget or receive-path variable before any PRACH/Android/NAS
ladder movement.
