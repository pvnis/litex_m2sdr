# Checkpoint CP1339 - SSB-Centered RX Bandwidth 3.84 MHz Remote Export

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Purpose

Test whether narrowing the srsUE bladeRF RX bandwidth below the default
`4.608 MHz` improves the live scheduled TRACK raw downlink metric at the best
local SSB-centered point. CP1337 proved that widening to `5.6 MHz` applies
cleanly but does not raise the gate metric.

This checkpoint keeps the current gate:

- mean `event=track_opportunity meas_snr`
- fraction of TRACK opportunities with `meas_snr >= -10 dB`

No `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic is changed, and no PRACH/Android/NAS
ladder movement is allowed before the raw gate improves.

## Verdict

CP1339 is below the raw scheduled-TRACK downlink gate. The `3.84 MHz` RX
bandwidth override was confirmed by active readback and produced a better mean
than the widened `5.6 MHz` point, but it still reached only mean
`-21.398 dB`, `0/76 >= -10 dB`, and zero PBCH hits. Do not treat the narrower
bandwidth as a ladder unlock. Further work remains raw-gated and should focus
on a different RF/link-budget or receive-path variable before any
PRACH/Android/NAS movement.
