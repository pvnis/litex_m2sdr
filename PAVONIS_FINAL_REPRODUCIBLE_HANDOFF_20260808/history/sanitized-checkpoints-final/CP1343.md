## Goal

The active gate is the live srsUE scheduled TRACK metric: mean
`event=track_opportunity meas_snr` and the fraction of TRACK opportunities
`>= -10 dB`. CP1342 rechecked the best no-anchor SSB-centered point and still
failed that gate:


CP1343 does not edit `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic and does not move up the
ladder. It runs an independent bladeRF timestamped capture at the same
SSB-centered receive geometry used by CP1342, to distinguish "external SSB is
not present at this receive point" from "srsUE scheduled TRACK windows remain
below gate."

## Verdict

CP1343 proves the external full-geometry downlink SSB is present in an
independent SSB-centered bladeRF capture at the CP1342 receive geometry. The
capture is continuous, unclipped, and decodes PBCH in `61/847` swept windows.
The corrected gNB full-geometry TX-dump guard is also clean.

CP1343 does not unlock the ladder. The active gate remains CP1342's live
scheduled srsUE TRACK metric: mean `-21.083464 dB`, `0/84 >= -10 dB`, and one
weak TRACK PBCH row. The new evidence points away from a broad "no external
SSB exists" or "gNB digital TX is dead" theory and back toward why the srsUE
scheduled TRACK windows/receive path see mostly sub-threshold rows even though
nearby independent raw windows can decode PBCH. Continue only raw-budget or
receive-path diagnostics judged by the scheduled TRACK mean SNR and `>= -10 dB`
fraction; do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits or move to
PRACH/Android/NAS before that live metric is raised.
