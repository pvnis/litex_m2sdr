# Checkpoint 1344 - SSB-Centered RX Gain60 Helper-Gain-Class Scheduled TRACK Probe

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1343 showed that an independent SSB-centered bladeRF capture at the CP1342
receive point decodes PBCH, but that helper path reads back as the bladeRF
high-gain class (`overall=60 dB`, `full=71 dB`). CP1344 tests the specific
remaining SSB-centered receive-gain question in the live scheduled srsUE path:
does raising srsUE RX gain from CP1342's `32` to `60` raise the scheduled
TRACK metric?

This is a raw-gate checkpoint only. It does not edit `ue_sync_nr.c`
sync/anchor logic and does not move to PRACH, Android, or NAS.

## Verdict

CP1344 closes the SSB-centered helper-gain-class theory as a scheduled-TRACK
fix. Raising srsUE RX gain to `60` at the CP1342/CP1343 SSB-centered point
does not raise the gate; it worsens relative to CP1342 (`-21.952 dB` vs
`-21.083 dB` mean), removes the weak CP1342 TRACK PBCH hit, and gives
`0/74 >= -10 dB` and `0/74 >= -15 dB`.

The active ladder gate remains unchanged: do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c`
sync/anchor edits or move to PRACH/Android/NAS until a live scheduled TRACK run
raises mean TRACK SNR and the `>= -10 dB` opportunity fraction.
