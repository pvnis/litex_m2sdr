## Goal

`TX_BACKOFF=3` to `TX_BACKOFF=4` did not raise the live scheduled TRACK gate.
CP1346 completes that immediate analog-overdrive relief bracket at the same
best local offset by testing `TX_BACKOFF=5`, RX gain `32`, `5.76 Msps`, and
`rf.freq_offset=+3500 Hz`.

## Verdict

CP1346 does not raise the raw scheduled downlink gate. At the SSB-centered
best-offset point, `TX_BACKOFF=5` still produces a sub-threshold scheduled
TRACK metric: mean `-21.872 dB`, best `-16.359 dB`, `0/75 >= -10 dB`,
`0/75 >= -15 dB`, and zero TRACK PBCH hits.

Together, CP1345 and CP1346 show that backing off from `TX_BACKOFF=3` to
`4` or `5` does not raise the scheduled metric. Because CP1346 had one
worker-late counter, it should be treated as a no-unlock result rather than a
perfectly clean final TX_BACKOFF5 closure.

The active ladder gate remains unchanged: no more `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
edits and no PRACH/Android/NAS work until a live scheduled TRACK run raises
mean TRACK SNR and the `>= -10 dB` opportunity fraction.
