## Verdict

CP1349 does not raise the raw scheduled downlink gate. Interpolating the
SSB-centered [interface-redacted] offset to `+3750 Hz` gives mean `-21.970 dB`, with
`0/76 >= -10 dB`, `1/76 >= -15 dB`, and zero TRACK PBCH hits.

This is better than the CP1348 `+4000 Hz` mean but still below CP1334/CP1342
and far below the active gate. CP1349 closes `+3750 Hz` as a frequency-offset
fix at the current SSB-centered `TX_BACKOFF=3`, RX gain `32`,
default-bandwidth point. The active ladder gate remains unchanged: no more
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits and no PRACH/Android/NAS work until a live
scheduled TRACK run raises mean TRACK SNR and the `>= -10 dB` opportunity
fraction.
