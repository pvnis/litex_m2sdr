# Checkpoint 1362 - Post-Reboot Independent bladeRF Raw DL Capture At CP1361 Point

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Result

RF-PATH DIAGNOSTIC PASS / LADDER STILL LOCKED.

CP1362 repeated the CP1343 independent bladeRF raw downlink capture at the
exact CP1342/CP1361 SSB-centered receive geometry after both NUCs were
rebooted and `[host-redacted]` returned. The independent capture is continuous and
unclipped, the raw RF capture decodes PCI1 PBCH one-shot, and the corrected
full-geometry gNB TX-dump analysis confirms digital TX PBCH is present before
and after DMA.

This checkpoint does not unlock the ladder because it is not a live srsUE
scheduled TRACK gate. It does, however, rule out a broad post-reboot "no
external SSB/PBCH" or "gNB digital TX dead" regression at the CP1361 receive
point.

## Goal

Isolate whether CP1361's failed live scheduled-TRACK metric was caused by the
external downlink disappearing after reboot. The diagnostic removes srsUE
attach/scheduler behavior and captures raw bladeRF samples while the live gNB
transmits the same SSB-centered point.

## Interpretation

CP1362 explains the CP1361 failure boundary more tightly:

- The live scheduled srsUE TRACK gate is still bad at CP1361:
  mean `-22.180489 dB`, `0/92 >= -10 dB`, zero TRACK PBCH hits.
- The independent raw bladeRF capture at the same RF point still contains
  decodable PBCH: one-shot PBCH OK and `35/847` sweep windows PBCH OK.
- Corrected full-geometry gNB TX-dump analysis confirms digital PBCH before
  and after DMA.

Therefore the next useful work is not PRACH/Android/NAS and not another
`ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler edit. The active branch remains a raw receive-path or
link-budget diagnostic explaining why independent captures decode while live
srsUE scheduled TRACK opportunities stay around `-22 dB`.
