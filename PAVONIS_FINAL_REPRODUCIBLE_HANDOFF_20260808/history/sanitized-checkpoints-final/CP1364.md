## Interpretation

CP1364 reproduces the CP1331/CP1332 class of result at the exact CP1361 best
`+3500 Hz` SSB-centered point: the live scheduled TRACK metric remains around
`-21.5 dB` mean with essentially no usable `>= -10 dB` opportunities and zero
scheduled PBCH hits, while the same srsUE bladeRF RX stream contains decodable
PCI1 PBCH offline in both early and late portions.

This strengthens the current receive-path/timing interpretation:

- broad external SSB disappearance is already ruled out by CP1362 and is again
  inconsistent with the CP1364 srsUE RX dump;
- broad gNB digital TX failure is already ruled out by CP1362 corrected TX
  dump analysis;
- clipping is not present in the CP1364 dump;
- default RXNOW itself can deliver decodable PBCH into the srsUE RF-plugin
  dump;
- the live scheduled static TRACK window still does not land on those good
  PBCH opportunities.

The raw gate remains locked. Future work should stay on raw RF/link-budget or
receive-path diagnostics until mean TRACK SNR and the fraction of
opportunities `>= -10 dB` improve.
