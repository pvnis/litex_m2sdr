## Interpretation

CP1367 is not a ladder unlock. For the exact current best SSB-centered
`+3500 Hz` point, widening srsUE to `11.52 Msps` and forcing `10 MHz` RX
bandwidth worsened the scheduled TRACK mean relative to the best 5.76 Msps
runs and produced no PBCH hits.

This closes the exact SSB-centered `TX_BACKOFF=3` / RX gain `32` /
`rf.freq_offset=+3500 Hz` / `11.52 Msps` / forced `10 MHz` branch as a raw-gate
fix. It does not close a deliberate live-CF32 transport patch, but it weakens
the simpler theory that the active failure is solved by changing only the
receive-rate/bandwidth class.

Keep the ladder locked on the raw scheduled-TRACK metric: mean SNR and
percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.
