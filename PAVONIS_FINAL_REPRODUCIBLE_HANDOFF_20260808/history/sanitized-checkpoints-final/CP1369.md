SSB-centered point. It does not justify moving to PRACH/Android/NAS, and it
does not reopen `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor scheduler edits. Continue only with
raw RF/link-budget or receive-path diagnostics until the scheduled TRACK metric
improves.
