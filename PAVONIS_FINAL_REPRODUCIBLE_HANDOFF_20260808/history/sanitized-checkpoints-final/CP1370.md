## Conclusion

CP1370 is a clean negative diagnostic. Disabling the M2SDR CS16-to-SC12
divide-by-16 mapping at the current best SSB-centered point did not raise the
raw scheduled TRACK budget. It was worse than the baseline: initial cell search
found no nonempty SSB candidate across 1000 trials, produced no PBCH CRC, and
never reached CAMPING/TRACK, so there was no scheduled TRACK SNR distribution
to average.

This closes `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_CS16_TO_SC12=0` at the current `TX_BACKOFF=3`
SSB-centered point as a raw-gate fix. It also strengthens the practical
conclusion that the existing CS16-to-SC12 mapping is needed for the live M2SDR
gNB path at this drive level. Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS, and do not
resume `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor scheduler edits until the live scheduled
TRACK metric improves.
