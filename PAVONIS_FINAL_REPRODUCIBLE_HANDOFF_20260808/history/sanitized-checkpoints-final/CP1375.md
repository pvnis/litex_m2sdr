## Conclusion

CP1375 is a clean no-unlock. The forced `4.608 MHz` RX bandwidth at
`7.68 Msps` applies exactly, reaches scheduled TRACK, and keeps gNB/UE health
clean, but still fails the raw downlink gate: mean TRACK-opportunity SNR is
`-21.892 dB`, `0/65` opportunities are at or above `-10 dB`, and there are no
TRACK PBCH hits.

This closes the exact `7.68 Msps` bandwidth bracket tested so far:
default `6.144 MHz`, forced `7.68 MHz`, and forced `4.608 MHz` are all below
the raw scheduled gate. Do not repeat this exact `7.68 Msps`/forced-`4.608 MHz`
receive-path point unless another variable has changed or a regression check
is explicitly needed. Further work remains gated on mean TRACK-opportunity SNR
and the percentage of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`, with no new
sync/anchor scheduler edits.
