## Conclusion

CP1380 is a clean no-unlock. The one-variable receive-frequency interpolation
from `+3500 Hz` to `+3250 Hz` at CP1373's `7.68 Msps`/default-bandwidth
receive point applies cleanly and reaches TRACK, but it does not raise the raw
downlink budget. Mean TRACK-opportunity SNR is `-22.071 dB`, `0/75`
opportunities are at or above `-10 dB`, there are no opportunities at or above
`-15 dB`, and there are no TRACK PBCH hits.

This closes exact mid-rate `7.68 Msps`/default-`6.144 MHz`/`rf.freq_offset=+3250 Hz`
as a raw-gate fix at the current SSB-centered point. It does not justify
moving to PRACH/Android/NAS, and it does not reopen `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
scheduler edits. If continuing frequency interpolation at this mid-rate point,
the non-repeated symmetric side would be `+3750 Hz`, but CP1380 does not make
that branch more likely to unlock the ladder by itself.
