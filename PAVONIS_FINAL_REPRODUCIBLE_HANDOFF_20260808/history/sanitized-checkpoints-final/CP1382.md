# Checkpoint 1382 - Independent bladeRF Raw-DL Capture At CP1373 7.68 Msps Geometry

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

CP1373 is the current least-bad live scheduled-TRACK receive point at
`7.68 Msps` and default `6.144 MHz` bladeRF RX bandwidth, but it still fails
the raw gate: mean TRACK SNR `-20.978 dB`, `2/74 >= -10 dB`, and zero TRACK
PBCH hits. CP1380 and CP1381 closed nearby frequency offsets below gate.

CP1382 checks whether the external RF at the exact CP1373 receive geometry
still contains decodable PBCH in an independent bladeRF capture, without UE
attach, PRACH, Android/NAS, or `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits.

## Conclusion

CP1382 is not a scheduled TRACK gate pass and does not unlock PRACH/Android/NAS
work. It is a clean diagnostic: at the exact CP1373 `7.68 Msps` / default
`6.144 MHz` SSB-centered receive geometry, independent bladeRF RF contains
decodable PCI1 PBCH and the corrected gNB digital TX dump contains decodable
PBCH before and after DMA.

This rules out a broad "no external SSB/PBCH at the CP1373 geometry" or
"gNB digital TX dead" explanation for the CP1373 scheduled-TRACK failure. The
active gate remains the live srsUE scheduled TRACK metric and likely points
back to receive-window/timing semantics or a live receive-path behavior that
the independent capture path does not reproduce.
