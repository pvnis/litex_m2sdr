## Interpretation

CP1383 reproduces the CP1364 class of result at the current CP1373 mid-rate
geometry:

- live scheduled TRACK remains sub-threshold with zero PBCH hits;
- the same srsUE bladeRF RX plugin dump contains offline-decodable PCI1 PBCH;
- the dump is unclipped and status-clean;
- gNB TX remains mechanically clean on the normal CS16 path.

This closes another broad receive-side "conversion/downsample destroyed the
samples" explanation for the actual srsUE RX dump path at CP1373 geometry.
It does not close scheduled receive-window, slot-phase, or timing semantics.
It also does not reopen TX-side live CF32: CP1369 already tested that branch
and failed the raw gate.

The raw gate remains locked. Do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS or edit
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic until the mean TRACK SNR and fraction of
opportunities `>= -10 dB` improve.
