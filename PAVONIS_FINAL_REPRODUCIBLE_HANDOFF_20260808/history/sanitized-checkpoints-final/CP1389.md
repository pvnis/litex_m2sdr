## Comparison And Conclusion

CP1389 does not raise the raw scheduled TRACK downlink budget. It is slightly
better than CP1388 CF32 but still worse than CP1373's least-bad scatter:

- CP1373, `7.68 Msps`, default `6.144 MHz`: mean `-20.978 dB`, `2/74 >= -10 dB`, zero PBCH hits
- CP1374, `7.68 Msps`, forced `7.68 MHz`: mean `-22.049 dB`, `0/71 >= -10 dB`, zero PBCH hits
- CP1375, `7.68 Msps`, forced `4.608 MHz`: mean `-21.892 dB`, `0/65 >= -10 dB`, zero PBCH hits
- CP1389, `7.68 Msps`, forced `3.84 MHz`: mean `-21.269 dB`, `0/67 >= -10 dB`, zero PBCH hits

The exact current-geometry `7.68 Msps`/forced-`3.84 MHz` receive-path point is
closed below gate. The `7.68 Msps` RX-bandwidth branch now has default
`6.144 MHz`, forced `7.68 MHz`, forced `4.608 MHz`, and forced `3.84 MHz`
all below the scheduled TRACK gate. Do not move up the ladder or resume
`ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler edits until a raw run improves mean TRACK-opportunity
SNR and the fraction of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.
