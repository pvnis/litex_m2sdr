## Conclusion

CP1400 rules out helper-style BIST/crossbar prequieting as the missing
`~25 dB` between the live gNB append path and the standalone LiteX split-frequency
helper. The prequiet commands succeeded, TX was proven live `CF32`, RX was
proven at the intended SSB-centered geometry, TX/DMA content was still
PBCH-decodable, and the append level stayed weak with zero PBCH decode.

Do not repeat unchanged prequiet + dual-sidecar live append. The remaining
frontier is still a live gNB runtime TX/RX path difference or real link budget.
The next useful isolation should target another live-only semantic difference,
most likely timed/discontinuous burst behavior versus the continuous standalone
helper, or a direct live-path/link-budget change that can move the raw
scheduled-TRACK metric.
