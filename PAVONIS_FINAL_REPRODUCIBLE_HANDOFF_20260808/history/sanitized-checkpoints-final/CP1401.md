through standalone LiteX split-frequency replay. It also exposes a helper
defect in the first timed-metadata player: a TX write error aborts RX capture,
making the timed results empty and non-diagnostic.

It only says the continuous control is healthy and the timed branch needs a
capture-after-TX-error rerun.
