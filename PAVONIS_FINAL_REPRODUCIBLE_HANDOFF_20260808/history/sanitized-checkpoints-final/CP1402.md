## Goal

Retest only the timed-metadata branch from CP1401 with a much smaller
prequeue/lookahead value: `PACE_AHEAD_S=0.001` instead of `0.075`. This checks
whether the CP1401 timed TX error was simply caused by queueing too much future
burst data before hardware time advanced.

| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| `timed_meta_none_file` | 2 | 0 | 551 | 277984 | 0.00078745 s |

Both timed captures were still empty because the CP1401 helper still suppressed
RX capture after the timed TX error. The failure moved only slightly earlier
than CP1401 and stayed around 278k to 279k queued samples.

## Interpretation

Reducing pace-ahead from `75 ms` to `1 ms` does not remove the timed
`writeStream` failure. CP1402 closes the simple "too much lookahead" theory,
but it remains non-diagnostic for RF because the helper still aborted capture
before any RX samples were written.

The next required diagnostic is to preserve RX capture after the timed TX error
and log the failing Soapy return.
