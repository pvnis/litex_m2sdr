## Interpretation

CP1409 confirms the CP1408 instrumentation works and closes another lower-path
failure mode: the live weak append is not accompanied by Soapy write-return
errors, worker late/underflow/acquire errors, `_dmaReleaseWrite()` late
release rows, or timed-commit slip trace rows. The persistent slips are all
negative, meaning these tracked timed releases are early relative to hardware
time, not late.

The append remains in the same weak class as CP1398/CP1400/CP1406, while
same-run pre/post-DMA TX still decodes PBCH. The current live-gNB CF32 and
CI16-to-CF32 conversion/downsample suspicion is therefore not the leading
explanation. The next useful branch should inspect or change AD9361/RF level
state or the real live TX/RX RF path under the gNB-owned process.
