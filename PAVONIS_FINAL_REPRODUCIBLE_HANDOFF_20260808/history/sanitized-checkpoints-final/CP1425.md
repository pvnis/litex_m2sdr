## Interpretation

CP1425 answers the user's CF32 concern in the same direction as the earlier
evidence: the live gNB TX path is actively using `CF32` at the Soapy boundary,
the TX guard remains PBCH-decodable before and after DMA, and Soapy/LiteX TX
counters are clean. Making the live gNB "emit CF32" is not a new config-only
fix; the remaining native-float idea is not supported by this checkpoint.

The raw ladder gate remains blocked:

- raw scheduled TRACK mean SNR is `-21.716985 dB`
- usable opportunities at `>= -10 dB` are `0/66`
- TRACK PBCH hits are `0`

The unexpected part is that the immediate gNB-owned LiteX append also regressed
to `0/50` targeted and `0/847` sweep, despite the no-arm path and good RX
readback (`66 dB`, no overflows). That means CP1425 is a useful failure
isolation point but not a clean CP1424-style companion RX baseline.
