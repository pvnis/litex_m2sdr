## Interpretation

CP1427 is mechanically clean and diagnostically negative.

It rules out the larger CP1425/CP1426 append cap/window as a sufficient
explanation for the companion append regression. Returning the CP1425 helper
to the CP1424 `0.25 s` append cap restores the expected file length and a
CP1424-comparable decode grid, but does not restore PBCH decode.

The remaining discriminator is the CP1424/CP1400 start/settle/helper path
itself, not srsUE concurrency and not append cap alone. The next useful
checkpoint should move one more variable from CP1427 toward CP1424, preferably
by running the CP1425 helper with CP1424 cap plus the CP1400/CP1424 `15 s`
remote settle first, or by directly comparing the CP1425 start helper against
the CP1400 start helper under the same cap and stop timing.
