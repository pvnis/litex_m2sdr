## Result

CP1428 failed to restore CP1424 append decode. Adding the CP1400/CP1424-style
remote `15 s` settle to the CP1427 path keeps the append in the expected
`0.25 s` file-length/RMS class, but decode remains fully negative:
`0/198` targeted and `0/847` aligned.

This rules out CP1427's local `12 s` wait versus CP1400/CP1424's remote
`15 s` settle/inspect as a sufficient cause of the CP1425/CP1426/CP1427 append
regression. TX remains a clean control and active live `CF32`.

Next useful discriminator is now the start helper itself: compare the CP1425
start helper against the CP1400 start helper under the same `0.25 s` cap,
stop/package timing, and no-srsUE control. Do not repeat CP1425, CP1426,
CP1427, or CP1428 unchanged, and do not resume concurrent NRSA until the
gNB-owned LiteX append baseline is explainable again.
