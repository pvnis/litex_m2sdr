## Result

CP1429 failed to restore CP1424 append decode. Reverting only the start helper
to CP1400 style under the CP1428 cap, stop/package, no-srsUE, and remote
settle structure still gives fully negative append decode:
`0/198` targeted and `0/847` aligned.

This closes start-helper identity alone as a sufficient cause under the
CP1428/CP1429 runner structure. TX remains a clean control and active live
`CF32`; RX readback remains at the intended SSB-centered geometry with normal
gain and zero overflows.

The remaining useful discriminator is no longer "CP1425 helper versus CP1400
helper" in isolation. The next checkpoint should rerun the known-positive
CP1424 wrapper unchanged as a same-day control, or perform a strict CP1424
wrapper/generated-runner versus CP1429 runner/env/log diff before returning to
NRSA. Do not resume concurrent srsUE/bladeRF raw-gate work until the
gNB-owned LiteX append baseline is explainable again.
