## Result

CP1430 reproduced the CP1424 positive gNB-owned LiteX RX append baseline on
the same day as the CP1425-CP1429 investigation: immediate readStream append,
state dump disabled, no arm-file path, RX centered at `1839650000 Hz`, and TX
live `CF32` all remain healthy. The append decodes (`56/198` targeted and
`68/847` aligned), TX is clean, release counters are clean, and [host-redacted]/nuc7
cleanup checks pass.

The important new discriminator is the decode geometry. CP1430's positive
append decode uses Fs/center/SSB `11520000 / 1839650000 / 1839650000`, matching
the RX-side SSB-centered append. The CP1425-CP1429 negative append summaries
were generated with decode center `1842500000` while the RX sidecar reported
`1839650000`. That makes the CP1425-CP1429 append failures suspect as
offline-analysis artifacts rather than RF regressions.
