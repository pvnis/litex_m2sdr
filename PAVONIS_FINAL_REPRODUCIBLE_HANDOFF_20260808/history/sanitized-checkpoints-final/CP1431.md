## Interpretation

CP1431 overturns the CP1425-CP1429 companion-append regression conclusion. The
saved gNB-owned LiteX RX append files from all five checkpoints are decodable
when the offline decoder is centered on the RX/SSB frequency. The previous
`0/50`, `0/198`, and `0/847` companion-append failures were caused by a
decode-center mismatch, not by a demonstrated loss of the LiteX companion
append path.

This matters most for CP1425. CP1425's concurrent srsUE/bladeRF raw
scheduled-TRACK run still failed the real ladder gate:

- `66` TRACK opportunities
- mean `event=track_opportunity meas_snr=-21.716985 dB`
- `0/66` opportunities at or above `-10 dB`
- zero normal TRACK PBCH hits

But CP1431 shows the same CP1425 companion gNB-owned LiteX append was actually
decodable offline when analyzed at the correct center. Therefore CP1425 is now
a cleaner isolation result: the gNB/LiteX companion append and TX-side health
were not the blocker, while the bladeRF/srsUE scheduled TRACK raw metric
remained sub-threshold.

CP1426 through CP1429 are also reclassified. Their no-srsUE companion append
captures are positive under corrected decode geometry, so they no longer prove
append-path regressions from helper identity, append cap, local wait, or remote
settle. They remain useful as clean positive LiteX append controls, but their
old negative summaries should not be used as RF evidence.
