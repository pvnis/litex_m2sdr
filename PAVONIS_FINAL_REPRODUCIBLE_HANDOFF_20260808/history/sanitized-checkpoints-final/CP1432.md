## Goal

Prevent a repeat of the CP1425-CP1429 false companion-append regression by
creating a future CP1425-style concurrent srsUE/bladeRF runner whose RX append
analysis uses the RX append center, not the transmit carrier center.

NRSA, Android, PRACH, or any `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edit.

## Interpretation

CP1432 does not raise the raw link budget and does not itself prove a new RF
result. It removes a known analysis trap from the next concurrent-run tool:
future RX append decode artifacts should not falsely report `0/50`,
`0/198`, or `0/847` because they were decoded at the TX carrier center.

This directly supports the CP1431 interpretation and the user's proposed
self-RX isolation avenue: in CP1425, the same-window M2SDR self-RX file decodes
after the center fix while bladeRF/srsUE scheduled TRACK stays near `-22 dB`.
The next RF ladder step can therefore focus on the bladeRF/srsUE/OTA receive
path and raw scheduled TRACK SNR, with a guarded companion self-RX capture if a
fresh same-window control is needed.
