## Interpretation

CP1433 is not a new raw TRACK-gate datapoint because srsUE/bladeRF never
entered TRACK. It is, however, a strong same-window isolation checkpoint:

- gNB TX is clean and live `CF32`.
- pre-DMA, post-DMA, and digital TX all contain decodable PBCH.
- gNB Soapy/release/worker counters are clean.
- the M2SDR gNB-owned self-RX append decodes in the same run
  (`10/50` targeted, `85/847` aligned sweep).
- bladeRF/srsUE, at the same nominal CP1425 RF geometry, sees `1000`
  empty acquisition trials and zero TRACK opportunities.

This points away from live TX and toward the bladeRF/srsUE/OTA receive path, or
a transient acquisition/receive-path regression on `[host-redacted]`/bladeRF. It also
validates the user's proposed same-window self-RX diagnostic avenue: once the
deinterleave/scaling/control-path was guarded by CP1432, the self-RX side could
decode while bladeRF/srsUE failed.
