## Interpretation

CP1434 extends CP1433's isolation:

- CP1433: same-window M2SDR self-RX decoded and gNB/TX were clean, but
  srsUE/bladeRF acquisition saw `1000` empty trials and zero TRACK
  opportunities.
- CP1434: with srsUE removed, an independent bladeRF metadata capture at the
  same receive geometry is continuous and unclipped, but still has no PBCH
  decode (`0/847`), while corrected TX guard and M2SDR self-RX remain positive.

This moves the failure from "srsUE acquisition state might be broken" toward
the external bladeRF/OTA receive path under the CP1433-style CF32+self-RX gNB
window. It does not prove the bladeRF hardware is globally broken, because
CP1382 previously decoded independent bladeRF PBCH at the same `7.68 Msps` /
`6.144 MHz` receive geometry using the older CS16 no-self-RX gNB capture
harness. The leading branch is now to separate:

- CP1433/CP1434 CF32+self-RX gNB window effect,
- transient bladeRF/OTA receive degradation,
- or a remaining path-specific difference between the independent capture and
  historical CP1382.

## Next

Do not edit `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic. A useful next checkpoint is a
small A/B around CP1434: rerun the known CP1382 independent bladeRF capture
class, or a CF32 independent capture without M2SDR self-RX, to determine
whether the external bladeRF PBCH loss is tied to the CP1433-style CF32+self-RX
window or is now persistent on the bladeRF/OTA receive path.
