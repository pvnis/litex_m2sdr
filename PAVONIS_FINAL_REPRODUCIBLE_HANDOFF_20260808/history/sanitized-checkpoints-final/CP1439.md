# Checkpoint 1439 - No-gNB BladeRF Gain-Response Baseline At CP1436 Geometry

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

After CP1438 showed that the no-gNB bladeRF noise baseline is essentially the
same level class as failed TX-on CP1436, test whether the bladeRF receive path
still has a visible gain-dependent noise-floor response. This isolates a gross
RX-control failure, without running gNB, srsUE, Android, PRACH, NAS, M2SDR
self-RX, or `ue_sync_nr.c` changes.

This checkpoint is not a raw scheduled-TRACK datapoint. It is a no-GNB
receive-chain control baseline.

## Interpretation

CP1439 rules out a gross bladeRF RX flatline or fully ignored gain-control
path: the raw no-GNB noise floor responds strongly to requested gain, rising
about `+6.7 dB` from gain0 to gain32 and about `+22.0 dB` from gain0 to
gain48. The CP1439 gain32 short capture also matches the CP1438 gain32-class
1 s noise baseline, so CP1438's no-GNB noise result is reproducible.

The readback caveat matters: `bladeRF-cli` printed the same overall/full gain
readback for every requested gain, even though raw RMS changed substantially.
Do not use that `overall/full` readback alone to conclude that gain requests
are or are not applied.

This does not fix the live downlink. It narrows the leading branch: the bladeRF
ADC/RX stream is alive enough to show gain-dependent noise, while CP1438 shows
that live gNB TX-on did not visibly raise the gain32-class bladeRF samples over
no-GNB. That is most consistent with physical RX path/coupling, antenna/cable
state, or another RF routing/coupling issue between M2SDR TX and bladeRF RX,
rather than a total bladeRF receive-chain software flatline.

## Next Step
