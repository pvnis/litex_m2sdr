## Interpretation

CP1440 validates the same-window discriminator. The live gNB/M2SDR side is
healthy enough in this exact window to produce decodable self-RX PBCH and
decodable pre/post-DMA TX guard PBCH, with clean gNB TX counters and active
`CF32` TX readback. At the same time, bladeRF/srsUE at requested gain `48`
sees `1000` empty acquisition trials and never reaches TRACK.

That result points away from live TX content, the CP1432 decode-center split,
native-CF32/CI16 conversion, and gNB scheduler/release counters as the
immediate explanation. It strengthens the leading branch that the remaining
failure is in the external bladeRF/OTA receive path, RF coupling/routing, or a
transient bladeRF/srsUE acquisition-state issue.

CP1440 also closes "try higher bladeRF/srsUE gain after CP1439" as a simple
software-only recovery attempt for this guarded concurrent path: requested
gain `48` was applied at stream start, yet acquisition stayed empty.

## Next Step

Do not rerun CP1440 unchanged and do not resume `ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler/anchor
