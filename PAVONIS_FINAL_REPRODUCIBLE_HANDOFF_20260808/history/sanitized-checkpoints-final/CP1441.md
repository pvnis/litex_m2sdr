## Interpretation

CP1441 separates srsUE acquisition from bladeRF raw receive at the CP1440 gain
class. The M2SDR self-RX path is again healthy and decodable in the same
window (`22/50` targeted, `109/847` aligned), and corrected TX guard plus gNB
counters remain clean.

The independent bladeRF raw capture at requested gain `48` still does not
decode PBCH: one-shot KO, sweep `0/847`, only two CSI PCI1 windows, and weak
same-window pre-DMA correlation. Unlike CP1434 gain32, the bladeRF capture
level is much higher (`RAW_RMS_REL_[date-redacted]=0.112006394`, one-shot `-19.02 dBFS`,
max `952`) but the extra level is not coherent PBCH energy and is not clipped.

This closes "srsUE acquisition alone is hiding a gain48 raw bladeRF decode" as
a likely explanation. It strengthens the external bladeRF/OTA receive path,
RF coupling/routing, or bladeRF receive-state/overdrive/noise hypothesis. It
does not unlock the ladder and it is not a raw scheduled-TRACK metric.
