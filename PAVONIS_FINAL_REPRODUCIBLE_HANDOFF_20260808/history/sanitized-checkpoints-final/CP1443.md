## Interpretation

CP1443 shows bladeRF RX bandwidth matters somewhat: narrowing from `6.144 MHz`
to `3.84 MHz` improves the controlled-tone SNR. It does not recover a clean
stable receive path. The same live-geometry tone still fails the CP847 quality
gate and remains unstable in 10 ms windows.

This keeps the leading branch on the bladeRF/OTA receive side. The next
software-only receiver split should test filter/path/channel behavior or a
more aggressive bandwidth bracket, but a physical path/coupling inspection or
controlled path swap remains more decisive than further scheduler work.

## Next Step

Do not rerun CP1443 unchanged. If staying software-only, test one of:

- bladeRF RX filter `bypass` at the CP1443 `3.84 MHz` bandwidth and gain48.
- a stronger bandwidth bracket around the tone, such as `2.0 MHz`, while
  watching for edge effects around the `+500 kHz` tone.
- RX channel/path/coupling swap if the hardware allows it.
