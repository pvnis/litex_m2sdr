## Interpretation

Filter bypass is useful for level and SNR at BW3.84/gain48, but it does not
fix the long-window coherence/stability problem. This points away from
bladeRF's normal FIR filter as the sole failure cause. It remains consistent
with the CP1441-CP1443 leading branch: external bladeRF/OTA receive path,
coupling/routing state, bladeRF receive-state/noise/overdrive, or another
physical/control-path issue that creates level without stable 10 ms coherence.

Do not rerun CP1444 unchanged. Do not resume scheduler/anchor edits from this
result. Before returning to raw scheduled TRACK, require both raw level and
10 ms tone stability to improve.

Useful next splits:

- Physical/coupling/RX path swap if available.
- If software-only, bracket narrower bladeRF BW around the bypass case while
  watching edge effects.
- A same-window M2SDR self-RX versus bladeRF raw capture remains a useful
  discriminator if the self-RX instrument can be kept stable.
