## Interpretation

The CP1445 instability is not just the original `+500 kHz` tone placement. A
near-`+100 kHz` capture tone still produces high SNR with a wide 10 ms
frequency-bin spread. This keeps the leading branch on receive-path/coupling,
bladeRF state/noise/overdrive, or a persistent spectral/control-path artifact
rather than a single expected-tone placement issue.

Do not rerun CP1446 unchanged. This result still does not unlock scheduled
TRACK or Android/NAS work. The next useful branch is either physical/coupling
inspection or a live-PBCH/SSB-compatible receiver-path split, because BW2.0 is
too narrow to treat as an SSB/PBCH recovery.
