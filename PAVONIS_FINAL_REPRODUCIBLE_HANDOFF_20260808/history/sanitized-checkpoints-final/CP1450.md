## Verdict

FAIL for external M2SDR-to-bladeRF tone quality.

Forcing bladeRF RX [interface-redacted] mode to `FPGA` was accepted and active during the
capture, and the helper restored `Host` afterward. The external tone remained
present at a healthy level, but it stayed in the same bad 10 ms bin-step /
phase-instability class as CP1449. This closes simple bladeRF `[interface-redacted]`
selection as a software-only recovery path for the external receive problem.

This is not a raw scheduled-TRACK metric datapoint and does not unlock the
ladder.

## Interpretation

CP1450 closes bladeRF RFIC `[interface-redacted]=fpga` as a simple external-path
coherence fix. The result is still level-positive but globally incoherent over
10 ms windows. This reinforces the CP1448/CP1449 boundary:

- internal RFIC BIST is coherent;
- external M2SDR-to-bladeRF path is present and gain/filter/BW/[interface-redacted]
  responsive;
- the external path still has a receive/coupling/front-end/spectral coherence
  failure that software knobs tested so far do not remove.

The user's self-RX suggestion remains useful, but that discriminator is already
represented by CP1425 after CP1431's corrected decode-center analysis and by
CP1433/CP1440/CP1447: M2SDR self-RX can decode in the same window while the
bladeRF side remains weak or PBCH-negative. CP1450 deliberately avoided rerun
of that branch and tested a different one-variable bladeRF control.
