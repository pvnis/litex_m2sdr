## Interpretation

CP1458 closes the remaining safe current-geometry BW2.0/gain48 filter variant.
The normal filter lowers the bladeRF no-TX floor by about `3.8 dB` compared
with bypass, but the M2SDR TX-on capture is still indistinguishable from
no-TX. M2SDR self-RX proves the transmitter was active and stable in-window.

Together with CP1453, CP1454, CP1455, CP1456, and CP1457, this leaves no
remote-only evidence that the current external LiteX tone is reaching bladeRF
RX1/RF3(B). The leading branch remains external bladeRF/OTA receive/coupling/
routing/front-end state or physical path, not live M2SDR TX generation, srsUE
scheduler logic, explicit RX2, retune-reset, filter bypass, gain48 floor
masking, or the CP1446 `+100 kHz` tone placement.

CP1458 is not a raw scheduled TRACK metric datapoint and does not unlock the
ladder.

## Do Not Repeat

Do not rerun BW2.0/normal/gain48 on/off tone capture unchanged. It is
no-TX-like with `-0.001 dB` TX-on RMS delta.

Do not return to `ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler/anchor changes until a receive-path
change raises the raw live TRACK metric.
