## Findings

The active gate is unchanged: further ladder movement is still gated on the
raw live scheduled TRACK downlink metric, specifically mean
`event=track_opportunity meas_snr` and the fraction of opportunities at or
above `-10 dB`. Recent controlled runs remain around `-20` to `-22 dB` mean
with low or zero usable opportunity fraction and zero useful TRACK PBCH.

CP1467 closes the remaining safe read-only/in-process bladeRF state gap after
CP1465 and CP1466. During active RX, the bladeRF can capture one metadata chunk
and read RFIC/RF-switch state in the same process without showing RX
power-down, VCO unlock, invalid state, asymmetric gain readback, or BIST/test
latch. The RF switch closes while RX is enabled and returns open after disable.

CP869 already recorded the active route label for this class of RX path:
`RX1 <= RF3(B)`. Earlier idle `OPEN` route prints are disabled-state snapshots,
not proof of an always-open active RX route.

The same-window M2SDR self-RX idea is valid as a diagnostic pattern, but the
unchanged experiment has already been run in the current branch. CP1425 and
CP1431 prove that saved gNB-owned M2SDR RX append files can decode in the same
window where bladeRF/srsUE scheduled TRACK remains near `-22 dB`. CP1433,
CP1440, and CP1441 add guarded same-window M2SDR self-RX positives while
bladeRF/srsUE or independent bladeRF receive remains acquisition-negative or
PBCH-negative. If this branch is revisited because the self-RX instrument
itself starts fighting back, use the CP1424-style immediate/no-arm
`OCUDU_SOAPY_RX_USE_READSTREAM=1` path, keep state dump disabled, and decode
the RX append at center/SSB `1839650000` rather than the stale `1842500000`
analysis center. Do not rerun the CP1425/CP1433/CP1440/CP1441 same-window
comparisons unchanged.

The cabled ladder runbook remains directionally consistent with the current
gate: do not move to PRACH, RAR, RRC/NAS, PDU/IP, or Android attach while the
downlink receive gate is below threshold.

## Conclusion

Status: approval-boundary audit complete, no ladder unlock.

There is no remaining safe, non-redundant software-only or read-only bladeRF
state check that is likely to raise the raw scheduled-TRACK SNR gate. The next
material branch needs one of:

1. A physical or independent receive-path/coupling/routing change.
2. Explicit approval for a bounded calibration/correction branch.
3. Explicit approval for a bias-tee or other hardware-affecting branch.
4. A materially different bladeRF external-RX/front-end path, not a rerun of
   CP1425 through CP1468.

Until one of those variables changes, scheduler/anchor edits, Android/NAS/PRACH
steps, and repeated same-window self-RX/bladeRF comparisons should stay gated
behind the raw downlink metric.
