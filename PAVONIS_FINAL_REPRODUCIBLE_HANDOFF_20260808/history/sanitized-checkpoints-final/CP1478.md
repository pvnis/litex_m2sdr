## Interpretation

CP1478 does not support a live-gNB-only cadence failure as the current primary
frontier. The known continuous helper control no longer reproduces CP1166
pass-class RF quality on the current same hardware/path. The live gNB leg and
the helper leg both arrive at about the same bladeRF raw level (`-29.5 dBFS`)
and both fail PBCH over RF, while live host-visible TX data remains decodable
pre/post-DMA and TX counters are clean.

The current boundary is therefore earlier than "live real-time cadence breaks
an otherwise good RF path". It is a shared current RF egress/coupling/receive
level or state regression that affects both helper and live runs at this
geometry. Do not move up to NRSA/PRACH, do not resume scheduler/anchor edits,
and do not rerun this exact helper/live pair unchanged.
