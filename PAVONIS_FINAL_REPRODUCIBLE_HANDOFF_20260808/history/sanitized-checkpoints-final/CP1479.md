## Interpretation

CP1479 explains the CP1478 weakness at the measured receive geometry: current
TX-on captures are essentially no-TX-floor-like. The CP1478 live gNB digital
TX path can still be PBCH-decodable pre/post-DMA, and the CP1478 helper/live
TX mechanics were clean, but the bladeRF receive capture sees only a sub-dB
lift over the no-TX floor.

This does not support moving up to NRSA/PRACH and does not support further
`ue_sync_nr.c` scheduler/anchor edits. It also weakens the specific
live-gNB-only cadence theory at the current boundary because the known helper
control is floor-limited too.

## Next Step

Restore or explain the external received-signal level before ladder movement.
The lowest-impact next software-side check is a TX-on/TX-off differential using
the known CP1166/CP1478 continuous helper at the same geometry, with a gNB-owned
LiteX self-RX sidecar if available, so the M2SDR egress can be confirmed in the
same window while bladeRF remains floor-like. If the self-RX still decodes but
bladeRF stays within about `1 dB` of no-TX, the unresolved boundary is the
external receive/coupling path or bladeRF receive state at that geometry, not
NRSA, PRACH, or live gNB cadence.
