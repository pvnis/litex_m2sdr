So the helper is weak but decoder-visible, while the live gNB remains below
that helper on PBCH/CSI metrics even though the same-window digital TX payload

## Verdict

CP1481 confirms that the live gNB path is not failing because host-visible
digital TX lacks PBCH or because the bladeRF capture is discontinuous/clipped.
The live gNB emits a weak same-window-correlated RF component, but it does not
produce helper-class decoder-visible SSB at bladeRF under the current geometry.

This supports staying in the live real-time TX cadence / presentation branch.
It does not unlock NRSA, PRACH, Android, NAS, or scheduler/sync-anchor edits,
and it does not approve a hardware-affecting branch.

Next checkpoint should be a software-only live-cadence diagnostic, not an
unchanged CP1478-CP1481 repeat: compare helper and live in a tighter
self-contained back-to-back cadence/control run, or instrument the live TX
egress timing/cadence against the continuous helper while preserving the same
RF geometry and same-window digital PBCH guard.
