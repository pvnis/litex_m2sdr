## Verdict

CP1484 completes the requested back-to-back comparison. Live remains worse than
the continuous helper on the same hardware, but the helper control itself is
now degraded relative to the pass-class CP1482/CP1483 results even with the
same CP1481 payload and healthy raw amplitude. This is not a weak-power result:
the helper capture is about `-27.90 dBFS` with max `562`, yet one-shot PBCH
fails and the sweep drops to `34/847` PBCH windows.

The immediate frontier is therefore frame-scale coherence/frequency/window
stability across both helper and live, with live still the weaker case. The
next lowest-risk step is offline analysis of the CP1484 captures using the
existing CFO/SSB-offset sweep tools, especially because the helper refcorr peak
moved to `-9000 Hz` while CP1482/CP1483 were near `+[date-redacted]/+2500 Hz`. Do not
move to NRSA/PRACH/Android/NAS, do not edit `ue_sync_nr.c`, and do not start a
hardware-affecting branch.
