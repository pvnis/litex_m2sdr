## Interpretation

CP1488 makes the CP1484 degradation more specific. CP1484 is not simply lacking
a phase-calibrated copy of the reference waveform in the bladeRF capture: many
CSI-only and even no-CSI windows still show clear local correlation to the
expected repeated pre-DMA vector. The helper-vector phase calibration and broad
local CFO search can find coherent waveform islands in the degraded capture.

That does not make those windows PBCH-decodable. In CP1482, high local
correlation, stable `+2500 Hz` best frequency, and PBCH success mostly move
together. In CP1484, broad local correlation and PBCH success separate:
CSI-only/PBCH-negative windows can correlate strongly, while PBCH-positive rows
are often not the highest-correlation rows.

This weakens explanations based only on raw power, helper-vector capture phase,
full-window waveform presence, or one static CFO. The remaining boundary is
PBCH/SSB-specific coherence or demodulation structure inside locally correlated
windows.

## Verdict

Do not rerun CP1478-CP1488 unchanged. The current hardware/path/payload and
helper mechanics remain validated by CP1482/CP1483, CP1484 still shows an
intermittent helper coherence regression, and CP1488 shows that broad local
waveform correlation is not enough to predict PBCH decode in the degraded
helper run.

Recommended next checkpoint: stay offline and run a PBCH/SSB-specific local
analysis on CP1482 vs CP1484 windows, separating PSS/SSS/PBCH-bearing symbol or
subcarrier behavior if practical. If that does not isolate the split, the next
RF step should be a tightly controlled helper reproducibility bracket rather
than a hardware branch.
