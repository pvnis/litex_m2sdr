## Interpretation

CP1492 supports the user's latest theory direction: this is not a weak path or
path/power problem. The helper leg had stronger raw amplitude than CP1484 and
clean mechanics, yet produced `0/847` PBCH with `203/847` CSI. The live leg
also had clean host-visible digital TX and clean runtime counters, and it did
produce a one-shot PBCH OK at `-29.82 dBFS`, but its aligned sweep remained
sparse (`4/847` PBCH, `20/847` CSI) with very weak broad correlation to both
CP1145 and same-window pre-DMA references.

The good live one-shot proves the live path can hit decoder-valid frames on the
same hardware, but the low sweep density and weak broad correlation show the
coherence is not stable over the capture. The helper result prevents narrowing
this to live gNB alone; it is a frame/window-scale coherence or decoder-facing
handoff problem visible even in the continuous helper when the current state is
bad, with live still sparse and intermittently islanded.
