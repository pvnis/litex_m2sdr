live capture with a one-shot PBCH OK but sparse sweep success, test whether

## Interpretation

CP1493 strongly confirms the decoder-facing handoff failure on the CP1492 live
capture. A live 1 ms window that blind search marked PBCH KO is forced-decodable
with positive track SNR and stable payload `[0e 20 06]` across multiple
timing/CFO choices. That is not a weak-link result and not lack of PBCH content.

The helper result is mixed: one helper CSI-rich/PBCH-dead window can be forced
to CRC OK, but only in `2/54` cases and at low track SNR, while another helper
window with good track SNR does not decode. This keeps the helper degradation
in the same broad class but shows the live CP1492 blind-KO window is the more
actionable forced-recovery specimen.

The repeated `n_hf=0` requirement matches CP1490/CP1491 and keeps half-frame /
SSB candidate selection in the suspect set.

## Verdict

Do not start a hardware branch and do not move up the ladder yet. The next
highest-value work is source-level/no-RF inspection or instrumentation of the
blind PBCH search-to-track handoff: timing offset, coarse CFO, PBCH DMRS
selection/thresholding, and half-frame/SSB-candidate selection. The CP1492 live
CSI-KO window `win1p0ms_start0p016500s` is now the best small reproduction:
blind PBCH KO, forced track OK with positive SNR.
