## Interpretation

CP1496 validates the CP1495 PBCH DMRS threshold plumbing in live RF: the
thresholded blind path finds PCI1 and produces PBCH CRC success during initial
cell search / FIND.

It does not unlock the ladder. The scheduled TRACK metric remains in the same
failed class as the earlier raw gate: mean about `-22 dB`, no opportunities at
or above `-10 dB`, and zero TRACK PBCH hits. This keeps the active problem at
frame-scale live cadence/coherence or scheduled-window selection/quality, not
at a missing runtime threshold variable and not at a weak RF path/power branch.
