## Goal

Run the same live SSB-centered geometry as CP1496, but explicitly leave the
PBCH DMRS threshold at the default path. This checks whether CP1496's live blind
acquisition came from the new `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PBCH_DMRS_THR=0.25` override or
from a stochastic good live frame.

This checkpoint does not start a hardware branch and does not edit
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic.

## Interpretation

CP1497 makes the CP1496 threshold effect concrete. Back-to-back on the same
geometry, CP1496 with `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PBCH_DMRS_THR=0.25` acquired PCI1 at trial
`158` and reached FIND/TRACK, while CP1497 with the default threshold had
`1000` empty trials and never reached TRACK.

This closes "threshold plumbing may not matter live" as a theory. The threshold
override is needed for live blind acquisition in this current weak/coherence-
impaired class. It still does not solve the ladder gate: CP1496's scheduled
TRACK metric remains the active blocker at mean `-21.862015 dB` and `0/66`
opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

## Next

Keep `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PBCH_DMRS_THR=0.25` for future live acquisition rungs.
Further work should target the live cadence/coherence or scheduled-window
quality boundary that leaves TRACK sub-threshold after acquisition.
