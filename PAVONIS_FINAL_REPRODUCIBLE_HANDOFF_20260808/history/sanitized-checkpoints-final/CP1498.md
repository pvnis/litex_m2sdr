## Interpretation

CP1498 confirms the CP1496/CP1497 threshold conclusion under instrumentation:
`PAVONIS_SRSUE_PBCH_DMRS_THR=0.25` remains necessary and active for live
blind acquisition in this class. The thresholded run again reaches cell search
and FIND PBCH, but scheduled TRACK remains below gate at about `-21 dB` mean
with `0%` of opportunities at or above `-10 dB`.

The new result is the RX dump metadata. The raw file is internally consistent,
but the bounded default-RX_NOW dump contains `96/512` short/discontinuous
returns. That supports staying on the live cadence/coherence path rather than
moving to PRACH/Android/NAS or changing `ue_sync_nr.c` scheduling/anchor logic.

No hardware branch is justified from this checkpoint. The gNB TX side remains
clean, and the failure is still a live frame/window cadence or decoder-facing
scheduled-window quality problem.
