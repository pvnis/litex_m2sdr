## Interpretation

CP1500 reinforces the frame/window-local failure model rather than a hardware
path/power model.

The helper PBCH-OK controls behave as expected: public search decodes at the
default threshold and all lower thresholds, and forced track also decodes. One
helper CSI/PBCH-KO window (`0.056 s`) has the same threshold-gate shape seen in
CP1494: public search fails at default/`0.50` but decodes at `0.25` and below.
Another helper CSI/PBCH-KO window (`0.190 s`) is forced-decodable but not fixed
by threshold alone.

The live capture is stricter. The live `0.159 s` CSI/PBCH-KO window contains
PBCH-decodable content under forced timing/CFO (`9/90` OK), but public threshold
relaxation alone does not recover it. The live best-CSI window around `0.069 s`
does not recover under either bounded forced track or public threshold scan,
despite CSI `-1.3 dB`. Therefore high CSI SNR in this capture is not sufficient
evidence of a decoder-valid PBCH window.

This does not unlock the raw scheduled TRACK ladder gate. It narrows the next
offline question to what distinguishes CP1499 live `0.159 s` forced-decodable
content from live `0.069 s` unrecoverable content and helper threshold-rescued
content.
