# Checkpoint 1502 - CP1499 SSB search PBCH-DMRS trace

## Question

CP1500 showed three different CP1499 classes:

- helper controls decode through normal public search;
- helper `0.056 s` is rescued by lowering the blind PBCH-DMRS threshold;
- live `0.159 s` is forced-decodable but not rescued by threshold relaxation.

This checkpoint instruments the blind-search candidate handoff offline to find
whether the live failure is still the DMRS threshold gate, or whether public
search is choosing the wrong timing/CFO/PBCH-DMRS candidate before the gate.

No RF was run. No hardware branch was started. No `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor
logic was edited.

Key observations:

- The helper PBCH-OK controls remain normal: public default threshold decodes,
  and selected PBCH-DMRS correlations are above `0.5`.
- The helper `0.056 s` CSI-KO case is the known CP1494/CP1500 threshold-gated
  shape: public default fails, public `0.25` succeeds, and selected
  PBCH-DMRS correlation is about `0.276`.
- Live `0.159 s` is not a threshold-only case. Public search never gets a CRC
  even at near-zero threshold; its selected PBCH-DMRS correlations top out at
  only about `0.047`, while the forced CP1500 offset (`t=13400`,
  `cfo=80 Hz`) has PBCH-DMRS correlation `0.238` and CRC OK. The forced
  offset is `+67` samples after the public-search timing (`13333`), about
  `5.8 us` at `11.52 Msps`.
- Live `0.069 s` remains unrecovered: neither public search nor the selected
  forced probe finds a decodable PBCH.
- Helper `0.190 s` is forced-decodable but not public-decodable even at
  near-zero threshold. Its forced and public timing are aligned, so that one
  points more toward CFO/PBCH candidate handoff than gross timing.

## Interpretation

CP1502 keeps the hardware branch closed for now and narrows the software
boundary. The threshold override remains useful for the helper threshold-gated
class, but live `0.159 s` fails because public blind search lands on a
decoder-facing candidate that has low PBCH-DMRS correlation and CRC KO, while
a nearby forced offset is PBCH-decodable. That points at the blind
PSS/coarse-CFO/timing-to-PBCH handoff under live cadence/coherence impairment,
not a weak RF link and not merely the default `0.5` DMRS threshold.

## Next

Run a local timing/CFO neighborhood trace around the public-search anchor and
the forced-decodable offsets for live `0.159 s` and helper `0.190 s`. The
target is to determine whether a small timing refinement, a CFO handoff
change, or PBCH candidate-selection rule explains the forced-only cases before
touching runtime acquisition code.
