## Interpretation

CP1503 supports the user's no-hardware framing. The replay/path/power branch
remains closed for now: the saved live and helper windows contain decodable
PBCH content when the decoder is forced onto the right local timing/CFO
hypothesis. The live `0.159 s` failure is not explained by weak link budget or
by the PBCH DMRS threshold alone, because public search stays KO near zero
threshold while forced neighborhoods contain many CRC-OK islands.

The sharper boundary is public/blind SSB search handoff under frame-scale
coherence/cadence impairment:

- public timing/CFO/candidate selection can land on a decoder-facing candidate
  with low DMRS corr and CRC KO even when nearby forced hypotheses decode;
- the useful CFO hypothesis can differ materially from the source measure CFO
  or the CP1502 forced CFO, especially in `live_159_forced_only`;
- not every CSI-visible live window is recoverable, as shown by
  `live_069_unrecovered`.
