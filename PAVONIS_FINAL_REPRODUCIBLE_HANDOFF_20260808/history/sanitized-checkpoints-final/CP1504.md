## Method

the public candidate's effective CFO as:

```text

```

It then compares the public selected timing/effective-CFO rows against CP1503's
forced-track PBCH CRC OK timing/CFO island.

## Interpretation

`live_159_forced_only` has two distinct public-handoff misses. Public search
usually locks timing at `t=13333` but only tries effective CFOs from about
`-7031` to `-469 Hz` at that dominant timing, and all are CRC KO. CP1503 shows
near-public timing can decode, but only around `cfo=2500 Hz` in the tested
grid, leaving a `2968.750 Hz` gap from the nearest public effective CFO.
CP1503 also shows a separate later island around `t=13400`, which public PSS
selection does not choose.

`helper_190_forced_only` is the cleaner CFO/handoff positive. Public timing is
already aligned at `t=2335` in `12/13` rows, yet all public rows are CRC KO.
The forced OK CFOs at that timing are `-2500,-312,0 Hz`; the dominant public
effective CFOs miss those, with the nearest gap `468.750 Hz`. This weakens a
pure timing-offset explanation and points directly at CFO hypothesis/handoff or
PBCH candidate scoring.

`live_069_unrecovered` remains the negative guard. It has no forced OK island
in CP1503 and no public CRC OK row in CP1504, so any next prototype must not
turn this case into a false positive.

`helper_056_threshold_control` remains the threshold-control class. Public rows
already produce CRC OK under the lowered threshold path, and its public
effective CFOs are close to the forced island (`156.250 Hz` gap).
