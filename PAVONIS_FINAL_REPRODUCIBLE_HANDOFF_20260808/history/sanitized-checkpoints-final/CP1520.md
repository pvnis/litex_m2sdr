Run the requested same-hardware continuous-helper versus live-gNB comparison
after CP1519, without taking a hardware branch and without the large
capture-window DMA dump. This tests the current theory directly: the RF path
and power are fine under a continuous helper, while live real-time TX remains

## Helper Result

The continuous helper is a strong same-hardware positive control:

- one-shot RMS: `-27.05 dBFS`
- one-shot PBCH: `KO`
- aligned sweep: `311/847` PBCH CRC OK, `311/847` PBCH PCI1,
  `263/847` CSI PCI1
- top CSI/PBCH windows: CSI SNR up to `+13.7 dB`
- refcorr vs CP1481 pre-DMA reference: `0.748186184` at `+2500 Hz`

This is much stronger than the live leg and confirms the current physical
path/power are not the leading branch to approve.

## Verdict

CP1520 strongly supports the user's current theory. On the same hardware, in
immediate sequence, the continuous helper produces a strong decodable RF
baseline, while live gNB produces only sparse good frames even though host
pre-DMA/post-DMA TX and the guarded worker/write boundaries are clean.

Combined with CP1519, the remaining boundary is below host-visible
capture-window post-DMA contents and below the clean host submit/write path.
The next useful software-only work is lower-overhead effective-egress
instrumentation around LitePCIe reader / TimedTXArbiter / FPGA/AD9361 handoff,
or another similarly direct probe of live egress cadence. Do not rerun CP1520
unchanged, do not start a hardware branch, do not edit `ue_sync_nr.c`
sync/anchor logic, and do not move to PRACH/Android/NAS while the downlink
gate remains unstable.
