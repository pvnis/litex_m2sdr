## Interpretation

CP1526 strengthens CP1525's result. The live path is not absent on M2SDR
self-RX, but it is temporally sparse and much lower level than helper inside
the same [host-redacted]/M2SDR receiver-bypass path.

The useful discriminator is coverage and continuity, not whether one individual
window decodes PBCH. Local correlation is not a PBCH oracle: both live and
helper have decoder-dead rows with high local reference match. But helper
maintains expected-offset coherence across essentially the whole capture, while
live only shows a short coherence island around `0.104-0.1135 s`.

This points below CP1525's already healthy host-visible live TX bytes and keeps
the branch on effective live TX egress/cadence/coherence inside the M2SDR path.
It does not justify rerunning CP1525 unchanged or moving to bladeRF,
PRACH/NRSA/Android/NAS while `[host-redacted]` is still down.
