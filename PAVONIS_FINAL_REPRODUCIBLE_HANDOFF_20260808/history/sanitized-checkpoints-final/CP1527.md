## Conclusion

CP1526's caveat is real: the reference is a waveform reference, not a proven
same-wall-clock egress record. But CP1527 closes the simple "wrong
frame/reference phase" explanation for CP1526's sparse-live result.

The live-vs-helper split remains visible inside [host-redacted]/M2SDR without bladeRF or
[host-redacted]: helper replay is continuous and strong on the self-RX path, while
live gNB is PBCH-positive but temporally sparse and much lower level.

Next work should stay below clean host-visible bytes and target effective live
TX egress/cadence/coherence, or capture a true capture-window egress reference.
Do not rerun CP1525/CP1526/CP1527 unchanged, and do not move to PRACH/Android/
NAS or `ue_sync_nr.c` sync-anchor edits while the raw scheduled TRACK gate is
sub-threshold.
