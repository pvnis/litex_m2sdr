## Conclusion

CP1528 changes the frontier in two ways:

- Live self-RX can be much better than CP1525 in the same [host-redacted]-only class
  (`168/847` aligned PBCH versus `78/847`), so the live self-RX failure is
  intermittent/condition-sensitive, not a fixed dead path.
- The longer pre/post-DMA egress references are PBCH-healthy but still only
  locally correlate with sparse 1 ms self-RX islands. They do not by themselves
  explain the full improved self-RX decode field as a continuous reference
  match.

Next useful software-only work is to map the CP1528 self-RX decode windows
against worker trace/source-time/RX hardware-time evidence, especially the two
worker `late` rows and the high queue-proxy excursion. Do not rerun CP1525,
CP1526, CP1527, or CP1528 unchanged.
