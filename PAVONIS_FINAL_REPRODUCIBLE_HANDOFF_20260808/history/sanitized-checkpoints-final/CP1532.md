## Interpretation

CP1532 closes the immediate RX append/readStream metadata theory for this run:
the self-RX append file is continuous, timestamped, and free of chunk-level RX
anomalies across both PBCH-good and PBCH-dead windows. The live self-RX quality
gap is therefore not explained by missing appended samples, RX chunk time
regressions, no-time chunks, RX overflows, or readStream chunk splitting.

This checkpoint also reinforces the current condition-sensitive branch:
live gNB self-RX is not fixed-dead on the M2SDR's own receiver, but its quality
still varies run-to-run and remains less dense than the continuous-helper
control. The remaining useful direction is below host-visible bytes and below
the now-clean RX append control path: effective TX egress/cadence/coherence in
the LiteX/FPGA/TimedTX path, preferably through lower-overhead artifact-only or
default-off diagnostics rather than unchanged RF repeats.
