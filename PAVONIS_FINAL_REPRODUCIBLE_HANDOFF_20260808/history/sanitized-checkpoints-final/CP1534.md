## Interpretation

CP1534 confirms CP1533 at the coherence level without spending another RF run:
the live gNB self-RX path is PBCH-positive but temporally sparse, while the
continuous helper replay of the same live pre-DMA payload is dense and coherent
on the same M2SDR RX path.

This keeps the active fault below host-visible TX bytes, worker/write metadata,
and RX append metadata. The unresolved branch is effective live TX
egress/cadence/coherence in the LiteX/FPGA/AD9361 timed path, not bladeRF,
srsUE, RX append loss, RX chunk timing, OCUDU write returns, worker late rows,
dedup, or sequence gaps.
