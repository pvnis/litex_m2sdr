## Interpretation

CP1535 closes the CP1522 reachability blocker and gives the intended valid
measurement: TimedTX counters remain clean through the bladeRF capture window
while external RF is PBCH-positive but sparse. That weakens late burst,
underrun, dropped burst, and basic TimedTX descriptor/state failure as the
immediate explanation for the remaining live RF sparsity.

The useful boundary is now below clean host-visible TX, clean worker/write/RX
metadata from CP1533/CP1534, and clean read-only TimedTX counters. The next
ladder rung should test the actual raw scheduled TRACK gate again now that
`[host-redacted]` is reachable, then decide whether to continue down toward a
default-off effective egress/FPGA/AD9361 handoff trace.
