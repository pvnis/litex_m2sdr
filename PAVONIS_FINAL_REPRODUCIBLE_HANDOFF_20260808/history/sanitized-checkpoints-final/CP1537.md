## Interpretation

The requested binary test did not hit the simple failure case. Live gNB was
not sparse/dead in [host-redacted]-only self-loopback on this run: it produced `165/847`
aligned PBCH windows and `184/332` helper-grid targeted PBCH windows without
bladeRF or `[host-redacted]`.

That means CP1537 does not by itself prove "live sparse even in self-loopback,
therefore TX-side cadence." It does reinforce the softer branch conclusion:
the live runtime path is condition-sensitive and still lower-amplitude and
narrower-coherence than the continuous helper on the same payload and M2SDR
self-RX path. The external raw scheduled TRACK gate from CP1536 remains
blocked, but today's self-RX result says not to treat the current live gNB
runtime as simply dead on the M2SDR receiver.

Do not rerun CP1537 unchanged.
