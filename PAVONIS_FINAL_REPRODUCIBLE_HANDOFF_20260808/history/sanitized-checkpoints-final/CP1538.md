## Interpretation

CP1538 gives a stronger current bridge than CP1536 or CP1537 alone:

- The raw scheduled-TRACK gate remains hard-failed at about `-22.4 dB` mean
  with zero usable opportunities.
- In the same live gNB run, the M2SDR self-RX path is clean and strongly
  PBCH-positive.
- Host-visible TX bytes also remain PBCH-decodable before and after DMA.

This keeps ladder movement blocked below NRSA/PRACH/Android/NAS, but it rules
out "the live gNB is simply dead on M2SDR self-RX during this run." The remaining
split is between self-RX-visible live TX quality and the external bladeRF/srsUE
scheduled-TRACK receive metric.

Do not rerun CP1538 unchanged. If exact time overlap is needed next, extend the
self-RX append/metadata coverage beyond the current `0.885 s` capture and map
it against the srsUE run timing rather than repeating this bridge as-is.
