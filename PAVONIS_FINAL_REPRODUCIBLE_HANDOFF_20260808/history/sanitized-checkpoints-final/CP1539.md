## Interpretation

The live self-RX leg is not sparse/dead in this current run. It decodes PBCH
robustly on the M2SDR's own RX (`150/847` aligned sweep, `172/332` helper-grid
targeted) with clean RX metadata and healthy host-visible TX.

The continuous helper remains denser and higher-level on the same payload
(`226/847` aligned and `218/332` targeted on `none`, with about `22 dB` higher
RMS than the live append), so the live/helper cadence/coherence difference is
still real. But CP1539 does not satisfy the proposed discriminator condition
"live is sparse even in self-loopback." In this run, the current evidence points
away from a simple TX-side-only failure and back toward the split seen in CP1538:
self-RX-visible live TX can be good while the external bladeRF/srsUE scheduled
TRACK gate remains sub-threshold.

Do not rerun CP1539 unchanged. It is a current-state [host-redacted]-only A/B baseline; it
does not raise the raw scheduled TRACK gate because no bladeRF/srsUE raw gate
was run.
