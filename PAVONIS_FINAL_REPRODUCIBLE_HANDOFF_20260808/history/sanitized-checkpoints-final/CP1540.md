## Interpretation

CP1540 confirms the current split:

- Host-visible TX bytes and post-DMA material are PBCH-decodable.
- M2SDR self-RX is PBCH-positive inside the exact srsUE TRACK-era window.
- The external bladeRF/srsUE scheduled TRACK metric remains below gate and has
  zero TRACK PBCH CRC hits.

The "live gNB is sparse even in self-loopback, therefore TX-side cadence is the
primary fault" discriminator still does not trigger. The active blocker remains
the external bladeRF/srsUE scheduled-TRACK path or its interaction with live
OTA/cadence, not a dead live waveform at the M2SDR self-RX observation point.

Do not rerun CP1538 or CP1540 unchanged. If continuing software-only, the most
useful next step is offline analysis of CP1540's TRACK-era windows, especially
the isolated `-5.323 dB` CRC-KO opportunity, against the simultaneous self-RX
late windows before another RF repeat.
