## Interpretation

CP1541 closes a narrow but important uncertainty left by CP1540:

- CP1540's best raw scheduled-TRACK opportunity is not explained by an
  M2SDR self-RX dropout.
- The simultaneous self-RX range around that event is strongly PBCH-positive.
- The selected high-SNR raw TRACK rows are generally self-RX-positive despite
  all srsUE/bladeRF TRACK PBCH CRCs being KO.

This reinforces the current split: the live TX is decoder-visible at the M2SDR
self-RX observation point during the same late/TRACK interval, while the
external bladeRF/srsUE scheduled-TRACK path remains sub-threshold or
CRC-negative. The next software-only step should inspect the external
bladeRF/srsUE side of CP1540 opportunity 67, such as why a `-5.323 dB`
TRACK row with reasonable delay still has PBCH CRC KO, rather than rerunning
CP1538/CP1540 unchanged.
