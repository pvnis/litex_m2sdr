## Interpretation

The existing CP1540 srsUE log does not expose a direct cause for opportunity
`67`'s PBCH CRC KO:

- No RF overflow is logged from first TRACK onward.
- No state transition occurs at opportunity `67`.
- No q-CFO/q-delay update occurs after the initial accepted sync.
- The selected offset remains fixed at `1650` for all TRACK opportunities.
- Sidechecks/fallbacks were disabled by design.
- No external IQ or per-candidate PBCH dump exists in this artifact.

So CP1542 cannot decide whether the CRC KO is due to bad external bladeRF IQ,
candidate/timing selection inside the TRACK decoder, residual CFO/phase, or a
payload/CRC-level failure after apparently good measurement SNR. The current
log only says the fixed-q TRACK loop saw a high-SNR measurement and still
returned PBCH CRC KO.

## Frontier

CP1542 changes the next useful step: another unchanged CP1538/CP1540 RF repeat
would not add the missing evidence. The next material diagnostic should capture
external bladeRF/srsUE IQ or decoder candidate state around high-SNR CRC-KO
TRACK opportunities. Keep it default-off and bounded, and keep the raw TRACK
gate as the metric.
