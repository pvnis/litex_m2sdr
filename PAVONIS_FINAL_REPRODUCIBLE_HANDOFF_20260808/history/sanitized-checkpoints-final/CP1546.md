## Comparison To CP1545

CP1546 reduced startup clutter: CP1545 had `98` RF overflow lines before first
TRACK, while CP1546 had `7`.

It did not clear the runtime health problem: CP1545 had `55` overflow lines and
`28` UE-side TX failures from first TRACK onward; CP1546 had `66` overflow
lines and `36` UE-side TX failures from first TRACK onward.

The candidate/static-window split survives, but CP1546 is not a clean raw-link
baseline and does not raise the raw scheduled TRACK gate.

## Conclusion

Lowering unrelated cell-search/SSB trace overhead is not sufficient to produce
a clean passive candidate-state raw run. CP1546 keeps the important CP1545
observation alive, namely that action-disabled alternate candidate windows can
CRC while the scheduled static TRACK window stays KO, but it also shows the
UE-side RF health issue is tied to the TRACK-era diagnostic/runtime path, not
just startup trace volume.

Do not rerun CP1545 or CP1546 unchanged. The next software-only step should
audit or isolate the UE-side `rf_blade_imp.c:1549` TX-failed path and then use
the lightest candidate probe that can still expose alternate offsets under a
clean TRACK-era RF-health gate.
