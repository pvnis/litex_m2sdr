## Interpretation

CP1550 proves the CP1549 guard can be deployed and rebuilt on `[host-redacted]` and
run under the CP1546 passive probe wrapper with `PAVONIS_RADIO_SKIP_RF_GAP_TX_END=1`.
It also removes the specific CP1545/CP1546 symptom of UE-side
`rf_blade_imp.c:1549` `TX failed: Invalid operation or parameter` lines in
this run.

But the intended RF-gap EOB branch was not exercised: there were zero
`Skipping end-of-burst` lines and zero old `Sending end-of-burst` lines.
The run regressed earlier than the CP1546 frontier, at SFN sync, after heavy
pre-TRACK RF overflow logging. Therefore CP1550 cannot answer whether skipping
the RF-gap zero-sample EOB cleans the TRACK-era health gate.

Treat CP1550 as a successful guarded deploy/build checkpoint and a failed
pre-TRACK/SFN RF probe. Do not count it as a raw scheduled-TRACK metric
datapoint, and do not rerun it unchanged. The next step should diagnose why
this CP1546-derived guarded run failed to enter TRACK, using saved CP1550,
CP1546, and CP1540 logs before another RF repeat.
