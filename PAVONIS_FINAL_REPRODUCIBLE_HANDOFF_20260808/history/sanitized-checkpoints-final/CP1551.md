## Interpretation

CP1551 narrows CP1550's failure to the UE receive/acquisition/SFN side of that
run:

- The CP1549/CP1550 guard did not execute its RF-gap branch: there were zero
  skip-EOB lines.
- CP1550 also had zero old send-EOB lines and zero `TX failed` lines, so the
  CP1546 TRACK-era EOB/TX-failed symptom did not recur.
- CP1550's decisive difference is earlier: cell search took `613` trials
  instead of `15` or `25`, RF overflows were high during acquisition/SFN, and
  SFN never produced a PBCH CRC hit in `find_attempt`.
- The gNB summaries do not show late, timeout, underflow, acquire-error,
  partial-return, over-return, or release-late evidence that would explain the
  SFN failure from the transmitter side.

Therefore CP1550 should be treated as a pre-TRACK receive/acquisition
regression under the guarded binary, not as a measurement of the skip-EOB
hypothesis. The guard remains built and default-off-safe on `[host-redacted]`; its
runtime effect is still untested because the guarded run never reached the RF
gap branch or TRACK.

## Next Step

Do not rerun CP1550 unchanged. The next RF run should be a changed control that
separates "guarded binary/env caused acquisition instability" from ordinary
run-to-run acquisition instability before testing the TRACK-era EOB hypothesis.
A minimal option is a CP1546-class acquisition/health control on the same
guarded binary with `PAVONIS_RADIO_SKIP_RF_GAP_TX_END=0`, followed only if it
reaches TRACK by the guarded `=1` probe.
