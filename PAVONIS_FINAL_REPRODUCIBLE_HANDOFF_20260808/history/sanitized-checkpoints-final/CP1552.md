## Interpretation

CP1552 separates two things:

1. The guarded binary/source state can still acquire and reach TRACK when
   `PAVONIS_RADIO_SKIP_RF_GAP_TX_END=0`.
2. With the guard env disabled, the old RF-gap send-EOB path and UE-side
   `rf_blade_imp.c:1549` `TX failed: Invalid operation or parameter` symptom
   reappear.

Therefore CP1550's SFN failure was not proof that the guarded binary itself
cannot acquire. It was likely run-state/acquisition instability under that
specific guarded `=1` attempt. CP1552 also confirms that the CP1546/CP1548
EOB/TX-failed branch is still live on the guarded binary when the env is off.

CP1552 is not a raw-link improvement. The raw scheduled TRACK gate remains
sub-threshold and RF-health remains failed. But CP1552 makes the next changed
probe well-defined: rerun the same CP1552-class control with
`PAVONIS_RADIO_SKIP_RF_GAP_TX_END=1` now that the guarded binary has just
shown it can reach TRACK in the adjacent control.
