Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Purpose

Run a no-RF/no-SSH analysis of the saved CP1546, CP1552, and CP1553 UE logs to
separate the remaining TRACK-era overflow failure from the RF-gap EOB/TX-failed

| Checkpoint | TRACK opps | RF overflows before TRACK | RF overflows from TRACK | RF-gap action | RF-gap opp range | TX-failed range |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- | --- | --- |

| CP1553 | 70 | 71 | 65 | `31` skip-EOB | opp `32-69` | none |

The key ordering is consistent across the three logs:

- First TRACK-era overflow appears near opportunity `4`.
- RF-gap send/skip EOB appears much later, around opportunity `31`/`32`.
- CP1553 removes all `TX failed` lines while leaving TRACK-era overflows.

CP1553 SNR does not split cleanly by proximity to the skip-EOB messages:

- Opportunities within two log lines of a skipped RF-gap line: `31`, mean SNR
  `-22.0499 dB`, `0 >= -10 dB`
- Other opportunities: `39`, mean SNR `-22.4266 dB`, `0 >= -10 dB`

Passive candidate hits also do not require the skipped RF-gap branch:

- CP1553 direct hit `1/3` occurs at sidecheck/direct id `7`, before the first
  skipped RF-gap line at opportunity `32`.
- CP1553 later side/rolling/direct hits at ids `34` and `38` occur after
  skipped RF-gap is active, but the scheduled window remains sub-threshold.

## Interpretation

CP1554 separates the remaining overflow failure from the EOB/TX-failed branch.
The EOB path starts late in TRACK and is fixed by CP1553, but overflows begin
near the start of TRACK in every compared run.

That means the next blocker is not the old zero-sample EOB itself. It is a
broader UE receive/processing health problem, likely tied to the trace/passive
candidate workload or receive timing under the current raw-TRACK harness. The
raw SNR gate also remains uniformly sub-threshold, so scheduler/anchor changes
are still gated.

Do not rerun CP1554 unchanged. The next changed RF probe should separate
passive-candidate diagnostic load from the raw scheduled-TRACK baseline on the
guarded binary, with `PAVONIS_RADIO_SKIP_RF_GAP_TX_END=1` retained so the old
EOB/TX-failed symptom does not contaminate the result.
