## Interpretation

CP1555 verifies that the no-candidate wrapper did reach runtime with the
intended settings, but it does not answer the CP1554 question because it failed
before TRACK.

The result should be classified with CP1550-style acquisition/SFN instability,
not as evidence that disabling passive candidates fixes UE RF health. Since no
TRACK opportunities exist, there is no raw scheduled downlink metric and no
valid post-TRACK overflow comparison.

Do not treat CP1555 as a ladder unlock or a clean raw baseline. The next
changed control should separate no-candidate acquisition instability from the
skip-EOB/no-candidate hypothesis, rather than rerunning CP1555 unchanged.
