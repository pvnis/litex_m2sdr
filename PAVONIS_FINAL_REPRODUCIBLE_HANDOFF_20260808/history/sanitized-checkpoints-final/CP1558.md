## Interpretation

CP1558 closes the narrow question left by CP1555: the
skip-EOB-on/no-candidate combination can acquire, complete SFN sync, and reach
TRACK on the guarded binary. CP1555 was therefore another pre-TRACK
acquisition/SFN scatter instance, not a stable no-candidate skip-EOB blocker.

The raw link budget remains unchanged and below gate. CP1557 and CP1558 both
have `66` TRACK opportunities and zero scheduled PBCH hits; CP1558 is worse in
mean SNR (`-22.61 dB` versus CP1557's `-21.41 dB`) and still has `0/66` above
`-10 dB` or `-15 dB`.

The skip-EOB branch itself was not exercised in CP1558: there were zero
send-EOB lines and zero skip-EOB lines. The useful result is instead that
removing passive candidate diagnostics and enabling the guard does not raise
the raw scheduled TRACK metric. The one late UE-side `TX failed` line means
CP1558 is not as clean as CP1557's RF-health baseline, but TRACK-era RF
overflows are absent.

Do not rerun CP1555/CP1557/CP1558 unchanged. This branch now says that passive
candidate diagnostic load and RF-gap zero-sample EOB handling are not sufficient
to raise the raw scheduled-TRACK gate. Further work should still be judged by
mean TRACK-opportunity SNR and the fraction of opportunities `>= -10 dB`, and
should avoid `ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor edits, higher-ladder work, and
hardware-affecting branches without explicit approval.
