## Conclusion

CP1566 refines CP1565's conclusion: the accepted gNB detector event should not
be treated as a likely UE Msg1 detection. It is a late `best_preamble=42`
threshold crossing, while the UE sent five early `preamble_idx=0` bursts and
the detector never accepted preamble `0`.

The current hard blocker is therefore earlier than detector-to-MAC/RAR handoff:
the gNB detector is still not accepting the actual UE preamble-0 transmissions
at their expected occasions. CP1565 remains meaningful ladder movement because
downlink cell selection and UE PRACH TX are alive under force-continuous gNB
TX, but it does not prove gNB PRACH reception.
