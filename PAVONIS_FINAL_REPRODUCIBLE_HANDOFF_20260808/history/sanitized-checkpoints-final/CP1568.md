## Interpretation

CP1568 closes the "maybe the gNB is looking at the wrong PRACH occasion" theory for this run:

- UE `tti=4174/4334/4494/4654/4814` maps directly to detector `sfn=417/433/449/465/481 slot=4`.
- gNB detector rows occur within `2.6-4.1 ms` of the timestamped UE transmit logs.
- The detector's configured root/preamble span matches the UE's real PRACH config (`root_sequence_index=1`, `start_preamble_index=0`, `nof_preamble_indices=64`).

The current hard blocker is therefore not RAR/MAC handoff and not coarse PRACH timing. It is that the real preamble-0 detector metric is below acceptance threshold at the correct occasions.

## Caveat

The predemod and UL-symbol raw captures did not cover the actual late PRACH attempts:

- `pavonis_prach_predemod_manifest.tsv` capped at `1200` rows and ended before detector idx `1500`.
- `pavonis_ul_symbol_manifest.tsv` capped at `160000` rows and covered only the first `~11.4 s` after arming.

This is a capture-window mistake, not an RF conclusion. The next RF checkpoint should arm the detector/predemod dump after the deliberate delay and immediately before starting srsUE, and raise PRACH predemod raw/summary caps enough to include the actual PRACH attempts.
