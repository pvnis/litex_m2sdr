## Interpretation

CP1574 separates the CP1572 failure:

```text
CP1571: standalone raw RX alone sees the uplink tone strongly.
CP1572: standalone full-duplex with nonzero M2SDR TX tone fails TX worker and misses tone.
CP1574: standalone full-duplex with TX mode zero sees the uplink tone.
```

Therefore, full-duplex stream/control state is not broadly enough to suppress
the uplink tone by itself. The immediate CP1572 discriminator is nonzero M2SDR
TX activity/worker/write-path interaction, not the mere existence of active
M2SDR TX/RX streams.

This also keeps CP1570/CP1569 pointed at live gNB RX/predemod visibility under
real TX-active conditions, not a dead physical bladeRF TX1-to-M2SDR path.

## CP1562 RSRP Audit

The requested RSRP audit does not confirm a `~15 dB` scheduled-TRACK RSRP rise
in CP1562. Per-opportunity TRACK log aggregation gives:

```text

```

CP1562 was essentially unchanged versus CP1557 (`+0.25 dB`), and even
corrected/readback CP1563B was only about `+1.94 dB` versus CP1557. The large
RSRP lift appears in CP1564, after forcing helper-like continuous live TX
writes; CP1564 is about `+14.99 dB` versus CP1557 and about `+13.05 dB`
versus CP1563B.

That supports the coherence/EVM/cadence interpretation: attenuation alone did
not move the scheduled TRACK gate, while helper-like continuous live TX did.
