## Interpretation

CP1576 reproduces CP1569's uplink result under the CP1575-native continuous
downlink baseline. Native continuous TX is good enough for downlink acquisition
and cell select, and srsUE sends five strong, clean preamble-0 PRACH bursts.
The gNB looks at the right PRACH occasions, but the actual p0 detector metric
stays sub-threshold and the corresponding predemod samples remain floor-class.

Therefore CP1576 keeps the hard blocker upstream of PRACH threshold override,
Msg1/RAR/MAC, Android, or NAS. It also shows that replacing the CP1564 force
override with CP1575 native `tx_mode: continuous` does not by itself recover
uplink PRACH visibility under live TX-active conditions.

Do not rerun CP1569 or CP1576 unchanged. Do not run a threshold-only PRACH
override next. The next useful branch should isolate live gNB/M2SDR uplink RX
visibility under nonzero/native-continuous TX activity, building on CP1570-
CP1574 and CP1576, without returning to amplitude/RX/EOB sweeps.
