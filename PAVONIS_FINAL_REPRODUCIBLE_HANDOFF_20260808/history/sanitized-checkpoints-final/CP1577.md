show the controlled uplink tone.

## Interpretation

CP1577 closes the obvious CP1570 caveats. The current standalone physical
bladeRF TX1-to-LiteX/M2SDR uplink path is alive from CP1571, and zero-TX
full-duplex standalone M2SDR receives the same tone from CP1574. CP1577 now
shows that the live gNB, with native continuous downlink TX active and clean
bladeRF tone process control, still records a floor/spur-class RX append with
no `+100 kHz` tone.

Therefore the uplink blocker remains live gNB/M2SDR RX visibility under
TX-active conditions, not the CP1570 bladeRF timeout, not a dead physical
uplink path, not the CP1564 force-continuous override, and not PRACH threshold,
RAR, MAC, Android, or NAS. This also keeps CP1576's PRACH predemod floor result
credible: under native continuous downlink, downlink acquisition works, but
the live gNB RX/predemod input still does not visibly receive bladeRF uplink
energy.

Do not rerun CP1570 or CP1577 unchanged. The next changed software-only work
should isolate the live gNB/M2SDR RX path under TX-active conditions more
directly, while avoiding amplitude/RX/EOB sweeps and without editing
`ue_sync_nr.c` sync/anchor logic.
