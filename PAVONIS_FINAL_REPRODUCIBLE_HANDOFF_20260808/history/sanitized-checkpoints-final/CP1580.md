## Conclusion

CP1580 converts CP1578 from an invalid discriminator into a valid negative
one. With native-continuous live gNB control, matched CP1577-class RX readback,
clean bladeRF TX, and proven post-arm TX payload zeroing, the gNB-owned M2SDR
RX append still does not see the `+100 kHz` uplink tone.

This weakens "post-arm downlink payload RF energy masks the uplink" as the
immediate explanation. The remaining software-side suspects are earlier/live
TX-active control state, the live gNB RX/predemod path not matching standalone
RX, or M2SDR full-duplex stream/control behavior that persists even when the
post-arm TX buffers are zeroed.

Do not rerun CP1578 or CP1580 unchanged. The next changed software-only rung
should isolate whether zeroing from the first live TX write, rather than only
after the RX arm, changes the RX append tone result.
