## Conclusion

CP1581 is a valid negative follow-up to CP1580. Zeroing TX buffers from the
first live TX write still does not restore the bladeRF uplink tone in the
live gNB-owned M2SDR RX append.

This weakens both post-arm and pre-arm live downlink payload content as the
immediate cause of the uplink invisibility. The remaining software-side
suspects are live gNB TX/RX stream-control or full-duplex state, the live
gNB RX/predemod path differing from standalone RX, or another control-plane
interaction that persists even when live TX sample buffers are zeroed.

Do not rerun CP1580 or CP1581 unchanged. The next useful software-only rung
should remove or bypass live TX stream activation itself while preserving gNB
RX append/control, or directly compare live gNB RX append against a standalone
zero-TX full-duplex receiver under the same arm/tone timing.
