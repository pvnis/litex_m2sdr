## Conclusion

CP1585 is a valid negative discriminator for TX channel setup and TX stream
setup/write activity as the immediate live gNB RX-append uplink-tone blocker.
With CP1575 native-continuous gNB control active, TX channel configuration
skipped, TX `setupStream()` skipped, TX stream activation/write/deactivation
skipped, and all actual TX write/flush counters at zero, the gNB-owned RX
append still did not recover the bladeRF `+100 kHz` uplink tone.

This weakens "M2SDR TX setup or TX stream I/O suppresses uplink visibility" as
the leading explanation. The remaining software suspicion moves deeper into
the live gNB RX/control path, the gNB RX/predemod path versus standalone raw
RX, or another gNB control-plane interaction that persists even when no Soapy
TX stream is configured or written.

Do not rerun CP1585 unchanged. Do not turn the result into an amplitude,
RX-gain, or EOB sweep.
