## Conclusion

CP1588 closes RX sample-format/scaling mismatch as the immediate live gNB
RX-append uplink-tone blocker. Opening RX as CF32 and converting back to
`ci16_t` raises the append level into the expected presentation class, but the
bladeRF `+100 kHz` uplink tone is still not recovered.

The remaining blocker is deeper than M2SDR TX setup/I/O and deeper than the
OCUDU RX sample format. The next software-only work should inspect the live
gNB RX/control, frequency/content, or predemod path mismatch versus the
standalone CP1571 raw RX path. Do not rerun CP1588 unchanged, and do not turn
this into an amplitude, RX-gain, or EOB sweep.
