## Conclusion

CP1594 closes the raw-preconversion versus post-conversion suspicion. With the
CP1593 shared arm-state snapshot, the raw Soapy CF32 append and legacy
post-conversion append are correlated at `0.999955` and differ only at about
`-67.2 dB` relative to raw. The expected `+100 kHz` uplink tone is already
absent in the raw preconversion samples, so the CF32-to-`ci16_t` conversion and
legacy append path are not the immediate blocker.

Do not rerun CP1592, CP1593, or CP1594 unchanged. The next changed work should
stay software-only and inspect the live gNB RX/control path before or at the
Soapy `readStream()` boundary versus the standalone CP1571/CP1574 positive
receiver path. Do not turn this into an amplitude/RX/EOB sweep, do not edit
`ue_sync_nr.c`, and do not move to NRSA/PRACH/Android/NAS until the live
uplink visibility blocker is resolved.
