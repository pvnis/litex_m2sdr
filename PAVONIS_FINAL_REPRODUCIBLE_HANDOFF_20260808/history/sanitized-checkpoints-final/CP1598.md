## Conclusion

CP1598 validates CP1597's live-side anomaly as real but intermittent: one of
two back-to-back live no-TX-stream/RX-CF32 windows sees the bladeRF `+100 kHz`
tone strongly at the expected frequency, while the other remains strict-gate
negative because an off-target spur dominates despite expected-bin energy.

This changes the branch from "live gNB RX never sees the tone" to "live gNB RX
can see the tone, but tone visibility is state/window dependent and competing
spur/content can dominate." Do not rerun CP1597 or CP1598 unchanged, and do
not turn this into an RX gain or amplitude sweep. The next useful software-only
step is offline analysis of the saved CP1598 live1/live2 captures and state:
time-local expected-tone versus spur dominance, live1/live2 gain-state
differences (`0x2b0`, sidecar gain), and sens timing alignment. If another RF
run is needed later, it should be a materially changed diagnostic based on
that analysis.
