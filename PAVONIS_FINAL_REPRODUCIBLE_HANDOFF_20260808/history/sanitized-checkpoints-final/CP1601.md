## Conclusion

CP1601 finds one important false negative: CP1583 was tone-visible under the
corrected near-tone gate even though the old strict exact-bin gate failed. The
specific condition that recovered tone visibility was skipping TX stream I/O
while keeping TX setup/config in place.

CP1585 and CP1588 remain tone-negative under the corrected gate. That means
the stronger "skip TX setup plus skip TX I/O" bypass still does not reproduce
the tone, even with RX-CF32 level recovery. CP1577, CP1580, and CP1581 also
remain tone-negative.

Do not rerun CP1577, CP1580, CP1581, CP1583, CP1585, CP1588, CP1592, or CP1601
unchanged. The next changed live diagnostic should use the CP1600 analyzer and
focus on the CP1583-vs-CP1585 split: TX setup/config present with stream I/O
skipped is tone-visible; skipping TX setup/config as well is not.
