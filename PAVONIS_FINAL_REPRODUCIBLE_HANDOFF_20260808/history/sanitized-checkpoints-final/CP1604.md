configuration/readback, skip only TX `setupStream()`, skip all actual TX stream
I/O, and re-check whether the gNB-owned M2SDR RX-CF32 append sees the
[host-redacted]/bladeRF `+100 kHz` uplink tone under the corrected CP1600 near-tone

- No bladeRF calibration, bias, physical-path, or hardware-affecting branch.

- The run had to prove `tx_skip_setupstream_only=1`,
  `tx_skip_stream_setup=0`, `tx_skip_stream_io=1`, and CP1583-class TX

## Conclusion

CP1604 is a clean positive discriminator. With TX channel configuration and
readback preserved at the live DL state, skipping TX `setupStream()` itself
does not break live gNB-owned RX-CF32 uplink-tone visibility. The bladeRF tone
is strong in both the raw preconversion tap and the post-conversion append,
with zero RX overflows and no actual TX stream activation/write/deactivation.

This closes TX `setupStream()` itself as the CP1583-vs-CP1585 blocker. The
CP1585 failure is therefore tied to the broad setup-skip side effects that
remove TX channel configuration/readback or later TX config/reassertion paths,
not to the absence of a Soapy TX stream handle alone.

Next work should not rerun CP1604 unchanged. The useful next rung is to carry
the now-positive no-TX-stream/setupStream-only state up the uplink ladder
toward PRACH/predemod visibility, or isolate the specific TX channel
configuration side effect only if needed before that ladder move.
