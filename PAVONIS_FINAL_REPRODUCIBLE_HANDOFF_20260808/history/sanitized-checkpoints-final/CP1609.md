## Conclusion

CP1609 is a clean negative phase-sweep checkpoint.

The full gNB-owned RX append sees the sparse CP19 waveform in every phase, with
four or five append windows above `0.20` correlation and best scores around
`0.47-0.48`. However, none of the four `0/10/20/30 ms` sparse-file phases puts
the CP19 waveform into the live PRACH predemod windows: predemod best scores
remain only about `0.029-0.032`, there are zero predemod files above `0.20`,
and the detector accepts zero rows.

This strengthens CP1608's conclusion. The blocker is not normal live TX stream
state and not gross RF visibility into the gNB-owned RX append. A simple
source-file phase sweep is insufficient; the remaining issue is alignment
between actual over-the-air burst arrival and the gNB's PRACH predemod window
schedule.
