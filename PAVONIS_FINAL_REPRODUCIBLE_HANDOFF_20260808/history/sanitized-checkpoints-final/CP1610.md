gNB-owned RX append but absent from live PRACH predemod/detector windows,
without running another RF transmission.

## Conclusion

CP1610 confirms the CP1608/CP1609 failure is not simply "weak predemod."
Sparse CP19 bursts are present in the full append, but they are systematically
outside the live PRACH predemod capture intervals. The 10 ms source-file phase
sweep in CP1609 is too coarse for the observed full-fit tolerance of only
`1116` samples, about `96.875 us`.

The next changed RF checkpoint should not rerun CP1608/CP1609 unchanged.
Use a fine timing sweep around the CP1609 `phase20` result, centered near an
additional `+0.165 ms` delay from `phase20`'s first-burst placement. A small
micro-sweep around that point is safer than a single offset because the saved
runs still show run-to-run scheduling jitter.
