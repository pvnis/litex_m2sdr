## Conclusion

CP1611 is a partial timing success and a detector-delay failure. The coarse
CP1609 phase sweep did not align sparse CP19 with predemod; CP1611's `d120`
micro-offset does get CP19-like energy into live predemod and produces strong
preamble-0 detector rows, but those rows are rejected because the measured
delay is about `24-25` samples beyond the detector's `104.8` sample limit.

Do not rerun CP1609 or CP1611 unchanged. The next changed RF rung should be an
ultra-fine timing sweep around `d120`, biased a few microseconds earlier, to
try to reduce p0 delay below the detector limit while preserving the `d120`
predemod/detector visibility.
