## Conclusion

CP1612 closes the simple "move a few microseconds earlier from d120" fix. The
earlier offsets keep sparse CP19 visible in the full append but lose the
predemod/detector visibility that CP1611 `d120` had.

The important regression is that the same nominal `0.520120 s` point did not
reproduce CP1611's partial hit. Treat CP1611 `d120` as a valuable but
run-alignment-sensitive window, not a stable fixed first-burst offset.

Do not rerun CP1611 or CP1612 unchanged. The next changed work should be a
no-RF timing comparison of CP1611 versus CP1612 append detections and predemod
manifests, then use that to derive a same-run/adaptive timing strategy before
spending more RF on fixed microsecond offsets.
