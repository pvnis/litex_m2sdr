## Conclusion

CP1613 does not support another fixed microsecond start-offset sweep. None of
the CP1611 or CP1612 append CP19 detections fit a predemod window directly or
modulo the `160 ms` PRACH period. The miss scale is milliseconds to tens of
milliseconds, while the valid CP19 placement interval inside a predemod window
is only `0.096875 ms`.

CP1611 `d120`'s detector/predemod tail is therefore not explained by the
append-template detection landing correctly in the gNB predemod window, and
CP1612 `e120` proves that stronger append-visible CP19 energy alone does not
produce predemod visibility. Treat CP1611 `d120` as run/window-alignment
sensitive, not as a stable fixed first-burst offset.

Next changed work should avoid fixed-offset RF sweeps and instead make the
PRACH transmission timing predemod-window-driven: use the gNB RX/predemod
manifest or hardware-time readback to schedule the bladeRF CP19 burst into a
specific same-run predemod window, then remeasure detector p0 and predemod CP19
visibility.
