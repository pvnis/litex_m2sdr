## Conclusion

CP1614 is a mechanically successful but timing-invalid real-UE checkpoint.
It does not answer whether real UE PRACH bursts miss the predemod window or
arrive weak, because no real UE PRACH burst was transmitted.

The useful result is the failure mode: with CP1606-CP1613 append/predemod/
alignment instrumentation active, the UE reached cell selection but then spent
the run out of sync and never progressed to RA. Treat this as a DL/UE-progress
regression under the instrumented CP1614 setup, not as a PRACH timing miss and
not as a p0 delay-gate datapoint.

## Next

Do not rerun CP1614 unchanged. The next changed rung should first make the UE
emit PRACH under instrumentation, for example by shortening/disabling the
temporary `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_SYNC_GUARD_SLOTS=200` gate for one diagnostic
run, or by otherwise recovering CP1576-class UE downlink sync while keeping
RX append/predemod instrumentation active. Only after timestamped real UE
`PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_CFG transmitted` and `PRACH-TX` rows exist should the
CP1614 real-UE alignment analyzer be used to classify "missed window" versus
"hit but weak/delay-gated."
