## Goal

predemod, and alignment instrumentation active, but the UE never emitted PRACH.

wrapper bandwidth to `30.72 MHz` and gates the live TX readback on
`30.72 MHz`, keeping the CP1576 UE guard (`PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_SYNC_GUARD_SLOTS=200`).

## Conclusion

CP1615 is mechanically clean but still not a real-PRACH timing datapoint.
Restoring the Soapy TX/RX bandwidth readback from CP1614's `10 MHz` to
CP1576's `30.72 MHz` is not sufficient to recover CP1576-class UE downlink
sync or PRACH emission.

The failure remains before Msg1: the UE does not enter a state where the
temporary PRACH sync guard matters. A guard-only rerun is therefore not the
next best isolation after CP1615, because SFN sync never succeeded.

## Next

Do not rerun CP1614 or CP1615 unchanged. The next changed rung should isolate
why the CP1606-CP1615 append/CF32/readStream instrumentation path no longer
allows CP1576-class UE downlink progress. A useful next control is a
current-time CP1576-style no-append real-UE run, optionally with only the local
CP1615 event-alignment post-analysis added, to verify whether the known
PRACH-emitting path still reproduces on the current hardware before changing
UE PRACH guard or demod offset logic.
