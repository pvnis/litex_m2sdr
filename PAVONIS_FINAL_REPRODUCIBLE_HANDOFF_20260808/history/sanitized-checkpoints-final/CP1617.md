control still reached cell selection and SFN sync, but emitted zero PRACH under
the temporary `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_SYNC_GUARD_SLOTS=200` guard. CP1617 changes

CP1615 event-alignment analyzer.

Msg1 timestamps, then decide whether the real UE bursts miss the gNB predemod
windows or arrive on time but fail strength/delay gates.

## Conclusion

CP1617 is the first current-time rerun after CP1616 that restores real UE Msg1
timestamp rows. Disabling the temporary `200`-slot PRACH sync guard is enough
to make srsUE emit seven preamble-0 bursts under the no-append native-continuous
control.

The real UE bursts do not miss the gNB predemod/trace windows. All seven align
to the expected SFN/slot detector rows and p0 is visible in every matched row.
The immediate failure is that p0 is too weak for the detector threshold:
matched p0 ratios are only `0.263..0.455`, with `0/7` accepted. Two events also
fail the old delay gate (`p0_delay=115` and `129` versus the `104.8` limit),
but five events pass delay and still fail threshold, so CP1617 points first at
real-UE p0 strength/threshold/SNR rather than open-loop window placement.

CP1617 closes the CP1614-CP1616 "no real PRACH event rows" blocker and turns
the next rung into a real event-alignment experiment. Do not rerun CP1617
unchanged.
