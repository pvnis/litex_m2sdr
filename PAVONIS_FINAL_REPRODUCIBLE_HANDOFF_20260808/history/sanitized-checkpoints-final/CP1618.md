## Conclusion

CP1618 is mechanically clean and is the requested full-instrumentation guard0
real-UE rerun.

It closes the CP1614-CP1616 "instrumentation prevents real PRACH" blocker:
full raw/post append plus predemod instrumentation still allows srsUE to
cell-select and emit seven real preamble-0 Msg1 bursts with no UE RF overflow
or TX-failed lines.

It also closes missed-window as the immediate explanation under full
instrumentation: all seven real UE events match predemod/trace SFN/slot rows,
and all seven matched p0 rows pass the delay gate.

The new localized failure is append-to-predemod/detector visibility. The raw
and post append taps see the burst strongly (`~0.932` score, four windows
above `0.20`), but predemod remains weak (`0.1005` best, `0/1523 >=0.20`) and
the event-matched detector p0 rows stay below threshold (`0.253..0.434` ratio,
`0/7` accepted). CP1618 therefore points below the append tap and before or
inside the PRACH predemod/detector path, not at UE emission, RF timing window,
or gross RX append visibility.

Do not rerun CP1618 unchanged. The next useful work is no-RF/offline: compare
the CP1618 event-matched append windows against their corresponding predemod
raw files for frequency, offset, scaling, extraction window, and template
alignment. Only after that comparison should the CP651-CP733 demod-offset
branch be reused; CP1618's real event rows do not currently fail delay.
