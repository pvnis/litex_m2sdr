attempts aligned to gNB predemod/trace rows, but it also reported a strong
global raw/post append CP19 score. CP1619 is a no-RF offline check on [host-redacted] that
compares the exact CP1618 real-event append windows against their corresponding
predemod raw files.

The purpose is to decide whether CP1618's high append score belongs to the real
UE events or to an unrelated window, and whether the failure is post-conversion,
predemod extraction, or absent/weak real-event energy at the expected windows.

For each event it mapped the gNB predemod manifest `window_start_ts` into the

- the direct event-length raw append window.
- the direct event-length post append window.

analysis: `-6000..+6000 Hz` in `500 Hz` steps.

## Interpretation

CP1619 corrects the main CP1618 append interpretation.

The CP1618 global raw/post append score near `0.932` is real, but it does not
belong to the seven real UE PRACH event windows. At the event-matched windows,
raw append, post append, and predemod all score in the same floor-class
`~0.018..0.029` range, far below the controlled CP19 pass class.

The raw and post append event windows match each other essentially exactly
(`~1.0` correlation), so the legacy post-conversion path is not the cause of
the real-event mismatch.

The raw append event windows and corresponding predemod files do not match each
other (`<=0.0304` correlation). That means the direct metadata mapping from
CP1618 trace/manifest timestamps to append offsets is not sufficient to locate
the real UE waveform in the append, or the real UE waveform is absent/too weak
at the expected gNB RX append windows.

## Conclusion

CP1619 is a no-RF correction checkpoint. It supersedes the CP1618 statement
that the raw/post append tap saw the real UE PRACH strongly.

The corrected frontier is:

- full CP1606-style instrumentation with guard0 does not prevent real srsUE
  Msg1 transmission.
- all seven real UE events still align to gNB manifest/trace SFN/slot rows.
- p0 is seen at those rows and passes the delay gate in CP1618, but remains
  weak and unaccepted.
- the high CP1618 append CP19 score is unrelated to those seven real events.
- event-matched raw/post append and predemod windows are all floor-class.

Next work should remain off the open-loop sweep branch. The next useful rung is
to search the exact srsUE PRACH debug waveform/dumps from the CP1618 [host-redacted]
artifacts against the CP1618 raw append over a wider timestamp neighborhood
around each `PRACH-TX wall_ns`. That should decide whether the real UE waveform
appears elsewhere in the append or is genuinely absent/too weak in the
M2SDR-owned RX path.
