## Interpretation

CP1622 answers the user's real-UE question: the CP1618 real UE bursts miss the
detector-facing predemod window. They are not simply "in-window but weak", and
they are not a CP651-CP733 p0 delay-gate case at the `562/847` sample scale.

The historical demod-input crop knob can move the demodulator origin by tens of
microseconds inside a valid 1 ms predemod buffer. CP1618's real UE raw-hit
placement requires moving the predemod extraction window itself by about
`11.7..18.8 ms`. Applying another static `PAVONIS_PRACH_DEMOD_INPUT_OFFSET`
value cannot reach these bursts from the nominal CP1618 predemod files.

The immediate blocker is now the PRACH collection/window scheduling or
timestamp-domain mapping between the real UE occasion and the detector/predemod
window, not RF visibility at the gNB-owned raw append tap.
