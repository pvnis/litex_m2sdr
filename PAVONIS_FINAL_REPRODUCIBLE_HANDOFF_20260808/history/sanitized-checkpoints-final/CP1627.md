## Interpretation

CP1627 answers a different question than intended: a continuous 130 s raw
append is not a clean way to obtain the same-window real-UE PRACH evidence.
During this run the UE completed cell search/SFN sync but never reached the
CP1625-class PRACH configuration and never transmitted Msg1. The 130 s raw
append also generated enough disk traffic to make the small artifact package
fail until the corrupted marker was repaired.

Do not rerun CP1627 unchanged. The next changed rung should keep CP1624 delayed
collection but avoid a full 130 s continuous raw append. A narrower run should
start the UE normally, then arm a short raw append close to the historical
CP1625 real PRACH time, or implement a lower-volume same-window raw capture.
