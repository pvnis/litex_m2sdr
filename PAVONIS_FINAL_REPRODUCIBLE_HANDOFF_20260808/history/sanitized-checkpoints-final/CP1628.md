## Interpretation

CP1628 removes CP1627's early/full raw-append load as a sufficient explanation
for the current no-PRACH behavior. The UE still gets through cell search and
SFN/cell select, but it does not reach the later CP1625 PRACH/RACH config and
therefore cannot emit Msg1.

Do not rerun CP1628 unchanged. The next useful checkpoint is a current
CP1625-style PRACH-emission control, or a narrower UE/gNB downlink config trace
focused on why the CP1625 `config_idx=1/root_seq_idx=1/freq_offset=7/
num_ra_preambles=64` update is no longer appearing.
