## Conclusion

CP1632 restores the interpretable real-UE baseline that CP1627-CP1629 lacked:
the UE reaches RACH config, cell-select/SFN sync are successful, and seven real
Msg1/PRACH transmissions are logged and matched to gNB predemod/trace SFN/slot
rows.

It does not solve detection. The no-delay/no-sequence collection still gives
weak real p0 rows (`0.255/0.339/0.408` min/median/max), `5/7` delay passes,
and `0/7` accepted real UE p0 events.

The next changed RF rung is therefore a guarded real-UE attempt using the
CP1631 sequence diagnostic and the CP1630-derived delay table, with manifest
proof of selected sequence entries. Do not return to static-delay sweeps; if
the UE again fails before RACH config/Msg1, stop and diagnose that acquisition
regression rather than interpreting the sequence result.
