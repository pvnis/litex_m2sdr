## Conclusion

CP1633 is a valid mechanics/instrumentation checkpoint and an invalid
sequence-timing checkpoint. The CP1631 sequence path is proven active and
balanced across all predemod rows, with the exact delay table recorded in the
manifest. However, the UE failed at SFN sync and emitted no Msg1, so there are
no real UE PRACH events to evaluate against the sequence windows.

Do not spend another RF run on sequence timing until the UE acquisition
regression is handled. Next work should be a no-RF audit of CP1632 versus
CP1633 acquisition/runtime differences, or a minimal acquisition-only baseline
if RF is needed, not another delay-table or static-delay attempt.
