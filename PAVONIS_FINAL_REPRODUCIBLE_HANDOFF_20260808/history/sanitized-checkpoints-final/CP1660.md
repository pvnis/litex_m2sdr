after CP1659 regressed into LiteX release-late before UE start. CP1660 repeats


## Verdict

CP1660 is a useful TX/acquisition checkpoint and a void PRACH checkpoint:

```text
TX-cadence result: PASS
UE acquisition/SFN result: PASS
Msg1/PRACH timing result: VOID, no Msg1 emitted
```

Use CP1660's 30 ms TX offset plus all-timed chunks for the next real-UE
acquisition baseline. Do not interpret CP1660 as evidence about PRACH detector
timing, p0 delay, RAR, or window mapping.

The next changed rung should focus on why the UE does not receive/use a valid
RACH config and emit Msg1 after successful cell select/SFN. If measuring live
TX/RF latency again, the marker test must arm at least two separated points in
the same stream: one shortly after stream start and another around `60-120 s`,
because an early-only marker can falsely clear the suspected single-anchor /
untimed-continuation drift mechanism.
