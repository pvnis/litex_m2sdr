## Verdict

CP1666 restores the current CP1632-class RACH-config/Msg1 baseline when
all-timed chunks are disabled. That means the CP1660/CP1665 no-RACH-config
behavior is not a broad current SIB/RACH-config regression: it is tied to the
all-timed real-UE presentation/offset branch.

This does not move past Stage 6. The UE sent seven real Msg1 attempts, all
matched the expected gNB SFN/slot rows, but real p0 was still below the
detector threshold and `0/7` accepted. CP1666 therefore reopens the usable
PRACH-detector rung under native-continuous TX, while CP1658 remains the best
raw PBCH/TRACK downlink baseline.

## Next

Do not rerun CP1666, CP1660, or CP1665 unchanged. The next useful step is a
no-RF CP1666-vs-CP1660/CP1665 audit focused on the post-SFN UE downlink path:
why all-timed TX clears raw PBCH/TRACK but fails usable RACH config, while
native-continuous TX receives RACH config and emits Msg1. Any next RF all-timed
variant must gate on both DL/SFN and real RACH-config/Msg1 emission, not only
raw PBCH/TRACK.
