## Changes

- `srsRAN_4G/srsue/src/phy/nr/cc_worker.cc`
  - Added `PAVONIS_SRSUE_SIB1_TRACE_PDCCH_SUMMARY_INTERVAL`.
  - Default interval is `200`; `0` disables summaries.
  - No-grant SI-PDCCH searches now emit `event=si_pdcch_summary` once per
    interval with slot range, sample count, max EPRE/correlation, and the slots
    where those maxima occurred.
  - Immediate `event=si_pdcch` and `SIB1-DBG` warning rows are now limited to
    actual SI DCI/grant observations (`n_dl > 0`).
  - `event=si_pdsch_grant`, BCCH/SIB1, RACH config, `phy_set_config`, and
    worker-pool PRACH handoff traces remain immediate.
- `cp193_sens_start_stage6_high_peak_tx_now_ue_remote.sh`
  - Exports and records `PAVONIS_SRSUE_SIB1_TRACE_PDCCH_SUMMARY_INTERVAL`.
- `cp1660_run_stage6_txatt5_nativecontinuous_alltimed_lead30ms_guard0_sequence.sh`
  - Enables `PAVONIS_SRSUE_SIB1_TRACE_PDCCH_SUMMARY_INTERVAL=200`.
  - Summarizes `si_pdcch_summary`, immediate `si_pdcch`, and
    `si_pdsch_grant` counts separately.
