## Verdict

CP1681 is an invalid late-marker measurement because the inherited
`PAVONIS_SOAPY_RX_SUMMARY_MAX=1600000` cap stopped the append at
`816000000` samples, before the `+90 s` marker's expected RX sample
`1018837644`. It is useful only as a harness-cap diagnostic and as proof that
the early marker can be found in the native `10 ms` path.

Do not use CP1681 to clear or fail the late-run drift/jump theory. The corrected
rerun is CP1682, which keeps the early `+5 s` marker and moves the late marker
to `+60 s`, inside the existing append cap.
