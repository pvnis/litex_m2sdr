## Verdict

CP1687 is the midpoint recovery the SIB bracket needed. Native-continuous
single-anchor TX at `1.5 ms` restores the full SIB1/RACH-config path and the UE
emits seven real Msg1 attempts. This differs from CP1684 `2 ms`, which reached
only an SI grant and emitted no Msg1, and from CP1683/CP1680/CP1679 at
`5/10/30 ms`, which did not recover an SI grant.

The current empirical native-continuous SIB/RACH boundary is now:

- CP1672 `1 ms`: full SIB1/RACH config and seven Msg1 attempts.
- CP1687 `1.5 ms`: full SIB1/RACH config and seven Msg1 attempts.
- CP1684 `2 ms`: SI grant only, no SIB1 decode and no Msg1.
- CP1683/CP1680/CP1679 `5/10/30 ms`: no SI grant.

CP1687 does not finish Stage 6: gNB still has `msg1_or_rar_lines=0`,
`prach_log_detected_nonempty_rows=0`, and `p0_pass_thr=0/1533` despite seven
real UE Msg1 attempts. The ladder should therefore move back from SIB bracketing
to PRACH detector/window analysis at a SIB-positive native `1.5 ms` point.
