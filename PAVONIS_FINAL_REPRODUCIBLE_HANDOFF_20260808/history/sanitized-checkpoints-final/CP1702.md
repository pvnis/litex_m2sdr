Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

Run the CP1698-class all-timed `30 ms` lead, `1 ms` write-timeout real-UE RF
gate against the CP1701 OCUDU deadline-carry build. The success criteria were:

- readback proof that deadline carry is active
- low in-run `timeout_carry_pending_samples` around SI-era operation without
  relying on final stop-time drain
- explicit accounting for any deadline drops
- normal PBCH/SFN plus SIB1/RACH config before drawing PRACH detector
  conclusions

## Verdict

CP1702 is a mechanically valid RF gate of the CP1701 binary, but it fails the
ladder criteria. Deadline carry was active and bounded pending carry near
`1 ms`, but that was achieved by deadline-dropping `3,538,587,902` samples.
That makes the run invalid as a SIB1/PRACH ladder datapoint.

Do not rerun CP1702 unchanged. The simple `1 ms` stale-drop cap is closed as a
fix. The next work should stay software/no-RF until it changes the policy away
from "drop stale backlog to maintain cadence" and toward preserving the stream
without SI-era backlog, or at minimum treats any deadline drop as an immediate
invalid gate before spending RF.
