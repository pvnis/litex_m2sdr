# CP1718 - 20 ms Periodic Re-Anchor Strict Gate, Two-Point Marker, and HAS_TIME Audit

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Purpose

Before adding another TX backpressure policy, test the already-built periodic
re-anchor mode with all-timed mode disabled:

- `PAVONIS_SOAPY_TX_REANCHOR_INTERVAL_SAMPLES=230400`
- `230400` samples is `20 ms` at `11.52 Msps`
- `PAVONIS_SOAPY_TX_ALL_TIMED_CHUNKS=0`

The gate requested by the user was strict: pass the CP1705 [host-redacted]-only TX gate
and hold two same-stream markers at about `+5 s` and `+60 s` with
microsecond-class stability and native-class timeout counts. If both held,
periodic re-anchor could replace all-timed mode as the SIB1 path. In parallel,
audit the LiteX/OCUDU `HAS_TIME` path no-RF to explain why every-chunk
timestamps overload the ring while untimed writes do not.

## Audit Interpretation

Every-chunk all-timed mode turns each MTU-sized chunk, about `1022` samples or
`88.7 us` at `11.52 Msps`, into a timed DMA descriptor. The gateware has only
a small timed-descriptor FIFO and the driver is constrained by the looping DMA
ring. That shape is sensitive to host/driver backpressure and explains why
all-timed and dense-timed variants show large timeout/partial-return pressure.

Native single-anchor mode avoids this descriptor pressure: after the first
timed anchor, later chunks are no-time continuation frames and pass through the
arbiter immediately. That gives native-class low timeout pressure, but the
two-point marker branch proved it drifts over a real UE run.

CP1718's 20 ms re-anchor mode is the middle case. It cuts the timed-descriptor
density enough to stabilize the two markers exactly, but it still produces
`23490` timeout writes, `1236` partial returns, and a stop-time
`259401898` sample stream deficit. That fails the user's replacement criterion.

## Conclusion

CP1718 does not approve periodic 20 ms re-anchor as the SIB1 path. It passes
the two-point marker stability test but fails the CP1705 strict TX gate and
does not have native-class timeout/sample-return behavior.

Do not write a fifth backpressure policy solely because marker timing passed.
The next policy or presentation change must preserve RF lead and must gate on
zero stream deficit plus low/native-class timeout pressure before any UE start.
