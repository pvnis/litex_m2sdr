## Conclusion

The current hardware wiring matches the user's expectation of one strong
cabled/attenuated pair and one weak antenna/leakage pair:

- RX1 is the clear cabled/attenuated path in software evidence: it rises
  `+15.76 dB` over its no-TX floor and has CP847 median tone SNR
  `71.46 dB`.
- RX2 is consistent with the antenna or weak-leakage path: its RMS is only
  `+0.20 dB` over no-TX, although a weaker expected-frequency tone remains
  detectable.

Use RX1 as the current cabled receive path for future cabled smoke checks.
Treat RX2 as the antenna/weak path unless a future physical label or swapped
cable test proves otherwise.
