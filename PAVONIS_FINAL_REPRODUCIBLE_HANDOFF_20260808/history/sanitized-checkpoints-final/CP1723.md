## Interpretation

CP1723 rules out the simple hybrid of CP1718's `20 ms` re-anchor timing shape
plus CP1720's carry-on-timeout preservation. The readback confirms sparse
reanchors and non-all-timed chunks were actually active, but the stream still
falls into large sample deficit, carry saturation, overflow, deadline
lag/would-drop, and abandonment before any UE could be validly started.

This is not a ladder unlock and not a SIB1 datapoint. Do not rerun CP1723
unchanged and do not start UE on this TX shape.

## Verdict

CP1723 fails the no-UE strict TX gate. The active frontier remains: preserve
CP1658-class all-timed downlink timing, but the current all-timed/deadline/
carry family still creates long-run TX backpressure/loss. Further work should
be source-level or a materially different no-UE TX presentation/gate, not
[interface-redacted] CP1721/CP1723 constants.
