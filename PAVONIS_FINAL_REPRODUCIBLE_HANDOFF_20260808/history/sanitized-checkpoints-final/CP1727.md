## Interpretation

CP1727 confirms the CP1725 audit with live RF and the new CP1726 fields:

- `stream_deficit` is still the largest number because this sparse native-continuous/re-anchor shape counts logical elapsed stream duration.
- The direct returned-sample deficit is now explicit and much smaller, but still nonzero: `178612` samples at stop.
- The data-only logical deficit is also explicit and large: `185283742` samples.
- The carry queue latches immediately at the same onset as the first deadline-write timeout: logical `25`.
- The first lag follows at logical `26`; overflow begins at logical `142`.
- The queue reaches the configured `1152000` sample cap, then abandons/overflows future chunks and accumulates deadline lag/would-drop evidence.
- The CP1720 carry-on-timeout feature still works narrowly, because deadline-write abandonment remains zero, but it moves the failure into stale ordered-carry backlog rather than recovering the stream.

This is not a SIB1, PRACH, UE, raw-TRACK, or ladder datapoint. It is a no-UE TX diagnostic checkpoint.

## Verdict

`CP1727_FAIL_EXPECTED_DIAGNOSTIC`.

Do not rerun CP1723/CP1727 unchanged and do not start UE on this TX shape. Future TX-policy work should either materially prevent the stale ordered-carry latch or return to a known passing cadence such as CP1658 all-timed chunks before trying ladder movement.

The CP1726 fields are validated for future no-UE gates: use direct/data/logical deficit counters together, not `stream_deficit` alone, when evaluating sparse native-continuous or re-anchor presentations.
