## Interpretation

The pair classification is unchanged after CP1727:

- RX1 remains the cabled/attenuated receive path. It rises `+14.19 dB` over
  the CP1717 no-TX floor, has no clipping, and CP847 passes near the expected
  tone frequency.
- RX2 remains the antenna or weak-leakage path. It is slightly below the CP1717
  no-TX RMS floor in this run, CP847 fails, and the weak CP892 energy is not a
  usable cabled-path signal.

This recheck is also useful operationally: [host-redacted] bladeRF access is currently
working under the harness path, despite the earlier non-sudo `bladeRF-cli -p`
permission/in-use warning seen around CP1726.

## Verdict

`CP1728_PASS_HARDWARE_PAIR_RECHECK`.

Use RX1 for cabled ladder datapoints. Do not use RX2 as a cabled path unless a
future physical label or deliberate swapped-cable test changes this
classification.
