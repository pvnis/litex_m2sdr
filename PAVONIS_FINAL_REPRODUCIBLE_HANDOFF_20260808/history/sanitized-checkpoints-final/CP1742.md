## Interpretation

CP1742 is the decisive baseline check requested by CP1741, and it fails below
Msg1. Because the no-delay baseline now has the same failure class as
CP1738/CP1740, the immediate blocker is not the `321600` shifted PRACH
collection value. The real-UE all-timed `30 ms` ladder has regressed below
PRACH into SI-PDCCH/SIB1 acquisition.

This does not invalidate CP1737's measured PRACH delay hypothesis. It parks it:
the gNB cannot judge a PRACH collection window until the UE reliably reaches
real RACH config and emits Msg1 again.

## Checkpoint status

CP1742 is a void no-Msg1 baseline. It proves the next frontier is restoring or
diagnosing SIB1/SI-PDCCH acquisition on the current all-timed `30 ms` real-UE
path, not retrying `321600` or changing PRACH detector/window logic.
