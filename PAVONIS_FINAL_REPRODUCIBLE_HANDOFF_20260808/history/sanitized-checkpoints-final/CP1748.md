## Interpretation

CP1748 is a valid SIB1-fail datapoint. It proves that the bandwidth-valid
YAML-only demarginalization (`fixed_sib1_mcs: 0`, `ssb_block_power_dbm: -4`)
does not restore SIB1/RACH/Msg1 and actually worsens SI-PDCCH correlation
relative to CP1745.

The parked `321600` PRACH-window test remains parked. The current blocker is
still post-PBCH SI-PDCCH/SIB1 reliability on the all-timed `30 ms` real-UE
path.

The remaining direct SI-PDCCH robustness lever is not YAML-only in this tree:
SI DCI aggregation is hard-coded to `aggregation_level::n4`. A next no-RF rung
should add or force a controlled SIB1 DCI aggregation-level override before
spending more RF.
