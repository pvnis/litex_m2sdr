## Interpretation

CP1752 closes the immediate `n8` versus default-`n4` discriminator. The
patched binary's default path does not recover SIB1, and the run is clean with
zero LiteX late/post-late counters. Therefore the active blocker is broader
than the CP1751 `n8` override and broader than CP1751's single late-count
caveat.

Do not resume the parked `321600` PRACH-window test yet: there is still no real
Msg1 supply. Do not jump directly to `n16`; CP1751 already showed that raising
aggregation to `n8` can lift the SI correlation metric without producing a
decode, while CP1752 shows default `n4` is still no-grant. The next useful
step should stay no-RF and audit/identify a more targeted SI-PDCCH/SIB1
de-margining lever or presentation difference, especially an actual SI-PDCCH
power/candidate/CORESET knob rather than another blind aggregation increase.
