## Timing Residual

Wall-time alignment shows the fixed `321600` sample delay overshot in this run.
The UE burst happened before the delayed detector/manifest windows:

```text
ue_minus_trace_ms:
-2.358643, -1.766770, -2.114186, -2.740648, -1.802771, -2.201753, -1.940496

ue_minus_manifest_ms:
-1.495312, -0.886445, -1.284174, -1.797238, -0.931542, -1.373444, -1.116063
```

At `11.52 Msps`, the manifest-level overshoot is roughly `10k..21k` samples.
For this run alone, that implies a static value closer to about
`300k..311k` samples than `321600`, but the earlier CP1618/CP1735 spread means
this should not be treated as a reusable constant without measuring the
per-run timing source.

## Conclusion

CP1755 proves `GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB=6` can preserve the restored
SIB1/RACH/real-Msg1 supply while the PRACH collection-delay plumbing is active.
It also proves the specific static `321600` sample delay does not solve the
current PRACH detector problem: seven real UE Msg1 bursts were transmitted,
but the detector still saw zero nonempty rows and the matched delayed
predemod windows stayed floor-class.

Do not rerun `321600` unchanged. The next work should stay no-RF until the
CP1754/CP1755 residual timing is reduced to a specific testable change: either
a per-run measured PRACH collection delay, a detector/search window wide enough
to cover the observed millisecond-scale residual without pretending a static
constant is stable, or a source-level timing-reference fix between delayed TX
release and PRACH collection.
