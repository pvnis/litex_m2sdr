The external late-arm path armed after `phy_set_config_ok`, but UE PRACH starts

For the captured attempts, both raw energy and CFO-swept correlation against
the saved UE bladeRF PRACH debug SC16 buffers find strong bursts after the
nominal predemod windows:

The table's center offsets are relative to each predemod window start. The


That is roughly `+30.59 ms` and `+30.30 ms` after the nominal predemod window
start at `11.52 Msps`.

The saved UL-symbol manifest independently shows the same region: top
near-window raw-RMS symbols for the captured attempts are around `+357120` to
`+361236` samples after predemod start.

## Interpretation

CP1764 proves the UE PRACH bursts are present in the same-device M2SDR RX path
and are not merely weak/absent. They arrive hundreds of thousands of samples
after the nominal predemod windows, matching the timing-reference mismatch
theory.

Do not set a final static `PRACH_COLLECTION_DELAY_SAMPLES` from CP1764 alone.
Only two attempts were captured in the append, and their best template-start
offsets differ by `3334` samples (`0.289 ms`), already larger than the
approximately `0.1 ms` slack that made static delay fragile in earlier rungs.

The actionable current result is:

```text
candidate sample-domain window-start offset class ~= +349k to +352k samples
fixed-delay confidence = insufficient
source-level timing-reference mismatch = strengthened
```

Next useful rung: repeat the late-append measurement, or improve the late-arm
mechanism so more post-SIB1 attempts are captured without arming during SI
acquisition. Only if sample-domain offsets are stable across bursts and runs
should a new static delay be tried. If the spread persists, shift the PRACH
collection reference in source using the measured TX/RX timing relationship
instead of [interface-redacted] constants.
