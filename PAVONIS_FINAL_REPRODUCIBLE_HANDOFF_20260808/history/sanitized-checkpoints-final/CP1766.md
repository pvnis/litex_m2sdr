# CP1766 - srsUE PRACH Defer No-RF Deploy

Date: [date-redacted]

## Goal

Prepare a measurement-only way to let the late same-device M2SDR RX append arm
after SIB1 decode but before Msg1, avoiding CP1758/1760/1761's SI perturbation
from pre-UE append activity and CP1764's missed first five PRACH attempts.

## Change

Added default-off `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_DEFER_MS` in the srsUE NR RA MAC path.
When unset or `0`, behavior is unchanged. When positive, the RA procedure
selects the preamble, waits that many timer ticks before calling
`phy->send_prach()`, and still starts the RAR window from the normal later
`prach_sent()` callback.

The CP1733 wrapper now forwards `UE_PRACH_DEFER_MS` to [host-redacted] as
`PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_DEFER_MS` and records defer config/schedule/release counts
in the summary JSON.
