UE defer knob.

- Post-check `[host-redacted]` ping/helper SSH passed.

- gNB log proved `PAVONIS_GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB active value=6`.
- TX/release counters stayed mechanically clean for this branch:
  `soapy_tx_timeout_lines=0`, `litex_release_late_lines=0`, LiteX stop
  `post_late_count=0`.

  `cell_select_ok=true`, `sfn_sync_ok=true`, one SI-PDCCH grant, one SI-PDSCH
  grant, one BCCH/SIB1 decode, and one `phy_set_config_ok`.
- UE emitted seven real PRACH attempts at TTIs
  `4334/4494/4654/4814/4974/5134/5294`.
- gNB PRACH detector still did not see real Msg1:
  `1530` timestamped rows, `detected_nonempty_rows=0`, `accepted_rows=1`.

## Defer Plumbing Finding

The requested `UE_PRACH_DEFER_MS=1000` did not reach the final sudo-launched
UE process.

- CP1767 summary reported `prach_defer_config_lines=0`,
  `prach_defer_schedule_lines=0`, and `prach_defer_release_lines=0`.
- The saved `run_srsue_bounded.sh` did not include
  `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_DEFER_MS` in its `sudo -n env` block or env audit.
- Therefore CP1767 is a useful no-defer late-arm repeat, not the intended
  defer measurement.

## Conclusion

CP1767 adds valid sample-domain evidence that real UE PRACH bursts are present
in the M2SDR RX path about `347k..357k` samples after the nominal predemod
window start, but it is not the intended defer run. Fix the final UE helper
env propagation before the next RF run, then require nonzero defer config/
schedule/release logs before interpreting the measurement.
