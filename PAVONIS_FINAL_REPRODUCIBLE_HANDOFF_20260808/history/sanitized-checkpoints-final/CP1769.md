## Result

The run is void for PRACH timing and sample-domain statistics because the UE
never reached SIB1/RACH/Msg1.

- Harness passed: `overall_rc=0`, `gate_rc=0`, `late_gate_rc=0`, `ue_started=1`.
- Preflight and postcheck `[host-redacted]` helper SSH passed; postcheck ping passed
  `2/2`, `0%` loss.
- TX stayed in the CP1658 all-timed/native-continuous class:
  `tx_all_timed_chunks=1`, `tx_reanchor_enabled=0`, `write_timeout_us=1000`,
  `env_M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS=30000000`.
- TX side had no visible mechanical regression for this branch:
  `soapy_tx_timeout_lines=0`, `litex_release_late_lines=0`, LiteX stop
  `post_late_count=0`.
- The CP1768 helper fix did reach the UE process at least at config time:
  `PAVONIS_SRSUE_PRACH_DEFER_CONFIG value=1000 parsed_ms=1000 enabled=1`.
- The UE passed cell select and SFN sync, then failed SIB1:
  `800` SI-PDCCH summary rows, all `n_dl_max=0`, zero SI-PDCCH grants,
  zero SI-PDSCH grants, zero BCCH/SIB1 decode, zero `phy_set_config_ok`.
- SI `max_corr` was low: min/mean/max `0.108782/0.149717069/0.240398`.
- There were no PRACH attempts:
  `prach_defer_schedule_lines=0`, `prach_defer_release_lines=0`,
  `random_access_transmission_count=0`, `prach_tx_count=0`,
  `rf_blade_tx_dbg_sc16=0`.
- The late append correctly did not arm:
  `wait_sib1_for_late_rx_append_arm rc=43`,
  `soapy_rx_append_cf32=0`.
- The gNB detector saw no PRACH evidence:
  `1530` timestamped rows, `detected_nonempty_rows=0`, `accepted_rows=0`,
  no Msg1/RAR lines.

## Conclusion

CP1769 proves the final helper propagation fix reaches the UE process, but it
does not produce the requested all-seven-burst sample-domain statistics because
the UE fails before SIB1/RACH config. Treat it as a void no-SIB/no-Msg1 retry,
not as evidence against defer, late append, measured delay, or the combined
delay-plus-extra PRACH collection fix.
