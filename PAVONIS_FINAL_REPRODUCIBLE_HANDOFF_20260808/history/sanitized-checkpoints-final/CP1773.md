## Conclusion

CP1773 is a second consecutive no-SIB/no-Msg1 void with the combined
delay+extra settings active. It still does not reject the combined PRACH
collection fix, but the repeated no-SIB outcome means the next step should not
be another unchanged retry. A plausible measurement-side splitter is the
widened predemod raw dump volume: with `extra=12000`, each raw dump is about
twice the nominal window and the harness still writes up to `1700` raw
predemod files during the run. Before spending another RF attempt, audit and
use the runner knobs to disable or reduce predemod raw dumps while keeping the
detector trace and summary evidence.
