# CHECKPOINT 1778 - RAR-window offset diagnostic schedules RAR but UE does not advance

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Goal

Run one CP1775-shape RF test with the CP1777 default-off scheduler diagnostic
enabled:


diagnosis that accepted PRACH detections were reaching MAC/scheduler but being
dropped because their RAR response windows were still keyed to the nominal

## Conclusion

CP1778 proves the CP1777 diagnostic does what it was designed to do: it removes
the CP1776 stale-RAR-window expiry and lets the scheduler allocate RAR PDSCH
for accepted PRACH detections.

It does not prove end-to-end random access. The UE times out after PRACH with
no observed Msg2/Msg3/contention/RRC progress. In this run, the true same-SFN
real-p0 detector supply is only `1/7`, and three of the missed real p0 bursts
are over threshold but fail the detector delay gate by a small number of
samples. The split `rar_window_ref_slot` diagnostic is therefore useful proof,
but it should not become the final fix.

## Next step

Stay no-RF first. Do not [interface-redacted] `PRACH_COLLECTION_DELAY_SAMPLES` or
`PRACH_COLLECTION_EXTRA_SAMPLES` again from CP1778. The next source-level work
should move the whole UL/RACH timing reference by the live TX offset, or
otherwise make the detector's occasion attribution, internal delay gate, and
RAR response-window timing use one coherent compensated reference. That should
also explain why CP1778 can schedule one real RAR while the UE still misses the
RA response and why later strong p0 rows fail `p0_pass_delay`.
