## Result

CP1780 proves the CP1779 coherent timing-compensation plumbing works in the RF
harness and that the scheduler-only RAR-window offset was not accidentally
active.

It does not test the PRACH/RAR hypothesis because the UE never received SIB1 or
emitted Msg1. Treat CP1780 as a void no-Msg1 run for PRACH conclusions.

tweak:
