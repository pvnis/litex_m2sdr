## Interpretation

CP1785 confirms the CP1783 global-offset configuration can run cleanly a second
time with [host-redacted]/[host-redacted] helper SSH healthy, but it still does not test the
global-timebase hypothesis. The decisive CP1782 link, UE RAR receipt and Msg3
preparation, was not reached.

Do not use CP1785 to [interface-redacted] PRACH collection delay, PRACH extra width, or
RAR-offset logic. The active blocker is SIB1/RACH-config supply under the
current exact global-offset run shape. The next work should either perform an
unchanged retry until real Msg1 supply appears or do a no-RF comparison of
SIB1-era TX/correlation differences between the CP1733 pass and CP1784/CP1785
voids before spending more RF.
