## Interpretation

CP1788 moves the active blocker out of the global-offset hypothesis. The same
shape that was SIB1/RACH/Msg1-positive in CP1781 now voids before SIB1, despite
the RF wrapper and gates passing. The repeated failures in CP1784, CP1785,
CP1787, and CP1788 therefore indicate a current SIB1-supply regression in the
present binary/environment/RF state, not a proven PRACH-window, RAR-offset, or
global-UL-offset result.

Do not [interface-redacted] PRACH collection delay, PRACH extra width, RAR-window offset, or
Msg3 timing from CP1788. Next work should isolate the current SIB1 regression:
compare the post-CP1783 lower-PHY binary/source against the pre-CP1783 CP1781
binary/source and consider restoring the pre-CP1783 lower-PHY source/binary as
a software-only control before another ladder RF run. If source restore is too
heavy, rerun the raw scheduled-TRACK/SIB1 downlink gate to establish whether
the current downlink is still above the CP1658/CP1732 baseline.
