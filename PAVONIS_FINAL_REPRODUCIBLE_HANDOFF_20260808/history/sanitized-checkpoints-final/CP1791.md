## gNB And PRACH Detector Result

Initial review of the normal `gnb.log` found no `prach(...)`, RAR, Msg3,
PUSCH CRC, or HARQ-exhaust lines. CP1792 corrected this by checking the
binary-safe gNB log packaged at:

`cp1733_nuc4_artifacts_[date-redacted]CP1791_PRECP1783_TIMECOMP352536_EXTRA12000_POSCTRL/tmp/gnb_m2sdr_band3_ota.log`

That binary-safe log does contain the scheduler/PHY evidence. The summary
fields below are therefore incomplete for RAR/Msg3:

- `gnb.msg1_or_rar_lines=0`
- `gnb.prach_log_detected_nonempty_rows=0`
- `gnb.litex_release_late_lines=0`
- `gnb.soapy_tx_timeout_lines=0`

The detector trace contains six `accepted=1` rows. Four are real preamble-0
accepts near the UE PRACH attempts and at the expected compensated report
position:

- `sfn=340 slot=4`, `best_preamble=0`, `p0_ratio=1.244`, `rssi_dB=-48.30`
- `sfn=452 slot=4`, `best_preamble=0`, `p0_ratio=1.567`, `rssi_dB=-52.27`
- `sfn=564 slot=4`, `best_preamble=0`, `p0_ratio=2.567`, `rssi_dB=-51.74`
- `sfn=788 slot=4`, `best_preamble=0`, `p0_ratio=1.018`, `rssi_dB=-49.29`

Two later accepts are likely idle false accepts because the winning preamble is
not zero and p0 is below threshold:

- `sfn=804 slot=4`, `best_preamble=2`, `p0_ratio=0.513`, `rssi_dB=-93.71`
- `sfn=820 slot=4`, `best_preamble=57`, `p0_ratio=0.362`, `rssi_dB=-93.84`

The four real accepts line up with the coherent timing-compensation branch:
the reported detector TTI is UE PRACH TTI plus about 30 slots, matching
`352536` samples at `11.52 Msps` plus the CP1779 residual.

The binary-safe gNB log proves the accepts reach RAR and Msg3 scheduling:

- `0x4601`: `prach(...)` and RAR at slot `341.1`, Msg3 PUSCH attempts at
  `341.7..344.9`, all CRC KO, HARQ exhausted at `345.4`.
- `0x4602`: `prach(...)` and RAR at slot `453.1`, Msg3 PUSCH attempts at
  `453.7..456.9`, all CRC KO, HARQ exhausted at `457.4`.
- `0x4603`: `prach(...)` and RAR at slot `565.1`, Msg3 PUSCH attempts at
  `565.7..568.9`, all CRC KO, HARQ exhausted at `569.4`.
- `0x4604`: `prach(...)` and RAR at slot `789.1`, Msg3 PUSCH attempts at
  `789.7..792.6`, all CRC KO, HARQ exhausted at `793.1`.
- The two likely idle false accepts also schedule RAR/Msg3 attempts:
  `0x4605` at `805.1` and `0x4606` at `821.0`, both CRC-KO/exhausted.

## Conclusion

CP1791 is a positive control for SIB1/Msg1 supply: restoring the pre-CP1783
binary brings SIB1/RACH config and seven real UE Msg1 attempts back on the same
current hardware. That implicates the CP1783 lower-PHY global-offset
source/binary state as the SIB1-supply regressor seen in CP1784-CP1788, even
when the new offset was set to zero.

CP1791 passes the gNB-side RAR scheduling gate and reproduces the CP1781
frontier on the restored pre-CP1783 binary. It shows four real p0 detector
accepts at the compensated timing, and all four produce logged RAR plus Msg3
PUSCH receive attempts. The UE still never reports RAR receipt or Msg3
preparation, so Msg3 is still not confirmed UE-originated.

Do not [interface-redacted] PRACH window width, PRACH delay, or scheduler-only RAR offset from
CP1791. The next step is to fix the CP1783 global lower-PHY offset
implementation/regression so the global-UL-offset experiment can be run on a
SIB1/RACH-valid path; CP1784-CP1788 did not test the global-timebase hypothesis
because the CP1783 source/binary state regressed before SIB1.
