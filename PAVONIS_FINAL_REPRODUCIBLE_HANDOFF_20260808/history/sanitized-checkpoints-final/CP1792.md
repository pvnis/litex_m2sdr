## UE RAR/Msg3 Status

The UE-side result remains unchanged from CP1782:

- no `Setting RAR Grant`
- no `Set TA RAR`
- no `PROC RA NR: Setting UL grant and prepare Msg3`
- no Msg3 or contention marker

Therefore the gNB Msg3 CRC KOs are still scheduled receive attempts after the
gNB's own RAR decisions, not confirmed UE Msg3 transmissions.

## Conclusion

CP1791 should be reclassified from "SIB1/Msg1 positive but no RAR" to
"SIB1/Msg1 positive and gNB-side RAR/Msg3 scheduling positive, UE-side RAR
still absent." The restored pre-CP1783 binary not only fixes the CP1784-CP1788
SIB1 regression, it also reproduces and strengthens the CP1781 frontier with
four real p0 accepts reaching RAR scheduling.

The current blocker is no longer accepted-result handoff. It is the global UL
timebase/RAR-consumption frontier: the UE still does not show decoded RAR or
prepared Msg3, while the gNB listens for Msg3 and gets CRC KOs. CP1784-CP1788
did not test the global lower-PHY offset because the CP1783 source/binary state
regressed before SIB1. Next work should fix or reimplement the global UL
timestamp offset on top of the restored SIB1-valid baseline, with a default-off
no-op path that preserves CP1791 behavior before any RF global-offset retry.
