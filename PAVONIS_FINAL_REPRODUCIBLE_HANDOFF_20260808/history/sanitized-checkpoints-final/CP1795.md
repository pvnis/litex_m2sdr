## Conclusion

CP1795 is not a valid RAR/Msg3 datapoint because the UE never reached SIB1/RACH
or emitted Msg1. It does not prove or kill the global-timebase hypothesis at
the UE RAR-consumption level.

It is still an important regression signal: the nonzero global offset was
active from gNB start, the TX gates stayed clean, and the run regressed before
SFN/SIB1 immediately after CP1794's zero-offset pass. The false PRACH accepts
also still surfaced as stale RAR-window events under the nonzero global offset.

Do not [interface-redacted] PRACH delay/extra or scheduler-only RAR offset from CP1795. The
next work should stay focused on the global-offset path itself: either retry
the exact nonzero RF shape only because no-Msg1 runs are void, or first do a
no-RF source/timing audit of how the shifted timestamp reaches PRACH event
time attribution and whether the sign/reference is still producing stale
RAR-window timing.
