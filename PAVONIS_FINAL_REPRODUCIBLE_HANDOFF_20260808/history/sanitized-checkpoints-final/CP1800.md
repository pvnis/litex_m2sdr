## Result

- UE: `cell_select_ok=true`, `sfn_sync_ok=true`
- SIB1: `sib1_bcch_decoded_count=1`, `sib1_phy_set_config_ok_count=1`
- Msg1 supply: seven `Random Access Transmission` rows and seven `PRACH-TX`
  rows
- gNB detector: three accepted rows; one real p0 accept
  (`sfn=420 slot=4`, `p0_ratio=1.661`) plus later false accepts
- gNB scheduled RAR and Msg3 PUSCH attempts for `0x4601..0x4603`; all observed
  Msg3 PUSCH attempts were CRC KO.
- UE still had no `Setting RAR Grant`, no `Set TA RAR`, and no Msg3 prep
  marker.

## Conclusion

The patched UE binary can still recover SIB1/Msg1 when RAR trace is disabled.
This makes the CP1798/CP1799 failures trace-specific enough to fix the
instrumentation before using it as a RAR diagnostic.
