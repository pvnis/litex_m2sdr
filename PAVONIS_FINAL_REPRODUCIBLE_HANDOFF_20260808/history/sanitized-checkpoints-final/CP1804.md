## Conclusion

CP1804 is the first valid UE-side RAR trace datapoint. It proves the UE opens
the RA response windows and monitors RA-RNTI, but it does not decode any RA
PDCCH DCI or RA PDSCH, even when real p0 accepts cause gNB RAR scheduling at
the same wall-time epoch. The next rung should be no-RF/source-level analysis
of the RAR PDCCH/search-space/config mapping between gNB scheduling and srsUE
RA-RNTI monitoring. Do not spend more RF on PRACH window width, PRACH delay, or
RAR offset constants until the RA-PDCCH mismatch is understood.
