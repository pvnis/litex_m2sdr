## Conclusion

40-slot window, the UE finally sees one RA PDCCH/PDSCH decode path instead of
only `n_dl=0` checks. It still fails the checkpoint success criterion because
the UE never emits the downstream RAR markers or Msg3 preparation.

Do not [interface-redacted] PRACH collection delay/extra, RAR-offset shims, or the old
negative lower-PHY timestamp offset from CP1808. Next work should stay no-RF

and why the gNB normal log did not show RAR scheduling despite the accepted p0
trace row.
