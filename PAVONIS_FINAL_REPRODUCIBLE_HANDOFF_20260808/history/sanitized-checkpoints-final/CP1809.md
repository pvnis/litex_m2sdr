## Conclusion

CP1809 is mechanically valid but void for the RAR/Msg3 gate: the UE never
decoded SIB1/RACH config and never transmitted Msg1. It repeats the CP1784/
CP1785/CP1795 failure class under the exact negative global lower-PHY offset
shape, while CP1796 already showed that this exact implementation is not
source-equivalent to the validated CP1779/CP1794 sample-domain compensation.

Do not [interface-redacted] PRACH delay/extra, RAR offsets, or rerun this exact negative
lower-PHY timestamp-offset shape unchanged. The active next step remains no-RF:
either continue CP1808's UE RA-PDSCH-to-MAC-RAR audit, or design a coherent
source-level UL compensation that preserves the CP1794/CP1808 downlink/SIB1
baseline instead of subtracting `352536` samples from the lower-PHY RX
timestamp.
