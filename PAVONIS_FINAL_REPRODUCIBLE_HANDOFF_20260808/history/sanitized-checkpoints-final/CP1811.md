The new default-off trace prints only when `PAVONIS_SRSUE_RAR_TRACE` is active.
It records:

- `event=tb_decoded`: grant RNTI, RA-RNTI status, ack, payload presence/length,
  first payload bytes, current `proc_ra` RAR-RNTI, and whether dispatch goes to
  RA or HARQ.
- `event=ra_response_entry`: RA state, expected state, ack/payload, RAR-RNTI,
  preamble index, RX slot, and first payload bytes.
- `event=ra_response_unpack`: unpack success/failure.
- `event=ra_response_subpdu`: RAPID, RAPID match, TA, temp C-RNTI, and backoff
  fields per subPDU.
- `event=ra_response_accept` or `event=ra_response_no_match`.

## Conclusion

CP1811 successfully deploys the missing UE-side RAR handoff observability on
[host-redacted] without changing RF or gNB behavior. The next RF run should reuse the
CP1808 shape with trace enabled, not [interface-redacted] PRACH timing/RAR offsets:

- all-timed DL cadence;
- `GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB=6`;
- deferred UE PRACH;
- CP1808 corrected `prach.ra_resp_window: 40`;
- current gNB extended-RA-window validator;
- `PAVONIS_SRSUE_RAR_TRACE=1`;
- enough `PAVONIS_SRSUE_RAR_TRACE_LIMIT` headroom for the RA-PDSCH handoff.

Judge the run on the new UE trace first:

- whether `tb_decoded` sees `grant_rnti=0x39`, `ack=1`, payload bytes, and
  `dispatch_rar=1`;
- whether `ra_response_entry` fires;
- whether unpack fails, RAPID mismatches, or `ra_response_accept` occurs;
- then the original markers: `Setting RAR Grant`, `Set TA RAR`, Msg3
  preparation, and gNB Msg3 CRC.

No PRACH-window, RAR-offset, or exact negative lower-PHY-offset [interface-redacted] is
justified until this handoff trace classifies CP1808's CRC-positive RA-PDSCH
decode.
