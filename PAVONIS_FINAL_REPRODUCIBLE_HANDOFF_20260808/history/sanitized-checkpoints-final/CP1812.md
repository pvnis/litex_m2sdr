## Conclusion

CP1812 does not classify the CP1808 RA-PDSCH-to-MAC-RAR failure because it
never reaches SIB1/RACH/Msg1, and therefore never creates a decoded RA-RNTI TB
for the CP1811 trace to observe.

Treat CP1812 as a void trace-on RF retry and another current no-SIB1/SI-control
decode failure under the CP1808/CP1811 shape. It does not justify PRACH
collection delay, PRACH window, scheduler RAR-offset, or exact negative
lower-PHY-offset [interface-redacted]. The CP1808 handoff question still needs either a valid
SIB1/Msg1 trace-on retry or a no-RF comparison explaining why CP1812's SI
control metric collapsed before spending more RF.
