## Interpretation

CP1828 does not answer the RAR-grant question. It failed before SIB1/RACH and
before any UE Msg1. Per the new branch-1 rule, treat this as one scatter/void
run, not as proof that RAR tracing is perturbative. Do not change PRACH timing,
PRACH extra width, global lower-PHY offset, or RAR window constants based on
this run.
