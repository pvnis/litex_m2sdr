The decisive UE-side RAR/Msg3 markers are:

- `0x4602`: UE decodes RAR at slot `5811`, accepts RAPID `0`, TA `137`,
  sets the grant with `k=6`, fetches an `11` byte Msg3, buffers it in UL HARQ,
  and encodes PUSCH at `tti_tx=5817`. A retx-side encode also appears at
  `tti_tx=5857`.
- `0x4603`: UE decodes RAR at slot `6931`, accepts RAPID `0`, TA `111`,
  sets the grant with `k=6`, fetches an `11` byte Msg3, buffers it in UL HARQ,
  and encodes PUSCH at `tti_tx=6937`.

The first gNB RAR branch, `0x4601`, was transmitted by the gNB but has no
matching UE RAR accept in this trace, so only the `0x4602` and `0x4603` PUSCH
branches are confirmed real UE Msg3 evidence.

## gNB Side

The PRACH detector accepted three real preamble-0 rows:

- `sfn=468 slot=4`, p0 ratio `1.016`
- `sfn=580 slot=4`, p0 ratio `1.331`
- `sfn=692 slot=4`, p0 ratio `1.236`

The gNB RAR PDU trace emitted RAPID `0` grants:

- `0x4601`, TA `132`, `pdu_hex=[nas-redacted]`
- `0x4602`, TA `137`, `pdu_hex=[nas-redacted]`
- `0x4603`, TA `111`, `pdu_hex=[nas-redacted]`

The gNB then listened at the nominal PUSCH slots matching the UE trace:

- `0x4602`: first PUSCH at `581.7`, retx at `585.7`, then additional HARQ
  attempts through `597.4`.
- `0x4603`: first PUSCH at `693.7`, then additional HARQ attempts through
  `709.5`.

Msg3 CRC result:

- `15` total `PUSCH: rnti=0x460...` decode attempts.
- `0` CRC OK.
- `15` CRC KO.
- SINR range: `-33.1..-14.6 dB`.

The three UE-confirmed PUSCH encodes (`581.7`, `585.7`, `693.7`) all line up
with gNB decode attempts and all CRC-KO. CP1837 therefore closes "no real UE
Msg3 supply" for this run and shows that the generic non-PRACH shift did not
make Msg3 CRC pass.

## Result

CP1837 is a valid semantic rejection of the current generic non-PRACH shift
shape as sufficient for Msg3 CRC. It does not regress the ladder below RAR:
SIB1, Msg1, RAR decode/accept, Msg3 MAC fetch, UL HARQ buffering, and PUSCH
encode all occur for real UE branches.

Do not spend the next RF run on another unchanged retry or delay-constant
sweep. The next step should be no-RF first:

- Audit whether the shifted PUxCH path is actually the grid consumed by the
  PUSCH decoder for these grants, including request lifetime and repeated SFN
  cycles.
- Compare CP1832 and CP1837 PUSCH timing/SINR/slot geometry from saved logs.
- Treat the all-timed TX timeout/downlink-late storm as a live co-suspect for
  both DL grant visibility and UL quality before adding another backpressure
  or offset policy.
