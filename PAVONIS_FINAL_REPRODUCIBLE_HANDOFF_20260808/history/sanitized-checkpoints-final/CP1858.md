## Conclusion

The current failure is not a stale-result filter, expired RAR window, or
PUxCH/PUSCH ordering issue: RAR/Msg3 is never reached. It is also not simply a
missing collection window: CP1857's corresponding windows are present and
energized.

The remaining fault is earlier, at the PRACH detector input or correlation
interpretation. The leading question is why CP1857's energized windows have
poor preamble-0/ZC correlation compared with CP1775 at similar raw RMS. Next
work should stay no-RF and compare the source/runtime changes between CP1775
and CP1857 that affect PRACH sample preparation, frequency-bin/RB mapping,
scaling/normalization, and detector call parameters. Do not spend more RF runs
or [interface-redacted] RAR/PUxCH timing until this correlation regression is explained.
