## Conclusion

CP1860 strongly supports the manifest hot-path load hypothesis. Removing the
per-symbol sample scans restores real p0 detector acceptance and gNB RAR
transmission on the same RF shape that CP1857 ran with zero accepted rows and
max p0 ratio only `0.367`.

The ladder is now back above gNB-side Msg1/RAR, but not yet above UE-side RAR
receipt or Msg3. Since CP1837 previously reached UE RAR/Msg3 on the same
coherent branch, the next RF step can repeat the CP1860 metadata-only shape
once to seek UE RAR/Msg3 supply and then judge PUxCH ordering. If UE RAR/Msg3
stays absent, stop RF and do a no-RF RAR visibility/downlink-late comparison
against CP1837.
