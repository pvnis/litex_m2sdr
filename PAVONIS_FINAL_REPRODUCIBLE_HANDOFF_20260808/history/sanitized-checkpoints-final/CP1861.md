## Conclusion

CP1861 shows that metadata-only is not sufficient to make PRACH acceptance
reliable. CP1860 still proves the hot-path sample-scan load was a real
contributor because the same shape recovered strong p0 ratios and RAR grants
when scans were disabled, but CP1861 shows an additional intermittent
correlation/timing/coherence factor remains.

Stop RF here. Next work should be no-RF: compare CP1837, CP1860, and CP1861
around RAR visibility, detector p0 windows, downlink-late/timeout timing, and
the PRACH predemod/detector parameters. Do not run a third same-shape
metadata-only RF retry before that comparison.
