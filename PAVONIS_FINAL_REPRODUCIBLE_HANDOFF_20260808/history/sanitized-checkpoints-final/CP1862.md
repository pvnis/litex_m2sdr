## Conclusion

CP1862 repeats the CP1857/CP1861 PRACH-correlation failure class despite
metadata-only instrumentation being active. CP1860 remains the proof that
sample-scan hot-path load was a real contributor, but CP1861 and CP1862 prove
metadata-only does not make PRACH acceptance reliable.

The requested UE RAR-blind downlink comparison is not triggered by CP1862,
because there were zero accepted PRACH rows and zero gNB RAR grants. Do not
spend another RF retry on this exact metadata-only shape. The next work should
stay no-RF and compare CP1860 against CP1861/CP1862 at the PRACH detector input
and timing/correlation level, while keeping the RAR-epoch downlink comparison
parked until a run again emits gNB RAR grants but the UE stays blind.
