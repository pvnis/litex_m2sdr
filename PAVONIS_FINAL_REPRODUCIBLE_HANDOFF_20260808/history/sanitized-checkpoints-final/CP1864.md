# CHECKPOINT 1864 - No-RF CFO/gain PRACH join

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

Stay no-RF and extend the CP1860-vs-CP1861/CP1862 detector-input comparison
into a cross-run join over valid Msg1 runs. The hypothesis under test was that
run-scale CFO or gNB RX gain drift explains why identical or near-identical
shapes sometimes produce accepted p0 rows and sometimes collapse to
`~0.3-0.4` p0 ratio.

The RAR-epoch downlink comparison remains parked until a future run again emits
gNB RAR grants while the UE remains RAR-blind.

## Interpretation

The joined artifacts do not support run-scale CFO or gNB RX gain drift as the
leading discriminator:

```text
accepted runs do not cluster at smaller |cell-search CFO|
accepted bursts do not cluster at smaller |per-PRACH CFO|
gNB RX gain setting is constant at 40, with no saved readback variation
window RSSI distributions overlap between accepted and rejected bursts
p0_delay alone does not separate pass/fail
```

CP1860 remains unusual because it has one very high p0 outlier (`7.803`) and one
normal accepted p0 (`1.234`) while the same run also contains rejected strong
bursts. That pattern is more consistent with intermittent coherence/correlation
quality than a static CFO or gain setting.

## Decision

Do not implement CFO compensation or a CFO sweep from this evidence. The
condition requested for that branch, accepted runs clustering at small |CFO|,
is false in the saved data.

Next no-RF work should inspect the PRACH sample preparation/correlation path for
other run/burst-varying coherence inputs. If another RF discriminator is needed,
it should be event-triggered and bounded: capture only the strong PRACH windows
needed for offline replay, with metadata-only/default-off hot-path behavior
preserved.
