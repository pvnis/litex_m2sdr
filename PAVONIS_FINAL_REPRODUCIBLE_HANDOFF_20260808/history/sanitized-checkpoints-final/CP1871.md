## Conclusion

CP1871 validates the CP1870 late-arm control path under RF: when SIB1 config is
not reached, the segment trace remains unarmed and no idle-window segment TSV is
produced. It does not answer PRACH segment continuity, detector-input
coherence, PUxCH ordering, or Msg3 timing because there was no real Msg1/RAR
supply.

Do not rerun CP1871 unchanged as a PRACH-continuity datapoint. The next rung
should first restore or diagnose SIB1/RACH-config supply under this low-impact
late-arm shape, then re-use the same late-arm settings once `phy_set_config_ok`
and Msg1 supply are present.
