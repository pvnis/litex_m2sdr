## Settings Check

Relevant sequence settings:

- CP1857: non-PRACH shift `-352536`, `PAVONIS_PUXCH_ORDER_TRACE=1`,
  typed UL manifest.
- CP1860/CP1861/CP1862: non-PRACH shift `-352536`,
  `PAVONIS_PUXCH_ORDER_TRACE=1`, `PAVONIS_UL_SYMBOL_METADATA_ONLY=1`.
- CP1868: metadata-only and order trace, but non-PRACH shift `0`, segment trace
  pre-armed on the normal PRACH arm file; it consumed the cap on idle windows.
- CP1871: metadata-only, order trace, non-PRACH shift `-352536`, segment trace
  enabled with `PAVONIS_PRACH_COLLECTION_SEGMENT_TRACE_ARM_MODE=after_sib1`.

## Findings

- The SIB1-pass runs in this group have decisive SI-control correlation around
  `0.55` to `0.62` and at least one positive SI-control event.
- CP1868 is a clear no-SIB1 low-correlation point (`0.233910`, zero positive
  events).
- CP1871 is better than CP1868 but still below the recent pass class
  (`0.460243`, zero positive events).
- Soapy timeout count does not split SIB1 supply here: CP1860 and CP1861 pass
  SIB1 with about `126k-127k` timeout lines and around `1k` downlink-late lines,
  while CP1871 has `126083` timeout lines and only `308` downlink-late lines.
- CP1871 matches the CP1860-class settings on metadata-only UL tracing,
  PUxCH order tracing, and non-PRACH shift. The visible new knob is segment
  trace enabled in late-arm mode, but CP1871 proves it never armed and produced
  no segment TSV.

## Conclusion

CP1872 does not prove that late-arm segment tracing caused the SIB1 failure; it
also does not prove pure scatter. The clean next RF control is a current
CP1860-class supply run with segment tracing disabled:

- keep `PAVONIS_UL_SYMBOL_METADATA_ONLY=1`
- keep `PAVONIS_PUXCH_ORDER_TRACE=1`
- keep `PAVONIS_NON_PRACH_UL_RX_TIMESTAMP_OFFSET_SAMPLES=-352536`
- keep `PRACH_PREDEMOD_RAW_MAX=0`
- set `PAVONIS_PRACH_COLLECTION_SEGMENT_TRACE=0`

If that control restores `phy_set_config_ok` and Msg1 supply, the late-arm
segment-trace env/control path remains suspect enough to inspect before
re-enabling it. If it also voids below SIB1, the current blocker is SIB1 supply
or runtime RF scatter, not the segment-trace arm mode.
