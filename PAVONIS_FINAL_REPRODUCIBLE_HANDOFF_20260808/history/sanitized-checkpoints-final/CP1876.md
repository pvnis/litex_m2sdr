- Cell-search RSRP and EPRE are essentially unchanged across SIB1-pass and
  SIB1-fail groups, both across the day and in the recent shape-compatible
  subset.
- Cell-search SNR is somewhat lower in fail runs, but not a decisive link drop:
  CP1871 and CP1873 have pass-class cell-search SNR/RSRP/EPRE but still fail
  SIB1, and CP1875 is above the all-day pass-run SNR minimum.
- The actual splitter remains SI-control quality:
  recent pass runs have `si_best_corr=0.549892..0.623881` with positive
  SI-control events, while recent fail runs have `0.233910..0.473540` and zero
  positive SI-control events.
- The unexplained storm inversion is real and should be folded into the pending
  storm-placement join: recent SIB1-fail runs have far fewer downlink-late lines
  (`228` mean, CP1875 only `52`) than pass runs (`841` mean), while Soapy
  timeout counts stay in the same `~126k` class.
