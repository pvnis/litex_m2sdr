## Interpretation

The join does not support more config-level SI power or delay [interface-redacted]. It also
does not prove that downlink-late lines directly cause the no-SIB1 condition:
CP1862 passed with only `113` total downlink-late lines, while CP1879 failed
with `221`, and all runs sit in the same `~126k` timeout class.

The strongest remaining split is temporal/stateful rather than per-run health
count: CP1857-CP1862 passed SIB1/Msg1 before the CP1866 segment-trace rebuild
and later CP1878 profile-offset rebuild, while CP1868/1871/1875/1879 all fail
SIB1 after that period despite default-off gating. The next decisive control is
the requested binary restoration.
