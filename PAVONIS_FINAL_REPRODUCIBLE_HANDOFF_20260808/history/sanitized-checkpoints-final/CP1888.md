## Conclusion

CP1888 is a valid failed shifted-frequency discriminator. Correcting the SSB
ARFCN recovers PBCH acquisition at the shifted carrier, but it does not recover
SFN/SIB1, `phy_set_config_ok`, Msg1 supply, or PRACH p0 acceptance. This rejects
the simple frequency-selective path-interference hypothesis as the immediate
remote-only recovery.

Per the user's branch plan, the next action is the authorized SSH reboot of both
[host-redacted] and [host-redacted], followed by exactly one no-config-change original-frequency
CP1882-shape sanity control to test whether reboot restores coherence.
