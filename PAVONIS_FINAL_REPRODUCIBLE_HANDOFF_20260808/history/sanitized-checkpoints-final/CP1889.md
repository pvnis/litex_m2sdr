## Conclusion

Neither tested recovery variable restored coherence:

- Corrected `+4 MHz` shifted carrier in CP1888 recovered PBCH acquisition but
  not SFN/SIB1/Msg1/PRACH.
- Reboot plus no-config-change original-frequency control in CP1889 did not
  recover PBCH at all (`100/100` empty cell-search trials).

The active blocker is therefore not explained by a simple frequency-selective
interference fix or by a transient state cleared by reboot. The next step should
return to remote-only physical/state sanity checks before more ladder RF: verify
current bladeRF/M2SDR RF state and, if possible without lab intervention, compare
a low-cost tone/no-TX or self-RX baseline to distinguish external path/coupling
loss from live-gNB timing/coherence again.
