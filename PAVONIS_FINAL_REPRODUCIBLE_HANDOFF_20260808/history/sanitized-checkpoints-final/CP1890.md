## Conclusion

CP1890 confirms a real reboot-reset host-state change: CPU scaling governors
were `powersave` during CP1889's post-reboot RF control. That makes CP1889 an
invalid reboot-verdict datapoint for realtime behavior, as the user suspected.

The only clear reset-induced [interface-redacted] issue found was restored: all CPU governors
and EPP are now `performance`. M2SDR is present and operational by read-only
health checks, with stable required clocks and no relevant dmesg errors.
