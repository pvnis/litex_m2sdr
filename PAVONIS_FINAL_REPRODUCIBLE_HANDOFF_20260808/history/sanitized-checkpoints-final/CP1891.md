## Conclusion

CP1891 proves the user suspicion was correct: CP1889 was not a valid failed
reboot test. The reboot reset [host-redacted] CPU governors to `powersave`; after CP1890
restored them to `performance`, the original-frequency run recovered PBCH, SFN,
SIB1, real Msg1, gNB PRACH accept, gNB RAR, UE-side RAR acceptance, and UE Msg3
encoding.

The same-device M2SDR self-RX discriminator is not the right immediate next
step, because the coherence/path problem has recovered enough to pass the
downlink and RAR ladder. The active frontier is again the known Msg3/PUSCH
uplink decode failure: the gNB hears/schedules Msg3 but all PUSCH CRCs are KO.
