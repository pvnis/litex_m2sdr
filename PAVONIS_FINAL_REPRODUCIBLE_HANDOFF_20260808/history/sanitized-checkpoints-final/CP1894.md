## Interpretation

CP1894 is a valid SIB1/Msg1 and same-device sample-domain datapoint, but not a
Msg3/PUSCH datapoint. The ladder did not reach RAR/Msg3 because gNB PRACH
detection stayed empty.

The immediate frontier moves to no-RF detector-input and segment-trace
plumbing:

- why `PAVONIS_PRACH_COLLECTION_SEGMENT_TRACE=1` plus a touched arm file did
  not emit `pavonis_prach_collection_segments.tsv`;
- whether the detector is consuming the same sample windows that the append
  shows contain strong bursts;
- whether a detector-input replay can reproduce the zero-nonempty result from
  the saved append/UE PRACH templates without another RF run.

Do not [interface-redacted] Msg3/PUSCH or PRACH delay constants from CP1894. The RF path has
strong Msg1 energy; the detector-input path is the current suspect.
