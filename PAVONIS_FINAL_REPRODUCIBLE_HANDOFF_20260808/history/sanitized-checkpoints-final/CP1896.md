## Bounded Raw Capture

The bounded UL-symbol raw capture did not arm:

- `wait_rar_for_late_ul_symbol_arm rc=43`
- `ul_symbol_manifest_rows=0`
- `ul_symbol_raw_cf32=0`

This is not a detector perturbation and not a sample-domain datapoint. The
trigger watched UE stdout for RAR/Msg3 markers, but the UE RAR trace is
memory-buffered/flushed at exit; those markers are present in the final log but
were not visible to the live arm process in time. The next no-RF fix is to arm
from a [host-redacted]-local live gNB marker, such as `prach(ra-rnti=...)`,
`PAVONIS_GNB_RAR_PDU_TRACE`, or the first PUSCH schedule line, so the arm file
is touched before the first Msg3 PUSCH symbols.
