## Interpretation

CP1898 does not test the CP1897 gNB-side arm design, the Msg3 sample-domain
measurement, or the PUxCH ordering hypothesis. It voided before the trigger
condition by failing to produce an accepted PRACH on the gNB.

The useful information is that the rule-compliant instrumentation shape is
mechanically viable with append off, host gates on, bladeRF USB power management
checked, and no segment trace on the current binary. The PRACH acceptance miss
is scatter relative to CP1896, which accepted one real p0 row under the same
frontier shape.
