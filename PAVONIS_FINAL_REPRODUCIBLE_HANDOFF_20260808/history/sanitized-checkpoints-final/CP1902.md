## Interpretation

CP1902 does not test whether streaming arm restores PRACH acceptance and does
not answer the Msg3 sample-domain question. It voided before SIB1/RACH config.

The result does weaken a narrow "full-file log polling alone caused zero PRACH
accepts" theory, but it does not prove the streaming watcher is harmless because
the run never supplied PRACH. The next step is the user-requested stronger fix:
remove external gNB log watching entirely and arm from an in-process once-per-run
gNB event.
