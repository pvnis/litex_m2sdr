## Conclusion

CP1923 rejects the narrow theory that merely enabling the CP1919/CP1920
p0-only CFO-search path caused the current SIB1 collapse. With the search
disabled, the UE still reaches PBCH/SFN but SI-PDCCH remains below decode
class and no Msg1 is produced.

The current blocker is independent SI-control/SIB1 supply on the active CP1920
binary or lab path, not PRACH detector input. Do not spend more RF on PRACH
detector/CFO work until SIB1/Msg1 supply is restored. The next controlled
discriminator should be a binary/source restoration control against the
CP1909/CP1910 SIB1-pass gNB class, or an equivalent no-RF audit proving why the
active CP1920 build would affect SI before Msg1.
