## SI grant geometry

When direct SI-PDCCH is found, the geometry is the same in all compared runs:

- slot modulo 160: `1`
- SIB1 frame index: `1`
- grant: `S=2`, `L=12`, `mapping=0`
- DMRS: `type=1, typeA_pos=2, add_pos=2, len=single`

CP1909 and CP1910 decode the 80-byte BCCH TB on the first grant. CP1926 sees
the same grant class 41 times, including nine direct hits at or above `0.55`
correlation and all direct hits at or above `0.50`, but never emits
`bcch_dlsch_decoded`, `processing_sib1`, `phy_set_config_ok`, or UE Msg1.

## Interpretation

The SIB1 blocker is not explained by PBCH/channel quality. All six runs pass
cell select and SFN with overlapping RSRP/EPRE and healthy PBCH CRC. The
no-SIB1 runs have equal or better PBCH SNR than the pass runs.

The SIB1 blocker is not explained by host realtime state or bladeRF USB state.
All six summaries show performance governors/EPP on both hosts, M2SDR at
`5.0_GT/s_PCIe` x1, and bladeRF USB `5000` speed with power control `on` and
runtime status `active`. Thermal readings stay in the same `55-58 C` class.

The SIB1 blocker is not explained by the PRACH CFO-search source branch. CP1923
disabled CFO search on the CP1920 binary and still failed before SIB1/Msg1.
CP1926 restored the CP1917/pass-era `30f0...` binary/source and still failed.

The SIB1 blocker is not fixed by YAML-only de-margining. CP1928's
`fixed_sib1_mcs: 0` was active and valid, but it regressed to zero direct
SI-PDCCH hits. CP1748 had already failed the same branch with
`fixed_sib1_mcs: 0` plus `ssb_block_power_dbm: -4`.

The remaining live split has two failure classes:

- CP1921/CP1923/CP1928: low SI-control correlation and no direct SI grant.
- CP1926: direct SI grants look valid and repeated, but SI-PDSCH/BCCH TB never
  decodes.

CP1926 is now the most informative failure: it recreates pass-class SI control
and grant geometry without SIB1 TB decode. The next rung should not return to
PRACH/CFO or more MCS/power YAML controls. Stay no-RF and compare the SI-PDSCH
decode path around the first CP1909/CP1910 successful grant versus CP1926's
first few failed grants: DCI fields, selected MCS/TBS/RV, HARQ/process state,
PDSCH decode return/CRC, softbuffer/state reset, and any SIB1 TB handoff guards.
