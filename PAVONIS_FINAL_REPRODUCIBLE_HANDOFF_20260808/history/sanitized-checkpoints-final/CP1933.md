## Interpretation

CP1933 is not a reproduction of CP1926's "direct SI grant but no SIB1 TB"
failure. It is a valid recovered-SIB1 supply control proving that the new trace
can see the decisive SI-PDSCH point and that the pass path is:

`SI grant -> MAC action enabled with softbuffer -> SI-PDSCH crc=1 -> 80-byte BCCH/SIB1 -> phy_set_config_ok`.

Therefore CP1933 does not yet answer whether CP1926 failed at SI-PDSCH CRC or
after CRC OK. It does, however, provide a fresh rejected-class PRACH dataset
with valid SIB1/RACH supply and six strong predemod windows.
