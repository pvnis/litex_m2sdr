## Interpretation

CP1933 is a third replay class:

- It is not missing-energy; raw RMS is pass/rejected-control class.
- It is not a UE-generated waveform problem; CP1905 already showed the UE SC16
  PRACH waveform is essentially identical across CP1896/CP1904, and CP1933
  windows replay as p0 when recovered.
- It is not the same as CP1909: CP1909 recovers windows 0-4 under wide CFO,
  while CP1933 recovers only 4-5 under CFO-only and 3-5 under timing+CFO.
- The collapse is still coherent phase/CFO/timing at the detector input, not
  amplitude or SIB1/RACH supply.
- One static live CFO is still not justified: the accepted CFO values vary
  across runs and windows, and the first three CP1933 windows remain below
  threshold even under the current wide timing+CFO grid.
