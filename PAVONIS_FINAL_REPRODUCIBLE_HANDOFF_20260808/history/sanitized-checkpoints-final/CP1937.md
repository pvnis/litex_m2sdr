## Idle-PRACH Load Check

CP1937's idle PRACH processing time does not show a clear CFO-search overload
relative to the CP1933 pass-control:

```text
CP1933 PRACH log rows: n=1929 mean_t_us=565.748 min_t_us=484.900 max_t_us=779.000 gt1000=0
CP1937 PRACH log rows: n=1931 mean_t_us=555.148 min_t_us=474.900 max_t_us=3031.200 gt1000=1
```

The one CP1937 outlier above 1 ms should stay on the health watchlist, but the
mean is not higher than CP1933. On this evidence, the CP1937 no-SIB1 result
should be treated as SIB1-supply scatter or a supply-side regression, not as a
proved CFO-search load regression.

## Interpretation

CP1937 validates that the CP1936 restored-source minimal CFO-search binary and
wrapper can run mechanically on RF, but it is void for the detector policy:
there was no SIB1/RACH config and no UE Msg1 supply.

Do not score `PAVONIS_PRACH_DEMOD_INPUT_CFO_SEARCH` from this run, and do not
add timing-search source work from this run. The next step should preserve the
CP1936 binary state and perform a no-RF supply/load join against CP1933 before
spending another RF attempt, using summary JSON and short filtered log
aggregates only.
