## Interpretation

CP1940 rejects the narrow conclusion that the enabled live CFO-search loop
alone caused CP1937/CP1939's SIB1 supply failure. With CFO search off, the same
CP1936 binary/RF shape still fails before SIB1/RACH/Msg1 and SI EPRE/correlation
remain in the degraded CP1939 class.

The active blocker is now current SI/SIB1 supply/path drift under the CP1936
binary or current lab state. The live CFO-search policy remains untested on RF
because there has still been no valid Msg1-supply run since CP1933.
