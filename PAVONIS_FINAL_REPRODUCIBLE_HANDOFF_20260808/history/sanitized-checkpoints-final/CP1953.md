## Interpretation

CP1953 proves the bounded late-armed RAR-window trace plumbing works and that,
after each Msg1, the UE opens the expected 40-slot RA window and searches
RA-RNTI `0x39`.

It is not a UE RAR-receipt datapoint because the gNB detector did not accept a
Msg1 and did not transmit a RAR in this run. The UE's `n_dl=0` RA-PDCCH results
are therefore expected: there was no gNB RAR PDCCH to decode.

The useful next step is no-RF replay of the saved strong predemod windows
against the CP1949/CP1950 accepted-class windows, not another live retry or a
RAR/PRACH timing knob.
