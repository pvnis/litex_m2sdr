## Interpretation

CP1954 confirms the CP1911-CP1912 conclusion on the fresh CP1949/CP1950/CP1953
data: the accepted/rejected PRACH split is detector-input coherent CFO/timing,
not missing raw energy and not a UE-generated waveform problem.

This still does not justify a single static live CFO or delay knob. Best
accepting transforms are per-window, often near the `+/-25 kHz` search edge,
and CP1953 has windows that remain unrecovered or only become high-correlation
outside the delay gate.

Next source work should not [interface-redacted] live constants. The principled path is to
revisit the detector-input coherent correction/search implementation with the
fresh CP1954 evidence, while keeping SIB1/Msg1 supply and RAR-window tracing as
gates for any later RF trial.
