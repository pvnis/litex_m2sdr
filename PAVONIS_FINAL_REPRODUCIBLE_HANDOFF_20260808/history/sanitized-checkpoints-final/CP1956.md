## Interpretation

CP1956 does not answer whether CFO search helps PRACH detection. The UE never
decoded SIB1, emitted no Msg1, opened no RAR window, and saved no strong
predemod window. Therefore this is a supply/acquisition void, not a
detector-policy datapoint.

Because this is one no-SIB1/no-Msg1 void after CP1953's restored SIB1/Msg1 pass,
do not blame the CFO-search implementation yet. The next RF step is one
unchanged CP1956-shape retry. If that also voids before SIB1/Msg1, stop PRACH
policy work and split CFO-search load versus current downlink supply before
running more detector experiments.
