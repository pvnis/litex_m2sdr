## Interpretation

CP1957 is the second consecutive CP1956-shape no-SIB1/no-Msg1 void. It still
does not answer whether CFO search improves PRACH detection, because the UE
never emitted Msg1 and no strong predemod windows were captured.

The active branch must now stop detector-policy RF trials and split two
possibilities:

- CFO-search binary/runtime load or related CP1936-source state is degrading
  downlink supply before SIB1.
- The current downlink/path supply has shifted independently, and the repeated
  voids are not caused by CFO search.

Next work should be no-RF first: join CP1953/CP1956/CP1957 with the earlier
CP1937/CP1939/CP1940/CP1943 supply controls on SI correlation, timeout/late
counters, host state, and active binary/search settings. Only then choose the
minimal RF split, likely a CFO-search-off control on the active CP1936 binary
versus a restored no-search control if needed.
