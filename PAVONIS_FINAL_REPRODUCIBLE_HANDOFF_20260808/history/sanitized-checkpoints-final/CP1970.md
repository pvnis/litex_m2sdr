## Conclusion

CP1970 rules out runtime-enabled stage-1 policy and bounded stage-1 trace as
the sole cause of the SIB1 supply collapse. The active `c255...` binary still
voids before SIB1/Msg1 even with `PAVONIS_PRACH_DEMOD_INPUT_STAGE1_POLICY=0`
and `PAVONIS_PRACH_DEMOD_INPUT_STAGE1_TRACE=0`.

The remaining split is now binary/source class versus current lab downlink
supply. The next valid action is to restore or redeploy the known CP1961
no-search `30f0...` binary/source state and rerun the same supply control. If
SIB1/Msg1 returns, the `c255...` stage-1 binary/source class is the supply
regression even when disabled. If no-search also voids, the lab downlink supply
has shifted back into the low-correlation class and detector work remains
blocked.
