## Interpretation

CP1979 separates two things:

- Instrumentation: PASS. The new low-rate same-interval tracking fields are
  present, stable, and usable for future joins.
- RF/SIB1 supply: FAIL before SIB1/Msg1. SI-PDCCH correlation is far below the
  passing class, and SI EPRE is also low.

The new tracking fields do not support a large same-interval UE tracking
CFO/delay drift as the direct cause of this run's SI no-grant state. The UE
remains in sync, sync CFO is bounded around `1.0-2.9 kHz`, delay stays within
about `-2.05..+1.62 us`, and correlation against SI max-corr is near zero.

The bigger regression versus CP1972 is SI EPRE: CP1972 had mean/max EPRE
`33.439/33.719` with max_corr `0.463855`; CP1979 has mean/max EPRE
`23.666/24.130` with max_corr `0.251952`. Cell-search PBCH is still healthy, so
the active blocker is below SIB1 but now looks like an SI-era downlink
level/coherence/path state change rather than a simple UE tracking-loop CFO or
delay drift.
