Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

Compare CP1972 and CP1979 without RF to explain why CP1979 dropped into the
low-SI-EPRE/no-SIB1 class after CP1978 UE tracking instrumentation, even though
CP1979 used the frozen CP1972 supply-control shape.

## Interpretation

CP1980 does not find a gNB config, M2SDR TX readback, host realtime, bladeRF
front-end setup, or manifest/env explanation for the CP1972 -> CP1979 SI
EPRE/correlation drop.

The evidence now points at a run-to-run SI-era downlink coherence/level change
after successful acquisition, with two unresolved suspects:

1. Current RF/path/internal-clock scatter around the internal-XO coherence
   branch.
2. The CP1978 UE instrumentation binary as a low-probability but still
   unclosed A/B variable.

The downlink-late count is lower in the worse CP1979 run (`117` versus `857`),
so total downlink-late volume is not a simple monotonic explanation.
