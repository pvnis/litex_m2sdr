## Slot-Phase Evidence

The strongest discriminator is the slot phase of the EPRE maximum.

- CP1972: `max_epre_slot % 160 == 1` on `796/800` rows.
- CP1979: the EPRE maximum is phase-scattered, with top modulo-160 counts:
  `113:100`, `33:94`, `143:87`, `133:80`, `103:78`, `73:73`, `63:67`,
  `153:59`, `13:51`, `53:45`.

So CP1972's high SI energy is locked to a stable expected occasion class,
whereas CP1979 does not show the same phase-locked SI energy. That argues
against a pure printf/field-format artifact and points to a real
PDCCH-measurement/coherence difference at SI time.

## Source Audit

In `cc_worker.cc`, the SI EPRE and correlation are computed first from:

- `ue_dl.pdcch_info[k].measure.epre_dBfs`
- `ue_dl.pdcch_info[k].measure.norm_corr`

Then the summary helper receives `phy.get_sync_tracking()`.

The getter in `state.h` is a mutex-protected copy of `sync_tracking`. The writer
in `worker_pool.cc` stores the latest `srsran_ue_sync_nr_zerocopy()` outcome via
`phy_state.set_sync_tracking(...)`.

Conclusion from source:

- The CP1978 getter cannot change the already-computed `pdcch_info` EPRE/corr
  values for the current call.
- It does, however, add one mutex-protected metadata read on the SI-search worker
  path. Therefore CP1978 is unlikely to be a field-format bug, but it is not
  fully cleared as a timing/observer perturbation without the prepared
  pre-CP1978 RF A/B.

No `PAVONIS_SRSUE_SI_CANDIDATE_TRACE` or direct `event=si_pdcch` rows were
present in either stdout log, so this checkpoint cannot inspect per-candidate
EPRE/corr distributions.

## gNB-Side Spot Check

The gNB binary-safe mirrors confirm both runs use the same all-timed CS16 shape:
`all_timed_chunks=true`, `write_timeout_us=1000`, `force_continuous_stream=false`,
`tx_format=CS16`, and no worker/release late or underflow counts in the parsed
early summary rows.

The parsed early Soapy deltas are similar:

- CP1972: timeout delta mean `40.0`, write-deficit delta mean `42131`, partial
  return delta mean `2.5`.
- CP1979: timeout delta mean `39.714`, write-deficit delta mean `42333`, partial
  return delta mean `2.571`.

This does not explain the CP1979 SI EPRE collapse. It is also only a spot check
because the binary-safe mirror interleaves later logs heavily; CP1980's full
summary JSON remains the authoritative run-level counter source.

## Conclusion

CP1983 narrows CP1979's low SI EPRE from "maybe a summary/reporting artifact" to
"a real low/phase-unlocked SI-PDCCH measurement class in the UE PDCCH search
path." The CP1978 tracking fields are read-only relative to the already-computed
measurement values, but their worker-path mutex read remains a plausible
observer/timing perturbation until the RF A/B is run.
