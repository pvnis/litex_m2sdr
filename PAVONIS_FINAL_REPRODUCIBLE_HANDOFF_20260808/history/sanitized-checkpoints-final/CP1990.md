Reusable discriminator:

- high/phase-locked: `max_epre_slot % 160 == 1` on about `792-796/800` rows.
- low/scattered: zero high-EPRE rows and mostly `max_epre_slot % 10 == 3`,
  with `max_epre_slot % 20 == 13` as the dominant family.
- early-pass: SIB1/Msg1 arrives before aggregate summary rows are emitted, or
  only a single direct SI row is visible.

## Interpretation

CP1990 rules out the idea that low/scattered SI summaries are arbitrary noise or
a malformed summary row. The low class is phase-unlocked relative to the normal
SI energy bucket, but it is still structured around a repeatable odd-slot
search lattice. That points at a systematic timing/reference/occasion mismatch
or coherence loss at SI search time, not at a random parser artifact and not at
simple TX storm placement.

The dominant low family is about two slots later modulo 10 than the high family
(`1` versus `3`) and usually lands in the modulo-20 `13` family. CP1990 does not
yet prove whether that offset is produced by UE SI-search occasion selection,
gNB SI/PDCCH scheduling phase, or RF/coherence loss that makes a neighboring
search lattice look strongest.
