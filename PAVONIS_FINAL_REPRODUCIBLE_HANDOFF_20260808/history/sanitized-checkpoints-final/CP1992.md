# CP1992 No-RF NR SI-RNTI Search-Gate Audit

Date: [date-redacted]

## Scope

Follow CP1991's slot-provenance result by auditing whether the low/scattered
SI family is caused by a different UE-side SI scheduling gate/window, or by the
same UE blindly searching SI-RNTI across many slots and reporting the strongest
candidate measurement.

No RF runs, rebuilds, deploys, source edits, or gateware changes were
performed.

The NR SIB1 acquisition chain is:

1. After cell selection completes, RRC logs "Starting SIB1 acquisition" and
   calls `rrc_handle.mac->bcch_search(true)`.
2. `mac_nr::bcch_search(bool enabled)` only sets the boolean `search_bcch`.
3. `mac_nr::get_dl_sched_rnti_nr(tti)` returns `{SRSRAN_SIRNTI,
   srsran_rnti_type_si}` whenever `is_si_opportunity()` is true.
4. `mac_nr::is_si_opportunity()` returns `search_bcch` directly. There is no
   NR SI-window, Type0 occasion, or `tti` modulo filter in this path.
5. `cc_worker::decode_pdcch_dl()` calls `get_dl_sched_rnti_nr(dl_slot_cfg.idx)`
   and runs SI-RNTI PDCCH search whenever the returned RNTI is valid.
6. `make_phy_search_space0_cfg()` configures SearchSpace0 as common-0,
   CORESET0, DCI 1_0, `duration=1`, candidates `AL4=4` and `AL8=2`, for six
   candidates total.
7. `srsran_ue_dl_nr_find_dl_dci()` iterates all present search spaces whenever
   called. For SearchSpace0, `ue_dl_nr_find_dci_ss()` measures all configured
   candidates.
8. `srsran_pdcch_nr_locations_coreset()` computes candidate NCCE locations from
   the CORESET/search-space candidate counts. It does not reject a slot based
   on Type0 periodicity. For common search spaces, `Y_p_n` remains zero; slot
   index affects UE-specific search-space randomization only.

## Log Sanity Check

I parsed only the exact saved CP1972 and CP1979 UE stdout logs and only
`event=si_pdcch_summary` rows.

```text
CP1972_high:
  rows=800, samples=200 on 800/800, interval=200 on 800/800, candidates=6 on 800/800
  max_epre>=30: 796/800
  max_corr>=0.4: 13/800
  top max_epre_slot % 160: 1 on 796/800

CP1979_low:
  rows=800, samples=200 on 800/800, interval=200 on 800/800, candidates=6 on 800/800
  max_epre>=30: 0/800
  max_corr>=0.4: 0/800
  top max_epre_slot % 160: 113/33/143/133/103/73/63/153
  top max_epre_slot % 20: 13 on 532/800, 3 on 259/800
  top max_epre_slot % 10: 3 on 791/800
```

This confirms the high and low families are being generated by the same summary
cadence and the same six-candidate SearchSpace0 measurement set. The low class
is not produced by fewer searched candidates, a different summary interval, or
a sparse Type0-only MAC search window.

## Interpretation

CP1991 showed the actual gNB scheduled SIB1 phase is `slot % 160 == 1`.
CP1992 adds that the UE's NR SIB1-acquisition code does not restrict SI-RNTI
blind search to that scheduled phase. Once `bcch_search(true)` is active, MAC
asks PHY to search SI-RNTI in every TTI until SIB1 is acquired, and PHY measures
the same six SearchSpace0 candidates in each searched slot.

Therefore:

- High/phase-locked rows mean the scheduled SIB1/Type0 phase is energetically
  dominant in the continuous blind-search stream.
- Low/scattered rows mean scheduled-phase energy is not dominant enough, so the
  summary reports whichever weak/rejected CORESET0 candidate has the largest
  EPRE in that 200-slot interval.
- The low-family slot buckets are not an RRC/MAC scheduling bug and not proof
  that the gNB moved SIB1.

## Closed / Still Open

Closed by CP1992:

- The low/scattered family is not caused by MAC selecting a different SI window
  or Type0 occasion.
- The same six-candidate SearchSpace0 measurement path is active in CP1972 and
  CP1979.
- The SI summary stream is continuous during SIB1 acquisition because
  `search_bcch` gates all TTIs, not only scheduled SIB1 slots.

Still open:

- Why the scheduled `slot % 160 == 1` SIB1 phase sometimes dominates
  candidate EPRE and sometimes collapses below unrelated weak CORESET0
  candidates.
- Whether the next approved RF A/B with the CP1981-prepared pre-CP1978 UE
  binary recovers high/phase-locked supply, early-pass supply, or remains
  low/scattered.
