## Interpretation

CP1993 closes the "wrong SIB1 resource geometry" branch. The low/scattered
family is not caused by a different generated gNB YAML, a different Band 3
carrier/SSB placement, a different SIB1 slot phase, a different SIB1 PDSCH
grant, or a different UE CORESET0 measurement geometry.

CP1972 proves that this exact geometry can produce a strong scheduled-phase
PDCCH metric: `max_epre_slot % 160 == 1` on `796/800` rows. CP1979 proves that
the same scheduled geometry can collapse below unrelated weak CORESET0
candidates: `0/800` rows above `max_epre >= 30` and no rows above
`max_corr >= 0.4`.

The active blocker remains supply/coherence on the scheduled CORESET0/SIB1
phase, not scheduling/resource provenance.

## Closed / Still Open

Closed by CP1993:

- CP1972 and CP1979 use byte-identical gNB YAMLs for the tested cell.
- The gNB schedules SIB1 in both runs at the same phase:
  `SFN % 16 == 0`, slot `1`, absolute `slot % 160 == 1`.
- The gNB schedules the same SIB1 PDSCH grant in both runs:
  `rb=[0..8)`, `tbs=80`.
- The UE summaries measure the same CORESET0/candidate geometry in both runs:
  `duration=2`, `start_rb=0`, six candidates, 200-sample intervals.
- The low/scattered CP1979 family is not a SIB1 resource/frequency/slot
  geometry mismatch.

Still open:

- Why the scheduled CORESET0/SIB1 phase sometimes dominates the UE metric
  strongly and sometimes falls below unrelated weak candidates under the same

  high/phase-locked, or low/scattered SI class.
