# CP1994 No-RF SS Power Offset SIB1 Mapping Audit

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Scope

Continue the CP1979 SI-EPRE provenance comparison without RF, rebuilds,
deploys, source edits, or gateware changes. This checkpoint checks whether
`GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB=6` / `PAVONIS_GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB=6`
is merely a stale wrapper setting, whether it maps only to CSI-RS/SSB, or
whether it actually reaches the common-channel SI-PDCCH/SIB1 PDSCH transmit
power path.

This is also a correction to the CP1993 "next" question: the knob is not
absent from the current `ocudu` source tree.

## Source Mapping

Local source audit:

- `ocudu/lib/scheduler/config/csi_helper.cpp:18-42` reads
  `PAVONIS_GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB`, accepts `-3/0/3/6`, and prints
  `PAVONIS_GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB active value=...` on activation.
- `ocudu/lib/scheduler/config/csi_helper.cpp:318-327` stores that override in
  the common NZP CSI-RS resource as `pwr_ctrl_offset_ss_db`.
- `ocudu/lib/scheduler/pdcch_scheduling/pdcch_resource_allocator_impl.cpp:149-153`
  and `:197-201` copy `pwr_ctrl_offset_ss_db` into UL and DL PDCCH TX-power
  context.
- `ocudu/lib/scheduler/support/sch_pdu_builder.cpp:211-218` copies the same
  value into the SI-RNTI / DCI 1_0 common-search PDSCH power information.
- `ocudu/lib/fapi_adaptor/phy/p7/pdu_translators/pdcch.cpp:28-30` applies the
  PDCCH power profile to DCI DMRS/data power offsets.
- `ocudu/lib/fapi_adaptor/phy/p7/pdu_translators/pdsch.cpp:40-62` maps the
  FAPI `dB6` profile to `+6 dB` and applies it to
  `ratio_pdsch_data_to_sss_dB`.

The CP1753 branch-map statement is therefore still consistent with source:
this env path is the common-channel power lever for PDCCH/PDSCH, not just a
YAML label or an unused CSI-RS field.

## Interpretation

CP1994 closes the "SS power override did not cover SI-PDCCH/SIB1 PDSCH" branch
for the local/current `ocudu` source and for the saved CP1972/CP1979 runs.
The `+6 dB` common-channel power path is source-present and runtime-active in
both compared runs.

Therefore the CP1972-vs-CP1979 split is not explained by the power knob being
absent, unexported, or applied in only one run:

- CP1972: same knob active, high/phase-locked SI EPRE
  (`796/800 max_epre>=30`, `max_epre_slot % 160 == 1` on `796/800` rows), but
  still no SIB1.
- CP1979: same knob active, low/scattered SI EPRE
  (`0/800 max_epre>=30`, scattered top buckets), no SIB1.

The active blocker remains same-geometry scheduled CORESET0/SIB1
supply/coherence collapse under a common-channel `+6 dB` fixture, not a
missing or one-sided SI power override.

## Closed / Still Open

Closed by CP1994:

- The `PAVONIS_GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB` hook exists in `ocudu`.
- The hook feeds common NZP CSI-RS `pwr_ctrl_offset_ss_db`, which is then
  propagated into PDCCH and SI/common-search PDSCH power paths.
- CP1972 and CP1979 both show runtime activation of `active value=6`.
- The CP1972 high/phase-locked versus CP1979 low/scattered split is not caused
  by this knob being absent or applied only in CP1972.

Still open:

- Why the same scheduled SIB1 geometry and same active `+6 dB` common-channel
  power fixture can produce high/phase-locked SI energy in CP1972 and
  low/scattered SI energy in CP1979.
- Whether the CP1981-staged pre-CP1978 UE binary A/B returns early-pass,
  high/phase-locked, or low/scattered behavior.
- If the A/B remains low/scattered, whether an independent bladeRF DL IQ
  capture aligned to scheduled `slot % 160 == 1` shows the scheduled-phase
  energy in the air or not.
