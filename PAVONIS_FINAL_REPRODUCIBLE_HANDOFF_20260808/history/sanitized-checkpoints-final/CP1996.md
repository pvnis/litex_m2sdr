## Interpretation

CP1996 narrows the active blocker from "scheduled SI energy missing or wrong
phase" to "scheduled-phase SI-PDCCH decodability/correlation margin."

The high/no-SIB1 class is not low-power at the UE metric, not an alternate SI
phase, not a wrong CORESET0 resource, and not a missing common-channel `+6 dB`
power fixture. It is a repeatable state where scheduled-phase EPRE is high
around `33.3 dB`, but the best SI-PDCCH correlation stays below the direct
decode class:

- Early-pass direct SI decode: `max_corr ~= 0.623..0.668`.
- High/no-SIB1 best scheduled rows: `max_corr ~= 0.458..0.464`.

This is consistent with a coherence/EVM/candidate-quality problem at the
scheduled SI-RNTI PDCCH, not a bulk link-budget or scheduling-provenance
problem.

## Closed / Still Open

Closed by CP1996:

- High/no-SIB1 versus early-pass is not separated by scheduled-phase EPRE.
- It is not separated by cell-search SNR/CFO/RSRP.
- It is not separated by TX attenuation/readback, USB presence, or host
  realtime gate state.
- Downlink-late totals remain a co-suspect for runtime quality but are not a
  stable direct splitter at this level.

Still open:

- Which lower-level SI-PDCCH variable drives `max_corr` from the direct-decode
  class (`>=0.62`) down to the high/no-decode class (`<=0.464`) while EPRE and
  scheduled phase stay normal.
- Whether existing traces can expose candidate-level DCI decode failure reason,
  CCE/candidate index, aggregation-level behavior, DMRS correlation components,
  or PDCCH equalizer/EVM without hot-path sample dumping.
