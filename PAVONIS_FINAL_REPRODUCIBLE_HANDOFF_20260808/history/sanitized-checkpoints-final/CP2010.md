## Interpretation

CP2010 is a valid low/scattered supply datapoint, not the target
high/phase-locked candidate-trace case. It therefore does not answer the
CP2001/CP2004 candidate questions: `sync_error_us`, fixed `cfo_hz`, and
candidate `(L,ncce)` identity are unavailable because no candidate rows were
emitted.

Per the user-approved decision tree, this low/scattered outcome retriggers the
CP1945-style independent bladeRF DL IQ scheduled-phase discriminator.

The candidate trace was per-process for this diagnostic only. Future RF runs
should leave `PAVONIS_SRSUE_SI_CANDIDATE_TRACE=0` unless explicitly re-enabled.
