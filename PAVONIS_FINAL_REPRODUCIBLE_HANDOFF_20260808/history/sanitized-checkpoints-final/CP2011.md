## Packaging Caveat

The CP1945 stop/package shim failed on the [host-redacted] TX tar:

```text
stop_package_nuc4_live_digdump rc=255
scp_get_nuc4_tx_tar rc=1
overall_rc=1
```

The stop log shows the known CP1945-family issue where process matching includes
the stop shim itself while trying to interrupt/terminate the detached harness.
Consequently same-window [host-redacted] TX dump/timed-DMA local analyses were skipped.

This does not invalidate the independent bladeRF IQ capture: the RF raw file,
metadata tar, capture health, PBCH one-shot, aligned sweep, and reference
correlation all completed successfully. Cleanup ran after the nonzero exit and
final process checks were clean.

## Interpretation

CP2011 proves that immediately after CP2010's low/scattered UE run, the restored
live gNB/M2SDR downlink can still put a decodable PCI1 PBCH/SSB waveform into
the independent bladeRF RF path at strong quality. Therefore CP2010's
low/scattered SI summaries should not be interpreted as "scheduled-phase energy
missing from the air" or as a dead gNB/M2SDR/bladeRF RF path.

The remaining frontier is the UE/srsUE scheduled SI-PDCCH measurement/decoding
path or run-window/coherence interaction: CP2010's srsUE process measured weak,
alternate-phase SI candidates, while CP2011's independent bladeRF IQ capture
decoded the downlink cleanly without srsUE running.

Do not rerun CP2011 unchanged just to recover the missing TX tar. If TX-side
same-window dumps are required later, fix or bypass the CP1945 stop/package shim
first so it cannot match and kill itself.
