## Narrow Timing Sweep

To rule out residual slot-cut error, I cut fresh CF32 snippets directly from the saved SC16 captures. The conversion was first verified byte-identical to an existing CP2016 snippet using `int16 / [date-redacted]`.

Sweep geometry:

- CP2016 best live window: SFN `672`, base `1054981`, slot `6721`
- CP2017/CP1945 best live window: SFN `1024`, base `4167748`, slot `1`
- Offsets: `-1704..-1624` samples, step `4`

Results:

- CP2016: `21` offsets, `13` DCI hits, `0` PDSCH CRC OK, median PDSCH-minus-PDCCH EPRE `-9.47 dB`
- CP2017/CP1945: `21` offsets, `9` DCI hits, `0` PDSCH CRC OK, median PDSCH-minus-PDCCH EPRE `-9.42 dB`

The finer timing sweep does not recover PDSCH or remove the PDSCH-DMRS deficit. This closes residual coarse timing as the primary explanation for the current PDSCH failure.

## Interpretation

The DCI and grant semantics are not the visible splitter:

- Live CP2016 and CP2017 decode the same SI-RNTI DCI fields as the synthetic-good control.
- The same `cinit`, PRB range, symbol range, MCS, and TBS decode successfully when generated synthetically.
- The `-X` harness has PDCCH/PDSCH EPRE parity and CRC OK on both synthetic controls.

The live-IQ splitter is the physical/channel presentation of the SI PDSCH:

- Live PDCCH is strong: EPRE near `33 dB`, corr up to `0.747`.
- Live PDSCH-DMRS is consistently about `9.3..9.5 dB` below live PDCCH.
- Live PDSCH SNR is only about `3..5 dB` median and CRC-KO even when DCI is CRC-OK.

This reframes the next rung away from harness validation, DCI packing, and slot-boundary [interface-redacted]. The next no-RF audit should inspect why the live gNB/M2SDR path presents SI PDSCH about `9.5 dB` below SI PDCCH despite CP1994's source-level claim that `GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB=6` feeds both common PDCCH and common PDSCH. Start with source/runtime mapping of SI PDSCH power and any per-channel scaling between PDCCH DMRS and PDSCH DMRS; only return to RF after a specific source or runtime hypothesis is ready to test.
