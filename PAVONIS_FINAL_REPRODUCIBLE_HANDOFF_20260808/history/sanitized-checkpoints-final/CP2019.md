## Predicted Deficit

For the Pavonis fixture `GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB=6`, `power_control_offset_profile_nr=0`, and SI PDSCH `num_dmrs_cdm_grps_no_data=2`:

- PDCCH DMRS amplitude: `+6 dB`
- PDSCH data ratio: `+6 dB`
- PDSCH DMRS ratio: `+6 + (-3) = +3 dB`
- Final PDSCH DMRS amplitude: `-3 dB`
- Predicted PDSCH-DMRS minus PDCCH-DMRS: `-3 - (+6) = -9 dB`

CP2018 observed:

- CP2016 live median PDSCH-minus-PDCCH EPRE: `-9.46 dB`
- CP2017/CP1945 live median PDSCH-minus-PDCCH EPRE: `-9.31 dB`
- CP2016 narrow timing median: `-9.47 dB`
- CP2017/CP1945 narrow timing median: `-9.42 dB`

The source path predicts the measured RF-file symptom to within about `0.5 dB`.

## Interpretation

CP1994 was correct only through scheduler/FAPI population: the `+6 dB` value is carried into both common PDCCH and common PDSCH structures. The final PHY semantics are not equivalent:

- PDCCH treats the value as a positive amplitude offset.
- PDSCH treats the same value as a ratio and then negates it when generating data and DMRS.
- The PDSCH DMRS beta term makes the final SI PDSCH-DMRS deficit approximately `9 dB`, matching CP2018.

This is now the leading explanation for the live SI PDSCH CRC wall. It also explains why PDCCH can be CRC-OK while PDSCH fails with low SNR under the same decoded grant.
