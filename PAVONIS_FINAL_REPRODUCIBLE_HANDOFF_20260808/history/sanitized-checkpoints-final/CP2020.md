## Timing Provenance

Independent derivation:

- Sample rate `11.52 Msps`
- SCS `15 kHz`, so `Nfft = 768`
- Normal CP at this FFT size: `CP0 = 60`, `CPn = 54`
- PBCH `t_offset` is SSB symbol-2 origin, not a slot boundary
- Slot start is therefore two OFDM symbols before PBCH symbol 2:
  `(60 + 768) + (54 + 768) = 1650` samples

The old `-1728` point is a coarse sweep-grid/cut-arithmetic landmark from treating PBCH `t_offset` as if it were a slot origin. Fine hits at `-1696/-1664/-1632/-1600` in CP2016 and `-1696/-1664` in CP2017/CP2020 sit around the independent `-1650` slot-start correction. It is not an air-side SI displacement.
