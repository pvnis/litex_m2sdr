## Scope

CP2019 identified a `~ -9 dB` SI PDSCH-DMRS deficit caused by divergent PDCCH/PDSCH power-sign semantics. CP2020 revalidated the `-X` controls, so this checkpoint locally build-smokes the scoped default-off OCUDU override that was already present in the workspace.
