## Interpretation

CP2023 did not reach the state CP2018 was meant to fix.

The CP2018 problem requires a live window with SI PDCCH already strong and
decoded: PDCCH EPRE around `33 dB`, PDCCH corr above the `0.5` gate, DCI CRC OK,
and then PDSCH-DMRS around `23-24 dB`. CP2023's SI-PDCCH summaries are instead
already at `23-24 dB` and below the correlation gate before any DCI/PDSCH grant.

The `PAVONIS_GNB_SI_PDSCH_POWER_SIGN_COMPAT` override affects SI-RNTI PDSCH
power mapping. It is not expected to raise SI PDCCH EPRE or turn a low/scattered
pre-grant run into a DCI-OK run. Therefore CP2023 validates runtime activation
but neither proves nor disproves the PDSCH power-sign fix.
