## Scope

CP2026 prepares the next bounded RF diagnostic without starting radios. The goal is
to test the CP2021/CP2022/CP2025 SI PDSCH power-sign override in the DCI-OK
scheduled-IQ class, instead of depending on UE SIB1 luck as in CP2023.

- `cp2026_run_si_pdsch_power_sign_independent_dl_iq_capture.sh`

capture harness, but uses a fresh dynamic default stamp:

`$(date -u +%Y%m%dT%H%M%SZ)CP2026_SI_PDSCH_POWER_SIGN_INDEPENDENT_DL_IQ`
