  resulting PDSCH config, sets the softbuffer/payload, and calls
  `srsran_ue_dl_nr_decode_pdsch(...)`.

  an absent grant.

## Timing And CFO Note

Short provenance note:

- True slot boundary correction from PBCH timing is about `-1650` samples:
  PBCH `t_offset` is SSB symbol-2 origin, and at 15 kHz with `Nfft=768`,
  `CP0=60`, `CPn=54`, the slot-start correction is
  `(60 + 768) + (54 + 768) = 1650` samples.
- CP2016 DCI hits cluster at `-1696/-1664/-1632/-1600`; the practical boundary
  is therefore `-1664 +- 32` samples, consistent with the independent
  derivation.
- `-1728` was a coarse cut-arithmetic landmark from the earlier sweep grid, not
  a genuine air-side displacement.
- CFO reconciliation remains unchanged: UE cell-search was around `-3.7 kHz`,
  CP2011 file-test PBCH around `-3.0 kHz`, and CP2012 residual
  `-1.25..-1.75 kHz` came from a broad CRC-KO coarse-cut context. Do not [interface-redacted] a
  static downlink CFO knob from CP2012 residual CFO.
