CP2040 rules out the predicted `O(100)`-sample SI timing displacement and does
not yet reach a semantic DCI/CRC question. The immediate blocker is PDCCH DMRS
correlation/coherence below the live `0.5` decode gate despite high EPRE and

whether the live PDCCH estimator is using a CFO/timing correction different
from the validated offline path. Do not lower the live decode threshold or run
