# Checkpoint [date-redacted]: No-RF CP2040 CFO-vs-Correlation Model

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

Test the proposed two-symbol coherent-average model on existing CP2040 data:
whether candidate PDCCH DMRS correlation follows
`corr ~= corr_max * cos^2(pi * cfo_hz * T_sym)`. If true, the CP2040
correlation deficit would be explained directly by inter-symbol phase rotation.

## Results

Parsed `1200` candidate entries from `200` CP2040 SI candidate-trace events.
The live gates remain `corr_thr=0.5` and `epre_thr=-80.0`; decode attempts and
CRC-OK attempts are both zero.

All-entry distribution:

- `corr` median/mean/max: `0.1289855 / 0.152387735 / 0.439256`
- `abs(cfo_hz)` median/mean/max: `4680.2605 / 4360.5274 / 6948.575`
- Pearson `abs(cfo_hz)` vs `corr`: `+0.30396`
- corr counts: `>=0.40: 6`, `>=0.45: 0`, `>=0.50: 0`

Cosine-squared model fits:

- `T_sym=66.667 us`: fitted `corr_max=0.19744`, `R2=-1.11093`, `RMSE=0.16071`
- `T_sym=71.429 us`: fitted `corr_max=0.18890`, `R2=-1.25293`, `RMSE=0.16603`
- free grid `T_sym=0.1..120 us`: best `T_sym=0.1 us`,
  `corr_max=0.15239`, `R2 ~= -0.000001`, `RMSE=0.11061`

Subgroup controls do not rescue the model:

- Deduplicated `600` rows: same negative fixed-`T_sym` fits.
- Best-per-event `200` rows: `T_sym=66.667 us` gives `R2=-13.03119`;
  `T_sym=71.429 us` gives `R2=-15.44945`.
- Dominant identity `L=2,ncce=0,format=2,bits=37` (`400` rows):
  `T_sym=66.667 us` gives `R2=-10.77897`; `T_sym=71.429 us` gives
  `R2=-12.70180`.

## Interpretation

The simple two-symbol phase-rotation model does not fit CP2040. The observed
trend is the opposite of the proposed pure rotation loss: correlation weakly
increases with `abs(cfo_hz)` in this trace, and the best fixed-symbol model is
worse than a constant mean predictor. The free-`T_sym` fit collapses to a
near-zero `T_sym`, which is a flat model, not an OFDM-symbol phase-rotation
explanation.

Therefore the CP2040 correlation deficit is not explained by
`corr_max * cos^2(pi * cfo_hz * T_sym)` using the printed candidate CFO values.
This does not disprove a phase-coherence defect in the air or TX chain; it says
the current live candidate `cfo_hz` field is not sufficient by itself to explain
the SI-PDCCH correlation deficit.
