## Updated Recommendation

CP1982 said **no-go for immediate migration** because SI/SIB1 supply was still
unstable and a migration would mix a major timing-stack replacement into an
unresolved blocker.

CP2044 changes the diagnosis enough to update the decision:

- **Go** for a separately approved no-RF coherent-cadence fix/migration branch
  plan.
- **No-go** for in-place migration of the current Pavonis branch.
- **No-go** for RF validation, deploy, or gateware changes without explicit
  approval.
- **No-go** for live threshold relaxation or a live UE PDSCH phase-ramp
  workaround as the fix.

The reason is specific: the new blocker is no longer just "unstable SI supply";
existing DCI-OK air captures can be made to decode SI-PDSCH by correcting
symbol-phase coherence. That points at the TX/timing/coherence architecture,
which is exactly what the LiteX-M2SDR upstream timed-TX migration or a smaller
local coherent-cadence source branch would address.
