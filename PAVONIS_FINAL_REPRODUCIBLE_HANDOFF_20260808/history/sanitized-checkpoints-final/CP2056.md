## Verdict

PASS, no-RF.

The CP2054 env-filter failure mode is closed at the wrapper/start-shim level:
the next explicitly approved CP2031/CP2035 RF diagnostic should launch srsUE
with both SI candidate trace and applied-CFO provenance trace enabled.

This does not answer the applied-CFO hypothesis itself. The single approved RF

requires explicit user approval. The non-void CP2054 ladder result remains
valid: current supply can reach SIB1/PDSCH CRC OK, real UE Msg1, and gNB RAR
encoding, but the UE remains RAR-blind/no-Msg3.
