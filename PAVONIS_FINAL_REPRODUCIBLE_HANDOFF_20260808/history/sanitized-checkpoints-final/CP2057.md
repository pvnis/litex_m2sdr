## Verdict

PASS, no-RF join.

The CP2054 RAR failure is now more specific than "RAR absent":

- The UE opens RAR windows and monitors RA-RNTI `0x39`.
- The gNB transmits two RAR PDCCH/PDSCH grants on the same RA-RNTI.
- At the first RAR slot, the UE sees a strong RA-PDCCH-like signal at the
  correct slot/RNTI (`max_corr=0.721`, `max_epre=30.178`) but still returns
  `n_dl=0`.
- At the second RAR slot, the UE sees only floor-class RA-PDCCH metrics.

This points the next no-RF/source audit at RA-RNTI DCI decode semantics and
RAR common-search geometry/power/coherence, not at PRACH supply or RAR-window
timing. In particular, check why a strong first RAR PDCCH candidate at
`ss_id=1`, `format=1_0`, `cce=0`, `al=4`, `rnti=0x39` does not become a UE
`n_dl>0`/RA DCI, and keep the gNB downlink timeout storm as a co-suspect for
the second RAR's floor-class metrics.

The applied-CFO repeat remains separately pending explicit RF approval after
the CP2056 propagation gate.
