single-RF datapoints without running RF. The goal is to isolate whether CP2060

## Verdict

CP2061 narrows the CP2054 -> CP2060 regression to gNB PRACH detector
coherence/correlation, not UE SIB1/Msg1 supply and not raw PRACH-window
amplitude.

Key conclusion:

- CP2054 and CP2060 both have `7` UE PRACH transmissions and comparable
  strong predemod raw levels.
- CP2054 has two high-ratio detector rows (`p0_ratio=2.082` and `3.584`) and
  sends two RARs.
- CP2060's strongest p0 detector ratio is only `0.365`, so no RAR is sent.

Next no-RF discriminator should replay the saved CP2054 and CP2060 strong
predemod windows through the same offline PRACH detector with the already used
CFO/fractional timing/phase sweep controls. If CP2060 recovers only under a
different CFO/phase/timing correction while CP2054 does not need it, the
remaining blocker is run-scale UL detector-input coherence. Do not [interface-redacted] PRACH
thresholds, RAR payloads, or live delay constants from CP2060.
