## Results

| Mode | CP2054 accepted | CP2060 accepted | CP2054 max p0 | CP2060 max p0 |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| baseline | `2/7` | `0/6` | `3.583` | `0.365` |
| direct | `2/7` | `0/6` | `52.814` | `0.431` |
| stage1_policy | `5/7` | `5/6` | `16.781` | `7.405` |

Baseline reproduces the live split exactly:

- CP2054 accepted idx `1` and `2`, matching the live accepted windows.
- CP2060 accepted `0/6`, matching the live no-RAR run.

The small direct sweep does not rescue CP2060:

- CP2054's already-accepted windows become much stronger under CFO-only
  transforms:
  - idx `1`: CFO `-2750 Hz`, `p0_ratio=16.855`, delay `28`
  - idx `2`: CFO `+2250 Hz`, `p0_ratio=52.814`, delay `100`
- CP2060 remains `0/6`, max `p0_ratio=0.431`, so phase-only, small CFO,
  integer-only timing, and fractional-only timing are not sufficient.

The CP1965 stage-1 timing+CFO grid recovers CP2060:

- CP2060 idx `0`: shift `-256`, frac `-0.5`, CFO `+21000 Hz`,
  `p0_ratio=4.379`, delay `13`
- CP2060 idx `1`: shift `-224`, frac `+0.5`, CFO `+24000 Hz`,
  `p0_ratio=3.688`, delay `2`
- CP2060 idx `2`: shift `-96`, frac `+0.5`, CFO `+26000 Hz`,
  `p0_ratio=1.148`, delay `96`
- CP2060 idx `3`: shift `-192`, frac `-0.5`, CFO `+32000 Hz`,
  `p0_ratio=4.826`, delay `0`
- CP2060 idx `5`: shift `-352`, frac `+0.5`, CFO `+37000 Hz`,
  `p0_ratio=7.405`, delay `1`

The only unrecovered CP2060 window is idx `4`; its best stage-1 row remains
below threshold with `p0_ratio=0.315` and delay `130`.

CP2054 also improves under the same stage-1 grid:

- Baseline accepted `2/7`.
- Stage-1 accepts `5/7`.
- Newly recovered CP2054 windows use CFO `+20..+27 kHz` and negative shifts.

## Interpretation

CP2062 confirms CP2061's diagnosis:

- CP2060 was not missing PRACH energy.
- CP2060's live failure is detector-input coherent timing/CFO/correlation.
- The same compact correction family that recovered CP1961 also recovers
  CP2060's saved windows.
- The small direct/phase sweep not recovering CP2060 matters: the needed
  correction is not a static phase-only or small-CFO perturbation; it is the
  larger detector-input timing+CFO class.

This does not approve live stage-1 enablement. Earlier CP1967/CP1968 showed
the old stage-1 source/binary branch could perturb SIB1/Msg1 supply before the
detector question was reached. The next step should stay no-RF and audit how
to port or reintroduce the CP1965 policy into the current gNB source branch
without the CP1967/CP1968 supply regression, with default-off gating and build
proof before any future RF.
