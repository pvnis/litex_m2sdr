## Verdict

CP2065 is a low/scattered scheduled-SI supply collapse after successful PBCH/cell selection. It is not evidence that stage-1 runtime cost broke the run, and it is not evidence that applied-CFO propagation is missing.

The important new fact is narrower: scheduled-SI EPRE can drop about `9 dB` relative to good runs while PBCH/cell-search EPRE remains normal. That keeps the downlink scheduled-phase supply/coherence problem active and explains why CP2065 never reached the PRACH/RAR questions.
