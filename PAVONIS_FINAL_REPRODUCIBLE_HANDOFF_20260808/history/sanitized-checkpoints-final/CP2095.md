## RAR Result

RAR was not transmitted. This run therefore does not answer the CP2057 RAR
question yet.

The failure is currently explained by stage-1 search latency. The accepted
PRACH event reached the scheduler at slot `491.0`, after the gNB response
window for PRACH slot `484.4` had expired:

```text
[491.0] ra-rnti=0x39: Could not transmit RAR within the window=[484.5..488.5), prach_slot=484.4, slot_tx=491.0
```

The window notation is in `slot_point` frames/slots, so `[484.5..488.5)` is the
configured `40`-slot RA response window, not a four-slot window. The UE trace
also shows `ra_window_length=40` and `n_dl=0` RA-PDCCH opportunities. This is a
late PRACH-result delivery problem, not a RAR decode, RAPID, or geometry result.
