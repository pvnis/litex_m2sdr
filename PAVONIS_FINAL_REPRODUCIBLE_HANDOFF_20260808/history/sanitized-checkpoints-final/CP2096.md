- idx `2`: best accept `shift=-96`, `frac=+0.5`, `CFO=25000 Hz`,
  p0 ratio `1.032`, delay `101`.

`wide_combo` replay recovers three windows, but the best accepting points span
positive shifts and lower CFOs:

- idx `1`: `shift=+32`, `frac=0`, `CFO=18500 Hz`, p0 ratio `4.966`.
- idx `2`: `shift=+224`, `frac=+0.5`, `CFO=19000 Hz`, p0 ratio `3.760`.
- idx `3`: `shift=+144`, `frac=+0.5`, `CFO=7000 Hz`, p0 ratio `2.258`.

The live stage-1 policy does not currently use `frac=0` except baseline, and a
single wide live envelope covering CP2095 plus all CP2096 replay-positive
points would be too expensive for the RA window. The actionable low-cost
envelope from CP2096 is the deployed-policy point `shift=-96`,
`CFO=21000..25000 Hz`.

## Judgment

CP2096 proves the `~17` candidate class is fast enough but the CP2095
`-192/+34 kHz` accepting point is not run-stable. The next RF iteration should
use a similarly cheap envelope around the CP2096 deployed-policy accepting
points:

- shift `-96..-96`
- CFO `21000..25000 Hz`, step `1000`
- max candidates `12`

Keep RAR candidate trace enabled. If this accepts Msg1, judge whether RAR is
transmitted and then answer the CP2057 RAR question from the UE trace.
