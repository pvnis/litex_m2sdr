## Decision

The active ladder frontier moves above live Msg1 acceptance and above
RA-PDCCH visibility. Stop [interface-redacted] PRACH window/envelope as the primary next
step. The next work should be no-RF first:

1. Audit the UE RA-RNTI path from CRC-OK RA-PDCCH candidate into RA-PDSCH
   decode and MAC RAR handoff.
2. Compare the gNB RAR grant shape (`ra-rnti=0x39`, RAPID `0`, rb `[0..3)`,
   `tbs=9`) with the UE-side decoded RA-PDCCH/DCI geometry at slot `6291`.
3. If another RF run is needed, increase or late-arm the RAR-window trace so
   the RA-PDSCH decode and handoff rows for the actual RAR slot are not capped
   before interpretation.

No thresholds, delay constants, scheduler payloads, RAR payloads, source
timing branches, gateware, or downlink knobs were changed in CP2101.
