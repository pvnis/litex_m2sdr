## RAR Visibility Split

The gNB transmitted one RAR for `ra_rnti=0x39` after the accepted Msg1. The
UE opened the matching response window:

- `prach_tti=8814`
- `ra_window_start=8817`
- `ra_window_length=40`
- `response_window=40`

At the expected end-of-window slot corresponding to the gNB RAR slot, the UE
RAR trace recorded:

- `slot=8857`
- `rnti=0x39`
- `n_dl=0`
- `candidates=1`
- `max_epre=31.774`
- `max_corr=0.031554`

There are no `PAVONIS_SRSUE_RAR_CANDIDATE_TRACE` rows and no
`PAVONIS_SRSUE_RAR_PATH_TRACE` rows in this run. Because the normal RAR trace
shows `n_dl=0` at the expected RA-RNTI slot, this is not a CP2101-style
post-RA-PDCCH-CRC handoff datapoint. For CP2106, the CP2057 question is
classified as RA-PDCCH threshold/no-candidate visibility at the UE, not CRC KO,
geometry mismatch, RA-PDSCH decode, or MAC RAR handoff.

The two gNB Msg3 CRC KOs at `0x4601` are scheduler/listener follow-on work
after the gNB sent RAR. They are not evidence that the UE decoded the RAR or
transmitted Msg3, because UE-side markers remain zero:
`Setting RAR Grant=0`, `Set TA RAR=0`, `Setting UL grant and prepare Msg3=0`.

## Decision

CP2106 is a valid ladder datapoint: the compact negative grid is low-cost and
can produce a live gNB-accepted Msg1 plus transmitted RAR. It also adds another
good downlink roll to the descriptor-fix tally.

It does not answer the CP2101 post-CRC RAR handoff question because the UE did
not decode an RA-PDCCH candidate in this RAR slot. The next RF attempt should
keep the same compact CP2106 grid and traces once, seeking another RAR-bearing
sample. If the next RAR again has `n_dl=0` at the expected RA-RNTI slot, pivot
from PRACH envelope iteration to RAR downlink visibility/instrumentation. If it
reproduces CP2101's RA-PDCCH CRC OK, use the CP2102 RAR path trace sequence to
split RA-PDSCH/grant/MAC handoff.

Keep thresholds, delay constants, scheduler/RAR payloads, source timing
branches, gateware, and downlink knobs frozen.
