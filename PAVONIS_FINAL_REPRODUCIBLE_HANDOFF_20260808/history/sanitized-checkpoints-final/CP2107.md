## Decision

Stop iterating the PRACH stage-1 envelope for now. Msg1/RAR supply is
sufficient; the immediate blocker is that the UE is not seeing a decodable
RA-PDCCH candidate for gNB-transmitted RARs in the repeated CP2106/CP2107
class.

Next work should stay no-RF first: compare CP2101's RA-PDCCH-CRC-OK RAR slot
against CP2106/CP2107's `n_dl=0` RAR slots on timing, slot placement inside the
response window, RA-RNTI/DCI geometry, and RAR PDCCH/PDSCH scheduling fields.
Only run more RF after that comparison produces a specific discriminating
change or confirms the next diagnostic instrumentation target.
