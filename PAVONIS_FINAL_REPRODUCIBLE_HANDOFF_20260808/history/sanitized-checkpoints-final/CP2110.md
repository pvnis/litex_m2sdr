## Interpretation

CP2110 answers the CP2057 question for this valid RAR transmission as
threshold-skip/no-candidate visibility at the UE, not CRC KO and not geometry
or payload mismatch after a decoded DCI. The gNB scheduler/PDU layers emitted a
correct-looking RAR allocation and payload, and the UE monitored the scheduled
RA-RNTI slot but observed floor-class/no-candidate energy and correlation.

The new timing fact is that the scheduler used the stage1 reported PRACH slot
`532:4` as the RAR-window reference, while the UE's own PRACH marker for the
attempt was `529:4`. That places the RAR PDCCH at system slot `5337`, exactly
the end of the UE's `5297..5337` response window. This makes the next suspect a
RAR timing-reference or edge-window presentation issue, not more PRACH envelope
[interface-redacted].

## Decision

Stop PRACH stage-1 envelope iteration for now. The next step is no-RF:

1. Compare CP2101's one RA-PDCCH CRC-OK candidate at slot `6291` with CP2110's
   scheduler row and UE no-candidate slot on RAR-window placement, AL/NCCE/SS,
   PDSCH TD/symbol/CRB/power, and UE EPRE/corr.
2. Audit the source path from PRACH detection/result attribution into
   `ra_scheduler.cpp` to determine whether the RAR response window should be
   referenced to the UE actual PRACH slot / collection source slot instead of
   the stage1-reported shifted slot.
3. Only after that audit, consider a single targeted RF change such as
   scheduling RAR earlier in the UE response window or shifting the RAR
   reference. Do not [interface-redacted] RAR payloads, thresholds, PRACH envelope, delay
   constants, or gateware for this failure.
