## Interpretation

CP2113 proves the CP2112 report-slot override works mechanically and changes
the failure class. CP2110 was `RAR transmitted, UE blind` with the RAR scheduled
at the UE response-window edge after a `+30` reported-slot displacement.
CP2113 removes that displacement, reaches an accepted p0 Msg1 at the gNB, but
the gNB sends no RAR at all.

The active blocker is now between accepted PRACH detection and RAR scheduling:
either the accepted result is filtered/dropped before MAC/scheduler, or it
arrives too late for the actual source-slot RA response window. The `5.2 ms`
stage-1 search time on a baseline-accepted row is the leading latency suspect,
but it should be confirmed no-RF before another RF run.

## Decision

Stay no-RF next:

1. Trace the accepted PRACH result from `prach_processor_worker.cpp` through the
   FAPI translator, MAC RACH indication, and `ra_scheduler.cpp`.
2. Determine whether CP2113 produced no RAR because the source-slot RACH
   indication was stale/late relative to `ra_resp_window=40`, or because the
   accepted result did not reach scheduler state at all.
3. If lateness is confirmed, add a default-off stage-1 early-exit policy so a
   baseline-accepted PRACH row stops before the expensive grid; then run one RF
   diagnostic with both `REPORT_SLOT_OFFSET=0` and early-exit enabled.

Do not retune PRACH envelope, RAR payload, RAR threshold, delay constants, or
gateware for this failure.
