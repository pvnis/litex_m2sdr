## Decision

CP2120 is a valid ladder datapoint:

- Downlink remains good-class, extending the post-descriptor-fix early-pass
  tally.
- Stop-on-accept plus source-slot reporting can produce a gNB RAR in the
  current shape.
- The current blocker is RAR downlink visibility at the scheduled RAR slot, not
  Msg1 supply or RAR scheduler latency for this run.

Next work should stay no-RF first and compare the RAR-slot geometry/slot phase
and UE RAR-correlation metrics across CP2101, CP2106, CP2107, CP2110, and
CP2120. CP2101's CRC-OK RA-PDCCH row remains the useful contrast; CP2120 adds
a clean scheduler-traced no-candidate row at slot `6614`.

Do not rerun broad artifact-directory greps for CP2120. The useful paths are
the CP2120 summary JSON, CP2086 analysis JSON/MD, exact gNB RAR trace lines,
and short UE RAR trace windows around slot `6614`.
