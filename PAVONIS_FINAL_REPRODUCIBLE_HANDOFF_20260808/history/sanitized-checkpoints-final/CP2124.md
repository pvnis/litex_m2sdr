## Verdict

CP2124 is the first clean post-fix ladder run to reach UE-decoded RAR and gNB
Msg3 PHY processing. The active frontier moves from RAR production/visibility
to Msg3/PUSCH CRC. RAR production is not declared permanently solved from one
roll, but this run strongly confirms the CP2121 `mod160=51` phase finding.

in-process UL-symbol arm (`PAVONIS_GNB_INPROCESS_UL_SYMBOL_ARM=1`,

timing/power can be analyzed without relying on fragile external log-tail
arming.
