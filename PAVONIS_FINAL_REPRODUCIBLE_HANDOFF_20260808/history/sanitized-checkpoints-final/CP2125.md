## Actual Propagation

The in-process arm flag propagated:

- `gnb_inprocess_ul_symbol_arm=1`
- `gnb_inprocess_ul_symbol_arm_event=rar`

But the CP2031 wrapper layer hard-coded the UL-symbol capture settings after
the outer environment was applied:

- `ul_symbol_arm_mode=after_rar`
- `ul_symbol_summary_max=4096`
- `ul_symbol_raw_max=96`
- `ul_symbol_raw_peak_min=0.001`
- `ul_symbol_raw_rms_min=0.00025`
- `ul_symbol_arm_poll_period=16`

Therefore command-line overrides to `after_gnb_rar`, lower thresholds, and
other caps did not stick through the CP2116 -> CP2035 -> CP2031 chain.

capture controls, so the next attempt should bypass CP2031 or use a one-off
direct CP1807 runner that sets the intended `after_gnb_rar` in-process arm
shape explicitly.
