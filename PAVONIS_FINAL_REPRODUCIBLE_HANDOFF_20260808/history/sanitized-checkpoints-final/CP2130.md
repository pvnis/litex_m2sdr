## Decision

but the run did not reach real UE Msg3.

The next blocker is why CP2130's RAR PDCCH was invisible to the UE despite
gNB RAR scheduling and early-pass SIB1. Before another RF run, compare CP2130
against CP2124's UE-RAR-positive run at the RAR epoch: gNB RAR PDCCH/PDSCH
geometry, scheduled slot phase, DCI/CORESET/search-space identity, power fields,
and UE RA-PDCCH max-corr/EPRE at the matching RA-RNTI opportunities.
