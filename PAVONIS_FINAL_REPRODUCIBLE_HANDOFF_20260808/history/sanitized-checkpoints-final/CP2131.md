## Verdict

CP2124 exact RAR slot `8691` decodes at the UE. CP2130 exact RAR slot `8691`
is monitored by the UE but remains `n_dl=0`, corr `0.009309`, EPRE `-20.550`.

Therefore the CP2130 split is not RA-window coverage, RAR payload, RAPID,
or decoded-PDSCH-to-MAC handoff. It is RAR-epoch downlink presentation/supply at
the UE, even at a slot phase that has decoded in a previous run.

Modulo phase remains useful but is not sufficient: CP2130 also has a high-EPRE
RAR at mod160 `54` (`slot 6454`, EPRE `32.226`) that stays low-corr
(`0.086125`), while its mod160 `51` RAR at `8691` is floor-class.
