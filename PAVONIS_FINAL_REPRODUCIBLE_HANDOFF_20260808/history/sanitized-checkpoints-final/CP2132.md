Verdict:

The binary-safe timeout/downlink-late join does not name the RAR-hole splitter.
Timeouts are present near both decoded and failed RARs: the decoded CP2101 slot
has `43` timeouts within `+/-50 ms` and `6` within `+/-5 ms`, while the CP2130
floor slot has `46` within `+/-50 ms` and `9` within `+/-5 ms`. Conversely,
CP2110 has a floor-EPRE RAR with zero timeouts inside `+/-100 ms`. Downlink-late
is also not a clean splitter: high-EPRE/low-corr RARs have zero downlink-late
events inside `+/-50 ms`, while the decoded CP2101 slot has one downlink-late
inside `+/-10 ms`.

This keeps two failure classes separate:

- `floor_epre`: the exact scheduled RAR slot is monitored but appears at the UE
  floor. The logs do not prove a directly coincident timeout or downlink-late
  event caused it.
- `high_epre_low_corr`: scheduled-slot energy is present around `32 dB` EPRE,
  but RA-PDCCH correlation remains `~0.03..0.09`. This class has no local
  downlink-late signature in the joined windows and is not explained by the
  binary-safe logs.
