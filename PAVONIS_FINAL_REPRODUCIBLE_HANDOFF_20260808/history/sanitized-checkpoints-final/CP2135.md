## Verdict

The new post-reseat one-way DL path is not in the pre-reseat good class. The
capture mechanics are clean and the gNB TX window/readback is present, but the
bladeRF sees a no-PBCH/no-CSI floor-class signal about `25-31 dB` below the
closest pre-reseat references. This is a material physical-path or RF-routing
level shift until proven otherwise.

Do not retune gain, attenuation, thresholds, delay constants, payloads, or
gateware in response to this checkpoint. The next requested step remains one
full frozen CP2124-shape ladder calibration run, but it must be judged as a
calibration/path-state run, not as a ladder attempt. If it also voids at
downlink acquisition, the post-reseat baseline diff should flag all pre-reseat
RAR/SI/PRACH calibration classes as historical-only until the RF path is
restored or deliberately recalibrated.
