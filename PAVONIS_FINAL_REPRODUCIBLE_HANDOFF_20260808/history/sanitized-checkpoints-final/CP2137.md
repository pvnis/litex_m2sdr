## Relation to CP2135

CP2135 independent bladeRF DL smoke on the reseated path was severe
floor-class: RMS `-42.43 dBFS`, raw RMS rel2048 `0.007560422`, max `75`,
PBCH/CSI `0/847`, about `25-31 dB` below CP1945/CP2038 references.

CP2137 shows the path is not simply dead for the live UE flow: the UE decoded
PBCH/SIB1 with normal CFO and strong direct SI-PDCCH rows. The post-reseat
state is therefore mixed:

- independent no-UE bladeRF DL capture is materially low versus old references;
- live srsUE downlink acquisition/SIB1 can still pass;
- uplink PRACH supply is marginal/intermittent, with only a subset of strong
  windows near the old high-RSSI class and no detector accept in this run.

## Verdict

CP2137 is a calibration/path-state run, not a ladder advancement. It rebuilds
the post-reseat baseline and blocks the immediate RAR-hole trace experiment
because there was no gNB RAR to trace.

Do not retune gains, attenuation, thresholds, delay constants, payloads,
scheduler behavior, or gateware based on this single calibration run. The next
safe step is no-RF comparison of CP2137 strong predemod/stage-1 coordinates
against CP2124/CP2130 accepted-class data, or a physical/path confirmation if
the user wants the new independent bladeRF DL level restored before more RF.
