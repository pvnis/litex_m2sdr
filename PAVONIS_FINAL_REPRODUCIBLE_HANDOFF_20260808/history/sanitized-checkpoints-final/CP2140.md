## Verdict

The CP2139 envelope did not transfer to this live run. This was not because the
grid was too expensive: the health gate is same-class and the observed worst
stage-1 time matches the bounded estimate. It also was not a downlink/SIB1 void:
the UE decoded SIB1 and transmitted seven PRACHs.

The likely explanation is run-to-run PRACH phase/timing-family drift. CP2139 was
designed from CP2137's accepted offline windows (`705:4`, `817:4`, `929:4`,
`17:4`, `129:4`), while CP2140's high-energy family is `529:4`, `641:4`,
`753:4`, `865:4`, `977:4`. Next step should stay no-RF and replay CP2140's six
saved strong windows through the same harness to determine whether another
small env rectangle exists or whether static envelopes are losing to per-run
phase/timing drift.

RAR-event tracing remains parked because gNB RAR production did not occur.
