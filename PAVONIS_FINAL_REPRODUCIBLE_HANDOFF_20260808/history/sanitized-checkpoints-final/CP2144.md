explained by the helper's gain/bandwidth/freq-offset split alone. The helper is
still useful as a calibration instrument, but future independent DL baselines
should use the srsUE-matched receive config (`1842506000 Hz`, `9.216 MHz`,
gain request `40`) unless intentionally reproducing the historical CP2135
shape.
