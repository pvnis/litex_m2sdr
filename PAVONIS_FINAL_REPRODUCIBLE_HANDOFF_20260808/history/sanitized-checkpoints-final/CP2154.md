## Interpretation

The CP2144/CP2148 DL tone collapse is not explained by byte-level CP903 helper
invocation drift. The transmit vector, M2SDR replay parameters, bladeRF RX
frequency/rate/bandwidth/gain/filter/sample count, and capture scripts are
materially identical to CP2143. The low CP2144/CP2148 RX1 result is therefore a
path/state outcome, not a command-line/config change in the CP903 tone helper.

The later CP2151 same-shape recovery means the CP903 DL tone helper itself is
usable as a calibration instrument when the RX1 path/state is good. CP2144 and
CP2148 should be treated as low-state observations, not as a new golden
reference and not as evidence that the helper invocation changed.

For CP2135, keep the distinction clear:

- CP2135 used the independent bladeRF DL capture helper, not the CP903 CW tone
  helper.
- CP2144's no-RF helper rebaseline already found that the CP2135-style and
  srsUE-matched no-TX floors are nearly identical (`0.005545567` versus
  `0.005706251` rel2048), so simple gain/BW/frequency-offset config split alone
  does not explain the CP2135 floor-class independent capture.
- Future independent DL calibration should still use the srsUE-matched helper
  config (`1842506000 Hz`, `9.216 MHz`, gain request `40`), but CP2154 does not
  prove the CP2135 deficit was caused by CP903 tone-helper drift. It points to a
  separate independent-capture-helper calibration/path question.
