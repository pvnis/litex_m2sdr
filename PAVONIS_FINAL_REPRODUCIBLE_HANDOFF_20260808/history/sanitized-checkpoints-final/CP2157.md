## Verdict

CP2156 was not primarily a missed-envelope case. The saved PRACH window is too weak/incoherent for the detector even under the broad CP2141 replay family. The CP2153B fixed candidate list remains unreliable as a live Msg1 supply, but CP2157 does not justify adding more candidates for the CP2156 window.

The next RF ladder attempt should prioritize collecting or producing more recoverable PRACH windows, not another unchanged CP2153B/CP2155B retry and not threshold relaxation. If capture visibility is the immediate question, use bounded strong-window capture with a lower diagnostic floor and a higher raw cap; keep detector semantics unchanged.
