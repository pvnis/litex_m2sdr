## Verdict

CP2158 resolves the immediate CP2057 split as: not CRC-KO, and not floor-EPRE/missing-air energy. The UE skipped RA-PDCCH decode because correlation stayed below the `0.5` gate even at the exact RAR slot where EPRE was very high.

There is also a geometry co-suspect: the gNB sched trace reports aggregation level `4` at NCCE `0`, while the UE candidate trace at the exact slot reports candidate `L=2`, NCCE `0`, bits `37`. If those fields are both actual aggregation levels, the UE is not evaluating the same candidate the gNB scheduled. That makes the next step a no-RF SearchSpace1/CORESET0/RAR DCI geometry audit, not threshold relaxation.
