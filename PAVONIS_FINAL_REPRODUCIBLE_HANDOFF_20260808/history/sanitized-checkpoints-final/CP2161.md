## RAR-Epoch Join

All three gNB RAR FAPI groups were identical where it matters: slot phase
`mod160=51`, RA-RNTI `0x39`, AL4, NCCE0, 37 DCI bits, SS power offset `+6 dB`,
and a 9-byte RAR PDSCH/TX_DATA payload. The UE outcome split below them:

| RAR slot | UE EPRE | corr / CRC | first timed worker row | estimated timed source due | TX timeouts in first 20 ms |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 2131 | -20.755 | 0.0093 / 0 | +12.080 ms | +40.032 ms | 11 |
| 3251 | -20.412 | 0.0428 / 0 | +12.094 ms | +40.123 ms | 11 |
| 6611 | +33.327 | 0.7417 / 1 | +1.074 ms | +29.141 ms | 1 |

The estimated due time combines the first timed-row wall delay with its common
`-27.95..-28.07 ms` worker slip. The two floor RARs are therefore presented
about `10.9 ms` later than the decoded RAR.

The descriptor contents are not corrupt. Across all `65536` rows and the exact
RAR windows there are zero short non-EOB rows, zero late rows, zero dedup rows,
and zero duplicate HAS_TIME source/release timestamps. CP2047 report mode also
finds all rows full-size (`1022` samples). There are no downlink-late events in
the first 60 ms or at any of the three exact RAR slots.

The wall-time worker gap straddling slots `3251` and `6611` is about `20 ms`,
but the source-time gap is only one descriptor (`0.088715 ms`). This is a
worker service/backpressure stall, not an intentional timestamp gap. Its phase
relative to the reactive RAR is the splitter: the floor RAR resumes in the
middle of the stall at `+12 ms`; the decoded RAR resumes at `+1 ms`.

## Verdict

CP2161C moves the ladder back to Msg3 and resolves the RAR-hole mechanism at
the current instrumentation boundary:

- scheduler/FAPI content and power are clean;
- CP2074B descriptor flags/geometry are clean;
- floor RARs coincide with a roughly 11 ms late timed-worker presentation and
  11 immediate write timeouts;
- the decoded RAR has normal roughly 29 ms source-due timing and one timeout.

The reactive downlink hole is therefore a TX worker service/backpressure phase
problem below FAPI and above (or at) the timed DMA submission boundary, not a
RAR geometry, power, payload, UE threshold, or RF-path problem. Do not lower
the UE correlation gate or change RAR/scheduler semantics.

Next stay no-RF and trace the approximately 20 ms wall stall in the Soapy
worker/write path: identify which timeout/poll branch blocks progress while
source timestamps remain contiguous, and whether existing timeout retry/carry
or the queued coherent-continuation work can remove the service gap without
changing slot attribution. Any fix must first pass the CP2047 descriptor gate
and the existing TX-health gate before another ladder run.
