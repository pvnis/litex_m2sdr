## Stage1 Semantics

Stage1 receives the complete collected buffer. Its energy gate scans that
buffer, then each candidate transforms it with `source = index + shift + frac`.
The frozen candidate shifts (`-192` or `+64` samples) move the effective origin
by only `-16.67 us` or `+5.56 us`. They cannot recover an arrival displaced by
milliseconds outside the collection start.

CP2167 later proves CP2165's approximately `-87 dB` rows are startup-era
windows whose SFNs alias the later UE occasions after SFN wrap. They do not
measure a live UE burst. The source conclusion remains: once collection runs,
extras cannot expose samples before the delayed start and stage1 cannot bridge
millisecond-scale origin error.

## Verdict

The current `12000` extra samples extend only after a `352536`-sample start.
CP2167 supersedes the immediate action: post-refresh PRACH requests become
persistently late before collection, so measure that slot/context delta before
retrying the bounded broad capture. Keep all scheduler, RAR, threshold, gain,
attenuation, Msg3, and gateware freezes; the `50 us` TX refresh remains armed.
