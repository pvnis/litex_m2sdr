## Correction to CP2165 Interpretation

CP2165's 29 stage1 rows were also startup-era windows. Their SFNs happened to
match later UE transmit SFNs after the 10.24-second SFN wrap; they were not
same-epoch detector windows. Therefore CP2165 did not prove that a live UE
burst missed the `30.602..32.644 ms` sample span. It proved that all actual UE
occasions arrived after the PRACH worker had entered the persistent
request-late regime.

The CP2166 source semantics remain correct, but its broad calibration window is
still untested because no armed PRACH collection occurred.

## Verdict and Next Step

CP2167 finds a more fundamental post-refresh timing fault than collection
origin: after a deterministic startup transition, every PRACH request reaches
the worker after its nominal slot has already passed. No delay or broad-window
calibration can execute in that state.

Stay no-RF and add one default-off, metadata-only, row-capped late-context trace
at the two `on_prach_request_late()` branches. Record requested slot, current RX
context slot/symbol/timestamp, collection target state, and compensation. Build
and deploy with normal proof, then run a short diagnostic to measure the exact
slot/sample delta across the one-time transition. Do not [interface-redacted] collection delay,
stage1, thresholds, gain, RAR, Msg3, or gateware before that delta is known.
