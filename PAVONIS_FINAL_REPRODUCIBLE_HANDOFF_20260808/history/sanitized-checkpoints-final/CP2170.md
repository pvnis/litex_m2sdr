Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Purpose

CP2169 ties the refreshed run's PRACH request lateness to 48608 abandoned TX
samples. Re-evaluate the already-built synchronous same-chunk retry under the
new low-timeout 50 us full-ring-refresh regime without reintroducing the closed
timeout-carry or deadline branches.

## Runner change

The frozen CP1807 wrapper now exposes three wrapper-only controls:

- `PAVONIS_SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_RETRY_OVERRIDE`, default `0`
- `PAVONIS_SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_RETRY_MAX_OVERRIDE`, default `0`
- `PAVONIS_SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_RETRY_US_OVERRIDE`, default `1000`

They are propagated through the remote environment and translated by the [host-redacted]
detached launcher into the existing OCUDU controls
`OCUDU_SOAPY_TX_TIMEOUT_RETRY`, `_MAX`, and `_US` inside the generated gNB
runner. The existing outer launch still forces retry/carry/deadline defaults
off; the generated runner applies the explicit wrapper override last.

When retry remains disabled, the original readback gate is unchanged. When it
is explicitly enabled, an alternate compact readback gate requires the exact
enabled/max/timeout values while retaining all-timed, continuous, attenuation,
write-timeout, carry-off, and deadline-off checks. Settings are recorded in
the sequence summary.
