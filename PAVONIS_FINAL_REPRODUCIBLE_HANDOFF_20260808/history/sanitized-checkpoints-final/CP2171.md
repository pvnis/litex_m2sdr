# CP2171: synchronous retry plus full-ring refresh gNB-only gate

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

Stamp: `[date-redacted]CP2171_RETRY_FULL_RING_GNB_ONLY_GATE`

## Shape

CP2169's frozen no-UE shape was retained: all-timed TX, 30 ms lead, 50 us
full-ring refresh, 1 ms base write timeout, normal PRACH compensation, and the
CP2168 late-context trace. The only behavioral change was the already-built
synchronous same-chunk retry at enable/max/timeout `1/2/1000 us`. Carry and
deadline modes remained off.

Readback proves `tx_timeout_retry_enabled=1`, max attempts `2`, retry timeout
`1000`, carry off, and deadline-write off. Host, readback, pre-UE health, stop,
package, and postchecks pass; wrapper overall/gate/late-gate are `0/0/0`, and
binary-safe timeout/downlink-late/release-late lines are `16/0/0`.

## Strict result

The acceptance gate fails:

- `timeout_retry_attempts=36`
- `timeout_retry_successes=2`
- `timeout_retry_exhausted=16`
- `timeout_retry_recovered_samples=[date-redacted]`
- `timeout_retry_abandoned_samples=181162`
- `write_deficit=data_write_deficit=18892`
- `partial_ret=6`, `timeouts=16`

The late-context trace again reaches 64 rows. All requests remain late before
target establishment. Unlike CP2169's gradual onset, retry blocking makes the
first row already 29628 samples (2.572 ms) late. The run then stays near the
three-to-four-slot class: mean 40953 samples (3.555 ms), maximum 48552 samples
(4.215 ms), with slot deltas `2:3, 3:50, 4:11`.

## Verdict

FAIL for mechanism and ladder acceptance. Synchronous retry reduces directly
attempted write deficit but does not eliminate it; exhausted calls abandon
larger logical tails, and the added blocking establishes the RX-context lead
before the first observed late request. Do not increase retry count or timeout,
and do not revisit carry/deadline queues.

The remaining structural mismatch is capacity: 256 descriptors of 1022 samples
at 11.52 Msps hold 22.711 ms, less than the configured 30 ms timed lead. A
20 ms lead fits within the hardware ring while keeping all-timed cadence. The
next bounded gNB-only gate should use 20 ms lead, retry/carry/deadline off,
50 us refresh, and the late trace; require zero write deficit and zero late
rows before any UE ladder run.
