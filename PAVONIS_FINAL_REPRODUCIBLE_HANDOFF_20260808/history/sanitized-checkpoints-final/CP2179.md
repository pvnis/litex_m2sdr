## Result

The CP2177 shape was repeated with only the already-built synchronous
same-chunk retry enabled: two retry attempts, 1 ms each. Carry and all deadline
branches remained off. Runtime readback proved retry `1/2/1000`, all-timed TX,
20 ms lead, refresh 50 us, and pool64.

The CP2178 authoritative pre-UE gate correctly fails and starts no UE. Final
health remains free of PRACH request lateness: 397 normal empty windows, no
late-context file, and zero downlink/release-late events.

Retry does not recover a chunk:

- `timeout_retry_attempts=24`
- `timeout_retry_successes=0`
- `timeout_retry_exhausted=12`
- `timeout_retry_recovered_samples=0`
- `timeout_retry_abandoned_samples=133610`

Final direct counters remain nonzero:

- `write_deficit=data_write_deficit=12744` samples (`1.10625 ms`)
- `timeouts=12`
- `partial_ret=1`

The smaller deficit versus CP2177 comes from the producer spending time in
failed retries, allowing later calls to find a drained queue. It is not sample
recovery and still abandons a much larger logical tail.

The new shared health gates work as intended: the latest periodic summary
fails before UE, the parsed final stop row is present in summary JSON, and the
final jq gate sets overall return code 1 despite zero timeout-warning lines.

## Verdict and next

FAIL, decisive. Close synchronous bounded retry again; do not increase its
attempt count or timeout. Historical deadline/carry variants are also closed
by CP1715-CP1727 and must not be rerun unchanged. Stay no-RF and audit the
deadline/reference math against `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS`: the startup
producer requests about 81 ms by logical write 100, while the plugin applies
the RF time offset below OCUDU. Any next source fix must pace/retry against the
actual RF deadline without queueing stale samples or abandoning logical tails.
No PRACH, scheduler, threshold, gain, delay, or gateware changes.
