## Partial-return audit

This 480 is recovered, not lost. LiteX `writeStream()` first fills any existing
DMA remainder. If that consumes only part of the caller request and acquiring a
fresh buffer reaches its timeout, it returns the positive `samp_avail` count.
OCUDU records that call as partial, advances `sent_total` by the returned count,
and loops over the unsent tail with an advanced sample pointer and timestamp.

The authoritative end-to-end counters prove completion:

- `stream_samples=736289130`
- `samples_ret=736289130`
- `stream_deficit=0`
- no timeout, error, carry, deadline, or retry abandonment

The periodic pre-UE row has the same state: raw write deficit 480 and one
partial, but stream deficit zero with all abandonment/error counters zero.
`data_samples_req - data_samples_ret` double-counts the re-requested 480 and is
therefore an API-pressure metric, not an unrecovered-stream metric.
