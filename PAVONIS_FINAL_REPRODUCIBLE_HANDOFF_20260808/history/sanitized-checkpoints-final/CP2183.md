## Gate correction

The shared runner no longer treats raw per-call `write_deficit` or
`partial_ret` as unrecovered sample loss. Those remain visible diagnostics.
Both the latest periodic pre-UE gate and final parsed stop-summary gate now
require exact zeros for:

- `stream_deficit`, `timeouts`, and `errors`
- retry exhaustion and retry-abandoned samples
- carry overflow, abandonment, pending samples, and deadline-dropped samples
- deadline-write breaks, timeouts, and abandoned samples

This measures logical stream delivery and every known early-return/drop path.
It accepts a positive partial only when subsequent calls recover it and the
full stream is returned.
