# CHECKPOINT 2187 - TX-Lead / UL-Compensation Derivation

Date: [date-redacted]
RF: none

## Question

Explain why CP2184-CP2186's corrected 20 ms TX-lead shape has good downlink but
floor-class PRACH collection while the frozen UL compensation remains the
CP2161-era `352536` samples.

## Evidence

The bladeRF TX metadata maps each scheduled Msg1 timestamp to realtime. Joined
to the nearest same-coordinate gNB detector row, CP2186 attempts 2-7 place the
detector about `10.4..11.2 ms` after estimated UE RF emission and see only
`-93.8..-94.5 dB` RSSI. The same method on CP2161C's energetic attempts places
the detector about `2.1..2.8 ms` after emission. Current host time-sync offsets
are `-477 us` on [host-redacted] and `-509 us` on [host-redacted], only about `32 us` apart, so
NTP does not explain the roughly 9 ms run-class difference.

CP2161 used a 30 ms LiteX TX time offset. CP2184-CP2186 use 20 ms to fit the
worker ring and corrected deadline path. Moving RF emission 10 ms earlier also
moves every UE uplink transmission 10 ms earlier relative to the gNB lower-PHY
timeline, but the old UL compensation was retained.

At `11.52 Msps`, 10 ms is exactly `115200` samples:

```text
352536 - 115200 = 237336
```

Source audit confirms the signs:

- `PAVONIS_PRACH_TIMING_COMPENSATION_SAMPLES` directly becomes the positive
  PRACH collection delay; lower means an earlier window.
- `PAVONIS_NON_PRACH_UL_RX_TIMESTAMP_OFFSET_SAMPLES` retags PUSCH/PUCCH/SRS
  input timestamps by the signed value. The historical negative value maps the
  late physical samples back to their nominal slots; reducing TX lead requires
  reducing its magnitude by the same 115200 samples.

## Verdict

The next coherent shape is derived, not swept:

```text
PAVONIS_PRACH_TIMING_COMPENSATION_SAMPLES=237336
PAVONIS_NON_PRACH_UL_RX_TIMESTAMP_OFFSET_SAMPLES=-237336
```

Keep report-slot attribution zero, stage1/stop-on-accept/TA compensation, RF
settings, thresholds, scheduler/RAR semantics, and corrected TX delivery
unchanged. For bounded capture telemetry, use the existing arm and raise its
energy floors to the stage1 class so pre-Msg1 noise cannot consume the budget.
