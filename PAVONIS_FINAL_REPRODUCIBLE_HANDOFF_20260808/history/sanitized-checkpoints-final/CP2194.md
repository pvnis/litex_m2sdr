## EVM verdict

`total_evm_valid=0` and `symbol_evm_count=0` are an instrumentation capability
result, not low EVM. Source audit shows the PUSCH factory instantiates an EVM
calculator only when EVM is the selected SINR method or PHY metrics request it.
The current method leaves that calculator null.
