## Health And Ladder

This is an exact frozen retry of CP2196. Post-run wrapper audit confirms CP2188
already enabled the RAR-event worker trace in both runs, so CP2197's outer
worker-trace exports were redundant and did not change effective behavior.
Controller, pre-UE, final TX, and host postchecks pass. Final TX has zero stream
deficit, timeout, error, deadline failure, abandonment, downlink-late, and
release-late rows; the known 480-sample positive partial is recovered.

Cell search is good (`+9.163 dB`, CFO `-3649.5 Hz`), SFN and SIB1 pass, and the
UE sends seven Msg1 attempts. The gNB accepts five Msg1s and emits five normal
RARs. The first two RAR PDCCHs decode at the UE (`corr=0.864/0.671`); the next
three remain high-EPRE but fall below the unchanged gate (`0.139/0.422/0.357`).
The UE accepts temp C-RNTIs `0x4601/0x4602`, applies TA `100/116`, and encodes
one Msg3 for each. Both reach the gNB but CRC is KO.

## Real PUSCH EVM

Only two of 25 scheduled PUSCH listens contain a UE waveform:

| Slot | RNTI | EPRE / RSRP | Selected SINR | TA residual | CFO | Total EVM | CRC |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| `3570` | `0x4601` | `-40.07 / -59.05 dB` | `-26.03 dB` | `-1.042 us` (`-12.0` samples) | `+969 Hz` | `14.54` | KO |
| `4692` | `0x4602` | `-41.56 / -53.03 dB` | `-19.91 dB` | `+0.358 us` (`+4.1` samples) | `+941 Hz` | `7.92` | KO |

All 11 data-bearing symbols in each burst have extreme EVM; there is no single
bad symbol or boundary. The implementation computes decision-directed EVM on
equalized symbols before descrambling. Values `14.54/7.92` correspond to
EVM-derived SINR `-26.95/-21.67 dB`, agreeing with the independent selected
post-equalization SINR. This is a real demodulation-quality result, not a trace
unit or capability artifact. Timing remains inside CP and CFO is modest.

## Grant And Worker Audit

UE and gNB agree on both Msg3 grants: RAR frequency assignment `112` maps to
RBs `[8,11)` in the 52-RB BWP, time allocation 0 maps to all 14 symbols, `k=6`,
MCS 0, TBS 11 bytes, and the same temp C-RNTI. Grant/resource mismatch is not
the observed cause.

The 65,536-row event-armed worker trace spans `5.813 s` in both wall and source
time and is structurally clean: zero late, dedup, or short non-EOB descriptors.
Per-RAR cadence residuals are only `-0.336..+0.321 ms`; local service gaps do
not separate decoded from failed RARs. FAPI is clean for all five RARs. The
within-run high-power PDCCH correlation collapse is therefore below the Soapy
descriptor layer in this run.

## UE Host-Waveform Control

The UE TX debug captured the two exact Msg3 SC16 waveforms (files 3 and 5).
Offline FFT analysis finds the expected occupied subcarriers `[-216,-180)`,
uniform power across all 14 symbols, and `49-52 dB` occupied-to-background
contrast. Removing the waveform's bulk `+1.1..+1.2 kHz` rotation gives
nearest-QPSK EVM `0.210/0.221`, equivalent to about `+9.8/+9.4 dB` EVM-derived
SINR. Every data symbol is coherent in the same class.

The host waveform is therefore correctly placed and decodable-class before the
bladeRF. The gNB-side EVM implies approximately `31-37 dB` of coherence quality
is lost after that host capture, across bladeRF TX, RF path, M2SDR RX/lower-PHY
presentation, or upper-PUSCH processing.

## Next

Use the already-approved bounded late/event raw path: arm on the first gNB RAR,
capture 128 M2SDR UL symbols with raw thresholds zero, and keep full-rate append
off. This is enough to span the expected `k=6` Msg3 slot. If the first RAR is
UE-blind the raw result is void and may be retried once. Correlate the captured
time-domain Msg3 against its exact UE host waveform and independently FFT its
RBs. Raw coherent plus upper EVM bad means shifted-grid/upper-PHY processing;
raw already incoherent means the loss precedes the upper processor. No power,
threshold, scheduler, timing, payload, or gateware change is justified first.
