## Health And Ladder Result

The exact CP2199 RF shape completes with controller exit `0`. Both realtime
host gates pass; bladeRF is SuperSpeed, authorized, active, and forced on. The
authoritative TX health is clean: stream deficit `0`, Soapy timeouts `0`,
errors `0`, deadline failures `0`, downlink-late `0`, and release-late `0`.

The UE reaches SIB1 and sends seven Msg1 attempts. Stage 1 selects three rows,
the detector accepts six rows, and the gNB allocates and encodes three RARs.
The UE decodes none, prepares no Msg3, and emits no PUSCH. CP2086 classifies the
run `rar_transmitted_ue_blind`.

The downlink roll is weak but usable: cell-search SNR is `-1.008 dB`, CFO

## RAR-Hole Split

All three exact RAR candidates use the expected RA-RNTI `0x39`, AL4
(`cand0_L=2` in UE notation), NCCE0, CORESET0, and 37 DCI bits. Their EPRE is
high, so none is a missing-power slot:

| RAR slot | EPRE | Corr | UE result |
|---:|---:|---:|---|
| `3883` | `32.811` | `0.423445` | threshold-skipped |
| `6121` | `33.595` | `0.538390` | decode attempted, CRC KO |
| `7243` | `32.988` | `0.075148` | threshold-skipped |

This run contains both known high-power RAR failure classes: low correlation
before decode and above-threshold CRC KO. Geometry and power are closed for
these events; the remaining defect is candidate coherence/presentation.

## Raw-Capture Verdict

The RAR-PDU event arms the raw path and writes `128` CF32 symbols, but the
capture is not temporally usable for Msg3. The first RAR is scheduled at system
slot `3883`, with its Msg3 allocation at `3889`. The first raw symbol is only at
`sfn=394 slot=4 symbol=4` (system slot `3944`), and the capture ends at
`sfn=395 slot=3 symbol=5`. It therefore starts `61` slots after the RAR and
`55` slots after the expected Msg3.

Source audit explains the delay mechanically. The lower-PHY raw path checks
the arm file only every `1024` symbols by default, approximately `73 ms` at 15
kHz SCS. The in-process RAR event touches the file promptly, but the lower-PHY
cache observes it too late. Thus CP2200 is raw-Msg3 void for two independent
reasons: the UE sends no Msg3, and the capture window misses the scheduled
Msg3 slot even if it had.

## Decision

Do not run a third unchanged first-RAR capture. Stay no-RF and replace the
arm-file polling dependency with a default-off bounded in-process target that
publishes each allocated Msg3 system slot to the lower PHY and writes only its
14 symbols. This removes the 1024-symbol latency, covers later RARs when the
first is UE-blind, and reduces raw work relative to the current 128-symbol
window. Keep all RF, threshold, timing, scheduler, payload, and gateware
controls frozen.
