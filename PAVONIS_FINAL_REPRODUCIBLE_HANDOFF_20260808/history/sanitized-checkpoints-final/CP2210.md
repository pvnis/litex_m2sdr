## Signed Target Join

Target 0 matches UE host waveform `pavonis_rf_blade_tx_dbg_000005.sc16` and
occupies the exact expected subcarriers with `34.93 dB` inside/outside power.
Unlike CP2205, signal fills target symbols 0-9 and symbols 10-13 are floor.
The signed analyzer correlates nine independent host symbols at
`0.750..0.781` and measures lag `-3320..-3319` samples, median `-3320`: the
Msg3 starts before the captured PUSCH slot.

Moving the collection earlier by 3320 samples gives a per-run ideal offset of
`-241118 + 3320 = -237798`.

CP2205 is the complementary class: accepted stage1 `shift=-192`,
`cfo=45 kHz`, RAR TA 0, and Msg3 lag `+3782` at offset `-237336`, giving ideal
`-241118`. The two exact runs therefore require different non-PRACH offsets:

| Accepted PRACH class | Observed offset / lag | Derived ideal |
|---|---:|---:|
| negative shift / high CFO | `-237336 / +3782` | `-241118` |
| positive shift / low CFO | `-241118 / -3320` | `-237798` |

The `7102`-sample cross-run movement exceeds the `3782` static knob change, so
another static midpoint is invalid. The RAR TA is not the missing conversion:
srsUE applies `TA * 16 * Ts`; TA 106 is about 636 samples at 11.52 Msps, not
3320. Accepted PRACH raw-window energy also moves several thousand samples
between the runs, corroborating a run timing class rather than a fixed cable
delay.

## Verdict

PASS and decisive. The SI instrumentation is clean and the measured offset
substantially improves PUSCH but exposes a run-dependent sign reversal. Do not
[interface-redacted] another constant and do not infer a 32-sample/TA rule.

Next implement a default-off dynamic non-PRACH offset selection published only
after an accepted stage1 PRACH: negative-shift/high-CFO family selects
`-241118`; positive-shift/low-CFO family selects `-237798`. The lower PHY must
atomically consume that value before Msg3 and realign once on change. Preserve
slot attribution and all PRACH/stage1/TA, TX, RF, scheduler, payload, and
threshold controls. Prove disabled cost/state and build/deploy no-RF before one
target-capture validation.
