## Dynamic Offset Path

The gNB receives the exact CP2211 configuration: fixed startup offset
`-241118`, dynamic policy enabled, negative mapping `-241118`, positive mapping
`-237798`, trace limit 64.

Five PRACH occasions select stage1. All are negative `-192/-0.5`, with CFO
`20` or `35 kHz`, search time `811..1431 us`, and `2..3` candidates. Each
selection produces one publish and one lower-PHY consume. All five consumes
report `changed=0`, correctly retaining the negative-family `-241118` value.

The gNB accepts five Msg1s and emits five RARs. The UE decodes the second RAR,
accepting temp C-RNTI `0x4602`, TA `0`, RAR slot `8043`, and initial Msg3 slot
`8049`; it also transmits one retransmission at slot `8079`.

## Msg3 Result

The gNB receives Msg3 but both decodes are CRC KO. Initial slot `8049` reports:

- EPRE `-45.494 dB`, RSRP `-60.802 dB`;
- SINR `-29.301 dB`;
- timing estimate `+0.112 us`, CFO `+301 Hz`;
- total EVM `15.535`.

The retransmission at `8079` is floor-class at EPRE `-94.401 dB`.

All five exact targets complete and 70 shifted pre-PUxCH symbols are saved.
Target0 is correctly floor-class because the UE accepted RAR/target1. Target1
has a `41.32 dB` occupied-band lift at the Msg3 subcarriers, but energy exists
only in symbols 0-4; symbols 5-13 are floor. A widened offline join against the
two identical Msg3 host waveforms finds host symbols 10-13 at signed lag
`-7632.5` samples (`-7633..-7631`, corr `0.7468..0.7823`). Therefore the
waveform begins roughly nine OFDM symbols before the scheduled target window.

With shifted-path semantics, this run's one-variable ideal is approximately:

`-241118 - (-7632.5) = -233485.5` samples.

That differs by about `7.6k` samples from CP2205 even though both runs selected
the same negative stage1 family. The sign-only mapping is therefore rejected.
The CP2211 publish/consume implementation is mechanically correct; its input
model is not sufficient.

## Verdict

CP2086 class: `msg3_reached_gnb_crc_not_ok`.

The descriptor-fix downlink tally gains another clean good-class run. The
remaining blocker is run/occasion-scale coarse UL timing, not Msg1 supply,
RAR delivery, target ownership, fixed-sign family selection, or downlink
cadence. Stay no-RF next: join saved strong PRACH-window coarse position against
the exact Msg3 ideal offsets for CP2205, CP2210, and CP2212. A dynamic policy
must use that per-run coarse observable, not stage1 shift sign or RAR TA.
