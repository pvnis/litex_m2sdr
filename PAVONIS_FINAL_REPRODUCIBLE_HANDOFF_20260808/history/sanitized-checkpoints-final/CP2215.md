## PRACH-edge model

The exact UE-host/strong-window wall-time pairs are strong window 2 for target2
and strong window 4 for target4. Recomputing their bounded energy edges with
8-sample blocks gives `-5468` for both. Historical accepted edges at the same
resolution are:

- CP2205: `+3574`
- CP2210: `-868`
- CP2212: `-3172`
- CP2215 A/B: `-5468/-5468`

First remove each historical run's independently recomputed guard interval
error using CP2213 metadata. All runs then satisfy:

`Msg3 residual = PRACH edge + scheduler interval error - 3786.5`

Historical fitted constants are `-3777`, `-3788`, and `-3786.5`. For CP2215,
the scheduler interval error is `+3`; the model predicts `-9251.5` versus
observed `-9254.5/-9252`, errors `3.0/0.5` samples.

For TA0, the required gNB non-PRACH offset is therefore:

`offset = -237331.5 - accepted_PRACH_edge`

For edge `-5468`, this predicts `-231866.5`, within two samples of the measured
median. The edge comes from an accepted window's longest above-threshold energy
run (8-sample RMS blocks, threshold geometric mean of p20/p80, calibrated host
active stop 10404); no open-loop phase or sign classification is involved.
