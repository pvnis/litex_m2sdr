All three gNB RAR FAPI rows are structurally identical and scheduled at slots
`1325/2445/5805` (`mod160=45`, RA-RNTI `0x39`, AL2, 37 bits, power offset
`+6`). The exact UE candidates are:

| Slot | EPRE | Corr | Threshold | Result |

| 2445 | 33.714 | 0.406006 | 0.5 | skipped |
| 5805 | 33.043 | 0.087927 | 0.5 | skipped |

Thus the CP2057 question is answered for this run: all RARs are
high-EPRE/low-correlation threshold skips before CRC, with correct candidate
geometry. The UE decodes no RAR and emits no Msg3. The 42 target files are
no-UE controls; the gNB's nominal 30 Msg3 CRC-KO listens do not judge uplink
timing or the dynamic offset.

CP2215's UE-visible RARs occurred at `mod160=43/44`, so CP2218's `mod160=45`
alone does not establish a forbidden phase. This remains the known stochastic
RAR downlink supply hole rather than evidence against CP2216.
