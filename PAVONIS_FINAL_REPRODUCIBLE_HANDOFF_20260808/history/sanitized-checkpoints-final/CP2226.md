## Msg1 and RAR

The UE transmitted seven PRACH bursts. Six strong predemod windows reached the
stage-1 energy gate; all six were selected and accepted by the gNB. Stop-on-
accept limited the work to 31 candidate evaluations, with maximum search time
`5875 us`. The gNB encoded and scheduled six RARs.

The accepted-edge publisher emitted six generations and the generic non-PRACH

The UE decoded four RARs, accepted four grants, and prepared four Msg3
transmissions. Their TA commands were `122`, `179`, `98`, and `0`. The gNB
reached Msg3 decoding but recorded `60` CRC-KO and zero CRC-OK attempts.

## Sample-Domain Join

Four RAR-decoded target windows have strong PUSCH-band energy, approximately
`33.0..34.3 dB` above the surrounding spectrum. Two have matching coherent UE
debug waveforms within the capture cap:

```text
target  TA   PUSCH lag       accepted symbols  residual CFO
1       122  -734 samples    13                +400 Hz
3       179  -1073 samples   12                -2825 Hz
```

The lag convention is negative when the waveform arrives earlier than the gNB
target. Within each target, symbol lags are stable to three samples, far better
than the roughly 50-sample cyclic-prefix budget. The coherent UE debug metadata
also preserves the requested PRACH-to-Msg3 intervals exactly, so this is not a
capture-timestamp jitter artifact.

The residual has an exact timing-advance signature:

```text
target 1: -6 * 122 = -732 samples; measured median -734 (error -2)
target 3: -6 * 179 = -1074 samples; measured median -1073 (error +1)
```

The accepted-edge correction removed the earlier multi-millisecond Msg3 error,
but the generic non-PRACH collection reference does not account coherently for
the RAR timing advance applied by the UE. Existing
`PAVONIS_PRACH_STAGE1_TA_COMPENSATE_SELECTED_SHIFT=1` only compensates the
stage-1 transform's selected PRACH shift; it does not close this residual.

## Verdict

CP2226 moves the frontier past Msg1 and RAR supply. Msg3 is present, strong,
and sample-stable at the gNB, but the two measurable arrivals are displaced by
exactly `-6 samples * RAR TA command`, causing all PUSCH CRCs to fail.

Stay no-RF next. Trace the UE and gNB TA unit conversions and verify whether the
deployed UE applies NR numerology scaling before choosing a fix. Do not add a
new static delay constant: the evidence calls for one coherent TA-aware timing
reference or correction, not another run-specific offset.
