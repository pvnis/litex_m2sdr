## Decoder controls

The existing srsRAN PUSCH BLER test was built and run at the exact
3-PRB/MCS0/11-byte/full-slot geometry. Its AWGN curve is:

```text
nominal SNR -4.5/-4.0/-3.5/-3.0/-2.5 dB
average EVM  0.788/0.776/0.764/0.752/0.739
BLER         0.270/0.080/0.0152/0.001/0
```

The native OCUDU `pxsch_bler_test` was also built and run at BWP/PRBs 3,
MCS0, one layer and one receive port. With ten LDPC iterations, nominal
`-2 dB` produced measured mean SINR `-1.65 dB` and BLER `1/3000`; nominal
`-1 dB` produced measured mean SINR `-0.57 dB` and `0/3000` failures.

These controls do not prove the live signal is AWGN-equivalent, and the live
gNB uses a six-iteration limit rather than the control's ten. They do show
that CP2240's measured SINR/EVM is not intrinsically a near-certain CRC
failure. Repeated KO at the best class needs exact runtime configuration and
decoder-stat proof before more RF [interface-redacted].
