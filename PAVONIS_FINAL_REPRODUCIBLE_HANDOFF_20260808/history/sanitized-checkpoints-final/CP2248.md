## Wider offline result

The `wide_combo` replay evaluates `8987` transforms per window and recovers all
six. No single transform covers all six. The smallest clean cover uses one
empirical CFO/fractional family and two nearby integer shifts:

| candidate | accepted windows | minimum ratio |
| --- | --- | ---: |
| `+16:-0.5:-25000` | `0,2,3,4,5` | `1.846` |
| `+64:-0.5:-25000` | `1` | `2.074` |

Exact ratios for the first transform are `2.233, 1.846, 2.302, 2.038, 2.249`
on its five accepted windows; accepted delays are `0..1`. The complementary
transform accepts window 1 at delay `2`.

The `-25 kHz` search coordinate is an empirical recovery axis, not a physical
LO claim. The two shifts are only 48 samples apart while the apparent CFO
family changes across runs, consistent with the prior finding that the search
axes absorb per-occasion timing/phase presentation.
