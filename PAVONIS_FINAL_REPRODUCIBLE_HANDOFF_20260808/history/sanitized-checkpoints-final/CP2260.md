slew, neutral edge calibration `-237332`, stage-1 cap `30`, and compiled PUSCH
decoder default `6`. Power, thresholds, RAR, scheduler, collection, and
gateware settings remained frozen.

All wrapper and post-run gates pass. The authoritative TX stop summary reports
zero Soapy timeouts, zero downlink-late, zero release-late, zero stream deficit,
one partial return, and the familiar 480-sample aggregate data-write deficit.

Runtime lattice telemetry proves the feature stayed active:

total_slew_samples=-2878 max_slew_samples=1

The anchors are expected resets at non-contiguous tracking segments. Every
individual correction remained bounded to one sample per slot.

## Sample-Domain Timing Join

The eight-waveform UE dump cap preserved the first two Msg3s. The third Msg3
occurred after the cap was exhausted and is waveform-join void, although its
gNB EVM measurement is valid.

| TTI | Requested residual | Target median lag | Lag minus residual | Median correlation |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 6450 | +7 | +16.5 | +9.5 | 0.775169 |
| 9810 | -17 | -9 | +8 | 0.770253 |

For both saved waveforms, 11 of 14 symbols cluster at the reported dominant
lag; the three periodic outliers are OFDM/DMRS correlation aliases. The
unexplained `+8..+9.5`-sample residual is comfortably inside the 54-sample
short CP and is substantially tighter than the modeled `-44..+44` bound.

Three lattice-slewed transmit calls have 11,519 samples instead of 11,520.
That is the expected one-sample negative correction, not truncation corruption.
The CP2256 analyzer was tightened to accept only 11,519 or 11,520 samples,
record the observed count, and pad one terminal sample only for offline symbol
splitting; any larger mismatch remains fatal. TTI 6450 uses this path, while
TTI 9810 is an unmodified 11,520-sample dump.

## Verdict And Next Rung

CP2260 closes the run-variable pre-symbolizer timing defect positively: the UE
UL lattice places real Msg3 inside the gNB cyclic prefix and restores the
strong-EVM class on three independent attempts. It does not yet pass Msg3 CRC.

The CP2251 ten-iteration decoder control is now qualified rather than void.
Run the identical frozen lattice shape once with only
`expert_phy.pusch_dec_max_iterations=10`. Judge propagation first, then Msg3
CRC on strong-EVM rows. A CRC recovery promotes ten iterations into the attach
shape; persistent CRC KO at the same EVM moves the frontier to decoder/LLR
semantics without reopening RF timing.
