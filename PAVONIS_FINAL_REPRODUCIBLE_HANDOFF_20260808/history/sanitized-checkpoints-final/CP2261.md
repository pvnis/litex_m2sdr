## Saved-Waveform Discriminator

TTI 9489's host SC16 is clean (`0.228374` QPSK EVM). Its requested Msg3
timestamp residual is `-1100` samples. TA command 182 advances the UE by 1092
samples at 11.52 Msps, leaving only `-8` samples of lattice residual. The
lattice itself therefore remains coherent.

The matching gNB target has normal energy through symbols 0-11 but symbols
12-13 collapse to near-floor. This is an early-burst/window-tail loss, not a
corrupted UE waveform. The attempted large-lag full-slot complex correlation
is not used: uncorrected inter-device CFO and per-symbol phase make that metric
non-identifying. The direct timestamp equation and tail loss are sufficient.

## Source Audit

Two coupled source defects explain the run mechanically.

1. `pavonis_adjust_prach_stage1_time_advance()` receives the selected stage-1
   shift as if it were measured in PRACH detector delay bins. It is actually a
   raw 11.52 Msps sample shift. For the first Msg1, selected shift `+120`
   changes the RAR TA from an inferred unadjusted `62` to `182`, one TA command
   per raw sample. At this geometry, 120 raw samples are about 20 TA units, not
   120. The second row similarly changes an inferred `55` to `119` for raw
   shift `+64`, instead of approximately `+10.7` TA units.

2. `pavonis_publish_dynamic_non_prach_ul_offset_from_edge()` currently computes
   `calibration - edge - rar_ta_samples`. The lower-PHY consumer passes
   `timestamp + offset_samples` to the shifted PUxCH path. A UE advanced by TA
   arrives earlier, so those samples require a positive timestamp relabel;
   subtracting TA moves the gNB collection reference in the opposite direction.
   CP2261's first row proves the arithmetic: calibration `-237332`, edge `-868`,
   and TA `1092` produce deployed offset `-237556`; the corrected sign produces
   `-235372`.

## Verdict And Next Rung

CP2261 is a valid TA-semantics discriminator and a decoder10 headroom void. Do
not retry RF unchanged. Stay no-RF and fix both semantics under the existing
default-off stage-1 TA and edge-include-TA flags:

- convert selected raw shift/fraction to detector time-resolution units using
  the actual lower-PHY sampling rate before adjusting/replaying TA;
- add, rather than subtract, the resulting RAR TA samples when relabeling the
  non-PRACH receive timestamp.

Add focused unit/algebra tests, rebuild and deploy with disabled-path proof,
then rerun the lattice plus decoder10 shape. A strong-EVM row will finally
judge decoder headroom; CRC recovery moves the ladder to Msg4/attach.
