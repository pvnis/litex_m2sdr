## CP2262 arithmetic proof

The new code is active. Each selected raw shift `-448.5` is converted before
TA adjustment, and the corrected RAR TA command clamps to zero rather than
being inflated in raw-sample units. Edge-derived non-PRACH offsets are:

- `321.4`: edge `-4804`, TA `0`, offset `-232528`
- `433.4`: edge `-4796`, TA `0`, offset `-232536`
- `545.4`: edge `-4812`, TA `0`, offset `-232520`
- `657.4`: edge `-4780`, TA `0`, offset `-232552`

These satisfy the CP2262 equation `calibration - edge - scheduler_error + TA`.
Because no RAR is emitted, this checkpoint proves the arithmetic path only;
it cannot judge Msg3 symbol energy or CRC.

## Decision

Keep the CP2262 binary and all frozen timing/RAR semantics. Prepend the proven
`-448:-0.5:+31 kHz` and `-448:-0.5:+29 kHz` candidates to the existing explicit
candidate list. With stop-on-accept, they become candidates 2-3 after baseline
instead of 25-27, reducing expected stage-1 latency to roughly `1-2 ms` and
leaving RAR-window headroom. No threshold, delay constant, RAR window,
scheduler, payload, power, calibration, or gateware change is justified.
