# CP2264 - Proven CP2263 recovery class moved to the front of stage-1

Date: [date-redacted]

## Objective

Remove the deterministic CP2263 RAR-window expiry without changing collection
delay, RAR window, scheduler semantics, detector threshold, power, calibration,
or gateware. CP2263 selected only `-448:-0.5:+29/+31 kHz`, but reached those
points at candidate 25 or 27 after `13.966..15.685 ms`.

## Change

New wrapper:

`cp2264_run_ta_semantics_fast_candidate_lattice_pusch_10iter.sh`

It preserves the CP2261/CP2262 controls:

- UE UL TX lattice `1`, maximum slew `1 sample/slot`
- edge calibration `-237332`
- PUSCH decoder iterations `10`
- stage-1 cap `30` and stop-on-accept inherited from the frozen inner shape
- all CP2246 RAR worker/PUSCH runtime traces and downstream controls

Only the explicit candidate order changes. The list now starts with:

1. `-448:-0.5:+31000`
2. `-448:-0.5:+29000`

The prior 14 explicit candidates follow unchanged. The baseline candidate still
runs first in `prach_processor_worker.cpp`, so the two new front entries become
runtime candidates 2 and 3. The existing rectangular fallback remains behind
the list and the total cap remains 30.

## Expected discriminator

At the CP2263 per-candidate cost, an accepted `+31 kHz` event should stop after
about two evaluations total and an accepted `+29 kHz` event after about three,
roughly `1-2 ms` instead of `14-16 ms`. Together with the unchanged `~30.6 ms`
collection delay, that should leave approximately `7-8 ms` of the 40-slot RAR
window available.

One bounded CP2264 RF run is unlocked. Judge health first, then require stage-1
selection at candidate 2 or 3, no stale-RAR warning, gNB RAR production, UE RAR
decode, corrected TA/edge arithmetic, full-symbol Msg3 energy, and only then
decoder-10 CRC.
