## Verdict and next discriminator

The CP2265/2266 collapse is below the scheduler/FAPI boundary and is not
explained by power, CFO, host realtime state, USB power state, device
temperature, or logged transport errors. It remains either M2SDR TX
presentation/coherence below the descriptor boundary or bladeRF/srsUE receive
presentation.

Before another full ladder attempt, run a bounded [host-redacted]-only all-timed gNB TX
with two instruments from run start: the existing capped Soapy TX worker
descriptor trace and an immediate M2SDR same-device self-RX PBCH capture. No UE
is needed. Interpretation:

- self-RX weak/coherence-degraded: TX-side below-FAPI defect;
- self-RX good with descriptor clean: bladeRF/srsUE receive side;
- descriptor churn: CP2074B mechanism/regression;
- descriptor clean but self-RX weak: below-Soapy/gateware/RF TX presentation.

The CP2264 fast-candidate and CP2262 corrected-TA ladder experiment stays
parked and unjudged until this supply discriminator is green.
