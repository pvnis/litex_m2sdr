M2SDR self-RX downlink discriminator. The capture itself is mechanically good,
yet RX readback is `1747500000 Hz`, the normal gNB uplink center, instead of the
DL/SSB self-RX center `1839650000 Hz`. Do not decode or interpret this file as
downlink supply.

`PAVONIS_SOAPY_RX_FORCE_CENTER_HZ=1839650000` and
`OCUDU_SOAPY_RX_BW_HZ=7680000` paths to the CP2075/CP2268 wrapper, preserving
