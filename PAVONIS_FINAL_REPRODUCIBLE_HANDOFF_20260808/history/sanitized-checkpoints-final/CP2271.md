## Verdict

The all-timed gNB stream is coherent and decoder-visible at the M2SDR's own RX
while the Soapy TX descriptor path is clean. This closes the current
M2SDR-TX/FAPI/descriptor branch for the CP2265/CP2266 supply collapse. The
active defect is now on the external receive leg: physical M2SDR-to-bladeRF
path, bladeRF RX state/presentation, or srsUE receive processing.

This does not reopen amplitude, scheduler/FAPI, TX attenuation, timed cadence,
or thermal theories. Absolute self-RX append amplitude is not directly
comparable to bladeRF CF32 because the two paths use different presentation
scales.
