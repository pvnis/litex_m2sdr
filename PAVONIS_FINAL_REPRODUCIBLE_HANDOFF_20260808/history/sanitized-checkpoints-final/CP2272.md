## Composition

The new wrapper reuses the established CP1223 independent capture runner, the
CP2026 all-timed/power-sign gNB start helper, and the CP2135 safe stop/package
helper. It changes only the bladeRF receive geometry from the old CP2135
helper settings to the CP2144-proven srsUE-matched settings:

- RX1/channel 0
- center `1842506000 Hz`
- sample rate `11520000 Hz`
- bandwidth `9216000 Hz`
- gain request `40`, AGC off in the reused capture helper
- normal RX filter
- `2,880,000` samples, exactly 250 ms
- SSB target remains `1839650000 Hz`, expected PCI 1

The gNB side remains all-timed, TX backoff 10/readback attenuation 5 dB,
`PAVONIS_GNB_POWER_CONTROL_OFFSET_SS_DB=6`, and SI-PDSCH power-sign
compatibility enabled by the unchanged CP2026 helper.
