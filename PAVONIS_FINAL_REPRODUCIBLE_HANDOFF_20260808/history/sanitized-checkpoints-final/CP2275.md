## Verdict

This is the decisive same-run split. The gNB/FAPI/Soapy/M2SDR TX stream is
descriptor-clean and decodes over M2SDR self-RX, while the external bladeRF
copy of that run is waveform-correlated but not NR-decodable. The active
downlink supply defect lies after the same-device TX/self-RX observation point:
the external RF/attenuator path, bladeRF analog/digital receive presentation,
or the inter-device clock/sample-rate relationship. It is not srsUE-only
processing because independent raw IQ already fails, and not ordinary bulk
CFO.
