## Verdict

CP2276 is execution-valid and capability-void due to measured clipping. It
does prove that the physical M2SDR-to-bladeRF path carries the expected complex
waveform with high coherence; it does not prove decoder capability at the
current level.

The next receive setting is determined from the measurement, not an open-loop
sweep: reducing gain request by 16 dB, from 40 to 24, projects RMS from
`-9.16` to approximately `-25.16 dBFS`, close to the unclipped decoder-positive
class and CP2275's live level. Repeat the exact replay once at gain24. This is
capture-instrument calibration only; gNB/srsUE ladder gain and all TX controls
remain unchanged.
