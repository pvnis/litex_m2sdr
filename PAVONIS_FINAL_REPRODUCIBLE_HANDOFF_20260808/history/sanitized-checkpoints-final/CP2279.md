## Source audit

Cell search and SFN synchronization do not share acquisition state:

- `cell_search` scans a rolling overlap window of `slot_sz + ssb_sz` and
  returns decoded PBCH, CFO, timing offset, SSB index, and MIB.
- `cell_select_run()` retunes to the same carrier, configures a separate
  `ue_sync_nr` object in `FIND`, and discards the successful searcher's CFO,
  timing offset, and decoded MIB.
- SFN `FIND` receives isolated `1 ms` buffers and starts with CFO zero.
- `sfn_sync_max_trials` is fixed at 100.

This creates both a finite 100 ms reacquisition lottery and a chunk-boundary
hole that cell search's overlap window does not have.

## Exact saved-IQ control

`cp2279_replay_ue_sync_nr` links the exact current `libsrsran_phy.a` and feeds
CP2275's live bladeRF SC16 capture through the deployed
`srsran_ue_sync_nr_zerocopy()` path. It does not edit synchronization source.
CP2275 is the same file that CP2277 decoded at `16/24` PBCH and `24/24` CSI.

With zero initial CFO, the capture's natural chunk phase fails the live
100-call limit and synchronizes only at call 138. Across 23 receive-chunk
phases spaced by 512 samples:

- `18/23` synchronize within 100 calls
- `21/23` synchronize within 250 calls
- two phases remain FIND-only through 250 calls

The persistent misses cluster where the isolated 1 ms chunk cuts the SSB.
Thus a raw decoder-positive capture can deterministically fail the live SFN
procedure even when RF samples are valid.

Seeding the natural phase with CP2278's measured `-3843 Hz` CFO moves sync from
call 138 to call 58. CFO seeding alone does not close every boundary phase.
Conversely, the selected aligned phase at sample [date-redacted] plus the same seed
synchronizes at call 28 with final CFO `-3786.835 Hz` and small delay
`-5.383 us`.

## Verdict

The active srsUE blocker is a lossy cell-search-to-SFN handoff, amplified by
the marginal/run-variable scheduled SSB supply. This is specific enough to
unlock a default-off source fix without changing RF, thresholds, or the
100-trial bound:

1. carry the successful cell-search CFO into `slot_sync` before SFN FIND;
2. use the successful cell-search `t_offset` for one initial receive-boundary
   alignment, placing the repeated SSB safely inside the 1 ms FIND buffer.

Implement this in `sync_sa.cc`/`slot_sync`, not `ue_sync_nr.c`. Keep disabled
behavior byte-for-byte equivalent and add only cached configuration plus a
single metadata summary. Gate with the CP2279 saved-IQ replay before deploy or
RF. Candidate order, TA, PUSCH, thresholds, power, scheduler, and all RF
settings remain frozen.
