## CFO alias mechanism

The PUSCH channel estimator derives CFO from the phase difference between the
first two DMRS symbols, symbols 2 and 7. At 15 kHz SCS their start separation
is 5.359375 subcarrier epochs, or approximately 357.292 us. Therefore:

```text
alias period       = 15000 / 5.359375 = 2798.83 Hz
unambiguous range  = approximately +/-1399.42 Hz
```

The reciprocal inter-device offset is about `+4 kHz` on the M2SDR uplink,
consistent with the UE's downlink cell-search CFO near `-3.8 kHz`. It aliases
to roughly `+1.0..1.24 kHz`, exactly the live estimator rows. The enabled
channel-estimator compensation therefore removes the aliased value rather
than the true offset, leaving enough inter-symbol rotation to force every
strong Msg3 CRC KO. Applying about `-4 kHz` before OFDM restores the waveform.
