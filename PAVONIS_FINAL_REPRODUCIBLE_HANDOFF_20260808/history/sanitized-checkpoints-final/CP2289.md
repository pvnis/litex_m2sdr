## Source audit

The UE path is now mechanically bounded:

- `proc_sr_nr::sr_opportunity()` increments `sr_counter` only when an actual
  configured SR occasion is pending and not blocked by a measurement gap or
  PUSCH.
- `cc_worker::work_ul()` calls `get_pending_sr()` only without a PUSCH grant,
  selects a PUCCH resource only when UCI is nonempty, and then calls
  `srsran_ue_ul_nr_encode_pucch()`.
- The new `PAVONIS_SRSUE_SR_PUCCH_TRACE` records the SR opportunity result,
  selected PUCCH geometry, and successful encode. It is env-cached once,
  row-capped, default-off, and does no sample inspection.

The gNB always creates a logging PUCCH processor decorator, but normal logger
levels provided no evidence in CP2288. The new `PAVONIS_PUCCH_RESULT_TRACE`
records every enabled processor invocation and result: slot/system slot,
format, PRB/symbol geometry, HARQ/SR shape, status/SR verdict, normalized
detection metric, EPRE, RSRP, SINR, CFO, and processing time. It is also
env-cached once, row-capped, default-off, and metadata-only.
