## Verdict

LADDER-SUPPLY PASS, STATIC-CORRECTION FAIL. The unchanged CP2305 retry reaches
Msg1, RAR, UE Msg3, gNB Msg3 CRC OK, and RRC Connected with clean TX health.
The CP2304 `+12.2/-72.0 degree` correction is proven active, but all 64 strong

through the production detector shows that this run needs a substantially
different correction which also changes across its four consecutive SR slots.
Static phase constants are therefore rejected as the live fix.

The four exact strong-invalid grids at system slots `4260/4280/4300/4320`
reproduce without the CP2304 correction at metrics
`0.809233/0.944583/0.955023/0.912586`. Their best expected-geometry
ICS0/OCC0 corrections are:

| System slot | Symbol step | Subcarrier step | Metric |
|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| 4260 | `+5.6 deg` | `-105.2 deg` | `4.381876` |
| 4280 | `+7.5 deg` | `-108.5 deg` | `4.568770` |
| 4300 | `+4.8 deg` | `-113.1 deg` | `4.765879` |
| 4320 | `+2.0 deg` | `-117.3 deg` | `4.929440` |

CP2303 instead required `+10.3..+13.3 deg/symbol` and
`-68.9..-73.7 deg/subcarrier`. The phase slope is run-variable and the
CP2306 subcarrier optimum drifts by about `12 degrees` over four consecutive
opportunities. The CP2304 midpoint can reduce rather than improve the raw
metric on this run. Geometry-only alternate ICS/OCC passes are artifacts of
the same slope and do not match the configured resource.
