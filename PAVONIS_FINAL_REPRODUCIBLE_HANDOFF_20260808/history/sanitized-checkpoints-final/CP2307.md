## Design

- Existing primary correction remains `+12.2/-72.0 degrees`.
- New cached envs:
  - `PAVONIS_PUCCH_F1_PHASE_SEARCH_CANDIDATES`
  - `PAVONIS_PUCCH_F1_PHASE_SEARCH_TRACE_LIMIT`
- CP2307 bank: `8:-96,6:-104,4:-112,2:-120,0:-128`.
- Search is disabled when the candidate string is unset or invalid.
- Search runs only after the primary result is invalid and EPRE is at least
  `-60 dB`; the production detection threshold is unchanged.
- Candidates use precomputed phasors, stop as soon as all eligible entries are
  valid, and emit at most the cached trace-row cap.
- Scope remains production PUCCH format 1. PRACH, PUSCH, other PUCCH formats,
  SRS, timing, scheduler, UE, and all thresholds are unchanged.
