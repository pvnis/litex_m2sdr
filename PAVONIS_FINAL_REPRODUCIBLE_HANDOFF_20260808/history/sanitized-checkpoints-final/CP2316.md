session and later receives RRC Release. qcore starts cleanly, establishes NGAP
with the gNB, and records no Initial UE or NAS message.

The gNB PUSCH trace locates the missing handoff:

- Msg3: `1` CRC OK, TBS `11`, EPRE `-39.008 dB`, SINR `+5.806 dB`.
- Post-Msg3 PUSCH: `127` CRC KO for the same C-RNTI.
- `23` real strong KO rows average `-53.760 dB` EPRE and `-6.234 dB` SINR;
  the first is TBS `528`, followed mainly by TBS `141` retries.
- `104` lower-energy KO windows average `-67.320 dB` EPRE.

The UE source embeds the registration NAS message in RRCSetupComplete. Since
qcore sees no InitialUEMessage and all post-Msg3 PUSCH is CRC KO, the active
failure is the uplink RRCSetupComplete/NAS PUSCH, not qcore configuration or
PUCCH scheduling.

log `25288720...`.
