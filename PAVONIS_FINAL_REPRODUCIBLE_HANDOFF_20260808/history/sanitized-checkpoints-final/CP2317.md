healthy. CP2316's Stage 8 first failure is specific to the OTA post-Msg3 uplink:
the gNB decoded Msg3 at `+5.806 dB` SINR / `-39.008 dB` EPRE, then saw the
RRCSetupComplete/NAS PUSCH class roughly 15 dB lower and repeatedly CRC KO.

grants, UE power-control inputs, transmit amplitude, timing, and gNB receive
metrics. Do not reopen qcore/NAS or PUCCH/SR, and do not spend another RF run
until the comparison produces a narrow testable correction.
