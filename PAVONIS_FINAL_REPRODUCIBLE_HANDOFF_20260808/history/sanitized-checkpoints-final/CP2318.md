# CP2318 - Post-Msg3 PUSCH no-RF audit and gain-control gate

Date: [date-redacted]

## Question

Why did CP2316 decode Msg3 but fail all post-Msg3 RRCSetupComplete/NAS PUSCH,
and what is the narrowest next RF control after CP2317 exonerated the current
protocol/core stack?

## Compact sample-domain/log join

`cp2318_analyze_post_msg3_pusch.py` joins UE PUSCH runtime configuration to the
nearest bounded bladeRF TX event by wall time, then joins the UE slot to the gNB
PUSCH result. It reads the existing CP2316 logs only and emits compact JSON.

- UE bladeRF TX events: `280`
- UE PUSCH runtime configurations: `49`
- gNB PUSCH results: `128`
- Exact UE/gNB system-slot joins: `35`
- Exact strong/floor rows: `24/11`
- Exact CRC OK: `1` (Msg3)

The floor-energy splitter is exact. All `11/11` exact floor rows have bladeRF
TX `status=-14`, which libbladeRF defines as `BLADERF_ERR_TIME_PAST`; all
`24/24` exact strong rows have `status=0`. The local bladeRF adapter reports the
late event upward but returns `nsamples`, so the UE upper layers believe the
burst was sent even though the timed TX was rejected. The `93` gNB result rows
without a corresponding UE PUSCH-config trace are all floor class.

## Power accounting

The strong post-Msg3 rows are not caused by a UE digital-amplitude collapse.

| Class | TBS | PRBs | MCS | UE TX RMS | gNB EPRE | gNB SINR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Msg3 | 11 | 3 | 0 | `0.3646` | `-39.01 dB` | `+5.81 dB` |
| First data | 528 | 24 | 9 | `~0.356` | `-52.44 dB` strong | `-6.26 dB` |
| HARQ/data | 141 | 36 | 0 | `~0.300` | `-53.82 dB` strong mean | `-6.23 dB` mean |

Holding nearly the same total digital power while spreading from 3 PRBs to
24/36 PRBs predicts `-9.10/-12.44 dB` per-RE EPRE shifts. The measured strong
shifts are `-13.43/-14.81 dB`, leaving only `-4.33/-2.38 dB` unexplained.
Therefore most of CP2316's apparent 15 dB deficit is grant-bandwidth power
spreading, while the intermittent floor rows are timed-TX rejection.

The bladeRF runtime readback proves TX gain request/actual `60/60 dB` and a TX
gain range ending at `66 dB`. A `+6 dB` gain control should lift the strong
low-rate TBS141 retries to roughly `0 dB` SINR class, enough to test whether
RRCSetupComplete/NAS reaches the gNB even if the existing time-past loss rate
remains. It does not change PRACH timing, TA, PUCCH correction, scheduling, or
payload semantics.

## No-RF runner gate

The two-host base runner previously failed to carry its already-supported
`UE_TX_GAIN_DB` input through `remote_env_prefix`, so setting the variable at a
top-level wrapper had no effect. CP2318 adds the missing quoted export and
matching argument only. The existing [host-redacted] helper then rewrites `tx_gain`
in the per-run config and records `UE_TX_GAIN_DB_OVERRIDE`.

## Verdict

Run one fresh bounded RF control with the CP2316 shape and only UE bladeRF TX
gain changed from 60 to 66 dB. Require propagation proof from the generated UE
config and launch/result env before interpretation. Judge health and the normal
ladder first, then post-Msg3 PUSCH CRC, qcore InitialUE/NAS, registration/PDU
session, and bladeRF `TIME_PAST` count. If the low-rate strong retries decode,
continue through OTA data. If they remain CRC KO despite the expected 6 dB
lift, capture one bounded strong post-Msg3 waveform for native offline replay;
do not reopen qcore, PUCCH, or PRACH.
