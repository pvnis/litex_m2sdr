The UE detail log later reports out-of-sync at slot 3630, about 210 ms after
the initial Msg4; that is downstream of the 64 ms Msg4 failure and does not
explain the first missed transmissions.

CP2316's RRC-positive control used the same Msg4 geometry: one AL4/CCE0
C-RNTI DCI 1_0 and 22-PRB/TBS309 PDSCH. Geometry and scheduler semantics are
therefore not the splitter. Existing SI and RAR traces do not instrument
C-RNTI PDCCH candidate metrics, so the UE-side skip/CRC question is currently
unobserved.
