## Question

After two consecutive clean Msg3 CRC-OK runs stopped before RRC, can the UE
classify the initial Msg4 and retransmission as PDCCH threshold-skipped, PDCCH
CRC-KO, or DCI-decoded/PDSCH-KO without changing any receive threshold or
scheduler behavior?

## Patch

Baseline: the active [host-redacted] `cc_worker.cc` at
`a58ed1001191deb760724962f4cf47498da29e9777ce26a09e730074b5e945d8`, which
contains the current CP2008 trace lineage and does not restore the CP1978
tracking-state implementation.

The patch adds one default-off, environment-cached, row-capped C-RNTI trace to
`srsue/src/phy/nr/cc_worker.cc`:

- `PAVONIS_SRSUE_CRNTI_CANDIDATE_TRACE`
- `PAVONIS_SRSUE_CRNTI_CANDIDATE_TRACE_LIMIT` (default 256)
- `PAVONIS_SRSUE_CRNTI_CANDIDATE_TRACE_MIN_CORR` (default 0.20)

Rows are emitted only for `srsran_rnti_type_c` search/decode events. PDCCH rows
carry candidate EPRE/correlation, active thresholds, geometry, attempted/CRC
state, sync/CFO provenance, and candidate identity. PDSCH rows classify skip or
decode and include CRC, allocation, HARQ, EVM, CFO, and SNR metadata. The patch
does no sample scanning and writes no files.

## Verdict and next step

PASS. The diagnostic is default-off, cached, bounded, metadata-only, and
deployed without changing PDCCH/PDSCH thresholds, payloads, geometry, timing,
or scheduler behavior. Run one fresh frozen ladder attempt. Before interpreting
it, require all three values in `launch.env` and `result.env`. If Msg4 is
scheduled, join its exact new-transmission/retransmission slots to the C-RNTI
rows and classify threshold skip, attempted CRC-KO, or PDSCH CRC-KO. A run that
does not reach Msg3/Msg4 is supply-void for this question.
