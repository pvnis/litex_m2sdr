Msg4 PASS and local RRC Connected PASS. CP2328 answers the CP2323/CP2324

## Post-Msg3 PUSCH join

The standard gNB PUSCH log is joined to 53 exact UE runtime configurations and
their nearest bladeRF timed-TX events. Of 47 exact joined results, only the
3-PRB/TBS11 Msg3 at slot 5490 is CRC OK (`+4.8 dB`, status 0).

- TBS528/24 PRB: five real status-0 transmissions, all CRC KO, mean SINR
  `-29.04 dB` (`-35.2..-26.0`), mean digital RMS `0.34338`.
- TBS141/36 PRB: 35 real status-0 transmissions, all CRC KO, mean SINR
  `-28.31 dB` (`-41.3..-23.9`), mean digital RMS `0.29993`; six other exact
  rows have bladeRF `TIME_PAST`.
- The gNB logs 132 PUSCH results total; 85 later scheduled rows have no matching
  UE runtime configuration and are floor/non-transmission windows.

The same parser on CP2316 is the control. With essentially identical digital
RMS, its status-0 TBS528 result is `-6.3 dB` and 29 status-0 TBS141 results
average `-6.12 dB`. CP2328 therefore loses about 22 dB of post-Msg3 PUSCH
coherence without losing Msg3 power.

## Splitter

This is not a global UL path or power collapse. CP2328 PUCCH remains strong and
the adaptive format-1 bank validates 15 rows at mean EPRE `-35.283 dB`, mostly
with selected correction `+8 degrees/symbol, +90 degrees/subcarrier`. CP2316's
38 valid strong rows instead select `+4/-178` at mean `-35.715 dB`. The current
PUCCH bank masks a run-dependent symbol/subcarrier phase class that the PUSCH
path does not compensate.

The run-scale timing evidence points the same way. Dynamic non-PRACH offset is
`-231772` in CP2316 versus `-233338` in CP2328. Relative to Msg3, status-0 data
PUSCH bladeRF timestamps average `+360` nominal-lattice samples in CP2316 but
`+256` in CP2328, a roughly 104-sample class movement near or beyond the useful
CP budget. The exact sign/magnitude still requires a post-Msg3 raw waveform
join; do not [interface-redacted] a constant from these indirect metrics.
