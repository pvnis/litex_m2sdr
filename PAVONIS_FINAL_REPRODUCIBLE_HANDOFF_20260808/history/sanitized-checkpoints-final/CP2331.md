## Phase recovery

A linear frequency-domain phase correction across subcarriers recovers the
exact transport block without a symbol-phase correction:

| Correction | CRC | SINR dB | EVM | LDPC mean | payload prefix |
| --- | --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | --- |
| 0 deg/subcarrier | KO | -26.69 | 18.64 | 10 | zeros |
| +136.3 deg/subcarrier | OK | -1.94 | 1.0525 | 3 | `3d003f00...` |

Every integer point from `+111` through `+151 deg/subcarrier` passes CRC, and
all 121 fine points from `+130.0` through `+142.0` pass. The best sampled EVM
is at `+136.3`. Adding a symbol-phase ramp does not materially improve EVM;
the estimator absorbs that axis.

The same-run Msg3 is the decisive control. TTI9816 passes at zero phase with
SINR `-5.12 dB`, EVM `1.3778`, and payload
`[hex-value-redacted]`. Applying `+136.3 deg/subcarrier` destroys it
(`-30.05 dB`, EVM `21.21`, CRC KO). The defect therefore appears between
Msg3 and connected-mode PUSCH; it is not a static run-wide RF/channel phase
slope.

## TA handoff provenance

The exact UE TX timestamp join explains the transition. Relative to Msg3 at
slot9816/timestamp96635402, every exact post-Msg4 data PUSCH is displaced from
the 11520-sample slot lattice by:

```text
+317, +343, +343, +306, +305, +301 samples
```

That range is centered on the run's RAR TA command 52, which is exactly 312
samples at this geometry. The required `+136.3 deg/subcarrier` correction is
also in the same phase-wrap class as a roughly 312-sample step.

The source audit identifies the handoff boundary. `sync_sa` subtracts
`ta.get_sec()` when it queues each future worker context. RAR decoding can
update `ta_control` while an already-queued Msg3 worker retains the old value.
At transmit completion, `sync_sa::worker_end()` uses the queued timestamp and
contains an explicit disabled TODO for applying the current TA. Thus Msg3 and
later data can use different TA states even though the gNB's dynamic
non-PRACH offset has already consumed the RAR TA.

PASS, no RF. The active theory is now specific and testable: stale queued TA
at the RAR-to-Msg3 handoff creates the 312-sample connected-mode step. Next add
a default-off, metadata-only trace of queued TA versus worker-end TA; do not
change timing behavior until one live milestone run proves the mismatch.
