Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Patch

The current CP2332 srsUE trace baseline gains one new default-off switch:

`PAVONIS_SRSUE_TA_HANDOFF_APPLY_CURRENT`

At `worker_end()`, the code starts with the timestamp already adjusted by the TA
recorded in the queued worker context. When the switch is enabled and the
worker transmits, it computes `missing_ta = current_ta - queued_ta` and:

- subtracts a positive missing delta from the TX timestamp;
- adds the magnitude of a negative delta;
- does nothing when the delta is zero.

This avoids double-applying TA and handles both increasing and decreasing TA.
The existing bounded trace now records `apply_current` and `applied` on mismatch
rows. Environment parsing remains function-local static/env-once. With trace
and correction off, `ta.get_sec()`, timestamp mutation, and trace I/O are all
skipped; timing behavior is unchanged.

The flag is propagated through all four CP193 launch/result layers and the
CP1807 quoted child environment/result sidecar. The CP2335 wrapper enables
trace/correction/limit `1/1/64` over the otherwise frozen CP2330 shape.
