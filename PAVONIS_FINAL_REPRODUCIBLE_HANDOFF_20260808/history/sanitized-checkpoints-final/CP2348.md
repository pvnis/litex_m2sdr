## Shape and health

`PAVONIS_UL_SYMBOL_MSG3_TARGET_LATE_ARM=1` switch added. Manifest and sequence
proof show target capture/max/late-arm `1/8/1`, MCS9, and the private qcore

has zero Soapy timeouts, zero downlink-late rows, one historical 480-sample

so this is another good/early-pass downlink-class run after the descriptor fix.

## Capture verdict

The bounded writer saves eight complete targets, 14 symbols each (`112` CF32
files/manifest rows), with one arm marker, eight publications, eight
completions, and one limit marker. However, target index zero does not appear
until system slot 9973, and all eight targets are later temp-C-RNTI slot-3
allocations (`0x4605..0x460c`) from new random-access attempts. None of the four
post-arm TBS44 SecurityModeComplete grants enters the target table.

Source audit names the splitter: target publication exists in the RA scheduler
and `ue_fallback_scheduler`, while normal connected-mode PUSCH is built through
`ue_cell_grid_allocator::set_pusch_params()` and has no publication call. The
late arm is correct; the scheduler tap is incomplete. Replaying the saved files
would analyze unrelated Msg3s and is therefore not performed.

formed. This is metadata-only and must retain the existing capture/late-arm/cap

proofs before one frozen retry. Do not [interface-redacted] SecurityModeComplete timing, phase,
power, MCS, or decoder behavior before its exact shifted grid is on disk.
