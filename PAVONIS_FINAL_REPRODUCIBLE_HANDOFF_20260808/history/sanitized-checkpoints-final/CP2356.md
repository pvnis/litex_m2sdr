# CP2356 - RAR supply collapse is high-energy, descriptor-clean, and phase-linked

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Question

Why did the two CP2353 diagnostic runs stop before InitialContextSetup despite
clean host/TX health?

## Exact RAR join

CP2354 schedules six RAR PDCCH/PDSCH occasions. All six are present at the UE
with high EPRE `32.819..33.980`, not floor energy:

- slots `1004` and `2124`, both `mod160=44`, decode CRC OK at correlation
  `0.624570/0.900036`;
- the other four are threshold-skipped at correlation
  `0.056283..0.426606`.

CP2355 also schedules six RAR occasions and all six are high-energy at
`31.917..33.667` EPRE, but all are below the unchanged `0.5` gate:
`0.004964, 0.010330, 0.485481, 0.027133, 0.139965, 0.420551`.
Their phases are `mod160=41` or `43`.

The trace covers all exact scheduled rows in both runs; this is not a cap or
window-coverage artifact. Candidate CFO (`-5.8..+5.6 kHz`) and sync error
(`-6.0..+5.8 us`) do not separate decoded from skipped rows.

## TX-side join

- CP2355 FAPI emits the same valid RAR geometry on all six occasions: RA-RNTI
  `0x39`, AL4/CCE0, 37-bit payload `0300000000`, power profile `nr`,
  `powerControlOffsetSS=+6`, and power-sign compatibility on.
- The event-armed CP2354 and CP2355 worker traces each contain 65,536 rows over
  about 5.81 seconds. Both pass the descriptor gate: zero short non-EOB, late,
  dedup, or duplicate HAS_TIME-source rows.
- Both runs have zero Soapy deadline timeouts, downlink-late rows, errors, or
  stream deficit.

Thus the RAR hole is not missing scheduler/FAPI output, low power, the Soapy
descriptor branch, or an observable CFO/sync metadata class. It is the known
high-energy/low-correlation reactive-slot presentation defect below the clean
descriptor boundary.

The phase evidence has strengthened: CP2354 supplies two CRC-OK RAR DCIs at
`mod160=44`; CP2352's visible CRC-OK RAR candidates are at `47/49`; CP2124's
known pass is at `51`. CP2355's `41/43` class supplies none. Phase is not a
root-cause claim, but it is now a bounded scheduler-side supply discriminator.

## Verdict and next

PASS no-RF. Stop bare retries and do not lower the UE correlation threshold or
raise power. Audit `ra_scheduler.cpp` for a default-off diagnostic that skips
otherwise-valid candidate PDCCH slots until a configured `slot % 160` target,
while remaining inside the existing RAR response window. Target `47` first:
it has repeated CP2352 CRC-OK evidence and requires only a 3-6-slot delay from
the current `41/43/44` allocations, within CP2355's 14-slot window headroom.
Build/deploy no-RF with disabled-path and postponed-slot telemetry proof before
one RF diagnostic.
