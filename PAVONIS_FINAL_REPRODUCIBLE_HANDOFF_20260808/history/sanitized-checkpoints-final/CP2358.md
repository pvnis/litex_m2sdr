## Verdict and next

Valid negative RF discriminator. Phase `47` is neither causal nor sufficient:
it reproduces the same high-energy/low-correlation RAR hole despite clean FAPI,
descriptor, and TX health. Do not repeat phase targeting, lower the UE gate,
or change power. Return the selector to its default-off state outside this
diagnostic and compare the lower-TX presentation of exact RAR occasions between
the CP2352 UE-sighted run and CP2358, prioritizing existing bounded artifacts
before adding another live trace. The InitialContextSetup frontier remains
valid but is supply-blocked at UE RAR delivery.
