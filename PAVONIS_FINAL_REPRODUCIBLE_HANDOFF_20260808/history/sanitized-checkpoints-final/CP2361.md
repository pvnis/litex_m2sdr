## Ladder result

The four exact phase-47 RAR slots were `2287`, `3407`, `4527`, and `5647`.
All were high-energy at the UE (`32.528..33.758 dB` EPRE), but their PDCCH
correlations were `0.023229`, `0.021847`, `0.442003`, and `0.624587`.
Only slot 5647 crossed the unchanged 0.5 gate and decoded CRC OK.

The UE accepted that RAR with temporary C-RNTI `0x4604` and TA command 45.
The gNB then decoded Msg3 at slot 5653:

```text
PUSCH: rnti=0x4604 ... tbs=11 crc=OK iter=1.0 sinr=7.7dB
```

This is the first RAR-reaching run to judge the TA fixture and it validates the
CP2335/CP2337 compensation: Msg3 is aligned and healthy, not merely detected at
the edge.

The gNB created the UE and generated `RRCSetup`. At slot 5680 it transmitted a
9-byte MAC PDU containing only the contention-resolution CE. The UE decoded
the C-RNTI PDCCH (`corr=0.851017`, CRC OK) and PDSCH (CRC OK, TBS 9,
`12.074 dB` SNR), then printed `Random Access Complete`. This does **not** mean
the 297-byte RRCSetup payload was transmitted: it remained in `dl_bo=297`.
The grant's `k1=4` places its HARQ ACK at slot `568.4`. That exact slot was not
passed to the format-1 detector: the gNB emitted its only
`Discarded uplink slot. Ignoring processing.` warning for `[568.4]`. The full
format-1 phase bank was configured, but it never evaluated this ACK. The gNB's
`ra-ContentionResolutionTimer` then expired and no later C-RNTI PDSCH carried
the RRCSetup. CP2361 therefore reaches successful contention-resolution
reception but stops at the discarded uplink ACK slot before RRCSetup delivery.

## Lower-PHY capture verdict

The capture wrote 64 complete files of 11,520 complex CI16 samples each. It
started at slot 2607, wrapped the 10,240-slot system-slot counter, and ended at
2447. As a result, only current-cycle RAR slot 2287 is in the retained window;
the filenames for 3407/4527/5647 refer to the preceding cycle and are zero.
This is a cap/cycle-identity limitation, not file corruption.

The exact current-cycle blind slot is
`pavonis_dl_slot_capture_062_slot2287_port0.ci16`: 23,028 nonzero int16
components, maximum magnitude 24,414, and RMS 2,762.399. After direct
int16/32767 conversion, the validated RA-RNTI common-search harness decodes:

```text
slot=2287  ncce=0  AL=4  EPRE=+25.78  corr=1.000  CRC=OK
ra-rnti=0x0039 dci=1_0 f_alloc=0x30 t_alloc=0x0 mcs=0
```

The simultaneous live UE row for the same slot has EPRE `33.602 dB` but
correlation `0.023229` and does not attempt decode. The file harness's PDSCH
CRC KO is not evidence against the waveform: its RA path hardcodes symbols
`(1,13)`/TBS10 while the actual FAPI PDSCH is `(2,12)`/TBS9. The PDCCH result
is the valid discriminator.

## Verdict and next

CP2361 is a milestone. TA compensation is validated by Msg3 CRC OK at
`+7.7 dB`. A blind live RAR is ideal and semantically correct at the lower-PHY
boundary but badly decorrelated at the simultaneous UE, while descriptor and
Soapy health remain clean. The RAR damage is introduced after lower-PHY sample
generation: Soapy conversion/write, M2SDR driver/DMA/gateware/RF presentation,
or the UE receive path.

The immediate ladder frontier is the discarded PUCCH HARQ ACK slot after the
decoded contention-resolution CE. CP2328 is the positive control: its Msg4
grant at slot `551.7` produced a valid `ack=1` format-1 result at the expected
`k1=4` slot `552.1`. Before another RF run, extend the existing late-armed
metadata-only PUxCH ordering trace with PUCCH enqueue/task and discard events,
then join those to lower-PHY writes for the exact ACK slot. This decides
whether its shifted grid never arrives or arrives after the slot is retired.
In parallel, inspect existing bounded post-DMA/digital TX hooks for an
event-triggered exact RAR capture below Soapy; do not add a continuous dump or
retune the now-proven TA/PRACH shape.
