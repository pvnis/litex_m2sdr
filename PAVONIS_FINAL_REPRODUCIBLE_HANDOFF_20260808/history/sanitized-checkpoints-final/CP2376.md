## Change

`cp2376_prearmed_netlink_userplane_probe.sh` replaces the CP2373-CP2375 1 Hz
`sudo ip netns exec ... ip addr` polling loop.

The helper cheaply waits for `/var/run/netns/[interface-redacted]`, starts one blocking
`ip monitor address` process inside `[interface-redacted]`, and reacts only to the
`[ip-redacted]` netlink event. It then records `[interface-redacted]` counters, sends ten quiet
pings at 100 ms spacing to `[ip-redacted]`, and records packet deltas plus one
PASS/FAIL result. It never reads RF logs and does not repeatedly fork
namespace commands on the active UE host.
