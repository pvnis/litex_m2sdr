## Judgment

CP2385 is valid for health, downlink-class tally, PRACH acceptance, and RAR
supply. It is void for the Msg4-MCS2 question because no Msg4 was scheduled.
It also proves the low/scattered downlink class can still occur after the
descriptor fix even with a clean sparse descriptor trace, so "lottery
eliminated" remains unproven and the residual defect is below the traced Soapy
descriptor layer.

Take one exact fresh-stamp retry under the standing supply-scatter rule. Do not
change MCS, RF, thresholds, stage1, RAR, timing, TA, credentials, or packet
instrumentation. Require an actual MCS2 Msg4 schedule before judging the new
control.
