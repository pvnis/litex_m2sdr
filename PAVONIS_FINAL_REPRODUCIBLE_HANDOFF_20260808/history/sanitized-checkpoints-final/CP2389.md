uplink DCI, the accepted ConRes-only MCS2 policy, normal SRB0 MCS behavior, or

## Finding

The first focused test correctly failed because OCUDU's default Common
SearchSpace1 advertises only `{0, 0, 1, 0, 0}`: one AL4 candidate and no AL8
candidate. The control therefore has two explicit, coupled startup settings:

- `PAVONIS_GNB_FALLBACK_DL_DCI_AGGR_LEV=8` selects AL8 only at the fallback
  downlink common-PDCCH allocation. The fallback uplink allocation remains the
  source-default hardcoded AL4.
- The runner adds `cell_cfg.pdcch.common.ss1_n_candidates: [0, 0, 1, 1, 0]`,
  retaining the existing AL4 candidate and adding one AL8 candidate.

Both controls are default-off. `max_msg4_mcs` remains default9, the frozen
wrapper keeps `PAVONIS_GNB_MAX_CONRES_MCS=2`, and no gate, power, payload,
timing, RF, or decoder behavior changed.
