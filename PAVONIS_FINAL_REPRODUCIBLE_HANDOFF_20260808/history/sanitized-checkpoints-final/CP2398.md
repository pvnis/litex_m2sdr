## Result

CP2398 proves CP2397's detached-child handoff: the generated live harness
records the separate phone binary and
`PAVONIS_SOAPY_PHYSICAL_CHANNEL_OFFSET=1`. It then stops at the pre-UE TX
readback gate with `rc=22`, before the filtered ADB monitor starts.

The gate failure is an instrumentation semantic error, not a channel1 failure.
`radio_soapy_tx_stream.cpp` writes its sidecar by iterating the stream's
logical `nof_channels` from zero and directly labels that loop index. In a
one-channel stream its row is therefore always `0`; it is not the physical
M2SDR channel selected by `radio_soapy_impl.cpp`. CP2397's new expectation of
row1 was invalid and has been reverted to the certified row0 behavior.
