# CP2406: Physical TX1 tone path is live

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich
RF transmitted: yes, bounded controlled tone

## Result

CP2406 extends the proven CP2143 tone helper with an explicit `TX_CHANNEL`
parameter whose default remains channel 0. It then replays the unchanged
CP2143 `-2350000 Hz` CF32 tone vector through M2SDR physical TX channel 1 at
the CP2143-safe settings: `1842500000 Hz`, `11.52 Msps`, `7.68 MHz`, and
`5 dB` attenuation. The bladeRF independently captures RX1 and RX2 at
`1839650000 Hz`, `7.68 Msps`, `3.84 MHz`, gain 32, normal filter.

The bounded run completed with `overall_rc=0`:

| Port | Median tone SNR | RMS rel2048 | Peak component | Clipped |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| RX1 | `6.645837 dB` | `0.005917491` | `58` | `0` |
| RX2 | `14.252303 dB` | `0.007165579` | `65` | `0` |

RX2 is the stronger observer and its tone SNR is only `2.08 dB` below the
CP2143 antenna-side RX2 reference (`16.334740 dB`). The archived [host-redacted] log
records `TX_CHANNEL=1`; `test_play.py` reports playback on channel 1 and exits
normally. Its source applies the selected channel to sample rate, frequency,
gain, bandwidth, and `setupStream()`.

## Interpretation

The M2SDR physical TX1 output and antenna-side path are not dead. This closes
the physical-port/output fault raised by CP2404/CP2405. It does not prove that
the live NR waveform has adequate margin: CP2404's live gNB used the same
`5 dB` attenuation yet RX2 was indistinguishable from no-TX. The live defect is
therefore in live gNB channel1 waveform presentation or its effective NR
link/coherence margin, not basic TX1 selection or a disconnected output.

The next discriminator is a known PBCH-positive gNB waveform replayed through
the now-proven physical TX1 path. A decodable replay with a live-gNB failure
localizes the problem to live cadence/Soapy presentation; a replay failure
localizes it to channel1 OTA NR waveform margin. Do not involve the phone until
that split is resolved.
