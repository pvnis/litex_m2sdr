# CP2407: TX1 PBCH replay is decoder-negative

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich
RF transmitted: yes, bounded known-vector replay

## Result

CP2407 replays the preserved CP1481 live-gNB pre-DMA CF32 vector on physical
M2SDR TX1. The input hash is `00cfbb63754c...`; an immediate no-RF decode of
its first 2,000,000 samples returns PCI1, PBCH CRC OK, and the expected MIB.
The RF shape matches its historical pass geometry: TX `1842500000 Hz`,
`11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, attenuation 5, amplitude 1; bladeRF RX
`1842500000 Hz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain 36, normal filter. The only new
selection is `TX_CHANNEL=1`.

All replay, capture, fetch, and cleanup steps pass with `overall_rc=0`. Both
one-second captures are complete and unclipped:

| Port | RMS dBFS | Max | One-shot PBCH | 250 ms PBCH | 250 ms CSI PCI1 |
| --- | ---: | ---: | --- | ---: | ---: |
| RX1 | `-45.10` | `60` | KO | `0/847` | `0/847` |
| RX2 | `-36.09` | `131` | KO | `0/847` | `0/847` |

RX2 receives about 9 dB more total power than RX1, but no window contains a
decoder-visible PCI1 SSB.

## Interpretation

The known PBCH-good baseband vector does not produce decodable PBCH through
the direct-CF32 TX1 replay path. This is consistent with insufficient
channel1 OTA NR waveform margin, but it is not yet decisive: CP1482's
historical pass used the patched CS16 continuous helper, while CP2407 uses
`test_play.py` direct CF32. The replay method therefore needs a byte-identical
physical-TX0/cabled control before CP2407 can be attributed to channel1.

Next rerun the exact CP2407 command with only `TX_CHANNEL=0`. A channel0 PBCH
pass proves the helper and localizes CP2407 to channel1 OTA margin; a channel0
failure invalidates this direct-CF32 method as the discriminator and requires
the historical CP1166 CS16 helper on both channels. Keep the phone parked.
