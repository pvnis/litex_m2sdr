## Verdict

The remote power branch is exhausted. CP2425 is slightly lower than CP2417's
ATT5/+6 sweep median (`-18.239` versus `-18.026 dBFS`) and slightly lower than
CP2424's ATT0/unmodified median (`-18.167 dBFS`); neither the extra hardware
drive nor coherent SSB boost creates an observable scheduled SSB or phone cell.
The controller, core subscriber, phone RAT/selection state, channel mapping,
SDR output, and software waveform controls have independent passing proofs.

The prerequisite for a real-phone registration baseline is now a physical
channel1 Band-3 coupling improvement. After that change, first rerun the
partial-write-safe CP2409 TX1-to-RX2 PBCH control at gain56 and require a
decode-positive result. Only then rerun the standard CP2401 phone baseline;
do not compensate by lowering decoder thresholds or adding more software
amplitude.
