TX2 result: the isolated CP1166 helper used the explicitly selected source-tree
module, not the rejected `/usr/local` duplicate.

## Channel-mode finding

The source audit found a separate deterministic defect. `setupStream()` places
single-channel TX2 samples in DMA frame channel B and selects AD9361 TX2, but
`activateStream()` writes `CSR_AD9361_CH_EN=0x1` for every one-channel stream.
Gateware defines bit 0 as channel A and bit 1 as channel B. Thus TX2-only data
occupies channel B while channel B is disabled. This directly predicts the
observed CP2406/CP2409 split: a full-scale tone can remain visible through
leakage, while the 22.5 dB-backoff OFDM vector falls roughly 25 dB below the
working TX0 result.

Next checkpoint: minimally derive the gateware channel mask from the selected
stream channels, prove channel0 and dual-channel behavior are unchanged, build
and deploy with backup discipline, then rerun the known CP1166 TX2 waveform.
