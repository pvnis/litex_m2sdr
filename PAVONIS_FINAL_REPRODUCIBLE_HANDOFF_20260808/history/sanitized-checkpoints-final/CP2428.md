## Source-level explanation

The CP2427 mask hypothesis was incomplete. The AD9361 gateware PHY explicitly
states that 1R1T accepts and emits only the IA/QA half of each frame; `tx_count`
never reaches the IB/QB cases in that mode. The Soapy driver selected analog
TX2 correctly, but still interleaved channel-1 samples into the frame's IB/QB
lane. Changing only `CH_EN` to channel B therefore enabled data that the 1R1T
PHY never presents to the RFIC.

The correct abstraction is:

- in 1R1T, the sole selected Soapy channel always maps to digital frame lane A;
  the AD9361 `rx1tx1_mode_use_{tx,rx}_num` selects physical RF channel 1 or 2;
- in 2R2T, Soapy channel 0/1 maps to frame lane A/B respectively.

This applies symmetrically to TX interleave and RX deinterleave. Next implement
that four-site lane mapping and restore the single-channel gateware mask to
`0x1`; channel0 and dual-channel behavior remain unchanged. Then repeat this
same bounded replay.
