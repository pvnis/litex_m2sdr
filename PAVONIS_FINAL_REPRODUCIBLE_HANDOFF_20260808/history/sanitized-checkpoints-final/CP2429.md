# CP2429: One-channel frame-lane fix passes the no-RF gate

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich
RF transmitted: no

## Change

The LiteX Soapy stream path now separates API/RF channel identity from the
digital frame lane used in 1R1T:

- a one-channel TX stream interleaves its sole buffer into frame lane A;
- a one-channel RX stream deinterleaves frame lane A into its sole buffer;
- a two-channel stream retains channel 0 to lane A and channel 1 to lane B;
- `CSR_AD9361_CH_EN` is restored to `0x1` for 1R1T and remains `0x3` for 2R2T.

The AD9361's `rx1tx1_mode_use_tx_num` and `rx1tx1_mode_use_rx_num` continue to
choose physical RF channel 1 or 2 independently. Four data-copy sites were
changed: old/new-buffer RX deinterleave and old/new-buffer TX interleave. No
timing, descriptor, gain, frequency, gNB, or gateware source changed.
