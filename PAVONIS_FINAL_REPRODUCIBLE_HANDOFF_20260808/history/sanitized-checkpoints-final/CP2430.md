## Verdict

The apparent TX2/OTA link-budget failure was a deterministic LiteX Soapy
1R1T packing bug, not insufficient M2SDR radiated power. In one-channel mode,
the driver used physical channel index 1 as DMA frame lane B even though the
1R1T PHY consumes only lane A; analog TX2 selection is a separate AD9361
control. Mapping the sole buffer to frame lane A restores the structured NR
waveform immediately.

The earlier CP2406 full-scale tone was leakage from the incorrectly packed
lane, explaining why it looked healthy while ordinary OFDM failed. CP2409,
CP2410, CP2424, and CP2425 are now historical pre-fix channel1 results, not
evidence that sub-meter OTA lacks power.

Next establish an unclipped gain36 TX2 baseline with this module, then apply
the same source/module to the frozen channel1 live-gNB path and resume phone
cell search/registration. The RX-side symmetric lane fix should also be kept;
it corrects one-channel physical RX2 presentation by the same framing rule.
