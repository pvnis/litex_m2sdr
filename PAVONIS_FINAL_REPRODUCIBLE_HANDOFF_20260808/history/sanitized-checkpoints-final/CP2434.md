## Verdict

The 1R1T lane fix is necessary and proven by CP2432, but it does not by itself
make this live-gNB phone run visible to the handset. The next discriminator is
not another phone-setting or power sweep: capture this exact live TX2 shape on
bladeRF RX2 at gain36 and run the validated PBCH/CSI classifier. A decode-
positive independent capture would move the blocker to handset acquisition or
cell selection; a decode-negative capture would prove the helper-to-live
cadence/presentation gap remains on TX2 despite the corrected lane.
