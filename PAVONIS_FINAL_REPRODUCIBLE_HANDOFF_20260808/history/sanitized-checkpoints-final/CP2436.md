## Source audit

The current fixed source is
`19cb6246f938f1935a316e87a7ab221ac7eae3255c9b4c963003c2aaaa78d409`.
Both CP2432B and CP2435 enter the same `writeStream()` interleave sites. For a
one-channel stream, both the existing-remainder and fresh-buffer branches map
the sole logical buffer to frame lane A before `releaseWriteBuffer()`.

`releaseWriteBuffer()` queues the already framed `StreamPacket`. The TX worker
copies `pkt->samples * frameChannels * bytesPerComplex` from that packet into
the acquired DMA payload and zero-fills only the unused tail. It does not
reselect a channel or repack lane B. `_dmaReleaseWrite()` then writes the timed
header and timestamp before exposing the complete payload to DMA. Therefore
the CP2429 fix is active on both helper and live paths; there is no hidden live-
only channel packing branch.

The OCUDU stop summary's one `480`-sample positive partial return is recovered:
final `stream_deficit=0`, no timeout/error, and it does not appear as a short
driver descriptor in the available summary window. It remains a watch item but
does not explain the captured PBCH failure by itself.

CP2432B's helper calls `writeStream(..., flags=0, timeNs=0)` throughout and is
PBCH-positive. Thus the remaining controlled split is timed metadata/live chunk
formation, not the worker copy or physical lane.
