## Verdict

The all-timed caller plus HAS_TIME-remainder-suppression interaction is not the
live PBCH failure. The same failure survives as a one-anchor continuous stream.
Together with CP2438's exact-metadata pass and CP2432B's continuous-helper pass,
the remaining live-only variable is real-time OCUDU producer cadence and its
buffering depth, including whether the current 16-packet worker pool leaves too
little headroom to absorb scheduler jitter after the initial anchor.

Do not change metadata policy, power, gain, lane selection, phone settings, or
gateware next. Audit historical worker-pool/ring experiments and quantify the
live versus helper enqueue-rate/headroom difference before selecting one
bounded buffering/cadence discriminator.
