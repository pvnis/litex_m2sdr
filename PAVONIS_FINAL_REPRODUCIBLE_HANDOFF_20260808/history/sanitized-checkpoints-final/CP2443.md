# CP2443: Cadence history and time-offset control selection

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Closed candidate: worker-pool depth

CP2174-CP2177 already built, deployed, and runtime-proved a TX-only worker-pool
override. Pool64 was active as `64/64/64` for packet pool/work FIFO/free FIFO,
but improved startup loss by only two to three descriptor equivalents versus
pool16 and did not remove timeouts/partial loss. That branch explicitly closed
queue depth and prohibited raising it further. It is not repeated here.

## Historical cadence join

| Checkpoint | Presentation | TX offset | Downlink result |
| --- | --- | ---: | --- |
| CP1575 | native continuous, all-timed off | 1 ms | raw TRACK pass, `33/69` PBCH |
| CP1666 | native continuous, all-timed off | 1 ms | SIB1/RACH config, seven Msg1 |
| CP2442 | native continuous, all-timed off | 30 ms | RX2 `0/847`, phone no-cell |

CP1658 shows that 30 ms plus all-timed could pass historically, so 30 ms is not
declared universally invalid. However, after CP2442 closes all-timed policy,
the exact historical one-anchor pass control is 1 ms. This is a binary
restoration discriminator, not a lead sweep or queue-depth retry.

The phone-only run stops before any UE uplink, so changing the TX offset cannot
confound PRACH/Msg3 compensation in this measurement. UL timing remains parked
until downlink PBCH/cell acquisition returns.
