# CP2444: Phone-run TX time-offset runtime proof

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich  
RF transmitted: no

## Change

CP2395 now derives a validated expected TX offset from `TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS`
(default `30000000`) and rejects non-numeric values. Before declaring gNB
propagation ready, a dedicated [host-redacted] proof requires:

- live gNB `/proc/$pid/environ` contains
  `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS=<expected>`;
- Soapy readback contains
  `env_M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS=<expected>`;
- the proof emits `PHONE_GNB_TX_TIME_OFFSET_PROOF=PASS`.

The proof has its own fresh-stamp artifact and feeds `PROPAGATION_RC`, so a
mismatch prevents phone monitoring/capture from being accepted. Existing
all-timed and physical-channel proofs remain in force.
