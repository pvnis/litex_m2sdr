## Historical correction

CP2442's packaged readback proves it ran at `[date-redacted] ns`, not the nominal
30 ms in its path label and report. CP1666's successful 1 ms one-anchor control
predates the deadline-write policy and has clean release/timeout health. The
true restoration shape is therefore 1 ms, all-timed off, and deadline-write
off, not CP2446's 1 ms plus deadline-write-on combination.
