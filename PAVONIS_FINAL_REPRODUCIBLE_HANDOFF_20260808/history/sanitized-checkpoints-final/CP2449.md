Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Branch decision

CP1695-CP1715 already exhaust timeout retry, carry, stale-drop, and deadline
policies. They leave deficits, create SI-era backlog/correlation loss, or
abandon most of the stream. CP2448 therefore does not justify another
backpressure policy.

The next discriminator uses the healthy 20 ms one-anchor live shape and the
existing bounded lower-PHY capture. With 30 kHz SCS and 20 ms SSB periodicity,
scheduled SSB slots are on global `slot % 40 == 0`. Capture port0 remains the
correct logical lower-PHY port; the proven phone Soapy mapping subsequently
routes logical port0 to physical M2SDR channel1/TX2.
