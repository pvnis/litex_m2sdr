## Capture-axis void

Configuration and binary-capability gates prove
`PAVONIS_DL_SLOT_CAPTURE=1`, `mod=40`, `rem=0`, `max=64`, `port=0`, but zero
files were captured. The sequence log gives the exact reason: phone mode uses
`PAVONIS_GNB_ONLY_STOP_BEFORE_UE=1`; after its 180-second hold it deliberately
logs that the shared ARM remains untouched and stops the gNB. The existing
lower-PHY capture only arms in the normal UE-start branch.

CP2450 is therefore valid as another clean external negative and void for the
intended lower-PHY comparison. It does not answer whether the SSB is clean
before Soapy.
