## Verdict

The current phone gNB generates a valid, stable SSB at the lower-PHY boundary.
Phone configuration, SSB scheduling, MIB content, and current lower-PHY sample
generation are exonerated. The live corruption is introduced after the
lower-PHY capture point: OCUDU Soapy chunk/conversion, LiteX plugin/worker/DMA,
gateware/RF presentation, or the external receiver.

The exact helper already passes through the current plugin/gateware/RF path, so
the next boundary is the existing capped Soapy-input CF32 dump after OCUDU
chunking/conversion. Capture about 21 ms without a metadata file, decode it
offline, and keep the 64-slot lower-PHY capture plus external RX2 for the same
