## Verdict

The single timed anchor followed by indefinite untimed continuation is not the
live failure mechanism. Removing the anchor entirely preserves clean transport
health but does not recover the air waveform. CP1564's historical recovery
therefore does not transfer to the current live TX2 path.

CP2458's exact DMA proof covers only the first 21 ms after stream start, while
the independent capture starts after runtime propagation gates. The next split
is same-stream age: use the existing bounded plugin-input and post-framing DMA
hooks behind their existing shared late-arm file, trigger them near the
external capture, and compare/decode that later 21 ms window. No timing,
backpressure, power, waveform, or gateware change is justified first.

An initial fresh-stamp launch was blocked locally by sandboxed SCP before
either host or RF; it is infrastructure-void and not a datapoint. The reported
run uses stamp `[date-redacted]CP2462_PHONE_LIVE_FORCECONT1_RX2_RF`.
