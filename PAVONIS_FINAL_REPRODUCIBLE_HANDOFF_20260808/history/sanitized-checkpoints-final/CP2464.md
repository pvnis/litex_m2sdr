## Decode and health

The late-armed plugin input decodes PCI1 PBCH CRC OK. Reconstructed DMA lane0
also decodes PCI1 PBCH CRC OK with CSI SNR `+60.3 dB`, RMS `-26.38 dBFS` and
max1525. Therefore structured SSB content remains correct near the external
capture, not only during CP2458's first 21 ms.

TX health remains valid: zero Soapy timeout/downlink-late/release-late and zero
final stream deficit. One 480-sample partial return is fully recovered. Worker
stop has one initial timed anchor, `2087463` untimed continuations, and zero
late/underflow/acquire errors.

External RX2 is still blind at `-32.23 dBFS`, max679, unclipped, with `0/847`
PBCH/CSI. All 46 phone rows remain no-cell.

## Verdict

Age-dependent live userspace framing or ring-slot contents are closed. The
exact buffer in `_dmaReleaseWrite()` immediately before
`LITEPCIE_IOCTL_MMAP_DMA_READER_UPDATE` is clean both at startup and near the
failed external capture. The remaining live/helper split is publication and
consumption below that snapshot: kernel reader occupancy/order, gateware DMA
consumption, or underflow behavior that current terminal counters do not expose.
