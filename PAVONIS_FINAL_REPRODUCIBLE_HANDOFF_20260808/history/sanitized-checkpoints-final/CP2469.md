## Kernel finding

The zero-copy TX publication model is not a conventional per-descriptor
producer gate. `litepcie_dma_reader_start()` loads all 256 descriptors, sets
`PCIE_DMA_READER_TABLE_LOOP_PROG_N` to 1, clears counters, and enables the
reader. The `LITEPCIE_IOCTL_MMAP_DMA_READER_UPDATE` handler only assigns
`chan->dma.reader_sw_count = m.sw_count`; it does not update the FPGA descriptor
table, stop the reader at `sw_count`, or otherwise make one slot newly visible.
Hardware continuously cycles the complete mmap ring once enabled.

This corrects the wording around CP2464's snapshot: the ioctl is a software
flow-accounting update, not the physical publication event. CP2468 still proves
the live producer remains 143..248 published buffers ahead of the sampled
hardware count, so steady-state producer starvation is closed. What remains
open is whether the passing helper establishes or maintains materially different
looped-ring distance/startup state, or whether the difference lies after DMA
consumption in the header/timed-TX path.
