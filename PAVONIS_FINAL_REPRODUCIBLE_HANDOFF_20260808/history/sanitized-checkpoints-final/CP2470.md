## Verdict

Producer lead, ring fullness, host publication gaps, and descriptor size/order
do not explain the live/helper split. The passing helper can run much closer to
the looped reader than live without corruption. The repeated HAS_TIME metadata
is the remaining visible difference in this pair, but it is not yet causal:
the current CP2432B zero-HAS_TIME continuous helper also passed without a worker
trace.

Next trace that zero-HAS_TIME continuous helper on the current module and TX2/
RX2. If it remains PBCH-positive with zero timed descriptors, HAS_TIME/timed
arbiter semantics are closed and the split reduces to how live real-time data
is presented to the looped ring versus a fast continuous player. Do not change
kernel mode, reader priming, source, waveform, or power first.
