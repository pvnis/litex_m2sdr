# CP2471: Zero-HAS_TIME helper closes timed metadata and ring depth

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Control

CP2471 replays the preserved CP1481 live-gNB waveform through the established
CP2432B zero-HAS_TIME continuous helper on current LiteX module `3dc9cd8e...`.
The geometry is TX2, 11.52 Msps, 10 MHz bandwidth, 1842.5 MHz center,
attenuation 5 dB, amplitude 1.0, 90 loops; bladeRF RX2 uses gain 36. The
existing metadata-only worker trace is enabled from stream start and capped at
120,000 rows.

The RF control passes strongly:

- one-shot PCI1 PBCH CRC OK, CSI `+17.0 dB`, RMS `-29.76 dBFS`, max 585
- first 250 ms: `426/847` PBCH CRC-OK PCI1 and `284/847` PCI1 CSI
- best CSI `+18.6 dB`
- wrapper rc0 and both hosts cleanly idle after the run

## Worker trace

The CP2047 gate passes over all 120,000 rows. Every descriptor carries exactly
1022 samples. There are zero HAS_TIME, short non-EOB, late, deduplicated,
handle-gap, or ring-slot-mismatch rows.

The producer lead (`handle + 1 - hardware reader count`) spans 1..256 buffers
with mean 189.764. Nine rows reach eight buffers or less, 6,697 reach 128 or
less, and the largest wall-clock publication gap is 12.007 ms. This passing
helper is less deeply queued and has materially larger publication gaps than
the failing CP2468 live gNB, whose late window stayed at 143..248 buffers and
had a 2.281 ms maximum gap.

## Timestamp provenance

CP2468's live trace records advancing source/release `timeNs` metadata while
CP2471 records zero, despite both presenting zero HAS_TIME descriptors. This is
not presently a physical splitter. In the live CP2074B source,
`_dmaReleaseWrite()` writes a zero DMA-header timestamp whenever HAS_TIME is
clear, and the timed-TX gateware takes untimed descriptors directly to STREAM
without comparing their timestamp. Both runs use that same deployed module.

## Verdict

The passing zero-HAS_TIME control closes timed-anchor semantics, HAS_TIME
consumption, descriptor size/order, producer lead, ring fullness, and gross
host publication cadence as explanations for the live/helper RF split. Live
still fails externally despite exact plugin-to-DMA bytes and healthy late-run
descriptors; helper passes through the same module and physical TX2/RX2 path.

The next discriminator is a startup-capped worker trace on the unchanged live
gNB shape, contrasted with CP2471's handle-0 trace. It should test reader-enable,
initial ring fill, and first-descriptor ordering without changing waveform,
power, kernel mode, or source. Before RF, preserve an explicit empty worker-arm
override through the wrapper and prove the trace starts with the stream.
