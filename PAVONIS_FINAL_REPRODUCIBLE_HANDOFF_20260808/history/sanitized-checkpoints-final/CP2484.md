# CP2484: Startup self-RX passes while external RX2 fails four seconds later

## Runtime gates

The corrected diagnostic executes as intended on the phone/channel1 path:

- physical RX2 readback `1839650000 Hz`, `11.52 Msps`, `7.68 MHz`, gain
  `59 dB` automatic;
- exactly 2,880,000 CF32 samples and 5,648 metadata rows;
- zero RX overflow, chunk overflow, or timestamp regression;
- exactly 256 worker rows, reader enable at handle/sequence127;
- two startup handle jumps skipping 25 handles;
- zero Soapy timeout, downlink-late, or release-late lines.

The run is intentionally detection-void under CP1894. Phone/PRACH/RAR/Msg3
outcomes are not ladder evidence.

## Same-device result

The established CP826/CP1385 decoders see coherent PCI1 PBCH on M2SDR RX2:

- targeted: `6/110` PBCH CRC OK, best CSI SNR `+13.5 dB`;
- aligned 250 ms sweep: `92/847` PBCH CRC OK, best CSI SNR `+13.7 dB`.

Hits extend through the capture, including windows near 240 ms. The live TX is
therefore coherent after the LitePCIe reader, gateware, AD9361 TX, and
same-device RF coupling during the startup 250 ms. Persistent reader/gateware
corruption is closed, and the two bounded-trace handle jumps are not sufficient
to destroy the waveform.

## External result and timing caveat

The simultaneous-run bladeRF RX2 capture remains weak and undecodable:
`-32.53 dBFS`, max `631`, `0/847` PBCH and `0/847` PCI1 CSI. The phone has no
private PLMN or service.

These captures are not the same window. Self-RX starts at
`[date-redacted]`; the external capture log starts at `[date-redacted]`, about
4.105 seconds later. CP2484 therefore closes persistent TX corruption but does
not distinguish late-onset TX degradation from an external bladeRF/path issue.
