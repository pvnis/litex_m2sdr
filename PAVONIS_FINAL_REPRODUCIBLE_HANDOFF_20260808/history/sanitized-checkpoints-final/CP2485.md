# CP2485: Late same-window self-RX discriminator gated no-RF

## Purpose

CP2484 proves startup self-RX coherence but leaves a roughly 4.1 second gap to
the failed external capture. Arm the same-device capture immediately before
the bladeRF capture to distinguish late-onset TX degradation from the external
receive leg.

## Change

CP1807 now honors an explicit RX append arm while preserving its historical
per-stamp default when unset. The CP2480 runner keeps immediate append as its
default but permits an explicit `0`. A dedicated late wrapper sets immediate0
and gives both the RX append and CP2404's pre-capture probe one identical fresh
arm path. CP2404 already verifies that path in the live gNB environment and
touches it immediately before starting the bladeRF capture.

## No-RF proofs

- All three scripts pass `bash -n`.
- Unset CP1807 arm expands to the historical generated path.
- Explicit `/tmp/cp2485-explicit.arm` survives unchanged.
- The RF gate exits rc2 when approval is absent.
