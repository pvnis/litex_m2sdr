# CP2489: Exact overlap achieved; live external RX2 remains negative

## Validity and timing

The absolute-time gate worked. [host-redacted] touched the self-RX arm `0.156 ms` after
the target, bladeRF started `0.290 ms` after target, and the first M2SDR append
row arrived `0.123 ms` after bladeRF start. The measured receiver intervals
overlap for `123.671963 ms`.

The run is intentionally detection-void because full M2SDR RX append is on.
Controller propagation, monitor, fetch, host-state, and packaging gates pass.
The controller's `rc=1` is its established no-cell result, not a package or RF
health failure.

## Same-window result

Within the first 120 ms of proven overlap, M2SDR self-RX decodes PCI1 PBCH in
`11/11` Pattern-A windows. CSI SNR is `+8.6` to `+12.8 dB`. The append has
2.88M samples, 5,648 rows, and zero overflows or timestamp regressions.

External bladeRF RX2 has `0/422` PBCH and zero PCI1 CSI windows over its filled
125 ms, at `-34.25 dBFS` and max `473`. TX health is clean: zero timeout,
write deficit, stream deficit, downlink-late, release-late, or error counts.

## Instrument caveat

The first X2 implementation passed 2.88M to `bladerf_sync_rx`, but X2 counts
aggregate channel samples. It therefore filled only 1.44M samples per channel
and returned after about 124 ms; the written RX2 file's second half is zero.
The filled first half is real and fully overlaps self-RX, so the synchronization
mechanism and simultaneous negative are useful. The new X2 capture instrument
has not yet passed a known-good helper positive control, so it is premature to
make the external-path split authoritative.

## Next

Correct the X2 aggregate count while retaining 2.88M samples per channel and
strict-build/no-RF gate it. Then run the corrected instrument against the
known-good current-module helper shape. A helper decode validates the
instrument and promotes the CP2489 simultaneous split; a helper failure means
the X2 capture implementation, not the live TX path, needs further work.
