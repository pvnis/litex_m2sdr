# CP2490: X2 aggregate-count fix passes no-RF

CP2489 showed that `bladerf_sync_rx` counts aggregate X2 complex samples. The
capture now requests `2 * samples_per_channel`, retains both documented lanes,
and writes exactly the requested per-channel count to separate RX1 and RX2
files. It also rejects a count that would overflow the unsigned aggregate.

The change is confined to the diagnostic capture utility and its controller;
no gNB, Soapy, gateware, RF gain, power, timing policy, or phone setting
changed.
