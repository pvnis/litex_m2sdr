# CP2492: X2 capture fails the known-good helper control

The corrected instrument produced full `46,080,000`-byte RX1 and RX2 files.
Capture ran from `1784190399.796649321` to `1784190400.795380071`; helper loops
span `1784190398.084306` through `1784190412.556742`, so a missed TX window is
ruled out.

Helper mechanics are clean: 96 loops, 192M samples, 386,688 writes, zero
errors/short writes, and zero driver late/underflow events.

RX2 is `-30.92 dBFS`, max `454`, but `0/847` PBCH and zero PCI1 CSI. RX1 is
lower at `-40.69 dBFS`, max `139`, and one-shot PBCH-negative. Thus channel
swapping does not rescue the result.

## Verdict

The X2 instrument fails its positive control despite complete files and proven
overlap with a healthy helper. CP2489 remains provisional and cannot be used
to condemn the external leg. The synchronization mechanism is still useful,
but X2 streaming/configuration is an observer until validated.

The next discriminator is the established single-channel CP2459 capture shape
on the same helper. A pass isolates X2 mode as the defect; a failure means the
helper or physical path shifted and must be rebaselined first.
