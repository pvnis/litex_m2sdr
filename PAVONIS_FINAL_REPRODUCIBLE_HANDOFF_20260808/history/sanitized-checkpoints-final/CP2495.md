# CP2495: Absolute-time SISO RX1 helper control passes

The CP2494 absolute-time SISO instrument passes its mandatory positive control
on the unchanged CP2493/CP2459 helper path. This promotes it as the trusted
instrument for the next simultaneous live-gNB, M2SDR self-RX, and external
bladeRF comparison.

- Capture semantics: `BLADERF_CHANNEL_RX(0)`, `BLADERF_RX_X1`, SC16 Q11.
- Geometry: `1842500000 Hz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `36`.
- Capture: `11,520,000` complex samples, `46,080,000` bytes, unclipped.
- Scheduled start error: `+13.464416 ms`; actual capture duration
  `997.513373 ms`.
- Helper: unchanged current-module timed TX2/ATT5 shape, 96 loops and 192M
  samples, with zero errors or short writes.
- One-shot RX1: PCI1 PBCH CRC OK, CSI `+18.3 dB`, RMS `-40.26 dBFS`, max
  component `161`.
- First 250 ms aligned sweep: `424/847` PBCH CRC-OK PCI1 windows,
  `342/847` PCI1 CSI windows, best CSI `+19.0 dB`.

This is the same strong class as CP2493 (`400/847`) and CP2459 (`406/847`).
The absolute-time wait and single-channel capture do not create the X2
observer failure seen in CP2492. CP2489 remains non-authoritative because it
used X2, but the synchronization concept can now be repeated with this proven
RX1 instrument.

Next build and no-RF gate a simultaneous live phone-gNB diagnostic using one
shared future epoch for the SISO RX1 capture and late-armed M2SDR self-RX.
Only after measured overlap may their PBCH results be compared.
