# CP2508: On-air SIB1 is selectable; phone policy remains the pre-PRACH blocker

The CP2506 pre-PRACH stop is not explained by a barred or unsuitable Pavonis
cell. This no-RF audit verifies the exact live configuration and decodes the
exact 80-byte CP2498 over-air SIB1 transport block with OCUDU's own ASN.1
implementation.

The CP2506 generated YAML has no cell-selection override, so OCUDU resolves its
documented defaults. The direct air decode independently confirms:

- `q-RxLevMin = -70 dBm`;
- `q-QualMin = -20 dB`, present;
- PLMN [network-id-redacted], TAC `1`, NCI `5439488`;
- `cellReservedForOperatorUse = notReserved`;
- normal connection-failure control (`n1`, `s30`, offset `1`).

CP2498 already decoded the same signal's MIB as `cellBarred=notBarred` with
intra-frequency reselection allowed. CP2506 Android identity agrees exactly on
PLMN/TAC/NCI. Therefore no broadcast access/suitability field explains why the
phone remains searching without Msg1, and RF/PRACH/RAR/qcore/NAS [interface-redacted] remains
out of scope at this boundary.

The remaining evidence points to phone policy. CP2502 found the active OnePlus
00101 carrier bundle advertises NR availability `[1]` (NSA only), while the
platform default is `[1,2]` (NSA and SA). The modem can discover the n3 SA cell
under NR-only but does not initiate random access. Production engineering
routes are not a sanctioned remote workaround: the exact vendor APK hash is
`ddf23088...`, and `activity_sw`, `service_sw`, `shell_command_sw`, and
`engineer_order_sw` are all `false`. No privilege or lock bypass was attempted.

Android's standard `NETWORK_OPERATOR_SETTINGS` activity resolves to
`com.android.phone/.simsettings.OplusSimSettingsImpl`, and current network
selection is automatic. The screen is presently behind a showing keyguard, so
UI inspection/automation stops there. Once the operator unlocks the phone, the
next bounded discriminator is to inspect that standard UI and, if exposed,
manually select the visible `SENS`/00101 network during the exact frozen
NR-only run, with automatic selection and the full RAT mask restored afterward.
