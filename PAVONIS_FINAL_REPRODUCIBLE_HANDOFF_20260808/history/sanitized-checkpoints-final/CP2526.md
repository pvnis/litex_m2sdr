# CP2526: OxygenOS overlay provenance rules out a simple PLMN change

The installed CarrierConfig base and three overlay APKs were pulled read-only
and hash/integrity checked. A new strict-Werror binary-XML decoder reconstructs
the two relevant compiled XML resources.

The provenance is exact:

- `CarrierConfigOverlay.21341U.apk` applies
  `carrier_nr_availabilities_int_array=[1]` in `operator="ALL"`.
- Its `operator="TEST SIM"`, MCC/MNC00101 block enables IMS features but does
  not override NR availability, so it inherits the device-wide `[1]`.
- `OplusCarrierConfig.apk` has common default `[1,2]`, but the higher device
  overlay replaces it with `[1]`.
- The only device-overlay block restoring `[1,2]` is a 22-PLMN Jio whitelist.
  Those are real operator identities and are not a suitable private-lab
  workaround.

Therefore reprogramming the SIM from 00101 to another nearby test PLMN would
still hit `operator="ALL"` and inherit NSA-only `[1]`; do not spend an on-site
visit on that alone. All three overlays are enabled static overlays with
`mIsMutable=false`. The remaining valid choices are a different handset whose
stock CarrierConfig permits SA for the lab SIM, or an explicitly approved
bootloader/firmware change to this phone's device overlay.
