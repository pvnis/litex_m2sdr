# CP2552 - Phone NR-only plus n3 RF controller gate

## Question

Can CP2551's acknowledged n3 scan restriction be integrated into the frozen
phone RF controller without weakening restoration or changing the gNB shape?

## Method

- Stayed no-RF.
- Added a new controller that changes only the phone policy step after frozen
  gNB propagation: structural `RadioInfo` NR-only, then exact
  NGRAN/radio-band-3 selection through the CP2551 helper.
- Cleanup clears system-selection channels first, confirms/returns automatic
  selection, and restores the structural full-RAT choice and both mode keys.
- Added a ten-second modem settle and bounded full-mode retry to make an
  immediate early-exit cleanup reliable. The normal RF path already has a
  substantially longer apply-to-restore interval.
- Ran the combined apply/restore gate through the established
  `ssh_pexpect_run.py` path with an explicit timeout. Earlier direct-SSH
  attempts were tooling-void because the remote command was terminated before
  the immediate restore settled; explicit recovery passes were performed
  after each attempt.
- Verified the outer wrapper rejects execution with the approval flag unset
  and creates no run artifact under the fresh closed-gate stamp.

## Result

- Apply: app-driven `NR only`, live RAT `NR`, mode6 `23`, and n3 setter
  `callback=true accepted=true`.
- Restore: empty-list clear `callback=true accepted=true`, selection mode `1`,
  full RAT set including NR, `preferred_network_mode6=33`, and
  `oplus_user_preferred_network_mode6=33`.
- `CP2552_COMBINED_NO_RF_GATE=PASS`.
- `CP2552_OUTER_CLOSED_GATE=PASS`.
- Remote controller, system-selection jar, manual-selection jar, and
  RadioInfo helper hashes are verified before every action.

## Verdict

Pass. One bounded RF run is unlocked with the exact CP2500 frozen phone/gNB
shape and fresh identifiers. The sole experimental change is app NR-only plus
the acknowledged n3 scan constraint after propagation. Judge whether the
phone selects Pavonis n3 and emits Msg1; always clear the constraint and
restore automatic/full-RAT/mode33 afterward.
