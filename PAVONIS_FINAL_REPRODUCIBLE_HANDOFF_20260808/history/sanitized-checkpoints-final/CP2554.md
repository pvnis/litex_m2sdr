# CP2554 - Phone camps Pavonis n3 but remains emergency-only

## Question

Did CP2549 fail because a public n1 SA cell won automatic selection, and does
constraining the modem to n3 produce the first phone Msg1 toward Pavonis?

## Method

- Ran the frozen phone gNB/qcore shape with fresh identifiers.
- After propagation, applied the CP2552-gated app NR-only state and exact
  NGRAN/band-3 system-selection restriction.
- Captured the bounded phone monitor, filtered radio trace, and gNB summary.
- Cleared n3 and restored automatic/full-RAT/both-mode33. The outer controller
  later repeated the same successful restoration.

The local execution transport returned before the detached wrapper completed.
I incorrectly treated that as a stopped run and invoked [host-redacted] cleanup at
`[date-redacted]`, then explicitly restored the phone at `[date-redacted]`. The detached
wrapper nevertheless completed and packaged all artifacts. Therefore:

- pre-Msg1 selection is valid: n3 was active from `[date-redacted]` to `[date-redacted]`
  (about 119 seconds), with qcore present for the first 72 seconds;
- attach/core/data conclusions above Msg1 are void because of the intervention.

## Result

The n3 restriction worked and removed the public-cell confound:

- selected cell: `001/01`, `SENS`, PCI `1`, TAC `1`, NR ARFCN `367930`,
  band `[3]`, NCI `5439488`;
- service state: `getRilDataRadioTechnology=20 (NR_SA)` and `rRplmn=00101`;
- no public Salt n1 identity or ARFCN `432030` appears during the constrained
  result class.

But the modem did not consider Pavonis available for normal service:

- `registrationState=NOT_REG_SEARCHING`;
- `emergencyEnabled=true`, `availableServices=[EMERGENCY]`;
- `rejectCause=0`, so this was not a new network Registration Reject;
- phone monitor: `45` rows, `43` NR rows, zero selected private PLMN, zero
  in-service rows, and no cellular IP.

The gNB transmit path was healthy: zero Soapy timeout, downlink-late, or
release-late events. It saw `564` empty PRACH rows, zero nonempty PRACH rows,
and no encoded RAR. Thus the phone never emitted Msg1.

Restoration passes with automatic mode `1`, full RAT including NR, and both
preferred-mode values `33`. [host-redacted] is idle after the established cleanup.

## Verdict

The public-n1-selection theory is closed: it was a real confound, but not the
remaining blocker. CP2554 proves the phone can decode and camp the exact
Pavonis Band-3 NR-SA cell while refusing normal service before Msg1. The active
frontier is now why the modem classifies this HPLMN cell as emergency-only:
audit actual broadcast cell-access fields and phone forbidden-TA/NAS mobility
state before changing RF, gains, PLMN selection, or PRACH. Do not repeat n3
selection unchanged.
