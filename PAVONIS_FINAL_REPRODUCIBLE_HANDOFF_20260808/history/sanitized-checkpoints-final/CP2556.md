# CP2556 - Phone n3 reattach with bounded PRACH capture

## Question

While the phone is actively searching on the exact Pavonis n3 cell, does a
fresh radio reattach place a recoverable Msg1 waveform in the gNB's current
PRACH collection windows?

## Method

- Reused the frozen CP2554 phone gNB/qcore/n3 controller unchanged.
- Waited for acknowledged NR-only plus NGRAN/band-3 application.
- Touched the existing strong-predemod arm once on [host-redacted].
- Ran the previously proven mobile-radio off/on helper to force a fresh access
  attempt after arming.
- Kept the existing 16-window capture cap, energy floors, collection geometry,
  stage-1 list, RF power, gain, and all registration settings unchanged.
- Replayed the 16 packaged CF32 windows through the validated CP1965 detector
  harness in baseline and full compact stage-1 modes.

## Run health

All orchestration gates pass:

- n3 apply, arm touch, radio true->false->true, underlying run, phone restore,
  and [host-redacted] cleanup complete normally;
- final phone state is automatic selection, full RAT including NR, and both
  preferred-mode values `33`;
- gNB has zero Soapy TX timeouts and zero downlink-late events;
- full-rate Soapy RX append remains off.

## Phone and ladder result

The phone returns to the same raw RIL class after the cycle. All 75 captured
RIL registration rows are NR `NOT_REG_MT_SEARCHING_OP_EM`, denial `NONE`,
registered PLMN [network-id-redacted]; 47 carry the exact PCI1/TAC1/ARFCN367930 identity.
The phone remains out of service with no cellular IP.

The gNB sees `1152` detector-empty and zero detector-nonempty PRACH rows, no
accepted Msg1, and no RAR. TX health is clean, so this is not a downlink supply
or realtime-health void.

## Bounded-window result

The arm works and packages 16 CF32 windows of 23,520 samples each. Their span is
`[date-redacted]` through `[date-redacted]`:

- n3 was already present before the radio-off action at `[date-redacted]`;
- radio-on begins at `[date-redacted]`;
- five captured windows occur after radio-on, but the first post-cycle RIL row
  carrying the exact n3 cell identity occurs at about `[date-redacted]`, after the
  raw cap was exhausted.

Offline baseline replay accepts `0/16`; p0 ratios are `0.055..0.107`. The full
CP1965 compact timing/CFO grid evaluates 11,920 rows and accepts `0/16`; global
maximum p0 ratio is only `0.181` against threshold `0.6100000143`. These are
not hidden CP1961/CP2060-class PRACH windows.

The uncapped metadata-only stage-1 trace provides the later time series. In the
30 seconds after n3 reacquisition it has 187 rows, mean/max raw RMS
`-67.061/-63.254 dB`, and zero selected candidates. The radio-off interval is
nearly identical at mean/max `-67.113/-63.953 dB`. No nominal-PRACH-window
energy step accompanies the phone coming back on the cell.

## Verdict

CP2556 strongly weakens "phone Msg1 is present in the current collection
window but needs a wider CP1965 stage-1 envelope." It does not yet prove that
the phone emitted no uplink waveform: the phone's initial access may lie
outside the gNB's srsUE-calibrated 23,520-sample collection window, and the raw
cap ended just before post-cycle cell reacquisition.

Do not [interface-redacted] the gNB delay, stage-1 candidates, threshold, RX gain, or phone
policy from this result. The next discriminator should use the otherwise idle
bladeRF RX2 as an independent wideband capture at the Band-3 uplink center
`1747.5 MHz`, armed across radio-on and n3 reacquisition. A burst in that
capture localizes the problem to gNB collection timing; no burst localizes it
to modem/cell-suitability/access initiation.
