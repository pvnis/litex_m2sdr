# CP2557 - Independent RX2 uplink capture establishes background, misses reacquisition

## Question

Does an independent bladeRF RX2 capture at the Band-3 uplink center show a
phone-originated burst that the gNB's nominal PRACH windows miss?

## Method

- Reused the frozen phone gNB/qcore/n3 shape and exact restoration path.
- Captured RX2 only at `1747.5 MHz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `32` for
  exactly 15 seconds (`172800000` SC16 samples, `691200000` bytes).
- Cycled the phone radio off and on while the capture command was active.
- Kept the raw IQ on [host-redacted] and fetched only compact 1 ms summaries.
- Analyzed both full-band RMS and a `1.5 MHz` PRACH-oriented subband centered
  `-2.7 MHz` from the UL carrier center, matching the current `rb_offset=8`
  geometry approximately.

## Run health

All orchestration gates pass:

- n3 apply, bladeRF capture, radio true->false->true, base runner, phone
  restoration, and cleanup complete normally;
- final phone state returns to automatic selection, full RAT including NR, and
  both preferred-mode values `33`;
- the raw capture has the exact requested byte count, zero clipped components,
  and component absolute maximum `589`;
- the gNB health and frozen phone shape remain unchanged.

## Timing audit

- about 5.47 seconds before radio-off;

- about 3.53 seconds after radio-on but before exact n3 reacquisition;
- zero samples after post-cycle exact n3 reacquisition.

## Independent capture result

The full-band 1 ms baseline is median `-33.487 dBFS`, maximum `-21.321 dBFS`,
and maximum excursion `12.165 dB`. The PRACH-oriented subband is median
`-42.599 dBFS`, maximum `-28.553 dBFS`, and maximum excursion `14.046 dB`.

Strong, approximately periodic 1 ms groups occur throughout the recording,
including while the phone radio is off. Segment statistics relative to the
global PRACH-band median are:

| Segment | Blocks | Mean band dBFS | Max band dBFS | >= +6 dB | >= +10 dB |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Before radio-off | 5473 | -42.162 | -28.553 | 145 | 91 |
| Radio off | 6000 | -42.288 | -29.148 | 163 | 105 |
| Radio on, pre-reacquisition | 3527 | -42.344 | -30.417 | 83 | 53 |

The post-on segment does not gain power or burst rate. The recurring strong
groups are therefore background/non-phone energy and establish a matched
independent-receiver baseline; they are not evidence of phone PRACH.

## Verdict

CP2557 is valid as an independent RX2 background calibration and void for the
post-reacquisition phone-uplink question. The capture was armed too early and
ended before the phone reacquired the exact n3 cell. No phone-attributable
uplink step appears before reacquisition, but this cannot distinguish a later
out-of-window Msg1 from a modem that never initiates access.

Do not [interface-redacted] the gNB detector, collection delay, RX gain, RF level, or phone
policy from CP2557. The next run must force the radio cycle first and then start
a longer independent RX2 capture so the raw interval includes the delayed
exact-n3 reacquisition. Judge only energy that differs from the CP2557
phone-off background in both time and PRACH subband.
