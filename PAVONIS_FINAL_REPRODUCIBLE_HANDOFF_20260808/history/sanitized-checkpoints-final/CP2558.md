# CP2558 - Independent RX2 post-cycle steady-state uplink capture

## Question

After a forced phone radio cycle and exact Pavonis n3 reacquisition, does a
long independent bladeRF RX2 capture show sustained or repeated handset uplink
energy outside the gNB's nominal PRACH windows?

## Method

- Reused the frozen phone gNB/qcore/n3 shape and exact restoration path.
- Completed the phone radio off/on cycle before launching the independent
  capture.
- Captured RX2 at `1747.5 MHz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `32` for exactly
  30 seconds (`345600000` SC16 samples, `1382400000` bytes).
- Kept raw IQ on [host-redacted] and fetched only a compact 1 ms TSV/JSON analysis.
- Compared the full-band and `1.5 MHz`, `-2.7 MHz`-offset PRACH-oriented
  subband against CP2557's matched phone-radio-off background.
- Joined the sample interval to bounded RIL state transitions and the packaged
  gNB PRACH/stage-1 summaries.

The first launch candidate stopped before RF or remote process creation because
the controller sandbox denied its initial SCP socket. The fresh-stamp retry ran
with the approved network permission and is the only CP2558 RF datapoint.

## Run health

All retry gates pass:

- n3 apply, radio true->false->true, RX2 capture, analyzer, frozen base runner,
  phone restoration, and cleanup complete normally;
- the raw file has the exact requested byte count, zero clipped components,
  and component absolute maximum `631`;
- the gNB has zero Soapy TX timeouts, zero write deficit, zero deadline-write
  timeouts, and zero downlink-late lines;
- full-rate Soapy RX append remains off.

## Independent capture result

The full-band baseline is median `-33.483 dBFS`, maximum `-21.253 dBFS`, and
maximum excursion `12.230 dB`. The PRACH-oriented subband is median
`-42.594 dBFS`, maximum `-28.514 dBFS`, and maximum excursion `14.079 dB`.
These values reproduce CP2557 within `0.01..0.07 dB`.

Using CP2558's global PRACH-band median, the inferred held-state and subsequent
invalid-identity portions are:

| Segment | Blocks | Mean band dBFS | Max band dBFS | >= +6 dB/s | >= +10 dB/s |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Exact-n3 state inferred held | 26284 | -42.277 | -28.514 | 25.415 | 15.294 |
| Identity invalid | 3716 | -42.312 | -29.910 | 25.565 | 13.724 |

CP2557's phone-radio-off reference was mean `-42.288 dBFS`, with rates about
`27.17/s` at +6 dB and `17.50/s` at +10 dB. CP2558 therefore shows no level or
burst-rate increase attributable to a handset repeatedly transmitting while
it holds/searches the n3 cell. Its strongest groups retain the same periodic
background class seen with the phone radio off.

## gNB result

The gNB records `1150` PRACH detector rows, all `detected_preambles=[]`, and no
RAR. The metadata-only stage-1 trace has `1152` rows, `1141` energy-gate passes,
zero selections, mean/max raw RMS `-67.491/-61.641 dB`, and maximum search time
`19.967 ms`. This is consistent with the independent receiver finding no
sustained phone-specific UL excess, while remaining non-decisive for the missed
initial reacquisition interval.

## Verdict

CP2558 is a valid negative for sustained or repeated handset uplink during the
post-cycle held/searching state. It reproduces the independent background and
the gNB remains detector-empty. It is still void for a one-shot Msg1 at initial
reacquisition because sample recording starts about eight seconds after the
first exact-n3 report.

Do not [interface-redacted] gNB delay, stage-1 candidates, threshold, RX gain, RF level, or
phone policy. One final independent discriminator should start a 45-second RX2
capture before the radio cycle, preserving the CP2557 ordering but extending
the duration past the observed 18-second worst-case reacquisition. If that
onset-spanning raw has no phone-specific PRACH-band transient, the leading
branch becomes modem cell-suitability/access initiation rather than gNB
collection timing.
