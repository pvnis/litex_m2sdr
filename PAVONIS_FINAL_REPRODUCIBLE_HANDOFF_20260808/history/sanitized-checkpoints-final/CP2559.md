epoch that CP2557 and CP2558 failed to sample?

## Method

- Reused the frozen phone gNB/qcore/n3 shape and exact restoration path.
- Started an independent bladeRF RX2 capture before the phone radio cycle.
- Captured at `1747.5 MHz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `32` for exactly
  45 seconds (`518400000` SC16 samples, `2073600000` bytes).
- Kept the raw IQ on [host-redacted] and fetched only compact 1 ms full-band and
  `1.5 MHz`, `-2.7 MHz`-offset PRACH-subband summaries.

  PCI1/TAC1/ARFCN367930 state, gNB PRACH rows, and stage-1 metadata.

identifying Pavonis but is not starting an observable random-access procedure.
