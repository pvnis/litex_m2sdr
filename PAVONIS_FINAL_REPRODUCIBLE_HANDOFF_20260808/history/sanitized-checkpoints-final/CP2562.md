# CP2562: Phone reboot does not restore Msg1

## Method

The firmware denied `cmd phone restart-modem` from both shell and root before
changing state. A full phone reboot was therefore used. Boot ID changed from
`681335a1-2763-4b5d-a321-0a6aba0f7466` to
`dc7de55c-e549-4f71-b87f-a32cf51a46c8`; ADB returned, boot completed, the SIM
loaded, airplane mode remained off, and automatic/full-RAT/both-mode33 state
passed before RF.

Three launch artifacts are void and append-only: CP2562 first lost its local
controller after detached gNB start; CP2562B/CP2562C stopped before RF while
the orphaned temporary NR-only apply was still active. The established restore
controller returned the phone exactly to automatic/full-RAT/mode33 before the
valid CP2562D run.

CP2562D stamp:
`[date-redacted]CP2562D_PHONE_POSTREBOOT_N3_RF`.
It used the unchanged frozen phone gNB, NR-only mode23, NGRAN band-3
restriction, append off, and the standard trap-guarded restore.

## Result

- valid monitor rows: 46
- NR-SA rows: 22
- exact Pavonis n3 identity rows in radio log: 4
- emergency-only rows: 29
- RRC markers: 0
- NAS markers: 0
- gNB PRACH: 1,144 empty, 0 non-empty
- stage-1: 1,145 rows, 1,123 energy-gate passes, 0 selected
- stage-1 raw RMS: mean `-67.316 dB`, max `-59.412 dB`
- RAR: 0
- Soapy TX timeout/error/final deficit/downlink-late: all 0
- final phone restore: PASS

The phone again identified the exact `001/01`, PCI1, TAC1, ARFCN367930 n3
cell but remained emergency/searching and emitted no observable Msg1.

## Verdict

Stale modem state is closed as a standalone explanation. Do not reboot or
repeat the frozen n3 shape unchanged. Continue with COTS-UE-specific SIM/voice
policy requirements while preserving the proven RF and gNB configuration.
