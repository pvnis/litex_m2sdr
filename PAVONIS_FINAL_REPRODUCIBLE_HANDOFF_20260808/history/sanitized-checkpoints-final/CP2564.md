# CP2564: voice-policy-off n3 run remains pre-Msg1

## Run

One bounded RF run used the frozen CP2552 phone n3 shape with exactly one
phone-policy change: VoNR and advanced calling were disabled through the
CP2563 Binder helper. The wrapper required the true/true baseline, proved
false/false again after the n3 restriction was active, and restored true/true
on every exit. The valid fresh stamp was:

`[date-redacted]CP2564_PHONE_VOICE_OFF_N3_RF`

## Evidence

- Voice baseline true/true, active run false/false, and final restore
  true/true all passed; `setVoNrEnabled` returned 0 in both directions.
- The phone monitor recorded 46 rows, including 30 NR-SA rows. The fetched
  radio log contained 161 exact Pavonis cell rows and 123
  `NOT_REG_MT_SEARCHING_OP_EM` rows, but zero RRC or NAS markers.
- The phone never became privately registered or acquired a cellular IP
  address. Exact Pavonis visibility remained emergency/searching class.
- The gNB saw 1,152 empty and zero nonempty PRACH detections. Stage 1 produced
  1,155 rows, 1,145 energy-bearing rows, and zero selected candidates; raw RMS
  was mean `-67.225 dB` and maximum `-61.633 dB`.
- No RAR was allocated or encoded. Soapy TX had zero timeouts, errors, sample
  deficit, and downlink-late events.
- Final phone restoration passed: automatic selection, full RAT,
  `preferred_network_mode6=33`, and
  `oplus_user_preferred_network_mode6=33`; VoNR and advanced calling are both
  true again.

## Verdict

Disabling VoNR and advanced calling is not sufficient to make this phone emit
Msg1. It increases or preserves exact-cell dwell without advancing RRC/NAS.
Close the voice-policy branch and do not repeat it unchanged. The next clean
discriminator is transmitter frequency accuracy: establish the current
on-air CFO and validate OCUDU `ru_sdr.clock_ppm` sign no-RF before any bounded
calibration run. Preserve the CP2392 srsUE golden and all frozen phone RF,
broadcast, detector, and core settings.
