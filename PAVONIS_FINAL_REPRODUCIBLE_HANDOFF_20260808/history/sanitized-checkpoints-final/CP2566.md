# CP2566: current CFO measured; first calibration leg propagation-void

## Runs

Two shortened CP2497-style 250 ms bladeRF RX1 captures used fresh stamps:

- `[date-redacted]CP2566A_CLOCK0_RX1`
- `[date-redacted]CP2566B_CLOCKM0P548_RX1`

Both passed capture, exact timing, 247 ms same-window overlap, packaging,
offline analysis, and gNB health. Full same-device RX append makes them
ladder-void by design; they are downlink calibration runs only.

## Zero-ppm baseline

CP2566A's decoder-positive PBCH windows give a tight current CFO class:

- 8 PBCH CRC-OK PCI1 windows;
- CFO range `-1056.5..-963.1 Hz`, mean `-1009.8 Hz`;
- 100 PCI1 CSI windows, best SNR `+4.6 dB`;
- external RMS `-44.870 dBFS`;
- M2SDR self-RX `23/24` PBCH CRC OK;
- zero TX timeout, error, sample deficit, or downlink-late events.

This agrees with CP2497's one-shot `-1061.4 Hz` measurement. It supersedes
the initial use of CP2392's `-3747.710 Hz` for absolute calibration: CP2392's
srsUE RF config has `freq_offset = 6000`, so that cell-search value is not a
zero-offset external-instrument reading.

The revised first calibration candidate is `-0.548059701 ppm`, corresponding
to `-1009.8 Hz` at the downlink center and `-957.734 Hz` at the uplink center.

## Propagation failure caught

CP2566B was launched with `PAVONIS_GNB_CLOCK_PPM=-0.548059701`, but its
packaged generated gNB YAML contains no `clock_ppm` key. The active phone
ladder has evolved through the CP1807 helper pair; CP2565 added the passthrough
to the older CP1660/CP1733 pair. The B capture therefore remained zero-ppm:
its one-shot CFO is `-996.5 Hz`, essentially identical to A. It is void for
calibration direction/residual and must not be interpreted as a failed
physical correction.

CP2566B still provides a clean repeat baseline: 16 PBCH CRC-OK PCI1 sweep
windows, 79 PCI1 CSI windows, external RMS `-44.881 dBFS`, M2SDR self-RX
`24/24`, and clean TX health. The inconsistent low-SNR PBCH-window CFO aliases
are not used for calibration.

## Decision

Port the same default-off, one-key `clock_ppm` insertion into the actual
CP1807 stage/gNB helper pair. Require config-only disabled/enabled structural
diff and deployed-gNB `--dryrun` again. Then repeat only the calibrated RX1
leg with `-0.548059701 ppm`; a valid run must prove the key in its packaged
YAML before CFO is interpreted.
