## Residual-CFO limitation

Decoder-positive PBCH occasions no longer form one CFO cluster. Five unique
events alternate between approximately `+710..+828 Hz` and
`-[date-redacted]..-2160 Hz`; their occurrence-weighted mean is about `-376 Hz`, closer
to zero than CP2566A's `-1009.8 Hz` but not a stable physical-LO estimator.
This is consistent with the campaign's known per-occasion phase-presentation
structure contaminating the DMRS CFO axis.

An independent offline cyclic-prefix estimator was attempted over all three
captures. Its slot-phase peak/median score is only `1.02..1.03` and retained
per-symbol estimates span roughly `+/-6.4 kHz`, proving the external signal is
too weak for that aggregate estimator. Those outputs are diagnostic-invalid
and are not used to [interface-redacted] another value.

## Decision

The calibration mechanism and direction are proven sufficiently for one
direct phone discriminator. Run the exact frozen NR-only+n3 phone shape with
voice policy at restored true/true and only `clock_ppm=-0.548059701` added.
Judge first on phone RRC/NAS and gNB nonempty/accepted Msg1. The coherent RX
center also moves `-957.734 Hz`; keep the existing bounded stage-1 envelope
unchanged and record its selected shift/CFO if Msg1 appears. Do not iterate
ppm from the occasion-split PBCH estimates.
