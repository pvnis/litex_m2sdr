## Verdict

CP2572 rejects coherent `clock_ppm=-0.548059701` as a standalone fix for the
phone's pre-Msg1 behavior. It reproduces the same exact-cell,
emergency-searching, zero-PRACH-selection class under a fully valid controller
and package. Keep the calibration available as a documented frequency
correction, but do not spend another unchanged phone RF run on it.

Next work returns no-RF to handset/network eligibility policy. The remaining
high-value split is whether the CPH2409 treats the private `001/01` cell as
emergency-only despite open SIB1 and the test USIM, versus a modem-specific
initial-access policy. Audit the frozen `001/01` choice against the documented
OnePlus/SysmoUSIM test-PLMN guidance and current data-roaming/carrier-config
state before proposing one reversible PLMN/profile discriminator. Do not
change the CP2392 golden srsUE baseline.
