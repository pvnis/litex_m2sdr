## Decision

The no-RF gate passes. One bounded CP2575 RF run is now justified with this
exact shape and a fresh stamp. Judge propagation first: generated gNB PLMNs
must both be `90170`, qcore must log serving `901/70`, phone roaming must be
active during the hold, and the phone must identify the new serving PLMN.
Then judge Msg1, RAR, registration, and data. Regardless of result, require
full phone/roaming restoration and no stale process. Do not mix voice-off,
threshold, detector, RF-power, or scheduler changes into this first run.
