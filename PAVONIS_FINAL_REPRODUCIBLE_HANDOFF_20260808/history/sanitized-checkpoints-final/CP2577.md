# CP2577: roaming plus voice-off controller passes no-RF gate

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Objective

Prepare the final documented OnePlus policy discriminator without changing the
CP2575 network/RF shape: SIM `00101`, serving gNB/qcore `90170`, data roaming
on during the run, calibrated `clock_ppm=-0.548059701`, NR-only+n3, and both
VoNR and advanced calling disabled.

## Controller design

`cp2577_run_roaming_voice_off_phone_rf.sh` is a thin controller around the
hash-pinned CP2574 roaming runner. It:

- requires voice true/true before starting;
- changes only voice policy to false/false;
- invokes CP2574, which owns roaming, PLMN, RF, qcore cleanup, and phone-mode
  restoration;
- waits for the proven `PHONE_N3_APPLY=PASS` marker and re-reads voice
  false/false during the active interval;
- independently trap-restores voice true/true on normal or interrupted exit.

No threshold, detector, scheduler, payload, delay, power, gNB binary, qcore
subscriber, or CP2392 baseline change is present.

## No-RF proof

- shell syntax passes;
- base-runner hash is pinned to CP2576's cleanup-hardened CP2574 wrapper;
- approval-closed invocation exits 2 and creates zero artifacts;
- authorized phone voice round trip passes
  `true/true -> false/false -> true/true` with result zero;
- final `data_roaming6` remains zero.

## Verdict

The no-RF gate passes. One bounded CP2578 RF run is approved with this exact
controller and a fresh stamp. Judge propagation and restoration first, then
phone registration/Msg1, RAR, Msg3, attach, and data. If it remains pre-Msg1,
the locally testable documented roaming/voice policy recipe is closed rather
than a reason to continue random handset-setting changes.
