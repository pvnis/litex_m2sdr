## Tooling failure

The SSH helper stores CRLF lines. CP2579's outer proof anchored the expected
manual-selection row at `$` without removing the trailing carriage return.
The correct row was present, but `grep` returned false. Under `set -e`, that
invoked the outer trap and terminated the still-running nested controller
before its normal summary was written.

This classification is mechanical:

- apply log ends lines with bytes `0d 0a`;
- both the CP2579 and nested CP2577 final summaries are absent;
- cleanup/restoration logs are complete;
- the fetched package is present, but its zero PRACH result is not a valid
  full-duration manual-selection judgment.

The partial package has 1,152 stage-1 rows and zero selections, 1,148 empty
PRACH rows, no RRC/NAS, and clean TX health. Those are retained diagnostics,
not a negative manual-90170 verdict. Archive SHA-256:
`4f8d61ee18846ad13e200e2ed47908b1e23b20ac08a7ef933ad767d832193b9b`.

## Verdict

CP2580 is RF-executed but ladder-void due solely to the outer CRLF parser. The
manual request itself and all restoration paths worked. Normalize CRLF and
replay the exact saved proof no-RF before one fresh unchanged retry.
