## Objective

Use only saved CP2582 and authoritative prior artifacts to distinguish a wrong
gNB PRACH search lattice from a phone-side decision not to start random access.

## gNB search coverage

The CP2582 package contains the complete metadata-only stage-1 trace but no raw
UL windows. Its 1,155 rows all target slot 4 and advance exactly every 16 SFNs:
`17/4, 33/4, 49/4, ...`. Reported and nominal SFN/slot match in every row.

- 1,149 rows pass the energy gate and each evaluates all 30 configured
  candidates; six rows are energy-gated with zero evaluations.
- No row selects a candidate.
- RMS spans `-73.922415..-61.627716 dB`; the strongest row is SFN 513/slot 4
  at `-61.627716 dB`, still with zero selection.
- The harness confirms the existing `12000` extra-sample collection window and
  disabled full-rate append/segment trace.

This is complete nominal-occasion coverage, not a row-cap or wrong-slot blind
spot. Because CP2582 saved no raw samples, it cannot by itself exclude a phone
burst outside those windows.

## Verdict

The CP2582 gNB was not blind to configured PRACH occasions, and the phone was
still pre-RRC/pre-NAS with no denial. The strongest existing evidence remains
that the phone did not emit observable Msg1, but the independent onset-spanning
RX2 negative in CP2559 predates the 90170/manual/roaming recipe. The next
discriminator is one frozen CP2582-shape run with the established independent
bladeRF RX2 uplink capture starting before the phone radio cycle/manual request.
Judge any energy/burst anywhere in the 1747.5 MHz uplink band before changing
detector timing, thresholds, RF gain, broadcast fields, or handset policy.
