## Objective

or RF parameters: independent bladeRF RX2 capture must be armed after
n3/roaming/voice state is proven and before the manual 90170 request.

## Implementation

`cp2585_run_manual_90170_onset_bladerf_ul_sniff_phone_rf.sh` composes, without
editing, the hash-pinned CP2577 frozen runner, CP2579 manual-selection helper,
CP2559 RX2 capture helper, and CP2558 bounded analyzer.

- It waits for n3 apply, roaming active, and voice false/false.
- It arms a 45-second, 1747.5 MHz, 11.52 Msps, 10 MHz, RX2 gain32 capture.
- Only after the capture-arm proof does it issue persistent manual
  `90170/NGRAN6` and records start/end times on [host-redacted]'s clock.
- It retains all qcore, voice, roaming, RAT, and automatic-selection
  restoration gates and fetches only the compact 1 ms analysis plus capture
  summary; the 2.0736 GB raw remains remote pending the verdict.
