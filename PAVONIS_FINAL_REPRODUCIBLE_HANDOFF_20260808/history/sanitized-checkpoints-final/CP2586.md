# CP2586: manual 90170 selection produces no observable phone uplink

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Objective

Run the frozen CP2582 n3/90170/roaming/voice-off/manual-selection shape while
an independent bladeRF RX2 captures the start of the manual selection attempt
across the full Band-3 uplink channel.

## Validity and timing

All authoritative gates pass: n3, roaming, voice-off, capture arm, manual
90170 request, full capture, analyzer, frozen base runner, qcore cleanup,
roaming/voice/RAT/automatic-selection restoration, package, and final phone
state. The text-only detached gNB `RUNNER_RC=1` is its expected stop status;
all owning wrappers report rc0 and the controller marks the run valid.

- Capture: RX2, `1747.5 MHz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `32`, 45 seconds,
  exactly `2,073,600,000` bytes, zero clipping, component max `645`.
- The bladeRF command is armed at `[date-redacted]`; the exact 45-second payload
  is inferred from its end at `[date-redacted]` to begin at
  approximately `[date-redacted]`.
- Manual selection starts on [host-redacted] at `[date-redacted]`, about 0.57 seconds
  before sample payload, and returns mode2 at `[date-redacted]`.

The raw therefore covers approximately seconds 0.57 through 45.57 of the
actual manual network-selection attempt. It does not provide a pre-request
sample baseline, so comparison uses CP2559's calibrated same-device background.

CP2586 reproduces CP2559 background to hundredths of a decibel:

Five-second segments remain stable: full-band means drift only from
`-33.242` to `-33.321 dBFS`, and PRACH-band means from `-42.292` to
`-42.361 dBFS`, with no onset step. CP2586's strongest PRACH-band block is
`-28.451 dBFS`, well below CP2559's phone-off/background maximum of
`-22.880 dBFS`. Recurring short bursts are therefore the established
background class, not a new handset transmission.

## gNB and phone result

The gNB stage-1 trace has 1,162 slot-4 rows, 1,151 energy gates, 34,530
candidate evaluations, and zero selections. RMS spans
`-72.103..-61.807 dB`; there is no RACH indication or Msg3. TX health has zero
Soapy timeout, downlink-late, or write-deficit event.

The phone monitor remains NR but has zero RRC, NAS, service, or cellular-IP
progress. Manual selection still returns `binder_return=false` while changing
mode1 to mode2. Final ADB, roaming0, mode33/mode33, automatic selection,
full RAT, voice true/true, and qcore cleanup all pass.

## Verdict

CP2586 extends CP2559's no-phone-UL conclusion to the complete documented
90170 roaming/voice-off/manual-selection recipe. The phone identifies the cell
but does not emit an observable initial-access burst during the first 45
seconds of manual selection. Stop PRACH detector/envelope, RX gain, timing,
clock, and blind policy changes for the phone branch.

The next no-RF question is above PHY: audit why the hidden manual request
returns false, including the constructed `OperatorInfo` state/format versus a
framework network-scan result, and determine whether a standards-compliant
manual-selection request can be made without changing the frozen gNB. Keep the
2.0736 GB raw until that audit closes; it can then be deleted as background-
class data.
