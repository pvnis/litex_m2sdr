## Scope

keeps that geometry and every CP2600 phone/RF/core/timing fixture, while adding
only the remaining identity values from the documented working OnePlus n3
profile: PCI 500 and TAC 7.

## Decision

Run one bounded CP2602 production scan/select RF discriminator. Require live
sequence values index6/SS0=0/PCI500/TAC7 and packaged profile hash before
interpretation. Judge phone-reported PCI/TAC/SSB identity, scan classification,
selection callback, and Msg1. If the phone still returns `INTERNAL_ERR` before
Msg1, close the known OnePlus common-cell identity/profile fields and stop
iterating adjacent identity constants.
