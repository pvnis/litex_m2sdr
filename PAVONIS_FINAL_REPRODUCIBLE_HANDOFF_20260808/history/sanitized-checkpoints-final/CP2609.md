## Why this field is next

The known OnePlus-era srsRAN profile broadcasts a 10-slot response window.
The local OCUDU validator also treats 10 ms as the maximum on licensed
spectrum; Pavonis's Band-3 value 40 is accepted only because the custom
`PAVONIS_ALLOW_EXTENDED_RA_RESP_WINDOW` bypass was added for the delayed srsUE
RAR campaign. This makes response40 a concrete pre-PRACH COTS-compatibility
suspect, while first Msg1 can be judged before any shorter-window RAR issue.
