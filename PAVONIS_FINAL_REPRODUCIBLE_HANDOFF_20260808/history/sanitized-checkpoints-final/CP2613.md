## Coupled-field requirement

both ASN.1 `totalNumberOfRA-Preambles` and
`ssb-perRACH-OccasionAndCB-PreamblesPerSSB`. OCUDU likewise rejects a total8
configuration if `nof_cb_preambles_per_ssb` remains at its default64. The
discriminator therefore atomically sets:

```yaml

nof_cb_preambles_per_ssb: 8
```

not two independently [interface-redacted] fields.
