## Verdict

The second OTA pair is reciprocal: bladeRF TX2 reaches M2SDR RX2 strongly.
Together with CP2616's strong phone downlink, the phone's zero-Msg1 result is
not explained by a dead or grossly lossy channel1 uplink connector/antenna
path. Close the physical-geometry branch. This CW test does not claim that the
phone itself transmits Msg1; it proves the gNB's configured RX2 path can receive
an OTA uplink at the correct Band-3 UL center.
