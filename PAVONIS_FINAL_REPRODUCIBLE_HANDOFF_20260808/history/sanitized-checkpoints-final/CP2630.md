## Verdict

CP2630 is a valid RF execution and a phone-downlink-visibility failure. It
exonerates controller propagation, qcore listener/NGAP, bladeRF X2 selection,
and process startup. It does not yet prove that a decodable NR cell reached the
air from X2, because no independent receive-side waveform measurement was
armed.

Keep the CP2392 srsUE baseline untouched. Before changing phone policy, gNB
geometry, gain, or subscriber state, run a bounded bladeRF-X2 gNB transmit with
an independent M2SDR receive capture at the exact CP2630 DL/SSB geometry.
Decode PBCH/SIB1 and record level. An absent/invalid capture points to the X2
TX/antenna path; a valid capture moves the fault to COTS-phone
interoperability/selection semantics.
