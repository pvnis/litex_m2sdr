# CP2631: bladeRF-X2 gNB is not decoder-visible on M2SDR RX2

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## Purpose

Resolve CP2630's missing air-side proof without changing phone policy or gNB
geometry. Run the same isolated srsENB bladeRF-X2 downlink, take no phone
action, and capture it independently on the proven reciprocal M2SDR RX2 path.

## Gate and execution

The checkpoint-owned runner
`cp2631_run_bladerf_x2_gnb_m2sdr_rx.sh` is default-closed, fresh-stamp
guarded, hash-deploys the CP2629 qcore/gNB helpers and established M2SDR
capture helper, and trap-stops both services. It contains no phone action.

An initial fresh stamp stopped before qcore/RF because a generated command line
contained a literal patch marker. Both hosts were then proven idle. The command
block was corrected as a unit and the valid run used fresh stamp
`CP2631B_[date-redacted]`.

All mechanics passed:

- [host-redacted] and [host-redacted] preflights;
- qcore start and NGAP setup;
- bladeRF X2 `rx=2/tx=3` and srsENB start;
- M2SDR RX2 capture at `1842.5 MHz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `50`;
- status, service stop, packaging, and cleanup.

The capture has `5,752,800` CF32 samples / `46,022,400` bytes, SHA-256
`bbc080ee...`, RMS `0.00738747`, and maximum 1 ms RMS `0.0104322`.
The helper's kernel flag is a false positive caused only by
`Using non-ARM DMA buffer handling`; no actual kernel error was present.

## Decode and spectrum

The validated aligned PBCH sweep used the logged runtime geometry:

- center `1842500000`;
- SSB `1842050000` / ARFCN `368410`;
- Pattern A, expected PCI `500`;
- 48 overlapping 20 ms cases over 499 ms.

Result: `0/48` PBCH CRC OK and zero CSI cell detections. Every decoder row
returned PCI `0`/SNR `0`. Averaged spectrum shows:

- expected SSB core `-650..-250 kHz`: mean `-2.300 dB` over median,
  peak only `+0.857 dB`;
- a narrow DC-region feature: peak `+23.856 dB` over median;
- 1 ms RMS median `0.0073684`, p95 `0.0074800`.

Thus the raw RMS is dominated by the RX2 floor/DC feature, not a visible NR
SSB at the expected offset.

## Verdict

CP2631 reproduces CP2630 below the phone: the independent M2SDR receiver does
not see a decodable or spectrally elevated SSB from the srsENB bladeRF-X2
run. Do not interpret CP2630 as phone incompatibility yet.

Next bracket the exact `1842.5 MHz` RX2 geometry with (a) an identical no-TX
capture and (b) the proven bladeRF TX2 to M2SDR RX2 CW helper at gain `45`.
No-TX-like gNB plus strong tone isolates the problem to the srsENB/plugin TX
waveform path; a weak tone reopens the frequency-specific physical path.
