This changes the same physical path from CP2638's `0/48` PBCH to `48/48` with

## Decision

The independent bladeRF gNB waveform gate passes and unlocks a bounded phone
retry using the CP2639 helper. Keep the single-channel-layout policy isolated
and default-off; preserve CP2392. Judge the phone run in order: cell visibility,
camping/Msg1, registration and PDU session, then private user-data traffic.
Keep the one-time late SOB as a startup-health residual, not a current PBCH
blocker.
