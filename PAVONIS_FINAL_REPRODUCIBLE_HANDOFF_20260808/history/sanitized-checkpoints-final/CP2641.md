## Phone And RF State

The established scan lists one `901 70 5G` network and taps it. Across 60
monitor rows, the exact PCI500/TAC7/ARFCN368410/band3 cell is present in 51.
Its measured quality is stable:

- RSRP: 48 rows at `-85 dBm`, 3 at `-86 dBm`;
- RSRQ: all 51 at `-12 dB`;
- SINR: 48 rows at `+8 dB`, 3 at `+7 dB`.

The modem remains `NOT_REG_SEARCHING`, reject cause zero, emergency-only, and
manual selection eventually returns Qualcomm error 38. This is no longer a
pre-Msg1 failure.

## Ladder Result

The complete gNB log contains seven accepted PRACH events, assigning temporary
RNTIs `0x4601` through `0x4607`, with timing offsets 10 or 12.

The 3,219-byte NR MAC PCAP contains six records. Source-level PCAP provenance
proves UL records are written only after PUSCH CRC OK. Three attempts
(`0x4601`, `0x4604`, and `0x4607`) contain:

- an 11-byte UL C-RNTI PDU with LCID `0x34` CCCH48, i.e. CRC-OK Msg3 carrying
  a six-byte RRCSetupRequest identity;
- a following 960-byte DL C-RNTI PDU with LCID `0x3e` contention-resolution
  identity matching that Msg3 exactly;
- a DL CCCH LCID0 subPDU of length 277, the generated RRCSetup, followed by
  padding.

The UL-to-DL gaps are five slots in all three cases. No UL SRB1/RRCSetupComplete
record follows, no connected-user event occurs, qcore receives no UE context,
and no PDU session or data interface appears.

## Decision

CP2641 is the first real-phone run to prove cell visibility, repeated Msg1,
RAR reception, CRC-OK Msg3, and gNB contention-resolution/RRCSetup transmission.
The active frontier is now the phone's completion of that DL RRCSetup exchange.

The fixed NR PDSCH MCS is 10 while phone SINR is only 7-8 dB and each RRCSetup
transport block is 960 bytes. The next isolated discriminator should lower only
`nr_pdsch_mcs` to a robust value, preserving the working PRACH, UL MCS, cell,
layout, and phone policy. If RRCSetupComplete appears, continue through NGAP,
registration, PDU session, and private data. If it does not, instrument
RRCSetup HARQ/semantic reception rather than revisiting RAR or Msg3.
