# CP2646: phone ACKs RRCSetup but sends no decodable SetupComplete

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## RF Run

Fresh stamp `CP2646_[date-redacted]` ran the frozen CP2642 MCS4 phone shape
with the CP2639 bladeRF layout fix, CP2644 stale-user cleanup, and CP2645
bounded PUCCH ACK trace. Both trace flags were proven in the live srsENB
process environment. Host gates, qcore/gNB/phone startup, phone restore,
cleanup, packaging, and artifact retrieval passed.

Package hashes:

- [host-redacted]: `fb345c07...`;
- [host-redacted]: `b347867f...`.

The phone observed exact PCI 500 in 51/59 monitor rows. Two rows carried valid
NR quality at `-86 dBm` RSRP, `-12 dB` RSRQ, and `+9 dB` SINR. Voice and data
registration remained out of service throughout.

## Decisive HARQ Result

The gNB accepted one PRACH at slot 651 and received its Msg3/RRCSetupRequest.
For temporary RNTI `0x4601`, the CP2645 trace then recorded:

```text
slot=670 ack_count=1 valid=1 ack0=1 snr_db=19.891
```

This is a valid positive HARQ ACK for the RRCSetup downlink. It proves that the
phone decoded the C-RNTI PDCCH/PDSCH transport carrying contention resolution
and RRCSetup. The active failure is therefore not RRCSetup RF delivery, MCS,
or the phone missing the downlink grant; it is after RRCSetup reception and
before a CRC-OK RRCSetupComplete reaches the gNB MAC.

No connected-user marker, NGAP InitialUE, qcore UE, PDU session, or private
data followed. The Android radio buffer has no modem-internal RRC/PUCCH/HARQ
events, so it does not further localize the failure.

## Cleanup Result

At the 30-second setup/inactivity deadline, CP2644 emitted one
`has no NGAP context; releasing RRC-only user` marker. The old release-miss
warning count is zero and `Cannot fit pending UCI into PUCCH` count is zero.
Five later invalid ACK rows at about `-8.4` to `-10.1 dB` belong to the release
attempt after timeout and do not alter the positive setup ACK verdict.

The stale-user cleanup is proven live and removes the CP2643 four-user PUCCH
poisoning mechanism.

## Decision

The next discriminator is uplink control after setup. Add a second bounded,
metadata-only trace for scheduled SR PUCCH opportunities and all resulting
PUSCH decodes. A valid positive SR with no PUSCH means scheduler handoff is
broken; scheduled SR opportunities that remain invalid place the problem in
the advertised/detected SR resource; PUSCH CRC failures place it in the
post-setup UL grant or PHY. Keep RF, MCS, RRC payload, and thresholds frozen
until that trace names the failing transition.
