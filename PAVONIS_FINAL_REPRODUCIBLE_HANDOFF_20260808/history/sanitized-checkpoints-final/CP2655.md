## Interpretation

The 1,153-byte trace rows are scheduled gNB receive attempts, not proof of a UE
transmission. Their floor SNR means no post-setup energy reached the expected
PUSCH resources. CP2655 therefore does not prove that the phone queued
SetupComplete. It proves that merely replacing a decoded SR with the existing
scheduler event is insufficient.

The remaining fork is narrower:

1. The phone ACKs the transport block but rejects/fails to apply the
   masterCellGroup, so it never monitors the configured UE-specific C-RNTI
   grants and never queues SetupComplete.
2. The phone applies setup but does not decode the UE-specific UL DCI, or the
   encoded grant and gNB receive geometry disagree so transmission occurs
   elsewhere.

The `46.6 dB` same-run gap makes a generic weak UL link implausible; Msg3 and
the post-setup path are different semantic/resource phases.
