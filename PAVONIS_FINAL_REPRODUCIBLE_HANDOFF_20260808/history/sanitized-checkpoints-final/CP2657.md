## Execution Classification

The frozen CP2657 RF shape ran with SA CSI removal, resource0 compatibility,
and the post-ACK proactive UL grant enabled. Preflight, helper deployment,
qcore/gNB startup, phone preparation, RF operation, and trap cleanup passed.
The operator-selection helper returned nonzero because its UI scan did not
reach its completion predicate, so the normal status/package tail did not run.
Both host bundles were recovered after clean trap shutdown.

This is void for a final operator-selection/core-data verdict, but non-void for
the RF/RRC/core boundary: direct gNB MAC PCAP and qcore timestamps independently
record the phone's messages.

## RF And MAC Result

- CSI-disable marker: 1;
- RACH attempts: 3;
- 11-byte Msg3 receive rows: 7, `2` CRC OK;
- positive setup HARQ ACK rows: 6 across two successful RNTIs;
- post-ACK grant injections: 2;
- SR rows: 64, with the first valid SR now observed (`1/64`, `+9.502 dB`);
- 1153-byte post-setup PUSCH rows: 11, `4` CRC OK;
- CRC-valid post-setup PUSCH SNR: `+6.438`, `+2.200`, `+0.499`, and `+4.211 dB`.

This is a categorical change from CP2655, where all 25 post-setup receive
windows were floor-class and CRC KO. SA CSI removal unlocks real phone uplink
after RRCSetup ACK.

## Payload Proof

The NR MAC PCAP contains two successful phone exchanges. For each RNTI it has:

1. an UL LCID 1, 35-byte RLC AM data SDU in a CRC-valid 1153-byte grant;
2. a later UL LCID 1 RLC status PDU;
3. corresponding DL acknowledgements.

After removing the 12-bit RLC AM header and 12-bit SRB PDCP header, the repo's
own NR ASN.1 decoder identifies both first UL SDUs as:

```text
UL_DCCH_TYPE=rrcSetupComplete
RRC_TRANSACTION_ID=0
SELECTED_PLMN_ID=1
DEDICATED_NAS_LENGTH=23
```

NAS bytes and subscriber identifiers were not printed or stored in the report.
Decoder source `cdeafc7d...`, binary `a7a03802...`, and runner
`49afb9e9...` are pinned.

## Core Frontier

qcore records two registration attempts at the exact two SetupComplete uplink
times. It rejects both because the phone presents a stale/foreign temporary
5G-GUTI whose embedded PLMN and AMF identifiers do not match this private
`901/70` core. No authentication, PDU session, or private data follows.

The RF and gNB RRCSetup/SetupComplete frontier is therefore crossed. The active
blocker is qcore's temporary-identity handling: a nonmatching GUTI must be
treated as unknown and drive the standards-compliant identity path, rather than
terminating registration solely on stale PLMN/AMF metadata.
