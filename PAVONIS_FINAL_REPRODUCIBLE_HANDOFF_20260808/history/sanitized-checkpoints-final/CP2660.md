## Root Cause

The post-CP2660 no-RF authoritative USIM Binder read finds 14 EF_FPLMN entries
and `90170` is present. CP2550/CP2561's authoritative pre-CP2657 baseline had
13 entries and explicitly excluded `90170`.

CP2657 reached qcore and the old branch rejected the phone with 5GMM cause
`PLMN not allowed`. qcore source confirms that exact cause. The temporal and
protocol chain is therefore direct:

1. CP2657 first real phone registration reaches the old reject;
2. the modem/USIM adds serving PLMN [network-id-redacted] to EF_FPLMN;
3. CP2659 and CP2660 continue measuring the cell but emit no Msg1.

The CP2658 qcore fallback is not rejected by either run; it was never reached.
