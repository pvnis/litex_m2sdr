Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich

## No-RF Reboot Gate

The Android phone was rebooted while the gNB and srsUE were stopped. The
self-contained [host-redacted] helper observed ADB going offline, returning as an
authorized device, Android boot completion, and then performed the post-boot
checks.

Critical persistent result:

- EF_FPLMN count: 13;
- `90170` occurrences: 0;
- canonical list hash:
  `20825f15d52cb0f59cc8ffc4f83879e50b9c729a552b6084a4497535daf3ddab`;
- network selection mode: automatic (`1`).

This proves CP2661 changed the actual USIM record and that the modem reloaded
the repaired list after a full boot.

## Policy State

Full user RAT mask including NR, preferred mode6 33, Oplus mode6 33, roaming
off, and advanced calling true all survived the reboot. VoNR alone reset to
false, causing the combined helper to return rc1 after the critical FPLMN and
selection gates had already passed.

The established root voice-policy helper restored VoNR/advanced-calling to
true/true and verified readback. Final phone baseline is therefore:

- ADB: authorized device;
- selection: automatic;
- full RAT mask with NR;
- mode6/Oplus mode6: 33/33;
- roaming: 0;
- VoNR/advanced calling: true/true;
- EF_FPLMN: count13, no `90170`.
