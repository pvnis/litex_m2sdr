## Interpretation

CP2658 solved only the stale-GUTI form covered by its integration fixture. The
real phone uses stale GUTI=[subscriber-redacted] for initial context lookup but sends SUCI/SUPI
with its legitimate private lab home PLMN [network-id-redacted] in NAS. In this roaming test
shape, qcore must authorize the subscriber by the configured private SIM
database while using its own serving-network name `90170` for AKA. It must not
reject merely because the SUPI home PLMN differs from the serving PLMN.

## Decision

Stay no-RF. Add a separate default-off, cached qcore gate for an authorized
SUPI/SUCI whose home PLMN differs from the serving PLMN. Preserve SIM database
lookup, AKA, NAS security, RRC security, and registration checks; do not reuse
the GUTI condition or bypass authentication. Add default-off reject and enabled
full-auth integration tests. Only after that passes, remove the newly re-added
`90170` again, reboot/re-audit, and run RF with both identity gates active.
