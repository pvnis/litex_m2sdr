## Known parser defects

The operator-selection helper returned rc1 even though the raw TSV proves the
attach. Its summary compares the operator field without accounting for the
trailing delimiter (`90170,`), so it reported `private_plmn_seen=0` against 86
authoritative matching rows.

The gNB wrapper's `CONNECTED_COUNT=0` is also not an attach verdict; its text
counter does not cover this standalone log vocabulary. qcore PDU activation
and Android's in-service PDU interface are authoritative.

## Data probe verdict

The existing Android-shell probe found the correct interface and address but
failed before sending packets:

```text
ping: SO_BINDTODEVICE: Operation not permitted
DATA_WATCH_PING_RC=2
```

This is an instrumentation-void data result, not packet loss or a user-plane
failure. The unprivileged ADB shell lacks permission for `ping -I` on the
cellular interface. No successful private packet proof is claimed here.

After roughly five minutes qcore released the idle context. A subsequent
security-protected registration update was rejected because its outer TMSI
context was unavailable; this occurred after the proven initial PDU session
and remains a reconnect/persistence follow-up, not an invalidation of attach.
