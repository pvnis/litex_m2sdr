## Validated decode path

The srsRAN MAC pcap uses DLT 149 with UDP framing. On [host-redacted], Wireshark 4.2.2
was configured with the User-2-to-UDP mapping, the `mac_nr_udp` heuristic, and
the exact DRB mapping from the deployed 5QI 7 profile: LCID 4 to DRB1, RLC-UM,
12-bit RLC SN in both directions.

This decodes 132 uplink LCID 4 frames. The first DRB traffic starts at
`[date-redacted]`, after the gNB registered EPS bearer 1 / LCID 4 at
`[date-redacted]`. The first traffic contains sequential 18-bit PDCP data
headers starting at SN 0, followed immediately by valid IPv4.

Most decisively, the capture contains exactly 12 complete RLC-UM SDUs whose IP
source/destination are `[ip-redacted] -> [ip-redacted]`. They occur from
`[date-redacted]` through `[date-redacted]`, matching the three four-packet
CP2669 ping forms. Their PDCP sequence numbers are 16, 17, 22, 23, 33, 35, 40,
41, 42, 52, 54, and 55; intervening sequence numbers carry ordinary Android
background traffic. Each gateway packet is a complete 87-byte RLC SDU: a
three-byte PDCP header plus an 84-byte IPv4 ICMP request.

## Source audit

The deployed source and local source hashes match for RLC-UM, PDCP-NR, the
eNB PDCP wrapper, the GTP-U adapter, and GTP-U. The intended path is:

```text
MAC LCID4 -> RLC-UM -> pdcp::write_pdu -> PDCP-NR reorder/decap
  -> gtpu_pdcp_adapter -> gtpu::write_pdu -> UDP/2152
```

The configured radio bearer explicitly advertises `sdap-HeaderUL absent` and
the captured PDCP SDUs begin directly with IPv4 after the PDCP header. Thus the
unimplemented standalone SDAP layer is not the CP2669 uplink blocker. GTP-U
also emitted no missing-[interface-redacted] warning, invalid-IP error, or `sendto` error at
warning log level.

## Closed theories

- Android route selection or root ping semantics: closed.
- Phone modem not transmitting private packets: closed.
- Gross UL RF, PUSCH CRC, MAC LCID, or RLC segmentation failure: closed.
- Unexpected UL SDAP header: closed for this configured bearer and capture.
- Malformed IP at the air-side PDCP input: closed.
