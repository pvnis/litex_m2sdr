## Identity correction

CP2675 compared a successful srsENB `enb_id=411`/cell identity `105217` with
OCUDU `gnb_id=332`/NCI `5439488` as if the integer IDs had the same encoding.
They do not. OCUDU `gnb_id=411` produces NR NCI `411 << 14 = 6733824`, which
the phone reports on air. It does not reproduce cell identity `105217`.

Therefore the `332 -> 411` hypothesis is closed and must not be retried. Before
another RF identity experiment, determine the successful NR cell-identity bit
layout and whether OCUDU exposes the matching low sector/cell bits. Keep the
CP2392 OCUDU/M2SDR+srsUE and CP2674 phone goldens unchanged.
