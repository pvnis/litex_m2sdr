# CP2677: exact phone-proven NCI 105217 emitted by OCUDU

Status: PASS, NO RF. The deployed CP2392 OCUDU binary can reproduce the cell
identity used by the successful CP2674 phone baseline without changing any
other CP2626 broadcast or RACH field.

## Question

Can OCUDU represent the successful control's cell identity `105217` exactly,
rather than incorrectly treating srsENB `enb_id=411` as OCUDU's default
22-bit gNB ID?

## Source and schema proof

OCUDU constructs the 36-bit NCI as:

```text
(gnb_id << (36 - gnb_id_bit_length)) | sector_id
```

The deployed CP2392 binary remains hash `1add70ea...` and advertises
`gnb_id_bit_length` in the supported range 22 through 32. Its cell schema also
accepts `cell_cfg.sector_id`. The successful identity is represented by one
coherent identity tuple:

```text
gnb_id=411, gnb_id_bit_length=28, sector_id=1
411 << 8 | 1 = 105217
```

This also matches the successful srsENB construction `(enb_id << 8) +
cell_id` for `411/1`. CP2676's raw `gnb_id=411` used OCUDU's default 22-bit
length and therefore emitted the different NCI `6733824`.

## Emission proof

An isolated ZMQ config was derived from CP2675's exact CP2626-shape config.
Parsed comparison proves that only `gnb_id`, `gnb_id_bit_length`, and
`cell_cfg.sector_id` differ. No M2SDR or RF device was opened.

- qcore accepted NG Setup from the changed identity tuple.
- OCUDU logged one SIB1 and emitted the 36-bit cell identity
  `[hex-value-redacted]`, decimal `105217`.
- The CP2675 semantic comparator now finds `112/118` common named SIB1 tokens
  against the successful phone control, up from `111/118`; cell identity is no
  longer a difference.
- The remaining differences are the already classified post-Msg1 or
  non-blocking fields: SearchSpace1 AL8 candidate count, timing-advance offset,
  `msg3-DeltaPreamble`, `p-Max`, other-SI search space, `t311`, TX direct-current
  location, and ASN.1 container naming.
- qcore and gNB were stopped; no RF process remains.

## Decision

The exact-identity RF discriminator is unlocked. Run one bounded phone attempt
on the frozen CP2626/CP2676 radio shape with the three-field identity tuple as
the only semantic change. Judge TX health first, then exact phone-visible NCI,
operator target/camping, and Msg1. Do not retry raw `gnb_id=411` alone.
