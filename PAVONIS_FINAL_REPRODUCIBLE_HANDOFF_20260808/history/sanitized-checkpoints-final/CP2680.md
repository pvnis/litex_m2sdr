# CP2680: Phone policy is clean; MIB intra-frequency reselection is the next discriminator

This no-RF checkpoint closes the remaining CP2679 phone-state ambiguity and
finds one broadcast semantic that the CP2675 SIB1-only comparison could not
see. No RF, build, deploy, phone-state change, or canonical-file change was
made.

## CP2679 phone-state reclassification

The CP2679 Qualcomm radio extract does not contain a registration rejection.
Its repeated status fields contain `rejectCause=0` 493 times, no nonzero reject
cause, no error 38, and no forbidden/not-suitable/denied indication. It contains
612 `NOT_REG_SEARCHING` tokens and repeatedly reports emergency-capable/search
state, but no RRC or NAS access attempt. The wrapper's earlier count of 1104
"reject" strings was therefore a label-counting artifact, not 1104 modem
rejections.

The target PLMN is absent from the phone's forbidden-PLMN records. CP2679 also
already ran with the same policy as the successful CP2674 control: data roaming
was enabled during RF and restored afterward; VoNR and advanced calling were
disabled. CP2674 itself attached automatically while the manual operator scan
was incomplete. FPLMN state, roaming, voice policy, and scan completion are
closed as standalone explanations and must not be retried.

## Uplink observability gap

CP2679's 1908 stage-1 rows form a deterministic 16-residue SFN lattice. Only
`SFN mod 256 = 49` is elevated: 120 rows, mean raw RMS `-63.588 dB`, maximum
`-59.412 dB`, and 42 rows at or above `-62 dB`. Every other residue averages
about `-67.5` to `-68.3 dB` and has zero rows at or above `-62 dB`. This fixed
scheduled class is not evidence that the phone transmitted Msg1. If the MIB
test is negative, the next instrument remains an independent, bounded bladeRF
UL observer rather than more M2SDR detector-envelope [interface-redacted].

## MIB comparison

The successful CP2674 srsENB control and CP2679 OCUDU phone shape use the same
phone-visible SSB ARFCN `368410`, PCI `500`, Band 3 geometry, and successful
identity `NCI 105217`. The successful srsENB MIB is:

- common SCS `15/60`, `ssb-SubcarrierOffset=6`;
- `dmrs-TypeA-Position=pos2`;
- CORESET0 `6`, SearchSpace0 `0`;
- `cellBarred=notBarred`;
- `intraFreqReselection=allowed`.

The CP2679 generated OCUDU configuration has common SCS 15 kHz, CORESET0 6,
PCI 500, and the same SSB placement. OCUDU's MIB packer obtains SearchSpace0
from the common configuration, derives the other geometry from the cell
configuration, hard-codes `cellBarred=notBarred`, and independently hard-codes
`intraFreqReselection=notAllowed` in
`ocudu/lib/du/du_high/du_manager/converters/asn1_sys_info_packer.cpp`.

This is the sole newly named pre-access semantic. Under the standard it mainly
governs frequency reselection after barring and should not reject a nonbarred
cell, so it is a discriminator rather than a claimed root cause. Qualcomm
behavior is the relevant empirical question.

## Decision

Add a minimal default-off OCUDU override that changes only the MIB
`intraFreqReselection` value to `allowed`, prove the disabled path preserves the
CP2392 binary behavior, build/deploy it in isolation, and run one frozen
CP2679-shape phone RF test with a fresh stamp. Judge on phone camping and any
gNB PRACH/RACH indication. If it remains pre-Msg1, restore the isolated binary
and proceed directly to the bounded independent bladeRF UL observer.
