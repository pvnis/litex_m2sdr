## Audit correction

CP2692's all-timed policy is not sufficient to explain the current failure.
CP2693 proves that removing all continuation timestamps and restoring the
CP2497 continuous policy does not recover the current external waveform.

The installed source-tree Soapy module is exactly
`6f625ad51935712b46bd9be397f3454f4f51699a8550fb4c2465149ce3080cac`,
matching CP2497 and the passing CP2691 helper. CP2693 requested a capped worker
trace, but CP2246 supplied its historical RAR-event arm and no RAR occurred;
therefore no worker trace file exists. This run is valid for RF decode and TX
health but void for reader/descriptor occupancy or resynchronization claims.
