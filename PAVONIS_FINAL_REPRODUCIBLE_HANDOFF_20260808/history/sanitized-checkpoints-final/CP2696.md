# CP2696: Structured helper passes at the live 30.72 MHz TX bandwidth

CP2696 changes one setting from the current CP2691 helper baseline: M2SDR TX
bandwidth is `30.72 MHz` instead of `10 MHz`. The preserved CP1481 payload,
partial-write-safe CP1166 starter, physical channel1, 11.52 Msps, 1842.5 MHz
LO, ATT5, prime1 reader and dual bladeRF gain36/10 MHz capture are unchanged.

The M2SDR readback proves 30.72 MHz before and after stream activation. The
helper sends all 180,000,000 samples in 176,130 writes with zero errors and
zero short writes.

RX2 passes strongly:

- `-25.4458 dBFS`, max842 and unclipped;
- one-shot expected PCI1 PBCH CRC OK at CSI `+19.3 dB`;
- `379/847` aligned PBCH windows and `331/847` PCI1 CSI windows;
- best CSI `+20.9 dB`.

RX1 is the weak coupling leg in this run: `-44.7384 dBFS`, max65,
`0/847` PBCH but `334/847` PCI1 CSI windows, best approximately `0 dB`. RX2 is
the authoritative channel-1 observer and is decisively positive.

## Verdict

The live gNB's 30.72 MHz TX bandwidth and current AD9361 filter/calibration
state are not the external decode failure. CP2695 and CP2696 now form a direct
split on the same module, port, bandwidth and receiver: live has a perfectly
clean capture-epoch worker stream but `0/847`; the structured helper is
hundreds of PBCH-positive windows.

Before changing prime or gateware behavior, use CP2695's saved RX2 IQ for a
wide SSB-frequency/offset search. Same-device self-RX can hide a shared
absolute LO displacement; finding PCI1 away from the requested 1839.65 MHz
would explain self-RX-pass/external-fail without contradicting the clean
descriptors. If no PCI1 exists anywhere in the sampled band, then test helper
prime128 as the remaining current live/helper control difference.
