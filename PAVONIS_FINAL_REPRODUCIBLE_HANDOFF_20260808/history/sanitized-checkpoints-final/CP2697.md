# CP2697: CP2404 offset artifact corrected; live RX2 PBCH is strong

CP2697 stays no-RF and audits CP2695's saved external IQ across absolute SSB
offset. A 50 ms CP2696 helper positive control first validates the instrument:
at `-2.850 MHz` it decodes PCI1 PBCH CRC OK with CSI `+18.8 dB`.

The CP2695 RX2 sweep then tests 161 offsets from `-4.0` to `+4.0 MHz` in
50 kHz steps. It finds exactly one PCI1/PBCH-positive point: `-2.850 MHz`,
equivalent to analyzer SSB `1839656000` against CP2404's capture center
`1842506000`. That point is PBCH CRC OK at CSI `+23.6 dB`, CFO `+3315 Hz`.

The historical CP2404 analyzer instead used `1839650000` against the same
`1842506000` center, or `-2.856 MHz`. Its decoder did not tolerate the residual
under that coordinate and reported false `0/847` results.

## Corrected live results

Re-running the complete CP2695 aligned sweep at the discovered offset gives:

- RX2 one-shot PBCH CRC OK at `+23.2 dB`, corrected sweep `149/847` PBCH,
  `100/847` PCI1 CSI, best `+24.5 dB`;
- RX1 remains the weak leg: `0/847` PBCH, `74/847` PCI1 CSI, best `+1.0 dB`.

The correction is reproducible on every saved live RX2 file:

- CP2692: PBCH CRC OK, CSI `+23.9 dB`;
- CP2693: PBCH CRC OK, CSI `+23.8 dB`;
- CP2695: PBCH CRC OK, CSI `+23.6 dB`.

Therefore CP2692/CP2693/CP2695's external-waveform-negative interpretation is
superseded. Their TX-policy and worker-trace measurements remain valid, but
there is no live coherence failure in those captures.
