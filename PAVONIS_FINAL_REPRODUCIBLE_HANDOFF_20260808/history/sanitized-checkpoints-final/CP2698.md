## Verdict

The RF/configuration portion is valid, but the phone portion is void. The run
used the isolated native OCUDU binary `052c9e...`, exact phone NCI `105217`,
physical M2SDR channel 1, and TX attenuation `0 dB`. The packaged YAML is the
expected `fcf77cc8...`, both host realtime gates passed, and the live TX path
completed with zero Soapy timeouts, downlink-late events, partial returns, or
sample deficits.

The required phone stimulus did not happen. Before monitoring, the n3/NR-only
apply failed with `mode_rc=1 system_rc=99`; the fixed-coordinate RadioInfo
helper found zero preferred-network spinners. The subsequent 270-second phone
monitor therefore cannot judge OCUDU acquisition: its zero private-PLMN, NR,
service, camping, and IP rows are explicitly stimulus-void.

## Decision

Diagnose and clear the phone UI/apply failure no-RF, prove the authoritative
NR-only helper again, then repeat this exact RF/config shape under a fresh
stamp. Do not retune the OCUDU waveform, scheduler, thresholds, or phone NCI
from this void run.
