## Calibration Caveat

The capture files are valid, but the original CP2698 postprocessor reused the
CP2392 PCI1/SSB1839650000 geometry. This exact phone profile is PCI500 with SSB
ARFCN368410 (`1842050000 Hz`). Therefore its original `0/847` capture labels
are analysis-void and are superseded by CP2702.

## Decision

PCI500/SSB geometry. If MIB/PDCCH/SIB1 pass on air, proceed to an independent
bladeRF uplink observer rather than more DL power or PRACH detector [interface-redacted].
