## Analyzer Correction

No RF was used. CP2698/CP2701 captured the exact phone profile centered at
`1842500000 Hz`, but postprocessed it as the CP2392 PCI1 cell at
`1839650000 Hz`. The live phone profile is PCI500 and its CORESET0 index 6
places the SSB at ARFCN368410 (`1842050000 Hz`, offset `-450000 Hz`).

The CP812, CP814, and CP837 analyzers now accept a default-preserving
`--expected-pci` option. Their default remains PCI1. The exact-phone wrapper is
pinned to SSB `1842050000` and explicit PCI500. Regression controls pass:

- CP2696 PCI1 default: PBCH/CSI expected-PCI true, SNR `+19.3 dB`;
- CP2701 PCI500 explicit: PBCH/CSI expected-PCI true, SNR `+22.2 dB`;
- CP837 at `-450000 Hz`: PCI500 PBCH CRC OK and CSI `+22.6 dB`.

## On-Air SI Decode

The validated live-SearchSpace0 harness was run on saved RX2 IQ using PBCH
SFN576/t_offset481651, PCI500, CORESET0 index6, DL ARFCN368500, and SSB
ARFCN368410. Across 64 timing probes and SI occasions at SFN576 and SFN592:

- found DCI: `10`;
- decoded SI PDSCH: `10`;
- every decoded transport block is `80 B`, CRC OK;
- successful offsets are `-1720..-1592` samples;
- median PDSCH-DMRS EPRE is `24.46 dB`;
- median PDSCH SNR is `21.0 dB`.

One representative row decodes SI-RNTI DCI 1_0 at AL2/ncce0, grant
`f_alloc=0x150`, PRBs `(1,8)`, symbols `(2,13)`, followed by the complete
80-byte SIB1 transport block with CRC OK. The payload is retained in the local
log and is not reproduced here.

## Decision

DL MIB, SI-PDCCH, SI-PDSCH/SIB1, exact phone identity, and TX health are all
proven. The phone still does not camp and the gNB sees no Msg1. The next rung is
the previously queued bounded independent bladeRF RX2 uplink observation during
the exact phone run, timed around NR-only/n3 activation. This decides whether
the phone emits an unseen Msg1 or never transmits at all; do not [interface-redacted] the gNB
PRACH detector until that split is measured.
