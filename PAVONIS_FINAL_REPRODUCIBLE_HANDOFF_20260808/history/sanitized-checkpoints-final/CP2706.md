## Question

real-phone Msg1 positive control from the preserved CP2674 srsENB attach.

perturb the control. CP2618 already validates the independent M2SDR RX2 path at

  `1747500000 Hz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `50`.
- It keeps a bounded two-second CF32 ring in memory. The first `RACH:` line from
  the preserved srsENB run touches a fresh arm file; the observer then retains
  one post-event second.
- The three-second event window is converted to SC16 and analyzed by the
  unchanged CP2703 energy analyzer and CP2705 target-subband scanner.
- Full CF32/SC16 raw files are deleted remotely after analysis. Only bounded
  event snippets, summaries, and hashes are packaged.
- `cp2706_run_srsenb_phone_ul_positive_control.sh` invokes the unmodified
  CP2674 user-plane wrapper with a fresh stamp and deploys no gNB or phone
  binary/config changes.
