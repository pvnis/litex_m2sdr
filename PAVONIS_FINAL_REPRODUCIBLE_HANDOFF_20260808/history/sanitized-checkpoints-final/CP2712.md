## Verdict

This is the valid unchanged retry required by the CP2710 cold-start rule. The
gNB ran the exact NCI105217, physical-channel-1, ATT0 shape with only MIB
`intraFreqReselection=allowed` active. Propagation, exact YAML hash
`fcf77cc8...`, MIB value proof count `2`, and the complete phone monitor pass.

The RF/runtime health gate is clean: zero stream deficit, Soapy timeouts,
deadline timeouts, errors, retry abandonment, and downlink-late events.

The phone sees the intended cell strongly but does not camp or transmit Msg1:

- `48` rows report exact PCI500/TAC7/NCI105217/ARFCN368410/Band3;
- `26` exact-cell rows have valid `RSRP -68 dBm`, `RSRQ -11 dB`, and
  `SINR +22 dB`;
- all valid registration rows remain `NOT_REG_SEARCHING`, emergency-only,
  reject cause `0`, with no registered PLMN or cellular IP;
- the gNB records `1901` empty PRACH reports, zero nonempty reports, zero
  stage-1 selections, and zero RAR allocation/encoding;
- the stage-1 sidecar has `1904` rows and zero selections.

The wrapper's final exact-identity gate reported zero only because Android
formats the band field as `[3]` while the parser accepted only bare `3`. The
underlying TSV is complete and independently proves `48` exact rows, so this
is a telemetry false-negative, not an RF or semantic void.

## Decision

MIB `intraFreqReselection` is exonerated as the pre-Msg1 blocker. Do not rerun
this A/B and do not [interface-redacted] PRACH: the validated independent observer already
proved that the phone emits no configured-band Msg1 under OCUDU. Continue
no-RF with a path-aware structural comparison of the actual-air OCUDU SIB1 and
the CP2674 srsENB phone-golden SIB1, because the existing 112/118 comparison is
token-based and may hide hierarchy, list, or bit-string differences.
