# CP2720: srsENB versus OCUDU exposes a SIB1 cadence discriminator

Status: PASS, no RF.

## Structured Comparison

`cp2720_compare_broadcast_cadence.py` joins the successful CP2674 phone radio
log, the failed CP2718 radio log, CP2718's bounded SI-occasion TSV, and the two
scheduler sources. It does not inspect raw IQ or verbose gNB logs.

The two cells share a `10 ms` SSB periodicity and the same decoded
`ssb-PeriodicityServingCell=ms10`. Their SIB1 retransmission cadence differs:

- preserved srsENB opens the SIB1 window at slot 0 of every even frame, an
  effective `20 ms` retransmission period;
- OCUDU's scheduler expert default is `160 ms`;
- CP2718 proves that default on air-facing scheduler output: `1366` slot
  summaries, `1365` PDCCH rows, `1365` PDSCH rows, and all `1365` modular slot
  deltas are exactly `160`; every occasion is `slot mod 160 = 1`.

CP2718 wall-clock intervals have median `160.087 ms` (minimum `157.565`,
maximum `230.566`), but the slot-domain cadence is exact. This is not a missing
PDU or scheduler jitter finding; it is a configured repetition-rate split.

## Phone Join

For exact NCI105217 PS registration records:

- CP2674 srsENB: three `NOT_REG_SEARCHING`/emergency records followed by seven
  `ROAMING`/data records;
- CP2718 OCUDU: 55 records, all `NOT_REG_SEARCHING`/emergency.

All feasible decoded MIB/SIB1 field semantics were already matched in CP2718,
so this is the first concrete remaining below-SIB1 broadcast discriminator.
The finding does not claim the 160 ms behavior is standards-invalid; it names
a controlled compatibility test against the known-working modem stimulus.

## Decision

Add a cached, default-off OCUDU SIB1 retransmission-period override for `20 ms`
without changing SSB, payload, power, thresholds, PRACH, or scheduling of any
other channel. Build it in an isolated binary and prove a 20-slot SI cadence
no-RF before one frozen phone RF discriminator.
