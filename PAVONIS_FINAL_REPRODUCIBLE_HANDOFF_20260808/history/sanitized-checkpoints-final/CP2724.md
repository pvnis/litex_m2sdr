Date: [date-redacted]

Status: PASS, no RF.

## Finding

After CP2723 closes retransmission cadence alone, source plus live trace expose
one remaining scheduler-level compatibility discriminator in the same
SSB/SearchSpace0 geometry: successful srsENB transmits SIB1 at the Type-0
monitoring occasion `n0`, while OCUDU deliberately transmits it at `n0 + 1`.

The successful phone control's srsENB source opens the SIB1 window only when
`slot_idx == 0` and `SFN` is even. Its SSB scheduler also emits the 10 ms SSB at
slot 0. The matched profile is 15 kHz FDD, 10 MHz, CORESET0 index 6,
SearchSpace0 index 0, one SSB beam.

OCUDU's `sib1_scheduler.cpp` explicitly calls
`precompute_type0_pdcch_css_n0_plus_1()` with the comment that this avoids an
SSB/SIB1 collision. The helper is exactly `precompute_type0_pdcch_css_n0(...) +
1`. CP2723 proves this policy live: with 20 ms cadence, SI lands at
`slot_mod160 = 1,21,41,61,81,101,121,141`, whereas the successful srsENB policy
starts at slot 0 of even frames.

OCUDU's own comment states that the UE monitors SearchSpaceSet0 in two
consecutive slots starting at `n0`, so both policies are intended to be valid.
This checkpoint therefore does not claim a standards violation. It identifies
the next exact known-working-modem compatibility A/B after payload semantics
and cadence have been exhausted.
