path are exonerated. CP2734's no-cell result belongs specifically to OCUDU's
new SoapyBladeRF transmit presentation.

TX stream. OCUDU's internal samples use full int16 scale, while SoapyBladeRF's
native CS16 format is Q11 with full scale [date-redacted] and its one-channel CS16 path

32767, and SoapyBladeRF's CF32 path multiplies by [date-redacted] before libbladeRF.
