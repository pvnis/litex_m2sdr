## Result

The wrapper correctly stopped before phone preparation because the required
`PAVONIS_SOAPY_BLADERF_TX_REARM_AFTER_RX_SETUP active` marker was absent from
the gNB stdout. qcore and the gNB were stopped, no phone state was changed, and
both hosts passed the idle postcheck.

This was not a radio or lifecycle verdict. The isolated module hash and marker
string both verified, the gNB configured channel 1, connected N2, and reached
`==== gNB started ====` with normalized CF32 active. The complete stdout had:

```text
requested=0 active=0 siso=0 cf32=1 n2=1 started=1
```

The missing `PAVONIS_SOAPY_BLADERF_SISO_CHANNEL1 active` row is decisive: that
known-active branch also logs through `SoapySDR::logf(SOAPY_SDR_INFO, ...)`,
and OCUDU's logger suppresses that route at its current level. The new rearm
markers use the same route. Their absence therefore cannot prove the branch
was disabled.

## Verdict and next checkpoint

Classify CP2738 as RF-started but phone/RF-judgment void. Do not count it as a
no-cell result and do not alter lifecycle behavior. Add telemetry-only stderr
proof at the same setup-time request/success points, rebuild the isolated
module, repeat the no-sample enabled/disabled harness, and then retry one fresh
phone A/B with the mandatory marker gate intact.
