## No-RF helper correction

The first helper gate exposed two test assumptions rather than modem failures.
QCSuper buffers the PCAP header until shutdown, and its `--adb` diagnostic
transport may report `sys.usb.config=adb` while the diagnostic bridge is live.
The helper now gates start on a live process plus the `Enabled logging` marker,
keeps ADB authorized, and validates the PCAP only after a graceful SIGINT.

The final cross-SSH gate passed:

- start and status reported the same live PID;
- graceful stop wrote a valid 24-byte empty PCAP;
- the capture PID was gone afterward;
- phone USB state and authorization were restored to `adb` / `device`;
- deployed helper SHA-256 is
  `6a94d3990afff25588322340a6193a06ce27b79b2176c4f56579ccbc7149e9fd`.
