## Observer root cause

The new phase TSV resolves CP2762's transition failure:

```text

cleanup_enter rc=1 capture_started=0
```

The corresponding SSH output nevertheless contains the exact authoritative
remote marker:

```text

```

`ssh_pexpect_run.py` returned 1 after receiving the complete successful remote
output. The wrapper's `set -e` treated that transport/helper exit artifact as a
remote failure and aborted before capture start. This explains CP2762 and
CP2765 without implicating the gNB, remote control helper, or bladeRF.
