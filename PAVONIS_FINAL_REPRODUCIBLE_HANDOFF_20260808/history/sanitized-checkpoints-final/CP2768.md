## Analysis

The strongest events are almost entirely inside the configured 1.5 MHz PRACH
slice, but their estimated 90-percent occupied widths are only about
0.45–0.66 MHz and their centers move across roughly -2.2 to -3.1 MHz relative
to capture center. That is not the fixed ~1.08 MHz signature expected from the
configured long-sequence NR PRACH.

An onset-aware offline converter emitted 36 aligned CF32 windows. Existing
CP1965 replay results are:

- baseline: 0/36 accepted, maximum p0 ratio 0.092
- stage1 policy: 0/26,820 transforms accepted, maximum p0 ratio 0.140

Thus CP2767 proves strong UL-band activity during the phone run, but these
pre-event bursts are not detector-confirmed Msg1 and must not be used to [interface-redacted]
M2SDR collection timing. The first QCSuper UL-CCCH remains outside the capture.
