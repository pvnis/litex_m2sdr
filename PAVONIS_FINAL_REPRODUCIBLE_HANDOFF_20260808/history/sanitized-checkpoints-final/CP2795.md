# CP2795 - Paired phase collection passes; phone remains pre-Msg1

CP2795 ran the CP2794 three-phase 17 ms collector with the isolated native gNB
`9a74982f...` on M2SDR physical channel 1. The run is mechanically valid:
realtime preflights passed, RF wrapper rc was zero, TX timeout/downlink-late/
release-late/sample-deficit counts were all zero, and no process was left
running.

The new collection mechanism is proven live. The manifest has 567/566/567
rows for sequence indices 0/1/2, with delays `0`, `1474560`, and `1647360`
samples. Every shifted sequence reports the paired `+160` slot offset. Stage-1
ran 635/636/636 rows and 4438/4445/4445 candidate evaluations. It selected
zero preambles. Eleven bounded strong-window files were retained; their best
RMS was `-57.10 dB`.

The phone received the cell but did not enter service: 65/80 monitor rows were
NR, with zero private-PLMN/service rows. QCSuper contains 67 MIB, 66 SIB1, and
one generated UL-CCCH record, but no DL-CCCH. As established at CP2754, the
UL-CCCH record is modem message generation and is not proof that Msg1 went over
the air.

The independent 65 s bladeRF observer saw an energetic residue-89/90 family,
but it is not a clean PRACH witness. Its late capture clipped heavily
(`591718` clipped components), and the cross-host clock fit is weak
(`r=0.266`). Two events nominally land at the end of sequence-0 windows, while
the exact paired M2SDR rows remain floor-class at `-67.38/-67.02 dB` RMS.

Offline replay closes the detector axis for these saved M2SDR files:

- baseline: `0/11`, best p0 ratio `0.146`;
- CFO-only: `0/2211`, best `0.163`;
- deployed stage-1 grid: `0/8195`, best `0.174`;
- 155159 completed extreme timing/CFO evaluations: zero accepts, best `0.186`.

The remaining exhaustive tail was stopped because it was expensive and showed
no acceptance trend. Its partial JSONL was not retained; the compact counts
above are authoritative.

A channel audit prevents a false hardware pivot. CP2143's original UL golden
used M2SDR RX channel 0, but CP2618 subsequently proved bladeRF TX2 to M2SDR
RX2/channel 1 at `46.77 dB` over median. CP2795 directly read back RX channel 1
at the requested frequency and gain. Do not repeat the closed RX2 tone control.

Verdict: paired phase collection works, but CP2795 remains pre-Msg1 and its
saved windows do not contain detector-recoverable PRACH. Do not widen live
PRACH timing, CFO, or thresholds from this run. Return to the M2SDR-specific
downlink/cell-selection property that separates the passing CP2744
OCUDU/bladeRF run from the phone-visible but pre-access M2SDR runs.
