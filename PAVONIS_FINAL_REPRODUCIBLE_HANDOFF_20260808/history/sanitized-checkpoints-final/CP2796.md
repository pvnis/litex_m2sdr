# CP2796 - Long downlink sample-domain SFN continuity no-RF gate

CP2796 replaces the inconclusive cross-host/QCSuper timing inference with a
same-capture sample-domain test. QCSuper confirms that the MIB SFN MSBs begin
at BCCH-BCH payload bit 1, but it buffers DIAG records and its version-12
timing field is mostly zero. PCAP wall timestamps therefore cannot judge live
air-time SFN continuity.

The prepared RF diagnostic captures 65 seconds on [host-redacted] bladeRF RX2 at
`1842500000 Hz`, `11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, gain `32`. On [host-redacted] it extracts
128 bounded 25 ms slices every 500 ms from 0.5 through 64.0 seconds, packages
only those slices, and leaves the 2.995 GB raw file remote. Local analysis runs
the established `ssb_file_test` at PCI500/SSB `1842050000 Hz`, combines each
decoded PBCH offset with its slice start, and compares MIB SFN progression to
the exact sample-domain ten-millisecond frame count.

The analyzer passes a real known-good control using CP2701 RX2 IQ:

- 5/5 slices decode PBCH at PCI500;
- five distinct SFNs;
- zero nonzero SFN residuals and maximum residual zero frames;
- median SNR `22.8 dB`;
- continuity verdict PASS.

Local syntax, Python compilation, source hashes, fresh-path checks, and the
default-closed RF gate pass. The closed wrapper returns rc2 and creates no run
directory. The capture/control/extractor helpers are hash-proven on [host-redacted];
their detached no-RF lifecycle smoke passes. ADB is authorized and bladeRF is
idle.

After CP2795 had been reported and its compact observer TSV/summary retained,
its exact remote 2,995,200,000-byte raw capture was deleted. No other run
artifact was removed.
