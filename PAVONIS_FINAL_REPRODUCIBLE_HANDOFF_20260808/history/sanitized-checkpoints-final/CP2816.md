# CP2816 - One-shot plus all-timed fails at timestamp remainder gate

Stamp: `[date-redacted]CP2816_M2SDR_PHONE_DL_SFN_DMA_PROGRAM_ALLTIMED`.

CP2816 is a complete, mechanism-live RF run. Kernel one-shot markers rise from
two to three, the postcheck passes with program mode enabled, and cleanup plus
the resume-state check prove the parameter returned to `N` with no gNB left
running. The live gNB environment and TX readback both prove all-timed chunks
enabled; stale zero-fill remains off, prime remains 128, and worker trace is
off.

Realtime health is clean: zero Soapy TX timeouts, zero downlink-late lines, zero
write/stream deficit, 3,518,991,210 requested and returned samples, and no
worker late/underflow/acquire errors. Downlink remains strong at 127/128 PCI500
PBCH and median 21.0 dB SNR, but continuity is worse. Residual states are one
slice at zero, four at -8, and 122 at -10, with adjacent decoded transitions
of 75.707639 ms and 19.310243 ms. One slice fails PBCH.

The phone remains pre-Msg1: 67/83 monitor rows see NR, none camp or enter
service. The QCSuper capture has 152 records. Stage-1 has 1903 rows, 1891
energy-gated rows, 13,237 candidate-list evaluations, zero selections, maximum
raw RMS -60.953048 dB, and maximum search time 19,472 us; no RAR is produced.

The decisive source/runtime join is below OCUDU. Its stop summary records
3,665,616 `all_timed_writes` and 939,312 HAS_TIME sub-writes, but the LiteX
release/worker summaries contain only 2,288 HAS_TIME descriptors among
3,443,239 packets; dedup suppression is zero. The loss occurs in the existing
contiguous partial-remainder branch in `writeStream()`: when a timed caller
write lands inside an existing untimed DMA remainder within one MTU, CP2074B
keeps the partial descriptor and clears `SOAPY_SDR_HAS_TIME`. This avoided
short non-EOB descriptors under LOOP mode, but means the one-shot arbiter still
receives overwhelmingly untimed continuation and cannot reject late logical
waveform resumption.

Verdict: one-shot plus current all-timed is rejected as implemented, but the
all-timed mechanism itself was not delivered to DMA descriptors. Next add a
default-off contiguous-remainder timestamp backfill: derive the descriptor
start timestamp from incoming timed sample minus the existing remainder
offset, attach it to the full descriptor without releasing a short buffer, and
prove descriptor-level HAS_TIME coverage in no-sample/finite-write controls
before RF. Preserve CP2074B behavior by default.
