Stamp: `[date-redacted]CP2819_M2SDR_PHONE_DL_SFN_DMA_PROGRAM_ALLTIMED_BACKFILL`.

CP2819 is a complete, mechanism-proven RF run. Kernel one-shot markers rise
from three to four, the postcheck passes, and cleanup/resume prove program mode
returned to `N` with [host-redacted] idle. Live environment proof covers all-timed,
backfill, prime128, and stale-zero off. The packaged gNB log contains the 16

The backfill reaches the descriptor layer. OCUDU records 938,532 HAS_TIME
sub-writes; LiteX releases 929,281 timed descriptors among 3,440,410 packets,
versus only 2,288 timed descriptors in CP2816. This is a 406-fold increase and
delivers about 99% of the caller timestamp opportunities. The remaining
2,511,129 descriptors are continuation packets, not evidence that the patch
failed.

has SFN residual zero, and the transition count is zero across the 65-second
capture. Realtime health remains usable: zero Soapy TX timeouts, zero
downlink-late lines, zero worker late/underflow/acquire errors, and zero stream
deficit. There is one 104-sample partial return/write deficit, which does not
move the SFN timeline and stays on the watchlist.

Verdict: promote one-shot DMA plus all-timed plus contiguous timestamp backfill
as the current coherent M2SDR downlink shape. The frontier moves to re-baselining
