# CP2822 - Coherent PRACH phase-cycle no-RF gate

CP2822 adds a narrow wrapper around the CP2818 coherent-downlink run. Its only
runtime change is
`GNB_PRACH_TIMING_COMPENSATION_SAMPLES_OVERRIDE=0`, which prevents the legacy
237336-sample compensation from clearing the existing three-phase PRACH
collection sequence. One-shot DMA, all-timed writes, timestamp backfill, the
phase delays/candidate list, and stage-1 selected-shift TA compensation remain
unchanged.

The no-RF propagation probe passes. The real CP2188 layer maps the override to
`GNB_PRACH_TIMING_COMPENSATION_SAMPLES=0`; the isolated current stage preserves
`0:0,1474560:160,1647360:160`, stop-on-accept, and selected-shift TA
compensation. The wrapper also pins the CP2818, CP2188, isolated-stage, and
QCSuper-parser hashes. Syntax passes, and a fresh default-closed invocation
returns rc 2 with zero artifacts.

The live postcondition is stronger than environment proof: it parses the full
packaged predemod manifest and requires more than 1000 rows, every row
sequence-active and timing-override-zero, plus nonzero rows for indices 0/1/2
with their exact paired delays. It also emits a compact QCSuper NR-RRC summary.
