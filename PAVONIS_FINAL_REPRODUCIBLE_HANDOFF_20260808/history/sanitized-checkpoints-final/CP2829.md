# CP2829 - Independent UL event replay proves no phone Msg1

CP2829 stays no-RF and analyzes CP2828's independent bladeRF capture with a
compact 1 ms event join, a 160 ms residue-lattice check, two bounded 12 ms raw
slices, and the established offline PRACH detector harness.

The capture has 750 groups at least 6 dB above its PRACH-band median, but only
one group reaches 10 dB and the residues are diffuse rather than forming the
phone PRACH's 160 ms occasion lattice. The nearest group to the QCSuper
UL-CCCH event starts only `+1.577 ms` away, but is broadband: PRACH-band
`-34.941 dBFS`, wideband `-26.022 dBFS`, and only `-8.919 dB` of its energy in
the PRACH band. The strongest full-capture group occurs `+26.524 s` later and
is similarly broadband.

Bounded slices around both events are replayed through the actual detector.
The 30 baseline windows produce zero accepts, maximum p0 ratio `0.117`, and
maximum best-preamble ratio `0.158`. The full 22,350-row stage-1 timing/CFO/
fractional grid also produces zero accepts; maximum p0 ratio remains `0.117`
and maximum best-preamble ratio is only `0.212`. The UL-CCCH-nearest event's
best p0 ratio is `0.117` at delay 121. These are detector-negative broadband
events, not hidden PRACH.

Verdict: under the coherent M2SDR downlink the phone decodes MIB/SIB1 and
internally generates UL-CCCH, but does not radiate a detector-valid Msg1.
Therefore M2SDR collection delay, stage-1 CFO/timing envelopes, and gNB PRACH
acceptance are downstream of the current failure and must not be [interface-redacted] again
until the phone's pre-Msg1 decision is restored. CP2744 already proves the
same OCUDU profile reaches PRACH, RAR, service, and private IP when transmitted
by bladeRF. The next branch must isolate the remaining M2SDR-specific
downlink physical/tracking difference against that successful OCUDU/bladeRF
control while preserving the CP2819 coherent cadence.
