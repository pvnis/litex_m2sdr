# CP2832 - Fresh OCUDU bladeRF phone NR measurement control

CP2832 reruns the preserved CP2744 OCUDU/bladeRF post-RX-rearm shape with a
fresh append-only stamp and the bounded Android/QCSuper observers. Both hosts
pass read-only preflight, the bladeRF is authorized SuperSpeed on USB `4-2`,
the wrapper exits zero, and qcore, gNB, QCSuper, and phone state are restored.

The current phone still establishes strong, valid NR physical measurements on
this waveform:

- 92 monitor rows, 85 exact PCI500/TAC7/NCI105217 rows;
- 86 valid NR RSRP rows, all `-92 dBm`;
- 86 valid NR RSRQ rows, mean `-12.012 dB`;
- 86 valid NR SINR rows, mean `+7.442 dB`;
- 86 rows with `CellSignalStrengthNr` as the primary signal.

The run is not an access/ladder pass. The phone stays `NOT_REG_SEARCHING` for
77 rows, OCUDU sees zero PRACH/RAR/PUSCH, and qcore sees no registration or
user-plane activation. QCSuper records 74 MIB, 74 SIB1, one internally
generated UL-CCCH, and no DL-CCCH. Treat that access axis as void rather than
as a bladeRF regression; CP2744 remains the bladeRF attach/service milestone.

The physical control is decisive for the active branch: the same phone and
OCUDU broadcast produce valid NR RSRP/RSRQ/SINR through bladeRF today, while
CP2828's coherent M2SDR run produces none. Since CP2831 proves M2SDR PBCH,
PDCCH, and SIB1 PDSCH are digitally clean, the next bounded discriminator is
M2SDR SSB absolute OTA level at the phone. Audit and gate the already-built
default-off coherent all-SSB `+6 dB` control from CP2416 on the current
coherent fixture; do not alter SIB1, PRACH, scheduler, or detector logic.
