# CP2839 - CP2838 independent UL replay proves no hidden Msg1

CP2839 stays no-RF and analyzes CP2838's independent 65-second bladeRF UL
capture using compact 1 ms event aggregation, a 160 ms residue-lattice check,
three bounded 12 ms raw slices, and the established CP1965 detector harness.

The capture has 818 groups at least 6 dB above its PRACH-band median but only
two at 10 dB. Their modulo-160 residues occur once each; the broader candidate
set is diffuse, with no residue count above six. The event nearest QCSuper's
only UL-CCCH starts `10.946 ms` before it and is broadband: PRACH-band
`-33.108 dBFS`, wideband `-24.253 dBFS`, and PRACH-band fraction `-8.855 dB`.
The two strongest events have similarly broadband fractions (`-8.867` and
`-9.592 dB`).

The UL-CCCH-nearest event and both >=10 dB events were extracted as 12 ms,
138,240-sample SC16 slices. Preparation generated 45 detector windows. The 45
baseline cases produce zero accepts, maximum p0 ratio `0.133`, and maximum
best-preamble ratio `0.175`. The 33,525-row stage-1 timing/CFO/fractional/phase
grid also produces zero accepts; maximum p0 ratio is `0.158` and maximum
best-preamble ratio is `0.240`. Per-event stage-1 p0 maxima are `0.157`,
`0.158`, and `0.133`, all far below acceptance.

Verdict: CP2838 contains no hidden detector-valid phone Msg1. The visible UL
energy is broadband activity without the expected PRACH lattice or detector
correlation. PRACH collection delay, phase sequence, and stage-1 envelope are
again downstream of the failure. The next RF discriminator is the exact
CP2836 coherent SSB+6 phone shape without the independent bladeRF observer. A
measurement-state recovery would classify the observer/launch path as
perturbative; another failure would establish a run-scale phone tracking
lottery in the observer-free shape.
