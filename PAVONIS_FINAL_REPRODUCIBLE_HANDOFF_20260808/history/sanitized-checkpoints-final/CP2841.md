CP2841 stays no-RF and joins CP2836, CP2838, and CP2840 at the gNB-start,
bladeRF downlink-capture, phone-monitor, and operator-selection boundaries.

The M2SDR/air evidence does not separate the good and bad runs. CP2836 versus
CP2840 has PBCH `128/128` versus `127/128`, median SNR `22.25` versus
`22.2 dB`, median CFO `-1943.65` versus `-1943.9 Hz`, and zero SFN residual in
both. A common aligned 4,608-complex-sample window beginning at the decoded SSB
offset measures median component RMS `-43.647` versus `-43.662 dBFS`; median
component peak is `1085.5` versus `1079` counts. Launch timing is also aligned:
each phone monitor starts seven seconds after the gNB start helper completes.

row, and restores automatic selection plus the full all-RAT mask. Therefore it
