# CP2850 - Clean radio reset does not restore bladeRF Msg1

CP2850 repeats the exact CP2848 OCUDU/bladeRF phone-before-gNB control after
CP2849 removed the stale `[interface-redacted]` bearer. The ordering hook, NR-only phone
preparation, bladeRF TX rearm, qcore and gNB starts, QCSuper capture, full-RAT
restore, process stops, and packaging all pass. The known operator-selection
helper returns its expected nonfatal rc1.

The stale bearer stays absent: all 95 monitor rows have no cellular interface.
The phone nevertheless measures the private NR cell in 49 rows at a stable
`-92 dBm` RSRP, with matching valid RSRQ/SINR and primary-NR classification.
It remains repeatedly `NOT_REG_SEARCHING` and never starts a live access
procedure. The gNB records zero PRACH, RAR, PUSCH, or CRC-OK PUSCH; qcore
records zero Registration Request, NAS authentication/security, bearer, or
user-plane activity.

The 13,605-byte QCSuper capture contains 55 MIB, 55 SIB1, five internal
UL-CCCH records, no DL-CCCH, and no UL-DCCH. Because the gNB sees no PRACH and
no RAR, those UL-CCCH records do not represent a completed over-air RACH/RRC
exchange.

Verdict: CP2848's retained bearer was stale and CP2849 correctly cleared it,
but it was not the cause of the current no-Msg1 state. The failure remains
common to bladeRF and M2SDR while the phone can measure the cell. Stay no-RF
and re-audit the selected USIM's authoritative EF_FPLMN state with the proven
CP2661 Binder helper. If serving PLMN [network-id-redacted] is present, perform only the
already proven hash-gated surgical removal and modem reload before the next RF
control.
