# CP2858 - Continuous transition exposes service-gate timing bug

CP2858 executes the CP2857 wrapper. All preflights, qcore/srsENB/phone starts,
background selector start, and trapped cleanup pass. OCUDU is never started,
so this checkpoint is void for OCUDU and M2SDR behavior.

The first service gate times out without a private interface, but saved phase
logs prove real positive-RAN progress:

- srsENB receives three RACH attempts;
- the phone has 79 valid primary-NR rows at mean `-84.95 dBm`;
- qcore receives three UE RAN connections;
- qcore begins normal subscriber registration twice;
- one intervening security-protected update is rejected for stale/missing
  outer TMSI;
- no user-plane activation occurs before the timeout.

The background selector has not reached scan completion or state restoration
when the 240-second interface wait expires. Cleanup terminates the selector;
its trap restores automatic/full RAT, and `RegistrationComplete` then reaches
qcore exactly as srsENB's transport is being closed. The teardown race, not
missing RACH or broken RF, prevents service completion.

Verdict: retain the continuous-context approach but fix its gate ordering.
Stream-wait for qcore's first `Registering` event, terminate the selector while
srsENB and qcore remain live so its proven full-mode restore completes, then
wait for user-plane/interface service. Only after that gate passes may the
wrapper stop srsENB and start OCUDU. Do not retry CP2858 unchanged.

Artifacts include `cp2858_sens_phase_forensic.log`,
`cp2858_nuc_phase_forensic.log`, `cp2858_qcore_reject_detail.log`, and
`cp2858_summary.json`.
