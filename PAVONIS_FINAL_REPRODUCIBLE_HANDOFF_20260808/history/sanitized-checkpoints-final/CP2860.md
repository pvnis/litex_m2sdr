# CP2860 - Positive service marker lost to SSH return-code artifact

CP2860 executes the CP2859 event-gated transition. The repaired ordering works:
qcore registration is detected, the selector is stopped/restores full mode,
and the remote service wait writes
`CP2857_SRS_SERVICE=PASS [interface-redacted] [ip-redacted]/30`.

The local SSH helper nevertheless returns nonzero after writing that marker.
With `set -e`, the wrapper exits before its marker check and before any status
or OCUDU start. Trapped cleanup passes. CP2860 is therefore OCUDU/M2SDR-void.

Post-cleanup phase-log forensics independently prove the service marker:

- srsENB receives two RACH attempts;
- qcore receives two UE connections;
- one subscriber registration starts;
- one user-plane session activates;
- all RAN/core processes are stopped and phone restore passes.

Verdict: the continuous service setup is now proven. Harden only this SSH
boundary: capture its transport rc without `set -e`, record it, and require the
explicit remote service PASS marker as the authoritative completion gate. No
RAN/core/phone/RF change is needed. Do not retry CP2860 unchanged.

Artifacts include `cp2860_sens_service_forensic.log`,
`cp2860_nuc_service_forensic.log`, and `cp2860_summary.json`.
