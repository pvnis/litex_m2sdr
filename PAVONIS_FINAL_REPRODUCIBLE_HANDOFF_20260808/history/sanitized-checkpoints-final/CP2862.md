# CP2862 - Continuous context restores OCUDU radio access

## Purpose

Carry one established srsENB phone service epoch directly into OCUDU/bladeRF
without restarting qcore or restoring phone policy between the two RANs.

## Result

The transition wrapper completed with its context invariant and positive srs
service gate intact. The srs phase produced two RACH events, one qcore
user-plane activation, and `[interface-redacted]` with `[ip-redacted]/30`.

After stopping only srsENB and starting OCUDU, the gNB observed three PRACH
events, 16 PUSCH results, and six CRC-OK PUSCH decodes. Stream health remained
clean: zero acquire errors, TX timeouts, downlink-late events, underflows, and
time errors. Continuous phone context therefore restores the OCUDU radio-access
path that was absent in CP2853-CP2856.

This is not an attach or data pass. qcore deactivated the srs user plane when
that RAN disconnected. The subsequent OCUDU registration reached qcore, but
qcore rejected it while generating a challenge with `Unknown IMSI`; no second
user-plane activation occurred. The phone's retained private interface after
the swap is not proof of target-RAN data.

## Decision

Keep the continuous transition shape. Next, no-RF, compare the configured
private subscriber identity with the identities presented in the srs and OCUDU
phases without printing credential values. Resolve the fresh-identity rejection,
then require a new OCUDU-side user-plane activation and bidirectional traffic
before transferring this sequence to the coherent M2SDR fixture.
