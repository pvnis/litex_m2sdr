# CP2863 - Privacy-preserving qcore identity audit no-RF gate

## Finding

Qcore authenticates by exact lookup of the parsed IMSI string in its subscriber
map. CP2862's srs phase succeeded through an identity procedure, while OCUDU's
direct SUPI registration reached the same lookup and returned `Unknown IMSI`.
The current parser renders each BCD nibble as a decimal number, so a filler
nibble can become the two characters `15`; this is a concrete structural
candidate, not yet the verdict.

The phone has only one active subscription: subscription 6 on slot 0. It is
also the default data, voice, and SMS subscription. A dual-SIM switch is ruled
out as the immediate explanation.

## Instrument

`cp2863_qcore_identity_audit.py` reads the live qcore log and private subscriber
TOML after the RF observation window. It emits only counts, lengths, exact
membership, first/last equality, and structural relation classes. It never
prints identities, keys, OPC, or other credentials.

Local syntax/compile and synthetic exact/filler/different controls pass. The
deployed helper hash is
`75eb33a79b21cd6354f11f493fdfcedf227ba6b75cd4cbd45e02434a1bb90527`.
Against the real private TOML with an empty log, it reports one configured
15-digit entry and no identity values.

The continuous wrapper adds only default-off
`PAVONIS_CP2863_IDENTITY_AUDIT=1`. Its current hash is
`ae75f3fd888361fd238b3b525ca7dfaf266cc934cb1014c6c7e331c3771ba528`;

Disabled sequence behavior and the closed RF gate pass with zero artifacts.
