# CP2879 - MCS4 stabilizes primer; target attaches; phone route fails

The isolated MCS4 primer is decisive. The first 64 traced srs PUSCH results are
63 CRC-OK: the initial 11-byte grant is 1/1 and the new 512-byte large grants
are 62/63. This replaces the MCS10 profile's 1,153-byte large grants at only
3-4 CRC-OK out of 63. The private srs interface appears quickly.

The continuous handoff then reaches OCUDU. Qcore's activation count increases
from one to two, proving a fresh target-RAN user-plane activation. OCUDU records
eight PRACH events, 400 PUSCH results, 20 CRC-OK PUSCH, and zero downlink-late,
underflow, or time errors. The one audited identity exactly matches the private
subscriber.

Phone-to-core data does not pass: root-bound ping on `[interface-redacted]` returns rc1,
receives zero replies, and changes neither RX nor TX bytes. Because no packet
leaves the phone interface, this is consistent with Android route/policy state,
not evidence of an RF uplink CRC failure. The wrapper's fail-fast assertion
prevents the independent core-to-phone probe from running, so GTP user-plane
transport remains unclassified.

Promote MCS4 as the isolated primer fixture. Modify only diagnostics so both
directional probes always execute before the combined gate, and classify the
phone route lookup/ping failure without dumping Android routing tables. Then
repeat after a fresh modem epoch; core-to-phone decides GTP versus phone policy.
