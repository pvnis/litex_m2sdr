# CP2917 - M2SDR downlink persists; phone reacquisition fails

CP2917 runs the CP2916 early/late discriminator around the frozen CP2915
primer-to-coherent-M2SDR shape. All five 250 ms RX2 captures complete at the
expected 11.52 MB size. The first run of the new sequence then exits during
packaging because shell globs were expanded outside the `tar -C` directory.
The outer wrapper returns `1` and performs clean base/capture cleanup. This is
an instrument packaging defect, not an RF acquisition failure; the five raw
captures were complete and were recovered no-RF.

The activation watcher was armed at `[date-redacted]`, M2SDR startup completed at
`[date-redacted]`, and its 240-second bound stopped OCUDU at `[date-redacted]`. Therefore
the last two captures are valid post-stop controls, not evidence of stream
decay. The three on-air snapshots prove strong PCI500 PBCH and sample/wall-time
SFN continuity through 150 seconds.

The phone monitor starts during the srsENB primer and contains 93 rows. It has
20 valid primary-NR rows, mean RSRP/RSRQ/SINR `-84.2/-11.8/+6.0`, and its last
valid row is elapsed second 82. M2SDR starts at monitor second about 69; the
post-start airplane cycle finishes at about second 91. Thus the phone loses
valid NR before the reset finishes and never reacquires it, while the
independent bladeRF still decodes the same M2SDR signal at target +75 and +150
seconds. Target access remains zero and qcore sees no target RegistrationRequest.

The no-RF repair changes packaging to execute `tar` from inside the capture
root, extends a future wrapper's prearmed activation timeout to 300 seconds,
adds wall-time/SFN residual analysis, and removes analyzer scratch CF32 files.
Current hashes are sequence `a6a2a096...`, analyzer `0a81e93a...`, wrapper
`0e7a9b2b...`; the sequence redeploy hash passes. Remote and duplicate local
bulk data were cleaned, leaving 56 MB of source SC16 evidence and compact
results.

Verdict: close M2SDR downlink amplitude and frame-timeline persistence as the
cause of the phone's post-reset loss. Next stay no-RF and identify the installed
phone-side 5G/network-mode application and the exact working mode transition
used by the prior phone baseline. Do not change the M2SDR fixture.
