# CP2923 - M2SDR SSB cadence is good; single-opportunity decodability is not

CP2923 stays no-RF and analyzes the retained CP2917 +5/+75/+150-second SC16
captures against CP2640's phone-working bladeRF-transmit/M2SDR-receive capture.
The new analyzer uses direct complex correlation around a decoded SSB and
searches every nominal 230,400-sample/20 ms recurrence. It reads the CP2640
reference directly from the retained tarball and leaves no expanded raw copy.

Gross timing is healthy. Across the three M2SDR captures, median 1,024-sample
recurrence correlation is `0.8803..0.8883`; cumulative recurrence error is only
`-2..+4` samples over 11-12 periods. Fitted absolute sample-rate error is
`1.00..1.35 ppm`, median `1.013 ppm`. The phone-working CP2640 reference is
`1.053 ppm` with lower median recurrence correlation `0.6515`. Therefore the
M2SDR failure is not missing 20 ms SSB bursts, gross sample-clock error, or
poor burst-to-burst recurrence.

A narrower contrast remains decisive. CP2917's +75-second capture gives zero
expected-PCI PBCH/CSI detections in 24 exact 20 ms windows. A bounded 116-case
control over four windows and bulk CFO correction from `-5000` through
`+[date-redacted] Hz` in `250 Hz` steps also gives `0` PCI500 PBCH and `0` PCI500 CSI.
The CP2640 phone-working waveform gives `48/48` PCI500 PBCH in its 20 ms window
sweep. CP2917 still decodes from the complete 250 ms record, and CP2919 decodes
SI DCI/PDSCH, so this is not signal absence or corrupt system information. It
is a per-opportunity acquisition/coherence deficit hidden by long-record
integration.

Verdict: close gross SSB cadence and sample-rate drift. Keep the single-SSB
decodability defect open as the leading physical difference that can explain
the Android modem remaining emergency-only before RRC. Do not retune phone
policy, PLMN selection, PRACH receive windows, or broad RF level.

Next: run a hardware-substitution discriminator using a phone-working live
waveform through the M2SDR, and audit/compare the all-timed descriptor
presentation that can repeat a deterministic within-SSB defect every 20 ms.
Prefer a live srsENB-on-M2SDR control if the installed RF backends support it;
otherwise use a bounded known-waveform replay plus independent Msg1 observer.
