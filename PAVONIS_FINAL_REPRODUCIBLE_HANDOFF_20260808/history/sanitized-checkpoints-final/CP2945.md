# CP2945: channel-1 scheduled SI is overdriven and coherence-poor

Date: [date-redacted]

Existing CP2927 channel-0 and CP2940 channel-1 post-ready bladeRF captures were
replayed through the established SearchSpace0/SI harness with identical PCI500,
CORESET0 index 6, SearchSpace0 index 0, Band 3 frequency geometry, and a full
slot-0 boundary sweep from `-11520` through `0` samples in 64-sample steps.
No RF was transmitted.

The CP2927 channel-0 control produced two SI-RNTI DCI CRC-OK points at boundary
deltas `-1472` and `-1408`. At `-1472`, PDCCH EPRE was `-3.30 dBfs`, correlation
was `0.685`, and the decoded DCI 1_0 grant matched the known SIB1 allocation:
`f_alloc=0x150`, MCS 5, PRB `(1,8)`, symbols `(2,13)`, TBS 80. The neighboring
point reached correlation `0.765`; PDSCH SNR was `+10.5` to `+10.9 dB` although
the offline PDSCH CRC remained KO.

The CP2940 channel-1 capture produced zero DCI CRC-OK points in 181 probes. At
the same `-1472` boundary it measured PDCCH EPRE `+13.39 dBfs` but only `0.390`
correlation (best alternate candidate `0.452`); at `-1408` it measured
`+13.29 dBfs` and `0.258` correlation. Thus channel 1 presents roughly
`16.7 dB` more scheduled-SI energy than the decodable channel-0 control while
losing coherence. The raw channel-1 capture reaches component magnitude [date-redacted],
whereas channel 0 peaks at 331. Both captures used the same independent helper
and its actual RX2 gain readback was 60 dB, so this is not a helper-gain split.

Joined with the phone evidence, CP2674's working bladeRF gNB averaged about
`-86/-12/+5.1 dB` RSRP/RSRQ/SINR while the rare CP2938 M2SDR observations were
about `-75/-18/-3 dB`: the M2SDR phone leg is stronger but substantially less
coherent. The immediate testable cause is excessive channel-1 downlink drive,
not weak link budget or phone policy.

Do not repeat the phone run at `0.1 dB` M2SDR TX attenuation. Gate an isolated
channel-1 helper at about `11-12 dB` attenuation, then require all of: no raw
clipping, retained PBCH, SI-RNTI DCI CRC OK with correlation near the channel-0
class, exact RX gain40 readback, and clean stream health. Only then rerun the
registration-aware phone/data wrapper.
