Stamp: `[date-redacted]CP2950_CH1_ATT16_MULTI_SI_CAPTURE`.

The exact CP2948 ATT16 helper ran with clean host state, exact stream1/control0
gain40 readback, zero gain errors or stream faults, and clean qcore/srsENB/
program-mode shutdown. A post-ready one-second RX2 capture contains 11,520,000
samples, SHA-256 `0d06970e...`, RMS `-36.3461 dB` relative to [date-redacted], maximum
component `648`, and PCI500 PBCH CRC OK at `9.4 dB`, CFO `-889.6 Hz`, SFN 24.

The identical full-slot SI sweep covers six scheduled occasions (SFN 16, 32,
48, 64, 80, 96) with 1,086 probes. The first four have best PDCCH correlation
`0.234`, `0.312`, `0.159`, and `0.338`; the last two recover to `0.591` and
`0.866` and yield five DCI CRC-OK timing rows. Every decoded DCI has the exact
known SIB1 grant: DCI 1_0, `f_alloc=0x150`, MCS 5, PRB `(1,8)`, symbols
`(2,13)`, TBS 80. DCI-OK deltas are `-1408/-1280` at SFN80 and
`-1472/-1408/-1344` at SFN96.

Offline PDSCH CRC remains KO despite `9.1-14.3 dB` PDSCH SNR. This is not a new
gate: the CP2927 decodable channel-0 control also has offline PDSCH KO at
`10.5-10.9 dB`, while the same srsENB waveform has already driven real phone
registration. The validated DCI/grant and late `0.866` correlation satisfy the
scheduled-SI air gate.

The path is usable but shows an early within-capture coherence settling period:
power is target-class throughout while DCI appears only in the final 320 ms.
Move the registration-aware phone/service/data wrapper to the ATT16 helper,
preserving all other CP2943 logic, and use a fresh phone modem epoch. Continue
tracking acquisition latency and SI class; do not retune thresholds or payloads.
