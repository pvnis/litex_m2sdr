# CP2953: ATT16 phone reaches NAS security; uplink reliability blocks service

Date: [date-redacted] Europe/Zurich
Stamp: `[date-redacted]CP2953_PHONE_SRSENB_M2SDR_CH1_ATT16_SERVICE_DATA`

## Verdict

CP2953 is the strongest M2SDR real-phone run to date. The frozen ATT16 channel-1
shape produces 23 random-access indications and three complete RRC-to-qcore
connections. All three deliver Registration Request. The last connection also
delivers Authentication Response, allowing qcore to advance to NAS security
activation. The radio then becomes inactive before Security Mode Complete, so
the phone never receives `[ip-redacted]` and both data probes are correctly
skipped.

This is not a lifecycle, core, credential, gross RF-path, or Soapy stream-fault
failure. The active blocker is sustained bidirectional radio reliability after
RRC setup.

## Run Mechanics

- qcore and the ATT16 channel-1 srsENB start cleanly;
- physical stream channel 1 and logical RX gain-control channel 0 are active;
- M2SDR RX gain reads back `40.00 dB`;
- Soapy timeout, time-error, underflow, and late counts are all zero;
- phone preparation, radio capture, bounded registration wait, packaging,
  FPLMN post-audit, restore, and service cleanup pass;
- the operator-list scan returns `1`, but registration begins independently and
  the full-RAT restore passes;
- private-service wait reaches its bounded timeout and no data probe is run.

The wrapper's compact qcore counter reports zeros because it searches older log
vocabulary. The 33-line qcore log itself contains the authoritative progression.

## Uplink Trace Join

The bounded traces show two sharply separated receive classes:

| Channel | Rows | Valid/CRC OK | Invalid/CRC KO | Valid mean SINR | Invalid mean SINR |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| PUSCH | 64 | 11 | 53 | `+2.597 dB` | `-18.421 dB` |
| PUCCH ACK | 64 | 17 | 47 | `+8.571 dB` | `-10.445 dB` |
| PUCCH SR | 64 | 11 | 53 | `+6.996 dB` | `-10.415 dB` |

The 11-byte Msg3 class is `1/27` CRC OK; the 512-byte scheduled PUSCH class is
`10/37` CRC OK. For the first complete RRC connection, retransmission groups
usually begin near `-20` to `-29 dB` and eventually land near `+1.4 dB`, which
is enough to advance NAS but not to sustain it.

Trace caps matter: PUSCH and PUCCH-SR stop near [date-redacted], before the later two
qcore connections. Missing later PUSCH rows are instrumentation blindness, not
proof of missing traffic. The MAC pcap still shows three radio-activity clusters
aligned to the three qcore connections.

## Downlink Join

The phone first exposes valid NR measurements while the third NAS attempt is
alive. It alternates between a healthy class (`-73` to `-74 dBm`, RSRQ about
`-11` to `-12 dB`) and floor-like rows (`-124` to `-133 dBm`, RSRQ about
`-34` to `-35 dB`). This agrees with CP2950's occasion-dependent SI coherence
and explains why a strong instantaneous RSRP does not guarantee completion of
the reactive NAS exchange.

## Preserved-Golden Comparison

The CP2674 bladeRF phone pass requested and read back `50 dB` RX gain. Its
bounded PUSCH trace is `63/64` CRC OK with CRC-OK mean `+5.462 dB`; only the
first 1153-byte transmission is CRC KO at `-21.627 dB`. CP2953 uses otherwise
matching phone-side scheduler/config semantics but M2SDR RX gain `40 dB` and
is `11/64` CRC OK. This makes an isolated M2SDR `50 dB` RX-gain control the
next justified rung.

## Source Audit

The channel-1 Soapy mapping is mechanically coherent. srsRAN requests physical
stream channel 1. In LiteX 1R1T mode, the driver selects AD9361 RX2/TX2 but
packs that sole selected channel into DMA frame lane 0; single-channel
deinterleave/interleave therefore intentionally use frame channel 0. The gain
control remains logical channel 0 as required by the AD9361 1R1T mapping.
There is no wrong-buffer or double-channel evidence in this path.

## Next Rung

and data probes. Gate a separate helper that adds only
`--rf.rx_gain=50`, require stream1/control0 readback `50.00 dB`, and rerun from
a fresh phone epoch. Judge first on PUSCH/PUCCH validity and NAS Security Mode
Complete, then on private interface and bidirectional data.
