## Verdict

The in-process instrument works and clears the TX descriptor layer. After N3
rearm, the phone sends two random-access attempts. The second reaches valid
setup ACK, creates the arm file in-process, and records exactly 65,536 bounded
worker rows. CP2047 passes with zero short non-EOB, late, dedup, or duplicate
HAS_TIME timestamp defects.

The radio failure is now localized to grant acquisition for the next uplink.
Msg3 is strong and the 512-byte SetupComplete succeeds on its third PUSCH,
allowing qcore to open a UE context. The phone then has `0/64` valid PUCCH-SR
opportunities over the next 2.6 seconds, receives no new usable UL grant, sends
no next NAS response, and is released for inactivity after 30 seconds.

## Descriptor Verdict

The trace starts `133 us` after the valid setup ACK and spans `5813.832 ms`.
All 65,536 rows are 1022-sample zero-copy submits. There are 22 HAS_TIME rows,
zero short non-EOB rows, zero late rows, zero dedup rows, and no duplicate
source/release timestamps. CP2047 continuation mode passes with the historical
high HAS_TIME cap used by CP2075.

This closes post-setup Soapy descriptor churn as the cause of this failure.
The remaining observed blocker is the M2SDR uplink good/floor state at PUCCH
SR, with below-Soapy TX/gateware still an unproven downlink co-suspect rather
than the demonstrated stop point.

## Next

Do not change gain, attenuation, or M2 descriptor handling. Extend the existing
default-off setup-ACK UL-grant compatibility path with a bounded follow-up grant
budget: after setup ACK, convert each configured SR opportunity for that RNTI
into an `ul_sr_info()` event even when the decoded SR is invalid. This bypasses
only the demonstrated PUCCH-SR acquisition wall and gives queued NAS responses
repeated PUSCH opportunities; it remains isolated/default-off and does not
alter the golden. Gate build/deploy first, then run from a fresh phone epoch.
