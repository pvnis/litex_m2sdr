## Verdict

The unchanged retry exercises CP2967 and disproves invalid PUCCH-SR as the sole
post-setup blocker. The fourth RACH attempt reaches a strong CRC-OK Msg3,
valid setup ACK, and arms the 512-opportunity budget. At least the first 128
follow-up opportunities are injected and the phone sends many 512-byte PUSCH
transmissions. Fourteen later CRC-OK MAC PDUs contain only a short BSR with
buffer level zero plus padding; none carries a new LCID1 SDU.

Qcore receives the initial 96-byte LCID1 SetupComplete/NAS, creates a RAN
context, and reports the known stale/foreign GUTI IDs. It receives no subsequent
Registration/Identity NAS message and releases the context after 30 seconds of
inactivity. The branch supplies grants successfully, but the phone has no SRB
payload queued in response to the reactive downlink.
