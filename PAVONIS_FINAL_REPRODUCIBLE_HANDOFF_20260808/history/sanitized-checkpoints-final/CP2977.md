## Question

working stack was stopped before the phone could perform qcore's designed
`ims` to `internet` fallback.

## Qcore audit

The exact decision is in
`qcore/qcore/src/procedures/ue_associated/nas/uplink_nas.rs`:

- only exact DNN `ims` is rejected;
- qcore sends 5GMM Status with DNN-not-supported/not-subscribed cause;

The session allocator is DNN-agnostic after this check. It allocates the
private userplane, builds the PDU Session Establishment Accept, and stores the

profiles without exposing credentials:

The phone therefore has the intended fallback target. Do not edit APNs before
testing the existing policy.

## Verdict and next gate

Do not patch qcore yet. Gate one lifecycle-only discriminator that preserves
the exact CP2976 RF/core branches, performs the same full phone restore while
qcore and srsENB remain live, then holds them for up to 120 seconds while
watching for:

1. qcore `ims` rejection;
2. a subsequent `internet` PDU-session establishment and userplane activation;
3. phone `[ip-redacted]` and both private data probes.

If fallback succeeds, the blocker was teardown ordering. If the phone remains
`ims`-only for the entire hold, an isolated qcore `ims`-accept branch becomes a
specific next test. No RF, timing, subscriber, APN, or scheduler change is
approved by this checkpoint.
