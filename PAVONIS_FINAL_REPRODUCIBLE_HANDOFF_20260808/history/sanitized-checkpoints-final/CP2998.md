## Verdict and next rung

CP2998 validates CP2995/CP2996 as a real scheduled-phase downlink supply
regression. The external RF level is normal, but PBCH quality is marginal and
scheduled SI is uniformly incoherent/undecodable in this one-second window.
The phone and access policy are not the immediate blocker, and CP2993's HTTP
data proof remains parked for the next PDU-session roll.

This capture alone does not distinguish a run-start-decided bad class from a
late settling/recovery process. CP2950 recovered within its one-second capture,
whereas CP2992 first exposed the exact cell about 96 seconds after gNB start.
Before another phone run, hold the identical gNB-only shape and collect bounded
independent snapshots near 0, 60, and 120 seconds. Analyze each with the same
PBCH and scheduled-SI tools. Recovery at a later age names settling as the
access-supply mechanism; persistent weak SI names a run-class defect and calls
for a clean-vs-bad TX descriptor/below-Soapy comparison. Do not retune RF,
thresholds, phone policy, retry logic, or qcore during this discriminator.
