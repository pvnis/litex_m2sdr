# CP3000: SI-epoch trace capture is timing-void

Date: [date-redacted]

Stamp: `[date-redacted]CP3000_SI_EPOCH_TX_WORKER_TRACE_CAPTURE`.

CP3000 attempted to join a bounded 65,536-row M2SDR TX worker trace to a
one-second independent bladeRF capture. The frozen CP2985 channel-1/RX40/ATT16
shape, qcore, RF settings, and all protocol controls were unchanged. To avoid
the trace arm-file's per-row `access()` check, the capture was prearmed for five
seconds and the trace began at stream startup.

The trace is valid and mechanically clean. It contains 65,536
`zero_copy_submit` rows spanning 5.812120 seconds, all with 1,022 samples. The
CP2047 continuation gate passes with zero late rows, zero dedup suppressions,
zero short non-EOB rows, and no duplicate HAS_TIME source or release
timestamps. There are 28 expected startup/timed HAS_TIME rows. GNB health is
also clean: zero Soapy timeout, time-error, underflow, or late lines. The trace
SHA-256 is
`bfc8c471ffd70b279bb4fd0e0f54d15af337d2577b877e5790a2a8a4c3c75d2c`;
the CP2047 verdict SHA-256 is
`458dd7d3ba011d27db785052da3d8bca8bae585a9620945628134636e9f8b72a`.

The RF join is void. The capture spans epoch ns
`1784673331068135332..1784673334103523105`; the first descriptor is not until
`[hex-value-redacted]`, 2.743576 seconds after capture end. The capture is
accordingly floor-only (`-43.773 dB` relative to [date-redacted], max component 68, PBCH
CRC KO). It says nothing about SI or the descriptor-to-air path. Snapshot tar
SHA-256 is
`5435549c8b9ac20f90c7aeeeba820884d93d6791bfb732ebcc5bb7a6c94c27fa`.

Both services stopped and both hosts passed postchecks; [host-redacted] returned to M2
program mode `N`. Repeat once as CP3001 with the capture prearm delay changed
only from 5 to 10 seconds. The measured startup timing predicts that the full
one-second capture will then sit inside the 5.8-second descriptor span. Do not
draw a supply-layer conclusion from CP3000 and do not retry the phone yet.
