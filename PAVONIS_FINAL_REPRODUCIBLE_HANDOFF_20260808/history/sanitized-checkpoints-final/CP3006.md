## First missing control-plane stage

The phone enters NR-SA data service at monitor second52 and exposes the exact
cell for 46 rows. Qcore receives one new UE RAN connection, applies the approved
private-lab identity handling, and starts normal subscriber registration. It
then receives no authentication response. After about30.8 seconds the gNB
initiates `UserInactivity` release. There is no registration accept, PDU-session
activation, cellular interface, or private address. The phone later loses
service; its reported NR RSRP falls from about `-74 dBm` to the floor over the
same interval, keeping downlink supply on the co-suspect list.

The gNB-side workaround markers establish the exact active policy. The setup
ACK arms 128 follow-up UL grants and all 128 are injected. The setup-DL HARQ
policy arms budget1 and forces one NACK/retransmission. The PDU-session retry
policy never arms because registration does not reach that stage. Both HTTP
endpoints remain untriggered, so user-data judgment is void.

CP2992's successful M2SDR run had the same early failure mode: its first two UE
connections were released while qcore waited for authentication response. A
later connection survived and progressed through registration/PDU activation.
This supports a reactive-downlink delivery problem, not a new credential or
qcore parser problem.

## Next rung

The source audit confirms `PAVONIS_NR_SETUP_FOLLOWUP_DL_HARQ_RETX_ACKS` is a
bounded budget that converts otherwise decoded ACKs to scheduler NACKs, causing
additional retransmission of the setup-era DL transport block. Prepare an
isolated helper variant changing only that budget from1 to2. Keep the CP2985
binary, PDU-session retry budget2, 128 UL grants, RF gains/channel, qcore,
phone policy, and HTTP proof byte-identical. Gate it no-RF before one run. This
is explicitly a workaround experiment for the unresolved reactive-DL supply
defect, not a standards-default fix.
