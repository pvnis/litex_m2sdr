already contains samples copied from the previous DMA remainder, metadata from
an appended packet no longer replaces the prefix's timestamp or claims
`HAS_TIME` ownership. Returns that begin with a newly acquired packet preserve
their prior behavior. TX, DMA ownership, sample conversion, channels, gains,
and protocol code are unchanged.
