# CP3029: Compatibility-era post-target airplane reset still yields no Msg1

Date: [date-redacted]

The CP3028 bounded shape executed against the fresh phone epoch. The local
srsENB/M2 primer passed private service and produced one RACH. OCUDU then
started with the CP3025 native gNB, CP3023 SIB1 compatibility/n0 path, CP3027
in-window stage-1 list, continuous all-timed transport, and zero TX health
faults.

Both phone actions completed exactly as gated. The post-target airplane cycle
cleared the primer private address and returned the phone to the N3 profile;
the subsequent N3/system-selection reapply was accepted. The target activation
watcher nevertheless timed out after 240 seconds, so HTTP did not run. Cleanup
restored the phone and left [host-redacted] idle with M2 program mode N.

This is a valid Msg1-supply result. The target gNB has zero accepted PRACH,
RAR, PUSCH, CRC-OK UL, and RRC setup events. Its final stage-1 segment has 1279
opportunities, 21 energy-gated rows, 168 candidate evaluations, zero
selections, maximum RMS `-60.774 dB`, maximum peak `-49.894 dB`, and maximum
search time `4546 us`. The 168 evaluations are exactly 21 times the configured
eight candidates, proving the repaired in-window list ran rather than the stale
CP3026 list. Soapy TX timeout, downlink-late, underflow, and time-error counts
are all zero.

The phone monitor separates pre-reset visibility from post-reset acquisition.
Before the reset it briefly reported the target at about `-81 dBm` and `+20 dB`
SINR. In the 57 rows after the reset/reapply window, 44 retain PCI 500 identity,
but all 57 are out of service, none has a valid NR quality sample, and none has
the private address. The radio reset therefore converts the target from one
strong tracked sample to identity-only/floor presentation, matching CP2915's
old-binary behavior. The CP3023 compatibility fix does not change that outcome.

This closes the post-target airplane/N3-reapply branch on the native
compatibility target and validates the CP3027 candidate repair under RF. Do not
repeat CP2915, CP2922, or CP3029. The next distinct modem-state discriminator is
a full phone reboot while OCUDU is already continuously on air, followed by the
same bounded N3 policy restore. Earlier fresh-phone checkpoints rebooted before
the srsENB primer, allowing the primer context to become stale again before the
target appeared.
