# CP3030: Post-target full phone reboot gated no-RF

Date: [date-redacted]

CP3029 closed airplane/N3-reapply as an acquisition reset: the phone retained
PCI identity but never restored valid NR quality or emitted Msg1. CP3030 adds a
distinct default-off action that fully reboots the phone only after OCUDU/M2 is
already continuously on air.

The new [host-redacted] helper composes existing, pinned mechanisms. It runs the CP2662
full reboot helper, accepts its known RC1 only provisionally, then independently
re-audits ADB and boot state. It restores VoNR and advanced calling with the
existing on-device policy class, applies the established N3 profile, and
requires automatic selection, repaired forbidden-PLMN state, NR-only mode,
modes 23/33, and no stale private address. Thus an unrelated CP2662 RC1 cannot
silently pass through the branch.

The CP2893 wrapper now exposes
`PAVONIS_CP3030_POSTSTART_PHONE_REBOOT`, default 0. It is mutually exclusive
with the existing service-trigger, operator-reselection, and airplane-cycle
actions. Its helper hash is verified in the [host-redacted] preflight, its log is
fresh-stamp guarded and packaged, and its PASS marker is required before the
existing target-activation/HTTP wait. No RF, gNB, Soapy, core, detector, gain,
frequency, timing, threshold, or payload setting changed.

No-RF proofs pass:

- helper and wrapper pass shell syntax;
- the deployed helper's no-reboot probe verifies the CP2662 and N3 helper
  hashes, authorized ADB, and idle [host-redacted] state;
- the sequence probe places phone reboot after OCUDU start and before activation
  judgment;
- enabling both reboot and airplane actions is rejected;
- the exact local-primer/N3-preapply/reboot/activation/HTTP shape validates,
  exits 2 at the closed RF gate, and creates zero artifacts.
