# Checkpoint 3038: current-target M2SDR self-RX is negative; coupling unproven

Date: [date-redacted]

## Question

Does the current native-compatibility OCUDU target decode on M2SDR's own
immediate `readStream` receive path, eliminating bladeRF, USB, and the external
RX1 cable from the measurement?

## Isolation

The original current-target helper remains byte-identical at `9a10d195...`.
CP3038 uses an isolated copy with a default-off self-RX switch, unique output
paths, immediate append, and an 11.52-million-sample cap. No phone or external
UE starts. Per the permanent observer rule, both runs are valid only for this
downlink instrument question and void for PRACH/RAR/PUSCH conclusions.

## Runs

### CP3038: receiver-[interface-redacted] void

The append captured exactly 11,520,000 CF32 samples with zero overflow, but the
readback exposed a setup error: RX remained at the normal uplink center
`1747499042 Hz`, not the downlink SSB at `1839650000 Hz`. All PBCH results from
that file are void.

### CP3038A: corrected downlink self-RX

The isolated helper applied only the CP1424-proven self-RX controls while
append was enabled. Readback was:

- center `1839650000 Hz`
- sample rate `11.52 Msps`
- bandwidth `7.68 MHz`
- gain `71 dB`, automatic mode
- CS16 receive stream converted to the bounded CF32 append
- zero overflows

The file is exactly 92,160,000 bytes / 11,520,000 complex samples. Its metadata
has zero overflow and zero timestamp-regression counts. GNB health has zero
Soapy deadline-write timeout, downlink-late, underflow, and time-error events.

Offline decoding at the exact readback center found:

- targeted: `0/110` PBCH CRC OK, zero PCI-500 PBCH/CSI
- aligned 250 ms sweep: `0/24` PBCH CRC OK PCI 500

The CF32 SHA-256 is
`f5c75a34f97b5c59cee4e053011a6fad8b0dd98991dff8ccc0592759bbe82688`.

## Verdict

The corrected current-target self-RX is a real negative measurement, but it is
not yet proof of a current-target TX defect. The post-maintenance antenna/cable
geometry has no positive same-device coupling baseline, and the historical
CP1424 positive was measured on an earlier physical path. A negative capture
could mean either a bad current waveform or insufficient coupling from TX to
the M2SDR RX port.

Do not retune current self-RX gain or repeat CP3038 unchanged. The only useful
next split is the preserved golden target through the same forced-center,
same-device append. Golden positive/current negative isolates the current
target; both negative invalidate self-RX under the current physical geometry.
