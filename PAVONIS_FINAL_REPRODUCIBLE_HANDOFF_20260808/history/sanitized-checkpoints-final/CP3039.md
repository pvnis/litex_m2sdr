Date: [date-redacted] (Europe/Zurich)

## Question

Can the preserved CP2392 gNB produce a positive PBCH control through the same
immediate, forced-center M2SDR self-RX observer used for CP3038A?

hash `950694cb...`, the current pinned Soapy source/module, 5 dB TX attenuation,

## Verdict and next rung

The positive-control requirement remains unresolved, but the proposed golden
self-RX instrument cannot answer it without changing the preserved target.
Do not retry this shape or add a second-process M2SDR reader. CP3037 invalidated
the independent bladeRF helper, CP3036 made srsUE overflow-limited, and CP3039
invalidates golden in-process self-RX. The smallest remaining receiver that
uses the phone-visible RF leg is the phone itself.

Next use the existing bounded QCSuper NR-RRC observer with the exact current
native-compatibility target and classify whether the phone receives MIB/SIB1
before any PRACH-envelope, gain, threshold, or target-binary change. Keep the
observer bounded, redact all identities and NAS bytes, and judge absence only
after proving the QCSuper capture path is armed and live.

Consolidation remains blocked because OCUDU/M2SDR phone user data is not yet
proven.
