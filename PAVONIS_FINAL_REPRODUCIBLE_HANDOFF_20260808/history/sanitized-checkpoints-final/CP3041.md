## Phase-sequence finding

The current target hard-codes:

```text
PAVONIS_PRACH_COLLECTION_PHASE_SEQUENCE=0:0,1474560:160,1647360:160
```

At 11.52 Msps these delays are 0, 128, and 143 ms. Source inspection confirms
that the worker advances this three-entry sequence once per PRACH request. The
phone retries at about 11.06 seconds, close to 69 configured 160-ms PRACH
periods; 69 is divisible by three. Thus successive phone attempts can remain
locked to one sequence index. If that index is either delayed entry, every real
Msg1 is missed even though one third of unrelated opportunities use delay zero.

This matches the cross-run evidence:

- CP2746 had no collection phase sequence and measured elevated detector input,
  including a maximum of about `-53.2 dB`, during a phone UL-CCCH attempt.
- CP3033 sometimes saw energy with the current sequence, consistent with a
  different run-start sequence phase.
- CP3041 sees five phone access attempts but an entirely floor-class detector
  segment.

The aliasing explanation is strongly supported but still requires the direct
one-knob control.
