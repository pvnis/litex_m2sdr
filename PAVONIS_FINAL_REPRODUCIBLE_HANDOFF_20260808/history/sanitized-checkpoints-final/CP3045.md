## Verdict

The collection-phase sequence is no longer the main ambiguity. With sequencing
off, the exact nominal PRACH opportunities spanning the first two phone access
epochs contain no detectable phone burst, even under the broad established
offline grid. The open question is now where the phone's physical PRACH lies
relative to the gNB's advertised and collected occasion, or whether the modem
queues UL-CCCH without radiating a compatible Msg1.

Before more RF, compare the current advertised PRACH geometry and receive
occasion against the successful CP3019 srsENB/M2SDR phone baseline and the
current gNB source/config. Then choose the smallest bounded whole-period UL
measurement that can locate energy outside the nominal window. Do not [interface-redacted]
detector thresholds from these floor samples.

Consolidation remains blocked.
