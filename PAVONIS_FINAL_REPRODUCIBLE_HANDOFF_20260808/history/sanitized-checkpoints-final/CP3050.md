capture gNB `d6ca0a9a...`. The run is deliberately void for live detector,

## Raw capture

The capture produced 128 complete 23,520-sample CF32 windows and no malformed
file. Their wall-time span is target `-4.505` through `+15.815` seconds, covering
the first two phone access records. The archive is 6.6 MB under hash
`0f244357...`.

The windows form a background class rather than a clear burst class: median RMS
is `-67.410 dB`, maximum RMS is `-62.876 dB`, and maximum peak is `-48.652 dB`.
The nearest windows to the first two phone records are indices 28 and 98 at
`-67.420/-67.508 dB` RMS. Their wall offsets are `-26.124 ms` and `+49.104 ms`,
respectively.

## Offline detector result

Baseline replay accepts zero of 128 windows; maximum preamble-0 ratio is
`0.116` and maximum any-preamble ratio is `0.191`. The established broad
stage-1 replay performs 95,360 timing/fractional/CFO evaluations and also
accepts zero rows in zero windows. Its maxima are only `0.182` for preamble 0
and `0.251` for any preamble. The best preamble-0 point is shift `-256`,
fraction `-0.5`, CFO `+47 kHz`, but it is not close to the `0.61` threshold and
must not be promoted into the live list.

## Timing audit and verdict

The executed harness confirms current PRACH collection delay and timing
compensation are both zero, with a 12,000-sample tail extension. Historical
Pavonis runs measured run-dependent phone uplink phases and, in CP2828/CP2829,
showed that internal UL-CCCH intent can occur without a detector-valid Msg1 on
air. The older `352536`-sample srsUE compensation and later `237336`-sample
20-ms-lead compensation therefore cannot be selected from these background
windows alone.

CP3050 closes “Msg1 is hidden inside the first two nominal channel-1 windows.”
It leaves two possibilities: the actual burst is elsewhere in the 160-ms
period, or the phone generated access intent without radiating valid PRACH.
The next discriminator should reuse the established independent bladeRF UL
observer during the current successful-primer-to-M2-target handoff. Only a
detector-valid air burst and its measured phase should choose the next M2SDR
collection delay. Do not live-sweep delays or lower the detector threshold.

Consolidation remains blocked.
