# CP3051 - Current-target independent UL observer no-RF gate

CP3051 prepares the next discriminator without transmitting RF. It composes
the unchanged CP3040 channel-1 phone ladder wrapper with the established CP2758
bladeRF RX2 uplink observer. The outer wrapper waits for the target `gnb`
process, which cannot be confused with the local-M2 `srsenb` primer, and then
captures the first 65 seconds of target uplink at 11.52 Msps.

## Verdict

The CP3052 RF measurement is gated. It changes no gNB binary, Soapy module,
channel mapping, PRACH timing, detector threshold, phone policy, or protocol
configuration. Its only addition is an independent uplink observation covering
the first 65 seconds of the exact CP3048 current target. The result must first
answer whether a detector-valid phone Msg1 exists in air and where it falls in
the 160 ms occasion period; no live timing constant should be changed until
that result is known.
