CP3054 executes the CP3053 control with a fresh artifact stamp. The local M2
primer and the frozen OCUDU channel-1 target both start successfully, then the
phone completes a full reboot while the target is already transmitting. The
private N3 and voice policies are reapplied and stale private state is absent.
The run is a valid negative ladder datapoint: the phone sees a strong clean
cell after reboot, but neither the gNB nor qcore sees an access attempt.

## New discriminator

The exact gNB readback shows the channel-1 target transmitting at `0 dB`
attenuation. The phone's stable `-62 dBm` target-cell reading is materially
hotter than the historical channel-1 phone-success state. Earlier channel-1
srsENB/M2 controls found the `0.1 dB` class clipped and poorly correlated,
whereas `16 dB` attenuation produced decoded SIB1 and subsequently RACH plus
NAS authentication/security progress. This makes receiver overdrive or
front-end compression the leading concrete splitter for the current strong-
cell/no-Msg1 state.

CP3054 closes the stale primer/qcore/phone-context theory: a full reboot under
the correct channel-1 target does not restore Msg1. It does not reopen PRACH
delay, threshold, stage-1, or descriptor [interface-redacted]. The next test is one isolated
OCUDU channel-1 run at `16 dB` TX attenuation, with every protocol, timing,
detector, gain, binary, and phone-reboot control unchanged. The proven
CP2892/CP2893 baseline must remain untouched; use separately named helper and
handoff wrappers.
