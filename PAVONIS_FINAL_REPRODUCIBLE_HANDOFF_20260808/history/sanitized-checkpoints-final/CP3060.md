`-11 dB` RSRQ, and `+23 dB` SINR. There are zero in-service rows, zero private

Compared with effective-ATT0 CP3054/CP3056, the phone RSRP moves from `-62` to

This proves that the force path reached the correct physical TX chain and that
the earlier ATT0 state was materially hotter. Nevertheless, the phone still
does not emit a detector-visible access attempt.

The gNB has zero PRACH, RAR, PUSCH, uplink CRC-OK, and RRC setup events. Its
transport remains clean: zero Soapy TX timeouts, zero downlink-late events,
zero underflows, and zero time errors.

## Verdict

Downlink overdrive is closed as the current no-Msg1 cause. Both a strong
`-62 dBm` state and a level-aligned `-75 dBm` state give clean reported NR
quality but no air access. Do not [interface-redacted] PRACH receive timing, thresholds, stage-1
candidates, RX gain, or attenuation from this result: the primary evidence is
that the phone is not transmitting a detector-valid Msg1.

The next action is no-RF: compare the current OCUDU/M2 generated config,
