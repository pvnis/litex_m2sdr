# CP3064 - Filtered phone RACH plus independent UL observer no-RF gate

CP3064 gates the direct-air discriminator required by CP3063. No RF is
transmitted in this checkpoint.

## Composition

The new outer wrapper preserves CP3062 byte-for-byte as the owner of the
filtered Qualcomm observer, local srsENB/M2 positive-control primer, and frozen
OCUDU/M2 channel-1 ATT16 target. Once the first target `gnb` process appears,
it starts the established bladeRF RX2 observer at:

- center 1747.5 MHz;
- 11.52 Msps, 10 MHz bandwidth;
- RX2, manual gain 32, normal filter;
- 921,600,000 complex SC16 samples, exactly 80 seconds.

The raw capture remains on [host-redacted] until the five filtered RACH-attempt groups
have been aligned and bounded replay windows retained. Only compact 1 ms TSV,
JSON, and capture metadata are fetched initially.

## Duration-only helper change

The prior CP2559 capture helper hard-times out at 75 seconds, so it cannot
validly record CP3063's last attempt group near target +70 seconds after launch
overhead. The isolated CP3064 helper changes only the default sample count from
45 to 80 seconds, the process timeout from 75 to 105 seconds, and checkpoint
markers. After normalizing those fields, it is byte-identical to CP2559. The
isolated control similarly changes only names, the 80-second default, and its
wait bound; normalized comparison against CP2758 passes.

## Verdict

Run one fresh CP3065. The primer must again prove the filtered Diag observer;
the RX2 capture starts after primer teardown and therefore relies on its
established historical air-path controls rather than claiming a same-run air
positive. For the target, join filtered RACH attempts to exact RX2 sample
offsets, extract only bounded windows, and replay those windows through the
validated detector. A detector-valid RX2 Msg1 with an empty M2SDR detector
localizes the defect to M2 receive timing/presentation. No detector-valid RX2
Msg1 localizes the failure to phone RF transmission despite MAC intent. Do not
change live stage-1 candidates before that split.
