# CP3066 - Independent observer sees no valid phone PRACH

CP3066 is a no-RF analysis of the complete CP3065 bladeRF RX2 recording. It
uses the raw-file birth timestamp as sample zero and tests every filtered phone
RACH-attempt record without changing the frozen OCUDU or phone configuration.

## Bounded extraction

The extractor maps all 33 target Diag attempts and 70 nearby configured-band
energy peaks into 103 candidates. Each candidate has one 12 ms SC16 slice and
17 overlapping 23,520-sample CF32 detector windows, for 1,751 windows total.
All source intervals lie inside CP3065's continuous 80-second capture. The
bounded SC16 archive is 34,265,975 bytes; the extracted raw payload is
56,954,880 bytes and the detector windows total 329,468,160 bytes.

## Replay-geometry correction

The inherited CP1965 replay harness hard-coded `rb_offset=8`. That is not the
current phone-cell geometry: the frozen OCUDU YAML has
`prach_frequency_start=1`, the runtime warning reports start 1, and
`fapi_adaptor/phy/p7/pdu_translators/prach.cpp` copies
`msg1_frequency_start` into the detector context's `rb_offset`.

A minimal diagnostic-only harness makes the replay RB offset configurable.
With its environment variable absent it remains bit-for-bit equivalent to the
old RB-8 harness on the control manifest; an invalid offset 52 exits 2. This
closes an offline-instrument confound rather than changing any live code.

Candidate 63 is the only attempt-aligned transient with a notable subband
contrast. It is centered near -1.355 MHz rather than the advertised PRACH band
near -3.96 MHz. The exhaustive focused stage-1 check above proves that it is
not a recoverable PRACH at another RB placement either.

## Energy-domain result

At the advertised PRACH band, none of the 33 Diag event windows separates from
its controls: the largest event rise over local median is 0.260 dB and the
largest target-minus-control contrast is 1.211 dB. The highest target level is
-42.102 dBFS. For 32 of 33 attempts, even a frequency-agnostic search gives at
most 1.875 dB contrast; candidate 63 is the unrelated off-band exception.

## Verdict and next rung

The CP3065 observer is not timing-blind, and the negative is not caused by the
legacy RB-8 replay constant. Across every phone-reported attempt, the
independent air recording contains no detector-valid NR Msg1 at the advertised
band, under the validated timing/CFO stage-1 envelope, or at any alternative
baseline RB placement. OCUDU's zero live accepts are therefore not presently
an M2SDR RX-window or detector-input presentation failure.

The phone's `RACH Attempt` records establish MAC intent/state, not a valid
physical transmission. The frontier moves to explaining why the phone emits no
valid over-air Msg1 under the OCUDU broadcast/configuration. Stay no-RF first:
compare the exact PRACH, UL-BWP, power-control, TDD/duplex, and frequency
parameters delivered by the working srsENB/M2 primer against the frozen OCUDU
cell, then design a same-observer positive control that does not consume the
bladeRF RX2 channel. Do not [interface-redacted] the OCUDU detector or stage-1 envelope against
noise.
