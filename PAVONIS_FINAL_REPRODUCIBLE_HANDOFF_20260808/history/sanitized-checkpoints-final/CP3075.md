## Evidence

- Stamp: `[date-redacted]CP3075_TIMING230613_RETRY`.
- The frozen wrapper again carries timing compensation `230613`, report-slot

- The local M2/srsENB primer starts and filtered Diag records two RACH triggers

- The CP3073 continuous wrapper launches the older CP2664 qcore helper. That
  helper enables the foreign-home-PLMN accommodations but not the established
  unknown-update identity fallback.
- The preserved CP3019 full-data golden uses the CP2989 qcore helper, which
  enables that fallback and converts this stale update into the normal identity

  exact guard around the reject observed here.
- Cleanup passes: no radio/core process remains on either host, M2 program mode
  is `N`, and one authorized ADB device remains present.

No subscriber, temporary identity, device identifier, or NAS payload is
recorded.

## Decision

Do not retry CP3073 unchanged a third time and do not classify the primer as an
RF regression. Patch only the isolated CP3073 continuous wrapper to use the
already-proven CP2989 qcore helper, update its marker and hash assertions, and
leave the preserved CP3059/CP3019 paths unchanged. Gate that launcher change
without RF before retrying the `230613/0/40` target shape.
