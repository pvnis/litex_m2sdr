## Offline model

For each captured Msg3 target, the actual edge needed for alignment is the
published coarse edge plus the measured Msg3 CP residual. Across CP3094, all
eight such values are congruent to approximately `-138 mod 823` samples.
This is a physical PHY interval, not an arbitrary fitted delay:
`11.52 Msps / 14 symbols per 1 ms slot = 822.857 samples`.

The model snaps the measured edge to the nearest point on the lattice with
period 823 and anchor -138. Replay results are:

| Dataset | Reliable targets | Old absolute residual | Predicted absolute residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CP3092 | 6 | up to 374 samples | up to 4 samples |
| CP3094 | 8 | up to 367 samples | up to 5 samples |

The median predicted absolute residual is 3 samples in both runs. CP3092's two
excluded rows have CP correlation scores `0.160` and `0.107`; the six reliable
rows are an independent cross-run validation. The complete machine-readable
replay is `cp3095_edge_lattice_replay.json`.

## Patch

the generic non-PRACH offset. It preserves the raw edge in telemetry and adds
`edge_samples_applied`. The control is default-off:

- `PAVONIS_NON_PRACH_UL_RX_OFFSET_EDGE_LATTICE_PERIOD_SAMPLES=0` keeps the
  prior bit-for-bit calculation;
- the diagnostic shape sets period `823`;
- `PAVONIS_NON_PRACH_UL_RX_OFFSET_EDGE_LATTICE_ANCHOR_SAMPLES` defaults to
  `-138` and is used only when the period is nonzero.
