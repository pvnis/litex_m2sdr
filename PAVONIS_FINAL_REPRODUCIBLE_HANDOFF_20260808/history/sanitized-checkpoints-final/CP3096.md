## Geometry-correct replay

The legacy CP2282 production replay initially gave different metrics because it
hardcoded PUSCH `n_id` and DMRS scrambling ID 1. CP3096 uses 500. The
test-only harness now accepts optional `N_ID`, preserving default 1. With
`N_ID=500`, replay of targets 1 and 2 reproduces live SINR, EVM, time
alignment, CFO, and CRC closely, proving the saved shifted-path symbols contain
the decoder failure.

A bounded `-10..+10 kHz` CFO sweep at 500 Hz steps finds no CRC-positive point
for either strong target. Best EVM remains poor: `6.61` at `-9.5 kHz` and
`5.69` at `-6.5 kHz`. Bulk CFO alone is not the remaining variable.
