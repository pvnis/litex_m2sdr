## Pass/Fail Join

The immediately preceding CP3108 primer is the valid control. It required one
RACH procedure, completed private user-plane activation, and armed the
PDU-session downlink fixture. CP3110C required three RACH procedures, never
activated user plane, and never armed that fixture.

The qcore event sequence makes the split specific. CP3108 registered the
private test subscriber and activated the expected private data session about
two seconds later. CP3110C's first registration requested only the `ims` DNN,
which qcore intentionally ignores by the established CP2977 policy. Later
attempts entered the known unknown-update fallback and finally failed
subscriber lookup. No credential, subscriber identifier, or NAS payload is
recorded in this report.

The radio fixture itself remained active: CP3110C armed three setup follow-ups,
issued the corresponding forced retransmission feedback, and reached qcore on
all three RACH procedures. The preferred phone APN is still the enabled
`internet` default APN, and global mobile data remains enabled. The APN/config
drift theory is therefore closed for this failure.

## Verdict

`PRIMER-SUPPLY VOID / PHONE-CORE LIFECYCLE STATE`.

CP3109 remains unjudged. The SIM power-cycle alone no longer guarantees the
fresh phone/core data-session epoch that it produced at CP3087. Do not run
another full CP3110 shape until the established full phone fresh-epoch helper
and SIM cycle are followed by a green standalone CP2985 primer control. This
isolates recovery from the OCUDU target and avoids spending target RF windows
on a precondition failure.
