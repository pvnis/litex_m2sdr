# Checkpoint 3111: combined phone reset restores exact primer

Date: [date-redacted]

## Recovery

CP3110 showed that a SIM power-cycle alone no longer restored the srsENB phone
primer. CP3111 therefore applied the two established reversible operations in
the previously untested order:

1. full phone reboot and policy restore through the CP2969 helper;
2. SIM power-cycle through CP3086, followed by the CP3087 read-only policy
   audit.

Both operations passed. The phone returned in automatic/full-RAT state with
the expected roaming and voice policy, no pre-existing private interface, and
an authorized ADB endpoint.

## Lifecycle Finding

After clean process shutdown and phone-policy restore, both hosts were idle and
the M2SDR returned to program mode `N`, but the phone still retained its
private interface. The phone session therefore demonstrably outlives the
stopped core/RAN epoch. This is the direct mechanism behind the CP3110
next-run primer failures.

Before the CP3109 OCUDU target is retried, repeat the proven full-phone-reset
plus SIM-cycle sequence. No further standalone primer repetition is needed.
