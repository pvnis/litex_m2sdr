## Scope

CP3112 proved that the active common search space has one AL4 candidate and no
AL8 candidate, while the fallback scheduler was forced to AL8. CP3113 changes
only that contradiction. It does not rebuild or replace the gNB, edit the
starter, alter the generated cell config, or retain the failed contention-slot
rebase.

The isolated wrapper uses the unchanged CP3109 real gNB
`90a5db019d52b3f893572d96a8338eae67a81b1e4169e8eb12c4afab20375f8a`.
It preserves the accepted non-PRACH edge/CFO controls and bounded Msg3
diagnostics, exports `PAVONIS_GNB_FALLBACK_DL_DCI_AGGR_LEV=4`, and explicitly
unsets all four scheduler-rebase controls.
