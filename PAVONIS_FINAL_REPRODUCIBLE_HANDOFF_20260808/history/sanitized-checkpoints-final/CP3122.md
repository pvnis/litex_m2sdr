## Ordering and accounting

`cp3122_run_reproducibility_campaign.py` enforces:

1. ZMQ reaches its valid target before bounded hardware can start.
2. Bounded hardware reaches its valid target before a new phone RF attempt can
   start.
3. CP3121D is imported as one explicitly labeled pre-gate phone seed only
   after both earlier stage gates pass. Nineteen or more staged phone successes
   are therefore still required to reach phone N=20.

Every attempt gets a fresh stamp, a mode-700 directory, a privacy-safe JSON
summary, exact source/archive hashes, and one campaign-state row. Valid and raw
counts are separate. Two consecutive same-class voids stop the stage for
review. Raw caps stop unbounded retrying. Any directory left by controller
interruption is recovered as an explicit `interrupted_void`, so a disconnect
cannot silently improve a rate.

Each RF attempt ends with both-host idle checks, authorized ADB, and M2SDR
program mode `N`. Newly generated extracted bounded-run directories are
deleted only after their source archives and summaries are present and hashed;
the archives remain retained.
