## Root cause

`cp2380_prearmed_netlink_userplane_probe.sh` is intentionally an add-only
watcher. It waits for `/run/netns/[interface-redacted]` and for the [interface-redacted] address event; it never
creates the namespace.

The proven CP2392 sequence used the separate
`cp2378_prepare_ue_netns.sh prepare` precondition. The CP3122 campaign
deployed and armed the watcher but omitted that helper. Consequently raw 2,
raw 4, and raw 7 could never pass the packet gate even when the radio and core
path progressed.

## Controller correction

The campaign now:

1. Hash-pins and deploys `cp2378_prepare_ue_netns.sh`.
2. Creates `[interface-redacted]` before arming the add-only watcher.
3. Starts RF only if namespace preparation and watcher arming both pass.
4. Cleans the namespace after the run.
5. Requires cleanup success and namespace absence in the [host-redacted] postcheck.
6. Classifies prepare/arm failures as `runner_launch_void`.

The radio fixture, binary, PRACH envelope, gains, timing, scheduler behavior,
and packet probe are unchanged.

Raw 7 remains append-only in campaign state. Its aggregate interpretation is
radio/control success plus data-instrumentation void, not a negative attach
sample.
