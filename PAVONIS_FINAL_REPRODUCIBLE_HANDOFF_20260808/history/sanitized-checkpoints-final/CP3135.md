# Checkpoint 3135: empirical UL timing constants explained

Date: [date-redacted]

RF executed: no

Verdict: Tier 1 timing provenance is substantially closed. The approximately
230.6k-sample PRACH and non-PRACH compensations are not an unexplained DMA
delay. They are dominated by the configured 20 ms timed-TX release offset,
with one remaining fixed-class calibration residual of approximately
210-213 samples.

## Source-proven mechanism

The working M2SDR path exports:

```text
M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS=[date-redacted]
```

In the pinned LiteX Soapy source, each positive `HAS_TIME` descriptor is
released at:

```text
release_timeNs = pkt->timeNs + tx_time_offset_ns
```

The timed-TX gateware converts that timestamp into the RFIC sample domain and
holds the stream until its free-running AD9361 sample counter reaches the
descriptor timestamp. The counter increments once per IQ sample frame. It is
also the source for RX DMA timestamps, so the measured TX-to-RX displacement is
in one hardware sample domain rather than a cross-host wall-clock join.

At the active 11.52 Msps sample rate:

```text
20 ms * 11.52 Msps = 230400 samples
```

CP3072 measured valid phone PRACH energy at `230613 +/- 4` samples after the
nominal collection lattice. CP3100 established the working non-PRACH
compensation magnitude as `230610` samples. The decomposition is therefore:

| Path | Full magnitude | Programmed 20 ms | Residual |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| PRACH | 230613 samples | 230400 | 213 samples / 18.490 us |
| non-PRACH | 230610 samples | 230400 | 210 samples / 18.229 us |

The source-programmed release shift explains 99.9% of either constant. The
three-sample difference is 0.260 us and is within the four-sample PRACH
measurement spread.

## PRACH and non-PRACH semantics

The stage-1 detector can search shifted PRACH windows, but the selected shift
is explicitly added back into the reported timing advance when
`PAVONIS_PRACH_STAGE1_TA_COMPENSATE_SELECTED_SHIFT` is active. The RAR TA is
therefore not silently computed from the transformed window.

For connected uplink, OCUDU applies a signed timestamp remap before forming
PUSCH, PUCCH, and SRS symbols. The dynamic edge path measures the accepted
PRACH energy edge and snaps it to the 823-sample OFDM-symbol lattice. CP3096
and CP3100 selected the congruent canonical edge:

```text
-6722 = -138 - 8 * 823
```

That lattice step is why the residual connected-uplink phase can be held within
the observed few-sample boundary tolerance instead of inheriting a whole-slot
ambiguity.

## What remains unproven

The common 210-213-sample residual is a real, repeatable calibration term. The
RFIC-domain counter and timed-TX comparison rule out NTP, host scheduling, and
large clock-domain-crossing error. The retained sources do not identify one
exact AD9361, digital-filter, stream, or analog path stage whose documented
latency equals 210-213 samples. The 256-entry DMA ring and 128-buffer prime are
not an acceptable explanation by coincidence alone: timed descriptors are
explicitly gated by sample timestamp after buffering.

The honest mechanism statement is therefore:

> Programmed 20 ms RF release displacement plus an approximately 18.3 us fixed
> RF/stream calibration residual.

## Engineering consequence

The large empirical constants should be replaced by one coherent derivation:

```text
base_samples = round(sample_rate_hz * tx_time_offset_ns / 1e9)
ul_timeline_compensation = base_samples + calibrated_residual_samples
```

PRACH and connected UL should consume the same base term, with only the
measured three-sample/lattice rounding represented in their existing
channel-specific alignment logic. This removes the misleading appearance of
two independently [interface-redacted] 230k-sample values. A future hardware-latency
measurement may replace the approximately 210-sample residual; this checkpoint
does not invent that attribution.
