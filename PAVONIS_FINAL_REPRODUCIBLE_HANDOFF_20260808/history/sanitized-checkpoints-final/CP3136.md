## Method

`cp3136_analyze_primer_fixtures.py` verifies the campaign state, all 20 valid
phone attempt-summary hashes, and all 20 [host-redacted] archive hashes. It streams only
the archived primer log from each tarball and emits bounded event counts and
PUCCH SNR summaries. The retained CP3121D seed uses its immutable fresh-stamp
archive; the other 19 rows use their attempt manifests.

## SI-PDSCH power sign

The source and RF evidence agree exactly:

- profile-NR `+6 dB` reaches common PDCCH and SI PDSCH;
- PDCCH applies it as `convert_dB_to_amplitude(+6)`;
- PDSCH stores data `+6` and DMRS `+3 dB`, then applies
  `convert_dB_to_amplitude(-ratio)`;
- the predicted PDSCH-DMRS versus PDCCH deficit is `-9 dB`;
- retained live-IQ medians were `-9.46` and `-9.31 dB`;
- the scoped compatibility path changed the median to `-1.25 dB`.

This is a FAPI-to-PHY sign-contract mismatch, not an RF-level calibration
problem. The current default-off SI-RNTI-only override is a proven,
well-contained workaround. The product fix is to normalize profile-NR power
semantics at the translator/processor boundary and revise the generic unit
tests after standards and upstream review. Applying the SI override globally
without that review would merely hide the contract mismatch.

## Primer follow-up UL grants

Every successful primer armed the bounded 512-opportunity branch and hit its
128-row logging cap. Across the first 64 traced SR opportunities in each run:

| PUCCH SR class | Rows | Run-median SNR |
|---|---:|---:|
| native valid positive | 293 | +16.029 dB |
| invalid or negative | 987 | -10.675 dB |

The approximately 27 dB two-state split explains why native scheduling request
supply is intermittent. The fixture converts invalid/negative configured SR
opportunities into the existing scheduler request path. This is a PHY receive
accommodation, not a transport or scheduler root cause.

It also is not sufficient by itself. CP2971 exercised the synthetic grants but
received repeated zero-buffer-status uplinks and no next NAS response. The
real fix is to make PUCCH timing, power, and demodulation reliable enough that
native SR/BSR drives scheduling; retaining 512 synthetic requests can then be
removed.

## Primer reactive downlink

Every valid N20 primer:

- armed one setup and one PDU-session retry budget;
- consumed two forced NACKs in each phase, four per run and 80 total;
- reported all first 64 ACK rows as valid positive;
- had no non-positive ACK row in that cap;
- had a run-median valid-ACK SNR of `+15.920 dB`.

The workaround therefore does not compensate weak or ambiguous ACK reception.
It deliberately tells the scheduler that a strong positive ACK was a NACK.
Historically, one copy of the connected-mode request produced no next uplink,
while forcing the scheduler-shaped repeat unlocked the response.

This localizes the open defect to ACK-to-HARQ-process attribution or to
connected-mode payload delivery/upper-layer response after a valid ACK. The
retained evidence does not distinguish those two mechanisms. A real fix must
repair that association or use the appropriate RLC/RRC/NAS retransmission
behavior; false ACK semantics remain a known workaround and must not be
presented as standards behavior.
