# CP3139 - Standalone OCUDU phone attach no-RF gate

Date: [date-redacted]

## Question

Can the proven CP3121D phone/data shape be exercised without any srsENB
primer, while preserving the primer-based POC byte-for-byte and failing closed
before RF?

## Result

PASS. Tier 2 is ready for one cold, standalone OCUDU attempt.

No RF ran. No remote binary, config, helper, or service was changed. The four
CP3121 entry-point files remain byte-identical to their recorded hashes.

## New isolated entry point

`cp3139_execute_standalone_ocudu_phone_rf.sh` performs:

1. the proven fresh-phone epoch;
2. one SIM power cycle and policy audit;
3. qcore start on the host-local private RAN interface;
4. phone preparation with no srsENB process ever started;
5. pre-arming of the qcore activation and private HTTP endpoints;
6. the exact CP3121D OCUDU/M2SDR AL8 target on physical channel 1 at 16 dB
   attenuation;
7. a full phone reboot while OCUDU is already live;
8. target activation, four echo requests, and one MiB HTTP transfer in each
   direction;
9. bounded observation and complete teardown.

The RF shape remains pinned at PRACH timing compensation 230613 samples,
report-slot offset 0, RA response window 40, phase mode off, bounded Msg3
capture, the scoped SI-PDSCH power compatibility, and the deployed CP3120 AL8
gNB wrapper. The only protocol-sequence change is removal of the primer epoch.

## Standalone mechanics

`cp3139_run_standalone_ocudu_metrics_rf.sh` is an isolated copy of the
CP3121 continuous runner. Its standalone branch:

- requires `LOCAL_M2_PRIMER=0`, qcore RAN interface `lo`, and phone reboot
  under target;
- skips srsENB start, selector-driven primer registration, primer service
  wait/status, and srsENB stop;
- treats the pre-target qcore activation count as zero for the new target
  activation gate;
- packages only qcore, OCUDU, and phone artifacts;
- emits `STANDALONE_OCUDU=1` in the summary.

All original primer behavior remains in the isolated copy's non-standalone
branch and no original wrapper was edited.
