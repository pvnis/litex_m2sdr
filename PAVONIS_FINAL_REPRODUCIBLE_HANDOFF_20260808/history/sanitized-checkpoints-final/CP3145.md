# CP3145 - Sanitized checkpoint corpus

## Verdict

PASS. Phase 2 creates a copy-only, ordered checkpoint corpus without modifying
the original reports.

The final corpus contains `3096` sanitized Markdown reports representing
`3095` checkpoint numbers. The two legitimate CP72 reports are retained as
ordered `_A` and `_B` variants. CP3144 and this Phase 2 checkpoint are both
included.

## Method

`sanitize_pavonis_checkpoints.py` performs deterministic contextual redaction
for:

- absolute dates, timestamps, and wall-clock values;
- credential values and references;
- host, endpoint, address, interface, and attached-device identity;
- subscriber and SIM identity;
- labelled NAS payloads and long payload-like hexadecimal values.

Before general substitutions, the tool removes labelled sensitive payloads.
It then protects every remaining SHA-256 value, applies the other redactions,
restores the SHA-256 values, and requires the per-report SHA-256 multiset to
match the source. Reports are named `CP####.md`; duplicate reports use stable
alphabetic variants.

The sanitizer is fail-closed: it writes into a temporary sibling, scans every
candidate output for residual sensitive patterns, verifies count and hash
invariants, and publishes only after all gates pass.
