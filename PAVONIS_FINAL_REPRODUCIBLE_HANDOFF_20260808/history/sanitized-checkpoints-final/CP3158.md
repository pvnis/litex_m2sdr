## Method

The benchmark ran sequential 45-second transfers over HTTP on a dedicated
persistent server. The downlink rate uses bytes received by the phone. The
uplink rate uses bytes received by the core before its receiver-enforced
deadline. Interface deltas and sender counters are cross-checks, not the
reported throughput authority.
