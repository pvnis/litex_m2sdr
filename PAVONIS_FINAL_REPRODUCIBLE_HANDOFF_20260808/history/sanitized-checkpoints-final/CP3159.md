## Isolated Baseline

The first Phase B run is pinned to the existing conducted shape:

- 10 MHz Band 3 FDD;
- 52 PRB at 11.52 Msps;
- one spatial layer;
- gNB TX attenuation 5 dB;
- UE TX gain 66 dB;
- gNB maximum UE MCS 9.

This already reduces attenuation by 11 dB relative to the phone OTA shape.
The run must still prove downlink cell SNR at least 8 dB, well above the
approximately 3.8 dB real-phone class, before its throughput is accepted as
the high-SNR baseline.
