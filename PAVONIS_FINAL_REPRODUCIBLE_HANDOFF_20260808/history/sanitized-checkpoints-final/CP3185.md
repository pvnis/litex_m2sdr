# Checkpoint 3185: corrected 15 MHz OTA post-Msg3 partial

## Verdict

HONEST PARTIAL. The SSB fix succeeds over the air, but this run does not
produce a valid throughput result.

The UE selects the 15 MHz cell at `13.582 dB` SNR, decodes SIB1 and RAR,
prepares Msg3, and the gNB decodes both observed PUSCH transmissions with CRC
OK. Transmitter health is clean: zero Soapy timeout and zero downlink-late
events. This definitively closes the CP3180/CP3181 cell-acquisition failure.

The run then stops before private PDU-session establishment, so neither
sustained endpoint result exists.

## No-RF diagnosis

The successful CP3176 10 MHz controller uses the proven CP3167 gNB launcher.
That launcher exports the default-off compatibility flag that appends a native
`0:0` PUCCH format-1 phase candidate. The CP3179 15 MHz executor instead
selected the bare gNB binary, silently dropping this fixture.

The live contrast is decisive:

- CP3176: native candidate active; `124/128` phase-search rows select it;
- CP3185: native candidate absent; all `71` phase-search rows remain invalid;
- CP3185 nevertheless captures four strong PUCCH grids at roughly `-32 dB`
  EPRE;
- production-detector offline replay of those exact grids with native `0:0`
  correction validates all `4/4`, with metrics `78.602379..116.826744`.

This is not weak uplink RF or bad non-PRACH sample timing. It is an isolated
runner regression: the 15 MHz chain omitted a launcher already required by
the proven 10 MHz baseline.
