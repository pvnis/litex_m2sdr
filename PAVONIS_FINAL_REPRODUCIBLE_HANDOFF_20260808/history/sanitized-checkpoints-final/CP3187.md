# Checkpoint 3187: 15 MHz OTA traffic partial

## Verdict

HONEST PARTIAL. The corrected 15 MHz cell now acquires, attaches, establishes
the private data session, and carries sustained traffic in both directions.
The benchmark is not a formal pass because the client timed out waiting for
the final uplink receiver acknowledgement and did not mark its result
complete.

The radio result is valid:

- cell selection succeeds at `12.370 dB` SNR;
- the radio runner returns `0`;
- the native PUCCH fixture is active: `121/128` phase searches are valid and
  the native candidate is selected `114/128` times;
- PUSCH is `74/128` CRC OK with median CRC-OK SINR `5.435005 dB`;
- Soapy timeout and downlink-late counts are both zero.

The two receiver-authoritative files each contain a full 45-second
measurement:

- provisional downlink: `2.641916 Mbit/s`;
- provisional uplink: `0.022653 Mbit/s`.

These are `11.44%` and `86.44%` below the CP3176 10 MHz baseline,
respectively. This run does not support the prediction that bandwidth alone
raises sustained throughput.

## Tool failure

The core-side receiver completes both directions and records the full uplink
window. The UE-side client records the full downlink window, sends the uplink,
then raises `TimeoutError` while waiting five seconds for the final
receiver-ack line. Because the client does not persist its uplink sender
cross-check before that wait, `complete` remains false and the existing
analyzer exits nonzero.

This is benchmark bookkeeping after successful traffic, not radio or attach
failure. The values remain provisional until an unchanged radio run passes a
no-RF-hardened client.
