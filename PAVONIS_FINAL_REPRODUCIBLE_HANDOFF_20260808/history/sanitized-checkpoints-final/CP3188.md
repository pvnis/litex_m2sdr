# Checkpoint 3188: sustained-client ACK hardening

## Verdict

PASS, NO RF. The isolated benchmark client now waits up to 30 seconds for the
final uplink receiver acknowledgement instead of five seconds. It also records
an explicit acknowledgement status, keeps `complete=false` on timeout, and
returns nonzero rather than losing the reason for an otherwise complete
traffic window.

The traffic protocol, 45-second directional windows, server, gNB chain, UE,
and radio configuration are unchanged.

## Gates

- a loopback integration test delays the final ACK by six seconds and passes;
- controller validation passes;
- closed RF returns `2` with zero artifacts;
- the client is deployed to the UE role and its hash, 30-second timeout, and
  durable ACK-status marker verify remotely.
