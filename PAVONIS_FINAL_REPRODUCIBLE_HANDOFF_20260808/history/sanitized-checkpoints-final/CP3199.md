# Checkpoint 3199: SR-grant-size OTA partial

## Verdict

PARTIAL. The size-only candidate completed healthy 10 MHz all-wireless radio
operation and both 45-second receiver windows, but the older UE traffic client
did not persist its uplink result. The formal analyzer correctly rejected the
run. This checkpoint does not claim a formal sustained benchmark pass.

The run is valid for scheduler/radio analysis:

- PUSCH: `69/128` CRC OK;
- median CRC-OK PUSCH SINR: `18.915651 dB`;
- median PUSCH TBS: `800` bytes, versus CP3176's `528`;
- Soapy timeout/downlink-late: `0/0`.

The scheduler log contains `5,151` UL new transmissions with mean TBS
`641.3` bytes and maximum `4,737` bytes. There are 86 grants at `4,097` bytes,
showing the SR-only control was exercised. There are still 1,695 grants at

the SR-only control was filtered.

## Traffic evidence

The receiver-authoritative windows that did complete measured:

- downlink at the UE receiver: `2.637275 Mbit/s`;
- uplink at the core receiver: `0.188690 Mbit/s`.

Against CP3176, these are `-11.60%` DL and a tentative `+12.94%` UL; median
PUSCH TBS is `+51.52%`. The core receiver recorded the full 45-second uplink,
but the UE-side JSON contains only its completed downlink. The analyzer then
failed on the absent UE uplink record. This is the pre-CP3188 final-ACK
bookkeeping class, not a radio failure.
