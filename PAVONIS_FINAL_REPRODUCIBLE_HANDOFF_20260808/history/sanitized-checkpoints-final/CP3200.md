# Checkpoint 3200: final-ACK retry gate

## Verdict

PASS. The CP3199 formal-measurement blocker is isolated to the older sustained
client. A no-RF retry controller now selects the already-proven CP3188 client
without changing radio, scheduler, traffic duration, endpoints, or analysis.

## Change

The controller is copied from CP3169 and has two logical edits:

- select the local `cp3200_sustained_tcp_client.py`;
- update the module docstring.

The client is byte-identical to CP3188's hardened client. It waits up to
30 seconds for the final uplink receiver acknowledgement, records ACK status,
and remains incomplete/nonzero if the ACK truly never arrives.
