# Checkpoint 3202: scheduler cadence audit and no-RF gate

## Verdict

PASS. No fixed OCUDU grant-repeat throttle exists in the steady-state UL
scheduler. The scheduler evaluates eligible UL new transmissions every slot.
The existing `cell_cfg.pusch.min_k2` setting is a separate response-delay
control, and an isolated `4 -> 1` candidate passes the established 10 MHz ZMQ
attach, session and private gateway-ping chain.

No RF was opened.

## Existing-data audit

The binary-safe CP3176 and CP3201 scheduler logs were streamed through the
bounded parser without extraction or raw-log printing.

| Metric | CP3176 default | CP3201 SR4096 |
|---|---:|---:|
| steady UL newTx rows | 5,929 | 5,037 |
| `k2=4` rows | 5,929 | 5,037 |
| positive inter-newTx median | 2 slots | 2 slots |
| positive inter-newTx p90 | 30 slots | 40 slots |
| burst-size median / p90 | 1 / 4 | 1 / 4 |
| inter-burst median / p90 | 27 / 60 slots | 30 / 72 slots |

Consecutive one-slot grants and bursts up to 16 grants disprove a hard
per-grant cadence limiter. Most singleton bursts are separated by about
27-30 slots, which follows the arrival/loss/re-reporting of UE demand rather
than a scheduler sleep constant. The source agrees: each slot constructs a UL
candidate whenever effective BSR demand or the pending-SR fallback is nonzero,
subject to grid and HARQ availability.

`min_k2=4` controls the PDCCH-to-PUSCH response lead. Reducing it cannot create
more pending demand or change the one-slot in-burst capability. It remains a
valid isolated response-delay sensitivity, with a theoretical effect limited
to fresh-demand latency.

## Isolated candidate

The proven launcher and baseline binary are untouched. The side-by-side
launcher:

1. verifies the pinned proven binary hash;
2. requires one `-c` YAML argument;
3. rejects an existing `min_k2` setting;
4. copies the generated YAML and inserts only:

```yaml
  pusch:
    min_k2: 1
```

5. emits a hash-only activation marker;
6. preserves the proven native PUCCH candidate setting and executes the
   baseline binary.

Local syntax/Python gates pass. A remote config-only fixture proves exactly two
added YAML lines. The launcher/config hashes match deployment.

## Decision

Run one bounded all-wireless 10 MHz sustained benchmark with `min_k2=1`, the
SR fallback restored to 512, and every other CP3176 control frozen. Compare
throughput and radio health to CP3176. If it is neutral, retire/revert the
response-delay branch rather than stacking another scheduler change.
