# Checkpoint 3207: SR-period 10 ms OTA measurement partial

## Verdict

PARTIAL, with a positive signal that requires one clean confirmation. The
unchanged 10 MHz OTA chain attached and carried both sustained directions.
Both receiver-authoritative measurements completed, but a benchmark endpoint
race prevented the UE result from recording its uplink crosscheck and caused
the generic analyzer to fail.

This is not an RF or scheduler failure, and it is not yet promoted as the
formal cadence result.

## Frozen change

The only scheduler/config difference from CP3176 was the CP3206 isolated
candidate:

```yaml
pucch:
  sr_period_ms: 10
```

`min_k2=4`, the 512-byte SR bootstrap, native SR/BSR, bandwidth, MCS, spatial
layers, duplex, RF gain/attenuation, and every frontier fixture remained
unchanged.

## Radio health

- cell-search SNR: `14.755 dB`;
- gNB PUSCH CRC OK: `76/128`;
- median CRC-OK PUSCH SINR: `6.291 dB`;
- median PUSCH TBS: `528 bytes`;
- Soapy TX timeout/downlink-late: `0/0`.

The run is a valid radio and traffic datapoint.

## Provisional measurement

The receiver-authoritative endpoints recorded:

|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DL | 2.983260 Mbit/s | 3.221643 Mbit/s | +7.99% |
| UL | 0.167066 Mbit/s | 0.201818 Mbit/s | +20.80% |

The cadence parser independently supports the UL movement. Steady UL newTx
rows increased from `5,929` to `6,929`; median inter-burst gap fell from `27`
to `20` slots and p90 fell from `60` to `52` slots. All grants retained
`k2=4`.

## Tooling failure

The core-side server completed and durably wrote both 45-second directions.
The client hit `ConnectionResetError` during its final uplink `send()` as the
server's exact 45-second receive deadline closed. Its durable JSON therefore
contains the completed DL receiver result but not the uplink sender
crosscheck. The legacy analyzer assumes that optional field exists and raised
`KeyError`.

The core receiver's UL and UE receiver's DL values are intact, but the
checkpoint remains partial because the normal complete/crosscheck gate did
not pass.

## Decision

and bounded receive-close grace, prove it without RF, then repeat this exact
RF shape once. Do not alter SR cadence, scheduler behavior, RF controls, or
traffic duration.
