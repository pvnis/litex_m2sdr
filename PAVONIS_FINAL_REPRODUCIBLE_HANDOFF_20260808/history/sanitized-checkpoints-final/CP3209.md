# Checkpoint 3209: SR-period 10 ms OTA throughput pass

## Verdict

PASS for the sustained throughput sensitivity, with a separate legacy
packaging warning. Both endpoints completed, the final receiver ACK arrived,
the formal analyzer passed, and the RF health gates were normal.

Lowering the UE SchedulingRequest period from 20 ms to 10 ms improves sustained
UL throughput by `12.42%` in the formal run. It does not improve DL.

## Frozen change

The only policy/config difference from CP3176 was:

```yaml
pucch:
  sr_period_ms: 10
```

The run retained baseline `min_k2=4`, 512-byte SR bootstrap, native SR/BSR,
10 MHz/52 PRB, single layer, MCS ceiling 9, FDD, and the same RF controls.

## Formal benchmark

| Direction | CP3176 baseline | CP3209 | Delta |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| DL | 2.983260 Mbit/s | 2.660295 Mbit/s | -10.83% |
| UL | 0.167066 Mbit/s | 0.187816 Mbit/s | +12.42% |

The UE receiver is authoritative for DL and the core receiver is authoritative
for UL. Both requested 45-second windows pass the analyzer duration gate.

CP3207's receiver-authoritative partial independently measured
`3.221643/0.201818 Mbit/s` DL/UL. The two 10 ms runs both improve UL versus
CP3176; their mean UL is `0.194817 Mbit/s`, `+16.61%`, while DL variation is
not directionally consistent.

## Scheduler evidence

- steady UL newTx rows: `6,524` versus CP3176 `5,929`;
- median positive newTx gap: `6` slots versus `2`;
- median inter-burst gap: `21` slots versus `27`;
- p90 inter-burst gap: `59` slots versus `60`;
- burst-size median/p90: `1/4`, unchanged class;
- all grants: `k2=4`;
- median TBS: `512 bytes`.

The movement comes from more frequent demand opportunities and more scheduled
new transmissions, not larger grants or shorter PDCCH-to-PUSCH delay.

## Radio health

- cell-search SNR: `14.595 dB`;
- PUSCH CRC OK: `78/128`;
- median CRC-OK PUSCH SINR: `12.709 dB`;
- median PUSCH TBS: `528 bytes`;
- Soapy TX timeout/downlink-late: `0/0`.

## Packaging warning

The legacy radio runner returned 1 only because its tar step read the gNB log
while that file was still changing. The role archives were nevertheless
created and fetched, and every benchmark endpoint/analyzer gate passed.
Standard cleanup then restored zero gNB/qcore processes and a free private
user-plane port.

This warning does not invalidate the receiver-authoritative throughput result.

## Decision

Accept `sr_period_ms=10` as a modest UL-throughput improvement, not as a DL
lever or a complete ceiling removal. Before final report closure, source-audit
the next supported lower period and gate it independently at no RF. Test it
only if standards/config support and ZMQ attach/session remain clean.
