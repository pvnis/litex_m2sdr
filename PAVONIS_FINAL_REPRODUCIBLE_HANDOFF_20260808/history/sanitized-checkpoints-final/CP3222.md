# Checkpoint 3222 - Coherent UL timing restores connected data

## Verdict

**VALID PASS FOR THE UL TIMING FIX; THROUGHPUT TARGET STILL OPEN.**

The CP3221 non-PRACH co-shift propagated into the live gNB while preserving
the CP3220 PRACH path:

- UE bladeRF guard: `56000` samples;
- gNB PRACH collection/timing compensation: `253336` samples;
- non-PRACH scheduler-error compensation: `16000` samples.

This restored the complete private-lab srsUE path: cell acquisition, SIB1,
Msg1, gNB RAR, UE RAR acceptance, connected PUSCH, and sustained
bidirectional TCP data all passed. The gNB decoded `81/128` observed PUSCH
blocks (`63.28%`) with median CRC-OK SINR `20.37 dB`. Every judged upper-PUSCH
task started after its shifted grid symbols had been written.

The result proves CP3220's floor-class Msg3 was caused by applying the new
`16000`-sample transmit shift to PRACH while leaving connected-UL collection
on the old timeline. It does not prove normal bidirectional bandwidth.

## Throughput

The dedicated benchmark ran sequentially for `45 s` per direction:

| Direction | Authoritative bytes | Sustained rate |
|---|---:|---:|
| Downlink | `14184728` | `2.521721 Mbit/s` |
| Uplink | `757304` | `0.134631 Mbit/s` |

These rates remain in the constrained prior srsUE OTA class. CP3176 measured
approximately `2.983/0.167 Mbit/s` DL/UL, while CP3214 proved the unchanged
10 MHz stack can carry `7.508669/33.379038 Mbit/s` over ZMQ. CP3222 is
therefore a restored RF timing baseline, not the capability ceiling.

## Remaining Loss Mode

The bounded bladeRF trace contains `264` high-energy writes:

- `226` returned status `0`;
- `38` returned `TIME_PAST`;
- late-write fraction: `14.39%`;
- final lead: `38249..54141` samples, median `46605.5`.

The late returns are concentrated at lower lead: there are no `TIME_PAST`
rows at or above `52000` samples in this run. All `264` high-energy rows still
show the existing guard and PRACH-offset path active. This observer is
bounded, but it establishes that the `56000`-sample configured guard does not
leave a stable `56000`-sample runtime margin during sustained connected load.

gNB downlink health remained clean at zero Soapy timeout and zero
downlink-late events. Cell SNR was `13.953 dB`; the low throughput is not an
attach or gross link-loss result.

## Decision

Preserve CP3222 as the new connected timing baseline. The next isolated
variable is UE transmit lead under sustained load:

1. keep PRACH and non-PRACH compensation coherent with any guard delta;
2. change no scheduler, MCS, bandwidth, gain, power, kernel, FPGA, or gateware
   setting;
3. require a lower `TIME_PAST` fraction and higher PUSCH CRC yield;
4. retain the change only if sustained uplink throughput also improves.

The remembered USB LiteX result was produced outside this campaign and is
treated only as qualitative precedent, not as a numeric baseline or proof.
