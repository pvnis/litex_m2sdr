## Source audit

The installed libbladeRF reports version `1.10.0-git-0bf98844`. Its installed
header states that:

- `BURST_START` establishes a burst;
- flags-zero writes append inside that burst;
- `TX_NOW` is valid with `BURST_START`;
- `BURST_END` flushes the current partial sync buffer.

CP3233's first failed ordinary write is call 2314, while the preceding PRACH is
call 2267. Therefore the hidden TXNOW transition and approximately 45 further
one-millisecond writes completed before the first two-second status-`-1`
failure. This is delayed sync-worker failure after reopen, not immediate
metadata rejection.
