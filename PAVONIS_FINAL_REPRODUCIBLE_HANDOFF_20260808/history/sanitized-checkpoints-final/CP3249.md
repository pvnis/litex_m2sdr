# CP3249 - Bounded queue removes worker death, not time-past

## Verdict

TRANSPORT FAIL; LADDER VOID.

The 12-buffer/8-transfer branch prevents the repeated sync-worker death seen in
CP3239, CP3245, and CP3247, but its queue is still too deep for every
40,000-sample PRACH timestamp update. Do not interpret zero gNB Msg1 detections
as detector or RF evidence.

## Evidence

Runtime propagation is exact: bounded queue on, restart-each and reconfigure
off, guarded continuation on, and the system libbladeRF selected.

The runtime profile reports 12 buffers, 8 transfers, and a modeled 24,576
sample queue. Seven true PRACH writes divide as follows:

- status 0: 4;
- `TIME_PAST`: 3;
- update-timestamp rows: 6;
- one first-PRACH restart inherited from the frozen wrapper.

The split is deterministic by available hardware lead:

- 40,000, 40,877, and 40,375 samples: all `TIME_PAST`;
- 50,698, 51,948, and 51,198 samples: all status 0.

All four armed continuations return status 0 at exactly +56,000 samples.
There are zero USB transfer timeouts, zero status-minus-one rows, and zero
invalid-status rows. This closes worker death for this run and shows that the
remaining error is queue/pipeline depth, not replacement-worker reliability.

The 24,576-sample software-ring estimate therefore omits roughly one additional
fixed USB/device pipeline component. Official libbladeRF permits
`num_transfers=4` and requires only `num_buffers > num_transfers`.

Downlink and gNB TX health remain clean: cell selection succeeds at 16.991 dB,
with zero Soapy TX timeouts and zero downlink-late events. The UE emits seven
Msg1 attempts, but the transport gate fails and the gNB records no accepted
PRACH, RAR, or PUSCH.

## Decision

Do not repeat CP3249 and do not increase static PRACH lead or collection
compensation. Change one variable: reduce the default-off bounded TX profile
to 5 buffers and 4 transfers. That is the minimum documented transfer class
with one producer buffer and reduces the modeled software queue from 24,576
to 10,240 samples. Preserve every RF, timing, detector, scheduler, and library
selection from CP3249 for the next control.

The inherited first-only restart is recorded as a separate remaining fixture;
it is not changed in the queue-depth discriminator.
