# CP3251 - Minimum queue exposes EOB/continuation feedback

## Verdict

TRANSPORT FAIL; LADDER VOID.

The 5/4 queue is shallow enough for a 40,000-sample in-burst timestamp update,
but each successful per-PRACH EOB plus guarded continuation shifts the next
occasion toward the hardware clock and recreates `TIME_PAST`.

## Evidence

Runtime reports 5 buffers, 4 transfers, and 10,240 modeled samples. There are
zero USB transfer timeouts, status-minus-one rows, or invalid rows. The first
restart and all three continuations succeed; each continuation has exactly
+56,000 samples of lead.

The seven PRACH rows are:

| Occasion | Update | Hardware lead | Status |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 1 | no, first restart | 40,000 | 0 |
| 2 | yes | 40,000 | 0 |
| 3 | yes | 14,770 | time-past |
| 4 | yes | 25,592 | time-past |
| 5 | yes | 36,541 | 0 |
| 6 | yes | 11,687 | time-past |
| 7 | yes | 22,509 | time-past |

After the successful occasion 2 EOB/continuation, lead drops 25,230 samples.
Failed updates do not close the burst and recover 10,822 to 10,949 samples per
occasion. The successful occasion 5 EOB/continuation repeats the same low-lead
state.

This is the descriptor-loop analogue on the UE side: ending and reopening the
burst changes presentation state enough to feed back into the next scheduled
timestamp. Queue depth alone cannot remove that feedback.

Downlink remains healthy at 13.043 dB, with zero gNB Soapy TX timeouts and zero
downlink-late events. Zero gNB PRACH accepts, RAR, or PUSCH remain void because
the transport gate fails.

## Decision

Do not repeat CP3251, reduce the queue further, or increase static PRACH lead.
Keep 5/4 and change only bounded in-burst PRACH metadata from
`UPDATE_TIMESTAMP|BURST_END` to `UPDATE_TIMESTAMP`. This is libbladeRF's
documented continuous-stream form: one burst start, repeated timestamp updates,
and an EOB only when the stream shuts down.

The first-only restart and its one continuation remain frozen for this next
discriminator. Later PRACH updates must neither end the burst nor arm a
continuation.
