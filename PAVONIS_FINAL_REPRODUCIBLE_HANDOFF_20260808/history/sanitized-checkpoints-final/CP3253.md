# CP3253 - Continuous update clears PRACH transport

## Verdict

TRANSPORT PASS; VALID PRACH-SUPPLY FAIL.

This is the first seven-occasion run in the current throughput branch with a
fully clean bladeRF PRACH transmit path. The remaining zero-detection result is
now a valid gNB detector-supply result, not a transport void.

## Evidence

Runtime reports 5 buffers, 4 transfers, and 10,240 modeled samples.

- PRACH writes: 7/7 status 0.
- First restart/EOB: one row, flags `0x3`.
- Continuous updates: six rows, flags `0x8`.
- All six updates have burst-open before and after.
- Guarded continuations: exactly one, status 0 at +56,000 samples.
- USB transfer timeouts: 0.
- Time-past, invalid, and status-minus-one rows: 0.

The six update-only occasions retain 34,681 to 40,000 samples of hardware lead
without EOB/continuation feedback. This validates the documented single-open-
burst timestamp-update mechanism and closes repeated sync-worker replacement
as necessary for steady PRACH transport.

Downlink and gNB TX health are clean: cell selection succeeds at 14.796 dB,
with zero Soapy TX timeouts and zero downlink-late events. The UE sends seven
Msg1 attempts and the gNB saves 16 strong-window captures, but records no
nonempty PRACH result, RAR, or PUSCH.

## Decision

Keep the continuous-update transport shape. Before spending another RF roll,
replay all 16 saved strong windows through the established CP1965 stage-1
policy harness. That determines whether the current candidate list misses
recoverable windows or whether the detector input itself remains below the
accepted class. Do not change RF, timing compensation, scheduler, or static
lead.

The one initial restart remains a separate fixture; it is not yet claimed
necessary.
