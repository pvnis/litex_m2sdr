# CP3254 - CP3253 strong windows are not Msg1

## Verdict

NO-RF DIAGNOSTIC PASS; SAVED-WINDOW REPLAY IS NOT A MSG1 VERDICT.

The established CP1965 stage-1 grid accepts none of CP3253's 16 saved strong
predemod windows. A cross-run wall-time/SFN join proves those files are not the
seven Msg1 collection windows, so their negative replay cannot be used to
expand or reject the live Msg1 candidate envelope.

## Evidence

The replay evaluated 11,920 variants across 16 windows. It found zero accepted
variants; the best per-window p0 ratios range from 0.102 to 0.211.

Each saved manifest row matches exactly one stage-1 trace row by SFN, slot, and
RMS. A linear wall-time fit over those 16 anchors then maps the seven UE
`PRACH-TX` events to stage-1 trace indices 452, 459, 466, 473, 480, 487, and
494. Their residuals are 7.4 to 49.4 ms, within half of the 161.2 ms trace-row
period.

The matched Msg1 windows have:

- RMS from -70.25 to -66.85 dB.
- Peak from -59.43 to -55.67 dB.
- Six of seven passing the stage-1 energy gate.
- Thirty evaluated candidates in those six rows.
- Zero selected candidates.

CP3253's capture was configured with an RMS floor of -65 dB and a peak floor
of -55 dB, with a 16-window cap. None of the seven matched Msg1 windows met
either capture threshold. The 16 files therefore represent other stronger
collection opportunities exactly as configured; this is not a wrong-buffer,
wrong-slot, or stage-1-energy-capture defect.

## Decision

Keep the CP3253 transport, timing, RF, and detector settings frozen and take
one fresh retry under the single-scatter rule. Continuous update-only PRACH
transport is now deterministic, while detector acceptance has historically
varied between otherwise identical runs.

If the retry is also a valid zero-accept run, do not take a third unchanged
roll. Add a bounded way to retain the actual below-floor Msg1 windows and run
the wide offline search on those exact samples.

The remembered USB LiteX throughput result remains external qualitative
precedent only; this campaign did not produce or verify its numeric result.
