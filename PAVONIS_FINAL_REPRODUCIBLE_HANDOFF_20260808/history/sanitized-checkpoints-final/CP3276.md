# CP3276 - Fresh BSR OTA: Two Supply Voids

Date: [date-redacted]

## Scope

CP3276 enabled the CP3275 default-off fresh-RLC short-BSR path and its capped
metadata trace on the otherwise frozen CP3273 ladder shape. The first run and
the standing single-void retry both completed healthy RF execution but failed
before any RAR, UL grant, PUSCH, BSR, session, or user-data traffic.

## Attempt Table

| Stamp | UE Msg1 | gNB PRACH accepts | Stage-1 selected class | Search time | RAR-window misses | RAR | BSR rows |
|---|---:|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| `[date-redacted]` | 7 | 6 | `shift=80, frac=+0.5, cfo=-6500` | 3.717-3.883 ms, 7 candidates | 6 | 0 | 0 |
| `[date-redacted]` | 7 | 6 | `shift=64, frac=-0.5, cfo=+15000` | 11.412-12.452 ms, 21 candidates | 6 | 0 | 0 |

Both hosts passed the realtime-state gate. Both runs had zero Soapy TX
timeouts and zero downlink-late events. Each gNB stop summary had only the
known 480-sample terminal partial-return deficit. SIB1 and seven UE Msg1
transmissions were present in each run.

The feature controls propagated correctly in both UE result sidecars:

```text
PAVONIS_SRSUE_BSR_REFRESH_BEFORE_CE=1
PAVONIS_SRSUE_BSR_REFRESH_TRACE=1
PAVONIS_SRSUE_BSR_REFRESH_TRACE_LIMIT=4096
```

No BSR trace rows were expected or emitted because neither run reached a UL
grant. These runs therefore contain no evidence for or against the fresh-BSR
semantics or throughput hypothesis.

## Finding

The repeatable blocker was detector search-order latency. The gNB accepted six
preambles in each run, but each accepted RACH indication reached scheduling at
or just beyond the 40-slot RAR response window. The first run evaluated seven
candidates per accepted window; the retry evaluated 21. The two accepted
recovery classes already exist in the bounded stage-1 list but are late enough
to make the RAR result stale.

The next experiment should move these two observed classes to the front of the
same stop-on-accept list. That changes evaluation order, not candidate content,
acceptance policy, timing compensation, RAR semantics, BSR behavior, or RF
configuration.

## Verdict

**VOID FOR FRESH-BSR AND THROUGHPUT JUDGMENT.**

Two consecutive runs reached Msg1 and gNB PRACH acceptance but missed every
RAR window due to bounded-search latency. Do not spend a third unchanged RF
retry. Preserve the CP3275 candidate UE binary and rerun only after a no-RF
candidate-order gate.
