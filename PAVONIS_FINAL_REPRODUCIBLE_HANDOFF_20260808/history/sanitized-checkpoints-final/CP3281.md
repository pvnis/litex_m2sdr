# CP3281 - Short Bidirectional Fresh-BSR No-RF Gate

Date: [date-redacted]

## Purpose

CP3280 proved fresh-BSR semantics but attached too late for its 45-second DL
and 45-second UL phases to fit inside the fixed 240-second UE lifetime. CP3281
creates a new diagnostic runner with 10 seconds per direction. It is intended
to answer whether refreshed BSR materially changes uplink throughput before
the formal harness is extended.

## No-RF Proof

The CP3280 and CP3282 candidate lists are byte-identical and contain 21
transforms. After normalizing checkpoint/artifact identifiers and the private
benchmark port, a bounded script diff contains exactly two changes:

```text
--duration 45
--duration 10
```

Those are the validate-only and live controller invocations. The gNB/UE
binaries, RF parameters, timing compensation, stage-1 order, fresh-BSR flags,
transport policy, MCS, capture controls, scheduler, and 240-second radio
lifetime are unchanged.

The new runner passes `bash -n` and controller validate-only. With approval
absent, its RF gate exits 2.

## Verdict

**PASS: one CP3282 10-second-per-direction diagnostic is unlocked.**

This is a measurement-window change, not a throughput optimization. A
complete CP3282 result may be compared directionally with prior runs, but it
does not replace the formal 45-second sustained benchmark.
