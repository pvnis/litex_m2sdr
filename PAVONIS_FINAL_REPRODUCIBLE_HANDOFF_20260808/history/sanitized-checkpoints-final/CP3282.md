The scheduler newTx trace cap did not cover the late user-data interval, so
this checkpoint does not claim a complete scheduler-side demand distribution.

## Decision

Fresh BSR is retained for one formal measurement. Extend only the gNB/UE and
benchmark-server lifetimes; restore 45 seconds per direction and keep every
radio, PHY, timing, scheduler, transport, BSR, gain, and MCS control unchanged.
If the formal UL result is not materially above CP3273, retire this patch as a
semantic correction without practical throughput payoff.
