# Checkpoint 3286: Parallel TCP Access-Supply Void

## Verdict

**VOID for the four-stream throughput hypothesis.** The frozen downlink and
Msg1 supply were valid, but accepted PRACH results reached the scheduler after
the RAR response window. No endpoint traffic ran, so this checkpoint provides
no throughput comparison.

This is not a radio-health failure. Cell selection passed at 14.503 dB SNR,
the UE transmitted seven PRACHs, the gNB selected six candidates, and Soapy TX
timeout/downlink-late counts were both zero.

## Evidence

- All six selected PRACH rows used the same transform:
  `shift=-224`, `frac=-0.5`, `cfo=+60000 Hz`.
- The transform was candidate 15 including the baseline search.
- Search time was 7,574-8,703 us.
- The six accepted occasions were at SFN/slot 113/4 through 673/4.
- Every scheduler warning reports that the corresponding four-slot RAR window
  had already closed. Allocation failure counters for PDCCH, PDSCH, and PUSCH
  were all zero.
- The gNB allocated and encoded zero RARs; the UE accepted zero RARs and
  produced no Msg3/PUSCH.
- Four-stream server and client results were therefore unavailable.

The failure is search-order latency, not a negative result for parallel TCP.
The next retry may move this already-proven transform to the first override
position while preserving the candidate multiset, radio parameters, scheduler
settings, fresh-BSR-off state, and 45-second endpoint duration.
