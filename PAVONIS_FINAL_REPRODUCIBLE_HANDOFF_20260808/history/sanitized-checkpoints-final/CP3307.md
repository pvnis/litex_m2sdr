## Evidence

The 64-window manifest has six recurring elevated windows at indices
`14,21,27,34,41,48`. Their RMS range is `-59.460..-58.612 dB`; the next
strongest window is `-64.783 dB`. This clean level and cadence separation
prevents idle-window multiple-search hits from being mistaken for UE bursts.

Baseline replay accepts `0/6`. The current CP3306 policy also accepts `0/6`;
its best p0 ratios are `0.755..0.805`, but all maxima land at delay `129` and
fail the delay gate. The wider source-compatible timing/CFO replay accepts all
six.

The proposed transform accepts `6/6`, has minimum/mean p0 ratio
`2.168/2.2765`, and accepts `0/58` non-burst windows. On historical controls,
it rejects the CP3273 successful class (`p0=0.179`) and accepts both CP3302
successful windows (`p0=18.247..20.955`). Existing fallback coverage is
therefore preserved.
