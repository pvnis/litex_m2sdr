## Evidence

The burst indices are `11,18,25,32,39,46`, with RMS
`-59.458..-58.584 dB`. The next strongest window is `-63.232 dB`.

Baseline and current-policy replay accept `0/6`. Current-policy maxima are
strong (`p0=3.703..3.900`) but all land at delay `130`, outside the gate. The
wide replay recovers all six. The proposed transform has minimum/mean p0 ratio
`4.261/4.3795`, accepts `6/6` bursts, and accepts `0/58` non-bursts.

The transform rejects every tested established control: CP3273, both CP3302
windows, all CP3306 windows, and all three CP3309 accepted occasions. It will
therefore not preempt those known classes.

## Cost and Harness

CP3311-class bursts stop after one evaluation. Authoritative offline wall times
are `0.10 s` baseline, `35.31 s` current policy, and `435.90 s` wide analysis.
The wide grid is not proposed for live use.

An attempted eight-process replay was rejected: the harness detector trace
uses one fixed temporary path, so concurrent readers mixed telemetry. The
authoritative baseline, current-policy, and wide outputs were rerun
sequentially. Known-invalid parallel JSONL files were removed before packaging.
