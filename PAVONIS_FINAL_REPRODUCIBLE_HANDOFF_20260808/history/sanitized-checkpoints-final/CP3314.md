## Sustained Traffic

The benchmark preconnects four sockets per direction in `0.841 s`, then runs
the two directions sequentially for 45 seconds each.

- Downlink: `17,953,752 B / 45.0007 s = 3.191727 Mbit/s` at the UE receiver.
- Uplink: `2,425,400 B / 45.0001 s = 0.431181 Mbit/s` at the core receiver.

Duration and stream-count gates pass, both endpoints complete, and all four
uplink receiver acknowledgements return.

This is 15.9% above CP3273's `0.371974 Mbit/s` UL and 2.58 times CP3176's
`0.167066 Mbit/s`, but only 1.29% of CP3214's `33.379 Mbit/s` ZMQ uplink.
The remaining OTA gap is about 77.4 times.

## Interpretation

Preconnection resolves the post-downlink TCP connection transition and proves
real bidirectional transport. It does not resolve the underlying uplink
capacity limit: modal TBS remains `544 B`, and the valid sustained rate is
still sub-Mbit/s despite healthy median accepted SINR.
