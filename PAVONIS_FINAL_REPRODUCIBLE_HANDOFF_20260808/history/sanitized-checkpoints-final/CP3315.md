# CP3315 - CP3314 Scheduler, BSR, and PHY Join

Date: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS, no RF. CP3314 proves complete bidirectional transport, but its uplink is
still scheduler-supply-limited and wastes a material share of its larger
grants on an unreliable 64QAM regime.

## Evidence

The established CP3274 parser selects the densest 45-second uplink interval
from CP3314's binary-safe gNB mirror. OCUDU schedules `0.678734 Mbit/s` of
new-transmission payload while the receiver-authoritative endpoint obtains
`0.431181 Mbit/s`. The scheduler emits 4,705 new-transmission grants with
median TBS 512 bytes, mean TBS 811.45 bytes, and mean positive gap 9.55 slots.
All 374 short BSR rows report zero.

This is the same demand-starved shape as CP3273: CP3314 raises scheduled gross
rate by 21.44% and endpoint rate by 15.92%, but does not change the zero-BSR
state or modal 512-byte grant. CP3214's unchanged 10 MHz ZMQ control instead
has median TBS 4,612 bytes, 49,729/49,729 PUSCH CRC OK, and 33.379 Mbit/s
receiver-authoritative uplink.

The CP3314 PHY split is actionable. QPSK is 3,258/3,269 CRC OK and 16QAM is
692/770 CRC OK, while 64QAM is only 509/1,506 CRC OK. The common 4,612-byte
64QAM group is only 28/101 CRC OK. The active YAML sets
`pdsch.max_ue_mcs: 9` but has no PUSCH cap, leaving OCUDU's uplink maximum at
28. A PUSCH-only MCS-16 cap is therefore the next isolated sensitivity: it is
not the closed CP3170 downlink-MCS test, CP3201 SR-floor test, CP3212 cadence
test, or CP3284 fresh-BSR test.

## Decision

Keep CP3314 byte-for-byte as the control. No-RF gate a copied runner/config
that changes only `pusch.max_ue_mcs` from its default 28 to 16. Require config
and ZMQ validation before one bounded preconnected OTA run. If it does not
improve delivered uplink or useful scheduled bytes, revert it and continue
with a demand-saturation discriminator rather than stacking changes.
