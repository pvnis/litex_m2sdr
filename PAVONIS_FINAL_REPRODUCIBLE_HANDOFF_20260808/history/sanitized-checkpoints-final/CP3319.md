# CP3319 - PUSCH MCS16 Restores Balanced Bidirectional OTA

Date: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS. PUSCH maximum MCS16 is active and restores normal balanced
bidirectional throughput over the LiteX-M2SDR/bladeRF OTA path.

## Propagation

All three predeclared gates pass:

- The gNB log contains one CP3318 activation marker.
- The packaged sibling full config contains exactly one PUSCH block with
  `max_ue_mcs: 16`.
- The full PUSCH parse contains zero 64QAM rows.

The controller exits rc0, including the radio runner and all endpoint,
analysis, cleanup, duration, stream, and acknowledgement gates.

## Result

The cell is healthy at `16.743 dB`; Soapy TX timeout and downlink-late counts
are both zero. Four preconnected streams run sequentially for 45 seconds per
direction. Receiver authorities report:

- Downlink: `3.193046 Mbit/s`
- Uplink: `3.679060 Mbit/s`

The densest 45-second uplink interval schedules `4.017036 Mbit/s` of new
transmissions. It contains 13,015 newTx grants with median TBS 1,985 bytes and
median positive gap two slots. Full PUSCH is 13,005/13,183 CRC OK (98.65%);
16QAM is 10,922/11,100 CRC OK and QPSK is 2,083/2,083.

Versus CP3314, delivered uplink rises 8.53 times and scheduled gross rises
5.92 times. Delivered uplink is 22.02 times CP3176's original wireless
baseline. It remains 11.02% of CP3214's ideal ZMQ uplink, an honest remaining
9.07-times hardware-path gap, but it is no longer a reduced one-direction
lab proof: uplink now exceeds downlink on the same sustained test.

## Interpretation

The default MCS28 uplink repeatedly entered a 64QAM regime that decoded at
only about one third overall in CP3314. Capping at MCS16 removes those failed
large blocks, raises full-window CRC yield from 80.41% to 98.65%, and allows
the scheduler/TCP feedback loop to maintain larger 16QAM grants at much
shorter gaps. Reliability, not nominal constellation ceiling, was the
throughput lever.

Short-BSR rows remain zero, so this does not claim the earlier BSR reporting
issue is fixed. The result shows that reliable medium grants are sufficient
to restore balanced throughput despite that telemetry.

## Decision

Repeat this exact shape once with a fresh stamp. A second valid balanced result
promotes PUSCH MCS16 into the working POC profile; a divergent result keeps it
as a promising single-run sensitivity and reopens the supply-variation
question.
