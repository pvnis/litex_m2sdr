# CP3341: zero-guard 5 ms gNB-only gate

## Verdict

**VALID NEGATIVE.** Removing the guard does not make 5 ms sustainable.
CP3341 records 18 true pre-write deadline misses and five RF-late events.
Zero guard is therefore diagnostic only, not a working policy.

The failure is bounded slot-level jitter: deadlines are missed by 2–208 us,
not by a large producer stall or timestamp discontinuity.

## Trace Finding

```text
entry_gap_us_min_mean_max=33_1328.8_1929
tx_to_break_us_min_mean_max=8_619.3_1500
raw_lead_us_min_mean_max=-208_-63.5_-2
md_delta_expected_11520=18/18
```

Breaks form three clusters: 2, 4 and 12 events. Every metadata delta remains
exactly one 11,520-sample slot. The largest entry gap is 1.929 ms and the
largest in-call duration is 1.500 ms. This is cumulative slot-cadence jitter,
not a multi-millisecond halt.

All five `Real-time failure in RF: late` events occur in the final 12-break
cluster. Guard zero therefore lets the downstream timed-TX path approach or
cross the hardware boundary and confirms that a nonzero safety margin remains
necessary.

## Decision

Do not use or retry 5 ms/guard-zero. The next isolated bracket is 6 ms lead
with guard zero and all other CP3341 controls unchanged. The measured worst
miss is 208 us, so an added 1 ms is a bounded, evidence-derived step.

If 6 ms/zero is clean, add a 0.5 ms guard at the same lead before OTA. If it
still misses, its trace will determine the next lead bracket.
