## Decision

Do not move directly to OTA with guard zero. Preserve 6 ms lead and add a
0.5 ms guard in one bounded gNB-only run. That restores a real safety margin
while retaining 0.5 ms above the CP3341 worst-case miss after the 1 ms lead
increase.

If 6 ms/0.5 ms remains clean, promote it to one MCS16 OTA run with retimed
PRACH/non-PRACH constants derived from the existing lead-delta rule.
