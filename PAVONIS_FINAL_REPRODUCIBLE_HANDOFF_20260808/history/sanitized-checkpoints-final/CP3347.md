# CP3347: 6 ms MCS16 pre-UE gate void

## Verdict

**VOID.** This run does not test acquisition, uplink timing, latency or
throughput. The inherited stage wrapper stopped before starting srsUE because
its expected deadline guard remained [date-redacted] us while the gNB correctly read back
the CP3345 policy of 500 us.

## Evidence

The gNB starts and its device readback proves:

```text
tx_all_timed_chunks=1
tx_deadline_write_timeout_enabled=1
tx_deadline_write_guard_us=500
tx_deadline_write_max_timeout_us=50000
hardware_time_ok=1
env_M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS=6000000
```

The inherited stage manifest instead records:

```text
PAVONIS_SOAPY_TX_DEADLINE_WRITE_GUARD_US_OVERRIDE=[date-redacted]
PAVONIS_NON_PRACH_UL_RX_OFFSET_EDGE_CALIBRATION_SAMPLES=-237332
```

The sequence then reports:

```text
start_gnb_nativecontinuous_stage6 rc=0
gate_nativecontinuous_tx_readback rc=1
SKIP_UE gate_nativecontinuous_tx_readback_failed
UE_STARTED=0
```

No UE log, cell-search result, PUSCH result or traffic endpoint exists. The
execution summary's Stage 5 label is therefore a generic wrapper label, not a
radio acquisition failure.

## Cause

The outer CP3347 controller exports the new values, but two wrappers in the
frozen chain replace them unconditionally:

- `cp2302_run_pucch_grid_candidate_supply.sh` exports edge calibration
  `-237332`.
- `cp2188_run_coherent_20ms_ul_retime_ladder.sh` exports deadline guard
  `[date-redacted]`.

The CP3346 gNB launcher reasserts 500 us and edge calibration `-76052`
immediately before the gNB binary, so the device side is correct. The stage
manifest and pre-UE gate remain stale, producing an expected-versus-observed
false negative.

## Decision

Do not alter the frozen chain or CP3121 baseline. Add an isolated, append-only
stage-expectation shim that reasserts 500 us and `-76052` when the final
`cp1807` shell starts. No-RF prove the child-shell behavior, manifest
expectations and closed-RF gate before retrying with a fresh stamp.

The USB LiteX throughput recollection remains external operator context only
and has no bearing on this void.
