## Mechanism

`cp3348_stage_expectations_env.sh`. Each non-interactive local wrapper shell
sources that file. This reasserts:

```text
PAVONIS_SOAPY_TX_DEADLINE_WRITE_GUARD_US_OVERRIDE=500
PAVONIS_NON_PRACH_UL_RX_OFFSET_EDGE_CALIBRATION_SAMPLES=-76052
```

legacy CP2302 and CP2188 have made their hard-coded assignments. CP1807 then
uses these values for its manifest, remote environment and pre-UE readback
gate. The CP3346 gNB launcher independently retains the same values.
