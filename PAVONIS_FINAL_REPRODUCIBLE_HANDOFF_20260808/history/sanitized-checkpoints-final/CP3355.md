# CP3355: 6 ms / 200 us Long-Gate Preparation

## Verdict

**PASS (no RF).** The next experiment changes only the deadline reservation
from 500 us to 200 us and extends the transmit-only observation window to 420
seconds. The 6 ms lead, MCS16 policy, PRACH and non-PRACH timing compensation,
stage-1 candidate set, RF settings, candidate gNB, and frozen Stage 6 chain
remain pinned.

CP3354 measured 42 abandoned writes with positive raw hardware lead of
244--500 us. A 200 us reservation therefore directly tests whether those
buffers remain writable while retaining a bounded pre-deadline margin. This is
not an OTA or throughput result.

- The controlled launcher delta against CP3351 consists of fresh checkpoint
