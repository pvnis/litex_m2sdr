# CP3356: 6 ms / 200 us Long gNB-Only Gate

## Verdict

**FAIL. Do not promote this policy to OTA.** The bounded transmit-only run
completed its 420-second pre-UE dwell and read back the intended 6 ms lead and
200 us deadline guard, but the final TX accounting contains 37 pre-write
deadline breaks, 263742 abandoned stream samples, and 37070 unwritten data
samples. The UE was never started.

This closes the short-gate scatter problem from CP3345/CP3352: 60--120 seconds
was not a sufficient continuity qualification.

## Evidence

The stop summary reports:

- requested / returned: 4838104058 / 4838066988 samples;
- deadline breaks / actual deadline write timeouts: 37 / 0;
- abandoned samples / stream deficit / data-write deficit:
  263742 / 263742 / 37070;
- partial returns / generic Soapy timeout / downlink-late / errors:
  0 / 0 / 0 / 0;
- release-late trace rows: 16; worker late count: 17.

The bounded event trace contains exactly 37 rows. Every row preserves the
expected 11520-sample metadata progression, rejecting timestamp jumps. Entry
gaps are 102--[date-redacted] us and transmit-to-break times are 8--1927 us. Ten events
break before any sample is written.

Raw hardware lead at the break is -99--195 us, mean 126.2 us:

- 35 rows remain guard-only;
- two rows are genuinely late even with no guard;
- applying the 200 us reservation produces -299---5 us guarded lead.

This rejects the previously queued 6.5 ms / 500 us fallback without another RF
run. Relative to the measured rows, adding 500 us of lead and adding 300 us of
guard leaves a projected guarded range of -99--195 us, so the same two
negative events remain. At 6.5 ms / 200 us the projected range is 201--495 us;
at 7 ms / 500 us it is 401--695 us.

The stop helper passes, and the independent postcheck finds no gNB process and
UDP 2152 free. Wrapper rc1 reflects the failed pre-UE health and authoritative
stop gates, not a UE result.

## Decision

No-RF gate a 6.5 ms / 200 us policy with PRACH compensation 197016 samples
and non-PRACH edge calibration -81812 samples, then repeat the full
420-second gNB-only continuity test. If it fails, use the more conservative
7 ms / 500 us point. No OTA throughput result is valid until one of those
long gates returns every requested sample.
