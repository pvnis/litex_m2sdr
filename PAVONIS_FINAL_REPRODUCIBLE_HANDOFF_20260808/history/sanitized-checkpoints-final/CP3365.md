## Verdict

**PASS (no RF).** CP3366 reuses the exact CP3354 full four-stream MCS16
preconnected traffic controller. It replaces the failed 6 ms runtime policy
with CP3364's qualified 8.5 ms/500 us gNB-warning/Soapy-INFO policy and

edge calibration to -104,852 samples.

No traffic, UE, radio, stage-1, scheduler, MCS, gain, or attenuation control
changes.

  stage-expectation shim, coherent timing constants, and fresh checkpoint IDs.
- Shell syntax and the controller's validate-only path pass.
- Static semantic counts prove exactly one 8.5 ms lead, one 500 us guard, one
  -104,852 edge calibration, both expected 220,056 PRACH rows, and the exact
  launcher/hash/expectation paths.
- Remote validation proves the deployed launcher hash and readback:
  8.5 ms lead, 500 us guard, PRACH 220,056, edge -104,852, MCS16, gNB warning,
  and the pinned gNB binary.
- The RF gate defaults closed with exit code 2.
