# CP3366: Log-Warning MCS16 Pre-UE TX Failure

## Verdict

**FAIL: TX continuity before UE startup.** This is not a cell-acquisition or
throughput result. The 60-second pre-UE summary gate finds four deadline
breaks and explicitly skips the UE. The generic downstream
`Stage 5/UE acquisition` label is therefore misleading.

At 8.5 ms lead and 500 us guard, 759,300,904 samples are requested and
759,296,336 return. The run abandons 32,794 stream samples, records a 4,568
data-sample deficit, four generic Soapy timeouts, and one partial return.
No throughput number is valid.

## Evidence

The four break rows form one cascade at logical 44,673--44,677. Raw lead
remains positive at 319--445 us in every row; the 500 us reservation makes
guarded lead -181 to -55 us. Per-row abandonment sums exactly to the stop
summary's 32,794 samples.

The UE has not started, and application PHY INFO logging remains absent. This
means CP3364's clean 420-second result was a favorable roll: changing gNB
application logging to warning materially reduced one pressure source but did
not eliminate the periodic continuity failure.

The remaining Soapy instrumentation is still high-rate. In this 66-second
run the gNB log writes 742 worker and 742 release period rows. The driver
source independently reads `M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_WORKER_SUMMARY_PERIOD` and
`M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_RELEASE_SUMMARY_PERIOD` once at worker startup; both default
to 1,000 packets. At the observed packet size/rate this is about one summary
period every 88.7 ms, and prior 22.5--23.0 second roots lie near harmonics of
that output rhythm.

## Decision

Do not retry CP3366 unchanged. Keep 8.5 ms/500 us and every OTA/timing control
fixed. In an isolated launcher, raise both M2SDR worker and release summary
periods from 1,000 to 10,000 packets, retaining periodic plus stop telemetry.
Prove the env/read-once path without RF, then require another clean 420-second
gNB-only gate before returning to OTA.
