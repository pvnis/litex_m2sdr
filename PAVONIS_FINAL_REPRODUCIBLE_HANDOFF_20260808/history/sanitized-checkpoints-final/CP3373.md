- The first real PRACH entered with an already-open transmit burst, but the

  (`9.05546875 ms`) displacement.
- A later PRACH used the persistent coherent-guard bias plus timestamp update:

  (`10.658333 ms`) displacement.
- Connected PUSCH subsequently entered the radio API as timed, but reached the
  bladeRF metadata layer with timestamp `0` and flags `0`. It therefore
  continued untimed inside the PRACH-shifted open burst.

hardware-only scheduler-to-PUSCH latency. Moving result attribution cannot
repair samples that physically arrive about 10 ms late.
