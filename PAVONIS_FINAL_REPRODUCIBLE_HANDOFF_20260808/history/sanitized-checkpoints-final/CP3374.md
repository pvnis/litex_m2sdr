## Verdict

VOID for the intended PRACH collector timing. The UE-side native timeline was
fully active, but the live gNB still collected with the old `220056`-sample
timing compensation instead of the intended `104856`. This run does not reject
the CP3373 mechanism or timing model.

The run is nevertheless valid evidence that native UE continuation works
mechanically: cell acquisition and SIB1 passed, all seven PRACH transmissions
used ordinary untimed `11520`-sample continuation writes, and none used the
legacy restart, guard bias, timestamp offset, burst-only, or continuation
reopen paths.

zero. Every PRACH sidecar records timestamp `0`, flags `0`, no restart, no
guard application, and no timestamp-offset application.

The propagation contradiction is direct:

- outer wrapper/controller and checkpoint manifest: `104856`;
- every live strong-window manifest row:
  `collection_delay_samples=220056` and
  `timing_compensation_samples=220056`;
- mismatch: exactly `115200` samples, the term CP3373 removed.

The qualified CP3367 gNB launcher hard-codes and re-exports
`PAVONIS_PRACH_TIMING_COMPENSATION_SAMPLES=220056` after the outer controller
has validated its own value. It likewise hard-codes the old non-PRACH static
and scheduler-error values. The next run therefore needs a corrected isolated
launcher, not another collector estimate.

TX stop accounting also contains two deadline-guard breaks and `8732` unique
stream-deficit samples over the full no-access lifetime. This independently
prevents a throughput verdict, although it does not explain the exact
`115200`-sample PRACH configuration mismatch.
