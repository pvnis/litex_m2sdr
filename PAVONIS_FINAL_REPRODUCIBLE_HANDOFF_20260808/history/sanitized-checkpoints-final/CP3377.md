# CP3377 - Native PRACH residual no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS.** Seven CP3376 UE waveform / M2SDR receive-window pairs identify a
deterministic residual placement error. The median PRACH start is 7,092 samples
before the collection window, with only one sample of span across all seven
events. The derived collector delay is 97,764 samples.

No RF, source rebuild, protocol change, threshold change, or non-PRACH timing
change was used.

## Evidence

The analyzer applies a bounded -8 to +8 kHz CFO sweep in 250 Hz steps and a
-10,000 to +10,000 sample lag sweep. It computes normalized complex
correlation over each partial overlap, requiring at least 2,048 samples.
Action-window indices 8, 15, 22, 29, 36, 43, and 50 are the seven CP3376
slot-4 windows joined to the seven UE PRACH events.

| 2 | -7092 | 4428 | 4250 | 0.933544 | 97764 |
| 3 | -7093 | 4427 | 4250 | 0.930333 | 97763 |
| 4 | -7092 | 4428 | 4250 | 0.929108 | 97764 |

| 6 | -7093 | 4427 | 4250 | 0.932779 | 97763 |
| 7 | -7092 | 4428 | 4250 | 0.932613 | 97764 |

The lag sign and CFO implementation pass a deterministic synthetic control,
and regenerating the real-data result is byte-identical. Because the CP3376
collector was 104,856 samples and negative lag means the waveform began before
the window, the correction is:

`104856 + (-7092) = 97764 samples`
