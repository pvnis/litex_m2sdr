## Scope

The event-only trace marks eight post-DCI boundaries:

1. CRC-OK RA DCI leaves PDCCH search;
2. converted PDSCH grant is stored;
3. pending PDSCH grant is retrieved;
4. RA PDSCH grant reaches the decoder;
5. MAC receives the DL grant;
6. MAC returns the PDSCH action;
7. PDSCH decode completes;
8. decoded TB reaches MAC dispatch.

These calls occur only on accepted RA DCI / RAR processing. They do not scan
samples, poll logs, or write per sample. The deployed UE binary contains all
eight markers and retains hash
`3b86d1f57436a0c31c0af2b4cb68cb4bb89af147a26e2e9605d8485bb73b6eef`.
