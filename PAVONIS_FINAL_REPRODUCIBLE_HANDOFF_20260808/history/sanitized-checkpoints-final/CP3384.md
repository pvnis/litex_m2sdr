# CP3384 - RAR and Msg3 preparation pass; PUSCH CRC wall

## Verdict

**VALID PARTIAL.** The UE RAR path works end to end through Msg3 preparation.
All seven traced RAR callbacks execute in the expected RA state, unpack,
match, apply the UL grant and timing advance, and complete Msg3 preparation.
The first failure is now the non-PRACH uplink receive path: the UE transmits,
the gNB opens matching eleven-byte PUSCH opportunities, and all 65 decode
attempts are CRC KO.

## RAR And UE Evidence

- Cell/SIB1 pass at `14.270 dB`; CFO is `-3940.150 Hz`.
- Seven PRACH transmissions produce seven stage-1 selections and thirteen
  gNB RAR allocations.
- Seven immediate paths each contain task enqueue, task execute, expected RA
  state, successful unpack, sub-PDU match, RAR acceptance, grant return code
  zero, timing advance, and Msg3 preparation.
- Six accepted grants carry zero timing advance and one carries a nonzero
  timing advance.
- The bounded bladeRF dumps contain three complete repeated groups of one
  PRACH waveform followed by four high-energy 1 ms uplink waveforms. In the
  first group those uplink slots begin about 20.936, 38.936, 56.936, and
  74.936 ms after PRACH.

The older summary fields still report zero Msg3/PUSCH markers because those
markers use the same signal-termination-sensitive buffered trace identified
in CP3382. The immediate path and waveform evidence supersede those counters.

## gNB PUSCH Evidence

The gNB records 65 eleven-byte PUSCH results, all CRC KO:

- SINR range `-32.861 .. -11.700 dB`, mean `-20.881 dB`;
- reported time alignment `-2.554 .. +2.170 us`, mean `-0.357 us`;
- EPRE range `-70.767 .. -44.754 dB`, mean `-50.493 dB`.

The repeated scheduled slots and the UE's high-energy slot waveforms prove
that this is not a missing RAR callback, empty Msg3 buffer, or absent UE
transmission. The still-active non-PRACH sample shift/CFO presentation is the
next discriminator.

## Health

Downlink acquisition and every RAR path pass, with zero downlink-late,
partial-return, or hard-error lines. The final TX summary has eight deadline
breaks, `7,432` returned-sample deficit, and `60,478` stream deficit. This is
recorded as a co-suspect for retransmission quality, but it does not explain
the independently received low-SINR uplink waveforms.
