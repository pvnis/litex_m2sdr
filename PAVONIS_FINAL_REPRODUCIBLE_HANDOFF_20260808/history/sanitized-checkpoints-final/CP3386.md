# CP3386 - Msg3 is measured 7092 samples late in the shifted collector

## Verdict

**VALID MEASUREMENT PARTIAL.** The gNB is receiving the correct Msg3 waveform,
but the shifted non-PRACH path presents it about 7092 samples late relative to
the scheduled PUSCH slot. Three independent UE attempts produce the same
desired dynamic offset within one sample. This names a single measured
calibration change for the next run; no RF, gain, scheduler, threshold, or
source change is justified.

## Radio And Capture

- Cell/SIB1 passes at `13.874 dB`; cell-search CFO is `-3917.335 Hz`.
- Seven PRACH transmissions produce seven stage-1 selections and ten gNB RAR
  allocations.
- Seven UE paths reach accepted RAR, grant/TA application, and Msg3
  preparation.
- The gNB records 50 full-slot, eleven-byte PUSCH results, all CRC KO.
- Capture propagation is exact: 128 generic raw symbols, 112 shifted target
  symbols across eight targets, 256 generic metadata rows, and 16 UE SC16
  waveforms.

The first three shifted targets are floor-class because they precede the live
UE attempts. Targets 3 through 7 contain uplink energy. Targets 3, 4, and 5
pair uniquely with the first Msg3 waveform from three separate UE attempts.

## Sample-Domain Result

Whole-slot circular correlation is `0.5140 .. 0.5409`. More importantly, each
shifted target contains only the tail of the transmitted slot:

| Target | Current dynamic offset | Captured tail | Source lateness | Tail correlation | Residual CFO |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| 3 | `-104430` | `4427` samples | `7093` samples | `0.87677` | `0 Hz` |
| 4 | `-104424` | `4432` samples | `7088` samples | `0.87649` | `+50 Hz` |
| 5 | `-104432` | `4424` samples | `7096` samples | `0.88029` | `+50 Hz` |

The active prefix is followed by floor noise, so the circular lag is not a
positive 4428-sample arrival delay. It means the collector starts 7088–7096
samples too late and sees only the end of Msg3.

Adding each measured lateness to its active dynamic offset gives
`-97337`, `-97336`, and `-97336` samples. Expressed through the existing edge
calibration, the three estimates are `-97759`, `-97764`, and `-97756`.
The next isolated value is therefore `-97760`, an 8-sample-aligned midpoint.
It predicts residual placement of `-4 .. +4` samples, well within the cyclic
prefix. The `7092`-sample correction also independently matches the CP3377
PRACH waveform displacement that moved collection from 104856 to 97764.
