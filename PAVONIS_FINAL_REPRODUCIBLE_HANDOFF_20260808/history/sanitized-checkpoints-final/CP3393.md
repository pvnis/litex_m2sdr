# CP3393 - Corrected MCS16 attach and throughput reproduce

## Verdict

**VALID ATTACH/DATA; TRAFFIC WINDOW CLEAN; POST-TRAFFIC STRICT-HEALTH
PARTIAL.** The corrected M2SDR timeline reproduces a full attach and sustained
bidirectional session. PUSCH yield and uplink throughput closely match CP3390.
The only strict-gate failure is one deadline break first visible well after
the measured traffic interval.

## Reproducibility

- Live edge calibration is `-97760`; dynamic offset is `-97338`.
- One PRACH/stage-1/RAR path attaches.
- PUSCH trace: 127/128 CRC OK (`99.22%`), median SINR `21.155 dB`, median TBS
  `544 B`.
- Sustained traffic passes at `6.473 Mbit/s` downlink and
  `12.809 Mbit/s` uplink.
- The two valid corrected-timeline runs now bound uplink at
  `12.614 .. 12.809 Mbit/s` with `99.22 .. 100%` observed PUSCH CRC yield.

Relative to CP3324 ZMQ MCS16, CP3393 reaches `85.17%` downlink and `82.77%`
uplink. CP3390 reached `92.89%` and `81.51%`; the corrected hardware path is
therefore reproducibly in the same performance class, with downlink more
variable than uplink.

## Health Placement

The first PRACH occurs at TX-stream `+74.711 s`. The client preconnect takes
`0.404 s`, followed by two 45-second directions, placing traffic completion
near stream `+165.2 s`. Periodic TX summaries remain at zero breaks through
`+230 s`; the single break first appears in the `+240 s` summary.

The measured traffic window is therefore clean. Final strict health still
fails with one break, 1,022 returned-sample deficit and 11,520 stream deficit,
all during the later idle wait. There are zero partial returns and hard
errors. The outer rc remains honestly nonzero, but this post-traffic event
does not contaminate the sustained throughput bytes.

## Next Step

Promote the corrected timing into an isolated MCS28 launcher and no-RF gate.
The MCS16 result is reproducible enough to advance: further bare MCS16 repeats
would measure acquisition/TX scatter rather than answer a new throughput
question. Preserve the MCS16 launcher and artifacts unchanged.
