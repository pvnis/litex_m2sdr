# CP3396 - Corrected-timing MCS28 valid negative

## Verdict

**VALID NEGATIVE.** Explicit PUSCH MCS28 reaches attach and completes both
receiver-authoritative 45-second traffic directions, but it sharply reduces
uplink throughput. MCS28 is retired for the current M2SDR OTA path.

The sustained result is:

- downlink: `7.045821 Mbit/s`;
- uplink: `1.976477 Mbit/s`.

Downlink is within `0.19%` of CP3390 MCS16. Uplink is only `15.67%` of CP3390
and `15.43%` of CP3393. Against CP3214 ZMQ MCS28, it reaches `93.84%` of
downlink but only `5.92%` of uplink. This is an uplink MCS-policy result, not a
general access or downlink regression.

## Evidence

The run selects the cell at `14.04 dB`, establishes the private data session,
and passes duration, stream, endpoint, and acknowledgement gates.

The row-capped PUSCH prefix is directly comparable with CP3390/CP3393:

| Run | Policy | CRC OK | QAM64 rows | Median TBS |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| CP3390 | MCS16 | 128/128 | 0 | 528 B |
| CP3393 | MCS16 | 127/128 | 0 | 544 B |
| CP3396 | MCS28 | 117/128 | 94 | 544 B |

Of CP3396's 94 QAM64 rows, 83 decode (`88.30%`). Their median target code rate
is `0.92578125`; CRC-OK and CRC-KO median SINR are `28.68 dB` and `21.05 dB`.
The higher cap therefore selects the most aggressive table entry, lowers
reliability, and does not raise the bounded prefix's median payload.

This trace is capped at 128 rows and is not presented as a full-transfer
distribution. Endpoint JSON remains authoritative for sustained throughput.

Final strict TX health is partial: five breaks, a `5110`-sample write deficit,
and a `40226`-sample stream deficit over `4.818 billion` requested samples,
with no partial returns, hard errors, or downlink-late lines. The stream
deficit fraction is `8.35e-6`. CP3390 delivered 12.61 Mbit/s uplink despite
20 breaks, so this small final deficit does not explain the MCS28-specific
84.3% uplink drop.

## Decision

Keep corrected MCS16 as the proven fallback. The next bounded sensitivity is
MCS19, not another MCS28 retry. OCUDU's table maps:

- MCS16 to 16QAM at code-rate numerator 658;
- MCS19 to 64QAM at 517;
- MCS28 to 64QAM at 948.

MCS19 offers about 18% more nominal spectral efficiency than MCS16 while
avoiding MCS28's `0.9258` code rate. Gate it as a separate launcher and retain
all timing, PRACH, RF, traffic, and health controls.
