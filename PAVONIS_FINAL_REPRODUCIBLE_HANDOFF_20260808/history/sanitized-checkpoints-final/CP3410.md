# CP3410 - Inside-guard MCS19 OTA throughput

## Verdict

**PASS; NEW MEASURED HARDWARE UPLINK MAXIMUM.** The first valid MCS19
hardware roll sustains `14.9059 Mbit/s` uplink and `6.7441 Mbit/s` downlink
for 45 seconds per direction over four TCP streams. It improves uplink by
`16.4-18.2%` over the two corrected MCS16 hardware controls while retaining
`98.44%` bounded PUSCH CRC yield.

## Evidence

The radio ladder acquired the cell and completed the private data session.
The bounded PUSCH prefix contains `126/128` CRC-OK rows at median
`21.76 dB` CRC-OK SINR. Its 64QAM subset is `92/94` CRC OK at target code rate
`0.5049`, unlike CP3396's over-aggressive MCS28 subset at code rate `0.9258`.

The endpoint-authoritative sustained result is:

| Direction | Throughput |
|---|---:|
| Downlink | `6.7441 Mbit/s` |
| Uplink | `14.9059 Mbit/s` |

Relative to prior controls, uplink is:

- `+18.17%` versus CP3390 MCS16;
- `+16.37%` versus CP3393 MCS16;
- `96.32%` of CP3324's ZMQ MCS16 result;
- `7.54x` CP3396's rejected MCS28 result.

TX health is exact: `4,818,228,330` samples requested and returned, with zero
write or stream deficit, deadline breaks, deadline timeouts, partial returns,
hard errors, Soapy TX timeout lines, and downlink-late lines. The inside-guard
policy was enabled but saw no boundary attempt, so it remains proven
non-regressing rather than directly exercised.

## Decision

CP3410 replaces CP3390 as the measured M2SDR hardware uplink maximum. MCS19 is
not yet the established ceiling because its 64QAM CRC yield remains `97.87%`.
The next isolated rung is MCS20 with every other variable frozen. A throughput
gain with acceptable CRC health promotes it; no gain or a material reliability
loss retires it before considering MCS21.

The historical USB LiteX bandwidth recollection is external context, not a
Pavonis campaign result, and is not used in this verdict.
