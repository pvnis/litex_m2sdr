# CP3412 - MCS20 payload pass, TX-health partial

## Verdict

**STRICT TX-HEALTH PARTIAL.** MCS20 carries clean sustained traffic at
`6.9835/17.2588 Mbit/s` DL/UL and the bounded PHY prefix is `128/128` CRC OK,
but the authoritative TX stop gate fails. This is not promoted as the clean
hardware maximum.

## Evidence

The endpoint-authoritative 45-second, four-stream result passes in both
directions. Uplink is `15.78%` above CP3410 MCS19. All `94` bounded 64QAM rows
decode at target code rate `0.5537`; total PUSCH yield is `128/128`, with
median CRC-OK SINR `22.23 dB`.

The final TX summary records:

- `4,809,489,392` requested versus `4,809,477,128` returned samples;
- `12,264` write-deficit and `88,162` stream-deficit samples;
- `12` deadline breaks and one downlink-late event;
- zero Soapy timeout log lines, partial-return counters, or hard errors.

The new inside-guard source path is directly proven: all `13` attempts
succeeded and recovered `12,542` samples, with zero inside-guard timeout.
The remaining 12 trace rows entered with raw deadlines already `46-272 us`
late. They occurred 34.6-80.7 seconds after UE startup and are not eligible
for the positive-lead recovery path.

## Decision

CP3412 demonstrates that MCS20 has useful PHY and payload headroom, but its
overall runner status is correctly nonzero. Repeat the exact frozen MCS20
shape once under the single-health-void rule. A fully clean repeat promotes
MCS20; another TX-health failure retires it as an operational maximum and
leaves CP3410 MCS19 as the proven clean ceiling.
