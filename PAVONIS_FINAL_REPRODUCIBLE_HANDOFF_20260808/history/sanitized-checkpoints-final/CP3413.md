# CP3413 - MCS20 retry pre-UE TX-health void

## Verdict

**VOID FOR MCS20.** The unchanged retry fails the pre-UE TX-health gate, so
the UE is intentionally not started and no payload or PHY conclusion exists.

## Evidence

The stop summary records `744,913,988` requested versus `744,908,878`
returned samples, five deadline breaks, `5,110` write-deficit samples, and
`43,292` stream-deficit samples. There are no Soapy timeout log lines,
downlink-late events, partial-return counters, or hard errors.

All five breaks form a single six-millisecond cluster before UE startup. Raw
lead at entry is already `-141` to `-15 us`. The inside-guard source path
again behaves correctly: `7/7` attempts succeed, recovering `6,410` samples
with zero inside-guard timeout.

## Decision

Do not repeat the unchanged 8.5 ms lead. CP3412 already shows MCS20's clean
PHY/payload potential; CP3413 isolates the inability to obtain a clean
operational verdict to stochastic expired-deadline clusters that exist before
MCS scheduling is active.

The next no-RF branch is a coherent 9.0 ms lead: add 500 us physical TX
headroom and move PRACH compensation by `+5,760` samples and edge calibration
by `-5,760` samples. That projects every CP3412/CP3413 expired root back to at
least `+228 us` raw lead, where the 100 us guard and inside-guard policy can
handle it. It must pass a 420-second TX-only gate before another MCS20 run.
