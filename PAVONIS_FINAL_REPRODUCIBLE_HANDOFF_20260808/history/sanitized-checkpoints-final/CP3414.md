# CP3414 - Coherent 9 ms MCS20 long-gate no-RF proof

## Verdict

**PASS.** A coherently retimed 9.0 ms physical TX lead is ready for one
420-second transmit-only acceptance run. RF was not used.

## Rationale

CP3412 and CP3413's unrecovered roots entered only `15-272 us` after their
raw deadlines. Adding 500 us projects the worst roots to `+228 us` and
`+359 us`. The existing 100 us reservation then leaves positive write budget,
and the inside-guard source path covers the final boundary.

At `11.52 Msps`, 500 us is exactly `5,760` samples. The branch therefore
changes the physical lead `8.5->9.0 ms`, PRACH compensation
`97,764->103,524`, and edge calibration `-97,760->-103,520`. Static
non-PRACH shifts remain unchanged, matching the prior coherent lead-ladder
rule.

## Evidence

After normalizing checkpoint identity and those three coherent constants:

- the launcher is byte-identical to CP3411;
- the stage-expectation export/unset set is byte-identical to CP3404;
- the 420-second runner is byte-identical to CP3408.

Remote launcher hash/validate and fresh MCS20 config generation pass. The
runner validates live lead, guard, PRACH, edge, logging, capture-off, and
stop-before-UE controls. RF is default-closed with exit code two.

## Decision

Run CP3415 once for 420 seconds. No OTA ladder is permitted unless requested
and returned unique stream samples are equal and every deadline, deficit,
timeout, partial, driver, release, downlink-late, and hard-error counter is
zero.
