# CP3420 - Lead11 MCS20 OTA no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS NO-RF.** One full MCS20 OTA benchmark is unlocked under the accepted
11.0 ms timing.

## Evidence

The runner pins:

- TX lead `11,000,000 ns`;
- PRACH compensation `126,564` samples;
- edge calibration `-126,560` samples;
- MCS20 uplink and MCS9 downlink ceilings;
- the frozen 45-second, four-stream bidirectional method.

After normalizing timing values, launcher/expectation paths and fresh artifact
identity, this runner is byte-identical to CP3412. RF controls, 23 stage-1
candidates, static non-PRACH shifts, captures, logging, and endpoint method
are unchanged.

Controller validation returns zero. The path without the explicit approval
returns two and prints the closed-gate marker.

## Decision

Run CP3421 once. It must attach, complete both sustained directions, retain
healthy bounded 64QAM decoding, and finish with exact unique-stream TX
accounting. Compare throughput directly with CP3410 MCS19 and CP3412's
health-partial MCS20 result.
