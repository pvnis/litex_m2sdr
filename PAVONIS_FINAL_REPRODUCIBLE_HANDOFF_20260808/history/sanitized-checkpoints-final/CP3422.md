and default-closed. It combines CP3410's clean MCS19 ceiling with CP3419's

## Evidence

after normalizing checkpoint identity and the intended MCS config launcher:

- the same native gNB binary hash and all other runtime exports.

The sole semantic delta is the proven CP3397 config launcher selecting
`max_ue_mcs: 19` instead of MCS20. Remote config-only execution generated
exactly one MCS19 block with SHA-256
`e634d85b08e6ca24ae47d20c5f02654958cb1ebe1cf84f1f680063a398c5d942`.
The full runner normalizes exactly to CP3420 after artifact identity, port, and
launcher substitutions. Controller validation passes and the RF gate exits

## Decision

Run CP3423 once as a robustness confirmation. It must pass attach, sustained
bidirectional endpoints, bounded PUSCH health, and exact unique-stream TX
delivery. CP3410 remains the measured clean maximum regardless of whether
normal run-to-run throughput scatter makes CP3423 numerically higher or lower.
