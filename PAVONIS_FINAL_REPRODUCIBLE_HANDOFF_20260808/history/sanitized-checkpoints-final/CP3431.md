# CP3431 - PDSCH MCS13 M2SDR no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS.** One bounded M2SDR run is unlocked. The generated gNB config differs
from CP3424's accepted MCS19/lead11 config at exactly one YAML value: PDSCH
`max_ue_mcs: 9 -> 13`.

RF remains default-closed; invoking the runner without
`PAVONIS_CP3432_RF_APPROVED=1` exits with rc2.

## Timing and controller gate

The frozen operating point remains:

- TX lead `11,000,000 ns`;
- PRACH compensation `126564` samples;
- non-PRACH shifts `-125918/-122598`;
- edge calibration `-126560`;
- deadline guard `100 us`;
- inside-guard writes enabled;
- 23 stage-1 candidates;
- four streams, sequential 45-second directions.

The controller validates expected downlink MCS13, all timing relationships,
independent PRACH compensation, and the 23/30 candidate cap.

## Decision

Run CP3432 once and judge health first. A useful result requires normal
access, receiver downlink materially above the PDSCH9 hardware range,
bounded UE PDSCH proof of the MCS/TBS/CRC class, and retention of the MCS19
uplink operating class. A pre-UE void may be retried unchanged once.

Do not stack bandwidth, MIMO, or producer-stall changes.
