# CP3445 - 15 MHz acquisition passes; Msg1 is not detected

## Verdict

Honest partial. The current 15 MHz / 79 PRB / 15.36 Msps stack acquires the
cell and decodes SIB1 with the 13 ms TX lead, but the gNB accepts none of seven
UE Msg1 transmissions. No RAR, Msg3, data session, or throughput result exists.

## Evidence

- The corrected native-TX readback gate and the strict 60-second pre-UE TX
  health gate both pass.
- UE cell selection, SFN synchronization, and SIB1 all pass. Cell-search SNR is
  `10.56 dB`; CFO is `-3871.811 Hz`.
- The UE emits seven PRACH attempts. The gNB records zero accepted PRACH rows
  and zero RAR allocations.
- The final full-window TX summary has one deadline break and `6162` abandoned
  samples, with zero downlink-late events and zero hard write errors. This keeps
  producer headroom open but does not explain the seven receive-side floor
  windows by itself.
- Throughput tools correctly remain unstarted because random access did not
  complete.

## Classification

This run proves that the CP3437 15 MHz SSB correction and CP3442/CP3444 lead
increase are sufficient for downlink acquisition. It is not evidence for or
against a 15 MHz throughput gain. The PRACH result requires the no-RF
saved-window and timestamp join in CP3446 before any detector change.
