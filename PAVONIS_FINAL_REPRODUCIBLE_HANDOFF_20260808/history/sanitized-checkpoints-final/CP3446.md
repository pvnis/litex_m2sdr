# CP3446 - CP3445 is a clean zero-RACH pre-access scatter

## Verdict

No-RF diagnosis complete. The sixteen saved strong windows are not the seven
UE Msg1 occasions. They must not be used to widen the stage-1 envelope.
CP3445 matches the known clean zero-RACH pre-access scatter class, so one
unchanged fresh-stamp retry is warranted.

## Replay

An isolated CP1965-derived harness was rebuilt for the exact CP3445 geometry:
`15.36 Msps`, `79` PRBs, PRACH RB offset `8`. The historical harness and
deployed gNB remain unchanged.

- Baseline replay: `0/16` accepted, maximum p0 ratio `0.106`.
- Exact CP3445 23-candidate policy: `0/384` accepted rows, maximum p0 ratio
  `0.163`, maximum any-preamble ratio `0.220`.

Those negatives initially appear consistent with the live detector, but the
event-time join shows the files are the wrong occasions.

## Event-Time Join

All seven UE Msg1 wall times map to the matching gNB detector SFN/slot
(`193.4`, `305.4`, `417.4`, `529.4`, `641.4`, `753.4`, `865.4`) within
`0.926..2.107 ms` of cross-host wall time. Their detector RSSI is tightly
floor-class at `-66.38..-66.03 dB`.

The nearest saved strong window is at least `158.559 ms` away from a real Msg1
and most are much farther away. None covers an actual Msg1 SFN/slot. The
strong-window capture selected unrelated energy.

## Historical Control

CP3191 used the same 15 MHz geometry, PRACH placement conversion
(`79`-PRB carrier to the centered `75`-PRB srsUE grid), sample rate, generated
waveform level, TX guard, and PRACH timestamp offset. Its accepted preamble
arrived at `-49.48 dB`, roughly `17 dB` above CP3445's real Msg1 windows.
CP3190 immediately before it was itself a clean zero-RACH scatter.

## Decision

Do not change timing, CFO candidates, gains, or thresholds from CP3445's
unrelated strong files. Retry the exact CP3445 shape once with a fresh stamp.
If the second run again presents seven floor-class Msg1 windows, stop bare
retries and investigate the run-variable UL presentation/path state.
