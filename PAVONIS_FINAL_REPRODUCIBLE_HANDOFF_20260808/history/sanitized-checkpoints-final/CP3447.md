# CP3447 - Second 15 MHz floor-class Msg1 closes bare retries

## Verdict

The unchanged fresh-stamp retry reproduces CP3445. Downlink acquisition and
SIB1 pass, but the gNB receives real Msg1 occasions at floor level and accepts
none. Two consecutive valid floor-class runs close the bare-retry branch.

## Evidence

- Native readback and all three strict 60-second pre-UE gates pass.
- Cell selection, SFN synchronization, and SIB1 pass at `10.211 dB`; cell CFO
  is `-3844.514 Hz`.
- The UE emits seven PRACH attempts. Six carry the timestamp marker used by the
  join; every one maps to its exact gNB SFN/slot within `1.447..1.965 ms`.
- Joined detector RSSI is `-67.86..-66.15 dB`. No Msg1 is accepted and no RAR
  or Msg3 exists.
- Strong-window index 15 overlaps the `929.4` Msg1 event by `1.678 ms`. Its
  raw RMS is `-66.111 dB`; exact 15 MHz replay gives baseline p0 ratio `0.058`
  and best live-policy p0 ratio `0.117`, with no accept. This is an actual
  floor-class Msg1 control, unlike CP3445's unrelated strong files.
- The full-window TX stop summary has three deadline breaks and `26662`
  abandoned/stream-deficit samples, zero downlink-late events, and zero hard
  errors. Producer headroom remains open as a separate 15 MHz issue.

## Decision

Do not run a third unchanged retry and do not widen detector candidates from
floor-level samples. Stay no-RF and compare the accepted CP3191 path against
CP3445/CP3447 at the srsUE binary/config, bladeRF TX routing/gain/readback, and
M2SDR RX state. A new RF run requires a specific state difference or a bounded
directional tone/readback discriminator.
