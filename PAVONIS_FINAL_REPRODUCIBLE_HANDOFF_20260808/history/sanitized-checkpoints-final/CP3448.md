# CP3448 - 13 ms lead retained stale 11 ms UL timing

## Verdict

No-RF root cause and fix gate pass. CP3443 increased the 15 MHz TX lead from
11 ms to 13 ms to clear producer stalls, but left every UL collection offset
at its 11 ms value. CP3445 and CP3447 therefore listened approximately 2 ms
early for Msg1. This explains the repeated floor-class windows without
requiring an RF path or detector-correlation regression.

## Evidence

The CP3191 and CP3447 RF-safe UE settings match:

- sample rate `15.36 Msps`;
- bladeRF TX gain `66 dB`, RX gain `40 dB`;
- actual UL [interface-redacted] `1747505998 Hz`;
- identical centered 79-PRB to 75-PRB PRACH placement;
- matching generated waveform RMS/peak and successful TX status;
- normalized bounded UE runner SHA-256
  `c1bacc4973a3c9ac59ab6a0302b16a3f9762f4b2e1dc1f5d6b50b90bd2dd6c2f`.

The timing history is not matched:

| Shape | TX lead | PRACH compensation | Time |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CP3191 accepted 15 MHz | 20 ms | 316448 | 20.602083 ms |
| CP3437 original 15 MHz | 11 ms | 168752 | 10.986458 ms |
| CP3445/CP3447 | 13 ms | 168752 | 10.986458 ms |

The CP3437 value is coherent with its original 11 ms lead. CP3443 changed lead
by `+2 ms` but froze that value, leaving an exact `30720`-sample error at
15.36 Msps. PRACH is shorter than that displacement, so correctly attributed
SFN/slot detector calls can still contain only floor samples.

## Coherent Fix

The isolated controller changes one timing family:

- PRACH compensation: `168752 -> 199472`;
- non-PRACH negative shift: `-167891 -> -198611`;
- non-PRACH positive shift: `-163464 -> -194184`;
- edge calibration: `-168747 -> -199467`.

This applies `+30720` samples to the collection delay and the corresponding
`-30720` shift to connected-UL collection. RF, MCS, stage-1 policy, TX lead,
scheduler, and protocol behavior are unchanged.

## No-RF Proofs

- Python compilation passes.
- Exact source diff contains only labels and the four coherent timing values.
- Validate-only passes with 23 candidates and cap 30.
- The RF gate defaults closed with rc `2`.
- Closed-gate execution creates no result directory.

## Decision

One bounded 15 MHz run is now justified. Judge Msg1/RAR first. If access
returns, continue through Msg3 and the 45-second sustained benchmark. If the
corrected window is still floor-class, stop and use a bounded directional RF
control; do not add detector candidates.
