# CP3451 - Retimed 15 MHz is functional but not a capacity gain

## Verdict

The coherent `2 ms` UL retime is the missing 15 MHz functional fix. Its values
reach the runtime manifest, the UE attaches through RAR and Msg3, and a
bidirectional sustained user-plane test passes. The bandwidth result is
nevertheless a valid capacity negative at PDSCH MCS 9: `0.540068 Mbit/s`
downlink and `0.397201 Mbit/s` uplink.

## Evidence

- Runtime proves PRACH compensation `199472` and the matching non-PRACH and
  edge offsets. Cell selection, SFN synchronization, and SIB1 pass.
- Two UE PRACH transmissions produce 21 accepted detector rows, 15 gNB RAR
  allocations, one UE-decoded RAR, and Msg3 preparation.
- The bounded uplink sample is healthy: `128/128` PUSCH CRC OK, median accepted
  SINR `4.621 dB`, and median TBS `528 B`.
- Sequential `45 s` TCP tests pass in both directions. Receiver-authoritative
  rates are `0.540068 Mbit/s` DL and `0.397201 Mbit/s` UL.
- The bounded connected-downlink trace explains the low DL rate: only
  `105/202` PDSCH rows pass CRC. MCS 9 is `76/157` with mean reported SNR
  `-1.424 dB`; full-band MCS 9 grants are `32/67`.
- TX delivery is strict-clean: zero timeout, deadline break, abandoned sample,
  stream deficit, downlink-late event, or hard error. One `430`-sample partial
  return is fully recovered.

## Interpretation

CP3448's timing diagnosis is confirmed: the earlier 15 MHz Msg1 floor was
collection timing, not RF path power or an undetectable preamble. The wider
cell now works end to end.

It does not increase capacity in this RF state. The 79-PRB carrier spreads the
available signal over more resource elements, while the prior campaign already
showed that the M2SDR downlink has little margin above QPSK MCS 9. The roughly
half-rate PDSCH CRC yield makes the sustained negative real; the benchmark
endpoints and radio health both pass.

## Decision

Preserve the coherent 15 MHz retiming. Before closing bandwidth, test one
isolated lower PDSCH ceiling on this exact shape, ZMQ/no-RF gated first. Do not
reopen timing, stage-1, power, or the known-unsustainable 20 MHz path.
