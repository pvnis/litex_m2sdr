# CP3452 - Robust PDSCH makes 15 MHz useful over ZMQ

## Verdict

PASS. On the proven 15 MHz ZMQ geometry, changing only PDSCH maximum MCS from
9 to 7 sustains `9.015192 Mbit/s` downlink and `22.793159 Mbit/s` uplink.
This is a valid no-RF gain and unlocks one corresponding OTA run.

## Evidence

- Geometry remains `15 MHz`, `79 PRB`, `15.36 Msps`, SSB ARFCN `367450`,
  FDD, and one spatial layer.
- The gNB config differs from CP3183 by one semantic line:
  `pdsch.max_ue_mcs: 9 -> 7`.
- Local shell syntax and parser compilation pass. Deployed config, runner,
  parser, UE-role, and core-role hashes pass before startup.
- Random access, RRC, PDU session, address assignment, and private gateway
  connectivity pass.
- Both receiver-authoritative `45 s` endpoint records are complete:
  `9.015192 Mbit/s` DL and `22.793159 Mbit/s` UL.
- DL is `18.62%` above the CP3324 10 MHz/PDSCH9 ZMQ baseline.

The inherited CP3183 config logs at info level, so the optional scheduler
parser finds no debug `Slot decisions` rows. This does not invalidate the
endpoint result; the pinned config proves the MCS ceiling.

## Decision

Build a hardware-facing CP3451 variant changing only PDSCH maximum MCS from 9
to 7. Preserve the corrected 15 MHz timing, PUSCH19, 13 ms lead, RF settings,
and traffic method. Run it once after hash/config/default-closed proofs.
