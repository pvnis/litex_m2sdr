# CP3453 - 15 MHz/PDSCH7 hardware gate

## Verdict

PASS, no RF. The CP3452 PDSCH7 gain is isolated on CP3451's corrected
hardware shape. The only intended runtime change is PDSCH maximum MCS `9 -> 7`.

## Evidence

- The source gNB config differs from CP3451 by exactly the PDSCH ceiling.
- The controller reports PDSCH7 dynamically and otherwise pins CP3451's gNB
  launcher, 15 MHz geometry, PUSCH19, 13 ms lead, coherent four-value UL
  retime, 100 us deadline guard, gains, stage-1 policy, and traffic tools.
- Controller compilation and wrapper shell syntax pass.
- Validate-only passes with 23 candidates, limit 30, and MCS7.
- The RF wrapper is default-closed with rc2 and creates no result directory.
- Remote config-only generation passes. Its output contains exactly PDSCH7,
  PUSCH19, and warning-level logging; generated config SHA-256 is
  `1d698c5dd4cd4061403662b350bd313264d7e80943148185aa423a27fbb33999`.

## Decision

Run one bounded CP3454 OTA benchmark. A useful result must preserve attach and
strict TX health, materially improve connected PDSCH CRC yield over CP3451,
and beat the robust 10 MHz throughput baseline. Otherwise close bandwidth and
move to MIMO feasibility.
