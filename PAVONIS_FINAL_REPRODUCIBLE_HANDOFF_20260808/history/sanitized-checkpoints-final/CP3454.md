# CP3454 - 15 MHz/PDSCH7 pre-UE TX-health void

## Verdict

VOID for PDSCH and throughput. The strict pre-UE TX-health gate correctly
suppressed UE startup after two deadline breaks and `22544` lost samples.

## Evidence

- Runtime configuration identifies the intended PDSCH7/PUSCH19, 15 MHz,
  13 ms lead, coherently retimed shape.
- Native TX readback passes.
- Over the following 60 seconds, the pre-UE gate returns rc34.
- The stop summary records two deadline write timeouts, two deadline breaks,
  `22544` abandoned/stream-deficit samples, zero downlink-late events, and zero
  hard errors.
- The UE never starts. There is no cell, PDSCH, PUSCH, or throughput datapoint.

## Decision

Apply the single-void rule: retry the identical shape once with a fresh
checkpoint/tag/stamp. If it fails the same pre-UE gate again, close the OTA
bandwidth attempt and carry the result directly into producer-stall headroom.
