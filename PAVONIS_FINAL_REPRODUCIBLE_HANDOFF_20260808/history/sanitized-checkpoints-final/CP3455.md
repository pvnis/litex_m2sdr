# CP3455 - PDSCH7 retry is a propagation void

## Verdict

VOID for PDSCH7. The controller records MCS7 intent, but both packaged live gNB
configs contain PDSCH maximum MCS9 and the UE trace contains MCS8/9 traffic.

## Evidence

- CP3455 clears the pre-UE gate and reaches a connected traffic window.
- The execution summary records requested PDSCH7/PUSCH19.
- The packaged source and generated gNB configs both contain
  `pdsch.max_ue_mcs: 9`.
- The UE trace has 36 MCS8 and 147 MCS9 PDSCH decode rows. It cannot have been
  an MCS7 run.
- Source audit identifies the override:
  `cp2343_run_pdsch_max_ue_mcs9.sh` unconditionally exports
  `GNB_MAX_UE_MCS=9` after the controller supplies 7.

The incidental MCS9 radio window is weak and not a formal benchmark:
PDSCH is `102/199` CRC OK; core-side endpoint completion exists, but the UE
endpoint omits its uplink record. Receiver observations are `0.471338 Mbit/s`
DL and `0.247125 Mbit/s` UL. Final TX also has three deadline breaks and
`34838` stream-deficit samples.

## Decision

Stay no-RF. Copy the established runner chain and make the PDSCH9 layer
caller-preserving while retaining 9 as its default. Prove that the generated
runtime config is PDSCH7 before another OTA run. Do not interpret CP3455 as a
PDSCH7 negative.
