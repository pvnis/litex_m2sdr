# CP3472 - New downlink-ceiling campaign, no RF reconstruction

## Verdict

**PASS.** The separately named downlink-ceiling campaign is open, and the
existing evidence has been reconstructed without RF. The measured MCS9
ceiling remains valid, but the stronger claim that the M2SDR has a proven
16QAM-specific hardware defect does not yet follow from the archived data.

No baseline, runtime binary, radio configuration, or prior report was changed.

## Archived causal sequence

The four UE archives were parsed with one field-level tool:

| MCS | Modulation | RV0 CRC OK | RV0 rows | RV0 yield |
|---:|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 9 | QPSK | 218 | 225 | 96.89% |
| 10 | 16QAM | 1 | 9 | 11.11% |
| 11 | 16QAM | 67 | 132 | 50.76% |
| 13 | 16QAM | 1 | 97 | 1.03% |
| 16 | 16QAM | 0 | 87 | 0.00% |

The equal full-PRB MCS9/MCS10 payload is 1089 bytes. This confirms where the
measured cliff begins. It does not, by itself, localize the cause: MCS9 is from
CP3424, while MCS10 is a small scheduler-conditioned fallback population in
CP3435 rather than a balanced same-window experiment.

HARQ is important. RV2 yields are 85.71% at MCS10, 81.61% at MCS13, and
65.22% at MCS16. The same 16QAM codewords become decodable after combining.
That rules against a hard modulation mapping, scrambling, or decoder-geometry
incompatibility and instead identifies a first-transmission margin problem.

## Exact decoder control

A new `EXCLUDE_FROM_ALL` test drives the existing srsRAN PDSCH encoder,
demodulator, and LDPC decoder through AWGN with a perfect channel estimate.
The existing noiseless mapping test also passes MCS9, 10, 11, 13, and 16.

With 1000 independent blocks per point, the first 0.25 dB grid point at or
below 10% BLER is:

| MCS | Modulation | SNR at BLER <= 10% |
|---:|---|---:|
| 9 | QPSK | 4.25 dB |
| 10 | 16QAM | 5.75 dB |
| 11 | 16QAM | 6.25 dB |
| 13 | 16QAM | 7.75 dB |
| 16 | 16QAM | 10.50 dB |

MCS9 and MCS10 have the same 8712-bit transport block in this shape, yet the
legacy decoder needs 1.5 dB more AWGN margin at MCS10. This is not itself a
bug, but it means a marginal path can flip sharply at the modulation boundary
without a new M2SDR failure mechanism.

## Instrument and source audit

The logged `evm` is not transmitter-referenced EVM. `srsran_evm_run_b`
hard-decides the receiver's own LLRs, remodulates those decisions, and measures
distance to that moving reference. A symbol that crosses a decision boundary
can therefore move both the received point and the reference. This explains
why the value barely separates CRC-OK and CRC-KO rows and why its scale changes
between QPSK and 16QAM. Earlier reports correctly called it uncalibrated; CP3472
now records the exact reason.

The M2SDR CS16-to-SC12 path uses signed division by 16 and does not wrap the
low 12 bits. The frozen gNB uses 10 dB digital backoff with clipping disabled.
The C-RNTI PDSCH defaults to zero data and DMRS power offsets when no CSI-RS
power profile is configured, and one DMRS CDM group gives a zero relative
offset. No static PDSCH-data/DMRS amplitude mismatch is found in this source
path.

## Reframed conclusion

The MCS9 operating ceiling is real and remains the correct production pin.
What remains unexplained is narrower: whether the extra live margin loss is
ordinary path quality at the legacy decoder's sharp threshold, or distortion
introduced between OCUDU's clean grid and the received equalized symbols.

The current cross-run data cannot answer that because it lacks a balanced
same-window MCS9/MCS10 population and a transmitter-referenced constellation
measurement. Cell-search SNR is acquisition evidence, not a substitute for
scheduled PDSCH quality, and the old decision-directed EVM must not be used as
that measurement.

## Next gate

Build a same-run discriminator that alternates or otherwise balances fixed,
full-PRB MCS9 and MCS10 new transmissions under one RF state. Keep bandwidth,
power, timing, MIMO, and producer policy frozen. Trace only bounded metadata
and obtain known-symbol/transmitter-referenced error at the pre-quantization,
post-CS16-to-SC12, and receive boundaries. No RF run is unlocked until that
instrument and its ZMQ positive control pass no-RF gates.
