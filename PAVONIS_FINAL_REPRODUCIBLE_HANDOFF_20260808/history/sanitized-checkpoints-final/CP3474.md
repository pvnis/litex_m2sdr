# CP3474 - M2SDR same-run MCS9/MCS10 no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS.** One bounded hardware discriminator is prepared, hash-pinned,
read-only preflighted, and default-closed. No SDR stream was opened and no RF
was transmitted.

## Isolation

The run inherits the CP3424 robust shape: 10 MHz FDD, one layer, uplink MCS19,
11 ms transmit lead, PRACH compensation `126564`, non-PRACH edge calibration
`-126560`, 100 us deadline guard with inside-guard retry, attenuation 5 dB,
receive gain 40 dB, 23 Stage-1 candidates, and four 45-second TCP streams.

Two intentional downlink controls are added:

1. the configured PDSCH ceiling is 10, so both compared MCS values are valid;
2. the CP3473 selector explicitly alternates new transmissions MCS9/MCS10.

The selector remains default-off in the launcher and is enabled only by the
CP3475 runner. Its trace is capped at 512 rows. The UE C-RNTI trace is capped
at 4096 decode rows and records MCS, RV, CRC, PRBs, TBS, SNR, and CFO. No
full-rate append, sample scan, or live log watcher is enabled.

## CP3475 Judgment

Judge in this order:

1. attach, endpoints, TX timeout/late/deadline/unique-stream health;
2. selector startup marker and balanced MCS9/MCS10 rows;
3. UE trace propagation and per-MCS RV0 CRC yield under the same run/channel;
4. only if a stable per-MCS split exists, add known-symbol transmitter-reference
   error measurements at bounded pre-quantization/post-SC12/receive boundaries.

A no-cell or pre-UE producer failure is void and receives one unchanged retry.
Bandwidth, further MIMO, and producer-stall headroom remain locked.
