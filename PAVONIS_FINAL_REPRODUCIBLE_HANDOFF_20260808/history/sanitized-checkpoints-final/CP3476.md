# CP3476 - MCS A/B executable-boundary arm no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS.** The CP3475 propagation defect is closed no-RF. The source diagnostic
and main launcher remain default-off, while a tiny hash-pinned wrapper arms the
selector at the exact executable override boundary that the historical runner
cannot filter. No SDR stream was opened and no RF was transmitted.

## Mechanism

The default launcher accepts the inherited, proven PDSCH9/PUSCH19 generated
profile. The isolated armed wrapper verifies the launcher's SHA-256, emits a
once-per-run marker, then invokes it with selector enable 1 and trace limit 512.
The source selector still alternates MCS9/MCS10 new transmissions and leaves
HARQ retransmission MCS unchanged.

This is intentionally different from trying to pass a new environment variable
through dozens of historical wrappers. The arm is applied after those wrappers
have selected the executable, immediately before the launcher runs.

## Proofs

- Default launcher validation reports selector 0.
- Armed-wrapper validation reports the wrapper marker and selector 1.
- Fresh config-only execution reports, in order: wrapper active, launcher
  selector 1, PUSCH19 config active, and runtime exports selector 1.
- Generated config remains the historical PDSCH9/PUSCH19 file at SHA-256
  `e634d85b...d942`.
- The controller validates the frozen 10 MHz, one-layer, lead-11 ms,
  PRACH126564, edge-126560, four-stream shape with requested PDSCH9.
- The RF gate remains closed with rc2 unless CP3477 approval is explicit.

## CP3477 Judgment

Before interpreting anything, require all three propagation markers: armed
wrapper, launcher selector 1, and gNB selector startup. Then judge strict TX
health, attach/endpoints, balanced selector rows, and UE RV0 CRC yield by MCS.
The historical configured cap remaining 9 is not a confound: the default-off
diagnostic explicitly overrides only compared new transmissions, and CP3473
already proved the same 9/10 override cleanly over ZMQ.
