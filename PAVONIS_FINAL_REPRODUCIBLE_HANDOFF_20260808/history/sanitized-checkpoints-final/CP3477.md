# Checkpoint 3477: Same-run MCS9/MCS10 M2SDR discriminator

## Verdict

**VALID: the interleaved hardware run shows a reproducible-within-run MCS9
advantage, while the physical mechanism remains open.** The run attached,
completed both sustained traffic directions, propagated the selector through
the final launch boundary, and remained strict-clean at the TX stream level.

This is the first positive finding of the new downlink-ceiling campaign. It is
not yet a root-cause claim and does not unlock bandwidth, MIMO, or producer
headroom work. One unchanged repeat is required first.

## Shape and gates

- Frozen 10 MHz FDD, 52 PRB, one layer, all-timed M2SDR shape.
- New transmissions alternated MCS9 and MCS10 every grant; retransmissions kept
  their original MCS.
- Selector trace: 512 rows, exactly 256 MCS9 and 256 MCS10.
- Dominant traced allocation: 52 PRB, 1089-byte TBS, one layer (360/512 rows).
- Cell selection passed at 12.227 dB reported SNR and -4047.848 Hz CFO.
- Sustained endpoints completed: 1.246675 Mbit/s DL and 10.828937 Mbit/s UL.

The low aggregate DL result is consistent with the observed HARQ burden; it is
not used as the per-MCS discriminator because the endpoint aggregates both
branches.

## Same-run result

For all first-transmission trace rows:

| MCS | CRC OK / RV0 | RV0 yield | Median reported SNR |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| 9 | 21 / 59 | 35.59% | 1.865 dB |
| 10 | 12 / 63 | 19.05% | 2.213 dB |

MCS9 delivered **1.87x** the MCS10 first-transmission success rate. A two-sided
Fisher exact test gives `p=0.0442` for this observed split.

The stricter equal-geometry subset points in the same direction: MCS9 was
12/35 (34.29%) and MCS10 was 6/39 (15.38%), a 2.23x ratio. That subset alone is
not statistically decisive (`p=0.102`) because it is smaller.

MCS9 and MCS10 sit on opposite sides of the QPSK-to-16QAM transition while
having nearly identical nominal spectral efficiency. The similar median SNR
and equal-TBS result make a simple scheduler-load or low-SNR explanation less
plausible. The decision-directed EVM values are modulation-dependent and are
therefore not compared numerically across MCS9 and MCS10.

## Health

- Soapy TX timeout lines: 0.
- Downlink-late lines: 0.
- Deadline write breaks/timeouts/abandoned samples: 0/0/0.
- Stream errors and unique-stream deficit: 0/0 samples.
- One call-level 480-sample partial return was fully retried; final stream
  accounting remained exact.
- No scheduling-priority failure was present.

The discriminator is therefore not explained by the known producer-stall or
deadline-failure signatures.

## Interpretation and next gate

The campaign now has same-run evidence for a modulation-sensitive robustness
loss at the QPSK/16QAM crossover. It does **not** yet identify whether the loss
is produced before the M2SDR, in SC12/timed transport, in RF, or in the UE
receiver. The next checkpoint repeats this exact interleaved shape with no
configuration change. A second same-direction result will establish a stable
ceiling mechanism; an inversion will classify CP3477 as run-scale scatter.

Bandwidth, second-layer, and producer-stall headroom experiments remain locked
behind that repeat verdict.
