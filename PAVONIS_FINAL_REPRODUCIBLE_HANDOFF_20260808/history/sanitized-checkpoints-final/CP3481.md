## Mechanism conclusion

The MCS10 failure probability is strongly and monotonically margin-sensitive.
A hard constellation mapping, SC12 transport, or fixed timed-DMA corruption
would not recover from 43.10% to 80.95% under a sole amplitude change while
MCS9 and stream accounting remain clean.

CP3472 measured a 1.5 dB MCS10 decoder penalty at equal TBS. CP3481 increases
the live PDSCH DMRS estimate by roughly 1.2–1.4 dB and produces the predicted
large transition-region recovery. The unexplained ceiling is therefore closed
as **finite effective PDSCH margin plus the legacy decoder's modulation-
boundary penalty**, not an M2SDR-specific hard 16QAM defect.

ATT0 is the end of this power branch. Since MCS10 remains below the clean gate,
the robust one-layer profile stays at MCS9. Downstream bandwidth, MIMO, and
producer-headroom work is now unlocked only with robust MCS choices.
