# Checkpoint 3491: PRACH action-window timing-model correction

## Verdict

**PASS, NO RF: the +30,720-sample UL retime is disproven.** CP3489/3490 do
not expose a detector-to-report handoff defect. Their stage-1 searches are
present at the real Msg1 opportunities but the retimed windows never recover
preamble 0. Keep the 15 ms TX producer lead and restore the original physical
UL timing constants.

## Action-window join

A streaming analyzer joins each UE PRACH transmission to detector calls by
SFN/slot and bounded wall time. The UE transmits preamble 0 in every attempt.
All 18 accepted full-detector rows at other preamble IDs are unrelated false
alarms; they correctly do not become the target Msg1.

The decisive comparison is:

| Run shape | Traced Msg1 | Preamble-0 accepts | Max ratio |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| CP3485 lead13, original UL timing | 2 | 1 | 1.256 |
| CP3489 lead15, +30720 retime | 6 of 7 | 0 | 0.160 |
| CP3490 lead15, +30720 retime | 7 | 0 | 0.262 |

CP3485's accepted second Msg1 stops on candidate ordinal 19 and is followed by
three scheduler RAR allocations. In CP3489/3490 the stage-1 batches begin
roughly 2.5-3.6 ms after the matching UE log event and run all 30 candidates,
so the negative is not a missing action opportunity, trace cap, wrong preamble
identity, or stage-1 selection/report bug.

## Mechanism correction

`M2SDR_SOAPY_TX_TIME_OFFSET_NS` controls how far ahead timed TX samples are
submitted. Raising it from 13 to 15 ms increases producer deadline headroom;
the hardware timestamps still place the samples on the same RF timeline. It
therefore does not move the physical UE uplink arrival by 2 ms. CP3486's
assumption that every UL collection offset must shift by 30,720 samples moved
the PRACH window away from the previously demonstrated arrival class.
