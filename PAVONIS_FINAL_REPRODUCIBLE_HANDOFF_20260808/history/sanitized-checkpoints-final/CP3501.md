# Checkpoint 3501: fixed 16 ms recovers Msg1; PUSCH transport leaks PRACH policy

## Verdict

**ACCESS-TIMING PASS; PUSCH/THROUGHPUT INVALID.** Fixed 16 ms collection turns
six consecutive real Msg1 attempts into accepted detections, six transmitted
and UE-decoded RARs, and 30 gNB PUSCH results. The PUSCH rows are all CRC KO
because the inherited bladeRF policy misclassifies every high-peak connected
PUSCH block as PRACH. One producer deadline break also invalidates throughput.

## PRACH payoff

All seven action manifests prove sequence length1, index0, delay245760 samples,
timing override0.

| Attempt class | Count | Raw RMS | p0 ratio | Accepted |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Initial floor | 1 | -69.928 dBFS | 0.113 | 0 |
| Energetic | 6 | -57.543 to -57.092 dBFS | 1.202 to 4.313 | 6 |

The six accepts include three baseline-candidate detections and three bounded
transformed detections. The gNB schedules and encodes six RARs; the UE decodes
and accepts all six, applies all six timing advances, and emits uplink traffic.
The 15 MHz acquisition/PRACH blocker is therefore solved by the 16 ms phase.

## PUSCH finding

The gNB sees 30 11-byte PUSCH rows, all CRC KO. Median SINR is -18.413 dB,
median EVM8.755, and median EPRE -65.718 dB. Time alignment is centered near
zero (-0.109 us median, -2.344 to +2.293 us), so a gross collection-time miss
is not the leading explanation.

All 16 bounded high-peak bladeRF metadata records show forced SOB, forced EOB,
and `prach_time_offset_applied=1` with -21333 samples. Source semantics explain
why: with explicit PRACH context disabled, `prach_selected` is true for every
block, so amplitude-based `PRACH_BURST_ONLY=1`, first-high-peak restart, and the
PRACH timestamp offset leak into connected PUSCH. The robust reference has
explicit context enabled and no such leakage.

The minimal next change is only
`PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_PRACH_EXPLICIT_CONTEXT=1`. It preserves the current working
PRACH burst/offset policy when srsUE marks a PRACH call, but excludes PUSCH from
all PRACH-only branches. Do not change the fixed16ms collection, candidate
policy, dynamic non-PRACH offset, or RF controls.

## Producer caveat

Reactive runtime also records one timeout/deadline break, 5140 abandoned and
final-deficit samples, and one partial return. Downlink-late remains zero. This
does not erase the six accepted Msg1/RAR observations, but it makes CP3501 void
for PUSCH-quality and throughput conclusions. The next run remains
health-first.
