## Mechanism

branch recognizes a later true PRACH that would force SOB+EOB. It clears SOB,
EOB, and TX_NOW and substitutes `BLADERF_META_FLAG_TX_UPDATE_TIMESTAMP`.
This directly avoids CP3507's invalid second SOB while retaining the selected
PRACH timestamp and `-21333` offset.

RF shape, detector, producer, scheduler, and traffic controls are unchanged.
