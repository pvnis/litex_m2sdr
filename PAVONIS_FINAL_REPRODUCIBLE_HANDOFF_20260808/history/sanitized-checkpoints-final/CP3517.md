# Checkpoint 3517: dynamic six-phase PRACH no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS, NO RF: one bounded CP3518 run is unlocked.** The fixed 16 ms delay is
replaced by a deterministic per-occasion six-phase bank at 10, 12, 14, 16, 18,
and 20 ms. No source, binary, RF, detector, queue, scheduler, or traffic setting
changes.

## Derivation

CP3497 records PRACH request indices 458, 465, 472, 479, 486, 493, and 500 for
the seven UE Msg1 attempts: the exact stride is 7. With a six-entry sequence,
each attempt advances one phase because `7 mod 6 = 1`; therefore the first six
attempts cover all six delays regardless of the run-start index. The seventh
repeats the first phase.

The bank covers the historical same-run PRACH displacement range of 11.806 to
18.915 ms and includes the intermittently successful 16 ms class. It excludes
22 and 24 ms, both measured floor-class in CP3497. CP3501's six consecutive
energetic fixed-16 ms windows and CP3514's two accepts support a run-scale phase
model, while CP3516 proves 16 ms is not stable across runs.

This is bounded per-occasion calibration, not another static-delay retry and
not a widened per-sample scan.

## Proofs

- Sequence samples are `153600,184320,215040,245760,276480,307200`; each is an

- `gcd(request_stride=7, phase_count=6)=1`; coverage is six attempts.
- The controller reuses the hash-pinned CP3515 combined bounded/nonzero chain,
  Stage6 wrapper, UE wrapper, source, and plugin.
- Python/shell syntax, controller `23/30`, context=1, reopen=1, nonzero=1,
  bounded=1, six-phase validation, and default-closed rc=2 pass.
- No RF was used.
