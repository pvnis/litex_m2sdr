# CP3524 - Timestamp-only non-PRACH offset rejected

## Verdict

**VALID NEGATIVE FOR THE IMPLEMENTED TIMESTAMP-ONLY POLICY.** The complete propagation and access gates pass, but the gNB decodes `0/30` PUSCH rows. Applying `-21333` samples only to the first timed continuation anchor does not recover Msg3/PUSCH at 15 MHz.

This result does **not** reproduce or reject the full CP3451 transport shape. CP3451 presented every high-peak PUSCH as an independently timed SOB+EOB burst and applied the offset on every PUSCH. CP3524 shifted one anchor, then transmitted the remaining 17 real PUSCH writes as untimed continuations. That independent-burst behavior is the sole remaining transport differential and must be gated separately before it receives a verdict.

## Propagation And Separation

- UE launch and result environments both contain `PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_NON_PRACH_TIME_OFFSET_SAMPLES=-21333`.
- Seven PRACH rows apply only the existing PRACH offset; all return status `0`.
- One timed non-PRACH continuation anchor applies only the new offset and returns status `0`.
- Seventeen later real PUSCH writes apply neither offset because their continuation metadata is untimed.
- The shifted anchor has only `621` samples of TX-domain lead after the negative offset.

The policy therefore reaches runtime exactly as implemented. Its limited scope, not propagation failure, explains why it does not recreate CP3451.

## Access And Health

- Cell search: `11.328 dB`, CFO `-3604.223 Hz`; SFN and SIB1 pass.
- UE: seven Msg1 transmissions, six accepted stage-1 selections, six decoded RARs, six Msg3 preparations, and 18 PUSCH encodes.
- gNB: six RAR allocations/PDUs and 30 PUSCH decoder rows.
- TX health: zero timeout, downlink-late, hard-error, UE-TX-failure, and final stream-deficit events.
- One fully recovered partial return leaves a `430`-sample data-write accounting deficit, the established clean-class caveat.

## PUSCH Result

- CRC: `0/30`.
- SINR: `-29.773670` to `-13.008075 dB`.
- EPRE: `-68.598152` to `-59.662956 dB`.
- Time alignment: `-2.537537` to `+2.155558 us`.
- Dynamic gNB receive offset: `-195083` samples for all six Msg3 generations.

This is a stable, healthy, access-valid negative. Do not retry the timestamp-only policy unchanged and do not change its sign without new evidence.

## Next Gate

If the remaining CP3451 differential is pursued, implement it as a separate default-off diagnostic policy: each high-peak non-PRACH PUSCH is one timed SOB+EOB burst with the existing `-21333` offset. Preserve PRACH, gNB receive timing, RF, thresholds, and scheduler settings. One controlled run is sufficient because CP3501 already shows that legacy burst leakage was not universally successful.
