# CHECKPOINT 3532 - Collection-relative edge calibration confirmed

## Verdict

**PASS.** The CP3528 diagnosis is confirmed on air. With the final launcher
actually carrying `edge_calibration=-245755`, the first accepted PRACH edge
published the exact collection-relative non-PRACH receive offset, Msg3 decoded,
PUSCH recovered to `95/128` CRC OK, and both 45-second user-plane directions
completed.

The earlier 15 MHz uplink collapse was caused by retaining the calibration for
the old `199472`-sample PRACH collection delay after the delay changed to
`245760`. That imposed an exact `46288`-sample bias on every non-PRACH receive
offset. CP3532 removes that bias.

## Evidence

- Execution summary and live gNB config both report calibration `-245755`.
- The accepted edge event reports edge `-3136`, RAR TA `96`, scheduler error
  `0`, and published offset `-242523` samples.
- The deployed formula is exact:
  `-245755 - (-3136) - 0 + 96 = -242523`.
- Access: cell selected at `9.467 dB`, two Msg1 transmissions, one stage-1
  selection, one gNB RAR, one UE-accepted RAR, and one prepared Msg3.
- PUSCH: `95/128` CRC OK (`74.22%`), successful median SINR `12.191 dB`, median
  TBS `528 B`, and successful timing alignment from `0.010` to `1.170 us`.
- Sustained private user plane: DL `2.492 Mbit/s`, UL `0.203 Mbit/s`, 45 seconds
  each direction.
- Health: zero Soapy TX timeouts, zero downlink-late events, zero TX errors,
  zero final stream deficit. One 430-sample partial return was recovered.

## Interpretation

This is causal evidence, not merely another passing roll: CP3530 exercised the
same access shape with the stale calibration and produced `0/30` PUSCH at floor
EPRE; CP3532 propagated the derived value and restored sustained PUSCH/data.

Two UE-side diagnostics remain active: independent non-PRACH bursts and a
`-21333`-sample non-PRACH timestamp offset. Their necessity is not established.
They must be removed one at a time before this becomes the minimal 15 MHz POC.
The first removal is the timestamp offset, because independent bursts ensure
that a zero-offset result applies consistently to every PUSCH opportunity.
