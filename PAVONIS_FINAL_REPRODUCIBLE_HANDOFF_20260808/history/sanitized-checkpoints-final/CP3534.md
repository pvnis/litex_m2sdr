declare non-PRACH timestamp offset `0`, but the live UE environment and TX
metadata still carry `-21333`. The CP3525 Stage6 terminal is the final overwrite:
it hardcodes `-21333` in both its environment prefix and UE launch command.

The run is a valid old-fixture access/data control, not evidence that zero offset
works.
