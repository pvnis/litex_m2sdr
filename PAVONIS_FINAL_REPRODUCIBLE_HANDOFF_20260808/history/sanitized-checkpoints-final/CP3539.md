# CHECKPOINT 3539 - Exact UE-to-gNB compensation transfer no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS (no RF).** The required `21333`-sample relative displacement is moved
from UE TX into the gNB non-PRACH receive calibration as one exact derived
change. UE offset remains `0`; independent bursts remain enabled.

## Derivation

The lower PHY invokes shifted non-PRACH processing with
`timestamp + active_offset`. Removing the UE's `-21333` advance makes PUSCH
arrive `21333` samples later. Making the gNB offset `21333` more negative labels
those samples at the prior nominal time:

`-245755 - 21333 = -267088` samples.

This is an ownership transfer of an already-proven displacement, not a value
sweep. The corresponding collection-delay invariant becomes
`-267088 + 245760 = -21328` samples.

## Evidence

- Launcher differs from CP3531 only by calibration `-245755 -> -267088` and
  two telemetry labels.
- Controller differs from CP3537 only in calibration summary/environment fields
  and labels.
- The CP3537 chain and CP3535 zero-offset/burst-only Stage6 are byte-exact.
- Runner changes only controller/launcher paths and hashes, fresh identifiers,
  port, and gate names.
- Role-local launcher hash matches, and built-in validation prints calibration
  `-267088` with the same pinned base/config hashes.
- Controller validation prints UE offset `0`, burst-only `1`, calibration
  `-267088`, fixed delay `245760`, effective invariant `-21328`.
- RF defaults closed and returns `2` without approval.
