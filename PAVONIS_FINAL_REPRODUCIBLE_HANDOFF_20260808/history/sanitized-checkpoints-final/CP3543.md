# CHECKPOINT 3543 - independent timed bursts remain required

## Verdict

**VALID NEGATIVE.** Removing independent timed SOB+EOB metadata from
non-PRACH uplink writes preserves cell acquisition, Msg1 selection, RAR, and
Msg3 production, but collapses PUSCH decoding to `0/30` CRC OK. Independent
timed non-PRACH bursts remain a known transport workaround in the current
bladeRF/srsUE path.

The earlier ownership cleanup remains valid: the UE timestamp offset stays at
`0`, and the gNB remains the sole owner of the measured uplink displacement.

## Evidence

This is not the clean zero-RACH scatter seen in CP3542:

- Cell selection is strong at `11.033 dB` with CFO `-3918.07 Hz`.
- Seven Msg1 transmissions produce six stage-1 selections, six gNB RARs, six
  UE RARs, and six Msg3 transmissions.
- Runtime propagation proves UE offset `0` and burst-only `0`; 41 TX metadata
  rows carry offset `0`, no row applies an offset, and the seven forced
  SOB+EOB rows are PRACH rather than non-PRACH traffic.
- The gNB uses calibration `-267088`; all six dynamic publications satisfy
  the configured calibration/edge/TA formula exactly.
- PUSCH produces `0/30` CRC OK. EPRE spans `-73.620` to `-64.736 dB`, SINR
  spans `-43.244` to `-13.724 dB`, and time alignment spans `-1.928` to
  `+2.272 us`.
- Runtime health is clean: zero Soapy TX timeouts, downlink-late events, TX
  errors, or final stream deficit. One 430-sample partial write is recovered.

## Controlled comparison

CP3540 and CP3543 share UE timestamp offset `0` and gNB calibration `-267088`.
CP3540 retains independent timed bursts and reaches `74/128` PUSCH CRC plus
sustained bidirectional data. CP3543 removes only that burst policy and reaches
the same access stage but `0/30` PUSCH CRC. The contrast rejects fixture
removal under the current transport implementation.

## Interpretation

The physical displacement now has one coherent owner and no longer requires
the UE timestamp workaround. What remains is a separate timed-transport defect:
standard continuation streaming does not preserve the placement/coherence of
reactive non-PRACH uplink traffic well enough for PUSCH, while independently
timed bursts do. Keep the burst policy enabled and label it as a known
workaround rather than folding it into the timing calibration.

Do not repeat the burst-disabled live test. The next source-level cleanup is to
derive gNB calibration from the actual collection delay and the retained
effective invariant instead of hard-coding a value tied to `245760` samples.
