# CHECKPOINT 3545 - residual downlink margin campaign opened

## Verdict

**HONEST NO-RF BOUNDARY.** Existing balanced-MCS data are sufficient to bound
the MCS9/MCS10 margin difference and reject a hard 16QAM transport defect, but
they are not sufficient to localize the small residual penalty to the M2SDR
transmitter, receiver estimation, or the measurement model.

No RF, source, configuration, bandwidth, MIMO, power, or producer setting was
changed.

## Quantitative boundary

- Offline equal-TBS AWGN MCS10 penalty: `1.500 dB`.
- Live non-floor run-adjusted penalty: `2.098 dB`.
- Live 95% interval: `0.900` to `3.295 dB`.
- Residual live-minus-AWGN point estimate: `0.598 dB`.
- Residual 95% interval: `-0.600` to `1.795 dB`.
- Equal-geometry MCS10 RV0 yield rises from `43.10%` at the ATT5 control to
  `80.95%` at ATT0.

The residual interval crosses zero. Existing measurements do not establish an
additional hardware impairment beyond the decoder's known margin requirement,
though they permit up to roughly `1.8 dB` at the upper confidence bound.

## Measurement provenance

The live `snr_db` field is a receiver-side PDSCH DMRS-residual estimate, not an
injected or transmitter-referenced SNR. The legacy EVM helper regenerates its
reference from the receiver's hard decisions and therefore cannot identify TX
distortion. The AWGN control is a perfect-channel-estimate decoder threshold
test. CP3477, CP3478, and CP3481 all have strict-clean TX-health summaries, so
producer loss is not a discriminator within this dataset.

## Saved-artifact audit

Four representative CP3477/CP3481 role archives were inventoried without
extracting raw logs. They contain 49/46 gNB-side files and 24/24 UE-side files.
The IQ-like payloads are only PUCCH grids and UE transmit-debug waveforms:

- downlink PDSCH IQ captures: `0`;
- pre-DMA, post-DMA, or digital-TX reference captures: `0`;
- known-symbol TX/RX PDSCH pairs: `0`.

The archives therefore cannot support a transmitter-referenced EVM or
phase-error join for the balanced MCS9/MCS10 opportunities. Reprocessing the
same DMRS-residual rows would only restate CP3479's model.

## Decision

The historical phrase "unexplained downlink ceiling" is now split into two
claims:

1. The user-visible one-layer ceiling and the MCS9/MCS10 transition are
   explained as capacity plus finite margin; the hard mapping/transport theory
   remains closed.
2. The physical source of a possible small residual margin penalty is not
   localized, and the saved artifacts cannot resolve it.

If that residual is worth another RF roll, the next gate must first design a
bounded known-symbol PDSCH capture with a transmitter-side reference and a
same-opportunity receiver observation. It must not reopen power, bandwidth,
MIMO, or producer sweeps. Otherwise this branch should close as a bounded
negative and the campaign should proceed using robust MCS9.
