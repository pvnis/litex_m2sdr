# CP3547 - PDSCH stage discriminator no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS.** The CP3546 same-slot collectors are integrated into the exact CP3477
10 MHz, one-layer, attenuation-5 MCS9/MCS10 hardware shape. The run is
hash-pinned, bounded, transactionally restores the temporary UE preparation
shim, and remains closed without explicit RF approval. No SDR stream was
opened and no RF was transmitted in this checkpoint.

## Isolation

The radio and scheduler shape is unchanged from CP3477: 11 ms transmit lead,
100 us inside-guard deadline retry, PRACH compensation `126564`, non-PRACH
edge calibration `-126560`, uplink MCS19, 23 Stage-1 candidates, four 45-second
TCP streams, and the alternating new-data MCS9/MCS10 selector.

The only additions are the default-off CP3546 collectors. Each role writes at
most 16 selected C-RNTI RV0 `1089`-byte records after collection completes;
the producer-side PHY callback does no file I/O. The gNB records the completed
upper-PHY resource grid immediately before the gateway. The UE records the
same slot's FFT grid, extracted PDSCH RE, equalized RE, and channel estimates.
