# CP3548 - M2SDR PDSCH stage discriminator

## Verdict

**PASS: the unexplained downlink ceiling is localized below the gNB upper-PHY
resource grid and is already present in the UE raw FFT/PDSCH samples.** It is
not created by the gNB modulation mapper, MCS selector, UE equalizer, or
decoder. The remaining unresolved span is the gNB lower PHY through the radio
transport and RF path to the bladeRF receive front end and UE FFT presentation.

This is a stage-localization pass, not a throughput pass. The same run measured
only `0.250469 Mbit/s` downlink and `2.651179 Mbit/s` uplink over four parallel
45-second TCP streams.

## Run Validity

The RF shape is the frozen CP3477 10 MHz FDD, one-layer, attenuation-5
MCS9/MCS10 discriminator. Cell selection passed at `11.489 dB` and
`-3710.276 Hz`; uplink health was `93/128` PUSCH CRC OK. Soapy transmit
timeouts and downlink-late events were both zero. One partial return left a
`480`-sample write deficit across roughly `4.8` billion returned samples, with
zero stream deficit; this is retained as a strict-health caveat but cannot
explain the repeated slot-scale state split.

The inherited radio runner returned `1` because its UE wait reached the
wrapper timeout and radio-role packaging returned `1` after the data session.
Both traffic endpoint results were complete, both archives were fetched and
parsed, all seven bounded capture files were fetched, postchecks passed, and
the temporary preparation helper was restored. The RF data is therefore valid
while the aggregate controller verdict remains honestly nonzero.

The first launch is a no-RF setup void: its runner created the capture
subdirectory before transferring result-directory ownership to the controller.
The append-only v2 runner moves that creation after controller return, passes
syntax/controller/default-closed checks, and is SHA-256
`385191288f1c27c1bc5da3559025bd246fb32a77e04cd11f8ca5e44284e25bb0`.

## Positive Control

The offline extractor assumes the exact observed full-allocation geometry:
52 PRBs, symbols 2 through 13, Type-A `pos2` DMRS on symbols 2, 7, and 11,
and 6552 PDSCH data RE. It tests both DMRS comb deltas. Applied first to the
CP3546 ideal ZMQ capture, all 11 matching slots decode and fit at effectively
unity raw/CE correlation; median equalized EVM is at most `4.6551e-5`. The
mapping is therefore validated independently of the hardware result.

## Same-Slot Result

All 16 gNB rows and 16 UE rows join by system slot. Fifteen have the exact
6552-RE geometry; one MCS9 CRC-KO slot contains 6292 RE because of an extra
reserved-RE pattern and is excluded from the exact extractor, not hidden.

The completed gNB upper-PHY grids are clean. Every supported slot is finite,
the modulation metadata matches, and the transmitted data constellations have
zero mismatch and zero measured identity EVM. QPSK component magnitude is
constant at `0.353515625`; the 16QAM low component is constant at
`0.158203125`. The source grid neither weakens nor changes shape when the UE
falls to the floor state.

The receiver has two temporal states:

- High raw-fit: six records, median raw+CE fit `4.527 dB`, median equalized
  fit `3.691 dB`, raw RMS `4.131`, and channel magnitude `3.105`. All three
  MCS9 records decode; all three MCS10 records fail.
- Floor raw-fit: nine records, median raw+CE fit `1.159 dB`, median equalized
  fit `0.390 dB`, raw RMS `1.240`, and channel magnitude `0.869`. All nine
  records fail, including three MCS9 and six MCS10.

Overall yield is MCS9 `3/7` and MCS10 `0/9`. Equalization adds only about
`0.77-0.84 dB` penalty after the raw state has already separated. The
approximately `10.5 dB` raw-RMS and `11.1 dB` channel-magnitude collapse is
visible before equalization and recovers later in the same capture. This is
the first direct same-slot proof of the campaign's two-state downlink behavior.

## Decision

Close gNB upper-PHY mapping and UE equalization/decoder logic as the origin of
the unexplained ceiling. Do not move to bandwidth, MIMO, power brackets, or
producer-stall headroom yet. Bank this result in a new append-only final report,
then open a below-upper-PHY branch that adds the next bounded same-slot
transmit reference after OFDM/quantization or at the Soapy handoff. That branch
must separate lower-PHY generation from transport/gateware/RF/receive effects
without full-rate append or hot-path file I/O.
