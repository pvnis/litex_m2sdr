# Checkpoint 3554: Soapy Accepted-Stream Hardware Discriminator

## Verdict

**PASS_STAGE_LOCALIZATION.** In the valid bounded hardware roll, every one of
the `737280` CF32 samples positively accepted by OCUDU's Soapy `writeStream`
boundary is bit-for-bit equal to the corresponding CP3552-class lower-PHY CI16
waveform after the defined `1/32767` conversion. There are zero timeline gaps,
overlaps, carry rows, file-offset errors, or nonzero sample differences.

The completed upper grid also reconstructs through the accepted CF32 stream at
minimum correlation `0.9999999977202487` and maximum EVM
`0.00006752409074840686`, while all 15 exact UE FFT records remain CRC KO with
only `2.44-2.98 dB` median raw-fit SNR by MCS class. Therefore the unexplained
downlink ceiling is below OCUDU's Soapy write-acceptance boundary. The next
unproven boundary is the LiteX M2SDR plugin's CF32-to-SC12 interleave/DMA
buffer, followed by driver/gateware/RF and bladeRF receive.

Bandwidth, MIMO, power and producer-stall headroom remain locked.

## Run Health

The frozen 10 MHz, 1x1, ATT5 MCS9/10 shape selected the cell at `10.702 dB`
with CFO `-3735.643 Hz`. Uplink produced 36/66 CRC-OK PUSCH rows with median
successful SINR `5.451 dB`. Soapy TX timeouts and downlink-late events were both
zero, so the new collector did not move realtime health out of class.

The controller returned 1 honestly: the core attempted `440192` downlink bytes,
the UE received zero, and the duration gate failed. Capture fetch and UE-helper
restore both passed. That makes the traffic verdict a failure but does not void
the stage-localization evidence. One root-owned stale srsUE remained after
teardown, as in CP3552; it was removed by exact process identity and the helper
was verified byte-restored. Both hosts ended idle.
