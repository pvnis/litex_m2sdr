# Checkpoint 3556: M2SDR Post-Interleave Hardware Discriminator

## Verdict

**PASS_STAGE_LOCALIZATION.** The finalized LiteX M2SDR zero-copy DMA payload is
clean. After sequence-domain alignment, all `737280` accepted samples match the
plugin's defined SC12 quantization within one integer LSB per I/Q component.
The recovered DMA waveform reconstructs 15 completed upper-PHY grids at
minimum correlation `0.[subscriber-redacted]` and maximum EVM
`0.0015239092122199226`. The unused physical channel is identically zero.

The unexplained downlink ceiling is therefore below finalized zero-copy ring
publication, or on the bladeRF/srsUE receive side. OCUDU generation,
lower-PHY OFDM/CI16, Soapy CF32 conversion/acceptance, LiteX interleave,
framing and userspace DMA-ring content are now exonerated by same-run samples.

Bandwidth, MIMO, power and producer-stall headroom remain locked.

## Run Health

The frozen 10 MHz, 1x1, ATT5 MCS9/10 roll selected the cell at `4.453 dB` with
CFO `-3964.316 Hz`. Uplink produced 38/68 CRC-OK PUSCH rows with median
successful SINR `5.469 dB`. Soapy TX timeout and downlink-late counts were
both zero, so the DMA collector stayed in the native realtime-health class.

The controller returned 1 honestly: the core attempted `440192` downlink
bytes, the UE received zero, and duration failed. All 13 capture files fetched
and the UE helper restored. One root-owned srsUE remained after teardown and
was removed by exact process identity; both hosts ended idle. The stage result
is valid even though the traffic result failed.

## DMA Geometry And Timing

The collector saved 768 consecutive 8192-byte zero-copy frames. Every frame
has 1022 logical samples, two physical frame channels, four bytes per complex
sample, the expected sync header, flags zero, and header timestamp zero under
CP2074B contiguous-untimed continuation. Sequence, handle and file offsets are
all contiguous.

The retained `timeNs` metadata is not a reliable payload index for these
untimed frames: it reports 2948 total gap samples and 2930 overlap samples.
Their net is exactly +18 samples. Correspondingly, metadata predicts the
accepted stream at DMA offset 278, while payload correlation finds offset 260,
a residual of -18. Once aligned by payload sequence, correlation is
`0.9999992167849133` and every component differs by at most one SC12 LSB. This
closes the apparent timing mismatch as metadata bookkeeping, not waveform
corruption.
