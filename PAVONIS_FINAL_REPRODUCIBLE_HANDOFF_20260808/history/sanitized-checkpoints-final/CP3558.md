## Offline result

The analyzer reproduces the online grid from the captured FFT input with
minimum correlation `0.9999999999948446` and maximum EVM
`3.2112008e-6`. This validates both the capture and the source-derived OFDM
geometry.

Cyclic-prefix analysis sees median residual CFO `-37.459 Hz`. A bounded search
over CP-derived timing candidates and residual CFO improves the same-slot
known-grid score by only `0.011197 dB` median and `0.019710 dB` maximum. The
best timing values span only `-12..+11` samples and the best CFO values
`-31.713..0 Hz`. There is no hidden timing/CFO correction capable of restoring
the downlink.

Nominal and optimized known-grid scores are `4.896093` and `4.903093 dB`
median respectively. The degraded online grid is therefore a faithful FFT of
an already-degraded worker input, not an online processing artifact.
