# CP3559 - bladeRF-to-worker source audit, no RF

Timestamp: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS. The receive/synchronization handoff after `bladerf_sync_rx()` cannot
account for the CP3558 loss. The degraded signal is already present in the
SC16 samples returned by bladeRF, or earlier in the M2SDR/RF/air path.

No source was changed and no RF was used.

## Path audit

`rf_blade_recv_with_time()` receives interleaved SC16 directly into
`handler->rx_buffer`, then `srsran_vec_convert_if()` performs a fixed `/[date-redacted]`
conversion into the caller's CF32 buffer. `slot_sync::recv_callback()` passes
that storage directly to `ue_sync_nr`; there is no resampler or hidden filter.

`ue_sync_nr_recv()` can discard a positive sample offset or prepend zeros for a
negative offset, then performs an in-place CFO phase rotation. `work_dl()` may
perform a second in-place worker-CFO phase rotation before the FFT. The two CFO
operations are unit-magnitude and cannot lower SNR by themselves.

Across CP3558's sixteen selected buffers, only `5/184320` samples are exact
complex zero (`0.0027%`), excluding material zero-prefix corruption. A discard
or alignment error large enough to cause the observed ceiling is independently
excluded by CP3558's `-128..+128` timing search: best timing stays within
`-12..+11` samples and timing/CFO optimization gains only `0.011 dB` median,
`0.020 dB` maximum.

The current `PAVONIS_RF_BLADE_RX_DUMP` was deliberately not enabled. It calls
`fwrite` and `fprintf` from the receive path for each captured call, violating
the permanent no-hot-path-file-IO rule and adding no information after the
source/data proof above.
