# CP3561 - gain-corrected exact live-payload replay

Timestamp: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

VALID CONTROL; THE DOWNLINK CEILING PERSISTS. Raising only the independent
bladeRF capture gain from 24 to 40 corrected the under-driven CP3560
instrument point without clipping, but improved the exact known-grid score by
only `0.162 dB`. Continuous helper replay remains only `0.998 dB` above the
live CP3558 result.

This closes bladeRF capture gain and a large live descriptor-cadence penalty as
explanations for the current ceiling. It does not yet distinguish M2SDR
TX/gateware/RF quality from the inter-device air/bladeRF receive path.

## Run

The run repeated CP3560 byte-for-byte except for capture gain `24 -> 40`.
The helper continuously replayed the exact CP3558 post-OFDM payload at
`11.52 Msps`, `10 MHz`, `1842500000 Hz`, attenuation `5 dB`, 90 loops, and
1022-sample chunks. The payload SHA-256 is
`cd29826fafb5d4e61914653e30e54fd36e47788a26987af05aa03f024ae47722`.

The bladeRF captured 11.52 million samples at `1842506000 Hz`, `9.216 MHz`,
gain 40, and the normal filter. Replay, capture, transfer, correlation,
process checks, and cleanup all returned zero. No capture gaps, overruns,
underruns, or clips were reported.

## Results

- Capture level: `-18.203 dBFS`, max component `1417`, zero samples at or
  above the Q11 clipping threshold.
- Raw waveform correlation: `0.833394` at `-4000 Hz`.
- Exact selected-slot analysis: 16 records, best base delta `+26` samples and
  CFO `-3975 Hz`.
- Gain-40 replay score: median `5.894070 dB`, range
  `5.429165..7.652377 dB`.
- Gain-24 CP3560 replay score: median `5.731890 dB`.
- Gain correction: roughly `+15.75 dB` in raw level but only `+0.162180 dB`
  in known-grid score.
- CP3558 live score: median `4.896093 dB`; replay-minus-live is
  `+0.997977 dB`.

The generic PBCH result remains irrelevant because this is a connected-data
payload, not an SSB-bearing sequence.

## Frontier

The continuous replay does not restore a several-dB margin, even at a correct
capture level. The next bounded discriminator is a same-window comparison of
M2SDR self-RX and bladeRF receive against this exact replay. A clean self-RX
with degraded bladeRF receive places the ceiling in the air/bladeRF leg;
matching degradation in self-RX places it at or below M2SDR transmit/RF.

Bandwidth, MIMO, power and producer-stall headroom remain locked until that
physical-waveform split is closed.
