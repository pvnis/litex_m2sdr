# CP3563 - overlap void; M2SDR self-RX positive

Timestamp: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

VOID FOR THE PAIRED PHYSICAL-PATH SPLIT. The captures and analyses completed,
but corrected wall timestamps prove only `121.036 ms` of common capture time,
below the predeclared `500 ms` gate. A carriage return in the SSH log caused
the shell arithmetic to fail and the original wrapper to record a false
four-second overlap.

The M2SDR self-RX result is independently valid and strong. The bladeRF result
is retained as edge-window evidence only, not as a receiver comparison.

## Execution

Replay, self-RX, bladeRF capture, transfers, analyses, and cleanup all returned
zero. M2SDR transmitted the exact CP3558 payload at the frozen 11.52 Msps,
10 MHz, attenuation-5 shape. Its self-RX captured four seconds at the proven
split-frequency geometry. bladeRF captured two continuous seconds with zero
timestamp gaps, overruns, or underruns.

The M2SDR helper needed roughly five seconds to open and activate its streams.
Because the controller started bladeRF after a fixed one-second delay, nearly
all of the external capture preceded active helper TX/self-RX.

## Corrected overlap

- M2SDR self-RX start: `1785630847261954462 ns`.
- bladeRF capture end: `1785630847382990277 ns`.
- Actual overlap: `121035815 ns` (`121.036 ms`).
- Required overlap: `500000000 ns`.
- Gate: FAIL.

The bladeRF payload alignment begins about 64 ms after M2SDR self-RX starts,
leaving less than one complete 64 ms replay period before bladeRF capture ends.
That is consistent with only 14 analyzable records and makes its score
non-comparable to CP3561.

## Results

M2SDR self-RX is clean: waveform correlation `0.791810` at the expected
`+2.85 MHz`, 16 grid records, and median `18.747595 dB`. TX reports zero
timeouts and errors; RX reports one overflow across 46.08 million samples.

The edge-only bladeRF data has waveform correlation `0.329499`, 14 records,
and median `-5.745780 dB`. It also contains 732 near-clipped components out of
46.08 million scalar components. None of those values is promoted to a path
verdict because the overlap gate failed.
