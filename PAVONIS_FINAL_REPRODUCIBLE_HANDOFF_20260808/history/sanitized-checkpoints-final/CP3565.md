# CP3565 - valid M2SDR self-RX versus bladeRF split

Timestamp: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

VALID SAME-WINDOW DISCRIMINATOR. M2SDR self-RX receives the exact payload at
`19.537 dB` median known-grid quality while bladeRF receives the same interval
at `-5.771 dB`, a `25.308 dB` split. Corrected overlap is `1.411682351 s`,
well above the predeclared 500 ms gate.

This localizes the degradation after the M2SDR self-RX observation point for
this helper runtime. It does not yet distinguish the external RF path/bladeRF
receiver from an observer or stream-shape effect introduced by running the
CP1395 simultaneous TX/self-RX helper.

## Execution

The CP3564 controller waited for M2SDR `streams_activated` before launching
the prebuilt bladeRF capture. Replay, both captures, transfers, exact-payload
correlations, 16-grid analyses, and cleanup returned zero.

M2SDR TX reports zero timeouts and errors. M2SDR RX reports ten overflows over
46.08 million samples, but every selected record remains in the
`18.13..20.44 dB` class. bladeRF captured 23.04 million continuous samples
with zero timestamp gaps, overruns, underruns, or clips.

The external capture is unclipped (`max 619`) and its raw RMS is

the 25.3 dB same-window quality split.

## Caveat and next branch

CP3561's TX-only CS16 continuous helper gave bladeRF `5.894 dB`, whereas this
CF32 TX/self-RX helper gives `-5.771 dB`, an `11.665 dB` regression. The
present result proves a post-self-RX split but does not prove that bladeRF
alone caused it.

Stay no-RF first and score every repeated 64 ms payload period in CP3561 and
CP3565 to determine whether the external degradation is stable or onset-
related. Then run one matched TX-only CF32/chunk-510 bladeRF control if needed.
That separates simultaneous-RX observer behavior from the external path.

Bandwidth, MIMO, power and producer-stall headroom remain locked.
