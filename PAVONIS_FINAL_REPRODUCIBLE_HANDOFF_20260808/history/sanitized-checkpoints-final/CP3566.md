# CP3566 - replay-period stability, no RF

Timestamp: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

The CP3561-versus-CP3565 external quality gap is stable across complete replay
periods. It is not caused by startup, one bad alignment, or choosing an edge
period.

No RF was transmitted and no source or runtime state was changed.

## Method

The exact CP3558 payload repeats every 737280 samples, or 64 ms at 11.52 Msps.
For each saved bladeRF capture, the analysis took the correlation-derived
period phase, fixed the run's accepted timing/CFO solution, and scored all 16
known gNB grids in every complete period inside the proven active interval.

CP3561 contributes 15 complete periods. CP3565 contributes 21 complete periods
before M2SDR self-RX/TX ended; the post-TX tail is excluded.

## Results

| Capture | Periods | Median | Period range | Stddev |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| CP3561 TX-only CS16 | 15 | `5.724919 dB` | `5.473..5.894 dB` | `0.135 dB` |
| CP3565 CF32 + self-RX | 21 | `-5.915797 dB` | `-6.066..-5.771 dB` | `0.061 dB` |

The median period gap is `11.640716 dB`. Both classes are internally stable,
so CP3565's external degradation is a repeatable runtime-shape effect.

## Source audit

CP1395 takes the minimum of TX MTU 1022 and RX MTU 510, so simultaneous
TX/self-RX uses 510-sample CF32 writes. The established CP848 TX-only CF32
helper reads fixed [date-redacted]-sample chunks. It cannot serve as a matched one-variable
control without a new diagnostic helper.

## Next gate

Build a standalone, default-closed CF32 TX-only helper that uses the same
510-sample cyclic writes, payload, channel, RF geometry, attenuation, and
duration as CP3565. Capture only bladeRF. Recovery toward CP3561 isolates
simultaneous self-RX as the observer; remaining near `-5.9 dB` isolates the
CF32/chunk-510 transmit shape.

Bandwidth, MIMO, power and producer-stall headroom remain locked.
