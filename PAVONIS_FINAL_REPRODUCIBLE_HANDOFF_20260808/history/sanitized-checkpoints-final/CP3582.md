# CP3582: Corrected helper reproduces the external downlink ceiling

Date: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS. With active attenuation mechanically held at `5 dB`, the same-window
M2SDR self-RX remains clean while bladeRF reproduces the corrected independent
helper class. The live OCUDU record is only about `0.91 dB` below that external
best-helper measurement. The unexplained live-production ceiling is therefore
bounded to approximately 1 dB; the dominant remaining loss is in the external
RF/bladeRF receive margin relative to the M2SDR self-RX observation, not in
OCUDU waveform production, Soapy acceptance, or finalized DMA payload.

## Validity and health

Measured simultaneous overlap was `1.382506065 s`, above the `500 ms` gate.
All four lifecycle events passed: setup readbacks were `20->5 dB` for TX and
RX setup, followed by `5->5 dB` after both activations.

TX accepted `46,595,130` samples in `91,363` full writes with zero timeout or
error. Self-RX captured its full `46,080,000` samples with zero timeout/error
and two overflow notifications. Those notifications do not invalidate the
result: all 20 overlap periods are present and stable, and exact waveform
correlation is `0.806228399`.

The bladeRF capture completed normally with raw waveform correlation
`0.737008256` at `-4 kHz`.

The same-window difference is `16.229 dB`. That number is an observation-point
and receiver-path difference, not a claim of 16 dB radiated power loss: the
self-RX and external bladeRF paths have different coupling, front ends, gains,
and noise figures. What matters for production localization is that both are
stable and the external leg matches independent controls.

## Cross-run closure

The corrected bladeRF period median is:

- only `+0.062 dB` from CP3578's independent corrected helper (`+5.744 dB`);
- `+0.910 dB` over CP3558's live OCUDU selected-grid median (`+4.896 dB`);
- `+11.577 dB` over CP3565's attenuation-confounded external leg.

Thus CP3578 and CP3582 independently reproduce the same external ceiling, and
the live production path lands within about 1 dB. There is no remaining
evidence for a large descriptor-cadence, CS16/SC12, chunking, boundary,
plugin, lower-PHY, Soapy-write, or finalized-DMA penalty.

The original CP3565 `25.308 dB` physical split is superseded. It combined a
high self-RX observation with an external leg transmitted under the reset
`20 dB` attenuation. The corrected same-window split is `16.229 dB` and must
be interpreted as external receiver/path margin, not host waveform damage.

defect and honestly bounded at about 1 dB residual versus an exact-payload
helper. The proven bandwidth and rank constraints now dominate capability.
After banking this result, unlock the throughput ladder in the requested order:
