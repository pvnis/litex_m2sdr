# CP3587 - 15 MHz reaches RAR; Msg3 timing root cause

## Verdict

**Valid 15 MHz RF/access result; void for throughput; Msg3 failure root-caused.**
The real-phone 15 MHz/15.36 Msps/1x1 cell starts with the intended runtime
helper and clean transmitter health. The gNB accepts two Msg1 occasions and
encodes two RAR grants, but receives no decodable PUSCH and no RRC setup. Target
activation times out, so sustained traffic never starts.

The failure is not cell acquisition, downlink supply, PRACH supply, producer
stall, or RF power. It is a deterministic sample-rate conversion error in the
non-PRACH uplink timing fixture.

## Runtime evidence

- Runtime Soapy readback is 15.36 Msps; the 15 MHz overlay and one-channel
  wrapper are hash-pinned and active.
- Runtime helper markers prove physical channel 1, attenuation 16 dB,
  PRACH timing 307484 samples, report-slot offset 0, and response window 40.
- Two PRACH selections use shift `64`, fractional shift `-0.5`, and 5 kHz
  recovery CFO. The gNB encodes two corresponding RAR grants.
- RAR timing commands convert to 744 and 736 samples.
- No PUSCH row, CRC-OK row, or RRC setup follows.
- The bounded Msg3 instrument captures two 14-symbol target groups, confirming
  that the gNB did schedule/listen for the post-RAR uplink.

Producer health is valid: 2,222,315,320 stream samples in 2,314,911 writes,
zero stream deficit, zero timeout/error/deadline break/downlink-late/underflow/
time-error, and one 640-sample partial fully recovered. The cumulative logical
write-deficit counter remains 640 while stream deficit is zero.

## Exact timing defect

The valid CP3121D 10 MHz phone run used:

- calibration `-237332` samples;
- lattice period `823`, anchor `-138`, canonical `-6722`;
- canonical index `-8`, since `-6722 = -138 - 8*823`;
- published non-PRACH offset `-230610` samples;
- 213 PUSCH CRC-OK results.

CP3584 scaled each lattice number independently. At 15 MHz that produced period
1097, anchor -184, and canonical -8963. But `-8963 - (-184) = -8779`, which is
not divisible by 1097. The source therefore disabled the canonical branch. At
the same time the calibration remained `-237332` instead of being converted
from 11.52 to 15.36 Msps.

The two CP3587 edge publications consequently used preliminary snapped edges
3107 and -184 and published offsets -239695 and -236412. Relative to the
time-equivalent 10 MHz offset (`-230610 * 4/3 = -307480`), the gNB listened
67,785 and 71,068 samples early: about 4.41 and 4.63 ms. That explains two
RARs followed by zero PUSCH without invoking a new RF theory.

## Corrected model

Preserve the lattice branch index instead of independently rounding its three
members:

- period: 1097 samples;
- anchor: -184 samples;
- canonical: `-184 - 8*1097 = -8960` samples;
- calibration: `round(-237332 * 4/3) = -316443` samples;
- active-stop geometry: `10404 * 4/3 = 13872` samples.

With CP3587's observed RAR TA values, the source formula predicts offsets
-306739 and -306747, only 8 samples apart. This is the specific next test. No
PRACH, RF, gain, scheduler, producer, kernel, FPGA, or gateware variable needs
to move.

## Next gate

Create a no-RF wrapper that changes only calibration, active-stop, and the
canonical value above; keep period/anchor and every CP3587 variable fixed.
Require runtime proof that the canonical branch is enabled and published before
judging Msg3. Then run one bounded phone retry, judged on PUSCH CRC, RRC/data,
and finally sustained throughput versus CP3175.
