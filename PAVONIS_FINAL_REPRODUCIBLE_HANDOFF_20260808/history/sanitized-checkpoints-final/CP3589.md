# CP3589 - Phone 15 MHz bandwidth payoff

## Verdict

**PASS.** The real phone attaches to the private 15 MHz / 15.36 Msps / 1x1
cell, exchanges bidirectional user data, and completes the sustained 45-second
benchmark in both directions. This is the first valid 15 MHz phone throughput
result and proves that channel width, rather than antenna geometry or the prior
MCS-ceiling sweep, was the material downlink lever.

Sustained throughput is:

- downlink: **12.594944 Mbit/s**;
- uplink: **0.260252 Mbit/s**.

Against the frozen CP3175 10 MHz phone baseline of 8.331264/0.124854 Mbit/s,
that is a 51.177% downlink gain and a 108.445% uplink gain. The downlink gain is
close to the 1.5x bandwidth ratio and establishes a useful, physically credible
15 MHz endpoint.

## Timing fix validation

The CP3587 root cause and CP3588 no-RF correction are validated exactly. Two
accepted PRACH selections produce RAR timing values of 744 and 736 samples.
Both publications use canonical edge `-8960` and produce non-PRACH offsets
`-306739` and `-306747`, only eight samples apart. The gNB then records 224
PUSCH CRC-OK and 32 CRC-KO results, target activation passes in 22.461 seconds,
and four bidirectional echo requests return before the sustained measurement.

This supersedes CP3587's 4.4 ms early Msg3 listening failure. No PRACH, RF
gain, scheduler, kernel, FPGA, or gateware change was needed.

## Producer health

The 15.36 Msps run delivers 2,583,336,760 stream samples in 2,690,975 writes.
One 610-sample partial return is fully recovered: the logical write-deficit
counter is 610 while stream deficit is zero. Timeout, error, deadline-break,
deadline-timeout, downlink-late, underflow, and time-error counts are all zero.
The result is therefore valid for bandwidth and not a producer-stall artifact.

## Interpretation

The phone downlink now reaches 12.595 Mbit/s on the same private OTA geometry.
This closes the 10 MHz width ceiling and establishes 15 MHz as the new proven
single-layer baseline. Uplink improves but remains far below downlink, so the
uplink starvation/quality branch remains open rather than being hidden by the
headline downlink result.

The next capability rung is MIMO/rank on top of this validated width endpoint,
followed by producer-stall/deadline headroom. The working 15 MHz 1x1 profile and
the CP3588 timing values must be preserved as the comparison baseline.
