# Checkpoint 3599: downlink-ceiling campaign opening

Date: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS, NO RF. A distinct downlink-ceiling campaign is open, with its scope
narrowed by the completed CP3472-CP3582 mechanism work. The campaign will not
repeat the old MCS sweep, power bracket, known-symbol localization, lower-PHY/
Soapy/DMA captures, or exact-helper/self-RX split.

The current 15 MHz phone endpoint is explicitly capped at PDSCH MCS9. Its
12.594944-12.651808 Mbit/s downlink results are close to the QPSK transport
limit, so the scheduler and traffic endpoint are not leaving a large gap at
that configured operating point. The open question is whether the current
post-localization phone path can now cross to MCS10/16QAM reliably, and then
whether MCS11 produces the expected real throughput gain.

## Prior mechanism evidence retained

- CP3472: RV2 recovered most higher-MCS failures and the ideal AWGN control
  measured an approximately 1.5 dB MCS10 penalty over MCS9. A hard mapping or
  CRC-mask defect was not supported.
- CP3477: same-run MCS9/MCS10 hardware A/B at attenuation 5 dB measured RV0
  yield 35.59% versus 19.05%, with the same direction in the equal-geometry
  subset.
- CP3481: changing only attenuation 5 dB to 0 dB raised equal-geometry MCS10
  yield from 43.10% to 80.95% and downlink by 3.70x. This closed a hard-16QAM
  theory and established a finite-margin boundary.
- CP3545: the adjusted live MCS10 penalty was 2.098 dB with a 0.900-3.295 dB
  confidence interval; subtracting the 1.5 dB AWGN requirement left a 0.598 dB
  residual whose confidence interval crossed zero.
- CP3548-CP3558: gNB upper PHY, lower-PHY OFDM/quantization, UE FFT execution,
  and the observed online/offline FFT presentation were progressively
  exonerated. Degradation was already present at the UE raw FFT input.
- CP3582: corrected live and exact-helper external receive quality differed by
  only about 1 dB. Same-window M2SDR self-RX exceeded the external bladeRF leg
  by 16.229 dB. The dominant prior mechanism was external RF/receiver margin,
  not a large live producer-cadence defect.

These results are constraints on the new campaign, not experiments to rerun.

## Current endpoint audit

The CP3595 generated base configuration contains `pdsch.max_ue_mcs: 9`.
Runtime evidence proves the active cell is 15 MHz/79 PRB despite the packaged
base file retaining its 10 MHz default; the wrapper supplies the bandwidth
override. No evidence shows a runtime override of the PDSCH MCS9 cap.

CP3595 passes full phone attach and data at 12.651808 Mbit/s downlink with
256/256 bounded uplink CRC-OK rows and exact transmitter delivery. Map 0 is
also exonerated. Therefore the present ceiling is first a configured MCS9
ceiling. Calling it a new hardware defect before crossing that cap would be
incorrect.

MCS9 and MCS10 have nearly equal nominal spectral efficiency in the active
table: QPSK at 679/1024 versus 16QAM at 340/1024. The earlier equal-resource
case produced the same 1089-byte TBS at both indices. An alternating MCS9/MCS10
test is consequently a modulation-robustness discriminator rather than a
throughput lever. MCS11 is the first follow-up expected to raise payload rate.

## Ordered experiment

1. No-RF gate the existing default-off `PAVONIS_DL_MCS_AB` selector on the
   frozen CP3595 15 MHz/1x1/map-0 phone shape. Use MCS9/MCS10, one-grant
   alternation, a bounded trace, and no source change.
2. Prove the generated config differs only in the diagnostic enablement and
   that the selector emits balanced MCS9/MCS10 rows with the same geometry/TBS
   in a no-radio scheduler control.
3. Run one bounded phone RF A/B. Judge attach/data and producer health first,
   then per-MCS newTx/HARQ outcome. Aggregate throughput is secondary because
   MCS9 and MCS10 have effectively equal spectral efficiency.
4. If MCS10 is same-class with MCS9, disable the A/B selector and gate a sole-
   variable PDSCH max11 phone run. That is the first throughput-payoff test.
5. If MCS10 remains materially worse, treat the old finite-margin result as
   reproduced on the current path. Do not sweep power. Use the smallest
   current-path discriminator justified by the missing observation, while
   retaining CP3548-CP3582's clean-stage boundaries.

Further bandwidth, CP3597 MIMO/Msg4, and producer-stall/deadline headroom remain
parked until this branch reaches a decision.
