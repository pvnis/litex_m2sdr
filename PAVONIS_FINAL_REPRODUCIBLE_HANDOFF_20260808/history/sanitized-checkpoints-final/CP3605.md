## Mechanical proof

The new final wrapper pins the already-tested CP3597 gNB binary and delegates
to the CP3594 map-0/timing wrapper. Deployed and effective-environment probes
show selector enabled, MCS A/B 11/11, period one, and a 512-row cap. The
inherited edge values remain -316443, 13872, 1097, -184, and -8960 with map 0.

The exact CP3604 base-config hash and the existing 15 MHz overlay hash both
match on [host-redacted]. The local sequence proof preserves the full CP3121/CP3584/
CP3594 lifecycle and changes only the MCS selector. Both runner and executor
return 2 while RF approval is closed, the closed executor creates no attempt,
and the correctly rooted immutable-baseline check passes.

Two superseded no-RF probes are retained honestly. OCUDU has no `--dry-run`
CLI option, so that invocation stopped at argument parsing. A subsequent check
incorrectly expected 15 MHz fields in the base YAML even though the wrapper
supplies them through the separately pinned overlay. Neither probe opened a
stream or transmitted. The replacement base-plus-overlay hash proof passes.

## RF acceptance

The single endpoint must prove one CP3605 wrapper marker, one selector startup
row, and 512 bounded newTx rows all at MCS11. Access, user data, sustained
throughput, PUSCH health, exact producer delivery, and zero timeout/error/
deadline/downlink-late class are required before judging the ceiling. Compare
primarily against CP3601 all-MCS9 at 12.652736 Mbit/s and CP3604 mixed MCS9/10
at 12.784640 Mbit/s.
