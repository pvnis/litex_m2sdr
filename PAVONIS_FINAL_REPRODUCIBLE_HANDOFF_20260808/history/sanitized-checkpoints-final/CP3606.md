# Checkpoint 3606: phone 15 MHz all-MCS11 OTA endpoint

Date: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS. All-MCS11 raises sustained downlink to **14.544768 Mbit/s** while the
frozen 15 MHz/1x1/map-0 endpoint remains healthy. This is +14.9535% over the
CP3601 all-MCS9 control and +13.7675% over the CP3604 mixed MCS9/MCS10 run.
The dominant TBS rises by +13.7213%, nearly identical to the measured gain.

The unexplained current-path MCS9 ceiling is therefore resolved as a
configuration ceiling, not an inability of the phone/M2SDR path to carry
16QAM at a useful rate.

## Runtime proof

The final wrapper marker appears once and the selector startup marker appears
once. All 512 bounded newTx rows select MCS11, split 256/256 across the two
identical selector branches. The dominant geometry is 394 rows at 79 PRBs,
3713-byte TBS, and one layer.

Target activation completes in 44.445 seconds. One RAR allocation is followed
by 256/256 PUSCH CRC-OK rows. Sustained uplink is 0.234131 Mbit/s; uplink is
not the variable under test and remains in its known low, variable envelope.

## Producer health

The final transmitter summary reports 2,890,721,080 delivered samples across
3,011,167 writes. One 460-sample partial return is fully recovered. Stream
deficit, timeouts, errors, deadline breaks/timeouts, and downlink-late rows are
all zero.

## Decision

MCS11 becomes the current proven 15 MHz/1x1 phone endpoint. The result does not
yet establish the maximum robust MCS. Before resuming rank or producer-stall
headroom, reconstruct the already-tested higher-MCS endpoint evidence and
continue only at the first untested useful rung; do not replay closed MCS
points. Bank this payoff in a new append-only final-report version.
