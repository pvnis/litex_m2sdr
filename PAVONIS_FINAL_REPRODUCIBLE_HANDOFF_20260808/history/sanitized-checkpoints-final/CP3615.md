# Checkpoint 3615: phone 15 MHz all-MCS19 OTA endpoint

Date: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS for access and data, REJECT as the throughput operating point. The first
controller invocation used a wrong relative path and exited 127 before the
executor, RF, or attempt creation. Fresh `_02` is the sole RF attempt and is a
valid success.

All 512 bounded new-transmission rows select MCS19; the dominant grant is 79
PRBs, 6913 bytes, one layer. The phone attaches in 33.611 seconds and completes
both 45-second transfers, but downlink is only **12.623976 Mbit/s** and uplink
is 0.205853 Mbit/s. Downlink is 45.5535% below CP3613 MCS17 even though the
dominant TBS is 12.4614% larger. PUSCH also falls from CP3613's 256/256 to
236/256 CRC OK, with 20 CRC failures; 12 RAR allocations indicate a materially
rougher access epoch.

The producer remains exact after one recovered 520-sample partial: zero stream
deficit, timeout, error, deadline break, or downlink-late event. The regression
therefore is not a producer-stall result. MCS19 is functionally decodable but
not performant on this OTA endpoint. Test MCS18 as the missing bracket; MCS17
remains the current throughput maximum.

Evidence is in `cp3615_compact_evidence.json` and `cp3615_manifest.sha256`.
No credential, subscriber identity, device identity, host identity, NAS
payload, or user payload is included.
