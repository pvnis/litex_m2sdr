## Verdict

PASS for access and data, FAIL for MCS17 throughput reproducibility. This is an
unchanged repeat of the CP3613 all-MCS17 endpoint. All 512 bounded rows again
select MCS17 and the dominant grant is again 79 PRBs, 6147 bytes, one layer.
The phone attaches in 22.472 seconds and completes both 45-second transfers,
but downlink is **11.102808 Mbit/s**, 52.1142% below CP3613's 23.186008
Mbit/s. Uplink is 0.245394 Mbit/s.

The obvious non-downlink health axes remain clean: 256/256 PUSCH rows are CRC
OK, only two RAR allocations occur, and producer delivery is exact after one
recovered 670-sample partial with zero stream deficit, timeout, error, deadline
break, or downlink-late event. The selected MCS, dominant TBS, layer count, and
transport method also match CP3613.

Therefore CP3613 remains the observed peak, but it is not yet a reproducible
operating ceiling. The next campaign must explain the run-scale downlink split
between identical MCS17 endpoints before more MCS, bandwidth, MIMO, or producer
headroom work. First compare compact scheduling/HARQ and UE radio-quality
evidence from CP3613 and CP3618; add a bounded outcome trace only if existing
artifacts cannot distinguish BLER from application/transport pacing.

Evidence is in `cp3618_compact_evidence.json` and `cp3618_manifest.sha256`.

payload, or user payload is included.
