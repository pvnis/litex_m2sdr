# Checkpoint 3625: phone MCS17 late-HARQ ceiling

Date: [date-redacted]

## Verdict

PASS. CP3625 explains the previously unexplained downlink ceiling. The roughly
20 ms M2SDR transmit presentation offset is inherited as extra PUCCH feedback
delivery latency at the scheduler, exhausting all 16 downlink HARQ processes.
This is the concrete mechanism behind the approximately half-rate service
measured in CP3623.

The run is valid and lands in the low throughput class: attach completes in
44.697 seconds; the 45-second transfers sustain **11.538512 Mbit/s downlink**
and **0.264087 Mbit/s uplink**. All 512 bounded rows use MCS17; the dominant
grant is 79 PRBs, 6147 bytes, and one layer. The uplink is 256/256 CRC OK.
Producer delivery is exact after one recovered 550-sample partial, with zero
stream deficit, timeout, error, deadline break, or downlink-late event.

Both scheduler consumers are proven live. The stable completed-run log has 187
cell and 162 UE reports. In the 18 complete one-second periods with actual
downlink backlog, the scheduler records 338.111 HARQ outcomes/s and 167.722
`late_dl_harqs` skips/s. Failed PDCCH and failed UCI allocation are both zero.
The source increments this late counter only when every DL HARQ process is
allocated and waiting for ACK/NACK.

PUCCH HARQ indications arrive 23.894 ms behind their nominal slot, with only a
23.8-23.9 ms range. Subtracting configured `k1=4 ms` leaves 19.894 ms, or
305579 samples at 15.36 Msps. This agrees within 1168 samples (76 microseconds)
with the run-selected `-306747`-sample non-PRACH shift and the active 20 ms TX
offset. The lower PHY's shifted path preserves nominal slot attribution but
cannot remove the physical wall-time delay, so all 16 HARQ processes stall.

This closes MCS selection, upper-layer supply, RF level, PDCCH capacity, UCI
allocation, and producer delivery as primary causes of the ceiling. The next
intervention is a coherently reduced TX lead plus equal PRACH/non-PRACH retime,
first through a strict gNB-only producer gate. Do not move to bandwidth, MIMO,
or producer-stall headroom until that lower-latency operating point is measured.
