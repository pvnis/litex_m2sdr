# CP3627: Lead-13 HARQ-latency payoff, strict producer-health failure

## Verdict

`PASS_CAUSAL_HARQ_LATENCY_PAYOFF_STRICT_PRODUCER_HEALTH_FAIL`

The run is valid for attach, user data, throughput, and causal mechanism
analysis. It is not a qualified operating baseline because the strict producer
gate failed. The 13 ms shape must not replace the strict-clean configuration.

## Evidence

The frozen 15 MHz, 79 PRB, one-layer, all-MCS17 phone shape attached in
33.871 s. It sustained 31.155208 Mbit/s downlink and 0.391097 Mbit/s uplink for
45 s in each direction. All 512 bounded new-transmission selector rows used
MCS17; the dominant allocation was 79 PRB, 6147 bytes, and one layer. The gNB
allocated two RARs and processed 256/256 PUSCH CRC-OK rows.

The sole experimental change from the CP3625 mechanism run was the coherent
20 ms to 13 ms TX-lead reduction and its coupled UL timing. The dynamic
non-PRACH publisher selected `-199227` samples, exactly matching the CP3626
prediction. Against CP3625, mean PUCCH HARQ delay fell from 23.894 to
16.889 ms, HARQ outcomes rose from 338.111 to 719.511 per second, and
application downlink rose from 11.538512 to 31.155208 Mbit/s. This is a
170.011% increase over CP3625 and a 34.371% increase over the previous
31-checkpoint campaign peak of 23.186008 Mbit/s. The result confirms that the
old ceiling was primarily feedback-latency/HARQ-process exhaustion, not RF,
MCS selection, PDCCH allocation, or UCI allocation.

During 45 complete backlogged metric periods, acknowledged scheduler downlink
averaged 32.604444 Mbit/s, application goodput was 95.555% of that value, BLER
was 2.097%, and mean PDSCH occupancy was 0.719867 per slot. Failed PDCCH and
failed UCI allocation remained zero. The scheduler still recorded 265 late-DL-
HARQ skips per second, so the new 31 Mbit/s one-layer ceiling is not yet fully
explained.

The strict health failure is unambiguous in the final Soapy summary: 20
timeouts, 20 deadline-write breaks, 19,998 write-deficit samples, and 200,912
stream-deficit/deadline-abandoned samples. Wrapper warning-line counts of zero
are not authoritative; the internal final stream summary is. This producer
failure is a confound for any claim that 13 ms is a deployable endpoint, but it
does not erase the completed user-data measurement or the direction and size of
the latency effect.

## Decision

Bank CP3624-CP3627 as the completed HARQ-latency campaign. Open a new,
separately numbered downlink-ceiling campaign to explain why the lead-13 run
still schedules only 0.720 PDSCH/slot and reports 265 late-HARQ skips/s. Keep
bandwidth, MIMO, and producer-stall policy changes parked until that no-RF
analysis names the next causal variable.
