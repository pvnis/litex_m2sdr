# CP3629: Residual downlink ceiling closes as HARQ-process capacity

## Verdict

`PASS_RESIDUAL_DOWNLINK_CEILING_EXPLAINED_BY_HARQ_PROCESS_CAPACITY`

No RF, source edit, build, deploy, or configuration change was made.

## Evidence

Source semantics show that `avg_pucch_harq_delay` is post-nominal-UCI
scheduler lateness measured against the previous completed scheduler slot. It
omits both `k1=4 ms` and one current-slot phase. CP3627's full process cycle is
therefore 21.888889 ms, not 16.888889 ms.

Sixteen HARQ processes at that cycle support 730.964 feedback outcomes/s.
CP3627 measured 719.511/s, or 98.433% utilization. Outcomes plus all-HARQs-
waiting skips cover 984.511 opportunities/s, and PDSCH rate matches the outcome
rate in steady state. At the measured TBS mix, BLER, and application efficiency,
the model predicts 31.651144 Mbit/s application goodput versus 31.155208 Mbit/s
observed. The residual is 1.57%, not an unexplained half-rate scheduler defect.

CP3625 used only 61.060% of its source-derived process bound and is therefore a
low-supply contrast rather than a saturated capacity reference.

## Decision

Do not alter HARQ count, cadence, PDCCH, UCI, or grant policy. The one-layer
ceiling branch is closed. Resume the capability ladder from existing evidence:
increase payload per process through bandwidth or layers, and treat reduced-
lead/high-rate producer qualification as a separate headroom branch.
