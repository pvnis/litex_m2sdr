# CP3630: 15 MHz 2x2 clears attach and isolates connected rank-2 failure

## Verdict

`PASS_DISCRIMINATOR_RANK2_CONNECTED_DOWNLINK_FAILS_AFTER_SUCCESSFUL_ATTACH`

CP3630 is a valid discriminator and a failed throughput endpoint. It is not a
void and it does not replace any working baseline.

## Evidence

The frozen CP3597 diagnostic shape runs as genuine 15 MHz, 15.36 Msps, 2T2R
with physical channel offset zero. The phone activates in 33.724 s, the private
echo passes, two RARs are allocated, and all 151 bounded PUSCH trace rows pass
CRC. The phone completes its 45 s uplink transfer at 0.231440 Mbit/s.

The fallback geometry trace records 11 rows. Every row uses two cell TX ports
but one layer with no optional precoding. The setup-class allocation is MCS3,
301-byte TBS, 40 CRBs, symbols 1-13, and k1=4. Activation proves that this
fallback Msg4/RRC-setup path now reaches the phone; CP3593's old pre-CSI Msg4
failure does not reproduce.

After CSI, every one of 137 scheduler UE metric rows reports `dl_ri=2.0`.
Across the 39 rows with downlink HARQ outcomes there are 75 ACKs and 102 NACKs,
or 57.627% BLER. Mean active downlink is only 0.004384 Mbit/s and PDSCH
occupancy 0.004462/slot. The sustained downlink request receives zero bytes and
times out, while uplink continues. Failed PDCCH, failed UCI, and late-HARQ
counts are all zero.

The two-channel producer is strict-clean: 2,326,010,680 stream samples,
2,422,927 writes, one 610-sample partial fully recovered, and zero stream
deficit, timeout, error, or deadline break. This excludes access, core routing,
fallback Msg4, scheduler control allocation, and producer cadence as the
immediate splitter. The defect is the connected rank-2 PDSCH delivery path.

## Decision

Create a sole-variable control that keeps 2T2R, channel mapping, timing,
bandwidth, power, and diagnostics unchanged but sets PDSCH `max_rank: 1`.
Recovery proves rank-2 transport/precoding is defective. Failure would move the
suspect below rank selection into the two-port connected-data path.
