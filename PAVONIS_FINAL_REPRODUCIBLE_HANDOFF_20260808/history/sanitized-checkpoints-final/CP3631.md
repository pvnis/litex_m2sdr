# CP3631 - 15 MHz 2T2R max-rank-1 no-RF gate

## Verdict

**PASS; one bounded sole-variable OTA discriminator is unlocked.** CP3631
preserves CP3630's genuine 15.36 Msps, 15 MHz, two-transmit/two-receive-port,
physical-channel-zero, timing, power, traffic, metrics, and diagnostic-binary
shape. The only effective radio-config delta is `cell_cfg.pdsch.max_rank: 1`.
No source was changed or rebuilt and no RF ran in this checkpoint.

## Rationale

CP3630 attached and passed private echo and uplink, but every connected
scheduler row selected rank two and the downlink collapsed to 57.627% BLER and
zero sustained bytes. A rank-one cap under unchanged 2T2R separates rank-two
precoding/transport from defects below rank selection. Recovery will isolate
the rank-two path. Continued failure will move the defect below that decision.

  physical-channel offset zero, while adding only `PDSCH_MAX_RANK=1`.
- The local runner and executor both fail closed with rc 2, and the closed
  executor creates no attempt directory.
- The immutable CP3121 manifest passes.
- Deployed wrapper, rank overlay, and inherited MIMO overlay hashes match their
  local pins.
- Wrapper and environment probes resolve the exact CP3597 diagnostic gNB and
  CP3591 radio/timing chain.
- The real diagnostic gNB accepts the base template plus the inherited MIMO
  overlay and the new rank overlay in `--dryrun`.
- The executor independently retrieves whichever endpoint result exists. An
  incomplete downlink can no longer hide the phone-side result merely because
  the persistent core server remains armed.
